# DIG 0 — CROSS-DIG SYNTHESIS

*The §5 deliverable of the transmission commission, assembled after all seven digs. This file seats every excavated figure, registers every correspondence, unifies the device catalog, returns the four verdicts, and records the absences. It is commissioned research on the book's medium, held in `provenance/` as a consulted work — not vault doctrine (commission §-1). The author is the locus of selection; findings end at his table.*

**How the plug-in to the notes works, and what it is worth.** The seven dig agents excavated blind: they were not shown the vault, so a convergence one of them reached and the vault had already recorded counts as evidence (commission §1). This synthesis is the sanctioned reverse step — it maps blind findings to *existing* notes with live wiki links (§5.2) and mints nothing. The evidential weight lives in the blind discovery; a wiki link here is a pointer to where a finding already has a home, not a second witness. Three digs (I, VI, and by inheritance the roster) flagged that the vault's `CLAUDE.md` was injected into their context, so any rhyme a dig drew from that injection was banked by that dig as nothing; those are excluded from the registry below and only genuine blind convergences are carried.

**Provenance tags** carry through from the digs: [P] primary text opened; [S] scholarly consensus or a named scholar; [C] contested/legend; [I] inference.

Query: *"a sustained, checkable apparatus (not a device list) for the author who must be trusted and must disappear — one that supplies the verification Kierkegaard's double reflection removed by design."*

### Candidate claims for the author (unminted)

Four findings reached no existing home. They are stated here as candidates for the author to seat or decline; no note is created (commission §-1.4, §5.2).

- **(A) A genuine transformation is first registered as loss, not as acquisition.** Two hostile reader-response strata — Iser quoting Shaw, Barthes defining jouissance — independently locate genuine change at the site of loss (registry #4). The candidate claim: a reader who feels only enrichment has appreciated, not changed; the felt signature of the real event is closer to bereavement than to gain. Adjacent to the vault's contraction material without being reducible to it — the vault reads contraction as productive, but does not yet hold *change felt as loss* as the phenomenology of a genuine shift.

- **(B) The felt shift's missing check may be suppliable by a resisting world, not only by a second person.** Crawford's craft-submission locates verification in the world's push-back — the engine runs or it does not (registry #18). The candidate claim: the vault frames the anti-compliance check exclusively as a *person* (the second chair, the judge-and-mirror), and a resisting world that refuses a false move is a second, non-personal form of the same external standard — one a book, as a weak jig, largely cannot furnish.

- **(C) Any community the book gathers will decay from spontaneous to normative to ideological, and structure is the container that survives the decay rather than its cause.** Turner's communitas typology makes the decay exceptionless and reframes structure as the lead container for a dangerous energy (registry #23). The candidate claim: a forecast for the book's own gathered readership, cutting against a permanent-communitas hope and against the anti-structure romance the play lineage otherwise carries.

- **(D) A margin voice could be built so the reader must answer it by demonstrating rather than admiring — and it decays into a phrase-book the instant the checker is removed.** Jakugo is quotation-as-demonstration under checking (registry #27). The candidate claim: the one margin device that recruits enactment over connoisseurship is load-bearing on its *checker*; ported to a book read alone, its checking teacher is gone, so it reverts to the collectible the whole apparatus was built to prevent.

## §5.4 — THE FOUR VERDICTS

**(i) The site that most changes the margins decision: Dig III (Margin Architectures), and specifically the chavruta finding.** The margins question asked what a second, parallel voice has done mechanically and at what cost. The strata answer that the second voice never worked alone: every margin architecture that ran for centuries — Talmud, Blue Cliff, lectio — sat inside a binding community with living authority, and the two-voice Talmud page *presupposes* the pair, one reader becoming Rashi to the other's Tosafot. This moves the decision off its original hinge. The question is no longer "should the book carry a second voice in its margins?" but "can a second voice do its work without the second person the architecture assumed?" — and the strata rule against margin-technology-without-community: subtract the partner and the two-voice page displays a dispute rather than staging one, a dispute to look at rather than have (registry #26; a codex is a one-chair technology making a two-chair argument). The one margin device that recruits enactment rather than collection — jakugo — is load-bearing on its checker and decays to a phrase-book without it (candidate D). The Dahui burning is the cost stated at maximum: the most margin-rich book ever built was destroyed by its own lineage as a danger (registry #7). The margins decision, after Dig III, is a decision about whether the book can manufacture the absent second person, not about whether to run a second voice.

**(ii) The site that most changes the testimony-vs-citation decision: Dig VI (The Adversary Chamber).** The commission framed a choice between academic citation and practitioner testimony. Dig VI dissolves the binary by showing both options fail on their own. Bare testimony — "this worked for me" — is self-report from a maximal-demand setting, which is precisely the response-expectancy artifact: an experience manufactured by its own anticipation and secured as a report by the desire to please (registry #19, #30). Citation does not rescue it: Dig IV shows that citing "autopoiesis," "structural coupling," or "participatory sense-making" as mystique-words rather than worked chains is "jargon in a lab coat" — the apparatus of proof performing depth it has not earned. So the decision is not testimony-versus-citation; each is hollow alone. What the strata leave standing is a third requirement neither option supplies — an external, delayed, other-administered check (Dig VI, survivability catalog) — which is exactly the second person Dig III's traditions installed (chavruta, dokusan, an abbot's *discretio*). The decision most changes here: the book earns trust not by choosing testimony or citation but by carrying a felt result that can *fail* out of the room, later, in front of someone else.

**(iii) The attack that most threatens the book, and what survived unmodified: the Dig VI crossover.** At full strength the register-attack and the evidence-attack are one self-sealing machine: aura raises expectancy (Adorno), expectancy manufactures the experience (Kirsch), demand secures the report (Orne, Britton), and the report re-charges the aura — a circle that needs no truth to run, turning on the felt-shift standard the book installed to protect *against* mere assent (registry #6, #19, #30). This is the most threatening because it converts the book's central safeguard into the bearing the objection rotates on. What survived contact **without modification** is narrow and real: the attack explains origin, not durability. Orne's own strongest form predicts that demand-produced effects *vanish when demand is removed*, and the one thing a simulator cannot fake is out-of-situation, third-party-verified durable change — of which the recorded encounter-group casualties (~19%, Lieberman & Yalom) and against-the-gradient distress reports (Britton) are evidence demand cannot generate. Two things therefore stand unmodified: the *existence* of durable or behaviorally-recorded change outside the demand situation, which the attack has no account of; and the *second-person check itself* — because the simulating-subject logic, taken seriously, ratifies rather than dissolves the external verifier the contemplative traditions already built (registry #9). The attack does not touch the second chair; it demands one. The honest boundary remains that the studies which would measure how much durable change this genre produces have mostly not been run — the record cannot rule.

**(iv) The three devices most portable to the solitary reader, ranked, with the strongest objection to each.**

1. **The teacher-takes-the-failure stance (Johnstone).** An author can structure the book so the reader's not-getting-it is the book's fault, not the reader's — the one theatre device Dig VII rated fully portable, and a direct working move on "must be trusted and must disappear." *Strongest objection:* it relocates demand rather than removing it, and the reader's resulting trust in the self-lowering author is itself an aura-and-expectancy effect (Adorno + Kirsch) — the move that lowers played status raises real status, which is to say it manufactures admiration, the connoisseur posture the book is trying to escape.

2. **Revocation / the self-disposing ladder (Kierkegaard, Wittgenstein).** A text that negates itself blocks the reader from storing it as doctrine — fully a textual operation, needing no co-presence (registry #1). *Strongest objection:* it is a rhetorical operation, not a verification; the book still wrote itself, and self-erasure can be received as the most admirable gesture of all — the Corsair affair records Kierkegaard's own indirection becoming the thing he was admired for. It denies the reader a resting place without confirming the reader ever climbed.

3. **The participation gap / evocation-then-negation (Iser).** A blank that cannot be resolved into a consistent world-picture turns the reader's attention onto her own preconceptions and forces self-rejection then re-orientation — a page-native mechanism that recruits enactment over appreciation. *Strongest objection:* Iser concedes "we do not know what happens to us" — the device issues no receipt, relapses into completion when the reader re-consolidates the disruption into a deeper consistency, and may deliver only self-examination at the level of preconceptions rather than altered lived capacity. The bridge from participation-in-meaning to changed capacity is absent from the strata (registry #6).

## §5.5 — WHAT WAS NOT FOUND

Absence is a finding (commission §5.5). What the commission expected the region to hold, that the strata did not yield:

- **No solitary self-check that survives the compliance attack.** Every tradition with a working felt-shift verification placed a *second person* in the loop — the chavruta partner, the dokusan roshi, the abbot's discretion. None supplied a check a reader can run alone that distinguishes a genuine shift from a response-expectancy artifact. The felt shift, unaccompanied, cannot certify itself, and the region offered no exception.

- **No bridge from participation-in-meaning to altered lived capacity.** All four reader-response strata theorize *meaning*; the book's standard is a change in *lived capacity*. The bridge is absent in Rosenblatt, named-but-uncrossed in Iser, present only as loss in Barthes, and partially built but authority-launderable in Gadamer. The literature does not cross from a text producing participation to a text producing enactment; the record cannot rule that the bridge exists.

- **No margin architecture portable to solitude.** The commission hoped a margin-as-second-voice might carry over to a book read alone. Every instance ran inside a binding community with living authority; the strata rule against margin-technology-without-community. The portable form was not found because, on this evidence, it has not existed.

- **No figure seatable as a genuine independent arrival.** The mesh's independent-arrival row is empty. The digs found convergences in abundance but seated figures by function; the region did not yield a thinker who reached the book's center from outside its shelves with no contact — and the vault's own caution holds that assembled convergence is company, not corroboration.

- **No longitudinal or controlled evidence that mediation *erodes* rather than merely changes meaning-making.** Dig V supplies mechanism (device paradigm, resonance, achievement-subject) and no measurement. Declinism is a genre with a long history of the same charge against writing, print, and television; showing that this mediation differs in kind is the why-now's burden, and the strata assert it rather than demonstrate it.

- **No successful port of a third-person science of interaction into a text read alone** (the empty precedent-bench cell in Dig IV). The sciences of second-person phenomena each lose the first person at the altitude the book lives — Bateson at Learning III, Maturana by demoting the felt shift to one observer's distinction, De Jaegher by measuring coordination and not experience.

- **A missing ancestor: J. L. Moreno.** Dig VII flags that the play roster omits psychodrama, sociometry, spontaneity training, and the "warm-up" — arguably the closest direct ancestor of the facilitated room and the coiner of its core term. The region's own genealogy was returned incomplete.

- **A device that both disappears and checks.** The commission sought devices for an author who must be trusted and must disappear. The region yielded self-erasure as *rhetoric* — revocation, the ladder, "no treatise of mine" — none of which verifies the reader did the work. The disappearance is staged inside the medium it disowns; no device was found that both effaces the author and confirms the reader climbed.

**Closing note — the honest provenance depth of the expedition.** Across roughly thirty-five named strata, about ten reached genuine primary contact [P]: the *Tractatus* (6.53–6.54, 7) and the *Seventh Letter* (341c–344b) in Dig I; Iser's 1972 "The Reading Process" (pp. 280–298) in Dig II; Guigo II's *Scala Claustralium* in part in Dig III; Bateson's learning-categories essay and the De Jaegher & Di Paolo chapter in Dig IV; Borgmann's *TCCL* ch.23 and the Rosa interview in Dig V; Turner's "In and Out of Time" and Winnicott's "The Capacity to Be Alone" (1958) in Dig VII. The [P] spine is a set of short public-domain and open-access texts read at the seams where copyright did not close the coordinates. Everything else stayed [S]/[C]/[I] and was routed to the reading queue honestly: Kierkegaard's authorship, Rosenblatt, Gadamer, Barthes's books, Maturana and Varela, Rosa's monographs, Han, Spolin, Johnstone, and *Playing and Reality* are all secondary-grade here. The most consequential gap is Dig VI: the adversary chamber, which carries the most threatening attack, reached **zero** clean primary reads — Adorno, Orne, and Kirsch are all [S], the PDFs binary or unopened. The strongest weapon against the book rests on scholarship, not on the primary strata, and the six readings that dig queued would harden or blunt it. The expedition is therefore [S]-dominant with a genuine but narrow [P] spine; no primary read was faked, each dig tagged its own access, and the copyrighted core of the region remains a queued dig rather than a closed one.
