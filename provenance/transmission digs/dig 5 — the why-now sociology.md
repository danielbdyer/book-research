## FINDINGS

**Rosa — resonance's four moving parts.** (1) *affection* — being touched, bodily and cognitively; (2) *response* — answering the call, "self-efficacy in this encounter"; (3) *transformation* — one "comes out as a different person," and so does the other (co-production); (4) *elusiveness* — never guaranteed, outcome unpredictable [P: Rosa, interview w/ Schiermer, p.3]. The fourth is load-bearing: resonance "is not consonance, it requires the active presence of something that is beyond my grasp" [P: p.4-5, <25wd]; it "means becoming vulnerable and losing control" [P: p.5].

**Rosa — three axes.** Horizontal (people: love, friendship, democratic politics); diagonal/material (objects via work, sport, education, consumption); vertical (world/nature/life via religion, art, nature, history) [P: p.4].

**Rosa — Triple-A, the disease.** Modernity runs on making the world *available, accessible, attainable*, money and technology the horizon-extenders [P: p.2-3]. The pathology: aggressive reach mutes the world — "self and world turn pale, cold and indifferent. This... is the state of 'burnout'" [P: p.3, <25wd]. Resonance is burnout's opposite, explicitly *not* slowness — Rosa disowns "the prophet of deceleration" [P: p.6].

**Rosa — institutional conditions (condition-design at scale).** Resonance capacity is universal; its *axes* form in social-historical context: "A capitalist society which forces us into a mode of competition, optimisation and speed... enforces a non-resonant, instrumental, reified mode" [P: p.4, <25wd]. Structural driver — *dynamic stabilisation*: modernity needs growth/acceleration/innovation merely to hold station, so one must "run faster and faster each year just to stay in place" [P: p.6]. Prescriptions: cut competition; UBI via inheritance tax; work valued for resonance not productivity [S: Rosa 2016 p.760-761].

**Borgmann — the device paradigm.** A device splits into *commodity* (foregrounded, disburdening) and *machinery* (concealed): central heating delivers "warmth" while wood, splitting, tending recede [P: ch.23 p.1; S: Verbeek]. The irony: technology promised enrichment, removes engagement [S: Verbeek].

**Borgmann — focal thing.** *Focus* = hearth, which "sustained, ordered, and centered house and family" [P: ch.23 p.2, <25wd]. A focal thing "gathers the relations of its context and radiates into its surroundings," concrete, of commanding presence, "a center of orientation" — wilderness, music, gardening, the table, running [P: p.2-3].

**Borgmann — focal PRACTICE (the criterion that disqualifies the one-off).** Focal things prosper "in human practices only" [P: p.6]; a practice arises "through resoluteness" — a vow to engage regularly, or an implicit resolve that "matures into a settled custom" [P: p.15]. Decisive: "without a practice an engaging action or event can momentarily light up our life, but it cannot order and orient it focally" [P: p.14, <25wd] — grounded in Aristotle's *ethos* and MacIntyre's practice [P: p.14].

**Borgmann — worked mechanisms.** In running "effort and joy are one; the split between means and ends... is healed" [P: p.9, <25wd] — unlike treadmill exercise (a device procuring a health-commodity). The festive meal gathers family and tradition and enacts discipline; the Big Mac is its device-collapse — "the sequence of courses has been compacted into one object" [P: p.11-12, <25wd].

**Han — the achievement-subject (the argument).** In steps: (1) the disciplinary society of negativity — prohibition, "should not," an external enemy — has passed; (2) replaced by the *achievement society* of positivity — "unlimited Can," projects, motivation; (3) with no external Other to resist, violence turns systemic and self-generated — the subject is "master and slave in one"; (4) auto-exploitation is *more efficient* than allo-exploitation "for the feeling of freedom attends it"; (5) the surplus of positivity yields neuronal disorders (depression, ADHD, burnout), not infection [S: *Burnout Society* 2015]. Extensions: loss of *vita contemplativa* and lingering (*Scent of Time*); neoliberal "smart power" exploits freedom itself (*Psychopolitics*) [S].

**Crawford (satellite).** The *jig* (a constraint that offloads cognition onto the world) models how skilled practices build "ecologies of attention"; individuation happens by *submitting* to a craft's hard constraints and community, which "tell us the truth more reliably than the voices in our heads" [S] — against the buffered Enlightenment self.

**Odell (satellite).** "Nothing" is not detox but *refusal-in-place* — attention withdrawn from the extractive economy and redirected to bioregion and other species; the same argument in a popular register, disconnection-privilege self-flagged [S].

## DEVICES (mechanism / protects against / cost / where it breaks)

- **Uncontrollability (Unverfügbarkeit).** The sought good withdraws when grasped (snow melting on the hand) [S; P interview p.4-5]. Protects against the engineering posture. Breaks: you cannot *commission* the uncommandable — it resists institutionalisation by definition [I].
- **Resonance/echo/consonance triad.** Discrimination device: resonance keeps difference and includes the other; *echo* is fusion and exclusion (fascist "zones of resonance"); *consonance* is silencing harmony [S: Rosa 2016 p.742-743]. Breaks: the sort is *stipulated*, not felt.
- **Device paradigm.** Commodity/machinery split → disburdenment → disengagement [P ch.23]. Breaks: some devices *amplify* engagement (Verbeek).
- **Focal practice.** Resoluteness → regular communal devotion → settled ethos [P ch.23 p.14-15]. Protects against the episodic epiphany. Cost: leisure, skill, community. Breaks: privatises into "humble and scattered" personal regimen.
- **The jig (Crawford).** Outsource choice to a physical constraint so skill flows. Breaks: presupposes a world that pushes back — thin online [I].
- **Refusal-in-place (Odell).** Relocate attention rather than flee. Breaks: privilege-gated.

## FAILURES (mandatory)

- **Rosa — the fascism problem.** He concedes fascist formations hold "excellent techniques of resonance" and build "highly effective zones of resonance" [S: Susen citing Rosa 2016 p.742, <25wd]. His guard (naming "ideological resonance," p.319; and resonance≠echo) is a *normative stipulation*, not a phenomenological finding: from inside, ideological resonance feels like resonance. Susen: without "solid normative foundations" almost any constellation reads as "empowering." The felt quality cannot self-certify.
- **Rosa — power-blindness, admitted.** He grants "a book on the relationship between power and resonance still needs to be written" [S: Rosa 2016 p.757]; charged with "socio-ontological romanticism" (lifeworlds as "power-free realms of pristine intersubjectivity") and with being "apolitical," which he half-accepts [S: p.760].
- **Borgmann — the subjective-state trap (self-inflicted, aimed at the book).** He warns that taking a focal event "in the subjective sense" — as "transporting a person into a certain mental or emotional state" — makes it "fall under the rule of technology," because a machinery is then sought to procure that state "more instantaneously, ubiquitously, more assuredly" [P: ch.23 p.12, <25wd]. His own evidence (Sheehan, Capon, Wood) is uncontrolled first-person testimony. He also concedes "an inappropriate nostalgia clings to Heidegger's account" and only claims to dispel it [P: p.1].
- **Han — diagnosis without prescription.** No institutional remedy, no collective agent; his counters (deep boredom, contemplation, the "idiot," inoperativity) are individual and gestural, and his critique of hypermediation circulates as aphorism through the circuits it condemns [S: Karataş 2026].

## THE STRONGEST CASE AGAINST THE WHY-NOW
*Steelmanned from these strata's own critics. No rebuttal.*

1. **Declinism is a genre, not a finding [C].** Writing (Plato), print, the novel, radio, television were each charged in their hour with corroding the faculties of development. The why-now is that complaint's newest costume; showing *this* mediation differs in kind is the burden, and the strata assert it rather than demonstrate it.
2. **There was no Eden to fall from [S: Susen].** "Socio-ontological romanticism" projects a "power-free realm of pristine intersubjectivity" that never existed; instrumental and reifying relations are "always already" in the lifeworld before any device. The baseline is fictional.
3. **"Engagement" is an equivocation [S: Verbeek].** Borgmann slides from engagement-as-exertion to engagement-as-meaning; that effort falls does not entail meaning falls. Devices demonstrably *amplify* engagement (recorded music widens musical life). "Focal" is aesthetic preference promoted to ontology.
4. **The goods are class goods [S: Odell self-admitted].** Resonance, focal practice, doing nothing, craft-attention are affordances of the secure; the two-job worker cannot "prepare for the uncontrollable." The critique indicts the very conditions that grant its author the leisure to make it.
5. **The criterion is normatively empty [S: Susen].** If experiential quality is the test, reactionary experience passes — fascism has "excellent techniques of resonance." Absent an external standard the strata do not supply, the why-now cannot tell participation from capture.
6. **It is unfalsifiable and uncontrolled [S: Orne].** "Resonance," "focal," "epochal burnout" carry no operational measure; they are certified by the theorist's assertion and first-person testimony from primed settings — a demand characteristic.
7. **The medium refutes the message [S: Karataş].** The critique thrives as the attention economy's premium content — Han's aphorisms go viral, Odell's refusal a bestseller, Rosa's slowness an 800-page commodity. Were the diagnosis true it could not circulate so well.

## REMAINDER

The whole shelf is written from inside sated modernity, and its remainder is the standpoint it never occupies. **From outside comfortable modernity the why-now inverts** [I]: for the disabled user, the isolated queer adolescent, the diaspora migrant, the device *is* the vertical and horizontal axis — the phone carrying the sacred and the beloved across distance. The loss-narrative presupposes a prior fullness many never held, and the literature barely asks (Odell nods once to privilege; Rosa gestures at UBI). Against Adorno's instrument: the dig's most eloquent moments — Rosa's snow, Borgmann's "bread and wine," Han's *scent of time* — are the aura-language *The Jargon of Authenticity* attacks, and Rosa writes to spell out "an auratic, mimetic, erotic mode of life" [P: p.6-7], embracing the register the adversary weaponises. On whether mediation *erodes* rather than merely changes the activities of meaning-making, **the record cannot rule** — the strata supply mechanism, never longitudinal evidence.

## FELT-SHIFT AUDIT

- **Rosa:** resonance *is* the felt shift (touched, self-efficacy, transformed). But uncontrollability makes it uncommandable (refuses the life-hack reader — SOUGHT), and his reply to the normativity critique needs the first-person report *combined with a third-person observer* to sort genuine from ideological resonance [S] — so the felt shift alone is, by his own account, insufficient.
- **Borgmann:** the felt shift is *rejected* as standard — treating the "subjective state" as decisive is what commodifies the event [P p.12]; the in-situ standard is the *practice*, the settled ethos over time.
- **Han:** no verification standard; the diagnosis is assertion — maximally exposed to both instruments.
- **Crawford:** the dig's strongest anti-compliance standard — the *world's resistance* verifies (does the engine run?), checking the felt sense against reality [S].
- **Odell:** perceptual shift (noticing birds), weakly operationalised, privilege-exposed.

## SEAT VERDICTS

- **Rosa — load-bearing.** The strongest civilizational-scale scaffolding under the why-now *and* the sociological ground for the felt-shift's refusal of the instrumental reader — bolted to its own fascism/normativity fault.
- **Borgmann — ground for running machinery.** The device paradigm supplies the mechanism, the focal-practice criteria the working test (practice over episode); his subjective-state warning is the sharpest internal stress-test of the book's standard.
- **Han — directional.** A precise portrait of the reader's condition that points the direction but structurally cannot supply a remedy; carry the diagnosis, not a program.
- **Crawford — precedent bench.** A worked case that craft-submission builds a self, with an external verification standard worth borrowing.
- **Odell — precedent bench.** The argument's popular-register restatement; evidence of reach, privilege-flagged.

## SURPRISED CONVERGENCES

1. **Borgmann's subjective-state warning ↔ the felt-shift standard** (§0 Q3; the felt-shift itself). SURPRISED, high weight. Unbidden, he says treating a focal event as a "mental or emotional state" makes it commodifiable — a machine will be built to deliver the state [P p.12]. The felt shift is a subjective-state criterion; a why-now theorist warns it invites the exact life-hack capture the book fears. His fix, *practice*, a solitary-read book cannot install.
2. **Rosa's fascism concession ↔ genuine-vs-false shift / demand characteristic** (§0 Q2; Orne). SURPRISED, high weight. Felt quality cannot tell resonance from ideological echo; Rosa needs an external normative supplement, as would any felt-shift standard. His combine-first-and-third-person reply is a candidate anti-compliance move.
3. **Rosa embraces "aura" ↔ Adorno's Jargon** (§0 Q3; Adorno). SURPRISED. The scaffolding under the why-now is built from the aura/mimesis material the adversary chamber exists to attack [P interview p.6-7].
4. **Borgmann: practice over episode ↔ enactment over appreciation** (§0 Q3). SURPRISED-adjacent. "Momentarily light up" vs. "order and orient" [P p.14] is the connoisseur/enactor line, via Aristotle's ethos.
5. **Crawford's external verification ↔ anti-compliance testimony** (§0 Q4). SURPRISED. The craft "tells us the truth more reliably than the voices in our heads" — reality, not the room, certifies.
6. **Rosa's uncontrollability ↔ the felt-shift's anti-instrumentalism** (§0 Q5). SOUGHT (commission-flagged), lower weight. Resonance "cannot be commanded... only prepared for."

## READINGS TO QUEUE

1. **Rosa, *The Uncontrollability of the World* (2020).** Does uncontrollability ground a *normative* claim (we ought to relate to the uncontrollable) or only a phenomenological description? Stake: whether the anti-instrumental felt-shift has a philosophical floor or only an aesthetic one. Address: "the four elements of the aggressive relation"; "resonance as relation to the uncontrollable." Reached: [S]+[P interview].
2. **Rosa, *Resonanz* (2016), institutional-axes part.** Does Rosa give *operational* indicators of resonance-probability per institution (family, school, work, politics, religion, art, nature), or only illustration? Stake: whether "condition-design at scale" is buildable or rhetorical. Address: pp.317-321, 741-757, 760-761. Reached: [S] via Susen's page-cites.
3. **Han, *The Burnout Society* (2015) + *Psychopolitics*.** Does Han name *any* institutional or collective lever, or only individual contemplative gestures? Stake: whether the portrait yields anything actionable. Address: "The Pedagogy of Seeing," "Vita Contemplativa"; "Idiotism." Reached: [S].
4. **Borgmann, *TCCL* (1984) ch.9.** Does the machinery/commodity split survive the case where the concealed machinery is *another person's labor* (care, teaching, facilitation)? Stake: whether facilitation is a focal practice or a device. Reached: [P] ch.23 only; ch.9 via Verbeek [S].
5. **Crawford, *The World Beyond Your Head* (2015), the jig / "attentional commons" chapters.** Does individuation-through-craft-submission require co-presence, or port to solitary reading? Stake: portability of the anti-compliance standard to a book. Reached: [S].
