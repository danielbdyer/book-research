## FINDINGS

### Bateson — the learning hierarchy, verbatim-close [P]

Definitions quoted from the standalone 1971 essay (in *Steps to an Ecology of Mind*, 1972):

- **Zero learning**: a fixed specificity of response, right or wrong, *not subject to correction*. [P]
- **Learning I**: change in that specificity by error-correction *within a set of alternatives* — the set holds still; the choice within it changes (conditioning, extinction). [P]
- **Learning II**: change in the process of Learning I — a corrective change in the *set* of alternatives, or in how experience is *punctuated*. [P] This is **deutero-learning** — his 1942 "learning to learn" and "set learning" are named synonyms (essay p. 293; ref. 4). [P] Here character and self are built: "Selfhood is a product or aggregate of Learning II." [P]
- **Learning III**: change in the process of Learning II — a corrective change in the *system of sets* of alternatives. [P] The warning sits inside the definition: "to demand this level of performance … is sometimes pathogenic." [P]
- **Learning IV**: change in Learning III, which "probably does not occur in any adult living organism on this earth." [P]

Each level is a *distinct logical type* (Russell's theory of types): "change in the process of learning X" is categorically higher than X — the distinctness is logical, not quantitative. Spine under **claim (i): learning-to-learn is a real, distinct level.** [P/S]

**The danger stratum** (the boundary-marker the dig was sent for). Learning III is "difficult and rare," reached through "psychotherapy, religious conversion, and … profound reorganization of character," and "totally beyond the reach of language." [P] The payoff: "Even the attempt at level III can be dangerous, and some fall by the wayside. These are often labeled by psychiatry as psychotic." [P] Successful resolution collapses Learning II so "the identified self is no longer in charge of organizing the behavior" [P]; the creative resolution reveals a world where "personal identity merges into all the processes of relationship." [P] **The same self-dissolution phenomenology belongs to liberation and to breakdown, and he gives no marker to tell them apart.** [P] Keep this attached (contamination warning honored).

**Map Learning I/II/III → practitioner / facilitator / facilitation-of-facilitation** [I]. Seductive: I corrects within a frame, II changes the frame, III changes the frames that generate frames. It strains three ways: III is *pathogenic and rare*, not a trainable grade; the levels are *logical types*, not a career ladder; a facilitator-of-facilitators plausibly runs on high, flexible Learning II with no III-style self-collapse. Carry it as analogy, not biology.

### Maturana & Varela — the impossibility of instructive interaction [S; autopoiesis C/S]

The chain (from scholarship; *Tree of Knowledge*, 1987, not opened — queued):

1. A living system is **autopoietic** — a network of processes producing the components that produce the network. [C/S — not settled consensus]
2. This entails **operational closure**: every process is conditioned by others in the network. [S]
3. Closure makes the system **structurally determined**: what it can do is fixed by present structure, not by the environment. [S]
4. So an interaction can **trigger** but not **specify** a change — "the living being is a source of perturbations and not of instructions." [S]
5. Hence **instructive interaction is impossible**: nothing external installs its form into the system. [S]
6. What recurs instead is **structural coupling** — organism and medium as reciprocal sources of perturbation over a history. [S]
7. In nervous systems this yields **consensual coordinations of behavior**; coordinations of coordinations yield **languaging**. [S]
8. Language is therefore coordinated action in a consensual domain, not transmitted information — the spine under **claim (iii): meaning is generated in interaction, not transmitted.** [S]
9. The system's changes remain *its own*; a teacher, at most, perturbs — the spine under **claim (ii): perturb, never install.** [S]
10. "Everything said is said by an observer" — every distinction, this theory included, is drawn by an observer inside a domain. [S]

**Standing critique (observer/solipsism).** If cognition brings forth a world and every claim is an observer's distinction, the account courts **idealism/solipsism** — a quasi-Kantian in-itself/for-observer split with the observer smuggled in ungrounded. [S] Maturana's reply, "objectivity-in-parentheses," is a razor's-edge middle held by recurrent structural congruence; critics answer that the observer's metadomain is asserted, not earned, and the middle reimports the realism it disclaimed. The record cannot rule; mark autopoiesis-as-cognition [C/S].

### De Jaegher & Di Paolo — participatory sense-making, with evidence [P]

Read in full: the chapter variant "Making Sense in Participation" (cites the 2007 *Phenomenology and the Cognitive Sciences* 6(4):485–507 article as its ref. [2]).

- **Social interaction** (§N.3): a regulated coupling between two or more autonomous agents in which the regulation targets "aspects of the coupling itself," yielding an emergent autonomous relational organization that does not destroy the agents' own autonomy. [P] The interaction becomes *its own* system — Goffman, in-text: "a conversation has a life of its own." [P]
- **Participatory sense-making** (§N.4): "the interactive coordination of intentional activity affecting individual sense-making, whereby new domains of sense-making may appear that were unavailable to each solitary individual." [P] Spectrum: modulation → orientation → joint sense-making.
- **Coordination** = "the non-accidental correlation between two systems beyond what is expected"; it "does not generally require any sophisticated skill … it is … often difficult to avoid." [P] Schmidt & O'Brien's pendulum pairs cannot stop synchronizing once they watch each other; the social case is *relative* coordination (adult and child "walking together" without phase-locking). [P]
- **Breakdown-and-repair as generative** (flagged): "Recovering from a breakdown in coordination takes the role of a learning event whereby new contextual significance is acquired." [P] Worked case: a video-call delay inserts a pause; read as disagreement, it bends both speakers toward a moderation neither held. Rupture *does the meaning-making* — the science facilitation's rupture-repair craft has lacked. [P]
- **Evidence** (§N.5): (a) **perceptual crossing** (Auvray/Lenay/Stewart; Di Paolo/Rohde/Iizuka) — in a 1-D minimal space, individuals *cannot* tell a live partner from a non-contingent shadow copying them, yet the *dyad* reliably finds itself, because the interaction avoids the confusible situations; discrimination lives in the coupling, not the head. [P] (b) **Murray & Trevarthen's double-video** — 2-month-olds sustain live exchange but are distressed by a non-contingent *recording* of mother. [P]

## DEVICES

- **Bateson's Learning-III maneuvers** (in a "controlled and protected setting"): confront the patient's premises; make him *act* against them; demonstrate their contradiction; induce caricature — then **block the loopholes** ("like a mosquito biting on an iron bar"). [P] Mechanism: engineered contraries with escape sealed. Cost: the double bind that frees can injure, and the setting must be "protected" — a book read alone is not. Learning II itself is *measured* as fewer trials on each successive same-type problem (an inner change with an external signature). [P]
- **Controlled-perturbation probing** (De Jaegher §N.5): "Interactive factors affecting coordination may be uncovered by their signature response to controlled perturbation methods." [P] Read interaction-level structure by disturbing it and watching recovery.

**Polanyi (satellite) [S].** *The Tacit Dimension* (1966): "we know more than we can tell." Tacit knowing has a **from–to structure** — we attend *from* subsidiary particulars *to* a focal whole. The pianist who shifts focal attention to his fingers falters and stops (**destructive analysis**: focusing on particulars effaces their joint meaning). [S] Implication: a practice works only while its components stay subsidiary/unwatched, so **self-conscious technique degrades performance** — predicting that a reader told to watch her own felt shift may abolish it. [S/I]

**von Foerster (one paragraph) [S].** Second-order cybernetics folds the observer into the system ("objectivity is the delusion that observations could be made without an observer"). His **ethical imperative** — "Act always so as to increase the number of choices" — is a candidate facilitation ethic: open doors, not fences; ethics is enacted, not stated ("it is clear that Ethics cannot be articulated"). [S]

## FAILURES (mandatory)

- **Bateson.** The double-bind *etiology* of schizophrenia is empirically unsupported and was abandoned; its "schizophrenogenic mother" corollary did documented harm; Bateson himself later backed off the causal claim. [S] The method the book most wants (Learning III) is the one he could not measure, verify, or safely recommend.
- **Maturana & Varela.** They split: Varela turned to first-person **neurophenomenology** — the program's own admission that the biology had lost the first person; Maturana drifted toward self-sealing "biology of love" rhetoric. [S] Sharpest on-target attack: **"instructive interaction is impossible," applied reflexively, is a knife at the book's own throat** — if no text installs anything, the book cannot transmit, only perturb a reader who determines her own change.
- **De Jaegher & Di Paolo.** The interaction-autonomy criterion is attacked as **too broad** (two cells in a dish, two negotiating states qualify) and **normatively arbitrary** (why does coercion "destroy autonomy" but ordinary influence not?). [S] The constitution claim (Gallagher, 2010) is disputed; the evidence — a 1-D minimal paradigm — is thin for the weight placed on it. Enactivism argues with itself (Hutto: a false either/or) — the arguments are strata, not a chorus.
- **Polanyi.** The strong thesis that tacit knowing is *irreducible* is contested (Collins: much "tacit" knowledge is explicable). [S] And "don't watch your fingers" is a *performance-moment* rule wrongly generalized to the *learning phase*, where deliberate self-conscious attention is how skill is built.
- **von Foerster.** "Increase choices" is **formally near-empty** — it cannot rank choices, and a tyrant maximizing his own options satisfies it; second-order cybernetics also died as an institutional program. [S/I]

## REMAINDER

Third-person sciences of a second-person phenomenon; what each *loses* of the first person, and whether it admits it:

- **Bateson** loses the phenomenology at exactly the altitude the book lives — Learning III is "beyond the reach of language" — and *admits* it: its center is where his method goes dark.
- **Maturana/Varela** do not lose the first person so much as **dissolve its authority** — a felt shift is one observer's distinction, certifying nothing trans-observer; they do not admit this guts a "verifying experience." Varela's later turn is the off-stage admission.
- **De Jaegher/Di Paolo** name experience but *measure* only coordination dynamics; the first person is gestured at, not operationalized — an honest, unfinished loss.
- **Polanyi** loses the least (personal knowledge, indwelling), losing replicability instead, which he makes a virtue.

Against the **Adorno instrument**: "structural coupling," "autopoiesis," "participatory sense-making" are precisely terms that radiate significance independent of content; if the book *cites* them as mystique-words rather than *works the chain*, it is jargon in a lab coat — carry mechanism as mechanism or drop the word. Against the **Orne instrument**: the science does not rescue the felt shift and in places deepens the worry — Bateson gives self-dissolution no discriminating marker from psychosis (what a high-demand setting manufactures as compliance), and De Jaegher's "feeling of connectedness … getting easier" is exactly the report that coordination-*with-the-facilitator* produces whether or not anything durable changed.

## FELT-SHIFT AUDIT

What counts as *measurement*, and whether the felt shift survives it:

- **Bateson.** I/II have operational measures; **Learning III has none** — so the felt shift is the only access, and his warning is that it *cannot* distinguish liberation from breakdown. It fails as a reliable standard at the book's deepest altitude. [P]
- **Maturana/Varela.** Measurement is the observer's description of recurrent structural congruence; the felt shift is only another distinction-in-a-domain — the standard *demotes* it. [S]
- **De Jaegher/Di Paolo.** Measurement is relational-dynamical (relative phase, recurrence, perturbation-response); the felt shift survives as *corroborating phenomenology*, explicitly not as the evidence. [P]
- **Polanyi.** Verification is successful performance plus a *negative test* (focal attention destroys the integration) — closest ally to the book's standard, yet it predicts that making the felt shift focal could abolish it. [S/I]
- **von Foerster.** Measurement is the count of downstream choices; the felt shift is not addressed. [S]

## SEAT VERDICTS

- **Bateson** — *load-bearing*: supplies the distinct-level logic *and* the soberest boundary-marker, that the felt shift cannot self-certify at the depth of self-dissolution.
- **Maturana & Varela** — *ground for running machinery*: the citable biology under "perturb not install" and "meaning made in interaction," to be run with its contested tag and self-undermining edge visible.
- **De Jaegher & Di Paolo** — *load-bearing*: the only program that operationalizes rupture-and-repair and meaning-in-interaction with an evidence base.
- **Polanyi** — *stress-test*: the from-to structure threatens the book's own central instruction to attend to the felt shift.
- **von Foerster** — *directional*: a candidate ethic that points a way but is too formally empty to bear weight.
