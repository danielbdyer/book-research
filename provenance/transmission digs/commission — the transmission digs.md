# THE TRANSMISSION DIGS — commission (with vault adaptation)

## §-1 — VAULT ADAPTATION (how this commission runs inside the book-research vault)

This expedition runs as a generative multi-agent workflow whose artifacts land in the `book-research` vault. Six adaptations to the deliverable form (§6), made deliberately — not by being pushed around by it:

1. **Home.** The digs are commissioned research on the book's *medium* — works consulted, not vault doctrine (the author is the locus of selection; §2.10). They live in `provenance/transmission digs/`, named in the vault's spaced convention (`dig 1 — indirect communication.md` … `dig 0 — cross-dig synthesis.md`), with this commission beside them.
2. **Honest access.** Most primary texts here are copyrighted and not openable at the coordinates given. The **[P] tag is reserved for a primary text genuinely accessed**; everything else is [S]/[C]/[I], tagged honestly. No faked primary reads. Reproduce at most one short quote (<25 words) from any copyrighted source; public-domain texts (Kierkegaard, Plato, Wittgenstein's *Tractatus*, older translations) may be quoted more freely.
3. **The queue is a deliverable.** Every primary reading a dig cannot perform is routed to the vault's real reading queue (`ops/reading queue.md`) in its sharp-question form — a failable question, a stake, a page-level address. An unread primary text is not a gap; it is a queued dig. Each dig returns a **READINGS TO QUEUE** list for this purpose.
4. **Plug-in, then admission (author's decision, 2026-08-14).** In the dig run itself the findings stay in `provenance/` and mint no `notes/` claims (§2.10), and the cross-dig synthesis maps them to *existing* vault notes as a convergence registry of live wiki links. Then, by the author's decision, the findings are **admitted as first-class `notes/` claims — equal citizens, explored openly** — in a follow-on pass, with the digs kept here in `provenance/` as their provenance trace (the way a reading record traces a reading), and pruned as the project goes. What rests on a queued primary reading enters as an honest `nascent` claim citing the dig and the queued reading. The digs are not othered; only genuinely author-gated calls (the four verdicts, seatings) are reserved.
5. **Clean-room preserved.** The dig agents are **not** shown the vault; they excavate blind (see §1). The mapping to vault notes happens only afterward, in the synthesis — so a convergence discovered blind counts as evidence, exactly as §1 requires.
6. **Seats route to candidates, never to seatings.** Seat verdicts become candidate evaluations in `ops/queue/candidates.md`; the author seats.

Everything below is the commission as written.

# THE TRANSMISSION DIGS
### An excavation brief for the territory the book is standing on

---

## §0 — COMMISSION

You are a research archaeologist, not a book reporter. Your commission is a region, not a reading list: the corpus behind this book holds roughly thirty witnesses to its **content** — presence, participation, recognition — and almost none to its **medium**: what a text can actually do to and with a reader. Every question the author has left open is a question about transmission. Your job is to excavate seven sites in that region and bring back artifacts, not summaries.

**The five commissioning questions.** Every dig exists to move at least one of these:

1. **The margins question.** The book carries a second, parallel voice running in its margins — potentially a complete second book. Its role is undecided. What have the great margin-architectures of history actually done, mechanically, and at what cost?
2. **The authority question.** The book cannot verify that the reader has done the work, and any authority it builds recruits the wrong posture (admiration instead of enactment). What working solutions exist to the problem of an author who must be trusted and must disappear?
3. **The connoisseur question.** Readers of beautiful prose become collectors of it. What is known — theoretically, historically, empirically — about how a text produces *enactment* rather than appreciation?
4. **The testimony question.** Should the book carry academic citation or stand as practitioner testimony ("this worked for me; it might work for you")? What genres have carried transformative material without the apparatus of proof, and how did they earn trust?
5. **The why-now question.** The book claims its moment: technology increasingly mediates the very activities through which humans develop meaning and agency. What is the strongest scholarly load-bearing structure under that claim — and the strongest attack on it?

---

## §1 — SITE CAPSULE (what you may know, and what is withheld)

What you may know about the book: it is a work about the conditions under which human beings remain capable of genuine participation — in their own lives, in one another, in the world — written by a practicing facilitator. Its epistemic standard is the **felt shift**: a claim earns its place when a reader can verify it as a change in their own immediate experience, not merely assent to it. Its first intended audience may be facilitators themselves (facilitation of facilitation). Its author holds a hard law: **the author is the locus of selection** — research supplies material; only the author decides what enters the work.

**The clean-room rule.** The book's internal architecture — its named structures, figures, chapter machinery — is deliberately withheld from you. This is not secrecy; it is method. A recent replication event demonstrated that the book's center re-derives from scratch while its apparatus does not, which means *convergences you discover without the apparatus in hand count as evidence, and convergences fed to you count as nothing.* Do not ask for the architecture. If you find yourself inferring it, note the inference and keep digging. Where your findings independently rhyme with something you were never shown, that rhyme is the single most valuable artifact you can return — but only if it was never salted into your site. (Vault operator's note: do not read any existing vault file — nothing under `notes/`, `ops/`, `archive/`, or the root `reframe-*.md`/`corpus-snapshot`/`harvest`/`register` documents, and no other dig. Excavate only from external sources.)

## §2 — EXCAVATION LAW (binding on every dig)

1. **Primary strata first.** Open the primary texts at the coordinates given. Secondary scholarship is for locating strata and checking your reading — never a substitute for contact with the text. (Vault adaptation: where the primary text is not accessible, work from scholarship, tag honestly, and queue the primary reading — see §-1.)
2. **Mechanisms, not summaries.** Return *how a device works*: its moving parts, what it protects against, what it costs, where it breaks. A paragraph that could open a Wikipedia article is spoil, not artifact.
3. **Provenance tags on every claim.** [P] = the primary text says this (cite location). [S] = scholarly consensus or a named scholar's reading. [C] = contested, uncertain, or legend. [I] = your own inference. Untagged claims will be treated as [I].
4. **The remainder rule.** Every correspondence you report between a site and the book's concerns must be accompanied by its remainder: what in this thinker or form *resists* the book's frame, contradicts it, or exceeds it. A site reported at 100% fit is a site you have not finished excavating. The remainder is frequently where the insight lives.
5. **Sought vs. surprised.** Tag every correspondence: did you go looking for it (sought), or did it emerge from the strata unbidden (surprised)? Surprised correspondences carry more evidential weight and must be reported even when inconvenient.
6. **Failure sites are mandatory.** For every figure, locate at least one place where their method failed, was abandoned, was attacked on target, or produced the opposite of its intent. The book needs the fractures more than the monuments.
7. **The felt-shift audit.** For every practice, device, or verification standard you recover, state: could this be checked by felt shift? What was its *in situ* verification standard — how did its own tradition know it was working?
8. **No validation.** Do not praise the book, the project, or the fit of your findings. Flag overstatement wherever you are tempted toward it. If a commissioning question rests on a false premise, say so plainly.
9. **No harmonizing.** Where sites disagree with each other, preserve the disagreement in its strongest form. Do not synthesize adversaries into a chorus.
10. **No architecture proposals.** You return artifacts, catalogs, and verdicts. You do not propose structures for the book. The author is the locus of selection; your findings end at his table, not past it.

---

## §3 — THE DIG TEMPLATE

Every dig below follows one form. Your deliverable for each dig follows it too.

- **Strata & opening coordinates** — the figures and the specific texts/sections to open first.
- **The find** — the precise thing this dig exists to recover, stated as a question.
- **Excavation instructions** — ordered, imperative, specific.
- **Artifacts to return** — the deliverables, enumerated.
- **The remainder question** — the mandatory resistance-check for this site.
- **Contamination warnings** — the predictable false finds at this site.
- **Seat verdict** — close the dig by recommending a seat: *load-bearing / stress-test / independent arrival / ground for running machinery / directional / precedent bench / decline*, with a one-sentence justification per figure.

### DIG I — INDIRECT COMMUNICATION
*Commissioning questions served: 2, 4.*

**Strata & opening coordinates.**
- **Kierkegaard** (the central shaft): *The Point of View for My Work as an Author* (entire; it is short); *Concluding Unscientific Postscript*, the sections on **double reflection** and indirect communication (the "Something About Lessing" chapters); *Philosophical Fragments*, Chapter 1 — the Socratic teacher-as-**occasion** versus the teacher who brings the condition; the 1847 lectures/notes on the **dialectic of communication** (capability-communication vs. knowledge-communication).
- **Plato** (satellite): *Seventh Letter*, 341c–344d — the leaping-spark passage and the critique of writing. Note [C] the authenticity dispute and then explain why it does not matter for our use.
- **Wittgenstein** (satellite): *Tractatus* 6.54 (the ladder to be thrown away) — then the later Wittgenstein's abandonment of the ladder for *therapy*: philosophy as treatment, not doctrine (*Philosophical Investigations*, §§109–133 region).

**The find.** What is the complete working inventory of devices ever built for the problem: *an author who must be trusted and must disappear* — and where did each device break?

**Excavation instructions.**
1. From Kierkegaard, extract each distinct indirect device as a separate artifact: pseudonymity; double reflection; revocation (the *Postscript*'s ending that takes itself back); the maieutic ordering of the authorship; writing "without authority" as a formal stance. For each: mechanism → what posture it blocks in the reader → what it costs the author.
2. From *Fragments* Chapter 1, reconstruct the Socratic-occasion argument in ten steps or fewer. This is the closest classical statement of facilitation as an epistemic position: the teacher as midwife/occasion who can be forgotten. Report exactly where Kierkegaard's alternative (the teacher who brings the condition) departs from it — that departure is a live fault line for a book by a facilitator.
3. Locate the failures. [P/S] The Corsair affair. The late direct attack on the established church — the moment Kierkegaard *abandoned* indirection. Excavate why: what problem could indirect communication not solve? The limits of the method matter more to us than the method.
4. From the Seventh Letter: extract the spark passage's exact claims about what writing cannot do and under what conditions of "long communion" the kindling occurs. Note that its author wrote thirty books anyway. State the operating implication in one sentence.
5. From Wittgenstein: the ladder as a device (a book that instructs its own disposal), then therapy-not-theory as a second, later device. One paragraph each.

**Artifacts to return.** (a) The device inventory, each entry ≤150 words. (b) The ten-step Socratic-occasion reconstruction. (c) A failure report: where indirection broke and why. (d) The felt-shift audit for maieutics: how did Socrates/Kierkegaard verify the work was happening?

**The remainder question.** Kierkegaard's telos is the single individual before God; his register is contempt for the crowd; his indirection is soaked in personal suffering. What of this *cannot* be carried into a participatory, relational, plural project — and what warning does that incompatibility issue?

**Contamination warnings.** Do not soften Kierkegaard into a communication-theory consultant. Do not resolve the tension between occasion-teaching and condition-bringing; report it sharp. Do not treat the Seventh Letter as settled Plato [C].

### DIG II — THE READER'S HALF OF THE TRANSACTION
*Commissioning questions served: 3, 2.*

**Strata & opening coordinates.**
- **Louise Rosenblatt**: *The Reader, the Text, the Poem* (1978) — the efferent/aesthetic continuum and the transactional model.
- **Wolfgang Iser**: *The Act of Reading* (1978) — *Leerstellen* (gaps/blanks), the implied reader, negativity.
- **Roland Barthes**: *S/Z* (readerly/writerly); *The Pleasure of the Text* (plaisir vs. jouissance).
- **Hans-Georg Gadamer**: *Truth and Method*, Part I — the analysis of **play (Spiel)** as the mode of being of the artwork; fusion of horizons.
- Satellite: **Stanley Fish**, interpretive communities — one survey paragraph only.

**The find.** What does the strongest existing theory and evidence say about how a text produces *participation* rather than appreciation — and is there a third reading stance beyond Rosenblatt's two that the book would need?

**Excavation instructions.**
1. Rosenblatt: extract the operational definitions of efferent and aesthetic stance, then hunt specifically for what she says about the *conditions* (instructional, situational) that shift a reader between stances [P]. Her transactional evidence is condition-design data. Then answer directly: does her framework contain, imply, or foreclose an *enactive* stance — reading as doing? Tag your answer honestly ([P]/[S]/[I]).
2. Iser: build a small taxonomy of gap types from *The Act of Reading* — which kinds of blanks recruit the reader as co-producer, and which merely produce puzzle-solving pleasure (connoisseurship's cousin). The distinction between gaps that demand *completion* and gaps that demand *participation* is the artifact.
3. Barthes: recover the writerly/readerly distinction precisely, then excavate the failure at its center: why Barthes held the fully writerly text to be a limit-case, nearly impossible to produce [P/S]. That impossibility report is load-bearing intelligence for a book that wants writerly effects at scale. Map plaisir/jouissance onto appreciation/shift and report where the mapping strains (remainder rule).
4. Gadamer: reconstruct the play analysis — the game plays the players; the artwork's mode of being is the event of its being-played. Then extract fusion of horizons as a facilitation model: what does the *text* contribute, what does the reader, and what belongs to neither. Locate the internal stress-test: the Habermas critique (tradition and authority smuggled in) and what Gadamer conceded [S].

**Artifacts to return.** (a) The stance analysis with a verdict on the third stance. (b) The gap taxonomy with examples. (c) The writerly impossibility report. (d) The play reconstruction ≤400 words. (e) Felt-shift audit: which of these frameworks has any verification standard at all, and what substitutes for one where absent.

**The remainder question.** All four are theories of *meaning*; the book's standard is a change in *lived capacity*. Where exactly does reader-response theory stop short of transformation — and does anything in these strata bridge, or is the bridge simply absent from the literature?

**Contamination warnings.** Do not equate the aesthetic stance with enactment; it is precisely the connoisseur's stance in finer dress. Do not let Fish dissolve the text into community convention — one paragraph, then out.

### DIG III — MARGIN ARCHITECTURES
*Commissioning questions served: 1, 3, and the loneliness of solitary reading.*

This is a **formal** dig. Excavate page mechanics and reading protocols, not doctrine.

**Strata & opening coordinates.**
- **The Talmud page** (Vilna layout): Mishnah/Gemara at center; Rashi on the inner margin; Tosafot on the outer; later strata ringing them.
- **The Blue Cliff Record** (*Biyan lu*): the case / pointer / verse / interlinear commentary / capping-phrase (*jakugo*) architecture; and [C] the tradition that **Dahui Zonggao burned the printing blocks** (~1140).
- **Lectio divina** and the 12th-century mutation of the page: Hugh of St. Victor's *Didascalicon*; **Ivan Illich**, *In the Vineyard of the Text* (1993).
- Satellite: the *glossa ordinaria* as the standard-issue margin technology of medieval Europe.

**The find.** What has a margin-as-second-voice actually *done* in the traditions that ran it for centuries — mechanically, pedagogically, relationally — and what made reading a practice before it became consumption?

**Excavation instructions.**
1. Talmud: map the page as a machine. What surrounds what; how the eye is forced to move; how *makhloket* (disagreement) is preserved unresolved on the surface for centuries. Extract the **functional difference between Rashi and Tosafot** — the explainer voice versus the dialectical challenger voice — as two distinct margin jobs [S].
2. Then the crucial stratum: the page presupposes **chavruta** — paired study. Excavate the pairing: how partner-reading is structured, what the pair does that a solitary reader cannot, and whether the page architecture and the two-reader protocol are separable at all [S]. This artifact serves the solitary-reader problem directly.
3. Blue Cliff Record: diagram the five-layer case architecture. Then excavate the burning [C→S]: what is actually attested about Dahui destroying the blocks of his own teacher's masterwork because students were collecting commentary instead of practicing — motive, historicity, scholarly debate. Whatever the truth, this is the connoisseur problem enacted in 1140, inside the most margin-rich book form ever built. Report what the tradition did *next* (the kōan curriculum, capping-phrase answering — students responding *with* quotation as demonstration, not decoration).
4. Illich/Hugh: reconstruct Illich's thesis — the 12th-century shift from monastic *lectio* (the page as vineyard, read aloud, chewed, embodied) to scholastic *text* (the page as searchable object). Extract the specific **page technologies** that caused it: chapter divisions, indexes, layout, silent reading [S]. The author is designing page technology; he needs the causal inventory of the last time page technology rewired what reading *is*.
5. Lectio divina: recover the four movements (lectio, meditatio, oratio, contemplatio) as a reading *protocol* — how it was taught, paced, and verified.

**Artifacts to return.** (a) Two annotated page-diagrams (Talmud, Blue Cliff) in text form. (b) The chavruta report. (c) The burning report with tags. (d) Illich's causal inventory of page technologies. (e) The lectio protocol. (f) Felt-shift audit for each tradition's own verification standard (what did a rebbe, a Zen teacher, an abbot accept as evidence?).

**The remainder question.** Every one of these architectures ran inside a **binding community with living authority** — yeshiva, sangha, monastery. What happens to margin technology when the community and the authority are subtracted? Answer from the strata, not from hope.

**Contamination warnings.** Do not aestheticize the Talmud page; report its mechanics. Do not resolve the Dahui story into a tidy moral. Do not modernize lectio into "mindful reading."

### DIG IV — THE SCIENTIFIC SPINE
*Commissioning questions served: 3, 5, and the clinical-diagnostic reader who needs mechanism before feeling.*

**Strata & opening coordinates.**
- **Gregory Bateson**: *Steps to an Ecology of Mind* — "The Logical Categories of Learning and Communication" (Learning 0–III); "The Double Bind" material; deutero-learning.
- **Maturana & Varela**: *The Tree of Knowledge* — structural coupling; the impossibility of **instructive interaction**; languaging.
- **De Jaegher & Di Paolo**: "Participatory Sense-Making: An Enactive Approach to Social Cognition" (2007); De Jaegher's later "Loving and Knowing" line of work; the perceptual-crossing experimental paradigm.
- Satellites: **von Foerster** (second-order cybernetics; the ethical imperative: act so as to increase the number of choices); **Polanyi** (*The Tacit Dimension* — from–to structure of attention; subsidiary vs. focal awareness).

**The find.** What is the strongest *mechanistic* account — citable chain by chain — for three claims the book needs: (i) learning-to-learn is a real, distinct level; (ii) a teacher can only perturb, never install; (iii) meaning is generated in interaction, not transmitted?

**Excavation instructions.**
1. Bateson: extract the precise definitions of Learning 0, I, II, III with his own examples [P]. Then excavate the warning stratum: why Bateson held Learning III to be rare and *dangerous* — the proximity of level-III reorganization to psychosis as well as to liberation [P]. The book needs this sober boundary-marker verbatim-close. Map (as [I], tagged) Learning I/II/III onto practitioner / facilitator / facilitation-of-facilitation and report where the mapping strains.
2. Maturana/Varela: reconstruct the argument against instructive interaction in ≤10 steps, from autopoiesis through structural determinism to "a perturbation can trigger but not specify a change" [P]. This is the participation law as biology; the book needs the chain, not the slogan. Then locate the standing critique: the solipsism/observer problem — how they answer it and whether the answer holds [S].
3. De Jaegher/Di Paolo: extract the operational definition of participatory sense-making and the experimental evidence base (perceptual crossing; coordination dynamics). Specifically excavate what the literature says about **breakdown and repair** — coordination failure as generative — because rupture-and-repair is a facilitation craft in search of its science.
4. Polanyi: recover the from–to structure — we attend *from* subsidiaries *to* a focal target — and state its implication for practice-design: why a practice functions only while its components remain subsidiary (unwatched), and what this predicts about self-conscious technique [P/I].
5. von Foerster: one paragraph — the observer inside the system; the ethical imperative as a candidate facilitation ethic.

**Artifacts to return.** (a) The Learning 0–III definitions + danger report. (b) The ten-step instructive-interaction chain. (c) The participatory sense-making evidence memo, breakdown/repair section flagged. (d) The from–to implication memo. (e) Felt-shift audit: what, in each program, counts as *measurement* — and where the felt shift would and would not survive their standards.

**The remainder question.** These are third-person sciences of second-person phenomena. What do they each *lose* of the first-person that the book cannot afford to lose — and does any of them admit it?

**Contamination warnings.** Do not let Learning III drift into mysticism; keep Bateson's warnings attached. Do not cite autopoiesis as settled consensus — mark its contested status [C/S]. Do not present enactivism as monolithic; the field argues with itself, and the arguments are strata.

### DIG V — THE WHY-NOW SOCIOLOGY
*Commissioning question served: 5.*

**Strata & opening coordinates.**
- **Hartmut Rosa**: *Resonance: A Sociology of Our Relationship to the World*; *The Uncontrollability of the World* (*Unverfügbarkeit*).
- **Albert Borgmann**: *Technology and the Character of Contemporary Life* — the device paradigm; focal things and practices.
- **Byung-Chul Han**: *The Burnout Society*; *The Scent of Time*; *Psychopolitics*.
- Satellites: **Matthew Crawford** (*The World Beyond Your Head*); **Jenny Odell** (*How to Do Nothing*).

**The find.** What is the strongest institutional-scale scaffolding under the claim "technology increasingly mediates the activities through which humans develop meaning and agency" — and what are its axes, its criteria, and its best counterattack?

**Excavation instructions.**
1. Rosa: extract the full apparatus — the axes of resonance (horizontal: relationships; diagonal: things/work; vertical: art, nature, the sacred) and the defining characteristics, above all **uncontrollability**: resonance cannot be commanded, purchased, or engineered, only prepared for [P]. Then extract what Rosa says about *institutional conditions* that raise or lower resonance-probability — this is condition-design at civilizational scale. Then the internal stress-test: the critique that resonance theory smuggles normativity in through the back door, and Rosa's reply [S].
2. Borgmann: recover the device paradigm (commodity foregrounded, machinery concealed) and the criteria of a **focal practice** — what qualifies, what disqualifies, why focal practices resist commodification [P]. Use his own worked examples (the culture of the table; running). Report the standard critique: nostalgia/technological pessimism, and whether his criteria survive it [S].
3. Han: extract the achievement-subject diagnosis — self-exploitation without a master; positivity-excess; the loss of the *vita contemplativa* and of lingering [P]. This is a portrait gallery of the book's likely reader. Then run the remainder rule hard: Han diagnoses and does not prescribe — establish precisely where his account stops and what it structurally cannot supply.
4. Satellites: one paragraph each — Crawford's situated attention as jig-and-skill; Odell's attention-activism as a contemporary popular register for the same argument.

**Artifacts to return.** (a) The Rosa apparatus memo, uncontrollability section flagged as it bears directly on the felt-shift's refusal of the life-hack reader. (b) The focal-practice criteria list. (c) The Han portrait + the gap report. (d) A one-page "strongest case against the why-now" assembled from these strata's own critics — steelmanned, no rebuttal.

**The remainder question.** All of these are critiques written from inside comfortable modernity. What does the why-now claim look like from outside that position — and does the dig's literature ever ask?

**Contamination warnings.** Do not flatten Rosa into "slow living." Do not let Han's aphorisms substitute for argument — extract the argument. The counterattack memo must be written to *win*, not to be survived.

### DIG VI — THE ADVERSARY CHAMBER
*Commissioning questions served: 2, 3, 4 — by attack.*

**Strata & opening coordinates.**
- **Adorno**: *The Jargon of Authenticity* (1964).
- **The compliance stratum**: Orne on **demand characteristics** (1962); expectancy and placebo research; suggestibility literature as it touches transformational trainings and contemplative reports.

**The find.** The two attacks the corpus has not yet dared to seat: (i) the book's *register* is ideology — authenticity-language as aura, consolation sold as depth; (ii) the book's *evidence* is compliance — the felt shift as demand characteristic.

**Excavation instructions.**
1. Adorno: reconstruct the attack **in its own terms and at full strength**. The jargon's mechanism: words that radiate significance independent of content; the sacralization of immediacy; authenticity as a commodity of consolation for administered life [P]. Then — this is the artifact — write two pages of Adorno *aimed at this project's genre*: a beautiful book of presence-language, written by a facilitator, promising return-to-self. No rebuttals anywhere in this dig. Bring the weapon back intact and sharpened.
2. Only afterward, in a separate section: catalog (as [I]) which elements of a presence-book could survive the attack *only after modification* — and which cannot survive it at all. Do not soften the second list.
3. Compliance stratum: extract Orne's demand-characteristics construct precisely, then survey what expectancy/placebo research implies about self-reported shift in high-demand settings (workshops, retreats, guided reading). Locate whatever exists on distinguishing genuine durable change from compliance and hope — controls, longitudinal designs, their absence. Deliver the strongest scientific case that the felt shift is an artifact of the situation.
4. Close the chamber with the crossover question: where the two attacks *reinforce* each other (aura produces demand; demand produces reported shift; reported shift confirms aura) — the full circle of the objection, stated once, cleanly.

**Artifacts to return.** (a) The two-page Adorno attack. (b) The survivability catalog. (c) The compliance case memo with its evidence tiers tagged. (d) The crossover statement.

**The remainder question.** Inverted here: what does the *attack* fail to explain? Not as comfort — as data. (E.g., changes that persist outside the demand situation; traditions with internal anti-compliance tests.) One page maximum, evidence-tagged, no relief-seeking.

**Contamination warnings.** The gravest contamination at this site is charity. If your Adorno reads like he could be persuaded, dig again. Do not let the survivability catalog become reassurance.

### DIG VII — THE OWNED, UNCLAIMED LINEAGE
*Commissioning questions served: 3, and the loneliness problem; also the author's own provenance.*

**Strata & opening coordinates.**
- **Viola Spolin**: *Improvisation for the Theater* — sidecoaching; point of concentration; approval/disapproval as the enemy of spontaneity; her origins with **Neva Boyd** and settlement-house games.
- **Keith Johnstone**: *Impro*; *Impro for Storytellers* — status; blocking vs. accepting; "be obvious"; the teacher who takes failure onto himself.
- **D. W. Winnicott**: *Playing and Reality* — potential space, transitional phenomena; "The Capacity to Be Alone" (1958); the good-enough principle.
- **Victor Turner**: *The Ritual Process* — liminality; communitas and its three types (existential, normative, ideological); structure/anti-structure rhythm.
- Satellite: **Martin Buber** — *only if not already seated in the wider roster* — one survey pass: *I and Thou*; "Elements of the Interhuman"; the *between* (das Zwischen).

**The find.** The theatre-and-play lineage is the actual historical root of the facilitated room, and it is the author's own formative ground. What craft, developmental theory, and ritual mechanics does it hold that the contemplative and philosophical shelves do not?

**Excavation instructions.**
1. Spolin: extract **sidecoaching** as a device — instruction delivered *during* play, without stopping it, folded into the game's reality [P]. Mechanism, conditions, failure modes. Extract "point of concentration" as attention-design: how a focus object frees the player from self-watching (cross-reference Polanyi's subsidiary awareness from Dig IV and tag the crossover sought/surprised honestly). Recover the Boyd inheritance: games as democratic pedagogy for immigrant children — facilitation's social-work birth certificate [S].
2. Johnstone: extract status as micro-relational physics — the observable, adjustable transactions under every exchange [P]. Extract the offer-grammar: blocking vs. accepting. Extract the authority-dissolving devices: "be obvious," dare-to-be-dull, and the teacher's announced contract that failure belongs to the teacher. For each: what posture it produces in the room.
3. Winnicott: recover potential space precisely — the third area, neither inner fantasy nor external fact, where play and culture live [P]. Then "The Capacity to Be Alone": the developmental claim that solitude-capacity is built *in the presence of another* [P]. State its implication for a book meant to be read alone, as [I], one paragraph, no more.
4. Turner: extract liminality mechanics and the communitas typology — and specifically the **decay pattern**: existential communitas hardening into normative then ideological forms [P]. This is a weather forecast for any community the book might gather. Extract structure/anti-structure as rhythm rather than opposition.
5. Buber (conditional): the between as the locus of the real; I–Thou's untransferability into method. One page.

**Artifacts to return.** (a) A device catalog from Spolin/Johnstone — each entry: mechanism / posture produced / failure mode / portability-to-page verdict. (b) The potential-space and capacity-to-be-alone memos. (c) The communitas decay report. (d) Felt-shift audit: how does an improv teacher *know* — what is the room's own verification standard?
