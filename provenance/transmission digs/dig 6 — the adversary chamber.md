## FINDINGS

**Adorno, mechanism of the jargon** (*Jargon der Eigentlichkeit*, 1964; Eng. Northwestern 1973; Epoché/Gerber reading; SEP). [S] The jargon is not a vocabulary but a *tone*; its unit is the auratic word — *Geborgenheit*, *Begegnung* (encounter), *Anliegen* (concern) — which radiates significance independent of content, so saying it *feels* like meaning it. Moving parts: **tone over concept** (the *how* certifies the *what*, nothing falsifiable asserted); **sacralization of immediacy** (the lived outranks the mediated, buying the numinous while distrusting the concept that would test it); **non-falsifiability** ("a 'real' and a 'faked' authenticity cannot be distinguished," and objection is reread as the objector's own inauthenticity). Cost: oppression becomes a metaphysical condition, "the necessity for change" negated, the administered subject consoled with an untouchable inner core while outer conditions stand unchanged. Not bad philosophy but **ideology** — consolation sold as depth, resistance-in-tone that affirms the order.

**Orne, demand characteristics** (Orne 1962, *Am. Psychologist* 17:776–783). [S] Definition: "the totality of cues which convey an experimental hypothesis to the subject." The subject is a **problem-solver** who infers what is wanted and, wanting to be a *good subject*, supplies it — Orne's subjects persisted for hours at a pointless task because "this is an experiment" made the meaningless meaningful. Quasi-controls, since ordinary controls fail: **preinquiry** (cues without treatment); **postexperimental inquiry** (defeated by a "pact of ignorance"); and the signature **simulating-subject** design — instructed fakers past a blind assessor, so *if the fake is indistinguishable from the real, the treatment is unproven*.

**Kirsch, response expectancy** (Kirsch 1985, *Am. Psychologist* 40:1189–1202; 1997 review) — the crown of the compliance case. [S] Response expectancies, anticipations of one's own *nonvolitional* responses, "generate corresponding subjective experiences," are **self-confirming** and **sufficient** (unmediated). The experience itself, not merely its report, is manufacturable by the expectation of it.

**The high-demand field.** [S] Britton et al. 2021: 83% of a mindfulness sample reported ≥1 side effect, yet across ~7,000 studies fewer than 1% measured harms and open-ended formats *underestimate* them ~70% — participants stay "hesitant to report negative reactions… because of shame or a desire to please the researcher or instructor." [S] Lieberman, Yalom & Miles (1971/73): 16 enduring casualties among 209 encounter-group participants (~19%) — the base rate of harm in high-demand rooms; Finkelstein, Wenegrat & Yalom (1982) extend it to est/Lifespring LGATs; deceptive-placebo experiments move shame and rumination by expectancy alone (PMC11757295).

## ARTIFACT A — TWO PAGES OF ADORNO, AIMED (the weapon, no rebuttal)

*Reconstruction in Adorno's register, turned on the genre. [S] apparatus, [I] application.*

Consider the book that promises you your self back. It is beautifully made; distrust that first. Its beauty is not incidental to its argument — its beauty *is* the argument, and that is the jargon's signature. Where a thought would expose itself to refutation, the presence-book offers a cadence; the well-turned phrase closes with a click received in the body as assent, and the reader mistakes the pleasure of the prose for the truth of the claim. He has been given the sensation of depth at no charge to the concept — not a gift but a sale, and consolation is the commodity.

Observe the vocabulary: *presence, participation, the felt, the lived, coming home to oneself.* Each word arrives pre-warmed, radiating a gravity it did not earn in the sentence it stands in; the words do the work the argument declines. They cannot be false because they say nothing determinate enough to be false — true the way a chord is true. And the central promise — that you *already know* everything here, that reading is only remembering — forecloses instruction while flattering the pupil: nothing can be taught, so nothing can be tested; whatever you find was already yours, and the book takes credit for a wealth it merely named. The reader is crowned sovereign precisely so he will not notice he has been administered.

Note where the author stands: a facilitator, whose trade is producing experience in a room, who knows which words move a body to tears. That competence is now aimed through the page at a reader who cannot see the room or the others weeping, and so takes for the movement of his own soul a technique refined on hundreds before him. Each reader feels *uniquely addressed* — not evidence against mass-production but what mass-production of the intimate feels like from inside.

And the verification standard, the *felt shift* — here the ideology completes itself. The court of appeal is relocated to the one chamber no one else may enter and no evidence may reach: your interiority, at the moment of maximum suggestion, the beautiful voice still in your ear. A shift felt there confirms only that the conditions for feeling it were well built. Its unfalsifiability is sold as its rigor — *you* verify it, so it cannot be imposed; but a criterion only you can check, in the state the book engineered, is not a safeguard against imposition, it is imposition's cover. Feel nothing and the book is not refuted — you were not yet ready, not yet present, not yet authentic; the disconfirmation is metabolized as your own lack. This is the closed circle of every jargon: the doctrine's failure is always the subject's.

The genre presents itself as resistance — to distraction, acceleration, the administered life — and changes nothing in that life; it returns the reader consoled, feeling deeper and therefore quieter: the mood of opposition in the service of the order. That it soothes is not its excuse but its charge. The most administered thing in the world is the promise, sold at scale, that you were never administered.

## ARTIFACT B — SURVIVABILITY CATALOG [I]

**Keepable only after modification.** (1) *Presence-language* — only if every auratic word sits beside a determinate claim it would be false without; earn the halo or cut the word. (2) *The felt-shift standard* — only if paired with an external, later, other-verified check; a shift with no out-of-room confirmation cannot be told from a response-expectancy artifact. (3) *Beautiful prose* — only if made to *slow* assent (friction, refusal, withheld cadence), since fluency is the attack's delivery vehicle. (4) *The facilitator's authority* — only if the technique is disclosed and the effect's manufactured character admitted on the page.

**Not keepable.** (1) The *"you already know this / reading is remembering"* frame — a response-expectancy induction and unfalsifiability engine at once; its function *is* to foreclose testing. (2) *Any claim that the felt shift verifies the content* — the shift verifies conditions, never the proposition. (3) *Return-to-self as such* — an untouchable inner core offered as compensation for unchanged outer conditions is the ideology itself. (4) *Non-falsifiable interiority as court of appeal* — the artifact's signature.

## ARTIFACT C — THE COMPLIANCE CASE (tiered)

**Tier 1 — mechanism (strongest).** [S] Kirsch: response expectancy is *sufficient* and self-confirming — a reader expecting a shift has one, the content inert. This attacks the felt shift at its root: not "you misreported," but "the experience was manufactured by its own anticipation."

**Tier 2 — situation.** [S] Orne: reading this genre is a maximal-demand setting — invested reader, admired author, cueing that says *a shift belongs here*; by simulating-subject logic an outcome producible by demand alone leaves the content unproven, and a book can never run its own blind control. [S] Britton: shame and desire-to-please suppress *disconfirming* report.

**Tier 3 — absence of controls.** [S/I] The neighboring literature (LGAT, meditation, self-help) rarely uses active controls, blinding, or out-of-situation follow-up; self-reported effects shrink toward nonspecific when ritual, attention, and expectancy are held constant, and readers self-select in crisis (regression to the mean predicts improvement anyway). None correctable from inside a book.

## ARTIFACT D — THE CROSSOVER (stated once)

The two attacks are one machine. **Aura raises expectancy** (Adorno): haloed prose induces the reader to expect a shift. **Expectancy manufactures the experience** (Kirsch): the shift arrives, self-confirming, needing no content. **Demand secures the report** (Orne, Britton): in the high-demand frame the shift is reported and its absence is not. **The report re-charges the aura**: taken as proof of depth, it deepens the next reader's expectancy. The felt-shift standard — installed to protect *against* mere assent — is the exact bearing the circle turns on, since it instructs the reader to seek an inner change and trust it as verification. Aura → expectancy → experience → report → aura: self-sealing, needing no truth to run.

## DEVICES (the adversary's reusable instruments)

- **Simulating-subject test** (Orne): fakers past a blind assessor; indistinguishability = treatment unproven. *Portability to a book: nil* — the reader is always both subject and assessor.
- **Unfalsifiability detector** (Adorno): if no observation counts against the claim, it is ideology; a book installs it against itself only by naming a felt result that counts as failure.
- **Response-expectancy induction** (Kirsch): a felt shift *deliberately produced* by inducing its expectancy — expectancy-raising front matter manufactures its own evidence.
- **Low-demand assessment** (Britton, MedEx-I): structured, independent, shame-reducing questioning multiplies harm reports — the one instrument that turns demand *down*.

## FAILURES (attacked on target)

- **Adorno.** The *Jargon* turns on its author: his own prose is maximally auratic and difficult, charged with performing the non-falsifiable depth it condemns; he supplies no criterion separating legitimate presence-language from jargon, so the charge is itself unfalsifiable in his hands (Habermas: performative contradiction). Biographical: the theorist of the non-administered life called police on his occupying students (Frankfurt, 1969). The weapon cuts the wielder.
- **Orne.** The method leans on the self-report it distrusts — postexperimental inquiry needs truthful reports of awareness the "pact of ignorance" withholds. The construct is a near-universal solvent: almost any result can be reattributed to demand, dissolving real effects with artifacts, so it resists being made to fail.
- **Kirsch.** Charged with circularity (expectancy inferred from the outcome it explains) and partly self-disarming: if expectancy produces genuine physiological change, "only an artifact" no longer follows from "produced by expectancy."

## REMAINDER — what the attack fails to explain (as data, not comfort)

[S/I] **It explains origin, not durability or reality.** Orne's strongest form predicts effects *vanish when demand is removed*, and the one thing simulators cannot fake is out-of-situation, third-party-verified durable change — its near-absence here is the attack's strength, but the behaviorally recorded casualties are exactly what demand cannot produce, and the attack has no account of them. [S] **Kirsch cuts both ways:** response-expectancy effects register as real behavioral and physiological change, so "produced by expectancy" does not entail "unreal." [S] **Anti-compliance data sit inside the demand field:** Britton's dark-night work documents *distress* where the setting demands benefit — reports running *against* the gradient, which pure compliance cannot generate; that ~19% casualty and ≥83% side-effect rates surface at all shows desire-to-please does not fully govern report. [I] Some traditions also run internal anti-compliance tests, demanding demonstration over report. *The record cannot rule* on how much durable, externally-verified change this genre produces: the studies that would settle it have mostly not been run.

## FELT-SHIFT AUDIT

Could the felt shift be checked by a felt shift? That is the trap, exactly. The genre's *in situ* standard — bodily assent in the demand-warm state — is operationally indistinguishable from a response-expectancy artifact reported under demand and read through aura; it becomes admissible only when it carries what its setting cannot supply: an external, delayed, other-administered check. Absent that, it verifies conditions, never the claim.

## SURPRISED CONVERGENCES

- **Sought.** The commission asked where register-attack and evidence-attack reinforce; they do more — Adorno's *non-falsifiable interiority* and Orne's *demand characteristic* are the **same defect named from two disciplines**: an inner change generated by the situation and immune to disconfirmation. Critical theory and experimental psychology converge, without contact, on one structure. The chamber's central artifact.

Response-expectancy theory is a *mechanism* for exactly that failure: the felt shift confirms itself whether or not work was done.
