# DIG VII — THE OWNED, UNCLAIMED LINEAGE
*Strata: Spolin · Johnstone · Winnicott · Turner · Buber (conditional). Commissioning questions served: 3, the loneliness problem, the author's own provenance.*

Two adversary instruments from Dig VI held against every finding: **Adorno's jargon attack** (presence-language as aura) and the **compliance attack** (reported shift as demand characteristic; Orne). This site is the roster's most exposed to both — its register is the authenticity-talk Adorno indicts, its setting the highest-demand room in the corpus.

**Premise flag (no validation).** "The actual historical root of the facilitated room" is overstated: the room has roots of equal or earlier standing — Moreno's psychodrama and *spontaneity training* (coined "spontaneity"/"warm-up" before Spolin), Lewin's T-groups, Freirean adult education. Spolin-via-Boyd is *one* lineage; its distinction is theorizing play as **craft**, not primogeniture. [I]

## FINDINGS

**Spolin — attention-design against the self-watching eye.**
- **Point of concentration (POC).** Every game carries a single focus-object solved together; the focus, not the coach, runs the room [S: violaspolin.org; *Improvisation for the Theater* glossary]. The focal task occupies the attention that would otherwise watch the self, so spontaneity is not exhorted but *structurally freed*. This is Polanyi's from–to run as pedagogy: the POC is made **focal** so self and technique fall **subsidiary** (unwatched), and the practice degrades the instant a component is pulled back into focus. Crossover to Dig IV: **sought** — but the artifact is the *directionality*: Spolin engineers subsidiarity from outside by object-placement, where Polanyi only describes it. [S/I]
- **Approval/disapproval as the enemy of spontaneity.** Spolin held that authorities govern by approval and disapproval, and that this evaluative axis disables experience; her assessment is a non-judgmental group process [S]. The appreciation-stance is not a mild enactment — it is *manufactured by the evaluating gaze*, and doing appears only when the gaze withdraws.
- **Boyd inheritance.** Spolin's method descends whole from Neva Boyd's Hull-House recreation program (studied 1923–26), where traditional games were democratic pedagogy for immigrant children sharing no language — social group work, not theatre [S: Wikipedia *Neva Boyd*; ResearchGate 307434781]. Facilitation's birth certificate is a settlement-house document, and its original standard was *social adaptability*, not felt transformation.

**Johnstone — the observable physics of the room.**
- **Status as micro-relational physics.** Status is something you *do*, not are; every exchange runs a seesaw — one party's rise is the other's fall — legible in eye contact, stillness/fidget, spatial claim, foot position [S: *Impro*; smus.com]. Deflationary: relation reduced to adjustable, teachable transactions with no inner mystery. His instruction — play status *just above or below* your partner — makes the invisible operable.
- **Offer-grammar: blocking vs. accepting.** An offer is any move a partner makes; **blocking** negates and kills the scene, **accepting** ("yes-and") yields and builds [S]. Acceptance dissolves the defended self; the scene, not the ego, becomes the unit of agency.
- **"Be obvious" / dare-to-be-dull.** Originality is a trap; the first average response beats the invented-clever one, which is fear wearing wit [S: jamesclear; blas.com]. Removes the pressure to be interesting — the pressure that produces blockage.
- **The teacher takes the failure.** Johnstone announces that if students fail it is *his* fault; he chose the material [S: summaries, paraphrased]. The mechanism is exact: taking blame lowers his *played* status while raising his *real* status — only a confident expert dares it — and failure stops being frightening, which is the precondition for risk. A status-transaction weaponized against the fear that blocks.

**Winnicott — the developmental theory the philosophical shelf lacks.**
- **Potential space.** A third area, neither inner fantasy nor external fact, where play and the whole cultural life live; cultural experience is located "in the potential space between the individual and the environment" [S, short quote: *Playing and Reality* 1971, "The Location of Cultural Experience"]. Not inside the person, not in the object — *between*, and contingent on the other's reliability.
- **The capacity to be alone.** [P — archive.org full text, IJP 39:416–420, 1958] The founding paradox: "the capacity to be alone is based on the experience of being alone in the presence of someone." Mechanism: **ego-relatedness** (vs. id-relationship) — the infant, in the reliable, *non-impinging, non-demanding* presence of the mother, can "become unintegrated, to flounder," and there finds personal impulse, "a life that has reality in it." Solitude-capacity is *built by another* and later introjected; its climax he calls an "ego orgasm" — the satisfaction of playing or of a concert.
- **Good-enough / graduated failure.** [S] The good-enough other adapts almost wholly, then — in tune with the child's growing capacity — fails to adapt; the graduated failure is the developmental engine. An other who must, virtuously, withdraw.

**Turner — ritual mechanics and a weather forecast.** [P unless marked — "In and Out of Time," read in full]
- **Liminality.** "Threshold people are neither here nor there; they are betwixt and between the positions assigned and arrayed by law, custom, convention, and ceremonial." Statusless, likened to death and the womb.
- **Two models.** Structured/hierarchical society vs. "unstructured… relatively undifferentiated *comitatus*… of equal individuals who submit together to the general authority of the ritual elders." Communitas needs a congregation of liminal people and levels status among equals; it is not "community feeling" but this precise anti-structural modality.
- **The decay pattern (the forecast).** Existential/**spontaneous** communitas is "a phase, a moment, not a permanent condition"; the instant it organizes ("the moment a digging stick is set in the earth… we have the germs of a social structure") it hardens. **Normative** communitas is spontaneity mobilized into a system, its expressions "hedged about by rules and interdictions — which act like the lead container of a dangerous radioactive isotope." **Ideological** communitas is the utopian blueprint drawn from remembered spontaneity [S: SAGE *Communitas*; Haggar 2025] — the map, not the wind.
- **Structure/anti-structure as rhythm.** Not opposition but marriage: communitas is "nature in dialogue with structure, married to it as a woman is married to a man." Society alternates the two phases. [P]

## DEVICES (mechanism / posture / failure mode / portability-to-page)

| Device | Mechanism | Posture | Failure mode | Portable to a book read alone? |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **Side-coaching** (Spolin) | Instruction called *during* play without stopping it | Player stays in the now; correction without judgment | Slides into "the language of authority" — nagging reinstalls the approval it dissolves [S] | **No.** Its essence is live, timed, responsive intervention; the page cannot speak during play or see the moment. A margin note *stops* the play. |
| **Point of concentration** (Spolin) | A single focal task holds attention off the self | Spontaneity freed structurally, not exhorted | Focus too puzzle-like → connoisseur's problem-solving, not participation | **Partly.** A page can hand the reader a task; it loses the co-present field of others also concentrating, so self-watching returns faster. |
| **Approval-removal** (Spolin) | Withdraw the evaluative gaze | Doing replaces performing-for | Approval creeps back as the laugh, the note | **Partly, invertedly** — an author cannot watch (a native advantage); but the reader imports an internalized evaluator the page cannot silence. |
| **Status transaction** (Johnstone) | Adjustable seesaw of raise/lower, read off body and word | Relation made operable, demystified | Hardens into manipulation training (dominance, sales) — opposite of intent | **As analysis yes; as practice no.** The reader learns to *see* status; cannot run one with a book. |
| **Accepting offers** (Johnstone) | Yield to and build on the partner's move | Defended self dissolves; scene is the agent | Compulsive "yes" → spineless scene; accepting ≠ agreeing | **Concept only.** No live offer from the page to accept. |
| **Teacher takes the failure** (Johnstone) | Blame lowers played status, raises real status; failure de-fanged | Risk becomes safe; shame axis removed | Reads as charisma/false modesty; relocates demand, doesn't remove it | **Yes — most portable.** An author can structure the book so the reader's not-getting-it is the book's fault. Serves the authority question. |

Artifacts (b)/(c) — potential-space, capacity-to-be-alone, communitas-decay — are in FINDINGS and REMAINDER.

## FAILURES (one per figure)

- **Spolin.** Franchised (Second City; corporate "applied improv"), the method produced its opposite: spontaneity became a reproducible product, "yes-and" a management cliché, approval re-entering as the laugh. Side-coaching's own failure is the slide into authority-language. [S/I]
- **Johnstone.** Status physics, stripped of its liberatory frame, is a manipulation manual — the "status games" self-help genre is his tool turned to dominance. His mask/trance work reportedly disturbed some students, brushing the Bateson Learning-III danger zone. [S/C]
- **Winnicott.** The edifice fails where the early environment failed: impingement yields a false self, not play; the method has no purchase where basic trust is absent and cannot retroactively supply it to an adult who never had the non-impinging other. [S]
- **Turner.** The decay *is* the failure and is exceptionless in his account: communitas cannot be institutionalized without becoming normative-then-ideological. Later anthropology attacks the concept as romantic, power-blind, erasing internal difference/coercion. [S: Haggar 2025]
- **Buber.** I–Thou is constitutively non-operational; Buber resisted method, and encounter movements that tried to *do* I–Thou staged I–It performances of intimacy. Structural, not incidental. [S]

## REMAINDER (per device: what dies on the way to the page — no resurrection by analogy)

Theatre's devices assume co-presence, eye contact, a room, and a live other who responds and *survives*. Struck honestly: **side-coaching dies** (no live, timed responsiveness on a page); **status and accepting die as practice** (no partner to transact with); **POC and approval-removal are maimed** (they lose the holding field of co-concentrating others). What crosses is thin and specifiable: the *teacher-takes-failure* stance as authorial posture, and — the deep one — Winnicott's **non-impinging, reliable presence** as a design-spec for what a book must *be*. The remainder bites here too: Winnicott's other is alive, adapts to *this* infant, and survives being destroyed; a book cannot be destroyed and survive, cannot adapt, cannot answer. The bridge is real as a target and false as an equivalence.

**Buber's blade against the whole dig (das Zwischen).** [S: SEP; IEP; bopsecrets] The real is in the *between* — not in the I, not the Thou — and I–Thou is *untransferable into method*: the moment relation becomes a technique for producing relation, the other is an It. This indicts every device above and the book itself: any procedure that *manufactures* presence has already converted Thou to It. The lineage's reply is division of labor — Spolin/Johnstone build the *conditions* and refuse to command the event, as Rosa's uncontrollability requires (Dig V). Whether condition-design is itself already an It the record cannot rule.

## FELT-SHIFT AUDIT

Verification here is uniformly **third-person-observable co-present behavior plus shared felt flow** — precisely what a solitary reader cannot furnish. **Spolin:** the teacher *sees* it — the space-object made real, the player no longer self-watching; the group's perception is the instrument (felt, but *witnessed*). **Johnstone:** the scene flows (accepting) or dies (blocking) *visibly*; status reads off the body — failure is public and immediate. **Winnicott:** playing itself is the sign — the capacity to play *is* the evidence of health [S]. **Turner:** communitas is verified as felt "flow" and levelling among the co-present, unwitnessable alone by definition.

**Against Orne:** the improv room is a maximal-demand setting, so every reported shift is compliance-suspect. The internal anti-compliance tests are real but partial — Spolin's approval-removal and Johnstone's blame-taking both disable the approval-seeking that drives demand, yet they *relocate* demand (to group, to game) rather than abolish it, and co-presence keeps it high. **Against Adorno:** status is the inoculation — a deflationary physics of observable transactions, the opposite of authenticity-aura; where the lineage names procedures it resists jargon, where it says "spontaneity/presence/the now" it is fully exposed.

## SEAT VERDICTS (candidate evaluations; the author seats)

- **Spolin — ground for running machinery.** Supplies the craft the facilitated room runs on (POC, side-coaching, approval-removal); machinery, not thesis.
- **Johnstone — load-bearing (Q2/Q3).** Status + teacher-takes-failure is a specifiable working solution to trust-and-disappear and to appreciation-vs-enactment.
- **Winnicott — load-bearing (loneliness).** Potential space + capacity-to-be-alone is the one developmental theory making solitary transformation coherent and naming its precondition.
- **Turner — directional.** The communitas decay typology is an early-warning system for any community the book gathers.
- **Buber — stress-test (seat conditional on the wider roster, which the clean-room withholds).** The test every device must pass: does it turn Thou to It?

## SURPRISED CONVERGENCES (against §0's questions only)

1. **Spolin's approval/disapproval → the connoisseur question (Q3). SURPRISED.** A 1963 games manual already holds a mechanistic answer to "how does a text produce enactment not appreciation": appreciation is *not* a milder enactment but its active enemy, *manufactured by the evaluative gaze*; doing appears only when that gaze withdraws. I went in for side-coaching and POC; this was unbidden. Remainder: a book cannot withdraw the reader's *internalized* evaluator.
2. **Winnicott's non-impinging presence → the loneliness problem. Connection sought; the design-spec surprised.** The commission directed the solitary-reader implication (sought), but the 1958 text specifies more than asked — the precondition for being alone is a present, *reliable, non-demanding, non-impinging* other, which reads as an exact specification for what a book must be to be readable alone. Remainder: the specified other is alive and survives destruction.
3. **Johnstone's teacher-takes-failure → the authority question (Q2). Extraction sought; the mechanism surprised.** Taking blame *raises* real status while lowering played status — the precise shape of "must be trusted and must disappear." That one act does both at once was not anticipated.
4. **Turner's normative communitas as "lead container" → the gathered-community question (Q1 and loneliness). Forecast sought; the reframe surprised.** Structure is not communitas's betrayer but its *safety-container* for a dangerous energy — cutting against improv's anti-structure romance and any hope of permanent communitas.
