Method and coverage — read this first
Everything below comes from keyword searches over your past threads. Three limits shape
what this document can honestly claim.
Scope. Only conversations outside Projects are searchable from here. If any of the book
work lived inside a Project, it is invisible to this reconstruction and nothing below accounts
for it.
Dating. Timestamps attach to threads (last-updated stamps on retrieved chunks), not to
individual messages. One long thread (“Tracing therapeutic lineages through temple
builders”) returns chunks stamped both January 28 and May 28, 2026 — it ran for months.
So dates below are thread-level anchors. Where I say “July 7,” I mean “in the thread stamped
July 7,” not “at 2:14pm on July 7.”
The seed is off-record. The 28-chapter document arrives in this record already written, on
April 9, 2025, described in your own words as co-authored with an AI. Which AI, and when
the writing happened, is not recoverable here. Every question about the figures’ true origin
bottoms out at that wall.
Attribution convention: [D] marks your words from Human turns; [C] marks the AI’s words
from Assistant turns. This distinction carries most of the weight in Question 4, and I have
tried to be strict about it. Where a retrieved chunk was a model-written summary rather
than transcript, I say so.

Flagged: overstatements and constructed-looking
correspondences

1. The unanimity claims. “Zero disagreement across every lineage the book loves,” “total

depth by the same collaborator writing the chapters. The claim overstates by refusing to
