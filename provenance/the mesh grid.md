The Mesh Grid
A sorting and search instrument for the lineage of Firing on All Cylinders. Built weighted at
the apex — the top tiers get the exposition, the base gets a paragraph.
How to read the pyramid
Five tiers. The apex is narrow, expensive, and hard to earn. The base is wide, cheap to add,
and cheap to lose. Most rosters are built from the base up and then mistaken for an
argument. This one is built from the apex down.
Each tier has a test. If a name fails the test, it belongs one tier lower, no matter how much
you like it.
TIER 1 · LOAD-BEARING Remove it and the thesis changes shape. Test: can you state the
claim without this concept? If yes, not Tier 1.
TIER 2 · STRESS-TEST Attacks a load-bearing claim from a position you must survive. Test:
does it aim at the claim or at the culture around the claim? Culture-attacks are Tier 4
wearing armor. Value is proportional to damage; a comfortable adversary is decoration.
TIER 3 · INDEPENDENT ARRIVAL Reached the same claim down a different road. Test:
could they have gotten there without reading anyone in Tier 1? If they share a teacher, it is
not independence — it is a family resemblance, and it proves less than it appears to.
TIER 4 · GROUND FOR RUNNING MACHINERY Names and legitimizes something the book
already does without knowing what it is called. Test: is the apparatus already in motion? If
you would have to build it in order to use the name, it is not this tier.
TIER 5 · DIRECTIONAL Agrees on how things get made. Interesting on presence. Not
holding weight.
The second axis: provenance
Tier alone gives you a pile-count. Crossing tier against where the person got their
epistemics is what makes the grid generative, because it produces cells you can see are
empty. An empty cell is a search query.

The Tier 1 discipline
Every claim above should be able to name its bearer. Run this once: for C1 through C8, who
holds it? If a claim has no Tier 1 name, either it is not load-bearing or it is being carried by
the prose. The second is the dangerous case, and the prose is good enough to hide it.

TIER 2 · STRESS-TEST
The tier that decides whether the book is an argument or an aesthetic. Weight it heavily. A
thesis with no adversaries is a mood.
Sorted by which claim they aim at, because that is the only sort that reveals holes.
Against C2 (aletheia) — well defended, three-sided
Michel Henry (A) — the clearing is derivative. Auto-affection, immanence, Life feeling itself
with no distance; ontological monism as the name for Heidegger’s error. Attacks the central
image from below.
Nishitani (A/B) — the clearing stands in nihility, not emptiness. Attacks from beneath.
Thomas Sheehan (A) — and this is the sharpest of the three, which is easy to miss
because he is a scholar rather than a system-builder. Sheehan’s late project argues that
the mystical-numinous reading of Lichtung and aletheia is a mistranslation — that Heidegger
meant the a priori openness that makes meaning possible, not a luminous event of
disclosure. If Sheehan is right, the book’s central verb is built on a misreading that a
generation of secondary literature installed. That is not an attack on the claim; it is an attack
on the provenance of the claim, which is worse and harder to answer. He was on the
eleven-name list. He should not be treated as an optional scholarly footnote.
Note the concentration: all three are family A. See the search map.
Against C4 (felt-shift) — the largest hole in the project
Currently: nobody.
The claim doing the most work has the least defense. The candidates, all missing:
Nisbett & Wilson (1977) (D) — Telling More Than We Can Know. People confabulate
reports of their own mental processes and lack privileged access to them. This is the
canonical empirical strike, and it aims exactly where the book is thinnest.
Eric Schwitzgebel (D/I) — Perplexities of Consciousness. Introspection is unreliable even
about current experience, not merely about causes. Precise, unmalicious, devastating.
Steven Katz (E) — constructivism: the felt shift is trained by the tradition that produces it,
so its countersignature confirms the training, not the truth. Already on the eleven-name list.
Belongs here, seated against C4 specifically — not left floating as a general skeptic.
Until this cell is populated, “the body countersigns” is an assertion wearing the costume of a
standard.

Against C1 (the participation law) — empty
Nothing on the roster attacks the central claim. That is a strange thing to be true of a central
claim.
The attack exists and is old: participation is contamination with better branding. The
entire apparatus of blinding, randomization, and pre-registration was built because the
knower inside the system distorts it. Candidates: the replication-reform literature (D);
Popper and the unfalsifiability problem (I); Gellner’s The Psychoanalytic Movement (E) on
systems that metabolize every objection as further evidence.
That last one deserves attention. A book that says the instrument is inside the object has
built a structure in which disagreement can always be re-read as the disagreer’s
obstruction. Gellner is the diagnosis of exactly that move. It is the single most useful
uncomfortable name available.
Against C3 (the Stance) — empty
The available attack: gaze outward, attention inward is dissociation with good branding.
The depersonalization and derealization literature (D/C) describes the observing self as a
symptom, not an attainment. And Willoughby Britton’s adverse-effects research (D)
documents deadening, flattened affect, and detachment as outcomes of contemplative
practice.
For a book that opens reconsolidation windows and writes an Afterglow law, the harm
literature is not optional. Its absence is currently the book’s most defensible criticism.
Against C5 (compositional physics)
Richard Biernacki (E) — you built the instrument that finds it, and now you find it
everywhere. Aimed at Burnout = Obligation − Sanctuary and every molecule after it.
The diagnostic-reification critique (D/E) — the DSM’s history, and the RDoC turn away
from it, is the fully documented case study of a category scheme that became real by being
used. Available and unused.
Paul Meehl (D/I) — on theories in soft psychology that are unfalsifiable because everything
correlates with everything. The formal version of Biernacki’s charge.
Against C6, C7, C8 — reasonably held
C6: Han (E), and the internal contradiction — if practice is not for anything, why does the
book promise transformation? C7: Nāgārjuna’s snake-warning (B), Loy (B/A), Nishitani
(A/B), Weil (A/G). C8: Weil again, named already as its worthiest adversary. Correctly
seated.

The Tier 2 discipline
An adversary is only Tier 2 if the book could lose. Run each name against this: what
specifically breaks if they are right? If the answer is “we would phrase it differently,” demote
to Tier 4.
TIER 3 · INDEPENDENT ARRIVAL
Evidence rather than support. Weighted third because it is where the thesis stops being a
lineage and starts being a finding — but only if the independence is real.
The independence test is failing right now. Nishitani studied under Heidegger in Freiburg.
Corbin translated him into French in 1938. Henry built his system against him. Three of the
four names currently under consideration are in conversation with the same German. That is
a family quarrel, not a convergence.
The strong candidates, by family
Michael Polanyi (D → I) — the most valuable missing name in the project. A physical
chemist who arrived at indwelling — his word — tacit knowing, and the personal
participation of the knower, from inside the philosophy of science with no phenomenological
debt. We can know more than we can tell. The subsidiary-to-focal structure is the Stance
described by someone who came at it through crystallography. If C1 has one non-
continental witness, it should be him.
Heinz von Foerster (F) — second-order cybernetics. The observer inside the observed
system; objectivity as the delusion that observations can be made without an observer.
Arrived at C1 from engineering.
Harold Garfinkel (E) — ethnomethodology, 1967. Social facts are produced in the doing;
accounts are constitutive rather than descriptive; the breaching experiments are C1 as
laboratory demonstration.
Donald Schön (H/E) — The Reflective Practitioner. Knowing-in-action. And his image — the
swampy lowlands where the problems that matter live, versus the high hard ground of
technical rationality — is the de-management pass, drawn in 1983 by a management
theorist.
Wittgenstein (I) — Philosophical Investigations §129: the aspects of things most important
to us are hidden by their simplicity and familiarity; one cannot notice something because it
is always before one’s eyes. That is the WALL/DOOR/FLOOR veto reason, stated in 1953
with no Heidegger under it. Nothing hides like the always-there has an independent arrival

How to run this
Do not sort all thirty. The result is known: they are Tier 5, you said so yourself, and
confirming a premise is not research.
Run instead the one question, thirty times: does the thesis move if this one leaves? Expect
two or three yeses. Gendlin is one. Alexander is likely another. Those are the misfiled load-
bearers, and finding them is worth the whole pass.
Then use the empty cells as reading queue, in the order above.
The apex should stay narrow. Every promotion to Tier 1 costs something — a claim that
everything supports is a claim that nothing tests.

TIER 0 · THE PRECEDENT BENCH
A new tier, and it sits outside the pyramid rather than on top of it.
These names are not sorted by what they carry. They are sorted by what happened to
them when they tried this — a grand synthesis across contemplative and empirical
traditions, aimed at practitioners, delivered as a usable system. Their value is not agreement
or opposition. It is case history.
The grid needed this tier because Wilber would not sit anywhere else without distortion.
Ken Wilber — the nearest prior attempt, named as such in your own words: like The Theory
of Everything by Wilber in its systematization. His failure modes are unusually well
documented, which makes him the most instructive name on this bench:
The unfalsifiability charge. Everything fits inside AQAL, therefore nothing tests it. This
is Gellner’s objection landing on your nearest neighbor rather than on you — which
means you can watch what it did to him.
===PAGE 10===
The scholarship critiques. Frank Visser’s Integral World, Jeff Meyerhoff’s Bald
Ambition: sources cited toward a conclusion the system already held.
The 2006 “Wyatt Earp” episode, in which he answered his critics with contempt. It
cost him more than any critique did. A synthesis that cannot metabolize disagreement
declares something about the synthesis.
The color hierarchy that became a status ladder. People identifying as teal. A
developmental map handed to practitioners turns into a ranking of persons unless
something in the design actively prevents it.
That last one is the live question for you and it is not rhetorical. The metabolic arc —
Nascent → Privated → Full → Flourishing → Composting — is exactly the kind of scheme that
becomes “I’m mostly Flourishing, he’s still Privated.” What in the design prevents it? If the
answer is “the tone,” the answer is not sufficient. Wilber had good tone for twenty years.
Douglas Hofstadter, Gödel, Escher, Bach — also named by you, and the instructive
contrast. GEB succeeded at the breadth you want, and it succeeded partly by refusing to
deliver a usable framework. It performs its insight and never hands you the tool. Your book
hands over twelve primitives and a kata. That is a different bargain with the reader, and the
precedent does not transfer.
Gurdjieff / Ouspensky — the Fourth Way. The nearest structural ancestor to a mythic
operating system for the modern soul: total self-observation, a vocabulary, a school. Also
the clearest record of how such a thing acquires authority dynamics its founder did not
intend.
John Vervaeke — precedent in progress, already in your own stated cloaking. Watchable in
real time.
Mihaly Csikszentmihalyi — the other road: operationalize a single construct, get it into the
empirical literature, and pay a different price when flow becomes a productivity term. The
cost of being adopted rather than the cost of being dismissed.
WILBER, PROPERL Y SEATED
Your instinct was right — holonic categorization is not remotely all of it. He is already load-
bearing in at least two places without being credited, and he supplies one guardrail the
architecture currently lacks entirely.
1 · The Wilber–Combs lattice → TIER 1, for the state/trait throughline. States and
structures are independent axes. Anyone at any stage can have any state — but the state is
===PAGE 11===
interpreted according to the structure. The book already declares the ahwal/maqamat
distinction as the thing protecting it from spiritual bypass. That is the distinction. This is the
engine. It answers the question the entire psychedelic section must answer: why do people
have enormous experiences and not change? Because the state was interpreted by an
unchanged structure. Nothing else on the roster answers that with a mechanism.
2 · Lines of development → the title’s own logic. Firing on all cylinders presupposes
multiple semi-independent lines that can develop at different rates — world-class in the
cognitive line, infantile in the interpersonal one. That presupposition is the book’s diagnostic
premise, and it is a Wilber claim currently wearing a car metaphor.
3 · The pre/trans fallacy → TIER 2, against C2 and C7. And this is a hole the size of a
chapter. Pre-rational and trans-rational both look non-rational from outside and are
routinely confused. For a book that walks readers toward the collapse of the interface, the
gatekeeper fighting the clearing, and the deliberate destabilization at the peak — this is the
diagnostic that separates breakthrough from breakdown, and it is unnamed anywhere in the
architecture. Without it, the book has no principled way to tell a reader in dissolution from a
reader in regression. Neither does the reader.
4 · AQAL’s lower-right quadrant → the standing critique of the coaching field. The July
analysis found the shared blind spot of the American transformation canon: the radical
interiority of cause. Wilber’s four quadrants is precisely the structural objection —
interior/exterior crossed with individual/collective, and the Lower-Right (systems,
institutions, material conditions) is the quadrant every coach skips. He is already the
critique you reached independently. Use him that way rather than as a categorizer, and he
stops being furniture.
5 · Transcend and include → your native edit-verb. Koestler’s holon by way of Wilber, and
you use it as an instruction — “transcend and include v2.” It is already the compositional
logic of primitives → molecules → forms, and of the Haecceity Graph’s layered ontology. An
unattributed operating verb is worth attributing, if only so you can see what it commits you
to.
Net: one Tier 1 seat, one Tier 2 seat aimed at a genuine hole, one Tier 4 ground, and a Tier
0 case history. He was never a Tier 5 name.
THE COACHES — A BLOC, NOT ELEVEN NAMES
Crone, Katie, Singer, Hudson, Hendricks, the Conscious Leadership Group, Colonna,
Robbins, Wineland, Deida, Hübl.
===PAGE 12===
Individually most of them sort Tier 5. As a bloc they are Tier 2, and they aim at C5.
The five theories of where suffering lives — believed thought, resisted feeling,
disempowered state, disowned complicity, closed body — are five rival theories to the
compositional physics. Each is a complete competing account. Until now the C5 adversary
cell held only Biernacki, who attacks the method. These attack the content: they say
suffering has one location, and the molecular scheme says it has a combinatorial one. The
book has to either subsume them or beat them, and right now it does neither out loud.
Two members break out of the bloc:
Tony Robbins → TIER 2, seriously. The voluntarist pole, and nearly alone in it. He is the
only one who says direct where everyone else says allow. Your book resolves surrender-
versus-will through the middle voice — but a resolution is only earned if the opposing pole
was at full strength. Robbins at full strength is the claim that the not-doing is the disease.
Treat him as a punchline and the middle voice becomes a mood rather than a position.
Thomas Hübl → TIER 3, independent arrival at C5’s logic. What was never metabolized
lives on as absence. That is privation — arrived at through collective and ancestral trauma
work, with no Trika under it. Your own diagnostic sentence is this is Obligation privated of
Sanctuary. Same shape, different road, no shared teacher. This is a genuine found
correspondence and it is the strongest thing the coaches thread contributes.
And the bloc’s shared blind spot is a Tier 1 question for you, not for them. The radical
interiority of cause — the problem always relocated inside the person, where there is
leverage. Does the book inherit it? Burnout = Obligation − Sanctuary locates a burnout in a
personal configuration. The counterweights are already on your bench and currently unused
for this purpose: Han (auto-exploitation is structural), Illich, Rieff, Adorno on material
conditions going unexamined. This is a live seam and it is where a serious reader will press
first.
