What this prototype is not

Release and readership are the author's lane: “the sending will come — and it's not our concern right now” (ops/decisions.md, 2026-08-03). This page is private to him and is a way of thinking about the book.

Personal material appears only because the page is his.

The poems, the 4am letter, his father's sentence, and his reflections are held under the three conditions for personal material. On any public page each is his to place. The friend's name and the letter's text are left out.
