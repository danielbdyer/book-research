# Theories → Who Noticed This Too (area: `theories`)

### 1. The turn

ACC/IN's Theories page moves the reader's belief toward a winner: odds of being "closest to right," a scoreboard of adversarial tests, a quiz that sorts the visitor into a camp, and "Place your bet." The vault forbids that function at its root. The authorial stance says the vault "does not rank convergences as stronger or weaker evidence, and it does not convert agreement into support for a thesis," and that it "does not postulate efficacy" (`ops/methodology/authorial stance.md`); the field recording what a thinker carries for the book's argument "allocates reading; it does not rank evidence" (`ops/methodology/thesis-bearing and company.md`). A convergence is not a contender: it is one finding that several people reached by different roads, held as company rather than as proof. And the reader is sovereign, since the book is "auxiliary to a sovereign reader rather than the source or the certifier" of the reader's recognition (`ops/orientation.md`); a page that types the reader or tallies bets would act as that certifier.

The tracker's discipline about tests survives, since the vault has run real independence tests and dated their results; so the site tracks confidence and the book tracks provenance and debt. The stance permits provenance because it "serves accurate pointing rather than proof" (CLAUDE.md, "The center"), and "a finger aimed wrongly points at nothing": saying "no contact" when one thinker read the other is a wrong aim. The lead's candidate, a chart of how independent each arrival is, holds only if it stays categorical. A scalar bar reproduces the register of the mesh grid (an outside evaluation of the book's thinkers, received 2026-08-05), which calls independent arrival "evidence rather than support," a sentence `thesis-bearing and company.md` quotes and declines to adopt. The truer replacement records what kind of relation each finding is and what the book owes for it, because "The relation decides the remedy" (`notes/the apparatus descends from the settlement the book positions itself against.md`). Confidence turns into obligation.

The site's "different routes, same summit" inverts: the vault records the book's unanimity as "an artifact of the sampling" (`notes/the unanimity claim is an artifact of how the lineages were selected.md`), and its proposed handling is to "preserve difference before synthesis and keep the remainder" (`notes/curating traditions for their overlap manufactures the unanimity it then reports.md`), so this page's weight falls on where each thinker parts from the book. **Name: Who Noticed This Too** (the question asked by `the-constellation.md`, the vault file that reads every thinker by the finding they reached; `the-lenses.md`, Lens 5). **Promise:** "The findings the book did not invent, the people who reached them by other roads, where each of them parts from the book, and a dated record of which roads turned out to be one road."

#### 01 The convergences

**Card: Deep change is un-forgetting what was never lost.** Heidegger: "Being-true ('truth') means Being-uncovering" (*Being and Time* §44, M&R p. 262, via secondary, Grade B). Utpaladeva: the self "cannot manifest his own glory until his qualities have been brought to light" (ĪPK IV.1.17, Torella p. 218). Wittgenstein, with no Heidegger beneath him: aspects "hidden because of their simplicity and familiarity" (*PI* §129). **Where they part:** Heidegger later wrote that aletheia "may not be equated with truth" (1964, held from secondary research); the poles disagree on whether un-seeing is a veil or a contraction; Wittgenstein: "what is hidden... is of no interest to us" (§126). **Status:** survived; the roster around it did not. *Sources:* `notes/aletheia and pratyabhijna converge on truth as un-forgetting.md`; the Kashmir Śaiva loci record, item 5; the Wittgenstein record.

**Card: A person changes by taking part, not by watching.** The corpus: "with zero disagreement across every lineage the book loves: transformation is enacted participation inside a held container" (`arc-gloss: overlay four`). From outside the gathering: "the living being is a source of perturbations and not of instructions" (Maturana and Varela, p. 96); "Those who say 'Yes' are rewarded by the adventures they have" (Johnstone, *Impro*, p. 92). **Where they part:** each stops at inner experience; De Jaegher and Di Paolo call the interaction "typically fleeting" (2007, p. 504). **Status:** the roll-call is a selection; the outside arrivals were read 2026-08-30. *Source:* `notes/transformation is enacted participation inside a held container.md`.

**Card: The convergence that turned out to be a lineage.** Laozi: "The way never acts, yet nothing is left undone" (ch. 37, Lau). Heidegger: "the key word in Laotse's poetic thinking is Tao, which 'properly speaking' means way" (*On the Way to Language*, via secondary). **Where Laozi parts:** the Daodejing dissolves the striving self where the book restores a first person. *Source:* `archive/reading — laozi, daodejing (the wu-wei, ziran, and subtraction chapters).md`, items 1, 5, 6.

**Card: A state is given; a station is earned.** Al-Qushayrī: "States come without asking, whereas stations are acquired by the sweat of one's brow" (Knysh 2007, p. 78). Wilber: "a passing state has become a permanent trait" (*AQAL Journal* 1(1), p. 12). **Where they part:** one ordered road ("expansion is above the station of hope," p. 81) against two axes. *Source:* the state-and-station note.

### 3. What this parallel revealed about the book

**The independence test has never been run on the book's own route to its foundation, and the one partial check leaned toward the room.** The two traditions' independence survived May, but the book's reading of Heidegger came through a Landmark room in 2017, and the difference separating it from Erhard's (disclosure against creation) narrowed on 2026-09-02, when the Forum's own study named its goal "unconcealment." *Found* in `notes/the book's heideggerian foundation was never given the independence test.md` and the Erhard-verbs note. *Inferred:* a draft should state the traditions' independence and the book's route to Heidegger as separate claims.

**The vault still speaks the evidential register the stance forbids, and a public chart would print it.** "Counts for more" (`the-constellation.md`), "worth more than either alone" (the shared-teacher note), "the strongest class of evidence the book has" (the engineered-epiphany note), "strongest genuinely independent arrival" (the Deci–Ryan note): an `rg` for these phrases and the grid's quoted "materially stronger" returns five files. *Inferred:* the manuscript can say "with no contact" as history without "therefore stronger."

**Files disagree about the record.** Hübl's thinker page still records what he carries for the book as `independent-arrival` and calls the shared-teacher test "the test his arrival passes," while his claim note records the failure; and `the-constellation.md` names the participation law's outside witnesses differently (six including Lewin, then five) from the participation note and Lewin record (Polanyi, von Foerster, Garfinkel, Schön, Johnstone). *Found.* A draft should name witnesses, not count them.

**"Company" carries three senses:** a value for what a thinker carries, the standing of every convergence ("company, not proof"), and a demotion ("company, not an outside arrival," of Vervaeke). *Found* in `thesis-bearing and company.md` and the participation-law record, item 5. *Inferred:* the book's prose needs one sense.
