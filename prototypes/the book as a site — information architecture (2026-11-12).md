This document maps accelerateinward.com's information architecture against the research vault, organ by organ, and records what holding the two structures together showed about the book. The prototype site built from it is `prototypes/the book as a site (2026-11-12).html`, published privately to the author at https://claude.ai/artifact/VHkfiTfdCdsxQ7stX4tkoA. The site as read on 12 November 2026 is captured in `prototypes/the book as a site — reader reports/accelerateinward.com as read (2026-11-12).md`, and the six reports the prototype was assembled from sit beside it in the same folder.

## How it was made

The session read every page of accelerateinward.com in a headless browser, including the hidden layer beneath the footer. Six readers then each held one region of the site against one region of the vault, read only, quoting only from files in this repository and naming every count's method. The brief they shared is `the brief every reader received.md` in the reports folder. Its governing instruction was that a parallel is a turn, not a reskin: for each organ of the site, the reader asked what the organ does, then asked what the book's own law does with that function, and recorded the law by path where it forces the organ to turn.

## The one structural move: the order inverts

accelerateinward.com stacks a media company on top and hides a floor of experience beneath it, entered through an eye in the footer and announced as "not for reading." The book's front matter makes reading itself the undergoing — "This book is designed to be undergone, not understood… Reading is remembering" (`abridged` p. 1) — and the abridged edition calls the system vocabulary "scaffolding… the wooden forms a craftsman uses to coax a curve into existence and then removes when the curve holds" (`abridged` p. 3). So on the book's site the walk through the house is the ground floor, and the apparatus (the readings, the convergences, the company of thinkers) sinks beneath it, reached by the same small deliberate act as the site's eye.

## The parallel site

- **The house** (the ground floor; replaces the hidden layer). The three front-matter sentences over the door; seven rooms, each an idea made walkable and an invitation the reader may decline — the room you are already in, the room where a word is checked against the body, the pantry where grief is not finished alone, the room with two chairs, the orchard before the summit, the silent room at the summit, the room you return to; then the kitchen; then the question "who?", which the page cannot hear; then a placeholder for the author's last line and a blank leaf for the reader. Floors carry names, not numbers, with a return key, and nothing is marked done. A gold thread runs down the margin and breaks once, at the summit.
- **Where this came from** (replaces the Manifesto, the theory timeline, and the "This week" ticker). A disclosure written as a letter: accelerateinward.com's eleven manifesto sections each answered as kept, turned, or declined; "accelerate, or ripen"; the making dated from the poems to the vault's exit proposal; the vault's log, honest that it has been quiet since 4 September.
- **Beneath: the readings** (replaces Research). Counts of the work; an assistant that answers only from the reading records and declines two questions; six concerns listed in the vault's order rather than by size; one page per reading with fields for what the reading corrected in the book's account, where the thinker parts from the book, and whether the line is ready for the manuscript; tensions kept open rather than scored; the oldest texts read, with the poems beneath them; ten admission rules.
- **Beneath: who noticed this too** (replaces Theories). In place of odds, a table of where each finding came from and what the book owes it; a changelog of independence findings; the reader's own sentence and a finding the reader chooses, in place of the quiz; the convergence cards with both halves quoted and the refusals kept; the independence scoreboard; the charges and the handling under trial; the readings that could change a finding's standing; answer, refuse, or decline, in place of the bet; the plain-terms and corpus-names switch, in place of the plain-English and product-spec switch.
- **Beneath: the company** (replaces Library). "Where are you standing?" in place of the Guide that maps a path; the primary texts beneath the book in place of the picks; twelve thinker cards showing role, what the book loses if they leave, whether the vault read their own text, and where they part, in place of counts; browse facets that never sort; the flagged and not-yet-seated names, in place of "under the radar."
- **Reply** (replaces Create with us, the story board, the shows, the series, the masthead, Sponsor, Feedback, and Subscribe). One sentence the reader writes and keeps, dated; the open territory the earliest draft named and never wrote; forms the book could take; the readings as a series of questions that can fail; who parts from the book; what the book owes, in place of "presented by"; what the book cannot see; the next letter.
- **The map**. The side-by-side architecture, the table below, and the findings.

## What the parallel revealed about the book

Each finding says what was found and what was inferred; the evidence and paths are in the named report.

### For the manuscript

1. **The summit has not been written as the silence it is designed to be.** Found: the Psychedelic Moment in the abridged edition runs 2,172 words, the second-longest of fifteen units against a mean of 1,160, and carries REBUS, default-mode suppression, James's four marks, Griffiths, and Dölen; neither "mirror" nor "margin" occurs in it, while `walked` p. 22 and `first-telling` p. 12 describe the prose thinning and the margins going silent there. Inferred: the silent summit is a requirement the next draft of that unit has to meet against its own longest exposition. (`reader report — the house.md`)
2. **The walk ends in a kitchen, not a void, in four places** (`abridged` pp. 36, 39, 47; `walked` p. 21). Inferred: the last unit can use this whichever way the author answers whether the last movement is integration or a sending. (house)
3. **The book has not chosen its last line, and every call to act repeats the choice.** Found: the descriptions end "welcome home," the executed draft ends "Step in.", and the provisional first letter closes "Write your sentence. Then turn the page." Inferred: the author's decision about the last line governs the voice of any surface built for the book. (reply)
4. **The honest destination of a reader's reply is a person who knows the reader.** Found: "what a book cannot supply is then not a second person but a person who knows this reader, present or remembered" (`ops/second person ecology.md` §4). Inferred: the letters' answering instruction could name that destination, and Book I needs one stated place where it points away from itself, for which Engler 1984, p. 39, is the natural citation. (reply; company)
5. **Ordinary time is one gap seen from three sides.** Found: the return is "the thinnest position in the whole graph" (`ops/scaffold.md` J2); "almost none marks its finish inside the person" (`ops/arc ecology.md` §4); the earliest draft's unwritten titles group into "the design of lived time" (`ops/open corpus.md`). Inferred: this region is authorship, not extraction. (reply)
6. **The book's "why now" has an older source than AI, and the working title still carries the speed its doctrine refuses.** Found: poem #3's "the new paralysis of modernity / is dreaming so longingly about what could be / that we forget to live life in the now" is held nowhere in the graph; "Firing on All Cylinders" began on 21 June 2024 as "a peak-performance framing." Inferred: under the primary-material rule the poem's diagnosis governs. (manifesto)
7. **A drafting session should quote a thinker from the reading record.** Found: 21 of 47 records carry explicit correction language and 21 say an edition must be checked before a line reaches the manuscript; the record template has no field for either, nor for where the thinker parts from the book. Inferred: adding the fields is a structural change for the author. (readings)
8. **The chapter patrons are an unaudited credit line, and the mirror has no image note.** Found: bell hooks is unread; Donella Meadows holds two patron seats and has no page; "mirror" occurs 13 times across 7 of the 8 documents and no image note or glossary row carries it. (reply; house)
9. **The independence test has never been run on the book's own route to Heidegger**, which came through a Landmark room in 2017; the Forum's scholarly study names its goal "unconcealment" (Hyde and Kopp, *Speaking Being*, p. 5). Inferred: a draft should state the traditions' independence and the book's own route as two separate claims. (who noticed this too)

## What the prototype is not

- It is not a launch. The author owns release and readership.
- It is an instance of what it warns about: AI readers drafted it, and "AI-written authorship" is on the book's list of things it must not become. Following accelerateinward.com's sections also lets another site organize the book; a real page would follow the book's own order.
- Personal material — poem lines, the 4am letter, the father's sentence, the reflection — appears because the page is private to the author. Each is his to place on any public surface. The friend's name and the letter's text are omitted.
- The walkable house is one of two shapes still open (house or centerless field, `ops/decision sitting — 2026-09-03.md` item 14), and the house with its six-phase arc is Book II's banked material; Book I's shape is the four movements toward the facilitator's withdrawal. Named, ungraded floors with a return key survive either answer.
- Several halves are not yet the thinker's own words: Heidegger read through the Stanford Encyclopedia and May through Parkes, and Adorno, Wasserstrom, the Kyoto School, and the adverse-effects literature known from secondary accounts. The cards that rest on them carry the grade.
