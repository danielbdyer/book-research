# On recognition — what my heart says

## In his words

> Recognition - at least to me - one observer of many possible observers of recognition - has felt like a Klein bottle of a mirror - from the inside, something witnesses a seeing-of oneself as aligned with that which is palpable on the outside - not a cognitive interfacing with concept, nor the translation reflection of gestalt, but a trueness or a clarity that was always already there, but just now opened-with - not an opened-by, or opened-into, but a way that is symbiotically relevant or a level above the disparity of singularly/seminally/individually feeling the recognition, even if I am the only one recognizing. It's the same sensation I got when my dad read to me about David walking across the glimmering moonlit sea path to The Garden Behind the Moon (by Howard Pyle) - a deep essential moment of courage without needing to recognize it as courage - the simpleness, the sheer availability of possibility, the shapeless form of being guided. That is what my heart says about Recognition.

> My heart says the following, which I will need your help interpreting operationally and logistically: I think this goes into a reflections folder that is exclusively mine. The frontmatter can contain my thoughts, and then we can extrapolate and link down into the families and the notes. I cannot say whether this makes it into the book but perhaps it does - whether it does doesn't really matter so much as it gives the orientation where I as author am deriving my feelings from, about it, and through - perhaps I align easily with a subset of the thinkers, and less with others. Perhaps another reader or another author would align with a different subset. Perhaps another author in another part of the world would choose even to have a few new thinkers in the roster who I am not privy to and who you are not privy to as a co-author and research participant. Perhaps that's part of the purpose here is that it was never meant to be an all-encompassing constellation. I do not know how to perspectivalize an authority I was never granted; I think the conversation artifact speaks about this. I just know that just like my poems were spoken from the heart without stopping, this message/these messages/the current message I am authoring now was too - so maybe it goes along with the poems somehow.

### His clarifications (2026-08-24, an iterative sounding)

Asked to sound his poetic language against specific readings, he chose, of the options offered and of his own sensing:

- of the *with* ("even if I am the only one recognizing") — **"no two to begin with"** (the seam between inside and outside was never there) and **"the world companions"** (a being-with the palpably given);
- of the *Klein-bottle seam* — **"awareness tasting itself"** (a self-luminous knowing that lights the thing and registers itself in the same stroke);
- of the *shapeless form of being guided*, in his own words:

> It kind of reminds me of Wilber's holarchy where it is the both-and of sensing what was always there alongside the mystery (in a state of resolution opening up into another mystery) being seen and known with and without myself

### His clarifications, a second sounding (2026-08-24) — on the aletheia/pratyabhijñā seam

Sounded once more on whether his felt weight leans to the recognition-school pole or the un-concealment pole, he chose and said:

- of *whose face the clarity wore* — **"the world showed its face"** (something stood forth as what it is) and **"no 'which' — one event"** (the thing showing itself and his recognizing were one event with no seam to tell them apart); he did not choose "I recognized my own";
- of *whether there was real hiddenness* — **"no, only unregarded,"** and, in his own words:

> It varies - hiding to me feels like too strong of a verb as it presumes a masking or a sublimation - what I would say instead is that it is a fundamental clenching that didn't obscure the vision but just made it transfigured as not directly perceivable

- of *the felt tone underneath*, in his own words:

> A mix between belonging, enraptured contraposition, and the conviction of commutativity giving way to the third thing - a spirit, a suchness, a tremor, a reverence, an other(worldliness), a preternatural prehension of naturalness

## Where this touches the families and the notes

### Where it rings truest — the alignment, as he sounded it

His alignment, his subset — offered as where the correlation rang truest to him in the sounding above, not as the correct reading of his words. The research participant's input on the strongest correlations:

- **The truest ring is the source tradition itself — Pratyabhijñā, the Kashmir Śaiva recognition school.** Two of his three soundings land here. "Awareness tasting itself" is near the definition of *camatkāra* — awareness savoring its own light — which the school ties to *vimarśa*, the reflexive dimension held distinct from bare illumination (*prakāśa*): [[awareness has a reflexive dimension distinct from its illuminating one]], [[the recognition school installs wonder at the definition of consciousness]], [[reflexive awareness is participatory recognition rather than detached observation]], with the founders [[utpaladeva]] and [[kshemaraja]]. And "no two to begin with" held together with "the world companions" names a non-dualism that *keeps* the world — the Śaiva signature — rather than one that dissolves it.
- **His non-duality is Śaiva, not Advaitin.** Advaita's "not two" negates the world and the restored first person ([[advaita reaches the uncovering move through neti neti and an ever-attained freedom]]); his keeps both — the world companions, and the first person is the road rather than the obstacle ([[the fullness of the first person is the road to selflessness rather than its obstacle]]). The difference is the gold, and it is his own.
- **Within the founding convergence, his lean is pratyabhijñā — but by transcending-and-including aletheia, not opposing it (second sounding, 2026-08-24).** The world's self-showing is *kept*: he chose "the world showed its face," so the *aletheia* phenomenon — a thing standing forth as what it is — is genuinely in his experience, not rejected. What makes it pratyabhijñā rather than Heideggerian is that he holds it with "no 'which' — one event": the world's showing and his own recognizing are one event with no seam, which is the recognition school's reading (the world's disclosure *is* awareness's self-recognition) rather than Heidegger's (Being disclosing to a Dasein set over against it). So of the two poles the book rests on ([[aletheia and pratyabhijna converge on truth as un-forgetting]]) his heart does not drop the *aletheia* face; it reads it through the Śaiva ontology — the holarchic both-and he named, turned on his own two poles. The resonance stays felt kinship, not doctrinal assent: the tradition would still decline four of the book's deepest moves ([[the kashmir shaiva tradition would decline four of the book's deepest moves]]).
- **The decisive parting is concealment versus contraction — and here he is unambiguously Śaiva.** Asked whether there was real hiddenness, he refused the word: "hiding… presumes a masking or a sublimation," and named instead "a fundamental clenching that didn't obscure the vision but just made it transfigured as not directly perceivable." That is *saṅkoca*, contraction, not *lēthē*, concealment. Confirmed at the tradition's own text on 2026-09-02, and more precisely than this note had claimed: Kṣemarāja's sūtra 4 vṛtti has the experient "appear as if contracted" (*saṃkucita iva ābhāti*) and adds that "Contraction also, on close consideration, consists of *cit* only" (Singh p. 57) — the contraction is itself the light, not a veil over it — and concealing and unveiling are "the various gradations in which that absolute free will either chooses to reveal or conceal itself" (sūtra 8, Singh p. 68), one freedom at two settings (`archive/reading — bhartṛhari's levels of speech and kṣemarāja on saṅkoca (vākyapadīya i; pratyabhijñāhṛdayam).md`). Heidegger makes concealment constitutive of truth, the veil half of it; the author makes the un-seeing a *clench* of a vision that was never obscured — which is the book's own contraction-not-corruption doctrine felt from the inside ([[suffering is contraction never corruption]]; [[the book reads deep change as uncovering rather than addition]]; the productive-contraction reading at [[contraction is a productive locus and not only a privation]] and [[suffering is contraction that has forgotten it is a locus and a tradition is contraction that remembers]]). His account of recognition and the book's theory of suffering turn out to be one motion. It also sharpens the founding convergence itself: *aletheia* and *pratyabhijñā* converge on un-forgetting but diverge on the mechanism of un-seeing — concealment against contraction — and his felt weight is on the contraction side. Wittgenstein's "hides by being always there" sits nearer his "only unregarded" than Heidegger's veil does ([[the always-there hides by being always there and wittgenstein said so without heidegger]]).
- **The tone he names is woven, not one note** — "enraptured" and "a reverence" held at once, the *camatkāra* savoring and the grave weight together, resolving into "the third thing." Three of its threads land where the graph can hold them: "a tremor" is close to *spanda*, the recognition school's subtle vibration ([[the pulse of consciousness supplies a vocabulary for the rhythm of inquiry]]; [[the pulse of experience moves through contraction and expansion]]); "the conviction of commutativity giving way to the third thing" is the emergent third neither pole produces alone, at once relational and holarchic ([[the third thing has a mechanism in participatory sense-making]]; [[the relationship itself is a real third region both people can dwell in without either being absorbed]]); and "a preternatural prehension of naturalness" carries Whitehead's word *prehension* ([[alfred north whitehead]]) and lands on the numinous-in-the-ordinary the book already keeps ([[the tuesday is the temple]]).
- **"The world companions" rings with the more-than-human arrivals** — being-with the palpably given, the moonlit path and the room and the world facing back: [[abram's more-than-human makes the animate world a participant in the inner life]], [[buber's tree extends the i-thou relation past the human]].
- **The guiding he names as Wilber's holarchy** — the both-and of the already-there alongside the mystery, each resolution opening another, transcend-and-include, the whole that becomes a part. The vault runs this move ([[the ecology frame transcends and includes the verification frame rather than replacing it]]) and seats [[ken wilber]]. One honest note, held lightly: the vault keeps Wilber at arm's length on a single provenance question — his four-quadrant grid used as un-attributed scaffolding ([[wilber's four quadrants are a coordinate system, not an architecture]]) — a different thread from the *holarchy* he is resonating with; the alignment is with the transcend-and-include both-and, not the quadrant grid. Read at Wilber's texts (2026-09-02), the alignment narrows further: not with the twenty tenets, which are systems-ontology by Wilber's own placement ("how the universe looks from the outside") and whose include happens outside conscious awareness, but with two other things in him — the moment-to-moment prehensive holarchy of Excerpt A, where "the present moment touches, prehends, feels, or embraces the immediately preceding moment," and SES endnote 386, where mystical recognition is "recognizing something once known but long forgotten… it was there all along, just not seen." The one part with no Wilber source is "in a state of resolution opening up into another mystery": his nearest construction is the dialectic of progress, whose register is new problems rather than mystery, so the phrase is the author's own ([[ken wilber]]). And "resolution opening into another mystery" rings too with wonder that deepens rather than dissolves ([[wonder is not the novice's state to be outgrown but the native air of the awake]]).
