# reflections — the author's own, exclusively

This folder is the author's, exclusively. It holds reflections spoken from the heart without stopping, the way the poems were — primary material and orientation, not doctrine and not research. Its purpose is to hold the place the author derives his feelings about the book from, about it, and through: where his alignment with the material actually comes from, which the rest of the vault cannot supply because it is his and not the graph's.

Three things hold about everything in here:

- **It is primary, kin to the poems.** Like the poems (`sources/the poems.pdf`), a reflection is the author's offering and the book's personal layer. It grounds no doctrinal inference; a phrase the book shares with a reflection originates in the reflection. Whether any of it reaches the book does not change its standing — it is the orientation, not the argument, and it is complete whether or not it is used.
- **His words are inviolable.** The author's own text sits at the top of each file, verbatim, and is never edited, paraphrased, condensed, or "improved" by a session. Beneath a rule, the research participant may extrapolate — pointing down into the families and the notes where the reflection touches them — but that extrapolation is derived, marked plainly as not the author's, and claims no authority he did not grant. The pointers run one way, from the reflection down into the graph; the graph does not depend on them.
- **It was never meant to be all-encompassing.** The author aligns easily with a subset of the thinkers and less with others; another reader, another author, another author in another part of the world would align with a different subset, and might seat thinkers neither he nor a co-author is privy to. That the constellation is one author's resonance rather than a complete map is part of the point, not a gap to close. The vault already holds this from the outside at `notes/the unanimity claim is an artifact of how the lineages were selected.md`; here it is owned from the inside.

The folder is outside the note-graph checks by design, exactly as `sources/` is: nothing here is a graph note, so nothing here is validated, counted, or policed as one. It persists because it is committed to the author's private repository.
