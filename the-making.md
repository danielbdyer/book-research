# The Making — the eighth family, how the book is honestly made

*The book read by its own making: how it came to be, and the rules that keep the offering honest. Where the Arc reads the object walked in order and the Concerns read the doctrine, this family reads the book by **the integrity of its production** — the origin the record supports, the house rules the vault works under, the company-sort that keeps convergence company rather than proof, the direction of flow that keeps a life upstream of its record, and the framework held backstage. It is authored from `notes/methods.md` and the making sections of `notes/book-structure.md`, and it closes the coverage pivot: the last region no other family reaches. Its stance is the book's own — the honesty rules are not a debunking of the research but the apparatus that makes the offering trustworthy, "convergence is company, never corroboration" stated as a working discipline.*

## What this family is, and the apparatus

The book states one law about itself — "the book performs what it describes" — and the making inherits it: the vault is the law aimed at its own production. This family gathers the propositions about *how the book is made* and *how it stays honest*, most of which live in the methods map and the making sections of the structure map. The five questions specialize to the making: **Locus** — whose act is this, the author's or the vault's? **Encounter** — which rule or making-fact is this? **Recognition** — what does the making disclose about the book, and where is its honesty rule? **Perspective** — which findings are settled and which are the author's diagnosis held delicately? **Practice** — where the making enters the book (mostly as the front matter's honest offer). The governing discipline: the making is reported at the specificity the record supplies, the distances the record kept are kept (`ops/methodology/personal provenance.md`), and no finding here is turned into a disproof of the research — each is the integrity that lets the company stand as company.
