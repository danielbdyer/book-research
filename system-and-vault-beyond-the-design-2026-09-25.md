# Beyond the design: what the vault holds that the system did not foresee, and how the system grows to receive it

*This document records where the research vault holds material the system in `reframe-map-and-system-2026-09-25.md` did not foresee, and how the system grows to receive it. It is the second of three documents written on 2026-09-25 from one study. The first, `system-and-vault-collation-2026-09-25.md`, places the vault's existing material inside the system. The third, `system-and-vault-left-to-populate-2026-09-25.md`, records the places the system defines that the vault does not yet fill. The study's eight reader reports and their brief are kept in `ops/system readings — 2026-09-25/`, and they are cited here by label (O1 to O4, F1 to F4). The collation's Part X gives the key.*

*The design was built from the transcript of the reframe conversation alone, and it states one rule for everything it contains: each type, field, and relation names the function of the books it serves, or it is not built. This document applies the same rule to what the vault holds beyond the design. Material that serves a function of the books becomes an expansion of the system. Material that serves none belongs to the vault's own apparatus, which is a finding and not a fault. Some of what the vault holds does not extend the design but corrects it, often because the author has already decided the question; those corrections come first. Every expansion here is a proposal at surfaced standing. The author decides which, if any, the vault adopts.*

## How to read this

- **Part I** states the finding in brief.
- **Part II** lists the corrections: where the vault shows the design wrong, sorted by kind.
- **Part III** takes each body of material beyond the design in turn. For each it gives what the material is, why the design cannot hold it, the function of the books it serves, the expansion, and one real instance from the vault written in the expanded form.
- **Part IV** lists what stays outside the books' system, and the four pieces of the vault's apparatus the system should take in.
- **Part V** states the grown system in one place.
- **Part VI** names what this asks of the next generation of the vault.

The design's types are Concern, Pair, Practice, Figure, Encounter, Locus, Crossing, Durable perspective, Craft move, Passage, and Book. Its shared fields are Standing, Scale, Lens, and Provenance, and its registers are Commitments and the Remainder. The collation's Part II defines each in one line, and defines the vault's own words used below. Two of those words recur here. *Company* means thinkers who reached the same finding independently, held as company rather than as proof. A *nascent* note is a claim stated but not yet substantiated by a reading. The decisions register is the table of settled questions in `ops/decisions.md`.

## I. The finding in brief

1. **The vault corrects the design in about twenty places, and in ten of them the author has already decided the question.** The most consequential corrections:
   - Book II's dramaturgy is the psychedelic arc, by the author's decision of 2026-08-16. The design gave both books the four movements.
   - The design calls craft its largest gap. The vault built a craft category, and the author cut it on 2026-08-09.
   - The vault's `state:` field does not record the author's standing, by his decision of 2026-08-05.
   - The Sanskrit terms pair as contraction and expansion, with reflexive awareness held apart, by his decision of 2026-08-20. This leaves no basis for the design's reading of reflexive awareness and for its "one shape at four scales" table.
   - The sentence the design used for the aim of its whole axiom system is the model's, not the letter's.

2. **One expansion runs through all eight reports: a field recording the author's standing on each piece of content, and a mark on every quotation saying whose words it is.** The vault records his acts in eight separate places and never on the content they raise. Without that record, the model's words have been presented as the author's at least seven times.

3. **Three whole bodies of the books' material lie outside the design, and each serves functions the design names.**
   - The theory of suffering (the contraction concern, 48 notes).
   - Verification: how anyone knows something happened, with its counterfeits.
   - Sequence: Book II's decided arc, the house, the named units, and a sort of about sixty sequences by what moves a reader to the next phase.

   Four more bodies need the design's types widened: the letter as a form and a deed, the second person's roles, the manuscript's states and whose hand wrote each, and the making of the book with the poems beneath it.

4. **The vault's apparatus mostly stays outside the books' system, and four pieces come in.** They are the relations between draft states of one passage, the thirty directives as commitments the system keeps, the readiness conditions as a test on a passage, and the vault's own exit proposal as the operation the design's "structure serves encounter" lacks.

5. **The design enters the vault as one more reading of the graph, organized by what the books need, with two recording fields beneath it.** It does not enter as a schema that re-files the vault's 502 notes.

## II. Corrections: where the vault shows the design wrong

### A. Where the author has already decided otherwise

1. **Book II's dramaturgy is the psychedelic arc.** On 2026-08-16 the author allocated the two dramaturgies: "this can be book 1 and book 2." Book I takes the reframe's four movements toward the facilitator's withdrawal, and Book II takes the psychedelic arc toward a peak (`ops/decisions.md`, the row "Which dramaturgy spines the manuscript"; `ops/methodology/the two-book architecture.md`). The design resolved the question of the manuscript's spine as "the four movements, from the transcript" and gave both books the same movement. The transcript contains the word "psychedelic" 0 times (O4, O2, F2). The design's Part VIII rows for the spine are corrected by this row.

2. **Craft was built and then cut.** The design calls craft "the largest gap the overlay reveals". The vault opened a craft category on 2026-08-01 under the rule "Record the mechanism, never the verdict" (`ops/methodology/craft evidence.md`). By 2026-08-08 it held eighteen notes of exactly the design's kind, each with a mark that can be checked in the executed draft. The author retired them on 2026-08-09: "Cut the craft-about-drafts notes too", because "a research corpus studies the ideas rather than the manuscript's sentence technique" (`ops/decisions.md`). The notes are recoverable from commit `1efe050` (O4). The design's reason for craft, that a facilitator reader learns from the book's own moves, arose after the cut and after the two-book decision. Reopening the row is the author's act: the decisions register says a reversal "is a /rethink proposal with the author's decision, never a session's own act" (F4).

3. **The `state:` field is not the standing ladder.** The author's bound of 2026-08-05 reads: "`state:` **decouples.** It records how substantiated a note is: a maturity marker for the vault's own material, informing which pass to run next and nothing the book asserts" (`ops/methodology/vocabulary policy.md`). The design's ladder has to be a separate field. Mapping nascent to surfaced and full to believed would let a reading raise the author's standing (F4, F2, O1, O3).

4. **The unit is the proposition, with the concept allocated elsewhere.** On 2026-08-13 the author settled the unit question as "Both, allocated". The proposition-sentence is the markdown voice-bearing unit, and the named concept is the node of any future concept layer (`ops/decisions.md`). The design's Pair is therefore a way of reading pairs out of the propositions, not a replacement for them (F4).

5. **The Sanskrit terms pair as contraction and expansion, and the author declined a welded circuit.** The row of 2026-08-20 records that he "got confused with vimarsa/vikasa". Contraction (saṅkoca) pairs with expansion (vikāsa) as the pulse's two phases, and reflexive awareness (vimarśa) is "a distinct element held on its own relevance". In his words, "not necessarily interested in pairing together vocabulary terms that don't necessarily go together just because" (`ops/decisions.md`; O1). Two things in the design lose their basis:
   - Part IV's reading that "seeing the bubble is the reflexive move, vimarśa". The vault already holds it as model-elaborated.
   - The use of the seven-step Śaiva grammar as the first column of the "one shape at four scales" table.

6. **The false peak is already answered by the author's pick.** On 2026-08-20 he chose "peak resolves into return", which the register glosses as "the peak is an interior station, not the destination, which also answers the author's own false-peak hazard" (`ops/decisions.md`; O4). The design treats the false peak as raised once and never returned to. It stands at the author's decision.

7. **The four kinds of structure are four kinds, not one shape.** The decisions register reads the pulse, the five questions, the four movements, and the five nested orders as "four different kinds of structure at four altitudes (an operator, a method, a dramaturgy, an ontology)" (`ops/decisions.md`, the row "Does the book have one spine or the four the reframe offers?"). The directive that carries the ruling adds that "shared vocabulary across them is evidence of common parentage, not of common structure" (`ops/methodology/the four kinds of structure.md`). The design's "one shape at four scales" table lines the same material up as one shape, and it should give way to the directive (O4).

8. **The quadrants are registered as a lens, not seated as a field.** The decision of 2026-08-13 reads that "the four ontological roots, Wilber's four quadrants, and the plotted-lineage stack are registered lenses (research-frontier material), not re-seatings" (`ops/decisions.md`). A Lens field on every piece of content would install as schema what the author registered as a way in (F4).

9. **Kashmir Śaivism is already named the book's deepest well.** The author's word of 2026-08-16 treats the design's open question of how deeply the Śaiva underpinning is stated: "absolutely, it does seem to occupy that, and I think that's fine. It surprised me too" (quoted in `notes/the tradition the book owes most has no chair in its company.md`; F3).

The design, built from a transcript in which the felt shift "is never author-ratified", has no place for it (F2). Sounding B3, a question only the author can answer, now asks whether the felt shift or durability is his standard (`ops/practice-model ecology — candidate next steps.md`).

## III. What lies beyond the design, body by body

### 1. The author's standing, and whose words a sentence is

**What it is.** The vault records the author's acts in eight places (F4):
- a thinker's role, moved from candidate only by his decision;
- a tension's status, moved to resolved only by his act;
- placements, recorded only when he makes them;
- 119 decision rows;
- dated "author statement" citations;
- his three confidence tiers of 2026-08-04;
- the reflection's standing;
- the split provenance of the encounter record.

It records none of them on the content they raise. The provenance reconstructions mark whose words each sentence is ("[D] your words, [C] the AI’s, [A] hers as you transcribed", `provenance/provenance fourth addendum.md`, line 11), and the marks stop there. The cost is measured. The model's or an AI collaborator's words are presented as the author's in at least seven places; the third document lists each with its path. The most consequential is a sentence about the 4am letter, "The recognition didn’t require the mirror", marked [C] in its source and quoted in nine files without the mark (O2, O1).

**Why the design cannot hold it as it stands.** The design has the right ladder and the right rule, that only the author raises standing. But it has no place for the pointer to the act that raised a piece of content, no place for the sign by which he raised it, no mark for whose words a quotation is, and no rung for a session's own derivation.

**Function served.** Function 6, showing the author's crossing as an exemplar, "marked by how firmly he holds it". The commitment "The author is the first participant", whose test ("Did this pass through the author's own encounter?") cannot be applied without the record. And the principle "Retrieval may be delegated; authorship may not".

**Expansion.**
- **Standing becomes a field that only the author's acts set.** It sits beside `state:`, never replacing it. Its values:
  - *surfaced*, the default for everything a session, the corpus, the model, or a reading brought;
  - *encountered*, *inferred*, *believed*, *claimed*, and *written*;
  - *sent* and *answered*, for Passages.
- **Every rung above surfaced carries a pointer** to the dated act that raised it: a decision row, a dated author statement, the reflection, or a placement. No session writes a rung, only the pointer to his act (F3, F4).
- **An optional *sign* on each raise:** a felt shift, durability, a reading, a room (F1).
- **A *derived* tag beneath encountered** for a session's own inference, graded strict, plausible, or conjecture as the root derivations file already grades them. This reserves *inferred* for the author (F2).
- **A voice mark on every quotation from the transcript or the provenance record:** the author, the model, the collaborator, or a named third party. The transcript already carries the mark in its prompt and response headers, and the provenance files carry it as [D], [C], and [A]. A quotation the author ratified but did not write carries both marks, "the model's words, ratified by the author" (O1).

### 3. Verification: how anyone knows something happened

**What it is.** Most of the felt-shift concern's material is about verification rather than about inner life (F1): how the book, its author, its reader, and each tradition in its company tell that something occurred, what each sign certifies, and how each sign is counterfeited. The concern holds:
- the standard, "nothing enters the lattice the body has not countersigned" (atlas §III, quoted in `notes/nothing enters the lattice the body has not countersigned.md`), narrowed at the primary text to "the body countersigns *movement*… not *truth*" (`drafts/felt-shift — a surface to write from.md`);
- the counterfeit catalogue, Gendlin's four kinds of inward attention that are not focusing, and the Zen rule against self-certifying experience;
- the two sharpest attacks, Nisbett and Wilson, and Schwitzgebel, both read whole;
- the discrimination family, read at primary texts, whose finding is that "every criterion a reader alone can apply declines to evaluate, and every discrimination proper routes to another person";
- about 10,000 words of drafts defending the standard.

At least twenty of the concern's 79 notes are about certification or its counterfeit (F1). Six adversary readings are still unread (O3).

**Why the design cannot hold it.** No type, field, or relation asks how anyone would know something went right, or who can tell. Practice has "how it goes wrong"; the Commitments hold the ethical face ("Recognition belongs to the reader"); the ladder records that the author believes something and never by what sign. The design was built from a transcript in which the felt shift "is never author-ratified" (`the-two-books.md`; F2).

**Function served.** Function 2 (safety with enough friction), function 3 (a certificate is what stops the direction of attention from becoming the supply of a verdict), and function 8 (the contract: what the book will not do for its reader). Also Book II's authority, embodied realization. And Book I, whose reader is exactly the second person the discriminating guards route to.

**Expansion.**
- A **Certificate** field on Practice and on Passage (F1). Its parts:
  - the sign (at the moment, over a course, by what follows, by conduct, by another person);
  - who can run it (the person now, the person later, another who has been there, a clinician);
  - its counterfeit and who catches it;
  - whether a page can carry it.
- A **candidate commitment**, worded as the primary text narrows it: *the felt shift certifies that a process carried forward, not that a content is true* (Gendlin 1964, fn. 9). Its test: does this passage or practice produce a step that changes what can be said next? (O3, F2)
- A relation, **a Locus contests a Commitment**, so that the standard's adversaries have somewhere to attach, with the readings that answer each recorded as the commitment's defense (O3).
- Sounding B3 goes into the Remainder until the author answers it.

**Instance** (F1):

> - *Opens:* the pair sheer emotion / felt sense.
> - *Lineage:* Gendlin, read whole on 2026-08-28.

>   - the restraint is run by the person now, and the discrimination by the listener;
>   - the counterfeit is the fluent yes that "arrives fast", "cannot be re-contacted as a body-sense", and "has nothing new when revisited" (`notes/gendlin's focusing protocol already guards against the counterfeit shift.md`), caught by re-contact and by checking across days;
>   - a page can print the guards and not the discrimination.

## VI. What this asks of the next generation of the vault

Five moves follow from the three documents. Each changes the vault's structure, so each is the author's to decide, and each is sized so that it makes the system smaller in use rather than larger.

1. **Record the author's standing where the content lives, and mark whose words every quotation is.** This is one field and one mark. It stops the model's words from being presented as the author's, and it makes the profile's author columns computable. A session can back-fill *surfaced* everywhere, and *encountered* wherever a source is the author's own turn, statement, or reflection. Only he raises anything further.
2. **Render the profile as the reading by need.** Regenerate it from the fields, marking the difference between a session's crossing and the author's, so that it shows at a glance which answer from the author would unblock the most.
3. **Point the company and the readings at the two books.** Give each of the twelve thinkers who carry the book's thesis a Book I use and a Book II use. Read the facilitation lineage Book I needs, which the reading list set aside because it grounds no current note (third document, Part III).
4. **Put the author's layer first.** The questions only he can answer are already written: the soundings B1 to B7, the decision sitting's 22 items, the seven convergence notes that reserve his place, and the blank sentence at the end of each drafting document. The profile shows which of them unblocks Book I.
5. **Draw the exit.** Name the end condition in advance, as `ops/the vault's exit.md` proposes, so that "structure serves encounter" has an operation and not only a test.
