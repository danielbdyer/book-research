# source — the space around

Full title: "The Space Around — A field survey of what we haven't discovered yet." The document walks the book's argument in order and, at each idea, looks outward in three directions: crossovers (connections to material the project already holds), broadenings (new neighbors), and counterweights (opposition worth keeping close). It closes with a priority ranking of five findings and a summary judgment: the strongest finds were resistances, not allies.

The document differs in kind from its three siblings: they describe what the project holds; this one describes what it lacks. Two of its elements have been adopted as standing infrastructure in this vault: its admission rule (nothing gets listed unless it would actually change how the project thinks) is the admission rule in [[methods]], and its three-direction search is the standing method of [[research-frontier]].
