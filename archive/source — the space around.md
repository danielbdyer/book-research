# source — the space around

Full title: "The Space Around — A field survey of what we haven't discovered yet." The document walks the book's argument in order and, at each idea, looks outward in three directions: crossovers (connections to material the project already holds), broadenings (new neighbors), and counterweights (opposition worth keeping close). It closes with a priority ranking of five findings and a quoted summary judgment: "the strongest finds were not new allies but new resistances" (space-around, closing meta-observation).

The document differs in kind from its three siblings: they describe what the project holds; this one describes what it lacks. Two of its elements have been adopted as standing infrastructure in this vault: its admission rule — "nothing gets listed unless it would actually change how we think" (preamble) — is the admission standard in [[methods]], and its three-direction search is the standing method of [[research-frontier]].
