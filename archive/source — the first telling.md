# source — the first telling

Full title: "The First Telling — the unhurried version." The document presents the book to a first reader, in sections: the problem (self-knowledge that does not move anything), the reframe (folded rather than broken), the reclaimed word, the instrument (the somatic test, with a runnable version), the offering (the three layers: anatomy, alphabet, chemistry), the engine, the between, the practice, the construction (the document's section title is "how it's built"), the honesty (the book's known failure modes, disclosed), the withheld list (titled "left in the tin"), and the invitation.

The document has a standing that the other three lack, worth recording plainly: by the corpus's own central distinction, a telling addressed to a real reader is a step toward release. It is also the best source for the book's claims in their reader-facing form, and its "honesty" section is the source of record for the failure modes the book discloses about itself.
