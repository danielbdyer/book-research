# reading — the facilitated room's roots (lewin 1946, johnstone's impro)

**What was read.** Two of the several roots the facilitated room descends from, read at or near primary on 2026-08-30 as the cluster-B remainder (the centerpiece's independence): **Kurt Lewin**, "Action Research and Minority Problems" (*Journal of Social Issues* 2(4): 34–46, 1946) — the paper that carries the T-group seed and the action-research principle; and **Keith Johnstone**, *Impro: Improvisation and the Theatre* (1979) — the Status chapter, the Spontaneity chapter, and the teaching passages. No quotation is from memory; each is graded, and the paraphrases the vault had been carrying in quotation marks are flagged and corrected.

**How it is cited.** By author, work, and page. Grades: Lewin A (the 1946 paper read full, open access; page cites to the *Journal of Social Issues* pagination); Johnstone A (full text, archive.org/details/improimprovisati00john; page cites to the 1979/1981 Methuen edition). Both readings turned up wordings the vault had attributed loosely; the verified wordings replace the paraphrases in the notes and chairs.

**Why this reading.** Two threads met here. The participation-law convergence had one outside arrival still unread — Johnstone's improvisational theatre, the fifth of the five witnesses gathered from disciplines with no debt to the book's founding poles ([[transformation is enacted participation inside a held container]]) — and the facilitation-roots note carried an ungrounded claim that the facilitated room has several roots of equal or earlier standing than improv ([[the facilitated room has several independent roots and improv's distinction is theorizing play as craft]]). Lewin sits at both: he is the head of one of those roots (the T-group) and the untraced branch of est's genealogy ([[the apparatus descends from the settlement the book positions itself against]]), so reading him also settles whether he and Erhard are two independent arrivals at the participation law or one lineage.

## What the reading settled — Lewin

1. **He is a precedent, not an independent arrival at the participation law.** Lewin's claim in the 1946 paper is epistemological and methodological — action research is how a practitioner produces usable social knowledge by intervening, watching the effect, and revising — not the transformational claim that a person is changed by their own participation. "Research that produces nothing but books will not suffice" (p. 35) states the intervene-to-know stance; it does not say the intervener is themselves remade. This answers the first of the three questions the chair's seating left open and confirms the `precedent` load rather than promoting him to the independent arrivals.

2. **The T-group began in his workshop but was not finished by him.** The 1946 New Britain (Connecticut) workshop is where the discovery happened, in his own words: the participants who stayed to hear the trainers' evening review of the group's recorded dynamics benefited more from it than from the curriculum — the "tremendous pedagogical effect which these evaluation meetings ... had on the training process" (p. 41). But Lewin died in February 1947, and his associates — Leland Bradford, Ronald Lippitt, Kenneth Benne — formalized the training group and founded the National Training Laboratories afterward. "The group that studies itself was his invention" overstates it; he convened and documented the seed, and others grew the method into the T-group.

3. **The Erhard→Lewin genealogy is one shared channel, not two arrivals.** est descends to Lewin through a single line — the T-group to the encounter movement to Erhard Seminars Training — so Erhard (one of the six roll-call voices) and Lewin are not two independent arrivals at the participation law but one lineage with Lewin at its head. Counting Lewin as a separate outside witness would double-count that lineage, so the participation law's outside-witness set stays at five, not six. This is the half-traced genealogy the apparatus note already names (est "traced through 'Austin's performatives ⟿ Erhard' and never through Lewin's T-groups") now confirmed at the primary text on the untraced branch.

## What the reading settled — Johnstone

1. **The clause is "be obvious," not "be average."** "Be average" appears nowhere in *Impro*; Johnstone's word is *obvious* — "the more obvious he is, the more original he appears," and "An artist who is inspired is being obvious. He's not making any decisions, he's not weighing one idea against another. He's accepting his first thoughts" (pp. 87–88), with the imperatives "Don't try to be clever" (p. 33) and "Stop trying to be original" (p. 97). The internal-transmission dig had this right; the chair had drifted to "be average," and is corrected. *(Not verified: "a dull imagination gives flashes of genius" — not found in Impro.)*

2. **Status is a claim about the relational field, not a craft observation about performance.** Johnstone states it in general terms, with examples from the world rather than the stage: "In reality status transactions continue all the time" (p. 33); "every movement, every inflection of the voice implies a status" between friends (p. 37), figured as a see-saw ("I go up and you go down"); "once you understand that every sound and posture implies a status, then you perceive the world quite differently, and the change is probably permanent" (p. 72). His illustrations are a therapy group, ducks on a pond, and *Waiting for Godot* — the world, not the rehearsal room. The book's relational-field framing is well-grounded, and if anything the vault understated how far Johnstone pushes the claim. *(Not verified: "I should teach improvisation as status transactions" — a paraphrase; replaced by the p. 33 and p. 72 wordings.)*

3. **The teacher-takes-the-failure device ports to a solitary reader only as a structural analogue.** Johnstone's own version is embodied and live: "I play low status physically but my actual status is going up, since only a very confident and experienced person would put the blame for failure on himself" (p. 29), the students "almost certainly start sliding off their chairs," and verbal reassurance is not enough — the student "needs a teacher who is living proof ... that the imagination will not destroy you" (p. 84). What ports to a disembodied author is the *structure* — build the book so the reader's not-getting-it is the book's fault rather than the reader's — not Johnstone's actual mechanism, which needs a body performing low status in real time. This downgrades the transmission dig's "most portable" rating of the device. *(Not verified: "I try to make my students feel that no failure is theirs" and "a good teacher ... gets the blame when things go wrong" — both paraphrases; replaced by the p. 29 wording.)*

4. **The offer-grammar is confirmed as read, and stays a concept rather than a minted claim.** "Those who say 'Yes' are rewarded by the adventures they have" (p. 92); "Blocking is a form of aggression" (p. 94); "A block is anything that prevents the action from developing" (p. 97); good improvisers "seem telepathic; everything looks pre-arranged" because "they accept all offers made" (p. 100); and the scene is built by two imaginations combining additively (p. 27). This grounds the improv-as-craft distinction in the facilitation-roots note. It is held here rather than minted as its own note, because the offer material ports to the book only as a concept and no such note exists (claim-candidate only).

5. **The kept difference is confirmed and is the sharpest of the five outside arrivals.** Johnstone refuses solemnity — his method runs on lowering the stakes, and treating the work as important is itself a block ("Don't try to be clever," p. 33; what is said "not as important as the status played out," p. 49) — and he refuses the solitary form more sharply than any other witness: improvisation combines two imaginations (p. 27) and needs a partner and an audience, so nothing in his method survives a person reading alone. That refusal is the structural obstacle the book's solitary-reader form has to answer.

## What the reading did not settle

- **Moreno is still owed, and he is the sharpest point of the facilitation-roots note.** J. L. Moreno's psychodrama is, on the dig's reading, the closest direct ancestor of the facilitated room and the coiner of *spontaneity* and *warm-up* before Spolin used them; he was not read this round, so the facilitation-roots note is grounded to `privated` (two roots read, the closest ancestor owed) rather than `full`. A scoping read of *Who Shall Survive?* and *Psychodrama Vol. I* stays queued.
- **Lewin's *Field Theory in Social Science* (1951) is owed** for the person-and-environment function (the word *field*, and whether the unfreeze–change–refreeze model bears on the book's plasticity-window material or is a later simplification of him). The 1946 paper does not carry it.
- **The T-group's own documented harms** — whether they belong with the adverse-effects reading already queued — remain open; the 1946 paper predates the encounter-casualty literature.
- **The dictum stays a paraphrase.** "If you want truly to understand something, try to change it" was not found verbatim in the 1946 paper; it is the compressed form the literature attributes to Lewin, and the chair keeps it marked as attributed rather than located. "You learn what a system is by changing it" and "no action without research" are likewise the vault's paraphrases, not Lewin's wording.
- **Johnstone's *Impro for Storytellers* (1999)** was not opened; it would refine the narrative-structure material, not the status or offer findings.

## Seatings

The seating of every chair remains the author's. This reading moves Johnstone from a grid proposal to primary-read and leaves his `candidate` seat where it stood — the theatre is the author's own background, which makes the chair a homecoming rather than an import, but the decision is his ([[keith johnstone]]). Lewin was already seated (reference-lineage, precedent load, 2026-08-16); the reading confirms the load and corrects the "invention" overstatement, and does not change the seat.
