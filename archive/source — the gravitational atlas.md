# source — the gravitational atlas

Full title: "The Whole Field — A Gravitational Atlas of Firing on All Cylinders / Aletheia." The document compiles the project's foundational and reference material into a single nested network: twelve sections (§I–§XII) running from the central process (the reclaimed word, with aletheia and pratyabhijñā) through the container anatomy, the twelve primitives, the molecule chemistry, the engine, the speech ladder, the practices, the relational field, the full company of thinkers, the book-as-artifact, the kept tensions, and a closing summary that resolves the field into six major masses. Its colophon lists the materials it was compiled from: seventeen trial chapters, a sister ontology project, a second-edition treatment, several working threads, and a frank structural assessment.

The document carries its own notation (◉ major mass, ⚡ kept tension, ≈ isomorph, ⊖ privation, ⟿ feeds-into, § cross-link), which this vault records in substantiation layers and in `notes/figure glossary.md`. The colophon also carries the document's own accuracy warning: "the map will be wrong wherever the territory has since ripened" (atlas, colophon).

## Extraction state

A starter pass (2026-08-01) extracted the six masses' central claims, the kept tensions, and the structural material into roughly forty notes; every claim from that pass anchors to this document by section symbol. Large regions remain unextracted — the individual primitives (§III), the molecule catalogue (§IV), the engine detail and movement grid (§V), the practice curriculum (§VII), and most of the company roster (§IX). The unextracted regions are listed as pending work in `ops/queue/extractions.md`.
