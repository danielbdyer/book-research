# reading — bohm, on dialogue (1996)

**What was read.** David Bohm, the core "On Dialogue" essay, read whole at a posted copy of the text (retrieved 2026-08-24; the Schouten & Nelissen edition of the essay, the same text collected in *On Dialogue*, London: Routledge, 1996) — the essay on dialogue's nature, its contrast with discussion and negotiation, and the discipline of suspending assumptions. The wider *On Dialogue* collection and the "Dialogue — a proposal" statement (Bohm, Factor and Garrett, 1991) were not read whole. This is cluster A's Bohm slot — the read-on-demand figure named in `ops/reading queue.md` (tier entry 94, "pool of meaning") — and it grounds one harvest note.

**How it is cited.** `Bohm, On Dialogue` by the essay's argument; the essay carries no stable section numbers and the posted copy's pagination is edition-specific, so no page is cited. Quotations are Bohm's own words and are marked.

**Why this reading.** The harvest note `[[harmony is the joining of differences rather than a collapse into agreement]]` pairs Confucius's *hé ér bù tóng* with Bohm's dialogue, both from memory, and flags that "the *hé ér bù tóng* and 'pool of meaning' phrasings are queued so they do not blur into a generic 'listening well.'" The failable question: does Bohm's dialogue actually hold that a shared meaning forms *without* collapsing the differences into agreement — a chord and not a unison — in his own words, or is the note importing a stronger claim than he makes?

## What the reading settled

1. **The shared meaning is a creation *between*, not a merger — confirmed at the text.** Dialogue is *dia-logos*: "*dia* means 'through' — it doesn't mean two," so the image "is of a stream of meaning flowing among and through us and between us. This will make possible a flow of meaning in the whole group, out of which will emerge some new understanding … something creative" (Bohm, On Dialogue). The shared meaning is emergent and owned by no one, which is the note's "field of meaning" both people share — and it is the same ontology the vault's between-notes carry, reached from a physicist rather than a philosopher.

2. **It forms without agreement — suspension, not surrender — confirmed.** The discipline is "to suspend those assumptions, so that you neither carry them out nor suppress them. You don't believe them, nor do you disbelieve them; you don't judge them as good or bad. You simply see what they mean — not only your own, but the other people's as well. We are not trying to change anybody's opinion" (Bohm). So the differences are held in view rather than dissolved, and the point is "for people to realise what is on each other's minds without coming to any conclusions or judgements." This is the note's exact claim: harmony is the concord of things that stay different, a chord and not a unison.

3. **The refusal is Bohm's own, and sharp.** Dialogue is not discussion — discussion "is almost like a Ping-Pong game … the object of the game is to win or to get points for yourself"; it is not negotiation — "trading off, adjusting to each other"; and it is not consensus — "We are not trying to change anybody's opinion." Its spirit is "we are not playing a game against each other but with each other." So the note's kept refusal ("Bohm insists dialogue is not negotiation or consensus") is grounded, and it guards the finding from softening into "getting on the same page."

**Verdict.** The Bohm pole of `[[harmony is the joining of differences rather than a collapse into agreement]]` is grounded at the text; the note moves nascent → privated, its Confucius and Merleau-Ponty poles still owed. Bohm is a read-on-demand figure (entry 94), not a seated interlocutor, and this reading grounds the one note without implying a seating — the seating stays the author's.

## What the reading did not settle

- **The companion poles of the harmony note.** Confucius's *hé ér bù tóng* (Analects 13.23) and Merleau-Ponty's dialogue-as-"single fabric" are still from memory; the note rests on one grounded witness.
- **The wider *On Dialogue*.** Only the core essay was read; the "pool of common meaning" and the participation/proprioception-of-thought material of the fuller collection were not read whole, though the essay's "stream of meaning … between us" carries the note's claim.
- **The rest of cluster A's facilitation lineage.** A footprint check this session found that Grove (Clean Language), Heron & Reason (co-operative inquiry), and Freire & hooks ground *no* current nascent note — the "facilitation craft" the cluster-A gloss named them for is carried, in the actual harvest notes, by the recognition and maieutic thinkers (Heidegger's leaping-ahead, Laozi's *wu-wei*, Rogers, Montessori, Plato) and by the seated Minuchin, not by these four. So cluster A's grounding is essentially complete after Bohm; its remaining owed readings live in clusters B (the centerpiece's independence) and C (the affirmative-face / maieutic group), not in a further facilitation-lineage pass.
