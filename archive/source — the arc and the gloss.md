# source — the arc and the gloss

Full title: "The Arc and the Gloss — Second Edition. Firing on All Cylinders — the full storyboard treatment, five rooms integrated." The document is a chapter-by-chapter treatment of a fifteen-chapter arc in five parts, with front matter, a prologue, two interlude positions, an epilogue, and back matter. Each chapter is set out under a fixed set of headings — job, register, constellation, jewel, threshold — and several carry an undercurrent addressed to the author rather than to a reader. It states its own genre in its opening line: "not a draft, not a plan — a whiteboard from altitude" (arc-gloss: how to hold this). Its second edition adds what it calls five rooms — the facilitation craft, Karla McLaren's emotion work, the Heidegger–Erhard–Landmark speech lineage, the twenty-nine container-makers, and the coaches — and it signs off "storyboarded in the middle voice, from the ridgeline."

## Standing

This document has draft standing under `ops/methodology/source standing.md`: it is a plan for the manuscript, not a description of the project from outside it.

Its provenance is the reason the standing matters. The atlas's colophon lists the threads it was assembled from and names this document first: "assembled July 2026 from the living threads — the Arc & Gloss (2nd ed.), the seventeen trial chapters, The Third Drawer, the coaches' constellation, the valley teachings, the twenty-nine-plus-axis, the porch sittings, and the frank assessment" (atlas colophon). The walked document's colophon repeats the list in the same order. This document is therefore upstream of two of the four founding sources, and agreement among the three is not independent corroboration. The claim recording the consequence is [[the atlas was compiled from the arc and gloss treatment]].

One arithmetic point is left unresolved rather than settled here. This treatment's numbered units total seventeen — a prologue, fifteen chapters, and an epilogue — and the corpus repeatedly cites "the seventeen trial chapters." The colophons nonetheless list the two as separate threads, so the vault does not treat them as the same artifact.
