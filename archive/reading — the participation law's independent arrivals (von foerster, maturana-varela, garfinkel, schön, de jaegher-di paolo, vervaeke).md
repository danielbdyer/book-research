# reading — the participation law's independent arrivals (von foerster, maturana-varela, garfinkel, schön, de jaegher-di paolo, vervaeke)

**What was read.** The outside-the-lineage witnesses to the book's participation law — the rule that order, knowing, and meaning are enacted by participants rather than delivered to a spectator. Read at or near primary, 2026-08-30, as grounding-backlog cluster B (the centerpiece's independence): **Heinz von Foerster**, "On Constructing a Reality" (1973) and "Ethics and Second-Order Cybernetics" (1991/92); **Maturana & Varela**, *The Tree of Knowledge* (1987); **Harold Garfinkel**, *Studies in Ethnomethodology* (1967); **Donald Schön**, *The Reflective Practitioner* (1983); **De Jaegher & Di Paolo**, "Participatory Sense-Making" (*Phenomenology and the Cognitive Sciences* 6:485–507, 2007) and "Making Sense in Participation" (*Enacting Intersubjectivity*, 2008); and, partially, **John Vervaeke**, the four kinds of knowing (Vervaeke & Ferraro 2013; *Awakening from the Meaning Crisis*, 2019) and relevance realization (Vervaeke, Lillicrap & Richards 2012). No quotation is from memory; each is graded.

**How it is cited.** By author, work, and page/section. Grades: Garfinkel A (full open scan, Monoskop); Maturana & Varela A (full OCR, archive.org); De Jaegher & Di Paolo A (both the 2007 article and the 2008 chapter read full from the authors' hosted copies); von Foerster A on the two essays read, B on the *Observing Systems* aphorism (via Glasersfeld 1996); Schön B (the 1983 book lending-gated; verbatim quotes with page cites from an Open University reading-extract and peer-reviewed sources); Vervaeke A on the 2012 relevance-realization paper, B on the four-kinds taxonomy (the 2013 chapter paywalled; verified via his lectures and reputable secondaries).

**Why this reading.** The vault had recorded two objections against its own centerpiece: the field around the founding convergence mostly fails the independence test because it is downstream of Heidegger ([[the convergence's independence fails where three of its names share one teacher]]), and the participation-law lineages were gathered on one day for the property they are then reported to share ([[the unanimity claim is an artifact of how the lineages were selected]]). The remedy both objections name is arrivals from outside the lineage — empirical science, systems and design, social theory, analytic philosophy — witnesses with no debt to either pole. This reading supplies them at their primary texts.

## What the reading settled

1. **The participation law has verified independent arrivals from outside the lineage.** Each figure reaches the rule within its own domain, from a route with no contact with the book's founding poles: von Foerster from circuit design ("the environment as we perceive it is our invention"; "Reality = Community"), Maturana and Varela from cellular autopoiesis ("the living being is a source of perturbations and not of instructions," *Tree of Knowledge* p. 96), Garfinkel from sociology ("the activities whereby members produce and manage settings of organized everyday affairs are identical with members' procedures for making those settings 'account-able'," p. 1), Schön from professional pedagogy ("an epistemology of practice," p. 49), De Jaegher and Di Paolo from enactive dynamical systems ("new domains of social sense-making can be generated that were not available to each individual on her own," 2007 p. 497). So the participation law is no longer only a corpus roll-call; the family-resemblance and selection-artifact objections are answered for these names.

2. **Each keeps a real refusal at the same threshold — the interior — which is the book's step, not theirs.** Garfinkel refuses the transposition to inner experience (his subject is "observable-and-reportable," and he rejects the "psychological dope," p. 68); Schön's "situation that talks back" is an external material or client (pp. 131–132), and he refuses the dissolution of expertise (p. 61); von Foerster keeps the claim epistemological, framing participation as an "in principle undecidable" choice rather than a metaphysics; Maturana and Varela's felt shift would certify nothing trans-observer; De Jaegher and Di Paolo refuse the interaction as an *entity* (it is a "typically fleeting" operational process, guarded against "self-sufficiency of the social domain," p. 504) and are the most precise of the arrivals that the solitary reader breaks the mechanism ("a text does not adjust"). These refusals are the "arrived, with a kept difference" profile the convergence wants, primary-sourced rather than rhetorical.

3. **Maturana and Varela carry perturb-not-instruct to communication** ("Biologically, there is no 'transmitted information' in communication... saying does not ensure listening," ~p. 196), which answers the reading-queue's standing question: the book's "perturb, not install" applied to a teacher or a text is their mechanism fairly extended, not an appropriation. **De Jaegher and Di Paolo's *Linguistic Bodies* (2018)** re-models solitary reading as a "virtual dialogue" in which the absent producer is animated as an agent — extending the participation law *into* reading rather than exempting reading from it, consonant with "whatever transforms, the reader did it."

4. **Attribution corrections forced by the reading**, now applied to the notes and chairs:
   - von Foerster's `clause:` had appended "anything said is said by an observer to another observer," which mis-credits Maturana's 1970 dictum ("Everything said is said by an observer") to von Foerster; his own contribution is the corollary "and said *to* an observer."
   - Garfinkel's `clause:` was a paraphrase in quotation marks with an added gloss ("the doing and the describing are one activity") absent from the text; corrected to his p. 1 wording. The biographical claim that he stopped the breaching experiments because of the distress is unverified (the primary confirms distress and investigative intent, not the reason for stopping); softened.
   - Schön's `clause:` fused two passages (pp. 8–9, p. 42) with an added "where technical rationality works"; corrected to the pp. 8–9 verbatim. The "high ground overlooking a swamp" wording is *Educating the Reflective Practitioner* (1987, p. 3), not the 1983 text (p. 42) — a live citation trap.
   - De Jaegher and Di Paolo's construct is a "level of *analysis*," not "level of organization" (2007 p. 492); the "third thing … the couple's" summary and the ensemble/tempo illustration were the standing-wave paper's glosses, not the authors', who never use "third thing." The infants double-video citation had conflated the 2008 chapter title with the 2007 article year; both works carry it (Murray & Trevarthen 1985). "Synchrony without positive emotion" is Wiltermuth & Heath 2009, not the authors.

5. **Vervaeke is company, not an outside arrival.** His four kinds of knowing are a real, precisely-defined taxonomy and participatory knowing is a genuine agent-arena co-constitution, but he roots participatory knowing in Heidegger (*Awakening* Ep 32), so he is not free of debt to the book's founding pole; and participatory knowing is a sub-personal identity-level layer, not the bodily register of the felt shift, so the two only rhyme.

## What the reading did not settle

- **Johnstone's improvisational theatre** (the fifth of the participation law's outside arrivals) was not read this round and remains a grid proposal.
- **von Foerster's *Observing Systems*** itself was not opened for a page cite (the aphorism is B, via Glasersfeld); **Schön's 1983 book** was read at B (lending-gated), a borrow would upgrade to A; **the Vervaeke 2013 chapter** and the *Awakening* series at first hand are owed, and would settle which of his three load functions dominates (Fork 21).
- **The idealism flank** on Maturana and Varela (autopoiesis-as-cognition courting solipsism; the "objectivity in parentheses" reply, Maturana 1988) is kept marked contested, not resolved.
- **Murray & Trevarthen 1985** primary report was verified only as reported in the two De Jaegher & Di Paolo works (Grade A at those); the primary itself is a refinement owed for direct quotation.
- The seating of every chair remains the author's; this reading moves them to primary-read and leaves the seat value where it stood.
