# The vault inside the system: a collation

*This document places the material the research vault already holds inside the system designed in `reframe-map-and-system-2026-09-25.md`, the map of the reframe conversation that designed, from the transcript alone, a system stating what content the two books need (its Part VII, with the axiom conditions of its Part X). It is the first of three documents written on 2026-09-25 from one study. The second, `system-and-vault-beyond-the-design-2026-09-25.md`, records what the vault holds beyond the design and how the design grows to receive it. The third, `system-and-vault-left-to-populate-2026-09-25.md`, records the places the design defines that the vault does not yet fill.*

*The study ran eight readers in parallel, four on each of two model families, each reading one region of the vault against the design. Their reports, the brief they worked from, and the region each covered are kept in `ops/system readings — 2026-09-25/`, and this document cites them by label (O1 to O4, F1 to F4; the key is in Part X). Where two readers covered the same ground their readings were compared, and every disputed point was checked against the files before it was written here. The author asked for the collation "at a high level", so it counts and places bodies of material rather than listing notes, and it points to the reports for note-level evidence. Everything here stands at the lowest standing the design defines, surfaced material, which only the author raises.*

## I. The finding in brief

1. **The vault already holds nearly every part of the design, under its own names and usually in more detail than the design specifies.** The design's Locus is the vault's 64 thinker pages and 47 records of reading a thinker's own work. Its Crossing is the vault's 64 convergence notes. Its Book is the two-book architecture the author decided on 2026-08-16. Its Figure is the 16 image notes. Its Remainder is spread across four registers, each with its own admission rule. Its Commitments are an axiom register, a family file, and thirty standing directives. The vault's provenance is stronger than the design's.

2. **The vault holds most of this at a session's standing, and the author's own layer is thin everywhere.** Seven of the eight readers found this independently, each in a different region. None of the 64 convergence notes carries the author's own sentence about what survives the comparison, and seven of them reserve a place for it with a question addressed to him. The vault has one record of an encounter and one reflection in the author's own words. His acts are recorded, but in the decisions register (the table of settled questions, 119 rows), in source citations, and in dated statements quoted inside notes, rather than on the content they raise.

3. **The vault has no field for how far the author has taken a piece of content, and without one the model's words end up presented as the author's.** The vault's `state:` field records how far a note has been substantiated by reading. By the author's decision of 2026-08-05 it records "nothing the book asserts" (`ops/methodology/vocabulary policy.md`). The readers found at least seven places where a sentence written by the model or by an AI collaborator is presented as the author's: in a drafting document, a topic map, three notes, a survey, and nine files that quote an AI collaborator's sentence about the 4am letter without marking whose it is. The design made the same error once, in Part X's statement of the aim, and that sentence is now corrected in the map.

4. **The vault divides the material differently from the design in four places, and each difference has a recorded reason.**
   - The vault organizes its doctrine by six concerns that the author's poems already carry, where the design proposed fifteen from the transcript.
   - It keeps a standpoint, a thinker, and a reading record apart, where the design fused them into one Locus.
   - It keeps four kinds of open item apart, where the design kept one Remainder.
   - It holds its commitments in the three layers the author named, where the design kept one list.

5. **The fit is closest for the company of thinkers and the books' architecture, and weakest for practice, craft, passages, and encounters.**
   - Practices: nine notes, all in the felt-shift concern.
   - Craft moves: none, because the author retired the category on 2026-08-09.
   - Passages in the author's own hand: none. The one letter he sent is not held in the vault.
   - Encounters written as records: one.

## II. The terms

The design's eleven types, each in one line (all names are candidates):

| Type | What it holds |
|---|---|
| Concern | a live question the books serve, with its form in each book and what treating it as a concern hides |
| Pair | a distinction or a polarity: the books' native unit of conceptual content |
| Practice | a small, repeatable way of participating that lets a Pair or Concern be tasted |
| Figure | an image or story that lets the reader hold a Pair or Concern |
| Encounter | a dated, lived event, with the author's note on it |
| Locus | a cultivated way of seeing: a tradition, a thinker, or a practice lineage |
| Crossing | one Concern seen from two or more Loci, with what survives the difference recorded |
| Durable perspective | what the author has come to see that survived Crossings, in his own words |
| Craft move | a device by which a page directs attention and leaves meaning to the reader |
| Passage | drafted language, the smallest unit of the manuscript |
| Book | a projection of the shared material through one locus of practice: Book I with another, Book II with oneself |

The four fields every type shares: **Standing** (how far the author has taken a piece of content: surfaced, encountered, inferred, believed, claimed, written; only the author raises anything above surfaced), **Scale** (self, relation, or ecology), **Lens** (Wilber's four quadrants, used as coordinates), and **Provenance** (every quotation with an address). The two registers: **Commitments** (the axioms the books must keep, each with the function it protects and a test) and the **Remainder** (the open questions and unresolved forks, kept visible). The **profile grid** is one row per Concern, with the columns Question, Book I, Book II, Pair, Practice, Encounter, Company, Crossing, Durable perspective, and Exit.

The vault's own words that this document uses, each with its plain referent:

| Vault word | What it refers to |
|---|---|
| thinker's page (the vault's word is *chair*) | a note in `notes/` with `category: interlocutor`, recording what a thinker does for the book and where they part from it |
| convergence note (the vault's word is *isomorph*) | a note recording that two or more traditions reached the same structure |
| `state:` | how far a note has been substantiated: *nascent* (stated but not yet substantiated by a reading), *privated* (complete except in one named way, usually an unread primary work), *full*, *flourishing*, *composting* (superseded but still drawn on) |
| topic map | a page gathering the claims of one concern |
| family file | one of the files at the repository root, such as `the-concerns.md`, each of which reads the whole graph through one organizing idea; the vault also calls them lens families |
| drafting document | a draft document in `drafts/` that assembles one of the book's concerns for a drafting session and then stops where the author's own move begins; the vault calls these concern surfaces |
| sounding | a question only the author can answer, about his own experience or his intention for the book |
| tension | a disagreement inside the book's doctrine, kept open in a note rather than resolved, with a proposed way of handling it |
| `load:` | what a thinker carries for the book's argument: its thesis, an adversary, an independent arrival, company, a named practice, or a precedent |
| company | thinkers who reached the same finding independently, held as company rather than as proof |

## IV. Type by type

Each type below has four parts: what the vault holds, how cleanly it fits, what the vault has that the design lacks, and what the design has that the vault lacks.

### Concern

**What the vault holds.** Six doctrine topic maps (`recognition`, `contraction-not-corruption`, `felt-shift`, `container-anatomy`, `relational-field`, `the call and the answering`), each gathering its claims by theme with a gloss written for the map. `the-concerns.md` reads the six as "six faces of one", each with a finding, what it reveals and leaves open, grouped evidence, a list of what is still ungrounded, an open edge, and where it enters the book. The four drafting documents in `drafts/` each render one of them for the author. The learning arc places recognition as the first station of both books.

**Fit: with translation.**
- The vault states each concern as a finding, where the design states it as a question. Its headings read "recognition — deep change is un-forgetting, not addition" (`the-concerns.md`). The question form appears in the drafting documents and in a handful of nascent notes, one of which reproduces the design's own question (O1).
- The vault divides the concerns differently. Its six are the corpus's, and a full note records that the author's first fifteen poems already carry all six (`notes/all six doctrine concerns appear in the first fifteen poems.md`). The design's fifteen came from the transcript. They share only the name *recognition*.
- Three of the vault's six have no Concern in the design: suffering (contraction, not corruption), verification (the felt shift), and address (the call and the answering). The container matches the design's Form only in part.
- The relational-field map alone spans at least six of the design's Concerns: Relationship, Sovereignty, Power, Transformation, Participation, and Technology (O2).

**What the vault has.** A doctrine statement per concern, what it reveals and what it leaves open, a grounding status per piece of evidence, and a check that expires a section when the notes beneath it change.

**What the design has.** The concern stated as a question, its Book I form and its Book II form as separate cells, a stake, a quadrant, and an Exit named as a field. About two dozen notes in the whole vault mention Book I (a few of them meaning another book, such as Aristotle's *Physics* Book I), and 18 mention Book II. The concerns family's "Enters" lines still address the corpus's one-book arc, as in "running beneath the whole arc from preparation to integration" (`the-concerns.md`, recognition section; O1).

### Pair

**What the vault holds.** Pairs are everywhere, and they are stated as claim titles. A rough pattern count finds 138 pair-shaped titles vault-wide (F2). O1 counts 43 binary distinctions, 13 polarities, and 9 distinctions of more than two terms in the recognition and contraction region; O2 counts about 61 in the relational region. All five of the design's recognition pairs have homes. The vault holds many pairs the transcript never had, such as concealment against contraction, fold against fracture, state against station, and remembering against possession. `the-paradoxes.md` holds one polarity, influence without domination, as six roles must each hold it, with what collapses on each side.

**Fit: native in content, partial in structure.** No note records a pair's kind, the confusion it corrects, the collapse on each side, or the design's "way in", meaning a practice, figure, or encounter that lets the reader feel the pair before it is named. The Paradoxes family's "collapse costs" is the one native instance of the polarity fields.

**What the vault has.** Distinctions of three or four terms, such as the four ways the book's privative grammar works (O1). The role that holds a polarity (the Paradoxes family). A counterfeit on each pole: the false peak on expansion and, on contraction, the racket, the book's figure for a fixed complaint paired with a fixed payoff (`notes/the pulse has a counterfeit at each phase, a false peak of expansion and a racket of forgotten contraction.md`).

**What the design has.** The Pair's fields. And one caution runs the other way: the author settled the vault's unit on 2026-08-13 as "Both, allocated", the proposition-sentence for the notes and the named concept for any future concept layer (`ops/decisions.md`). A Pair should therefore be read out of the propositions, not substituted for them (F4).

### Practice

**What the vault holds.** Nine practice notes, and all nine declare the felt-shift concern (F2). They hold the basic posture and its six facets, the twelve practice forms, channeling, Focusing, the four-read diagnostic, the crisis triage, the rule that the book asks for attention before it asks for action, and one superseded completion signal. Practice content also sits inside claim and image notes, such as "Bowing to the Gatekeeper… The part, met with respect, will usually relax" (abridged ch14, quoted in the image note on the gatekeeper; O1).

**Fit: partial, field by field** (F1):
- *What it opens:* native for every practice.
- *Lineage:* native and page-addressed, for example Focusing to Gendlin, read whole.
- *Smallest living form:* native in the corpus's own words, for example the posture's "thirty seconds".
- *How it goes wrong:* the richest field, including Gendlin's four kinds of inward attention that are not focusing and a documented catalogue of counterfeits.
- *The invitation:* held as the corpus's summary for nine of the twelve forms rather than as a worded instruction.
- *Scale:* implicit.
- *The author's encounters with it:* absent. No dated instance records the author running any of these practices.

**What the vault has.** A condition for knowing whether a practice worked, queued as a pass over every practice (`ops/queue/extractions.md`, line 41). And a survey that sorts about seventy practice-models into eleven structural families and asks of each whether it opens a person *with* someone, opens a person *by* an intervention, opens a person *into* a place, or only guards (`ops/practice-model ecology.md`, §3).

**What the design has.** Practices for the concerns other than the felt shift. The vault has no practice note for suffering, the relational field, power, technology, or address. Several practices the transcript names have no note: noticing moments of contraction and expansion (transcript line 1037), "Can I relate before I intervene?" (16298), the repair sequence (16474), "What am I delegating?" (16567), and the inner facilitator's questions (13620–13632).

### Figure

**What the vault holds.** Sixteen image notes: eleven from the corpus's image system (the house, the orchard, the gold thread, the ink that listens, the fruit, the gatekeeper, the tuning fork, the circle, the messenger, the racket, the cusp), four from the reframe conversation (the catch, the score, patriotism for oneself, the hollow interaction), and the figure glossary. Figures from the traditions are quoted inside reading records, such as Utpaladeva's maiden and her unrecognized beloved, Plato's barren midwife, Polanyi's pianist, and Wittgenstein's ladder (O3).

**Fit: native.** Nine of the sixteen descriptions use the design's own verb, saying that a figure "carries" its concern (O4). The vault's unit is richer: an image, the concern it carries, and the Kashmir Śaiva term beneath it (`ops/scaffold.md`, §12).

**What the vault has.** The origin of each figure recorded in layers: the author's seed of April 2025, the compiled month of July 2026, the treatment's second edition, the reframe conversation. The lived origin of three figures: the gatekeeper, the gold thread, and patriotism for oneself.

**What the design has.** The risk field, what a figure over-determines, which no image note records as such (O1, O4). The design lists fourteen figures from the transcript. Four of them are image notes, and the recognition fable the author mentioned at line 156 is absent under that name. Reader O3 found that the verse naming the recognition school, Utpaladeva's *Īśvarapratyabhijñākārikā* IV.1.17, tells that story; the identification is an inference, and only the author can confirm it.

Marks in the provenance reconstructions showing whose words each sentence is: "[D] your words, [C] the AI’s, [A] hers as you transcribed" (`provenance/provenance fourth addendum.md`, line 11).

In the vault the interpretive note on an event is often a reconstruction or an AI collaborator's sentence, and the marks that say so stop at the provenance files. No note records a room the author facilitated (O2).

### Crossing

- 21 still hold one side "from memory, grounding owed";
- 48 record where a thinker parts from the finding;
- 38 test independence ("no contact", "independent");
- 39, written on or after 2026-08-21, state what the reader gains and what the convergence is for in the writing.

The Constellation family gathers them into a founding convergence and nine groups. Reading records name the crossing they leave to the author, and two carry a section titled "The crossing left to the author" (O3).

**Fit: native up to the author's note.** A convergence note carries the Concern (every one declares a topic map), the Loci, the correspondence with both sides quoted, the difference, and what resists synthesis. None carries the author's statement of what survives. Seven reserve the place with a question addressed to him, such as whether the bow to the gatekeeper was "a lived, wordless posture toward that part before he had any of these vocabularies for it" (`notes/the gatekeeper met with a bow is the threshold-guardian honored that rites of passage and internal family systems each named.md`; F3). His rulings on convergences exist as dated decision rows: 2026-08-06, on counting five arrivals as independent; 2026-08-13, recording the book's productive reading of contraction as its own departure from the tradition; and 2026-08-16, naming Kashmir Śaivism the book's deepest well ("It surprised me too").

**What the vault has.** An independence apparatus the design has no fields for:
- the dating test ("presented on 1 July 2026, three months after");
- the shared-teacher test ("three of the four names around the founding convergence are in conversation with the same German");
- the test for influence in both directions (Reinhard May's case, "run and passed on 2026-08-30");
- the rule that three documents compiled from one thread count once;
- the check that an agreement was not produced by gathering the traditions for the property they share.

All five are in `notes/aletheia and pratyabhijna converge on truth as un-forgetting.md` and its neighbors (F3).

**What the design has.** The author's note as the field that makes a Crossing his. The design predicted, from the transcript, that the Crossing column would be nearly empty. The vault shows it prepared everywhere and judged almost nowhere, which is a better position and needs its own mark in the profile.

### Durable perspective

Fifteen stations of the conversation in the author's words, gathered in the root file `reframe-deep-harvest-2026-08-20.md`. And the four drafting documents, each of which assembles the company and then leaves the author's statement blank: "So the durable perspective is not for this surface to state" (`drafts/the call and the answering — a surface to write from.md`).

**Fit: with translation.** The vault keeps the author's words verbatim and outside the claim graph, and it rules that a reflection "grounds no doctrinal inference" (`reflections/README.md`). It has no object whose subject is what the author has come to see, with the Crossings it survived.

**What the vault has.** One complete instance of the step the design describes. The recognition drafting document assembled the company and left its closing sentence blank. The author answered with his reflection ("occasioned-by: the recognition surface's mirror", in its frontmatter), and at his direction the book's founding position moved from concealment to contraction (`ops/completed.md`, line 40; O1).

**What the design has.** The Durable perspective as a type. The vault needs it as a place to receive what the drafting documents ask for.

### Craft move

**What the vault holds.** No notes of `category: craft`. The vault built the category on 2026-08-01, governed by the rule "Record the mechanism, never the verdict" (`ops/methodology/craft evidence.md`). On 2026-08-08 it held eighteen notes of exactly the design's kind, still recoverable from commit `1efe050` (O4). The author retired them on 2026-08-09: "Cut the craft-about-drafts notes too" — a research corpus studies the ideas rather than the manuscript's sentence technique (`ops/decisions.md`, row on prose craft). The seven devices the treatment designed are recorded, and only one was executed (`notes/the drafts execute one of the seven designed apparatus devices.md`). **Fit: partial, and in tension with a recorded decision.** The design calls Craft move its largest gap. The vault shows that the gap was built and then cut by the author. The cut came one week before Book I became the lead book, and the design's argument that Book I's reader learns from the book's own moves is new since then.

### Passage

## V. The four shared fields

### Standing

**The vault has no standing field, and it deliberately keeps apart three things the design's ladder would fold together** (F4):

1. **The standing of a document.** Is it descriptive, a draft state of the book, primary, a consulted work, a reading record, or an encounter record? This is a ladder of documents, not of content (`ops/methodology/source standing.md`).
2. **The substantiation of a note** (`state:`). The template defines "nascent" as "the proposition stated, not yet substantiated, waiting on writing", and "privated" as "a complete claim incomplete in a specific named way … it REQUIRES a privation field naming the absence, which is what stops the value being usable as a bare rank" (`templates/claim-note.md`). Readings move a note up this scale. Across the vault: 157 nascent, 54 privated, 131 full, 1 composting, 0 flourishing, 173 with no state.
3. **The author's standing on the content**, recorded act by act and never as a field. It lives in eight places (F4):
   - a thinker's role moving from candidate only by his decision (30 of 64 thinker pages still read `seat: candidate`);
   - a tension's status moving to resolved only by his act (1 of 13);
   - placements (none);
   - 119 decision rows;
   - "author statement, <date>" citations (35 notes; 43 carry an author class among their sources);
   - the three confidence tiers of 2026-08-04 (`ops/methodology/the book's current shape.md`);
   - the reflection's standing, "primary — spoken from the heart";
   - the split provenance of the encounter record.

**What follows.** The design's ladder is right that only the author raises standing. It would be wrong to read the vault's scale as the ladder, taking nascent as surfaced and full as believed. That would let a reading raise the author's standing, and it would contradict the decision that `state:` records "nothing the book asserts" (F4, F2, O1, O3). The correct reading: every note stands at *surfaced* until an author act touches it, and `state:` runs beside the ladder as a separate measure of how well read the note is. Two figures show the separation. The author's own words at transcript line 446 are held as a nascent note (`notes/allowing one's own sufficiency is a recursive condition for entering this reality.md`), while a session's Grade A reading of Laozi stands at full though the author has never met it (O1, O2).

**What the missing field costs.** Without a place to record whose words a sentence is, the vault has presented the model's words as the author's at least seven times (details and paths in the third document, Part IX):
- a drafting document credits two of the model's lines (815 and 6552) to "your own words";
- a topic map calls the model's five conditions "the author's own catalogue";
- an AI collaborator's sentence becomes "the author's side" of an independent arrival;
- one note calls the model's line 6289 "the author's own";
- a survey calls the model's line 15227 "the author's own craft";
- the fifteen facilitation-craft notes carry the model's outline with no mark of whose words they are;
- nine files quote an AI collaborator's sentence about the 4am letter, "The recognition didn’t require the mirror", without marking it, and two of them call it primary material.

The design made the same error once. It stated the aim with the model's sentence at line 3704 as if it were the letter's own. It also uses the ladder's word "inferred" for the author's act, where the vault writes "Standing: inferred" on session derivations (O1, O2, F2).

### Scale

No note records scale as a field. The three levels the design names appear once, at the level of the two books ("the two books explicitly occupy micro ↔ meso while the macro remains the horizon", `ops/methodology/the two-book architecture.md`), and as a reading in `the-lenses.md`, with seven levels of facilitation from facilitator and participant up to ecology and civilization. The vault more often uses "scale" in a second sense: the size of the unit a law applies at. Thirty-seven of the relational region's notes place a law "at the scale of a page", "scaled to one reader", or "at the scale of a room" (O2).

### Lens

No note records a quadrant. The vault holds Wilber's quadrants as "a coordinate system, not an architecture" (the note of that title, full) and registers them as one lens among others rather than as a new way of seating the thinkers: they "are registered lenses (research-frontier material), not re-seatings" (`ops/decisions.md`, 2026-08-13). The vault's reading of five of Wilber's own texts on 2026-09-02 found that this use "is a departure from Wilber rather than a reading of him": for Wilber the quadrants are ontological (`notes/ken wilber.md`; O3).

### Provenance

**Native, and richer than the design's** (F2, F3, F4, O3). Every note carries `sources:` with a location. The vault adds:

- the rule that a claimed correspondence quotes both sides and never quotes from memory (`ops/methodology/both halves of a correspondence.md`);
- the rule that documents compiled from one thread count once;
- a grade for how deeply a source was read (Grade A, Grade B, secondary, "corpus-relay only", "from memory");
- the rule that the account nearer an event is closer to what happened;
- the marks for whose words each sentence is ([D], [C], [A]);

**Two weaknesses.**
