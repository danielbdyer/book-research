# Reconciliation register — the 2026-08-13 reframe conversation

*Every place the ~72,000-word conversation evolves, drifts, or argues with itself, rendered as a numbered **fork** for the author to adjudicate. Each fork carries: the competing versions (quoted, with line anchors into the full transcript), what it gates, a recommendation (a recommendation only — his to decide), and the vault destination once resolved. Most-consequential first; the **8 that gate everything else** are collected at the end.*

*Status: **v1**, from the dedicated reconciliation lane. The five harvest lanes (claims, figures, definitions, structures, roots) may surface additional domain-specific forks, which will be appended. Nothing is written into the graph until the author calls each fork.*

> **The datum that governs the whole register.** The model produced **five separately-declared "final" architectures**, each materially different, on demand. That instability is the single most important fact here: no one of these is "the answer" to transcribe — each is a candidate to adjudicate. The conversation says it of itself (line 7855): *"the book cannot be an argument for participatory authorship if its own creation is fundamentally non-participatory."* By that logic this register is itself candidate wardrobe over the author's lived seed; the most faithful use of it is the least-architected.

## Fork 2 — Ecology-not-ontology vs the nested ontology (the invariant)

**The fork:** is the invariant a *movement/relationship* ecology, or a *things-that-exist* hierarchy?

The real ontology is relational rather than hierarchical*" (17280).

**Gates:** the deepest fork under the data model and the book's self-description; settling it dissolves most of Fork 1 and inoculates against a proprietary taxonomy.
**Recommendation:** **ecology-over-ontology is the invariant** (stated four times and defended; the nested ontology appears once, on demand, self-hedged). Hold the five orders as a *way-in* via the Vijñāna-Bhairava demotion test the vault already passed.
**→ Destination:** an `ops/methodology/` note ("ecology names a movement, not a taxonomy") + an `ops/decisions.md` row.

## Fork 4 — The two orthogonal two-book cuts (the "Book I/II" labels drift)
