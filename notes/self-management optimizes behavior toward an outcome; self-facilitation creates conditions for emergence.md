# self-management optimizes behavior toward an outcome; self-facilitation creates conditions for emergence

The reframe conversation contrasts self-management with self-facilitation by their mechanism and their aim. Self-management optimizes behavior toward an outcome: it starts from a specified end and adjusts conduct to approach it, so its logic is instrumental and its success is the outcome reached. Self-facilitation creates conditions for emergence: it starts from no specified end and instead arranges the inner and outer conditions under which a becoming can arise that was not fixed in advance, so its logic is generative and its success is the condition established. The two are therefore not competing techniques for the same task but operations of different kinds — one steers toward a known target, the other makes room for an unknown one. The contrast supplies the mechanism beneath the book's refusal to treat a practice as a means to an end, recorded at [[the practice is the expression of realization rather than a means to it]], since optimization is precisely the means-to-an-end logic self-facilitation sets aside.
