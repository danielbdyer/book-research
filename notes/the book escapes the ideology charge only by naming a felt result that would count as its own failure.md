# the book escapes the ideology charge only by naming a felt result that would count as its own failure

Adorno's detector for ideology is a single question: does any observation count against the claim? Where nothing the reader could feel or fail to feel would tell against the book, the book is ideology in his sense — consolation sold as depth, its failures always reread as the reader's own lack. The detector is portable against the book itself, and it has one and only one entry point. A felt-shift book can install the detector against its own claim by naming, in advance, a felt result that would count as failure — a determinate outcome the reader could reach that would mean the book did not do what it said. Absent such a named result, the felt shift is a non-falsifiable court of appeal: feel the shift and the book is confirmed, feel nothing and the book is not refuted but the reader was merely not yet ready. Naming the failing result is what converts the claim from a self-sealing one into a testable one, because it lets a reader's experience disconfirm the book rather than only confirm it.

The move is narrow and demanding. It requires the book to specify, before the reading, an experience that would falsify it, and to hold that specification when a reader reports it — which is the opposite of the reflex that metabolizes every disconfirmation as the reader's insufficiency.

## Substantiation

- The dig states the detector and its single portable use: "if no observation counts against the claim, it is ideology; a book installs it against itself only by naming a felt result that counts as failure" (transmission dig 6: DEVICES). This note carries the detector turned constructively on the book.
- The depth is thin and stated: whether Adorno himself supplies any criterion separating a legitimate claim from an ideological one is unread at the primary in dig 6, so the note is marked `nascent`. The queued reading tests exactly this (dig 6, READINGS TO QUEUE item 2); its answer decides whether the detector has a real gate or, in Adorno's own hands, indicts everything alike.
- The device is the constructive counterpart to the register attack: the same non-falsifiability that arms the charge is the property the book must deliberately break in its own claim.
- The move meets the compliance attack from the other side: a named failing result is administrable as the out-of-room check, since a reader can report reaching it to someone who did not induce the reading — which is why the escape from ideology and the answer to the demand-characteristic charge want the same second person.
