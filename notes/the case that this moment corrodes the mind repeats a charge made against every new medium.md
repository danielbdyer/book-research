# the case that this moment corrodes the mind repeats a charge made against every new medium

The book's why-now — the claim that this cultural moment fragments attention and threatens recognition ([[the contemporary attention crisis is ecological, not merely psychological, technical, cultural, or spiritual]]) — sits inside a long genre. Each major new medium was charged in its own hour with corroding the faculties it was supposed to serve: Plato's *Phaedrus* charged writing with destroying memory, and print, the novel, radio, and television each drew a version of the same complaint that the mind was being degraded by the newest vehicle. The steelman the objection builds from this history is not that the why-now is false but that it is generic. The claim that mediation erodes the activities of meaning-making is the standing complaint about every medium, and the burden of showing that this mediation differs in kind — that it erodes rather than merely changes — falls on the book, which the sociology asserts rather than demonstrates. The record cannot presently discharge the burden: the shelf supplies mechanism (the device paradigm, resonance, the achievement subject) and no longitudinal or controlled evidence that meaning-making is eroded rather than altered.

The objection matters because the book prizes kept opposition, and this is the honest opposition to its own why-now. It does not touch the book's timeless core — recognition as un-forgetting is not dated — but it does discipline the book's account of urgency: a "why now" that cannot show this mediation differs in kind is company for a recurring anxiety rather than a demonstrated novelty. The book already holds one structural concession adjacent to this evidential one: it keeps the civilizational scale as a horizon rather than a built structure ([[the civilizational book is a horizon not a structure to build]]).

## Substantiation

- This is the dig's steelmanned case against the why-now, stated without rebuttal (dig 5, the strongest case against the why-now, item 1). It rests on the historical pattern rather than on a reading [C per dig 5]: no primary text was opened for it, and the charge-by-charge history from the *Phaedrus* onward is held as general knowledge to be verified rather than as a citation.
- The absence the objection points at is a genuine gap in the shelf, recorded in the dig's synthesis: on whether mediation erodes rather than merely changes the activities of meaning-making, the record cannot rule, because the strata supply mechanism and never longitudinal evidence.
