# a gathered community decays from spontaneous to normative to ideological and structure is the container that survives the decay

Victor Turner named communitas the anti-structural modality in which a congregation of statusless, liminal people levels rank and meets as equals, and he was precise that it is not warm community feeling but this exact levelling. The dig recovers his forecast for what becomes of it. Existential or spontaneous communitas is a phase and a moment rather than a permanent condition, and it hardens the instant it organizes into a system. Turner's reframe of what the hardening is carries the finding. Normative communitas — spontaneity mobilized into a lasting system — is hedged about by rules and interdictions that he likens to "the lead container of a dangerous radioactive isotope" (Turner, "In and Out of Time"). Structure, on this reading, is not communitas's betrayer but the safety-container that lets a dangerous energy persist, and the decay continues one further step into ideological communitas, the utopian blueprint drawn from a remembered spontaneity.

The finding is a forecast for any community the book gathers. It cuts against the anti-structure romance the play lineage otherwise carries and against any hope of a permanent communitas, and it reframes the choice: a gathered readership cannot be held at spontaneous communitas, so the question is not whether to admit structure but whether the structure admitted is a container that keeps the energy live or a system that has already decayed into ideology.
