# the ontology is musical notation not the music

The author holds the project's ontology — the entities and structure a knowledge system uses to hold its material — as notation rather than as the thing itself. A score is useful and then invisible once the music starts; an ontology earns its place the same way, by quietly helping a person notice, connect, and remember, and it forfeits that place the moment it becomes more interesting to maintain than the inquiry it was built to serve. The working rule the author draws from the figure is that the scaffolding must keep disappearing, so the lived encounter stays in front and the model behind it. This is the vault's own displacement guard stated as a design principle, and the author names it in the same breath as the failure mode it guards against — the over-engineering that had repeatedly stalled the project before it began.

## Substantiation

- The figure and its rule are the author's own, dictated as he designed the system. He asks for "the minimum structure that keeps inquiry alive," so that "if the model draws attention to itself, it's probably carrying too much conceptual weight, but if it quietly helps you notice, connect, remember, then it's doing its job — almost like musical notation, useful but invisible when the music starts" (reframe conversation, 2026-08-13, transcript line 1215). He states it as the governing instruction: "Keep the ontology minimal, just enough to preserve lived inquiry. Treat the structure as musical notation, not the music. Simplify whenever it gets in the way of the lived encounter. Aim to support participation, not to model the mind" (reframe conversation, 2026-08-13, transcript line 1325).
- The principle is paired with the failure it prevents, also in the author's words: "already over engineering the system again, which was the recurrent blocker that I hit in getting into this system and using it in the first place" (reframe conversation, 2026-08-13, transcript line 1138). The figure is therefore a guard the author reaches for against a tendency he knows from the inside.
