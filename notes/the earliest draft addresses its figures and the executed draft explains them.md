# the earliest draft addresses its figures and the executed draft explains them

The author names the earliest draft's one written chapter his high-water mark of voice, and the difference between that voice and the executed draft's reduces to a single grammatical operation. The mythic draft addresses its textual objects as present company: "Hello, ink. Thank you for coming. We see you not merely as medium, but as witness" (mythic-os: page 9); "The ink nodded" (page 12); "Page 6 is waiting with its palms open" (page 10). The executed draft keeps the same figure and converts address to exposition under a simile hedge: "as though the page were a kind of patient listener" (abridged ch2), with only two residual animations surviving unhedged ("the book wants to commend," ch8 and ch15).

Apostrophe became exposition. The operation is transferable in either direction, which is what makes it craft rather than taxonomy: a drafting session that wants the high-water voice back knows the exact move — drop the hedge and speak to the thing — and a session that wants the executed register knows what was paid for it.
