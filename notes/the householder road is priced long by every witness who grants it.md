# the householder road is priced long by every witness who grants it

The claim that deep change is available inside ordinary life has an adversary who attacks its possibility (Weil), and it has witnesses who grant it — and the witnesses converge on its price. Ignatius's Annotation 19 adaptation "runs about nine months… the performing text in ordinary life is not the shorter option, it is the longer one." Al-Qushayrī's stations are earned "by the sweat of the brow." Maslow's plateau is "built by long living." Mehta: "the way to what is closest to us is the longest way back." Kornfield's after-the-ecstasy record: "nobody did the laundry alone," across years. Dewey's growth is "unspectacular, cumulative, and made of ordinary experiences well connected." Alexander adds the structural warning: "discontinuous change is the mechanism by which living structure is destroyed."

The convergence does not touch the doctrine — every witness grants that the Tuesday can be the temple. It lands on the vehicle: an arc traversed in one reading is peak-shaped, and the witnesses jointly say the householder road is not. The book's own aftermath material states what follows a peak; this convergence states what the peak's container should have been sized for, which is a design pressure the author's arc commitment has to hold or answer.

- The seven positions are quoted from their evaluations; Ignatius's is anchored to the Annotations read at his 2026-08-02 evaluation, Kornfield's and Maslow's to their consulted works, the rest marked derived — a mixed standing the claim inherits.
