# a felt shift is a move in participatory knowing, not a private sensation

The reframe places the book as one locus in an ecology of participation, and it locates the felt shift inside a wider account of knowing. In that account the felt shift is a move in participatory knowing — the fourth of the four kinds the conversation draws from John Vervaeke, "knowing through being involved in and co-identifying with an arena" (reframe conversation, 2026-08-13, line 2086) — rather than a private sensation the reader reads off in isolation. The distinction the conversation calls its central diagnosis makes the contrast exact: "Information about an experience is not the same thing as participation in the experience" (line 3486), so a sensation noticed and reported would be information about an experience while the shift itself is the reader's participation in one. The felt shift is therefore an act of participatory knowing rather than an interior signal that participation could be swapped for.

This re-grounds the felt-shift standard without settling anything the verification frame holds open. The correspondence between the book's felt shift and Vervaeke's participatory knowing is asserted within the conversation and rests on an unread source, so it is held as a seed at [[there are four kinds of knowing, and the felt shift lives at the edge of the participatory]] rather than certified here. And the standard's counterfeit tension is preserved intact, since fluent prose can still produce a bodily confirmation that carries no recognition ([[felt shift versus felt confirmation]]). Placing the shift as a participatory move does not answer that tension on its own terms; it names what kind of event the shift is, so the verification frame becomes a feature nested within the participation account rather than the ground beneath it.

## Substantiation

- The four kinds of knowing, in the conversation's words: "propositional - knowing that; procedural - knowing how; perspectival - knowing what is salient / relevant here and now; participatory - knowing through being involved in and co-identifying with an arena" (reframe conversation, 2026-08-13, line 2086), named later as "a natural bridge to Vervaeke and participatory knowing" (line 15352).
- The central diagnosis the contrast rests on: "Information about an experience is not the same thing as participation in the experience" (reframe conversation, 2026-08-13, line 3486), held at [[information about an experience is not participation in the experience]].
- The felt shift's own defense already states the participatory core clinically: a genuine shift is a step after which the implicit meanings are different, so something becomes sayable that was not sayable before ([[a genuine felt shift changes what can be said next]]).
- The re-grounding is the author's, recorded as author direction, 2026-08-14; whether the felt shift reliably occurs or certifies anything is not asserted here, only what kind of knowing it belongs to.
