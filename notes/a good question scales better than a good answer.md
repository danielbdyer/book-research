# a good question scales better than a good answer

Among the axioms of authorship the reframe conversation gathered, the author ratified one that governs what the book should chiefly offer: a good question scales better than a good answer. A question travels. It can be taken up by many people, carried into circumstances its asker never saw, and it keeps producing inquiry each time it is asked, so its reach grows with use. An answer is received once and, being received, closes; it settles the matter for its recipient rather than opening it for the next person. The axiom therefore tells the maker where to put his weight — into the questions the book leaves the reader holding rather than the conclusions it hands them — which is why the earlier form of the same counsel in the conversation was to guard the question longer than any given answer. It sits with the companion axioms the same passage states, that a framework succeeds by increasing the diversity of authentic insights and that the aim is polyphony rather than unanimity, and it is the mechanism beneath them: a book built on scalable questions leaves each reader authoring their own insight, while a book built on answers would have distributed one.

## Substantiation

The axiom appears in the conversation's synthesis of the authorship axioms: after "Aim for polyphony, not unanimity," it states "a good question scales better than a good answer" (reframe conversation, 2026-08-13, line 521). The author ratifies the set in his own turn: "it does, it feels coherent to me too" (line 526). Its earlier form, given as counsel to the author near a point of closure, is the same proposition as advice: "I'd say guard that question longer than you guard any given answer" (line 431).

This item is the author's own material, held under the three conditions of personal provenance — owned, held delicately, and in honor of what is becoming. The record shows an axiom co-articulated in the conversation's synthesis and ratified by the author rather than dictated verbatim, and the note keeps that distance.
