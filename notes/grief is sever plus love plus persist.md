# grief is sever plus love plus persist

The book analyzes grief as Sever + Love + Persist: love continuing after the connection is cut. The first-telling's own phrasing: "love still faithfully reporting for duty, with nowhere to land." The reclassification follows from the formula's components: what presents as a disorder is the ordinary operation of love under a condition the connection can no longer carry. The first-telling draws the same conclusion in its own words: grief is "love doing exactly what love does, under conditions of loss" (first-telling: the offering).

The formula carries a structurally unusual element: a catalyst. A witness sits inside the equation rather than beside it as optional comfort — the clinical statement of the requirement is recorded separately at [[grief cannot complete alone]]. This makes the formula the chemistry-level form of the book's relational claim.

## Substantiation

- First-telling, "The offering": "Grief is love continuing after the connection is cut — love still faithfully reporting for duty, with nowhere to land."
- Atlas §IV lists the formula with the witness marked as catalyst and cross-links it to the relational-field section (§VIII).
- Walked IV records the book's staging of the catalyst requirement in a canning scene: some preservations cannot be done alone.
- The space-around survey proposes three loans from real chemistry that would deepen the catalyst reading: activation energy, nucleation (the witness as nucleation site), and catalytic surfaces (holding reactants in the right orientation as a definition of facilitation). None has been researched for this project; all three are on the pending-evaluation list in `ops/queue/candidates.md`.
