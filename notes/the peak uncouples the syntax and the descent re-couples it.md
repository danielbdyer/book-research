# the peak uncouples the syntax and the descent re-couples it

The arc's deepest transition is carried in sentence structure, verifiable independently of the chapter's self-description. At the peak, the syntax uncouples: "The breath you just took feels like it has been happening forever, and also like it has just begun, and also like the distinction between the two does not apply here" (abridged ch11); "Whatever it is is also you and is also not you and the distinction does not, in this region of experience, mean very much" (ch11); "The trees mean. The light means. The breath means." (ch11). The descent's opening re-couples in four short declaratives: "The peak passes. The loops re-couple. Time returns to its ordinary motion. The body becomes, again, this body" (ch12), and integration proceeds in clipped imperatives: "Avoid stimulation. Sleep. Walk in the woods." (ch12).

One caution is part of the record: ch11 also announces its own strangeness, which is a different mechanism ([[the executed draft announces a register change before performing it]]); the syntax was verified separately from the announcement, so the two claims do not lean on each other.

## Substantiation

- Specimens verified in the extracted text: two "and also" chains and one doubled copula in ch11, the meaning-tricolon, the four-sentence re-coupled opening, and the imperative catalogue in ch12.
- Checkable: coordination density and sentence length measured across ch11 and ch12 — the chains and doubled copulas occur only in ch11, and ch12's opening returns to subject-verb declaratives; either pattern appearing in the other chapter is the exception to find.
- The state the syntax performs — boundaries losing application, then re-forming — is the high-energy material's territory, held at [[an enormous experience is interpreted by the structure it did not change]] and its neighbors.
