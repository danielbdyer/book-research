# the peak uncouples the syntax and the descent re-couples it

The arc's deepest transition is carried in sentence structure, and the pattern can be checked from the sentences alone, without relying on anything the chapter says about itself. At the peak, the syntax uncouples: "The breath you just took feels like it has been happening forever, and also like it has just begun, and also like the distinction between the two does not apply here" (abridged ch11); "Whatever it is is also you and is also not you and the distinction does not, in this region of experience, mean very much" (ch11); "The trees mean. The light means. The breath means." (ch11). The descent's opening re-couples in four short declaratives: "The peak passes. The loops re-couple. Time returns to its ordinary motion. The body becomes, again, this body" (ch12), and integration proceeds in clipped imperatives: "Avoid stimulation. Sleep. Walk in the woods." (ch12).

One caution belongs with this finding. Chapter 11 also tells the reader in advance that its prose will loop and grow stranger, which is a separate device recorded on its own ([[the executed draft announces a register change before performing it]]). The counts of coordination and sentence length above were taken from the sentences themselves rather than from that announcement, so neither claim rests on the other.

## Substantiation

- The specimens were verified in the extracted text: two chains of "and also" and one doubled copula in ch11, the three-part sentence in which the trees, the light, and the breath each mean, the four-sentence re-coupled opening of ch12, and the imperative catalogue that follows it.
- Anyone can check this by measuring coordination density and sentence length across ch11 and ch12: the chains and doubled copulas occur only in ch11, and ch12's opening returns to plain subject-verb declaratives. Either pattern appearing in the other chapter is the exception to look for.
- The state these sentences enact is one in which the ordinary boundaries between things stop applying and then re-form, which is the territory of the vault's material on intense states — including the finding that a state is interpreted by whatever structure receives it, so an experience can be enormous and still leave that structure unchanged ([[an enormous experience is interpreted by the structure it did not change]]).
