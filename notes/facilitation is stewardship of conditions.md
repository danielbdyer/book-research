# facilitation is stewardship of conditions

The reframe conversation names the ethical endpoint of Book I: the facilitator ultimately becomes a steward of conditions. Stewardship is set against causation. The honest claim a facilitator can make is not "I caused this transformation" but "I helped create a field in which something became possible." The distinction keeps the transformation owned by the participant while still crediting the facilitator with real work — the tending and holding of conditions. Power, on this account, becomes something to steward rather than to deny or to exercise as authorship over another's change. Stated as Book I's endpoint, the claim gathers the field material into a single honest sentence a facilitator can say afterward.
