# felt shift versus felt confirmation

The space-around survey records two limits on the book's verification standard, and recommends the book hold both rather than defend against them. The first limit is that the confirmation can be produced by the prose itself. The survey states it directly: "The click can lie. Processing-fluency research shows that ease of processing feels like truth — familiar, rhythmic, well-formed sentences produce a bodily yes that is about the sentence, not the world" (space-around: around the instrument). Where prose is fluent, a confirmation may register the sentence's construction and carry no information about the claim. The survey names the needed distinction: felt shift versus felt confirmation. The second limit is uneven access. The survey reports that interoception research finds the inner signal varying widely in clarity, and that it "can be muted precisely where the folds are deepest" (space-around: around the instrument) — in the readers carrying the most protective structure. For those readers the test returns nothing, and the survey's calibration note, drawn from Somatic Experiencing, is titration: small approaches, touch and retreat, with an explicit allowance for the reader the test does not yet work for.

The tension binds this vault as well as the book, and [[methods]] records the operational consequence: a well-formed note can read as true because it is well-formed, and the vault's verification checks structure and provenance while the somatic certification remains the author's.

## Substantiation

- Space-around, "Around the instrument," carries both counterweights and ranks them in its closing priority list as the additions that make the book's other claims more credible.
- The standard they limit is recorded at [[nothing enters the lattice the body has not countersigned]].
- The survey notes that the book's architecture already holds two partial answers: the plain-prose passes work as a counterfeit check by contrast, and the relational field works as a second check on a solitary reading.
- The first limit has a sharper statement available from the book's own foundations, and it names what a counterfeit click actually is. Both traditions the book stands on refuse the manufactured epiphany, so a bodily yes produced by an apparatus is, in their terms, the apparatus working rather than the world showing ([[both poles of the convergence refuse the engineered epiphany]]). That gives the honesty section the strongest form of this counterweight: the counterfeit is not merely a research finding about fluent prose, it is the failure the book's own sources predict.
- The grounding the survey called for arrived 2026-08-04, and it strengthens both limits while adding what the survey did not have — a defense. The first limit now has its measured catalogue, including the finding that the discernment is teachable and the honest middle that the counterfeit runs weaker than the genuine article on average ([[the felt sense of truth has a documented counterfeit catalogue]]). The second limit now has the dissociation that makes it dangerous: attention to the body and accurate reading of it come apart, and the gap is invisible from inside ([[attending to the body is not the same as reading it accurately]]). The defense comes from the standard's own source — a genuine shift is a step that changes what can be said next, which the counterfeit cannot do ([[a genuine felt shift changes what can be said next]]) — with the practice-level guards already written as instructions in the source protocol ([[gendlin's focusing protocol already guards against the counterfeit shift]]). And the sharpest refusal of the self-certifying experience turns out to come from inside the book's own lineages, with the relational check installed in its place ([[zen's makyo instruction refuses the self-certifying experience]]) — which converges with this note's existing treatment line: the relational field as the second check.
