# flourishing flow and facilitation converged on one stance in the author's own record

Three lines of the author's own work — modelling human flourishing, studying flow, and practising facilitation — run from 2024 and arrive at the same place. The author named the convergence himself on 20 May 2026, ten weeks before the corpus formalized it as the Stance: the domains he lists are "coaching, participation, container-work, emergence during/after immersion in flow state, poetry-as-meaning-praxis, facilitation," and the aim he states for them is "a life lived within this texture… but not precisely the study of this domain" (provenance addendum three §4, quoting [D] 20 May 2026).

Each line carries the presence motif from 2024. The flourishing modelling organized core practices as gravitational wells with presence practice as the first, and by June 2026 had resolved into a geometry whose load-bearing element is "Pure Presence — attention as the whole offering." Flow entered under the heading of "a decisive and immersive presence in the here and now" and produced the author's own coinage in March 2025: certain exertions "can cause a person to shift away from constriction and anxiety into deep time or contemplative time. Let's call these kairos rituals." Facilitation appears first as a description of the author — "your extraordinary capacity for holding space for others and the ongoing learning of how to hold space for yourself" (December 2024).

The second half of the finding is the return-changed motif, and its most compressed statement is the author's, from January 2026: "Birthing meaning from holding and releasing specific tensions like a design constraint. Automatic yet fully attended to." Its epistemology arrives in May 2026 in the same voice — "I'll know as I'm speaking how it's feeling for me" — truth discovered in the emergence rather than before it.

## Substantiation

- The addendum states the limit in the same breath as the finding, and the vault keeps them together: "this convergence is partly curation: flourishing, flow, and facilitation are your chosen domains — that three of your loves converge on presence demonstrates the coherence of your attention, not an external law of the three fields" (provenance addendum three §6). This is the weaker and truer claim beside the corpus's stronger one; it says the author's record converges, not that the fields do.
- The dating separates the thing from its name: "The thing is old; the proper noun is new." The practice and both motifs are present from 2024 under other names — holding space, kairos rituals, micro-flow, live ontology — while the Stance and the posture's Sanskrit name are corpus-era and largely the collaborator's drafting (provenance addendum three §6).
- The attribution follows the pattern the vault records elsewhere ([[the author curated and vetoed while the collaborator wrote the correspondences]]) and the addendum states it in one line: the author supplies the lived law, the collaboration supplies its formal wardrobe. The lived formulations are the author's — "emergence during/after immersion in flow state," "automatic yet fully attended," "I'll know as I'm speaking." The doctrinal dress is the collaborator's — "affected without captured," "consents to be the aperture," and Rosenstock-Huessy's *respondeo etsi mutabor*.
- The May 20 statement also carries the author's own naming of his contribution and his seat. He offered "lowercase 'h' holiness or reverence as territory next to the suchness of reality" as his addition beside the thinkers he reveres, and positioned himself at the axis of the builders' company rather than as its thirtieth member — the seat the corpus later formalizes as "both the author's seat and the book's subject" (provenance addendum three §4; atlas §IX.4). The session's rendering of his vocation: "the praxis of midwifing participation in another."
- One correction to a note this bears on. The space-around survey treats "automatic, yet fully attended" as a corpus phrase and matches it to the classical problem of *wu wei*. The phrase is the author's own, from January 2026, about facilitation ([[unrelated traditions converge on presence without agenda while remaining available to act]]). The survey was pointing at his sentence.
