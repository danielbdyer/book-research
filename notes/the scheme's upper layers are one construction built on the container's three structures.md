# the scheme's upper layers are one construction built on the container's three structures

The book stacks three layers on the container anatomy, and they are not three ideas that happen to sit together. The twelve verb-primitives are its operations, and the six base ones are the make-and-unmake pairs of the anatomy's three structures — Create and Dissolve, Bind and Sever, Maintain and Release. The molecule formulas are compositions of those verbs. The three loops are the same three structures running in time, under the tradition's names for will, knowledge and action. Each layer is defined out of the terms of the layer under it.

That is why the scheme cannot be evaluated a layer at a time. A judgment about the standing of any layer lands on all of them, because the lower layer supplies the terms the upper one is written in. The author's demotion of 2026-08-02, which made the scheme scaffolding rather than a centerpiece, and his refinement of 2026-08-04, which moved the schemes "from architecture to instrument," therefore reach the molecule catalogue and the engine as surely as they reach the corners. Nothing about that demotion depends on the layers being weak; it depends on their being one construction whose standing the author has settled.

Two facts about the construction are worth stating in their own right, because a drafting session would reach for both.

**The grammatical choice at the operations layer carries a claim.** Every one of the twelve is a verb, and the first-telling states the stake directly: "a self described in nouns can be finished, filed, concluded. A self described in verbs is always mid-sentence" (first-telling: the offering). The corpus attributes that stake to Bakhtin's unfinalizability and treats the parts of speech as protection against description hardening into verdict. The set is claimed closed on a drill in which every proposed thirteenth verb reduced to a combination of the twelve, and closure is what would qualify the set as an alphabet rather than a list. That report is qualified rather than accepted here, because no source names a candidate thirteenth or shows a reduction ([[every discrimination the molecule formulas are credited with is made by the prose beside them]]).

**The engine layer is a theology in mechanic's clothing, and the book keeps both faces on purpose.** The three cylinders correspond to icchā, jñāna and kriyā, the goddess's three powers of will, knowledge and action, so the book's most mechanical image is also its most theological. The treatment's image system states the equation outright — "The Engine / the Cylinders ↔ the three loops ↔ icchā, jñāna, kriyā — will, knowledge, action. A triadic theology in mechanic's clothing" — and the title constellation refuses to settle it: "the friction between mechanic and priest is the thesis in miniature — tune it, don't resolve it" (arc-gloss: front matter).

## Substantiation

- Walked III lists all twelve operations with their pairings and glosses and records the falsifiability drill; atlas §III records the reflexive fact the corpus flags for the author, that the book itself is built almost entirely from one primitive, Express.
- The executed prose defines the title's idiom structurally: "'Firing on all cylinders' is the felt condition in which all three are functioning and integrated" (abridged ch7). Atlas §X.5e carries the same cylinder equation as the treatment's image system.
- The composition of the six derived verbs from the six base ones appears only in the executed prose edition, and checked there it fails in three ways, which is the subject of the note on what the formulas can and cannot carry rather than of this one.
- The outside standard this construction would have to meet or decline is named and unmet: the space-around survey queues Wierzbicka's semantic primes as the sparring partner for any claim that the alphabet is found rather than invented, and the corpus's axiom does not answer it ([[the corpus names the standard its found-not-invented axiom has not met]]).
- The habit of putting a theological term to household work runs through the whole construction rather than only the engine, and it appears again in the production instruments where the per-chapter dial is identified with spanda ([[the per-chapter dial is a spanda ratio]]). The rule the habit follows is [[ordinary names keep the deepest structures visible]].
- **The dating belongs with the construction and is provenance rather than doctrine.** The corners were axiomatized in the author's own separate work in December 2025, three months before his first formal contact with the tradition whose name means the triad ([[the triad predates the author's contact with the tradition named the triad]]). What arrived later was the scheme's standing and its vocabulary, not its content.
