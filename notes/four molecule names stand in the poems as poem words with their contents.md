# four molecule names stand in the poems as poem words with their contents

The division between the poems (primary) and the system (scaffolding) is real and stays; this claim softens it honestly at the vocabulary level. Four of the formula catalogue's names appear in the poems as ordinary words, each carrying something close to its formula's content. Sanctuary: "the room seems to breathe with them – a sanctuary" (poems #2). Grief at its crossing: "the intersection of / grief and a reckoning" (poems #7). Paralysis as deferred living: "the new paralysis of modernity / is dreaming so longingly about what could be / that we forget to live life in the now" (poems #3) — the content of the catalogue's possibility-without-commitment diagnosis. Ambivalence as held opposition: "the ambivalence of yellow... surety while not / being sure" (poems #10), with "the great melancholy / of your ambivalence" beside it (poems #11).

One caution is part of the claim: these are common English words, so the word alone proves nothing — the content-fit carries the finding, strongest at paralysis and ambivalence, and the claim asserts vocabulary continuity, not that the poems contain the formulas.
