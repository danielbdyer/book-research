# four molecule names stand in the poems as poem words with their contents

Four of the names the book's formula catalogue uses appear in the poems as ordinary words, and each of the four carries something close to the content its formula later assigns it. The finding qualifies, at the single point of vocabulary, the standing division that holds the poems as the primary layer beneath the seven corpus documents and the system of primitives and formulas as scaffolding rather than the book's center — a division this note leaves otherwise intact. Sanctuary: "the room seems to breathe with them – a sanctuary" (poems #2). Grief at its crossing: "the intersection of / grief and a reckoning" (poems #7). Paralysis as deferred living: "the new paralysis of modernity / is dreaming so longingly about what could be / that we forget to live life in the now" (poems #3) — and the catalogue analyses paralysis as possibility with the commitment subtracted out of it, which is what the poem describes when it says that dreaming about what could be displaces living now. Ambivalence as held opposition: "the ambivalence of yellow... surety while not / being sure" (poems #10), with "the great melancholy / of your ambivalence" beside it (poems #11).

One caution belongs to the claim itself. All four are common English words, so the appearance of a word proves nothing on its own; what supports the claim is that each poem uses its word for the content the formula assigns that word, and the match between poem and formula is closest at paralysis and at ambivalence. The claim asserts that the vocabulary is continuous between the poems and the system. It does not assert that the poems contain the formulas.

## Substantiation

- All five passages were verified in the extracted text at the poem numbers given above.
- For the fifteen poems that predate the book, a phrase shared between a poem and the corpus originates in the poem rather than in the book; that rule of direction is held at [[the corpus quotes the author's poems rather than the poems quoting the corpus]], which records that the youngness line is poem five's and that several of the book's doctrines stand in the poems in the author's unassisted voice. Under it, the continuity in these four names runs from poem to book.
- The formula side was compared against three records. [[burnout is obligation minus sanctuary]] states the book's analysis of burnout as obligation with the sanctuary removed, which is the sanctuary content. [[grief is sever plus love plus persist]] states the book's analysis of grief as love still functioning under the conditions of loss, with a witness required inside the formula, which is the grief content. The paralysis content comes from the formula-by-formula check of the catalogue at [[every discrimination the molecule formulas are credited with is made by the prose beside them]], where paralysis is recorded as possibility with the commitment subtracted out. The ambivalence content has no entry in that check; its formula-side match rests instead on the atlas's own placement of Ambivalence among the held-tension molecules — "Tension (held oppositions): Possibility · Trust · Ambivalence" (atlas §IV) — which reads the poem's "surety while not being sure" as held opposition, and the gap in the catalogue is flagged at that note.
