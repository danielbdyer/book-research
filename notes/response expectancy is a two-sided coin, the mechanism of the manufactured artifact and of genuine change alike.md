# response expectancy is a two-sided coin, the mechanism of the manufactured artifact and of genuine change alike

The Dig VI crossover — aura raises expectancy, expectancy manufactures the experience, demand secures the report — is the attack the digs rated most threatening, because it turns the felt-shift standard into the bearing the objection rotates on. The author's disposition is to admit the crossover "with clarity and honesty that it is a core risk" while lessening its weight, refusing to let it become "doubting the truth of one's own intuition in a neurotic way" that is not productive. He holds response expectancy as a coin with two sides: the same mechanism that manufactures a hollow artifact is where change-practitioners like Tony Robbins and Nichole Hudson do real work, so that a result "produced by expectancy" is not thereby unreal. The mechanism is neutral as to outcome — expectancy is the shared engine of the counterfeit and the genuine — and which side runs is not settled by the mechanism being present, which is why the attack survives as an honest admission rather than as a defeat.

## Substantiation

- The author states the disposition in his own words: the impact should lessen, since the crossover "is sort of doubting the truth of one's own intuition in a neurotic way that I'm not sure is productive. I think we admit it with clarity and honesty that it is a core risk, but it's also where Robbins and Hudson and others do their work too, and it seems plausible to think it's a coin that has two sides" (author statement, 2026-08-16, open-decisions queue, B3).
- Held under the personal-provenance conditions — owned in his own answer, recorded delicately, in honor of what it carried him through — the author supplies the lived instance of expectancy working the productive side: "My theatre arts acting in high school being a way to overcome my stuttering and speech impediment because I was a different character invites me to hold this lightly" (author statement, 2026-08-16, open-decisions queue, B3). The record supplies exactly this specificity and no more: high school theatre, a stutter and speech impediment, and the mechanism of being a different character — a manufactured character producing a change in speech, expectancy on its genuine side.
- The cross-dig synthesis rates the crossover the most threatening attack and states its boundary: "the attack explains origin, not durability," and "the one thing a simulator cannot fake is out-of-situation, third-party-verified durable change" (transmission dig 0, 2026-08-13, §5.4, verdict iii). The boundary is what makes the productive side available — the mechanism accounts for how a shift begins, not for whether it lasts.
- Dig VI records the two-sidedness in Kirsch's own finding: response expectancy is "self-confirming" and "sufficient," and yet it "cuts both ways, since expectancy produces real behavioral and physiological change, so 'produced by expectancy' does not entail 'unreal'" (transmission dig 6, 2026-08-13).
