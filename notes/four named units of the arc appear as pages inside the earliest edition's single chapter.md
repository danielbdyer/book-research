# four named units of the arc appear as pages inside the earliest edition's single chapter

Chapter 1 of the mythic operating system edition is written as a sequence of titled pages rather than as continuous prose. Four of those page titles are the titles of chapters in the later fifteen-chapter arc: The House with No Ceiling, The Way of Ripening, The Afterglow, and The Geometry of Becoming. Two further page titles, The Gold Thread and the apostrophe to the ink, name entries in the book's image system.

The order differs, and the difference is the finding. In the earlier chapter the four run house, ripening, afterglow, geometry, with the poem between ripening and afterglow. In the arc they run house at the opening, geometry at the close of the entry movement, ripening at the close of the relational movement, and afterglow after the peak, spread across four of the five parts. The later architecture did not add these units; it separated them and gave each the distance it needed. What was one chapter's page sequence became the spine of a book.

This bears on how the corpus's structural material should be read. The arc that the atlas describes as four simultaneous overlays — a recognition plot, a descent of the word, a breath, and a company — is presented there as a design. The earlier edition shows the same sequence in an undifferentiated state, before the overlays existed to space it, which makes the overlays legible as an interpretation applied to an order that was already there rather than as the scheme that generated it.

## Substantiation

- The four page titles, with their pages: "Page 3: The House with No Ceiling," "Page 8: The Way of Ripening," "Page 10: The Afterglow," "Page 14: The Geometry of Becoming" (mythic-os: pages 3, 8, 10, 14).
- Their positions in the arc, with locators: the House with No Ceiling at `abridged ch1` and `arc-gloss: ch1`; the Geometry of Becoming at `abridged ch3`; the Way of Ripening at `abridged ch10`; the Afterglow at `abridged ch12`. The treatment storyboards the same four at the same four positions.
- The image-system pages: "Page 12A: The Gold Thread" and the unnumbered apostrophe that precedes Page 5, which addresses the ink as a witness — "We see you not merely as medium, but as witness. You do not just record. You accompany" (mythic-os, between pages 4.5 and 5). The later arc's second chapter is built from the same figure.
- Eleven of the earlier chapter's pages have no counterpart anywhere in the later arc; they are enumerated in `archive/source — the mythic operating system.md` and queued in `ops/queue/extractions.md`.
- The later arc's overlays are recorded at atlas §X and restated at `arc-gloss: the arc seen whole`; neither document states when the overlays were devised, so the priority reported here rests on the earlier edition's undifferentiated state rather than on any dating in the sources.
