# the peak is the one unit the performing law can honor only by withdrawing

Two of the book's laws collide at exactly one unit. The performing law says every device enacts what it describes, with the pacing "most destabilizing at the peak." The foundations' shared refusal says the breakthrough cannot be engineered: "An apparatus can prepare; it cannot cause." A performed peak would therefore be an engineered epiphany — the one unit where obeying the first law violates the second.

The corpus's staging already resolves the collision, without stating the rule it is obeying: at the peak "the prose form itself thins; the margin voices go silent; and 'the sentence that eleven chapters have been finishing finally completes at a mirror'" (walked X) — completion handed to the reader, the apparatus withdrawing at its own climax. Held as a claim, this converts the peak's staging from a stylistic fact into a doctrinal necessity that any future draft inherits: the more engineered the peak reads, the more it claims what both foundations refuse, so withdrawal is not one option among several but the only performance the law permits there.
