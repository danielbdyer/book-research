# the book may interpret only the author because interpretation is licensed where its subject can answer back

Reflection that returns authorship — "I wonder if…" — and reflection that takes it — "what you're doing is…" — differ at the grammar, and a printed page can only be the second kind toward its reader: a declarative about the reader's inner life asserts into an interior that cannot answer, correct, or refuse it inside the text, which is influence with the reply foreclosed. The author is the one person the book can interpret without that defect, because he is present to his own interpretation and can hold it provisionally. So the book's interpretive energy has exactly one legal channel — the author's own experience worked as the case study — and the reader's interior is reached in the interrogative or the optative, never diagnosed.

This gives the reframe's counsel "make your experience the case study, not the conclusion" a necessity it did not have as advice: the case study is where interpretation is *permitted*, not merely where modesty suggests it live. The claim is a first-principles derivation from the conversation's premises rather than a statement it makes, held nascent and marked as inference; its sentence-level corollary is that declaratives about inner life take the author or the conditions as subject, and the reader appears as the addressee of a question.

## Substantiation

- The grammatical distinction is the conversation's, applied there to facilitators: "'I wonder if...' is fundamentally different from 'What you're doing is...' The former returns authorship. The latter risks taking it" (reframe conversation, 15605–15621, model-elaborated). The author-as-first-participant premise, which makes the author the one interpretable subject, is his own ("the author is the first participant," ratified at 887–902). The case-study formulation is the model's, early and author-adjacent: "make your experience the case study, not the conclusion" (481).
- The derivation: a reader cannot answer a printed interpretation of himself in the moment it is made, so any such interpretation is structurally the authorship-taking kind; the author alone is co-present to his own interpretation and can mark it provisional. Strength: strict, given that print affords the reader no in-text reply.
- Standing: inferred from the premises; the model turns are works consulted, and the author-voice anchor (887) carries the premise the derivation leans on.
