# an in-situ felt shift cannot be told from a response-expectancy artifact without an out-of-room check

The adversary chamber ran the felt shift against its own standard and returned an indistinguishability. Asked whether a felt shift could be checked by a felt shift, the audit answers that this is exactly the trap: a bodily assent registered in the demand-warm state, the beautiful voice still in the reader's ear, is operationally identical to a response-expectancy artifact reported under demand and read through aura. Nothing available inside that state separates the two, because the state supplies both the expectancy that manufactures the artifact and the felt confirmation the reader would use to certify it. The consequence is precise: an in-situ felt shift verifies the conditions that produced it, never the proposition it seems to confirm. It becomes admissible as evidence only when it carries what its setting cannot supply — an external check, delayed past the warm state, administered by someone who did not induce it.

The claim narrows what the felt shift can be asked to do rather than discarding it. The shift remains a real event and a usable signal of movement; what it cannot do, unaccompanied, is certify that the movement tracked the content rather than the setting.

## Substantiation

- The dig's felt-shift audit states the result directly: the in-situ standard "is operationally indistinguishable from a response-expectancy artifact reported under demand and read through aura; it becomes admissible only when it carries what its setting cannot supply: an external, delayed, other-administered check" (transmission dig 6: FELT-SHIFT AUDIT). This note carries that result as a standalone proposition.
- The audit is the reasoning beneath the compliance tension's treatment; it is minted separately because it states a general criterion — the shape of an admissible felt-shift claim — rather than the attack itself ([[a felt shift reported in a high-demand setting may be a demand characteristic rather than a durable change]]).
- The depth is thin and stated: the indistinguishability rests on Kirsch's and Orne's mechanisms as characterized in scholarship, unread at the primary in dig 6, so the note is marked `nascent`. Whether the artifact persists once the warm state is removed — the property that would let a delayed check discriminate — is the queued reading (dig 6, READINGS TO QUEUE item 3).
- The three conditions are load-bearing together: external (outside the reader's own interiority), delayed (outside the warm state), and other-administered (outside the reader who is both subject and assessor). Removing any one collapses the check back into the state it was meant to escape, which is why the same three recur as the treatment of the compliance attack and as the requirement the second chair meets.
