# the executed draft carries the arc's sequence without the treatment's five rooms

The Arc and the Gloss treatment states what its second edition added: five rooms of material walked and carried in — the facilitation craft, Karla McLaren's emotion work, the Heidegger–Erhard–Landmark speech lineage, the twenty-nine container-makers, and the coaches with their five theories of suffering. The treatment calls the consequence its headline, and builds its fourth overlay on it.

The abridged edition follows the treatment's chapter sequence exactly, title for title, and contains almost none of that material. McLaren is named nowhere in forty-nine pages; neither is the Racket, nor channeling, nor any of the eleven coaches, nor the container-makers as a category. Erhard does not appear. What survives is three passing traces: one phrase calling a claim in the Way of Ripening "Landmark-inflected," Joe Hudson's name in the lineage list, and Authentic Relating in a list of influences in the note on sources. The five rooms reach the executed prose as a footnote.

Two readings fit the evidence and the vault holds both. The abridged edition may predate the treatment's second edition, executing the arc as it stood before the rooms were added — the treatment's own phrasing at the Alphabet of the Soul, that McLaren "joins him in this edition," is consistent with her being new there. Or the edition may postdate it and have dropped the rooms as part of abridging, which its self-description as an abridgement would license. No document in `sources/` dates either against the other.

The consequence is a qualification on what has been drafted. The arc's *sequence* has been executed once. The treatment's second-edition *content* has not been executed at all, at any length, in any document the vault holds.

## Substantiation

- The treatment states the addition and its scope: "five rooms have been walked and their furniture carried in… Everything from the first edition is preserved and re-set; the new material is rebalanced in, not poured over" (arc-gloss: the five rooms). Its fourth overlay is built entirely on the five rooms agreeing (arc-gloss: overlay four).
- Counts across the executed text, by name: McLaren 0, Erhard 0, Racket 0, channeling 0, container-maker 0, Katie 0, Robbins 0, Colonna 0, Wineland 0, Deida 0, Hübl 0, Crone 0, Michael Singer 0, Circling 0.
- The three surviving traces, in full: "This is also where the Landmark-inflected claim of this book becomes most fully visible: you are already complete" (abridged ch10); "Joe Hudson" in the lineage list (abridged: the lineage); and "Authentic Relating" among the practices named as influences on the Stance and the Kata (abridged: a note on sources).
- The absence is not explained by abridgement alone in at least one case. The treatment makes McLaren's grief teaching load-bearing — grief structurally requires witness and ceremony, which the corpus carries at [[grief cannot complete alone]] — and the abridged edition states the same requirement in its own vocabulary in the Three Loops and the Afterglow (abridged ch7, ch12) without attributing it. An abridgement that kept the claim and dropped the attribution is a different act from one that never had the attribution.
- The chapters where the treatment says the rooms dock are the chapters to check first in any reconciliation: the Alphabet of the Soul (McLaren beside Gendlin), the Molecules of Experience (the Racket added to the catalogue), the Three Loops (the coaches sorted by cylinder), the Third Entity (the facilitation craft seated), and the Engineer's Eye (the five theories as swappable lenses).
