# sorting a lineage by load turns it toward argument

On 2026-08-02 the author declined a drift the vault had developed on its own: "ranking convergences by evidential weight, describing what the book 'can safely lean on', reading a thinker's refusal as a counterweight that strengthens a position. That is the presupposed righteousness the author is declining" (`ops/methodology/authorial stance.md`). The convergence is company rather than proof, and the vault postulates no efficacy.

The instrument received on 2026-08-05 is built out of exactly that. It prices adversaries by damage — "Value is proportional to damage; a comfortable adversary is decoration" — calls independent arrival "evidence rather than support," says the adversary tier "decides whether the book is an argument or an aesthetic," and holds that "a thesis with no adversaries is a mood." Those are not incidental phrasings; they are the instrument's operating logic, and without them the sort has no criterion.

The author resolved the collision by scope rather than by choosing a side. The sort allocates reading — which cell to read next, which absence would change the book if filled — and the stance continues to govern everything the vault says. A load value never appears in a claim's prose as a reason a claim is true.

That resolution holds and is recorded as the treatment. What it does not do is dissolve the pressure, which is why this stays a tension rather than becoming a decision. The two frames answer different questions and the questions touch: *what should I read next* and *what does this book rest on* are separated by one step, and an instrument that answers the first well makes the second feel answered too.

## Substantiation

- The stance directive is quoted from `ops/methodology/authorial stance.md`, issued 2026-08-02 and unchanged; the grid's sentences are at `mesh grid: how to read the pyramid`, `tier 2` and `tier 3`. The author's resolution is recorded as a row in `ops/decisions.md` and operationalized in `ops/methodology/thesis-bearing and company.md`.
- The tension is `treated` rather than `live` because a treatment is under trial and stated: the scope separation, plus the rule that the grid's register is quoted where it is the finding and never adopted as the vault's voice. Whether the separation holds under use is what the trial tests.
- The pressure has a specific mechanism, and naming it is what makes this a tension rather than a worry. The stance's failure mode is that an apparatus built to hold claims will begin to defend them, and that "the drift is invisible because each individual sentence looks like rigor." A load axis is an apparatus whose every cell is a statement about how much a claim can bear. The drift would not announce itself; it would look like accuracy.
- Two structural facts keep the pressure asymmetric rather than balanced. The stance is the author's directive and the sort is a consulted work he cosigns at 65–75%, so where they conflict without his adjudication the directive wins. And the sort's own output is mostly absences, which are questions, while the stance's concern is mostly with assertions — so the instrument does its intended work almost entirely in the register the stance does not govern.
- The place they will actually collide is narrower than the whole vault and can be named in advance: the passages where the book states what its foundations are. A sentence like *this claim has three adversaries and one independent arrival* is a reading report; the same sentence placed in the manuscript is an argument from evidential weight. The boundary is the manuscript's edge, and the author is the one who crosses it.
