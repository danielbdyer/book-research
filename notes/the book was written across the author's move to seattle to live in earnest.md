# the book was written across the author's move to seattle to live in earnest

The author states the frame himself: "It is my journey to live in Seattle that has had me show up and try to live in earnest in my own time and way and to take the driver's seat in my own life" (author statement, 2026-08-03). The book's composition spans exactly that arc, and the record dates its stations.

Behind the move sits Houston, where the author trained as an Authentic Relating facilitator and held a facilitation community — "the people who knew Danny-as-facilitator," in the record's phrase for what was left. The Seattle rebuild is then documented step by step: an April 2025 circling event attended with the stakes named in his own words, "wanting to get involved in Seattle's intentional-relational community" — eight days after the seed's pages surfaced, so the rebuild and the book begin together; an August 2025 commissioned map of the city's relational ecology; and by May 2026, hosting at a scale of thirty-five, from his own script of five agreements, trio rotations with a witness role, and a closing circle (provenance reconstruction round two §2).

The observation this note exists to hold: the book and the move share one shape. The corpus's law is that transformation is enacted participation inside a held container — nothing transforms from the audience — and the years of the book's writing are the years its author left the audience of his own life: crossed the country, rebuilt the container-practice from nothing in a new city, and put himself back in rooms as the one holding them. The book is not a record of that move. It is the same move, made again in another medium, by someone who had evidence it worked because he was standing in it.

## Substantiation

- The author's 2026-08-03 statement supplies the frame and the phrase; the Houston training is his testimony of the same date, closing a gap the provenance work had twice named open.
- The rebuild's dates are the record's (provenance reconstruction round two §2); the coincidence of the circling entry with the seed's surfacing is exact in the record and is noted here as a coincidence of season, not claimed as causation.
- The lived warrant runs deeper than the move: the Landmark Forum in 2017 is where the participation law was first undergone ([[the central correspondence was lived nine years before it was assembled]]), so Seattle is the second enactment, at the scale of a whole life.
