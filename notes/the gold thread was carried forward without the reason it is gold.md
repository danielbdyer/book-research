# the gold thread was carried forward without the reason it is gold

The seed gives the figure a reason, and the reason is a colour change: "The thread — first rainbow, now gold — never stopped moving" (mythic-os: page 12B). Gold is what a rainbow thread becomes when it stops passing through another person's needle — "Not a rainbow thread through someone else's eye — but gold, because you are the seamstress now" (mythic-os: page 12A). The colour is the whole argument, compressed: a thread carried through someone else's eye against a thread held in one's own hand.

Every later document keeps the figure and drops the explanation. The atlas states it as a correspondence — "The Gold Thread ↔ the continuity loop ↔ sūtra means thread" (atlas §X.5c) — and the walked companion, the treatment, and the executed draft follow. The word *rainbow* appears in no document but the seed. None of the four descriptive sources says why the thread is gold rather than any other colour, and the executed draft's first chapter plants the image without the colour at all, as "the felt sense of continuity that runs underneath the social self" (abridged ch1). This is a revision relation with a name: the figure is **carried unchanged into** the later corpus, and its rationale is **dropped at** the same point.

What makes the omission worth recording is that the corpus is deliberate about it. The plainest telling instructs the reader to watch the figure — "Images planted in the first chapter — you'll notice a gold thread almost immediately — are tended across the whole arc" — and then lists it last among the things it is withholding on purpose: "**Left in the tin.** What I'm leaving out on purpose, so there's something to find… And the gold thread" (first-telling: how it's built; first-telling: left in the tin). The book's most carefully withheld secret is the one figure whose provenance the vault can now trace to the bottom.

## Substantiation

- The distribution is countable. *Rainbow*, *seamstress*, and *loom* occur only in the seed; *braid* and the whole colour layer (purple, pink, yellow, "carried color into someone else's tapestry") have no successor anywhere. What travels outward is the word *thread* alone.
- The public replacement is an etymology supplied by the collaborator in July 2026 and received as a discovery rather than a construction: "sūtra means thread… One image doing ontology and codicology at once," which the walked companion describes as feeling "planted, though no one planted it" (walked IX). A private reason was not argued away; it was overwritten by a better-travelled one.
- One further element of the book originates in the same passage and is recorded here because it is the seed's largest surviving export: the two words the design reserves for the book's last line. "The golden thread is you. **Welcome home**" (mythic-os: page 12B) precedes the treatment's "Last words already found… Welcome home" (arc-gloss: epilogue).
- The vault takes no view on whether the omission should be repaired. The reason is now recoverable ([[the gold thread is the rainbow thread re-owned]]) and whether a reader is told it is the author's decision — the material is personal, and `ops/methodology/personal provenance.md` governs it.
