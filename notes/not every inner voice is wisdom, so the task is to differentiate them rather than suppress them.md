# not every inner voice is wisdom, so the task is to differentiate them rather than suppress them

The reframe conversation holds that the voices a person hears within themselves are not uniformly wise, and it draws a practical consequence. Because some inner voices carry wisdom and others do not, the task self-facilitation sets is discrimination — telling the voices apart and discerning which to heed — rather than suppression, the wholesale silencing of them. Suppression fails on two counts the claim implies: it silences the wise voices along with the rest, and it treats the inner voices as an enemy to be defeated rather than a field to be read. Differentiation instead keeps all the voices audible and sorts them, which continues the book's diagnostic habit of reading what is present layer by layer rather than overriding it, recorded at [[the diagnostic method reads each structural layer with its own question]]. The posture also matches the book's manner toward a protective inner structure, which it meets with a bow rather than a fight, recorded at [[the gatekeeper is met with a bow never a fight]]: a voice that is differentiated is heard, and a voice that is suppressed is fought.
