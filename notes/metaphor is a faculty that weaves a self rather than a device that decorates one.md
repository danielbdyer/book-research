# metaphor is a faculty that weaves a self rather than a device that decorates one

The gesture asserts that metaphor is a faculty of self-construction: a person becomes coherent partly through the figures they reach for. Everywhere else the corpus treats metaphor as the book's own instrument — the image system at atlas §X.5 — which is a claim about how the book works on a reader, not how a reader works on themselves. The gesture is a contents entry at `mythic-os: contents`; the nearest written material is the thread prose at `mythic-os: page 12B`.
