# remembering and forgetting name one movement back to the ground you never left

The book's founding word is aletheia, un-forgetting — and its strangest company is a tradition that reaches the same ground by the opposite verb, calling the work forgetting. The gift to the reader is that the destination does not depend on which door you use. Whether your temperament reaches home by remembering — gathering back what was lost — or by forgetting — letting fall the self and its distinctions until only the ground remains — you arrive at the same place, the always-present you never actually departed. Its use to the writing is to turn an apparent embarrassment for a book built on un-forgetting into a deepening, and to welcome readers whose path is subtractive rather than mnemonic.

One caution the pairing must now carry, established by reading Reinhard May's textual case: Heidegger and the Zhuangzi are not a no-contact convergence. Heidegger demonstrably read Martin Buber's 1910 German Zhuangzi — the *zuowang* passage included — consulting it by 1930 (Petzet's report, in May's *Heidegger's Hidden Sources*). So the resonance between his un-forgetting and Daoist forgetting is company reached through a documented channel, not two lineages arriving apart. What survives independently of who read whom is the structural point below.

## Substantiation

- Zhuangzi, "sitting in forgetting" (*zuowang*), ch. 6, in Legge's public-domain translation: "My connexion with the body and its parts is dissolved; my perceptive organs are discarded. Thus leaving my material form, and bidding farewell to my knowledge, I am become one with the Great Pervader. This I call sitting and forgetting all things." The vault's earlier wording was a conflation of Graham ("part from the body and expel knowledge") and Watson ("the Great Thoroughfare") passed as one quotation; it is replaced here with a single attributed translation. Paired with Daodejing 40: "The movement of the Tao / By contraries proceeds" (Legge); 反者道之動, *fǎn zhě dào zhī dòng*, "returning is the movement of the Way."
- Heidegger's side, stated precisely: *Being and Time* §44 grounds truth as un-*concealment* — "Being-true ('truth') means Being-uncovering," aletheia read as "making manifest that which in some sense lies hidden" (§7) — not un-*forgetting*. The reading of aletheia through *lēthē* as the river of oblivion belongs to the later Heidegger (the Parmenides lectures, GA 54), which is copyright-gated and unread here, so the note's "un-forgetting" verb is grounded as un-concealment and owes the lēthē gloss a Parmenides-lectures citation.
- First-principles bridge — the part that survives the contact flag: if the obstacle to the already-present is a false overlay, then removing it can be described either as *remembering* what the overlay hid or as *forgetting* the overlay itself — one event, narrated from the two things it changes. The verbs disagree only about which object they take. This is stated as illumination rather than as an independent-arrival convergence, since the two named witnesses have a documented channel between them.
- Standing: privated — the Daoist poles read at primary, the Heidegger side grounded at §44 with the lēthē gloss owed, and the Heidegger–Zhuangzi pairing held as influence-suspect. The record for the contact finding is `archive/reading — reinhard may, heidegger's hidden sources (1996).md`.
