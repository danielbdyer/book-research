# the facilitator must become facilitable, permeability without loss of sovereignty

The reframe conversation turns facilitation reflexively upon the facilitator: the facilitator must become facilitable. It sets the requirement between two failures. A facilitator who cannot be influenced becomes rigid; a facilitator influenced by everything becomes diffuse. The mature position is permeability without loss of sovereignty — the capacity to receive, discern, integrate, and change while keeping the locus from which one participates. The claim closes the symmetry the facilitation material has been building: having asked the participant to remain sovereign while being genuinely changed, it asks the same of the facilitator, so that the one who tends conditions is also open to being tended. The conversation places this where it says the book reaches its culmination, the facilitation of facilitation.
