# the book earns trust by a result that can fail outside the room rather than by testimony or citation

The book earns a reader's trust by carrying a result that can fail outside the room rather than by choosing between practitioner testimony and academic citation, because the strata show each of those two options hollow on its own. The commission posed the choice as a binary — present the book's evidence as a facilitator's testimony or as scholarly citation — and the adversary chamber dissolves it. Bare testimony, the report that a passage worked for the one who lived it, is self-report from a maximal-demand setting, which is the response-expectancy artifact secured by the desire to please the one who induced it. Citation does not rescue the testimony: an interaction-science term invoked for the standing it lends rather than for a worked mechanism is, in dig 4's phrase, "jargon in a lab coat", the apparatus of proof performing a depth it has not earned. What the strata leave standing is a third requirement neither option supplies — an external, delayed, other-administered check — so the book earns trust by a felt result the reader can carry out of the reading and find fail, later, before someone who did not induce it.

The verdict relocates the compositional decision rather than answering it in the old terms. The question is no longer whether to speak as a practitioner or as a scholar, but whether the book can specify a result that a reader's own later experience, checked by another, could disconfirm — which is the same external check the felt-shift audit and the second chair both name.
