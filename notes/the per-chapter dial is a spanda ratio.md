# the per-chapter dial is a spanda ratio

One of the book's seven apparatus devices is a per-chapter dial recording the ratio of evocative to informative prose. The atlas identifies the instrument with the source tradition's cosmology: "the book's per-chapter dial is literally a spanda ratio" (atlas §IX.6a) — spanda being the tradition's term for the primordial pulse, the throb of consciousness. The walked document marks the register of the move: "notice how domestically the book employs a cosmological term" (walked IX).

The identification is a production fact with a doctrinal face. Setting a chapter's dial is an editorial decision about prose texture, and in the book's own vocabulary that decision is the tuning of a pulse — the design instrument speaks the metaphysics it regulates. This is the same double-face the title's engine carries (mechanical instrument, theological name), and the same domesticating habit the book's naming doctrine states: the deepest terms put to household work.

## Substantiation

- Atlas §IX.6a, under spanda: "the book's per-chapter dial is literally a spanda ratio (→ §X.7)"; the device itself is enumerated in the apparatus at atlas §X.6.
- Walked IX: "each chapter carries a spanda dial, an evocative-to-informative ratio; the same pulse drives the Haecceity Graph's daily heartbeat, and once read a running playlist."
- The dial's standing as a production instrument rather than a printed feature — and therefore what its absence from a draft does and does not show — is recorded at [[the drafts execute one of the seven designed apparatus devices]].
