# what is recognized cannot be un-recognized

The book promises that its contents are already known and that reading will remember them. The Sacred Risk of Staying Awake states what that promise costs, which no other document in the corpus does. If recognition returns something that was always present, it cannot be returned again to concealment by an act of will. The reader who remembers is not left with an option they did not have before; they are left without an option they did have.

The material names three specific losses that follow. Contact is made with the parts of a person that had stopped asking to be known, which cannot be undone by ceasing to attend to them. The personas that made the reader "lovable but lonely" become visible as personas, which costs their protection. And presence has to be chosen repeatedly rather than once, because a single recognition does not install a state.

The consequence for the book is a matter of consent rather than of tone. A work whose method runs through the reader has to tell the reader what it is going to do, and this is the corpus's only passage in which the promise is stated as a risk the reader is invited to accept rather than as a benefit they are offered. It is the one place where the reading contract is two-sided.

## Substantiation

- The statement: "To read this book fully is to risk remembering what you already know — and no longer being able to unknow it. It is to risk contact with the parts of you that stopped asking to be known. It is to risk shedding the elegant personas that made you lovable but lonely. And it is to risk choosing presence — not once, but again and again, when it would be easier to turn the page quickly and feel accomplished" (mythic-os: page 4).
- The material states its own purpose in disclosing this: "We say this not to frighten you, but to be honest." The passage sits immediately after the fourth wall falls and before the book's deeper material begins, which places it at the point where a contract would be signed.
- The corresponding passages in the other documents are one-sided. The front-matter promise recorded at [[reading is remembering]] states what the reader will gain; the executed draft's reading contract asks for slowness and for the practices to be done and warns only that confusion is information (abridged: how to read this book). Neither states a cost.
- The etymological ground is already in the corpus and supports the claim without stating it. *Aletheia* is constructed as un-forgetting, and Lethe is the river of forgetting (abridged: prologue; atlas §I). A structure named for the undoing of forgetting has no second undoing available.
- The claim appears in no descriptive source: one attestation, in the earliest draft.
