# the primary layer states its own limit on atomization

The system vocabulary decomposes experience into primitives and formulas, and the primary layer — which governs the corpus where the two conflict — carries its own prior ruling on how far decomposition may go. Poem #7: "that elements may not be split / into particles beyond their matter." Poem #11, on the unsolved equation: "the equation has no solution as / the variables continue to churn // when / there is a lovely elegance in the way that / it all works out sometimes isn't there?" Read under the direction rule, the poet set the boundary before the system-builder arrived: analysis is licensed down to the level where matter still holds, and an equation may work out without a solution.
