# the molecule catalogue divides by whether the construct existed before the formula

The chemistry has been treated in this vault as one thing, examined as one thing, and found wanting as one thing. It is not one thing. Sorted by whether the state a formula names already existed under its own name in some other literature, the catalogue falls into four groups, and the groups have completely different standing.

**Donated, with the source named.** The Racket = Complaint + Payoff, both fixed, is Landmark's, and the corpus says so without hedging: "Landmark donates a molecule to the periodic table" (arc-gloss: ch6), "the single sharpest diagnostic this room donates" (arc-gloss: overlay four). Grief's catalyst is McLaren's — "her grief-teaching supplies the chapter's most consequential footnote: some reactions require a catalyst, and the catalyst for grief is a witness" (arc-gloss: ch6). Burnout carries Byung-Chul Han at civilizational scale in the atlas's own cross-reference (atlas §IX.8f).

**Named by the corpus as uncited.** The outward survey identifies two lineages the book runs on and has never credited. The Privation Principle itself: "an ancient patent-holder we've never cited: Aristotle" (space-around: around the chemistry). And Sanctuary: "Bowlby and Ainsworth are, strangely, nowhere in the project… the secure base is Sanctuary with six decades of longitudinal data," with the generalization attached — "internal working models are molecules by another name."

**Attributed nowhere, with the lineage's own technical term reused.** Two, found in this vault on 2026-08-05 and present in no source.

*Foreclosure = Commitment − Defer* is James Marcia's identity status, defined in 1966 as commitment reached without exploration. The executed draft glosses it "You have committed but you have closed all other possibility," which is that construct, and *foreclosure* is Marcia's word.

*Enmeshment = Connection − Sever-capacity* is Salvador Minuchin's, from structural family therapy. The draft prescribes "restore the capacity to Sever, to set difference and boundary, without leaving the connection altogether," which is boundary-making as Minuchin defines it, and *enmeshment* is his word.

Both correspondences are quoted on the book's side and paraphrased on the thinker's, which is the defect `ops/methodology/both halves of a correspondence.md` was written for. Each chair now says so in its own body.

**No lineage found.** Obligation, Possibility, Paralysis, Trust, Stagnation, and the cluster formulas — Lifecycle, Threshold, Surrender. These may have ancestries nobody has looked for; what can be said is that no source names one and no search in this vault has produced one.

## Substantiation

- The sort is what the author's question asked for, and he named its clearest instance himself: more than one of these formulas is pointed at by a tradition the book already seats, Landmark being the obvious one. What the sort adds is that the pattern is much wider than the one case, and that the corpus half-knows it — two of the lineages are named by its own survey under the heading of things never cited.
- **The consequence for the finger-and-moon question is that it splits.** Where a formula compresses a construct that exists independently, that literature establishes the state and the formula translates it, so the moon is there whether or not the notation points well. Where no such construct exists, the formula asserts a state on the strength of the felt shift alone. Those are two different epistemic situations. The catalogue presents them identically, and that is the thing worth fixing.
- **The Racket's ancestry runs three deep and the corpus records all three without connecting them.** Landmark donated it; Bateson's double bind is called "the Racket's deep ancestor and its exculpation: some fixed complaints with fixed payoffs were installed as the only available solution to an impossible demand" (space-around: around the engine); and Kegan's immunity-to-change work is "almost line for line, the Racket with a worksheet," with the survey adding that "that crossover between Kegan and the Landmark room has never been drawn, by us or, as far as I know, by anyone" (space-around: around "insight that doesn't move anything"). One donated molecule with an ancestor, a contemporary twin, and an undrawn crossover is not a system component.
- **The book's own attribution rule is what makes this actionable rather than interesting.** The corpus states it as doctrine — "secrets are for the story, never for the sources" (atlas §IX preamble; first-telling: the offering) — and the vault's provenance rule descends from it ([[methods]], house rule 2). Two formulas use a discipline's technical term for a construct and name nobody. That is a debt with a named creditor, not a stylistic preference, and it is repairable in a clause each.
- Counted against the audit at `ops/formula audit.md`: seven of the fifteen named molecules have identifiable outside provenance. The proportion is the finding's real size and it is neither "the chemistry is borrowed" nor "the chemistry is invented."
- What this does not establish. It does not show that the borrowed constructs are correctly compressed — whether Marcia's foreclosure survives being written as a subtraction is a question for the reading, not for this sort. And it does not rank the four groups; a formula with no lineage is not thereby worse, only differently supported.
