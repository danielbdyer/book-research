# the voice names the move it has just made

When the voice makes a move that could lose the reader — breaking the fourth wall, withholding a definition, writing devotionally where explanation was expected — it names the move immediately, in the reader's own likely words. The objection is voiced by the book before the reader can raise it, which converts suspicion into collusion: the reader's watchfulness has been seen, so it can stand down.

This is the working answer to the failure the corpus worries about elsewhere — that resistance needs somewhere to land. In the earliest draft it lands in the prose itself, one page at a time.

## Substantiation

- The fourth wall, named as it falls: "And we have broken the fourth wall already, haven't we? Yes. That was intentional. Or rather, it was inevitable." (mythic-os: page 2).
- The withheld definition, named: "You may be wondering what this book is, still. You are not wrong to wonder. Wonder is the door." (mythic-os: page 3).
- The register itself, named: "You might think we write to explain — to make sense of the tangle. But here, we write to touch it." (mythic-os: page 5).
- The pattern in each case is three beats: the move, the reader's probable response spoken aloud, and a reframe that makes the response part of the design. The reframe never denies the response; it seats it.
- Checkable: each meta-move in the written chapter is followed within a page by a sentence about that move. The device does not appear in the executed draft's arc, whose fourth wall falls once in the front matter and is not named again.
