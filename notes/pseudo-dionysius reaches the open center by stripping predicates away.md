# pseudo-dionysius reaches the open center by stripping predicates away

The book's open center is reached by removing a concealment rather than by adding a predicate ([[the open center is made possible by the three structures and identical with none of them]]; [[the book reads deep change as uncovering rather than addition]]). Christian apophatic theology reaches it by the same subtraction from an unrelated Neoplatonic lineage: a ladder of negations ascends into a "divine darkness," a knowing that exceeds understanding by un-knowing. A reading of chapters 1 and 5 confirms it at the text. The move is company for both the open center, which the darkness enters by negation, and the privative grammar, since union is approached by stripping away rather than by accretion.

## Substantiation

- The book's half is on record: the three structures hold an open middle they are not, identified with *Lichtung* and *prakasa* (atlas §II).
- The apophatic half is now shown, from the Shrine of Wisdom public-domain translation (the reading record is `archive/reading — pseudo-dionysius, mystical theology 1 and 5.md`). Chapter 1: "leave behind the senses and the operations of the intellect, and all things sensible and intellectual… that thou mayest arise by unknowing towards the union… into the superessential Radiance of the Divine Darkness." Chapter 5's cascade: "He is neither soul nor intellect… nor greatness nor smallness… neither is He darkness nor light… nor can any affirmation or negation be applied to Him." The seated Eckhart's own via negativa, his treatise *On Detachment* (*Von Abgeschiedenheit*), was named but not separately read; the vault holds his *Gelassenheit* via Caputo and his Martha-over-Mary householder move. Read in translation, not the Greek.
- The refusal is kept. The negations terminate in a God beyond being — "the all-perfect and unique Cause of all things" — and the stripping is meant to leave the self behind in union with what is above being, not to send the first person home to a house with no ceiling, the same shape as the recognition tradition's own recorded refusal, recognition without the recognized.
- The disposition is reopened on narrower terms. The apophatic lineage was semi-shelved 2026-08-07 on the ground that disciplined non-resolution is a vault practice rather than a book claim; that pass did not weigh the open-center and privative-grammar angles the reading confirms, so the candidate stands.
