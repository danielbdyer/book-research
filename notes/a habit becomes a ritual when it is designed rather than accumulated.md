# a habit becomes a ritual when it is designed rather than accumulated

The gesture makes design the difference between a habit and a ritual. The four descriptive sources never use the word habit, so the corpus has no account of repetition; the nearest executed material asserts automaticity as a goal without examining it — a form is practised "until the form is no longer practice; it is who one is" (abridged ch14) — and whether an automatic practice can still pass the felt-shift test is unasked.
