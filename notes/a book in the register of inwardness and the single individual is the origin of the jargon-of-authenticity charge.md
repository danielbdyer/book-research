# a book in the register of inwardness and the single individual is the origin of the jargon-of-authenticity charge

The register the book is most tempted to write in is the one its sharpest adversary was built to attack. Theodor Adorno's *The Jargon of Authenticity* takes Kierkegaard as a founding target — Adorno's Habilitation was *Kierkegaard: Construction of the Aesthetic* — and its charge is that words like inwardness, the single individual, and authenticity radiate significance independent of any content they carry. A presence-book written in that register is therefore the paradigm case of what Adorno attacks rather than a bystander to the charge: it stands at the origin of the objection, maximally exposed exactly where its language is most eloquent. The exposure is data for the book to hold rather than a difficulty to soothe, since the words that carry the book's felt weight are the words the adversary names as counterfeit. The finding rests on Adorno's works held as an instrument, unopened this dig and queued in `ops/reading queue.md`.

## Substantiation

- Dig 1 holds Adorno against this site: *The Jargon of Authenticity* "takes Kierkegaard as a founding target," and inwardness, the single individual, and authenticity are "his exhibit-A of words that radiate significance independent of content," so a presence-book in the register is "not a bystander to the charge but its origin" (transmission dig 1, 2026-08-13, remainder — Adorno instrument), marked [S].
- The dig records Adorno's own credential for the aim — the Habilitation *Kierkegaard: Construction of the Aesthetic* (1933) — and holds both works as instruments not opened this dig.
