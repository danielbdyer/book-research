# the atlas was compiled from the arc and gloss treatment

The two largest founding sources state their own provenance, and both name the same document first. The atlas's opening line describes itself as compiled from a list of prior threads, and its colophon repeats the list; the walked document's colophon gives the same list in the same order. The Arc and the Gloss, second edition, heads both lists. That treatment is now held in `sources/` as a document in its own right.

The consequence is a rule about evidence rather than a fact about the book. Where the atlas, the walked document, and the Arc and the Gloss agree, the vault is reading one document three times: once as treatment, and twice as compilation of that treatment. A claim citing all three anchors is not thereby better supported than a claim citing one. The corpus's strongest evidence class is the isomorph — the same structure reached independently in an unconnected medium — and the compilation relation is the precise opposite of that: dependent restatement in a related medium. The vault's other two draft sources, the abridged edition and the mythic operating system edition, are named in neither colophon, so their agreement with the atlas is not dependent in this way.

## Substantiation

- Atlas §0 preamble: the document describes itself as "Compiled from the seventeen trial chapters, The Third Drawer ontology, the Arc & Gloss second-edition treatment, the guild expansion sittings, the coaches thread, the Kashmir Śaiva teaching threads, the temple-builders canon, the molecular ontology, and the structural assessment — cross-woven so the seams show."
- Atlas, colophon: "assembled July 2026 from the living threads — the Arc & Gloss (2nd ed.), the seventeen trial chapters, The Third Drawer, the coaches' constellation, the valley teachings, the twenty-nine-plus-axis, the porch sittings, and the frank assessment."
- Walked, colophon: the same list, prefaced "the prose twin of the Gravitational Atlas, assembled July 2026 from the same living threads."
- The treatment names its own genre in terms that fit an upstream input rather than a finished account: "not a draft, not a plan — a whiteboard from altitude. … The legibility is the deliverable" (arc-gloss: how to hold this).
- The dependence is one-directional and does not reach every claim: material the atlas draws from its other named threads — the trial chapters, The Third Drawer, the coaches' constellation, the valley teachings — is not affected by this relation.
