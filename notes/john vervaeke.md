# john vervaeke

John Vervaeke is a cognitive scientist and philosopher whose work on the meaning crisis treats human intelligence as more than the accumulation of propositions. His central term is relevance realization: the capacity to determine what matters from an effectively unbounded field of possible information, which he connects to meaning-making, wisdom, self-transcendence, and transformative experience. Beneath it he distinguishes four kinds of knowing — propositional (knowing that), procedural (knowing how), perspectival (knowing what is salient here and now), and participatory (knowing through being involved in and co-identifying with an arena). His epistemics come from cognitive science, the field the four-root lens names Root A ([[the four ontological roots organize the interlocutors by trained way of seeing]]); the vault's existing `discipline:` vocabulary records that field as `empirical-science`, the value carried in the field above and already used for the enactivist cognitive scientists de Jaegher and Di Paolo.

## What he carries

Vervaeke names what meaning is. Relevance realization relocates the book's civilizational question: the difficulty is not that information is abundant but that the systems mediating a person's sense of what matters are built to capture attention rather than to cultivate wisdom, so the crisis is a fragmenting of relevance rather than a surplus of information ([[relevance realization is the engine of meaning, and the attention economy fragments relevance rather than multiplying information]]). The diagnostic the author had been circling is a textbook Vervaekian failure mode, stated in the conversation as "The system can increase information while decreasing participation" (reframe conversation, 2026-08-13, line 2135).

His four kinds of knowing refine the book's own binary between an idea understood and a change felt in the body. The fourfold separates knowing *about* something from knowing *through* being involved in it, and it places the book's verification instrument, the felt shift, at the threshold of the fourth kind — the bodily arrival of a not-yet-worded knowing is participatory knowing rather than a proposition accepted ([[there are four kinds of knowing, and the felt shift lives at the edge of the participatory]]). The same boundary states the book's limit on transmission: information can be transmitted where realization cannot ([[knowing about is not knowing through — information can be transmitted, realization cannot]]).

The first function beside the thesis is **independent arrival**. Vervaeke is a cognitive-science witness to knowing-through, reaching the distinction between information and participation from naturalized cognition with no contact with the recognition tradition the book is grounded in. His arrival is evidence that the information-versus-participation boundary is not a private coinage. It does not, however, discharge the book's outstanding measurement debt: the felt shift still rests on one name and cannot be defended by company, and mapping it onto participatory knowing broadens the account without supplying the empirical defense the standard lacks.

## What he would refuse — the naturalism fork, kept open

Vervaeke's refusal is the fork the book must not close, and it is a genuine fork with the book's ground rather than a resonance to be smoothed. He "tends to naturalize these phenomena through cognitive science, philosophy, developmental psychology, contemplative practice, and cultural evolution" (line 2338). The book's deepest ground is the nondual Kashmir Śaiva account, on which consciousness is not a property occurring within reality but fundamental to what reality is ([[the kashmir shaiva tradition underlies the book rather than joining its company]]). The conversation states the distance in both voices and marks it a fork the project "should not resolve": Vervaeke says "Here's how participatory knowing and relevance realization might arise within a naturalistic account of cognition," and the Pratyabhijñā side answers "You're beginning from an assumption that consciousness is something occurring within an already-given world" (lines 2350-2360).

The correspondence the conversation opens with — the word both traditions orbit is recognition — is therefore held as company and not convergence. The conversation says so directly: what the author is developing "sits in a very interesting space between Vervaeke's naturalized account of meaning-making and the Śaiva account of recognition" (line 2072), and the two mean different things by the shared word. The refusal is kept so that Vervaeke survives being cited: he is the naturalist pole, and reading his relevance realization as a witness *to* the recognition ontology would collapse the fork his contribution depends on.

## What the primary reading settled (partial, 2026-08-30)

A partial primary reading was done (record: `archive/reading — the participation law's independent arrivals (von foerster, maturana-varela, garfinkel, schön, de jaegher-di paolo, vervaeke).md`). The 2012 relevance-realization paper was read in full (Grade A): relevance realization is offered as "the criterion of the cognitive," a set of "self-organizing mechanisms" rather than an algorithm ("there cannot be a relevance realization mechanism in any straightforward sense") — so it is the engine of *cognition/intelligence* at the paper's altitude, and the extension to *meaning*, wisdom and self-transcendence belongs to the 2013 wisdom chapter and the lecture series, which is where the vault should cite it. The four kinds of knowing were verified at Grade B (they are in Vervaeke & Ferraro 2013 and the *Awakening* lectures, not in the 2012 paper the `works` line had implied); participatory knowing is a genuine agent-arena co-constitution, "grounded ultimately in your participatory" as the deepest of the four.

Two cautions the reading adds. First, participatory knowing only *rhymes* with the book's felt shift rather than being it: it is a sub-personal, identity-level coupling, not a bodily register, so the felt shift is at most one experiential signature at the perspectival–participatory boundary — the vault's refusal to certify the two as one construct is correct and kept. Second, and bearing on the independence functions above: Vervaeke roots participatory knowing in Heidegger ("from Heidegger... a foundational kind of caring," *Awakening* Ep 32), so unlike von Foerster, Maturana and Varela, Garfinkel, or Wittgenstein he is *not* a witness free of debt to the book's founding pole — he is company for the participation law, not an arrival from outside the lineage, and the "independent arrival" function above should be read with that qualification. The "attention economy fragments relevance" framing remains the vault's own extension of his meaning-crisis diagnosis, not his verified phrasing. Still owed: the 2013 chapter and the *Awakening* series at first hand, which would settle which of the three load functions dominates (Fork 21).
