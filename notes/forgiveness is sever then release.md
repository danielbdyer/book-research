# forgiveness is sever then release

The book holds that sequence is real in its chemistry: some compounds are defined by their order of operations. Forgiveness is the canonical case: the operation runs Sever first and Release second. The first-telling states the failure case in the corpus's words: run in reverse, "releasing before you've ever severed," the compound "curdles into something that looks like peace and isn't" (first-telling: the offering). The mechanism is that the release has nothing severed to let go of; the ordering of two operations carries the book's entire critique of premature spiritual peace.

The corpus generalizes the point as bond order: in this chemistry, sequence is part of a compound's identity, and the reversed sequence is a different compound (walked IV's term for the reversed compound is bypass).

## Substantiation

- First-telling, "The offering," carries the formula and the reversed-order failure case.
- Atlas §IV records it under bond order, alongside isomerism and catalysis as the chemistry's precision terms.
