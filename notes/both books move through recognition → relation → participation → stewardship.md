# both books move through recognition → relation → participation → stewardship

The reframe conversation proposes that both books move through the same four developmental movements: recognition (what is actually happening?), relation (what am I in relationship with?), participation (how am I participating in what is happening?), and stewardship (given that I cannot control the whole field, what conditions am I responsible for creating?). The two books share the sequence and differ only in its endpoint: Book I ends in stewardship of the field, Book II in stewardship of the self. The conversation reads the four movements as a development — from awareness, through the turn from isolated subjectivity toward ecology, to the emergence of agency and sovereignty, to the responsibility for conditions where facilitation begins — and calls the paired sequence the most compact description of the two-book architecture it had found.

The four movements are one of several architectures the conversation generated, offered at a higher level of abstraction than the two loci and the mirrored concerns; the vault holds the arc as a candidate manuscript spine rather than a settled one. The arc matures toward the assumption of responsibility, and in Book I toward the facilitator's disappearance, which is a different shape from the vault's existing psychedelic arc that crescendos to a peak; the two shapes are kept distinct rather than merged.
