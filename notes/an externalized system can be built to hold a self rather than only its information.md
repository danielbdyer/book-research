# an externalized system can be built to hold a self rather than only its information

The gesture proposes that an externalized system — a second brain — can be built as a container in the book's technical sense: a boundary, a permeability, a duration. It is the anatomy's hardest untried case, because the container's contents are explicitly not the person. The corpus names the audience once, "Creators of inner architectures and Second Brains" (mythic-os: book overview), and never enters the territory.
