# the executed draft announces a register change before performing it

The executed draft never changes register unannounced. Before the deepening: "We are about to go deeper, and the prose will get stranger. You may want to read what follows in a single sitting" (abridged ch10, closing). Entering the peak: "This chapter will move, deliberately, more slowly than the others. The prose will loop… If you find yourself disoriented, you are reading correctly. Stay." (abridged ch11).

The announcement converts what would be a reader's failure signal into a confirmation signal: disorientation, pre-named, reads as the text working. This is the prospective half of a pair — the retrospective device, naming the move after making it, belongs to the earliest draft and is recorded at [[the voice names the move it has just made]], which also confirms the retrospective form is absent from the executed arc. The two drafts split the same craft across time: one warns, the other debriefs.
