# traditions are historically accumulated experiments in participation

The reframe conversation offers a way of holding the book's company of traditions that fixes their standing as evidence rather than authority. A tradition, on this reading, is not a doctrine to be believed or a metaphysics to be adopted but a body of practices, distinctions, and modes of attention that a community has tested over generations — an experiment in participation, kept because it went on disclosing something. The consequence is the one the book already practices: the project consults a tradition for what it has found rather than inheriting it for what it asserts, and it asks what becomes visible through a tradition rather than which tradition is correct. This is the front-side statement of the vault's standing rule that a crossover is company and never proof.

## Substantiation

- The phrasing is the collaborator's and is held as a candidate, not credited for what it argues: traditions "are historically accumulated experiments in participation" (reframe conversation, 2026-08-13, transcript line 18614). The seed beneath it is the author's own, who described the method as "collecting other traditions that make an explorable composite of all the loci of overlapping concerns, and then offering the durable perspective I can acquire from it" (reframe conversation, 2026-08-13, transcript line 2975) — a composite of experiments, weighed, not a lineage inherited.
- The claim adds a temporal and empirical framing to the sibling reading that a tradition is a trained standpoint. Where [[a tradition is a cultivated locus of recognition — a trained way of noticing — not merely doctrine]] names a tradition as a lens, this note names it as the accumulated record of what looking through that lens has found — which is what licenses the vault to treat the tradition as evidence.
- The framing carries its own guard forward. Gathering traditions for the concern they share is the very move that manufactures the agreement then reported, so consulting them as evidence must keep the selection-artifact caution ([[the unanimity claim is an artifact of how the lineages were selected]]) rather than reading their overlap as corroboration.
