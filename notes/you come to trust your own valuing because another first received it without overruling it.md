# you come to trust your own valuing because another first received it without overruling it

The reader gains their own compass back — and learns they did not have to wrench it back from anyone. The mark of growth is not defiance of others' opinions but the quiet relocation of "is this right for me?" from out there to in here. And the paradox that dissolves the fear: this homecoming of your own authority is occasioned by being met without judgment, so the relationship did not colonize your judgment — it handed it back. Its use to the writing is to correct "trust yourself" from a preachment of independence into the finding that self-trust is conferred relationally.

## Substantiation

- Rogers grounds the movement at the primary text: Condition 4, unconditional positive regard, is "a caring for the client as a separate person, with permission to have his own feelings, his own experiences," and one of his clients names the effect exactly — the therapist "fostering my possession of my own experience … that I am actually having it: thinking what I think, feeling what I feel, wanting what I want, fearing what I fear: no 'ifs,' 'buts,' or 'not reallys'" (Rogers 1957, "Unconditional Positive Regard"). Being prized without contingency is what lets a person begin to prize their own experiencing; the "locus of evaluation" *term* Rogers develops later (On Becoming a Person, 1961), still to read. Pratyabhijñā: liberation is recognition of a Self and freedom never absent, only unrecognized — the guru and scripture do not install the Self, they remind; the authority was always yours, the other only points (from memory, grounding owed).
- Independent (a Chicago clinic, the Kashmir recognition school). Refusal kept: Rogers holds this as observed process, not engineerable; the Śaiva aim (the Self) is not therapy's functioning self — cited as company across an abyss of aim, the return-of-authority move shared while the destinations stay distinct. Gendlin's felt sense, checked against your own body and never the therapist's, sits as a third witness native to the book.
