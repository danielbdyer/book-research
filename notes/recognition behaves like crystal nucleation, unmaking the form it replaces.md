# recognition behaves like crystal nucleation, unmaking the form it replaces

The drug ritonavir was manufactured for two years in one crystal form until a more stable polymorph appeared spontaneously in a single plant. After that the original form could no longer be produced anywhere, because invisible seed crystals of the new form had spread and any batch they touched crystallized the new way. The panel drew a threefold resonance. Recognition behaves like nucleation: once the more stable configuration is instantiated, the metastable one becomes unmakeable, which sits beside the book's claim that what is recognized cannot be un-recognized ([[what is recognized cannot be un-recognized]]). The seeding requires contact with an actual crystal rather than with its formula, which rhymes with the participation law's insistence that transmission runs through contact ([[transformation is enacted participation inside a held container]]). And a composition-only notation cannot say which polymorph one holds, the limit the completed formula audit already recorded for the molecule catalogue.

This resonance accompanies the recognition claim rather than testing it: crystallography is company for the shape of the irreversibility, not evidence that recognition is irreversible.
