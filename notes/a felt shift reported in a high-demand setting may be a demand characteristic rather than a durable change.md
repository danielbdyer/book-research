# a felt shift reported in a high-demand setting may be a demand characteristic rather than a durable change

The book verifies on the felt shift, and the compliance case holds that a felt shift reported while reading is exactly the outcome a high-demand setting produces whether or not anything was transmitted. The case runs in three tiers the dig separates. At the level of mechanism, Kirsch's response expectancy holds that the anticipation of a nonvolitional response generates the corresponding experience, self-confirmingly and sufficiently, so a reader who expects a shift has one and the content is inert. At the level of situation, Orne's demand characteristics hold that a subject who infers what the setting wants supplies it — and reading an admired author's transformational book is a maximal-demand setting, cueing that a shift belongs here. At the level of controls, the neighboring literature (meditation, self-help, large-group awareness training) rarely runs active controls, blinding, or out-of-situation follow-up, and readers self-select in crisis, so improvement is predicted by regression to the mean even with no effect. None of the three is correctable from inside a book, which can never run its own blind control.

The tension is live rather than fatal because the attack has a stated boundary: it accounts for the shift's origin and not its durability, which is recorded separately. What it removes is the felt shift's standing as self-sufficient evidence; what it demands in exchange is a check the setting cannot supply.

## Substantiation

- Orne defined the operative term as "the totality of cues which convey an experimental hypothesis to the subject" (Orne 1962; recorded at [S] in transmission dig 6, the primary unopened). Reproduced as the one short quotation this note carries; the demand-characteristic construct is his coinage and the concern is that a felt result producible by the setting alone leaves the content unproven.
- The depth is thin and stated: dig 6 reached no clean primary read of Orne or Kirsch, so this note stands on scholarly knowledge and review snippets and is marked `nascent`. The reading that would harden or blunt it is whether response-expectancy effects persist after the inducing situation is removed (dig 6, READINGS TO QUEUE item 3) — durability is the line between artifact and change.
- The suppression half of the situation tier has its own note: shame and the desire to please suppress the disconfirming report, so the absence of a negative report is not evidence of its absence ([[shame and the desire to please suppress the reports that would disconfirm a transformational setting's benefit]]).
- The treatment under trial is the survivability catalog's second line: the felt-shift standard is keepable only paired with an external, later, other-verified check. This is the same second person the contemplative traditions and Gendlin's listener already install, arriving here from the attack's own logic rather than from the book's lineage ([[the second chair is the verification standard's own requirement]]).
