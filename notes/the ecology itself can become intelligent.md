# the ecology itself can become intelligent

The claim generalizes the doctrine that the field between participants does work, extending it to intelligence. Intelligence need not be located only inside a single system; something intelligent can happen between participants, so the ecology of relations can itself become intelligent. The conversation lists the pairings it has in mind — human and AI, teacher and student, facilitator and participant, tradition and contemporary experience, person and text, self and environment — each a relation in which the intelligence belongs to the between rather than to either party. The conversation names this "relational intelligence," a candidate handle for the concern. It draws two consequences: for the vault's own data model, that representing only the nodes and not the relations would miss the phenomenon; and against the central danger, that the inverse possibility is a relational intelligence which increases human participation rather than replacing it. The claim is the conversation's model-assisted synthesis, held as candidate and kept nascent until the writing reaches for it.
