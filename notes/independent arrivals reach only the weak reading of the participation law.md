# independent arrivals reach only the weak reading of the participation law

The participation law's independence problem has a second face, and the six evaluations that solve the first face reveal it together. Every thinker who reached the law owing nothing to either of the book's two founding traditions refuses the same two upgrades of it. Michael Polanyi holds that "Indwelling is asymmetric: the knower is changed by dwelling in the particulars, and the crystal is not changed by being known." Heinz von Foerster "would refuse the ontological upgrade" because his claim is "epistemological and formal… not that reality is participatory." Harold Garfinkel "would refuse the transposition to the interior," being "pointedly uninterested in mental states." Donald Schön's practitioner converses "with a material… outside the practitioner." Ludwig Wittgenstein "would refuse the ontological reading flatly" and treats participation as a feature of grammar that dissolves a false problem rather than as a truth being disclosed. And De Jaegher and Di Paolo "would refuse the third thing as an entity" and make a claim about something happening rather than about something that is there.

So on the current evidence the book faces an exact choice, and each half of it is bought at the cost of the other. The thinkers whose arrivals turn the participation law into a finding rather than a position inherited from a lineage arrive only at its weak reading, which is epistemic and exterior. Its strong reading, in which participation is interior and ontological and the other party is a real participant, is held only by thinkers who fail the independence test. The convergence the book puts at its center can therefore assert independence honestly or assert strength honestly, and deciding which one to assert is a decision about what the book claims.

## Substantiation

- The six refusals are quoted from the evaluations written on 2026-08-05, each marked there as derived from works about the thinker rather than read in the thinker's own work. None of the six primary works has been read here, so this claim carries that standing forward and sharpens the question each queued reading has to answer.
- The independence test failed once before, and that failure is recorded at [[the convergence's independence fails where three of its names share one teacher]], which finds that three of the four thinkers positioned around the book's founding convergence studied with, translated, or built against Heidegger. These six thinkers pass the test, which is what makes their shared refusal decisive rather than incidental.
- Every arrival at the strong reading is in conversation with Heidegger, and that concentration is recorded in the crossing of discipline against what each thinker carries at `scripts/queries/lineage-sort.sh`. What this claim adds is the exchange that concentration produces.

**The count of genuinely independent arrivals was narrowed on 2026-08-06, during round three of the blind repeat pass.** Two of the six arrivals share an ancestor. The scholarship on Polanyi's epistemology records Gestalt perception psychology as a main inspiration behind tacit knowing, and Merleau-Ponty's phenomenology is documented as drawing on the same Gestalt theorists, so those two reached the law from one common source rather than separately; the mesh grid, a document the author supplied (`provenance/the mesh grid.pdf`) that sorts the book's thinkers by what each carries and where each got their epistemics, counts thinkers who share a teacher as a family resemblance rather than as two arrivals. Schön's knowing-in-action is documented as resting on Polanyi's tacit knowledge, so Schön and Polanyi are one arrival counted twice. The number of genuinely independent arrivals therefore falls from six to roughly three. What this note states — that the arrivals which pass the independence test refuse both upgrades — holds at either number, because it does not depend on how many there are. These findings were checked against published literature in a web research round, and the record of that round is `archive/research — counterpoint round background.md`, round three, which holds the citations and what each was checked for.
