# steven wasserstrom

Steven Wasserstrom's study of the Eranos circle — Scholem, Eliade and Corbin, meeting annually in Switzerland to compare the world's mystical traditions — argues that what the circle produced was not comparison but a new religiosity wearing scholarship's clothes. Its method took the visionary and symbolic content of each tradition as the real subject and treated law, ritual obligation, community and political history as husk. The result was a set of traditions rendered as inner experience, freed from anything a person would have to submit to, and available to a European reader who owed them nothing.

The charge is not that the scholars were careless. It is that the abstraction was the method, that it produced something with religious force of its own, and that the traditions being compared could not have recognized themselves in it.

## What he would refuse

Derived rather than sourced; *Religion after Religion* has not been read in this vault.

Wasserstrom would refuse the defence from sincerity. His argument does not turn on the Eranos scholars' motives, which were serious, and it is not answered by intending well — a point the vault already holds in another voice, since gestural subversion and the insufficiency of sincerity are exactly what [[andrea jain]] was seated for.

He would also refuse a use of himself as a rule about borrowing in general. His case is historically specific and its force comes from the details of what Eranos did; deployed as a general prohibition it becomes the kind of abstraction he was objecting to.

And he has his own critics, which the evaluation has to hold rather than suppress: the book was contested on its handling of Scholem in particular, and on whether the political reading it makes is warranted by the evidence. An adversary quoted without their own vulnerabilities is not being quoted at full strength.

## What the evaluation still requires

*Religion after Religion*, with two questions. Whether the charge is aimed at comparison as such or at a particular comparative method, since the book is doing comparison and needs to know which. And what, if anything, the study suggests would have satisfied it — because the author's stated responsibility is the road into the valley rather than abstention, and a critique with no admissible version of the thing it criticizes cannot help build one.
