# j l moreno

Jacob Levy Moreno was a psychiatrist who built psychodrama, sociometry, and spontaneity training, and who coined much of the vocabulary the facilitated room still runs on — the warm-up, the encounter, the here-and-now. He is present in this vault as an absence rather than as an excavated figure: Dig VII surfaced him not as one of its strata but as the ancestor its roster had left out, and the cross-dig synthesis recorded the omission as a finding in its own right, that the region's own genealogy was returned incomplete (Dig 0, §5.5). No dig opened a Moreno primary, so what the vault holds is the shape of the gap and the reason it matters, not a reading.

The reason it matters is a correction to a lineage claim. Dig VII's play strata trace the facilitated room to Viola Spolin's theatre games by way of Neva Boyd's settlement-house recreation, and flag that presenting that descent as *the* historical root of the facilitated room is overstated. Moreno coined "spontaneity" and the "warm-up" before Spolin's method took shape, and psychodrama, Lewin's T-groups, and Freirean adult education are roots of equal or earlier standing. The room the author came from has more than one ancestor, and the coiner of its core terms is not on the roster.

## Why the sort wants him

The book's practice curriculum is the author's own facilitation craft scaled to one reader, so the ancestry of that craft is not incidental to the book ([[the book's practices are facilitation moves scaled to one reader]]). Moreno is a candidate for the missing head of that lineage. His warm-up is a named technique for the very thing the book's entry movement attempts — building the conditions for spontaneity before anything is asked — and his insistence that the practitioner works inside the encounter rather than upon it is the stance the vault already states as its own ([[there is no empty room, the facilitator is inside the field, not outside]], [[facilitation is stewardship of conditions]]). He also stands beside a figure the vault already seats, Kurt Lewin, as a co-founder of the group methods the facilitated room descends from ([[kurt lewin]]).

His standing is precedent rather than thesis. Like the other precedents the vault reads, he is consulted for what became of the practice he made rather than for a doctrine he holds, and the seat taxonomy reads a precedent for its outcome ([[the nearest prior attempts are case histories rather than interlocutors]]).

## What he would refuse

There is nothing read to refuse yet: the provenance is the synthesis's absence-finding at [I], reconstructed from Dig VII's premise flag, with no Moreno primary opened. The likely refusal, stated as a hypothesis for the reading to test, is the same one the whole play lineage raises: Moreno's methods are irreducibly co-present and group-based — psychodrama needs a protagonist, auxiliaries, a director, and an audience in one room, and the warm-up warms up a group. A book read alone has none of that. Whether his spontaneity training carried a standard of felt transformation, or only of social spontaneity and adaptability as Boyd's did, is exactly what the vault does not yet know and must not assert.

## What his medium might contribute

Moreno's possible contribution to the book's medium is the earliest worked technique for the transition the book's opening has to manage: how a room, or a reader, is brought from ordinary readiness into the state where the real work becomes possible. The warm-up names that transition as a discipline rather than an accident. Whether any of it survives translation from a live group to a solitary page is unknown, and until the scoping read is done the contribution is a conjecture the note is honest to label as one.

## What the evaluation requires

A scoping read of *Who Shall Survive?* (1934) and *Psychodrama, Volume I* (1946), queued in `ops/reading queue.md`, with the question the synthesis set: whether Moreno's spontaneity training and warm-up hold a felt-shift standard of transformation or only a standard of social spontaneity — which decides whether he is the missing head of the book's facilitation lineage or one more co-present method that does not port. This is the one figure among the digs' surfaced ancestors whom no dig evaluated, so the evaluation has not begun rather than merely stalled.
