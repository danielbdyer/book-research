# how much structure makes recognition possible without determining what is recognized

The conversation names, as one of its deepest practical questions, how much structure is enough to make recognition possible without determining what gets recognized. It is the facilitation-altitude form of the question the author calls the question of his life: structure has to do enough to make an encounter possible and not so much that it dictates the encounter's content. The conversation marks the question as the hinge several concerns turn on at once — constraint and its productive reading, facilitation craft, the scripted question-forms of Clean Language, pedagogy, the book's own design, the record's architecture, and the pattern of interaction with an AI. It stays open because its content forbids a general answer: any fixed quantity of structure that guaranteed recognition would be determining what is recognized, which is the failure the question exists to avoid.
