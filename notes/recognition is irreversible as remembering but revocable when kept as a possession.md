# recognition is irreversible as remembering but revocable when kept as a possession

The book promises that what is recognized cannot be un-recognized: recognition undoes a concealment, and a structure named for the undoing of forgetting has no second undoing available ([[what is recognized cannot be un-recognized]]). Two lineages reached with no contact hand that promise its limiting clause. Zen refuses the self-certifying experience and refuses it even after enlightenment: "One who becomes attached to what he or she realizes through satori is also still lingering in the world of makyo" (Kapleau, Yasutani's lectures; [[zen's makyo instruction refuses the self-certifying experience]]). The author's own record holds the same structure from the far side: a witness kept as proof "forecloses the pursuit," so keeping the thing as proof guarantees the thing proven is never tested live ([[a witness kept as proof forecloses what it proves]]).

Composed, the two are one finding — a realization or a witness held as a possession forecloses what it realizes — and applied to the irreversibility promise they split it in two. Recognition as remembering is irreversible, exactly as the book says. Recognition kept as a possession — a state clung to, a proof retained, an attainment kept for its own sake — is revocable, and by both lineages' account it does not merely fade but degrades into the very concealment it undid.

The value of the clause is that it keeps the promise from over-claiming. The book can keep its strongest sentence, that the reading cannot be unremembered, without implying that a reader who grips the recognition has thereby secured it; the gripping is the one move that loses it.

## Substantiation

- Both halves of the underlying finding are quotable from held material, per `ops/methodology/both halves of a correspondence.md`. Zen's side is Yasutani's, quoted above and at [[zen's makyo instruction refuses the self-certifying experience]], with its precedent that even the founder-figures' realizations were refused by their teachers. The author's side is his own record, quoted at [[a witness kept as proof forecloses what it proves]] under `ops/methodology/personal provenance.md`, where the structure — custody rather than witness, the proof foreclosing the live test — is stated without the personal specifics being retold here.
- The two are independent arrivals in the vault's strict sense: a Zen instruction on attachment to satori and a contemporary reconstruction of one man's fixation, no shared source, one structure. Held as company rather than corroboration, per `ops/methodology/authorial stance.md`.
- The promise this limits is stated in the earliest draft alone: "To read this book fully is to risk remembering what you already know — and no longer being able to unknow it" (mythic-os: page 4), recorded at [[what is recognized cannot be un-recognized]]. The clause is the cost's own boundary: the risk is real for the remembering and does not extend to a possession made of it, which was never the irreversible thing.
- The distinction the clause turns on — a state that arrives against a station that is built — is the vault's bypass guard at [[state and station were distinguished a millennium before the lattice named them]], and it names why a possession is revocable where a recognition is not: a possession is a state held, and states do not hold.
