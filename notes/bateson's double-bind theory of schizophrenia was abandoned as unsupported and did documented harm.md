# bateson's double-bind theory of schizophrenia was abandoned as unsupported and did documented harm

The Bateson the book most needs comes attached to a failure the book must carry honestly. His double-bind account of the origin of schizophrenia — the claim that a repeated no-win communicative trap produces the disorder — is empirically unsupported and was abandoned by the field, and Bateson himself later backed away from the causal claim. Its schizophrenogenic-mother corollary, which located the cause in a parent, did documented harm to families before it was discarded. The damage matters to the book beyond due diligence, because it lands on the exact instrument the book borrows: the transformation the book's arc arrives at is Learning III, and Learning III is the level Bateson could not measure, could not verify, and warned was sometimes pathogenic. The method he offers at the book's altitude is the one his own record shows he could neither validate nor safely recommend.

The finding therefore bounds the borrowing twice. It requires the book to take Bateson's ladder without his etiology, and it requires the book to hear his safety warning about Learning III as coming from a man whose confident causal theory at the same altitude proved wrong.

## Substantiation

- Derived from scholarship: the 1956 paper and its later qualifications were not opened by transmission dig 4, and the abandonment and harm are reported at secondary depth.
