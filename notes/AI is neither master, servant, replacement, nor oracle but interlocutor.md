# AI is neither master, servant, replacement, nor oracle but interlocutor

The claim rejects four postures a person can take toward AI and proposes a fifth. AI is not a master that commands, not a servant that merely executes, not a replacement that stands in for the person, and not an oracle that delivers authoritative answers; it is an interlocutor, one participant among others in the ecology, something a person thinks and speaks with. The decisive consequence the conversation draws is that the quality of the relationship, rather than the capability of the tool, determines what emerges from the exchange. This places the stance toward AI, not AI's intelligence, at the center of the question. "Interlocutor" is the conversation's candidate handle for that stance, and the vault already carries kindred relocations of AI into the relational field — the account that calls a language model a violin rather than an oracle, and the image of the ink that listens. The claim is the conversation's model-assisted synthesis, held as candidate and kept nascent until the writing reaches for it.

## Substantiation

- The conversation states the seating and the four refusals in a single passage: "AI therefore becomes one participant within the ecology. Not: master. Not: servant. Not: replacement. Not: oracle. But: interlocutor. And the quality of the relationship determines what emerges" (reframe conversation, 2026-08-13: the ecology of AI).
