# the participation law has a witness who came to it through crystallography

The book's first law is that the instrument of knowing is not separable from what it knows ([[transformation is enacted participation inside a held container]]). Every thinker the corpus reports arriving at it is a phenomenologist, a contemplative, or a facilitator, which means the law is currently supported entirely from inside the traditions that hold it.

Michael Polanyi is not. A physical chemist who worked in crystallography before turning to the philosophy of science, he arrived at the personal participation of the knower in all acts of knowing from inside the account of how scientific discovery actually happens. His word for it is *indwelling*: the knower dwells in the particulars in order to attend from them to the thing known. His formulation of the underlying claim — we can know more than we can tell — is the tacit dimension, and the subsidiary-to-focal structure it rests on is a description of attention that does one thing while resting on another.

That last structure is the book's basic posture described by someone who came at it through a different door. The mesh grid calls him "the most valuable missing name in the project," and states what he would buy: "If C1 has one non-continental witness, it should be him" (`mesh grid: tier 3`).

## Substantiation

- Stated on the grid's authority and `nascent`: neither *Personal Knowledge* nor *The Tacit Dimension* has been read in this vault. Polanyi already sits in `ops/queue/candidates.md` under a different description — as support for the felt sense, "the felt sense as the engine of all expertise" — and the sort's correction is that the more valuable seat is elsewhere. He is the first name in the sort's third-ranked hole.
- What makes the reseating a real change rather than a relabel: as support for the felt sense he would be one more voice agreeing with Gendlin, both working the same territory. As a witness for the participation law he is the one name in the set who could not have got there by reading anyone the book stands on, which is the property the sort finds the whole company lacking ([[the convergence's independence fails where three of its names share one teacher]]).
- The discipline is one of three the sort finds near-empty across every load, and the grid's reading of that pattern is that it describes the reading done so far rather than the field: "That is not a coincidence. It is the shape of the reading that has happened so far" (`mesh grid: the family-level read`).
- A refusal to establish when the reading is done, because it is the thing most likely to be assumed rather than checked: Polanyi's argument is a defense of scientific knowledge, not a critique of it. He held that personal participation is what makes objectivity achievable rather than what makes it impossible, and a use of him that treats participation as licensing the dissolution of the knower's accountability inverts his position. That matters here because the objection this book has never met runs the other way — that participation is contamination ([[the participation law has no opponent and its oldest objection is that participation is contamination]]) — and Polanyi is standing between the two, which is precisely why he is worth having.
