# joe hudson

The corpus gives Hudson one position and returns to it from six directions. His theory of where suffering lives is that it is a feeling being resisted, and the work is to welcome it all the way through until it completes — "every feeling, fully welcomed, completes itself" (arc-gloss: ch7). Of the five theories the corpus surveys among its eleven contemporary teachers, that one is his alone.

What follows from holding it alone is the part that matters. **The book's referral network routes readers to him by name.** Walked IX states the routing directly: "A somatic misfire wants Hudson, not Byron Katie." That is a recommendation with a consequence — a reader in a particular kind of difficulty is told where to go — and the corpus makes it on the strength of one man's method. Beside it, five further functions: he is dual-seated among the container-makers as well as the coaches (atlas §IX.8); his VIEW frame is one of the dozen vocabularies in the convergence table for the basic posture (arc-gloss: ch13); his welcome runs the temporal loop in the practice material (arc-gloss: ch14); at the peak, "Hudson's welcome and Singer's watcher are, at the peak, not techniques but survival skills"; and the first telling reaches for him when it explains the resisted feeling to a friend.

**Both sorts filed him as company.** The mesh grid lists him in Tier 5 — "worth hearing about presence and the Stance, without holding weight" — and then again in its coaches section, where "individually most of them sort Tier 5." The vault inherited that. **This chair is the vault contradicting it**, and the contradiction is made on the same evidence the grid used and by the same method the grid taught: the grid's own Tier 1 discipline says that a claim with no Tier 1 name is either not load-bearing or is being carried by the prose, and the referral network is a load-bearing claim carried by a name filed as company. The same failure happened once before, and it is the reason the load axis exists at all: Gendlin's felt shift is the book's entire test for whether anything is true, and the contribution sort had him filed as one builder among twenty-nine, because asking what a thinker brings never asks what the book cannot afford to lose. Hudson is the second name to expose that blindness, and the full case is at [[the referral network sends readers to a name the sort filed as company]].

**What he would refuse.** The formula. His method is that a feeling is welcomed rather than analyzed, and that the analysis is frequently the resistance wearing a competent face — which puts him against the diagnostic apparatus in the same place Rogers is, and more sharply, because the book seats him inside the apparatus's own referral network. He would also refuse the solitary version: his work is done in groups and in coaching pairs, and the completion he describes has a witness in it.

**The load, and its evidence.** With `thesis-bearing`, assigned by the vault on 2026-08-05 against the grid. What happens to the book without him: one of the five theories of suffering loses its only holder, the referral network loses a destination and can no longer route a somatic misfire, the convergence table loses a vocabulary, and the temporal loop loses its practice. That is a structural answer rather than a judgment about his merit, and it is the answer the contribution sort could not produce.

**This chair has no second half at all, and says so.** Every quotation in it is from the corpus. Hudson is a living practitioner whose material is podcast and course rather than book, and none of it has been read or heard in this vault, so what is recorded here is the corpus's account of him rather than his account of himself. That is a weaker basis than any other chair in this group, and it is the basis on which the vault has assigned a load contradicting the grid.

## What the reading has to settle

Three questions, in the order that decides the most.

- Whether "every feeling fully welcomed completes itself" is his own formulation or a received one from the affect-completion tradition that runs through Gendlin and the body-psychotherapy line ([[wilhelm reich]]).
- What VIEW's four terms actually specify.
- Whether his own account of the method's lineage names anyone, since the corpus's other coaches turn out to have genealogies it does not print ([[tony robbins]]).
