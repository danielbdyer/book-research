# placing the forward devices at unit openings leaves the closes restful for the pause

The book runs a narrative engine and a practice engine that pull in opposite directions, and the tension between them is that forward hunger defeats the pause the practices require. The panel observes that the executed draft already closes its units restfully, which turns the craft problem from restraint into placement: the forward devices — promises held in view, planted fragments, the margin's subplot — belong at unit openings and mid-unit, never on a final page, so the pause the practices need sits in narrative slack rather than competing with a hook the reader has just been handed.

The offering is a cheaper alternative to the untested gate-practice treatment the tension carries, because it asks for arrangement rather than for a threshold the reader must perform before the text continues. Its check is empirical and unrun: where the executed draft's forward devices actually sit, opening or closing, decides whether the rule describes the draft or corrects it.
