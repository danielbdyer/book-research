# the gold thread ties the continuity loop to the book's own genre

The gold thread carries the felt continuity that runs underneath the social self, and it doubles as a fact about the book as an object: sūtra means thread, so the genre-word for every root text in the valley names this figure. The book is strung on the line it describes — ontology and codicology in one image.

## Substantiation

- Arc-gloss, the image-system: "The Gold Thread ↔ the continuity loop ↔ sūtra means thread. The genre-word for every root text in the valley is literally 'thread'; the book is strung on a gold sūtra. Ontology and codicology in one image." Atlas §X.5c carries the same equation.
- The figure's fullest prose is the earliest draft's: "The golden thread is not just what connects you to others — it's what connects you to yourself" and "The golden thread is you" (mythic-os: pages 12A–12B). The executed draft carries the felt form: "The thread tightens for a second. Everything in your life suddenly coheres" (abridged ch1).
