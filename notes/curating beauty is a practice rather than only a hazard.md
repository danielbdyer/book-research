# curating beauty is a practice rather than only a hazard

The gesture treats the curation of beauty as a practice with spiritual content. The vault's whole position on beauty is currently the hazard — fine prose recruits a connoisseur where the book needs a practitioner — so the corpus has a theory of beauty's danger and none of its use.
