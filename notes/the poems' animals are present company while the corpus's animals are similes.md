# the poems' animals are present company while the corpus's animals are similes

The primary layer keeps animals as present company. Poem #8, at a grief: "my two cats... it does not matter whether they knew the same as me. but they did." Poem #15: "The moth didn't know the light until it bumped into it," and "We low like creatures. nothing need be known for us to bray our message." Poem #16: "i am still animal enough to want recurrence." Against this, the corpus's complete animal inventory — checked across all seven documents — is four similes: "like a cat" and "like a fox" (mythic-os), "like a shy animal" (first-telling), "like a moth looking for a window" (abridged). Animals populate the poems as creatures the speaker is with, and they enter the book only as terms of comparison.

Two consequences follow for the book's stock of images. First, the book's grief formula places a witness inside itself as a required catalyst, and in the author's own record that witness was two cats: a witness the formula's clinical wording gives no way to name, and one the poem kept. Second, poem #15's image of a moth that does not know the light until it bumps into it says in a picture what the book's two foundations say as doctrine — that the light is learned by contact and cannot be delivered by instruction, so an epiphany cannot be engineered. That image is available from the primary layer if the book ever wants it.

## Substantiation

- All the poem passages were verified at their numbers. The corpus inventory was gathered by searching the seven extractions for animal terms and confirming that each hit sits inside a simile.
- None of the twelve image-category claims gathered in the topic map [[book-structure]], which collects the vault's claims about the book's shape and about the images it works with, records a creature.
- The refusal the moth's image states is held doctrinally at [[both poles of the convergence refuse the engineered epiphany]], which finds that Heidegger's account of enframing and the grace doctrine of the other tradition reach the same conclusion with no historical contact: unconcealment cannot be manufactured.
