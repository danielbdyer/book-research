# learning draws out what the learner already holds so the truest teaching midwifes rather than fills

You are not an empty vessel waiting to be filled with someone else's content; you are a fullness waiting to be drawn out. Nothing in the book has to be installed — the sentences can only occasion a knowing that is already yours, which is why reading it can feel like being handed back your own thought clarified. A teacher, on this account, does the humble work of a midwife: they cannot produce the child, only assist a birth that was always going to be the reader's own. Its use to the writing is to give the book its own job description — the text can only midwife — and to turn the humility of "I only point" from an apology into the very form of the teaching.

## Substantiation

- Plato grounds his pole at the primary text: anamnesis is drawing-out — "for all enquiry and all learning is but recollection" (Meno 81d), shown when an untutored slave boy is led by questions alone to a geometric truth he was never taught, "all these answers given out of his own head … they were all his own" (Meno 85b). Socrates names his own art midwifery (maieutikē) and is barren of wisdom, delivering only what is already in the other: "I am sterile in point of wisdom … the god compels me to act as midwife, but has never allowed me to bring forth," and his associates "have found in themselves many fair things and have brought them forth" (Theaetetus 150c–d). The companion poles are still owed: Rogers installs nothing but arranges the conditions under which the client's own movement unfolds (Client-Centered Therapy, 1951, unread); Dzogchen's pointing-out (ngo sprod) — the teacher points, the student recognizes their own face, the recognition never in the pointing (from memory, grounding owed).
- Refusal kept, and now placed at the seam: the anamnesis thesis rests on pre-natal knowledge of the Forms, offered as "a likely story" (Meno 81a–e), but the *maieutic method* (Theaetetus 150c–d) makes no appeal to the Forms — so the finding travels without the metaphysics, and the seam to keep visible is that "reading is remembering" is anamnesis-as-structure, not the doctrine of the pre-existent soul. Company, not proof.
- Standing: privated — the Plato pole read at the primary text (Meno, Theaetetus); the Rogers and Dzogchen poles still owed.
