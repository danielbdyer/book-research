# the cure the sociology prescribes may be a good only the secure can afford

The why-now shelf prescribes remedies for the muted, accelerated, self-exploiting condition it diagnoses: Rosa's resonance, Borgmann's focal practice, Odell's doing nothing, Crawford's craft-submission. The steelman observes that every one of these presupposes affordances of the secure — leisure, skill, community, a life with enough slack to prepare for the uncontrollable. The worker holding two jobs cannot arrange a focal practice or withdraw attention from the extractive economy, and Jenny Odell flags the difficulty against her own argument: the capacity to do nothing is unevenly distributed and self-flaggedly a privilege. The objection's edge is reflexive. The critique indicts the very conditions of leisure that granted its authors the standing to make it, so the remedy is available first to those least afflicted by the disease.

The objection ports directly onto the book. The book's practice — presence without agenda, the felt shift, the return to the Stance — is likewise easier for a reader with slack than for one without, and the book's founding promise is universal: you already know everything in this book, and reading is remembering. If the practice that reaches the remembering is a class good, the universality is compromised, because the book would address everyone and be practicable by the secure. This is the class form of the scale-of-address objection the vault already holds, which reads the structural version (an oasis of resonance leaves the escalatory structure untouched, [[hartmut rosa]]) and the market version (the cure sells inside the economy that produced the affliction, [[andrea jain]]); the class version is a third, about the distribution of the cure rather than about structure or market.

## Substantiation

- The class objection is the dig's steelman item 4, self-admitted by Odell [S per dig 5]; Odell, Crawford, Borgmann, and Rosa were each reached through scholarship and reviews rather than opened primaries, so the prescriptions attributed to them are reported rather than read, and the readings are queued in `ops/reading queue.md`. The note is held nascent accordingly.
- The vault holds the adjacent objection with a name attached: [[the compound diagnoses may relocate a structural condition inside a person]] states the general shape — a diagnostic that can only look inward cannot see conditions the person did not arrange — and this note adds the affordability edge, that even where the diagnosis is fair the cure may be out of reach.
