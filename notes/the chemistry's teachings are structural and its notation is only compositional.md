# the chemistry's teachings are structural and its notation is only compositional

The book's third layer writes states of experience as formulas — `Burnout = Obligation − Sanctuary`, `Trust = Bind + Commit + Defer` — and the formulas are lists: an unordered collection of terms joined by plus signs, with one subtraction available. What a formula can say is *which operations are present and which one is missing*. Nothing else is expressible in it.

Every teaching the chemistry actually delivers asks for something else. Trust and paralysis are distinguished by **where** the deferral attaches. Forgiveness is distinguished from spiritual bypass by the **order** of two operations. Grief is distinguished from a completable process by requiring an **agent outside the compound**. The Racket is diagnosed by its terms being **fixed** rather than by which terms they are. Enmeshment turns on a **capacity** rather than an act. Attending and committing share both their atoms and differ in **temporal extent**. And the defect the vault found on its own last week — that a minus sign cannot say whether the missing element was withheld or never on offer ([[the minus sign cannot say whether the missing element was withheld or never on offer]]) — is the same shape again, asking after the missing term's **provenance**.

Each of those is a relation: between two atoms, between an atom and time, between the compound and the world. None of them is a fact about membership. So in every case the corpus states the relation in words beside the formula and the formula does not hold it — "where Defer attaches," the word *then*, the label *catalyst: witness*, the phrase *both fixed*, a suffix, a parenthesis. **The notation records composition; the chemistry teaches structure; the prose closes the gap in every instance.** That is the general statement of which the vault's earlier molecule findings are all particular cases.

## Substantiation

- The full inventory is `ops/formula audit.md`: twenty-one formulas from the four sources that write any, each checked for whether its terms are defined, whether substitution holds, and where its teaching lives. Nine of the twenty-one are prose-carried in the sense above, and the nine sort onto a single table of relations the notation has no term for.
- The audit also produces three failures that are not about expressiveness but about arithmetic, and they are worth naming because they are checkable without agreeing to anything: `Stagnation = Lifecycle − Transform` collapses to Transform's own formula, so the state defined by lacking the operation *is* the operation; `Trust` and `Paralysis` cannot be both isomers and privations, because the privation formula leaves them sharing one atom out of three; and `Obligation = Commit + Attend + Maintain` substitutes down to the expression for Commit, which Burnout then inherits.
- Six of the twenty-one use a term the corpus never defines: *the boundary-form of the Knife*, *the not-yet of Create*, *Love*, *Persist*, *Complaint*, *Payoff*, *Connection*, *Sever-capacity*, and *Commitment*. Several of those are exactly the relational terms above, smuggled in as nouns — a *capacity* is a modality, and *both fixed* is a state of the terms rather than a term.
- **The confirming instance is the one relation the notation does write.** The minus sign says a term is absent rather than merely unlisted, which is a claim about the compound's relation to what it lacks. It is also, measured independently and before this audit, the only operator that predicts anything: across the executed catalogue, a formula with a minus is followed by an instruction and a formula with plus signs alone is followed by a description, without exception ([[the minus sign is what makes a molecule formula prescriptive]]). The notation's single relational operator is its single working operator.
- This claim is more general than [[the compositional notation does not license the operations its syntax implies]] and supersedes nothing in it. That note establishes that the formulas cannot be operated on — substituted into, decomposed — and this one says what they are missing that would make them worth operating on.
- What follows for the book is a fork rather than a verdict, and it is the author's: either the notation gains relational terms, which is a research program, or the relations stay in prose and the formulas become a display device rather than a diagnostic one, which is what the executed draft already largely does and is consistent with the schemes' demotion to instrument (2026-08-04). The vault states the fork and does not choose it.
- One precedent for the first route exists inside this vault and cost almost nothing, recorded in the audit: the `state:` field inherited `privated` from the same ontology project the molecules came from, found a bare privation mark unusable for the same reason the minus sign is, and now requires a `privation:` clause naming the absence.
