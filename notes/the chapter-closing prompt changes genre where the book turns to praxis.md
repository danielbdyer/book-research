# the chapter-closing prompt changes genre where the book turns to praxis

Every chapter of the executed draft but one ends with a short second-person instruction set off from the prose. Through chapter 13 these instructions are uniform in kind: they direct attention to a condition already present in the reader and explicitly decline to ask for more. Chapter 4 ends "Just notice. You do not have to fix anything yet"; chapter 5, "Notice without changing anything. The noticing is the practice"; chapter 7, "Don't try to fix anything. Just locate." Nothing is deferred and nothing is assigned.

At chapters 14 and 15 the genre changes. Chapter 14 ends "Choose one. Try it today. Tomorrow, try another," and chapter 15 ends "Tomorrow, try the Stack Scan once." These are tasks with a horizon, and they are the only two in the book. The switch coincides exactly with the point where the arc stops presenting architecture and begins issuing practice, which the treatment marks as the part where "invitations become instructions" (arc-gloss: part v).

The transferable point is that the book has a working rule it never states: an instruction may ask for attention at any point in the arc, and may ask for an action only after the reader has been given something to do it with. A prompt that assigns a task before chapter 14 would be borrowing authority the arc has not yet earned. This is checkable on any new chapter by reading its last three sentences and asking whether the reader could comply without leaving the page.

## Substantiation

- The full set of closing instructions, by chapter: ch1 "Pause. Let this settle. There is no hurry."; ch2 "Think now of one person who, when you are with them, you become more yourself"; ch4 "Just notice"; ch5 "Notice without changing anything"; ch6 "Don't try to fix anything. Just see."; ch7 "Don't try to fix anything. Just locate."; ch8 "What does the Third need from you right now?"; ch9 "Where in your life are you trying to collapse a tension that wants to be held?"; ch10 "What in your life is composting right now?"; ch11 "Remain here. Let the chapter settle."; ch12 "what did you do with it? What might you do differently, now, with the next one?"; ch13 "Right now: thirty seconds. No agenda."; ch14 "Choose one. Try it today."; ch15 "Tomorrow, try the Stack Scan once."
- Chapter 3 is the single chapter with no closing instruction. It ends on a transition into Part II instead — "We step back now to see the shape" — which is consistent with its recorded job as the hinge chapter that hands the reader from felt experience to the named map.
- The prose measurements corroborate the same turn. Mean sentence length across the fifteen chapters is 12.4 words; chapter 14 is the shortest in the book at 7.8 words and chapter 13 the second shortest at 10.0. The two praxis chapters that carry the instruction register are also the two whose sentences are most compressed, so the genre change is visible in the syntax as well as in the imperative.
- The atlas records a tension this pattern bears on directly: the book's forward momentum works against the stopping its practices require, treated with thresholds that require action before the text continues (atlas §XI.1b, carried at [[forward hunger defeats the pause]]). The executed draft does not use gate practices; its closing instructions are all skippable, which leaves that treatment untried.
