# lowering the demand of the assessment surfaces the disconfirming reports a high-demand setting suppresses

The one instrument the adversary chamber found that turns demand *down* rather than exploiting it is Britton's low-demand harm assessment. Where open-ended questioning in a setting that expects benefit underreports adverse effects — by roughly seventy percent against structured measures — a structured, independent, shame-reducing interview multiplies the harm reports that surface. The mechanism is the inverse of the compliance attack: if a high-demand frame suppresses the disconfirming report through shame and the wish to please, then deliberately lowering the demand of the question is what lets the disconfirming report be spoken. The finding is usable as a design principle rather than only as a critique — a book that verifies on a felt result can build its own low-demand assessment, inviting the reader to report what did *not* happen, or what went wrong, through a channel that is structured, that does not route through the admired author, and that carries no penalty of shame for a negative answer.

The instrument's limit is exactly the book's constraint. Its power comes from independence — an assessor who is not the party that induced the effect — and a book is not independent of itself. So the book can lower the demand of its own questions and can invite disconfirmation, but it cannot be the independent assessor the full instrument requires; that role still routes outward.

## Substantiation

- The dig records the instrument and its effect: low-demand assessment is "structured, independent, shame-reducing questioning [that] multiplies harm reports — the one instrument that turns demand down" (transmission dig 6: DEVICES), against a field where open-ended formats underestimate harms by about seventy percent (FINDINGS).
- The depth is thin and stated: the MedEx-I measurement study was unread at the primary in dig 6, so the note is marked `nascent`. The queued reading asks whether harm reports rise as assessment demand falls across the study's own conditions — open versus structured, instructor versus independent (dig 6, READINGS TO QUEUE item 4) — which is the evidence a felt-shift book would need before adopting the design.
- The instrument is the constructive counterpart to the suppression finding: one names the hazard, the other names the method that surfaces what the hazard buries ([[shame and the desire to please suppress the reports that would disconfirm a transformational setting's benefit]]).
- Categorized as a practice because it is an adoptable method the book can build rather than only a proposition about the world; its book-facing form is an invitation to disconfirm, and its irreducible outward remainder is the independent assessor.
