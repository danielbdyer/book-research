# the medicine sentence negates the familiar advice before naming the restoration

The diagnostic doctrine's prescriptions arrive in the executed draft through one repeated template: the culture's usual counsel is named and negated, then the structural restoration is named. "The medicine is not 'more discipline' or 'self-care' in the cosmetic sense. The medicine, precisely, is to restore Sanctuary" (abridged ch6). "The medicine is not to add more affection, which is what most couples try. The medicine is to restore difference" (abridged ch15).

The negation is load-bearing: it positions each prescription against the advice the reader has already tried, which is how the draft keeps the diagnosis-contains-cure doctrine from reading as advice at all — the restoration names a missing structure, not a behavior, and the contrast with the familiar counsel is what makes that difference visible in one sentence.

## Substantiation

- The exact phrase "The medicine is not" occurs four times in the extracted draft, with a fifth prescriptive instance in variant phrasing; the ch6 and ch15 specimens are quoted above.
- Checkable: search the draft for "The medicine is not" — each hit names familiar counsel before the structural prescription, and a prescription delivered without the negation is the exception to find.
- This is the prose layer above the notation finding at [[the minus sign is what makes a molecule formula prescriptive]]: the formula's minus sign points at what to supply, and this template is how the pointing reaches a reader's sentence.
