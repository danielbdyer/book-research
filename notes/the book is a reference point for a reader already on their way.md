# the book is a reference point for a reader already on their way

The book's reader is anyone already on their way, not only — as the record earlier had it — someone who has had a big experience and found it did not hold. The book does not offer to unlock everything at once. Its work is to be a reference point: to lend depth and words to ideas the reader has already been sensing but cannot yet convey, showing structure beneath things they half-know. The author stated it in his own words: the book is "a reference point to ideas that have not yet been explored by the reader — showing depth in parallel to things they might already know but not know how to convey," and the reader is "someone who is on their journey" (author statement, 2026-08-10).

This states the reading-is-remembering promise as a function. The book meets a reader who has been noticing, and hands them language and company for the noticing rather than a first delivery of the news ([[reading is remembering]]).

## Substantiation

- The broadened reader corrects, rather than contradicts, the earlier definition. The record of 2026-08-04 defined the reader as one who had already had the big experience and found it did not last; the author's statement of 2026-08-10 widens that to anyone on the way, so the post-peak reader is one case and not the whole audience. The decision row is in `ops/decisions.md`.
- The function the book performs is addition of depth and language rather than of information, which is why the book opens from a surplus of insight rather than a lack of it ([[the book opens from a surplus of insight rather than a lack of it]]) and addresses readers who already sense more than they can say ([[the book addresses people who overthink because they oversense]]).
- The posture toward such a reader is the one the book takes throughout: the reader is joined rather than instructed, having also been noticing without knowing the noticings had company ([[the book's honest origin story is noticing first and finding company after]]; [[the company is joined by the reader rather than consulted]]).
- The reference point stays a reference point and does not become an authority, on the limit the book holds everywhere: it can only be auxiliary to a sovereign reader ([[the book can only be auxiliary to a sovereign reader]]).
- First-telling states the same offer in the corpus's own terms — the deepest change is "not repair, and not addition. It's remembering" (first-telling: the reframe) — which is what the reference point gives words to.
