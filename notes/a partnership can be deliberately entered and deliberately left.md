# a partnership can be deliberately entered and deliberately left

The gesture treats a partnership as having designed thresholds at both ends, so that leaving well is a practice rather than a failure. Everywhere else in the corpus, decoupling means the three loops releasing at the peak; the chemistry has the components for a chosen ending — Sever, and the ordering rule in the forgiveness formula — and no molecule for one.
