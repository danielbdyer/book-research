# the correspondence vision was chosen and executed as a sent pilot letter

The forest panel's visionary sweep returned four visions of the finished object, and the first proposed the book as a correspondence: a hand-sized book of twelve to twenty dated letters, where the dating carries the disclosure constraint honestly because the writer of the later letters knows what the earlier writer did not. It opens with the promise done directly — the addressee already went, it did not hold, and the writer knows because he is the same kind of person — and it ends with a last line the reader can answer, followed by one blank leaf labeled as the reader's. Between letters the reader is asked to reply before going on, on the reasoning that a letter never answered has failed as a letter.

This is the one vision the author chose. He read the four visions and picked the correspondence on 2026-08-08, with the scope set to the full program plus a pilot letter; the decision rows are in `ops/decisions.md`, the program in `ops/correspondence program.md`, and the pilot deliverables in `drafts/`. The pilot letter was executed and sent — a fact asserted until 2026-08-16 only in the open-decisions queue, and reconciled to vault record on the author's word that day (open-decisions queue, D). The record keeps the specificity the record supplies and no more: the sending happened before 2026-08-14, and the recipients and exact date are not held in the vault.

The vision states its assumptions as bets the author could lose, following the rule that a design turning on an open question says which way it assumed. It settles the person to I-to-you; it settles the page-turn engine to address, so the reader continues because the next letter is to them; it turns the poems into enclosures, provenance shown rather than argued; and it gives the verification standard its least expensive defense, because the writer occupies the listener position that Gendlin's own founding paper says solitary inner work lacks. Its wager is that address can do the work of witness. The five refusals recorded on [[the book's native genre is the letter from a friend read alone]] name exactly where that bet would fail.

## Substantiation

- The vision is recorded at the forest panel of 2026-08-08, panel five, first vision, and the panel's own judgment ranked it first: it is the only vision whose form is derived from the chain's conclusion rather than laid over it, since auxiliary-to-a-sovereign and completed-only-by-being-answered are properties a letter cannot lack.
- The pick and its scope are recorded in the panel file's closing section and in `ops/decisions.md`; the sending is reconciled per the author's 2026-08-16 answer to the open-decisions queue, item D.
- Four independent panel routes landed on the same seam this vision is built on — the graph's sparsest crossing, what participation can mean for a reader holding a printed page alone — so the seam is not an artifact of one traversal. The correspondence is the visionary's route to it, beside the convergence scout's Vygotsky proposal, the connector's householder dependency, and the wild associate's zero-knowledge theorem.
- The vision's central wager gained measured support from the sibling generative round woven in after the panel ran: the working alliance holds its size when the exchange is written and mediated, with the limit kept that every measured case has a live responder, which is the same live-listener condition the least expensive defense turns on. The listener finding is Gendlin's own, quoted and anchored at [[the solitary felt shift rests on one name while the paired felt shift has convergent company]].
- The other three visions the panel returned stay recorded in the forest panel of 2026-08-08 as the roads not taken, so the pick can be revisited with its alternatives intact: a book-of-returns that pairs a walked path with named instruments sending the reader away, a single-walk account telling the whole book in strict order of knowing, and a two-voice form running an early knowing in the body against a later writer in the margin.
