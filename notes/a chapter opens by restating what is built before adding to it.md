# a chapter opens by restating what is built before adding to it

From the architecture's beginning onward, every unit of the executed draft opens with an inventory of what is already built: "We have built, by now, an alphabet (the primitives) and a chemistry (the molecules). But there is one more layer" (abridged ch7); "We arrive now at the praxis. The architecture has been laid. The mystical detour has been taken" (abridged ch13). Twelve of the fifteen units open this way. The three that open cold begin inside an experience rather than inside the argument, so the inventory opening appears in every unit that advances the argument and in none of the units that start the reader inside something felt.

The earliest draft solved the same problem in a different medium. In the mythic operating system edition the voice states on every page where the reader is physically standing — outside, at the porch, barely inside, in the depth — so that a reader who cannot yet say what the book is doing still knows their position in it ([[the voice keeps the reader located in a walkable space]]). The executed draft drops the house and tells the reader instead where they stand in the argument: what has been established so far, and what this unit adds to it. Both drafts keep the reader located; the earliest one locates them in a room, and the executed one locates them in the sequence of what has been built.

## Substantiation

- All fifteen unit openings in the extracted text were compared, and twelve carry the inventory while three open cold; the three cold ones are the chapters that begin inside an experience, and the ch7 and ch13 sentences quoted above are two of the twelve.
- Anyone can check this by reading each unit's first three sentences, where the inventory names already-built material before it names new material. The exception to look for is a cold opening in a unit that is not one of the three experiential entries.
- The move from locating the reader in a house to locating them in the argument is a change to one device across two states of the manuscript, which is the kind of change the per-chapter comparison at `ops/chapter concordance.md` records row by row. The device was revised into a new form rather than dropped.
