# the diagnostic method reads each structural layer with its own question

The book closes its practice curriculum with a diagnostic skill, which the corpus calls the engineer's eye: the trained capacity to look at a life or a room and "see what is happening. Not in moralized terms. In structural terms" (abridged ch15). The abridged edition specifies the skill as four moves, and each move reads exactly one of the structural layers the book has already built, so the diagnostic is the architecture turned back on a situation.

The four moves and their layers are these. The **Stack Scan** reads the three loops — for each, is it operating well, strained, or offline? — and "takes thirty seconds." The **Molecular Look** reads the chemistry: "what molecule is this? … what is privated? What ingredient, if restored, would complete the molecule?" — which "replaces moralization with chemistry." The **Field Read** reads the relational field: "what is the Third doing? Is it alive, thinning, fraying, dormant?" — which "replaces individual analysis with relational diagnosis." The **Metabolic Question** reads the arc: "where in the metabolic arc is this? Nascent, Privated, Full, Flourishing, Composting?" — which "replaces force with alignment." One read per layer, and the book's answer to a complaint is the reading rather than a verdict.

Two properties the corpus attaches to the diagnostic are the reasons it is filed as practice rather than as theory. It is offered to another person only as questions: the four moves "can be offered to another person as questions, gently, without telling them what their answers are … The right question is the most useful gift" (abridged ch15), which is the same auxiliary posture the rest of the practice curriculum holds. And it is defined as kind, because "seeing structurally is seeing without judgment" — "The Burnout person is not failing; they are missing Sanctuary" — which is the corpus's [[distress is a question about machinery not a verdict about character]] stated as a practitioner's move rather than as a doctrine.

## Substantiation

- **The four moves are the abridged edition's specification**, all in ch15 (pp. 43–44): the Stack Scan against the three loops, the Molecular Look against the chemistry, the Field Read against the Third, the Metabolic Question against the arc, each with the "replaces X with Y" clause quoted above.
- **The atlas frames the engineer's eye differently**, as a systems-craft toolkit rather than as four diagnostic moves: "the five reads · the Algorithm · the five lenses · the material voice" (atlas §VII), with the material voice carrying "some weather is the grid's … the cure for a structure is structural" and the working note "the engineer and the priest share an optometrist." The two drafts agree on the name and the function and differ on the enumeration; this note takes the abridged's four moves as the developed form because it is the one worked into a procedure with worked examples.
- **The three worked cases in ch15** run all four moves end to end — Work (diagnosed as `Burnout = Obligation − Sanctuary`), Love (`Connection − Eros`), and Crisis — and each ends in "a precise diagnosis with a precise prescription," the medicine read off the privation rather than added from outside.
- **The verification condition the corpus supplies**: the praxis stratum's standing rule governs the diagnostic as it governs every practice — "Everything here is verifiable by felt-shift or it doesn't count" (atlas §VII) — so the read that lands is the one the body countersigns, which is [[nothing enters the lattice the body has not countersigned]] applied to self-diagnosis.
