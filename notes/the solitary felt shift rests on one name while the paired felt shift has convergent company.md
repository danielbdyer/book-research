# the solitary felt shift rests on one name while the paired felt shift has convergent company

The book verifies in the body, and the vault's standing finding is that this single test has no convergent company: it is Eugene Gendlin's felt shift and his alone, so the form of support the book leans on everywhere else — several thinkers reaching one finding from unrelated traditions — is unavailable to it. That finding is exact, and it holds for the standard in one specific form: the felt shift as a solitary body's report, one person reading themselves alone. It does not hold for the felt shift joined to a second reader.

Two notes written after the single-bearer finding change its ground, and neither is company for the solitary shift — both are company for the paired one. Gendlin's own founding paper makes the listener part of the verification instrument: working alone a person circles without referent movement, and it is saying even a little to another person that produces it ([[the second chair is the verification standard's own requirement]]). Zen installs the same structure as a thousand-year institution: the compelling experience does not certify itself, and the teacher in dokusan, not the sitter, discriminates the genuine article ([[zen's makyo instruction refuses the self-certifying experience]]). These two are independent arrivals by the vault's own criteria — no shared teacher, different traditions, different centuries — at one structure: the body reports and the relation verifies.

So the single-bearer finding narrows to the solitary form. The solitary felt shift rests on one name, and the felt shift verified in relation has exactly the convergent company the standard was said to lack. What follows is not a repair of the solitary-reading wound — a printed book read alone is still short its second person (a codex is a one-chair technology making a two-chair argument) — but a change in what the standard's defense must do: the first-ranked reading problem stops defending introspection alone and starts defending the pair, and the solitary-reader problem becomes the standard's own operating condition rather than a tension standing beside it.

## Substantiation

- Both halves of the paired convergence are quotable from held readings, per `ops/methodology/both halves of a correspondence.md`. Gendlin's side, from the 1964 paper read whole here: working alone "he has gone round and round, feeling the same series of emotions and lacking any referent movement," while "even just saying to the other person a little of what one has been feeling and thinking produces direct reference and referent movement" (Gendlin 1964, fn. 13), and solo focusing is "always unsteady and difficult" (quoted at [[the second chair is the verification standard's own requirement]]). Zen's side, from Yasutani's lectures: verification is external, since "One who becomes attached to what he or she realizes through satori is also still lingering in the world of makyo," and dokusan is where the teacher tests the genuine article (quoted at [[zen's makyo instruction refuses the self-certifying experience]]).
- The two arrivals are independent in the strict sense the vault's evidence class requires: a therapist's clinical observation in 1964 and a Zen master's instruction in a lineage centuries older, with no shared source. That is the same independence the vault credits at both attacks on the standard blame the absence of correction by another person, where the standard's two sharpest opponents reach one diagnosis — solitary introspection goes uncorrected — from social psychology and analytic philosophy without contact.
- The convergence is held as company rather than corroboration, per `ops/methodology/authorial stance.md`. It does not rank as stronger evidence; what it changes is the condition the standard is defended in, moving the reading program from the solitary body to the pair.
- The single-bearer fact this narrows is stated at the mesh grid's tier one and recorded at the verification standard rests on one name and cannot be defended by company: the felt shift rests on one name, Gendlin's, and has no convergent company. This note leaves that true of the solitary form and adds the company the paired form has.
- What this does not establish: that the Second Chair as designed supplies what a live listener supplies. Gendlin's listener responds and a book cannot, so whether a designed pairing or a facilitator-reader does a listener's work is the open half of the treatment, kept open at [[the second chair is the verification standard's own requirement]] and [[writing to facilitators turns the one-chair reading into training for the second chair]].
