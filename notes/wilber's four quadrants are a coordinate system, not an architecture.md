# wilber's four quadrants are a coordinate system, not an architecture

The reframe conversation assigns Wilber's four quadrants a bounded role. The quadrants cross interior against exterior with individual against collective, and the conversation holds that in the book's ecology they function as a coordinate system rather than as an ontology: they answer where inquiry is looking, not what ultimately exists. The book's own account answers the question "What is happening?"; the quadrants answer only "Where is it happening?" Keeping the two questions apart is what prevents the model from becoming dependent on Wilber — the quadrants locate a phenomenon without dictating its nature, and the traditions the book hosts likewise become lenses rather than ontological categories.

The claim gives Wilber a constructive place the vault's earlier sorting did not. The book's nearest prior attempts, Wilber among them, are held mainly as case histories — cautionary records of what became of a synthesis of this kind. This claim adds the other half: used as a coordinate lens and demoted from architecture, the quadrants are usable without importing the totalizing framework whose failure the case history records.

## Substantiation

- The role, in the conversation's words: "Wilber's four quadrants then become a coordinate system, rather than an ontology," answering "Where are we looking?" while "Your ontology answers: What is happening?" and "Wilber helps answer: Where is it happening?"; the conversation states that "That distinction keeps the model from becoming overly dependent on Wilber" (reframe conversation, 2026-08-13, lines 17834-17853).
- The generalization to traditions: in the same passage "traditions should not become ontological categories" but "are lenses" (lines 17859-17861), which is the coordinate-not-architecture move applied beyond Wilber.
- Standing: reached in the conversation's shared portion, using Wilber as an exemplar rather than reporting a reading of him. No primary work of Wilber's has been read in the vault, so the claim records how the conversation positions the quadrants and not a verified account of his system; it is held as candidate material and kept nascent.
