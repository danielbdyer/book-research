# the units the treatment flags as unfinished are the shortest in the executed draft

The Arc and the Gloss treatment names two units as not yet carrying their weight: Erotic-Spiritual Intelligence, which it calls the shortest in the scaffold and describes as awaiting an unlocking rather than a writing, and the Kata, which it says has the most room to expand because its technical instruction was deferred to a companion volume. The abridged edition was written at a different scope and does not refer to the treatment's assessments. Counting its units puts Erotic-Spiritual Intelligence at 820 words and the Kata at 829, the second and third shortest of the fifteen.

The agreement matters because the two documents are independent on this point. A private craft note and a word count are different kinds of evidence, and their convergence turns an authorial impression into a measured fact about where the manuscript is thin. For a decision that has to pick one unit and finish it, this locates the two that would need the most new writing, and by elimination the ones that would need the least.

The treatment's own superlative does not survive the count. It calls Erotic-Spiritual Intelligence "the shortest chapter in the scaffold," and in the executed draft the Stance is shorter at 706 words. The treatment is describing its own storyboard entries rather than the prose, so the two statements are about different objects; the vault records the discrepancy rather than reconciling it.

## Substantiation

Extent of each named unit in the abridged edition, in words, shortest first: the Stance 706, Erotic-Spiritual Intelligence 820, the Kata 829, the Way of Ripening 835, the Geometry of Becoming 1012, the Afterglow 1050, the Engineer's Eye 1054, the Third Entity 1086, the Molecules of Experience 1115, the Generative Triad 1173, the Alphabet of the Soul 1254, the Ink That Listens 1305, the House with No Ceiling 1658, the Psychedelic Moment 2172, the Three Loops 2236. The Invitation runs 643 words and the Open Secret 1654. Mean unit length is 1160 words. The full table with each unit's source locators is in `ops/chapter concordance.md`.

- The two flagged units are quoted in full at [[the treatment records four authorship conditions in its undercurrents]], which also carries the two conditions that cannot be measured.
- The two longest units, the Three Loops and the Psychedelic Moment, are the structural core and the peak, which is consistent with the treatment's designation of each as a unit the arc is built around (arc-gloss: ch7, ch11).
- The Stance is short without being flagged. The treatment specifies its register as "still, spacious; the prose models the stance" (arc-gloss: ch13), so its brevity is a stated design rather than an omission — which is why extent alone does not identify an unfinished unit, and the treatment's flags are needed to read the count.
- Craft standing: this note reports two independent measurements and their agreement. No judgment about the quality of either chapter is recorded, per `ops/methodology/craft evidence.md`.
