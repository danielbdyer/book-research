# felt-shift

Held within the ecology, the felt shift this map gathers is one cultivated locus's trained way of noticing inside the reader's own self-facilitation ([[self-facilitation is creating conditions for one's own becoming, not self-management, optimization, or analysis]]): no locus, the body's report included, is sovereign over the reality it describes ([[no single locus or framework may become sovereign over the reality it describes]]), and the book cannot verify that the reader has done the work ([[the book must respect that it cannot verify the reader has done the work]]), so the standard recorded below and every counterweight on it sit beneath that ground as a nested feature with its guardrails, kept whole.

This map gathers the book's epistemology, which the center-of-gravity decision keeps undiminished: the felt shift is the lived-practice centerpiece's verification floor, not part of the demoted system vocabulary (`ops/methodology/center of gravity.md`). The standard comes from Eugene Gendlin's research into what predicts change in therapy. Walked III reports the finding in the corpus's words: the predictor was "whether a person could attend to a not-yet-worded bodily knowing and let language arise from inside it," and none of the field's assumed candidates — insight, interpretation, technique — predicted it. Gendlin named the physical event that accompanies an accurate word the felt shift; the first-telling describes it as "a loosening, an exhale you didn't plan" (first-telling: the instrument). The book adopts this as its final authority; the atlas states the rule as "nothing enters the lattice that the body has not countersigned" (§III) and assigns the body the deciding role in every doctrinal dispute the book's sources produce.

The instrument has documented limits, and the vault tracks them as a tension rather than smoothing them over: processing-fluency research shows that well-made prose can produce the click without the recognition, and interoception research shows the signal is muted in some readers precisely where it is most needed.

One consequence for this vault is recorded in [[methods]]: the vault verifies structure and provenance; it cannot run the somatic test. That certification belongs to the author.

The map's re-grounding is carried in the section "The reframe conversation: self-facilitation and the kinds of knowing" below, where the standard's practice turns inward and the felt shift takes its place in a wider account of knowing.

## The founding paper, read whole

The founding paper is read — Gendlin 1964, complete on 2026-08-05, with the record at `archive/reading — gendlin, a theory of personality change (1964).md` — and it changed this map's standing question.

- [[the felt shift certifies carrying forward rather than correspondence]] — the reading's central finding. Gendlin defines a symbolization's correctness as its experienced effect on the process and denies in his own footnote that it means the symbols match the referent. His certificate is movement; the book's use of it is truth. The narrowing shrinks the introspective demand the standard makes — noticing change against no-change, not describing inner contents — and it costs the book the power to underwrite doctrine with a shift.
- [[gendlin names four kinds of inward attention that are not focusing]] — his own counterfeit-detection, from a 1964 footnote: sheer emotion, the circumstantial orbit, the explanatory orbit, and self-engineering, each marked by the absence of referent movement. The self-engineering entry carries the first mechanism the vault holds for the surrender-and-will fork: willpower works only where experiencing already implicitly functions.
- [[the second chair is the verification standard's own requirement]] — the same reading's relational consequence: solo focusing is "always unsteady and difficult" in Gendlin's words, and the listener's responses are what produce movement, so the corpus's Second Chair device is required by the standard itself rather than only by the relational doctrine.

## The reframe conversation: self-facilitation and the kinds of knowing

The reframe conversation of 2026-08-13 recast the practice curriculum as facilitation scaled to a single person and located the felt shift inside a wider account of knowing. The notes below are the part of that layer that declares this map; the conversation's coinages are attributed once and held as candidates, and the concern is stated plainly wherever a name is used.

The verification standard's practice turns inward, and the inward practice keeps its own guards.

- [[self-facilitation is creating conditions for one's own becoming, not self-management, optimization, or analysis]] — the practice mode the reframe names for the self: arranging the conditions under which one's own becoming can happen rather than executing a technique on oneself, which is the curriculum's design law read from inside a single reader.
- [[self-management optimizes behavior toward an outcome; self-facilitation creates conditions for emergence]] — the mechanism beneath that mode, and the reason the felt shift can only certify it: an outcome aimed at is not an emergence, so optimizing toward a target forecloses the very referent-movement the standard reads.
- [[not every inner voice is wisdom, so the task is to differentiate them rather than suppress them]] — the discrimination the inward practice requires: the body's voices are not uniformly trustworthy, so the work is telling them apart under the shift rather than silencing them.
- [[witnessing without dissociation means experiencing emotion without being defined by it]] — the posture that keeps the somatic report honest, separating the practice's observing stance from the clinical detachment the basic posture is describable as dissociation and the harm literature is unread warns it can resemble.
- [[acceptance is willingness to perceive what is actually here, not approval]] — the precondition for an accurate reading: perceiving what is present without first approving it, so the body's report is not filtered by consent before the shift can check it.

The felt shift is placed within a wider epistemology, which locates what it can and cannot certify.

- [[there are four kinds of knowing, and the felt shift lives at the edge of the participatory]] — the standard set inside Vervaeke's four kinds of knowing and placed at the threshold of the participatory, which broadens the felt-shift-versus-confirmation distinction without discharging the one-name load the standard rests on.
- [[information → knowledge → understanding → recognition → wisdom is a ladder whose arrows are not automatic]] — why a somatic countersignature is needed at each rung: understanding does not climb to transformation on its own, and the shift is what marks a rung actually crossed rather than merely reached in words.
- [[the hollow interaction is illumination that cannot recognize itself]] — the counterfeit at the scale of a machine exchange: fluent output that lights everything and produces no felt recognition, felt confirmation arriving in the medium the reframe was conducted through.
