# a practice round is finished when the body has refused at least one formulation

**Composted by a reading (2026-08-28).** This craft offering proposed a completion signal for practice rounds and staked its own validity on a reading of Gendlin's *Focusing*; that reading (in Substantiation below) found the specific claim unsupported, so the note is kept as a record and drawn on by the corrected understanding rather than asserted as live. The proposal, as it was offered:

The practice forms specify a trigger and a gesture for each round but supply no test for when a round is finished, and the chain's one defense of the bodily standard is that the felt sense is precise enough to refuse a wrong formulation. Joined, the two give the missing completion signal a negative form: a round is over not when it feels complete but when it has produced at least one correction — a word the body refused, a formulation that had to be revised — and a round in which nothing pushed back was rehearsal rather than practice. The signal resists gaming for the same reason the defense holds, since a fluent reader can produce assent but cannot produce a refusal they did not feel.

One mechanism serves two chain positions at once — the practice curriculum's missing completion signal and the felt sense's one defense against fluency. Its failable check is a reading of Gendlin's *Focusing*: whether the six movements mark the stopping point at the shift or at the rejection decides whether the completion signal can be stated this way at all.

## Substantiation

- The offering is recorded at the forest panel of 2026-08-08, panel three, third entry.
- **Outcome of the failable check (Gendlin's *Focusing* read 2026-08-28).** The six movements — Clearing a space, Felt sense, Handle, Resonating, Asking, Receiving — terminate at the **shift ("Receiving")**, not at a refusal; and Gendlin relaxes even that: "IF DURING THESE INSTRUCTIONS … YOU HAVE SPENT A LITTLE WHILE SENSING … AN UNCLEAR HOLISTIC BODY SENSE … THEN YOU HAVE FOCUSED. It doesn't matter whether the body-shift came or not." The body's refusal of a non-resonant word is a *within-process* correction — "if you get a quick answer without a shift in the felt sense, just let that kind of answer go by" — not a completion criterion. So the proposal's specific claim, "a round is finished when the body has refused," is not what Gendlin says, and the note is composted.
- What survives and is drawn on: the completion *gap* is real ([[the practice forms specify when to begin and not how to tell one is finished]]), and the felt sense's refusal of wrong words is a genuine mechanism ([[the felt sense rejects formulations and that friction is what separates it from fluency]]) — but the honest completion signal Gendlin supplies is the shift itself, not the refusal, so a corrected completion claim, if minted, belongs beside [[a genuine felt shift changes what can be said next]] rather than here.
