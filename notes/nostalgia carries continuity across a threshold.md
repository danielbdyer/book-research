# nostalgia carries continuity across a threshold

The gesture treats nostalgia as the felt form of persistence through change rather than as sentiment. The earliest draft opens on it as its first truth — "The sweetness of becoming puckers into nostalgia whether you choose it or not" (mythic-os: page 1) — and the container anatomy gives its persisting corner no affective account at all. If the gesture is right, nostalgia is what the Floor feels like from inside.
