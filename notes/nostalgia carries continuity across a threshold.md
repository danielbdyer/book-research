# nostalgia carries continuity across a threshold

The gesture asserts that nostalgia does structural work: it is what keeps a self continuous while the self is changing, and it is therefore a feature of liminality rather than a failure to move on. The vault does not hold this. The container anatomy names a Floor whose job is persistence across time, and it gives that structure no affective face at all. If the gesture is right, nostalgia is what persistence feels like from inside, and the Floor acquires a phenomenology it currently lacks.

This note is a stub. Its state is `nascent`: the proposition is the gesture's, not the vault's, and it is recorded so the territory is reachable from the graph rather than only from the inventory in `ops/open corpus.md`. The gesture is a named unit of the earliest contents that no document develops; naming a territory is evidence the project has been to its edge, not evidence it belongs in the book.

## What the corpus supplies

- The gesture's own statement, from the written chapter: "The sweetness of becoming puckers into nostalgia whether you choose it or not" (mythic-os: page 1). The sentence is offered as the book's opening truth, before any thesis.
- Nostalgia appears once each in the atlas and the walked document and nowhere in the first telling or the survey, so the descriptive corpus effectively does not treat it.
- The gesture's title pairs nostalgia with "The House with No Walls," which is the house image under a name the arc does not use. Whether the pairing is deliberate or a superseded title is not determinable from the sources.

## What would close it

Closing it means deciding whether the persistence corner has an emotional signature, and whether nostalgia is it.
