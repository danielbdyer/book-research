# the voice keeps the reader located in a walkable space

The voice tracks the reader's position in a physical space, page by page: outside, then no longer outside, then at the porch, then barely inside, then in the depth. Spatial location moves in step with how much has been asked of the reader, so a reader who cannot yet say what the book is doing always knows where they are standing in it. Disorientation about meaning is survivable when location holds; the house conceit is working here as navigation, not decoration.

The device also meters consent. Each deepening is announced as a threshold before it is crossed, which turns progression into a series of small agreements rather than a current the reader is carried on.

## Substantiation

- The sequence, in order: "We're still not quite inside the house yet — but we are no longer outside." (mythic-os: page 2); "Now. Are you ready to leave the porch? Because Page 4 is a threshold." (mythic-os: page 3); "You are here. Inside now. But only barely. The air changes at the doorway, doesn't it?" then "We are in the depth now." (mythic-os: page 4); and a page that is nothing but the threshold held open (mythic-os: page 4.5).
- The corpus later states the conceit as design — "the rooms are the ideas, made walkable" (first-telling: how it's built) — and this chapter is the conceit already executed at page scale, before the design document existed.
- Checkable: the location sentences are enumerable, one or more per page through the chapter's first movement, and each names a position deeper than the last.
