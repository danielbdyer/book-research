# structure is the container that outlasts a community's decay rather than the cause of it

Structure outlasts a gathered community's decay rather than causing it, so a book that draws readers together cannot found a permanent fellowship, and structure is not what dissipates the one it briefly has. Victor Turner's account of ritual community, which he named communitas, holds that shared spontaneous belonging is a phase rather than a lasting condition: the moment it organizes it hardens, passing from an existential fellow-feeling among equals into a normative form hedged by rules, and from there into an ideological blueprint drawn from the remembered spontaneity. The passage is, in his account, exceptionless. The load-bearing part for a book is the reframe the passage carries: structure is not the betrayer that kills spontaneous belonging but the safe vessel that holds a dangerous energy, so the decay is intrinsic to the energy and is not the fault of the container that survives it.

For the book the consequence is a forecast and a correction. Any readership the book gathers will follow the same passage, so a hope for a permanent community around the book is a hope against Turner's rule. And the play lineage the book draws on carries an anti-structure romance — the assumption that structure is what deadens spontaneity — which this reverses, because on Turner's account the structure is what makes the dangerous spontaneous energy survivable at all.

## Substantiation

- The reframe's core rests on the primary text. Turner's "In and Out of Time," opened in full by dig 7 [P], gives structure and spontaneous communitas as a rhythm rather than an opposition — a dialogue in which the two phases alternate and are married rather than opposed — which already denies that structure is the enemy of spontaneous belonging.
- The sharper containment image and the three-stage decay sit in The Ritual Process (1969, ~pp. 132, 140), which dig 7 worked through secondary accounts (the SAGE encyclopedia entry on communitas and Haggar 2025) and did not open, so the note is `state: nascent`. Turner's side of the reframe, shown per `ops/methodology/both halves of a correspondence.md`: he likens the rules that hedge a mobilized communitas to "the lead container of a dangerous radioactive isotope" (Turner, The Ritual Process, via secondary [S]), which states structure as the containment of a danger rather than as the danger's cause; the book's side is stated above.
- The queued reading carries the claim's weight. It asks whether any normative communitas ever retained existential force — that is, whether the decay is truly exceptionless and the book's gathered community could escape it (dig 7, READINGS TO QUEUE item 4), bound for `ops/reading queue.md`; until it is done the universal "any community" reach of the claim stands on the secondary account.
- The finding is Turner's own, seated by dig 7 as directional — an early-warning system for any community the book gathers (dig 7, SEAT VERDICTS) — rather than an independent arrival at the book's center; under `ops/methodology/authorial stance.md` it is held as company and is not ranked as evidence.
