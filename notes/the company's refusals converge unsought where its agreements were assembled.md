# the company's refusals converge unsought where its agreements were assembled

The honesty rule over the company's unanimity is that it was selected for: the lineages were gathered on one day for the property they are then reported to share, so their agreement is company, never corroboration. The refusals have the opposite provenance, and the record states it twice without generalizing it. The genre note: "The strongest objection to this claim arrived on 2026-08-05 and it was not looked for." The sort's four-tradition finding: the chairs "were written on the same day without the pattern being looked for."

So the record now holds two provenance classes of convergence. Assembled agreement — curated, and reachable by the selection caveat. And unsought refusal — arriving against the gatherer's interest, which a sample curated for participation cannot have been curated to produce. The stance's no-ranking rule stays whole: neither class outranks the other as evidence, and the claim asserts only which caveat applies to which. What it buys is the honesty section's two-sided sentence — the agreements were gathered; the refusals arrived — and a reason the refusals-intact rule is load-bearing rather than decorative: the refusals are the part of the record that cannot be an artifact of the gathering.
