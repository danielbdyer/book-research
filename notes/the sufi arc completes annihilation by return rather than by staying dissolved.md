# the sufi arc completes annihilation by return rather than by staying dissolved

The book walks an arc whose peak is completed by its descent and integration, and its householder axiom holds that the ordinary Tuesday is where the work lands ([[the tuesday is the temple]]; [[the householder road is priced long by every witness who grants it]]). The Sufi structure of *fana* and *baqa*, in al-Junayd's sobriety doctrine, reaches the same finding: the self is annihilated (*fana*) and then subsists and returns (*baqa*), and the return is the completion rather than an afterthought. A reading confirms it at scholarly depth. Annihilation-and-return is one structure, an independent arrival at the book's claim that the peak is finished by the return.

## Substantiation

- The book's half is on record: the arc concentrates a peak and then restores the ground for integration (atlas §I), and the ordinary-life commitment is the householder axiom.
- The Sufi half is now shown, from scholarly sources on al-Junayd and al-Qushayrī (the reading record is `archive/reading — al-junayd and al-qushayri on fana and baqa.md`). al-Junayd founded the School of Sobriety (*sahw*) on the interdependence of *fana* and *baqa*, and al-Qushayrī quotes him: "Sufism means that God causes you to die for yourself, while endowing you with a life in Him" — *fana* as oblivion from creation and self, *baqa* as subsistence in God's attributes, with the "second sobriety" making the return to distinction the completion rather than a lesser state. Read at scholarly-secondary depth; the primary *Rasāʾil* and the *Risāla* were not read whole.
- The refusal is kept. Annihilation is *in God* and subsistence is *by God's* attributes, a theological destination the book's psychological register cannot assume; and al-Qushayrī frames *fana* as the top of a long ascent through repentance, renunciation, and patience, a communally and legally grounded path, so the self-authorizing solo version is exactly what the tradition guards against — the same refusal al-Qushayrī's seated note already carries, that a shaykh is required.
