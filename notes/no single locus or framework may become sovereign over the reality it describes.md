# no single locus or framework may become sovereign over the reality it describes

The reframe conversation states a constraint on frameworks, prompted by the author's own wariness of a totalizing coordinate-system, and names the exact point at which a useful framework turns into an overreaching one. Building a meta-framework is not the trap; the trap is allowing that meta-framework to become the privileged locus from which every other perspective is then interpreted. The conversation marks the moment of the turn: as soon as the framework announces that it now understands how all the perspectives fit together, it has quietly moved from facilitator to sovereign. The constraint the conversation credits to the author prevents that move — no locus gets to abolish the others — and the several loci remain different places from which reality becomes intelligible rather than one place that rules the rest.

The claim matters to the book, and to this vault, because both are frameworks that describe an inner reality without being licensed to govern it. The book draws on several traditions and must not install itself as the tradition that adjudicates them; the vault maps the book and must not mistake its map for the territory it charts. The constraint keeps every framework, the book's own included, answerable to the reality it only ever discloses in part.

- The turn in the conversation's own words: "The trap isn't constructing a useful meta-framework," but "allowing the meta-framework to become the privileged locus from which all other perspectives are interpreted"; at the moment it says "now I understand how all of these fit together" it "has quietly moved from facilitator to sovereign" (reframe conversation, 2026-08-13: the meta-framework becoming sovereign).
- The constraint stated as the author's: "Your constraint prevents that: No locus gets to abolish the others," with the loci held as "different places from which reality becomes intelligible" that need not "conquer the others" (reframe conversation, 2026-08-13: the meta-framework becoming sovereign). The passage is prompted by the author's earlier concern about a coordinate-system that would rank all perspectives.
- Standing: the constraint is the author's, articulated in the conversation's shared, model-assisted portion; it restates in the conversation's own occasion the vault's standing directive that no name for a concern outranks the concern. It is held as candidate material and kept nascent until the writing reaches for it.
