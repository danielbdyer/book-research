# delegation now reaches into domains constitutive of personhood

The claim states the book's reason for addressing its problem now. The novelty the conversation isolates is not that AI is changing everything but that the scope of what a person delegates has moved: the activities being handed to machines are the ones traditionally understood to constitute a person — writing, thinking, research, creativity, memory, interpretation, communication, and companionship. When delegation was confined to work outside the self, participation was not at stake; once it reaches the constitutive activities, the question of participation becomes urgent in a way it was not before. This is the temporal argument beneath the technology material, and it is what the conversation offers in place of the flat "AI is changing everything." The phrasing "constitutive of personhood" is the conversation's own. The claim is a model-assisted synthesis, held as candidate and kept nascent until the writing reaches for it.
