# the body knows a truth before the mind can word it and that foreknowing hands over the next step

Before a person can say what they mean, they can feel that they mean something — a whole sense, dense and wordless, already present at the edge. This is not a deficit waiting on language; it is knowledge in an earlier form, and it is generous. When the right word finally lands, the felt sense does not merely get labeled; it opens and hands over the next thing to say. A reader is never starting from nothing, because the body is already carrying the meaning forward, and the work is to listen for what it is reaching to become.

## Substantiation

- Two accounts hold that a whole meaning is present before its parts are spelled out. Gendlin, read at the primary texts: the felt sense holds "more" than what is already formed ("our more intricate experiencing"), and symbolizing it rightly carries the process forward — "an occurring that changes the implying … so that it is no longer 'the same' implying," and "carrying forward is always something that might not happen" (Experiencing and the Creation of Meaning, Preface, 1997; A Process Model, ch. II). The *pratibhā* pole is still owed: the Indic term names the spontaneous flash by which a sentence's meaning is grasped as one, beneath and prior to the sequence of words (Bhartṛhari's Vākyapadīya; from memory, grounding owed). Company, cited by address.
- Refusal kept, and load-bearing: what the foreknowing hands over is a *next step*, not a settled truth — Gendlin's carrying-forward certifies that the process moved, not that a content corresponds ([[the felt shift certifies carrying forward rather than correspondence]]); and the felt sense is often murky, does not always give its word, and cannot be strong-armed. The foreknowing is real, and also patient.
- Standing: privated — the Gendlin pole read at the primary texts (ECM Preface, 1997; A Process Model ch. II; the record is in `archive/`); the *pratibhā* pole owed, its scope varying by tradition and held as company rather than as identity with carrying-forward.
