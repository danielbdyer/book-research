# the minus sign is what makes a molecule formula prescriptive

The book's chemistry writes two kinds of formula, and in the executed draft the notation sorts them without exception. A formula built from plus signs alone names a condition and describes its composition: Sanctuary, Obligation, Possibility, Trust. A formula containing a minus sign names a suffering-state, and the term after the minus is the instruction: Burnout, Paralysis, Foreclosure, Stagnation, Enmeshment. Nothing else in the notation does this work. The plus-only formulas can be read for understanding; the minus formulas can be acted on, because the missing term is the thing to supply.

The transferable point is a constraint on writing rather than an observation about chemistry. A new molecule earns a place in the catalogue as a diagnostic only if it can be written with a minus sign, and a molecule written with plus signs alone is a definition that some later minus formula will need. This is checkable against any passage: read a formula, and the presence of a minus predicts whether the surrounding prose ends in an instruction.

## Substantiation

- The edition states the principle itself, without connecting it to the notation: "In the molecular vocabulary, the diagnosis is the prescription. Burnout = Obligation − Sanctuary. The minus sign tells you precisely what to restore. Paralysis = Possibility − Commit. Restore commit. Stagnation = Lifecycle − Transform. Restore transform. The structure of the problem contains the structure of its medicine" (abridged ch6).
- The sort holds across the executed catalogue. Plus-only, each followed by description: Sanctuary = Contain + Maintain + Release; Obligation = Commit + Attend + Maintain; Possibility = Defer + Create + Commit (abridged ch6); Trust = Bind + Commit + Defer (abridged ch8). Minus, each followed by an instruction: Burnout = Obligation − Sanctuary; Paralysis = Possibility − Commit; Foreclosure = Commitment − Defer; Stagnation = Lifecycle − Transform (abridged ch6); Connection − Sever-capacity = Enmeshment and Connection − Eros (abridged ch8).
- The one formula that resists the sort is the grief formula, which is written with plus signs and is not a description of a healthy condition. The corpus's version, recorded at [[grief is sever plus love plus persist]], reclassifies grief rather than diagnosing it, and the edition adds the missing term as prose rather than notation: "some reactions require a catalyst, and the catalyst for grief is a witness" (abridged ch6, restating the teaching the treatment attributes to McLaren at `arc-gloss: ch6`). The exception is informative — where the missing element is a person rather than an operation, the notation cannot carry it.
