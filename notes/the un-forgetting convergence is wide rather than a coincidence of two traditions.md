# the un-forgetting convergence is wide rather than a coincidence of two traditions

The book's foundation is not a private convergence of two voices but a wide one. Independently, with no line of contact, thinkers in Athens, Kashmir, Tibet, the Rhineland, North America, and a psychotherapy clinic concluded that the thing a person most seeks was already held, and that coming to know it is remembering rather than acquiring. The reader gains a floor under their own experience of "I think I already knew this": recognition is not naïveté but the shape many careful lifetimes settled into. What is most needed was never in short supply; it was only unrecognized, and the book is one more turning toward a fullness that predates it. Its use to the writing is to widen the floor without raising the evidential claim by a single degree.

## Substantiation

- The founding pair, named by address: Heidegger's aletheia, truth as un-concealment, the privative *a-* negating *lēthē*, forgetting (Being and Time §44; to verify); the Pratyabhijñā school's liberation as recognition of what never left (Utpaladeva, Īśvarapratyabhijñā-kārikā; to verify). The wider company — Plato's anamnesis, Advaita's already-attained freedom, Dzogchen's self-recognition, Eckhart's uncreated ground, Rogers' actualizing tendency, Gendlin's implicit — is developed in the sibling notes rather than repeated here.
- The honesty rule holds in full: these were gathered for the property they share, so their agreement is company, not corroboration. It does not raise the odds the finding is true; it says the reader stands in a large room, not that the room proves anything.
