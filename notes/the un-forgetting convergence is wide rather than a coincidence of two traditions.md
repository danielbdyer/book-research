# the un-forgetting convergence is wide rather than a coincidence of two traditions

The book's foundation is not a private convergence of two voices but a wide one. Independently, with no line of contact, thinkers in Athens, Kashmir, Tibet, the Rhineland, North America, and a psychotherapy clinic concluded that the thing a person most seeks was already held, and that coming to know it is remembering rather than acquiring. The reader gains a floor under their own experience of "I think I already knew this": recognition is not naïveté but the shape many careful lifetimes settled into. What is most needed was never in short supply; it was only unrecognized, and the book is one more turning toward a fullness that predates it. Its use to the writing is to widen the floor without raising the evidential claim by a single degree.

## Substantiation

- The founding pair, now read at address: Heidegger's aletheia grounded at *Being and Time* §44 as un-*concealment* ("Being-true ('truth') means Being-uncovering"; the privative reading through *lēthē* as oblivion is the later Heidegger's and is owed a Parmenides-lectures citation), and the Pratyabhijñā school's liberation as recognition of what never left, read at Utpaladeva's Īśvarapratyabhijñākārikā in the Kashmir Śaiva round (2026-08-28). The wider company — Plato's anamnesis, Advaita's already-attained freedom, Rogers' actualizing tendency, Gendlin's implicit, all read in sibling notes; Dzogchen's self-recognition and Eckhart's uncreated ground, owed — is developed in the sibling notes rather than repeated here.
- The aletheia pole's independence survived a direct test this round. Reinhard May's case that Heidegger drew on East Asian sources documents a Daoist and Zen channel — to Being, Nothing, the clearing, and the Way — but not to aletheia, and no channel at all to the Kashmir Śaiva pole (a Śaiva rival of the Buddhist emptiness May traces); so the founding pairing's independence holds, while the book owes an adjacent Daoist debt on the Heidegger side (`archive/reading — reinhard may, heidegger's hidden sources (1996).md`).
- The honesty rule holds in full: these were gathered for the property they share, so their agreement is company, not corroboration. It does not raise the odds the finding is true; it says the reader stands in a large room, not that the room proves anything.
- Standing: privated — the founding pair and most of the wider company read; Dzogchen and Eckhart owed.
