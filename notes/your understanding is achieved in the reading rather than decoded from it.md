# your understanding is achieved in the reading rather than decoded from it

The reader gains the assurance that the understanding they reach is genuinely made by them, now, in the act — not a meaning the author sealed into the page for them to extract intact. Meaning is not a parcel posted from writer to reader; it comes into being *in* the reader's own carrying-forward, freshly, as theirs. What the reader gains is authorship of their own comprehension: the book is not a download to be received faithfully but an occasion in which the reader's understanding is accomplished, so that being changed by it and being the author of the change are one event. Its use to the writing is the mechanism by which the book can perform what it describes without manipulating — it can only occasion the reader's own accomplishing, which is why what transforms is always the reader's own doing.

## Substantiation

- Merleau-Ponty ("The Body as Expression, and Speech," Phenomenology of Perception): thought is not an inner completed thing that speech then transmits — "the orator does not think before speaking, nor even while speaking; his speech is his thought"; thought and expression "are simultaneously constituted." Expression *accomplishes* thought; it does not report it. Gendlin: a symbol does not label a pre-formed meaning — it carries forward the felt meaning, and the new meaning exists only in and after that interaction; meaning is created in the crossing, not transported.
- Company: both refuse the conduit-and-translation model of understanding, one from the phenomenology of language, one from the phenomenology of felt meaning.
- Standing: nascent; the orator line is widely quoted and faithful but translation-dependent, to verify against a named edition.
