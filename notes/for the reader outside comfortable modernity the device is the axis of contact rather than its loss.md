# for the reader outside comfortable modernity the device is the axis of contact rather than its loss

The why-now literature is written from one standpoint: inside a comfortable, sated modernity that once held unmediated fullness and feels it receding. Its remainder is the standpoint it never occupies. From outside comfortable modernity the diagnosis inverts. Rosa's resonance runs along axes — a horizontal axis toward other people, a vertical axis toward world, nature, and the sacred — and for the reader the shelf does not picture (the disabled user, the isolated queer adolescent, the diaspora migrant), the device is those axes rather than their enemy: the phone is what carries the beloved and the sacred across a distance the body cannot cross. For such a reader the loss-narrative has no floor, because it presupposes a prior fullness that reader never held. The literature barely registers this — Odell nods once to privilege and Rosa gestures at a universal basic income — and neither occupies the excluded standpoint from which the device is a lifeline.

The inversion bears on the book twice. It qualifies the book's why-now, which cannot assume a universal prior fullness to have been lost, and it qualifies the book's reader-definition, since the reader "already on their way" is not the only reader and for some the mediated relation is the whole relation. The vault already holds one measured fact on the device's side: the working alliance keeps its strength when the exchange is written and mediated ([[the alliance holds its size when the exchange is mediated and written]]), which is the empirical counterpart to this inference — a mediated field can still do the field's work.

## Substantiation

- This is the dig's own remainder, an inference [I per dig 5] rather than a reading: no primary text supplies it, and the dig marks it as the standpoint the shelf structurally omits. It is admitted as a candidate the author may seat or decline, and the empirical question it opens — whether mediated relation erodes or merely changes the activities of meaning-making — the record cannot rule, because the strata supply mechanism and no longitudinal evidence.
- Rosa's axes (horizontal toward people, diagonal toward objects through work and skill, vertical toward world and the sacred) are the frame the inversion turns; they were reached in the Schiermer interview [P per dig 5], while the fuller theory in *Resonanz* (2016) is queued in `ops/reading queue.md`, which is why the note is held nascent.
