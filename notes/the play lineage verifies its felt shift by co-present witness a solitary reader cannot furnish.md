# the play lineage verifies its felt shift by co-present witness a solitary reader cannot furnish

The dig's felt-shift audit finds that verification across the whole play lineage is uniformly third-person-observable co-present behavior joined to a shared felt flow — precisely what a reader alone with a book cannot furnish. In Spolin the teacher sees it: the space-object made real, the player no longer watching the self, with the group's perception serving as the instrument, so the shift is felt but witnessed. In Johnstone the scene flows when offers are accepted and dies visibly when they are blocked, and status reads off the body, so the failure is public and immediate. In Winnicott the capacity to play is itself the sign of health, read by an observer. In Turner communitas is verified as a felt flow and a levelling among the co-present, unwitnessable alone by definition.

The finding sharpens the book's verification problem rather than adding a new one. The book verifies on a felt shift a solitary reader is supposed to register in their own body, and this lineage shows a whole family of practices that verify the same kind of event by putting a witness in the room. The lineage's own anti-compliance guards — Spolin withdrawing approval, Johnstone taking the blame — disable the approval-seeking that drives a demand-produced report, but they relocate the demand to the group or the game rather than abolishing it, and co-presence keeps it high. The witness the lineage relies on is the same second person the book's verification standard already needs and cannot seat on the page.
