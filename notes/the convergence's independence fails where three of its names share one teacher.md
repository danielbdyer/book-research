# the convergence's independence fails where three of its names share one teacher

The book's foundation is a convergence two traditions reached with no historical contact ([[aletheia and pratyabhijna converge on truth as un-forgetting]]), and the crossover research of 2026-08-03 assembled a field of thinkers around it. The mesh grid applies the independence test to that field and it fails: "Nishitani studied under Heidegger in Freiburg. Corbin translated him into French in 1938. Henry built his system against him. Three of the four names currently under consideration are in conversation with the same German. That is a family quarrel, not a convergence" (`mesh grid: tier 3`).

Every link in that chain is already in this vault, established by the crossover research itself and recorded in the notes for each thinker. What the vault had not done is put them side by side and read the result as a property of the set. Agreement among people who studied the same teacher is evidence about the teacher's reach, not about the world.

This is the second road to a finding the vault already holds by another. The first was about assembly: the lineages were gathered on one day for the participatory property they are then reported to share, so the unanimity is an artifact of selection ([[the unanimity claim is an artifact of how the lineages were selected]]). This one is about descent, and it survives independently of how the room was assembled — even a roster gathered blind would fail the test if its members share a teacher. Two different arguments reaching the same place is worth more than either alone, and it sharpens what the remaining work is: the convergence needs arrivals from outside the lineage, not more members of it.

## Substantiation

- Every relation is checkable in the vault's own record and each was checked. Nishitani of the Kyoto School studied under Heidegger in Freiburg; Corbin's 1938 translation — the first Heidegger into French — is at [[henry corbin]]; Michel Henry's construction of ontological monism as the name for Heidegger's error is at [[michel henry]]. The shared background with the full citation trail is `archive/research — crossover cluster background.md`.
- The grid's independence test is the one applied: "could they have gotten there without reading anyone in Tier 1? If they share a teacher, it is not independence — it is a family resemblance, and it proves less than it appears to" (`mesh grid: how to read the pyramid`).
- The finding is `full` rather than `nascent` because it rests on facts the vault established itself, from primary-source research already completed. The grid supplied the test, not the evidence.
- One correction to the grid's own count, from the vault's record: the fourth name in its set is Polanyi, who does pass the test, and the vault holds several further names in the crossover cluster the grid did not sort — Mehta, Caputo, Ratié, Lawrence, Biernacki, Katz, Sheehan, Jain. Of those, Mehta, Caputo and Sheehan are also Heidegger scholars, so widening the count makes the finding worse rather than better. Ratié, Lawrence and Biernacki work on the recognition tradition and are outside the German line but inside the other pole, which is the same problem seen from the far side.
- What this does not touch, and the distinction matters: the founding convergence itself is between Heidegger and the recognition school, two traditions with no contact, and nothing here bears on that. The failure is in the field assembled *around* the convergence, whose members are mostly downstream of one of its two poles. The book's foundation is not what fails the test; its supporting company is.
- The consequence for the reading queue is the sort's third-ranked hole: arrivals from empirical science, systems and design, and analytic philosophy — Polanyi, von Foerster, Garfinkel, Wittgenstein — because that is where a witness with no debt to either pole could come from ([[the participation law has a witness who came to it through crystallography]], [[the always-there hides by being always there and wittgenstein said so without heidegger]]).
