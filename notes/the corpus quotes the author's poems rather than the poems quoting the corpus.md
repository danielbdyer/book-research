# the corpus quotes the author's poems rather than the poems quoting the corpus

The author states the direction of quotation for the fifteen poems that predate the book: "any phrases you see like 'the youngness is just beginning' are actually quotes from my poems and not the other way around" (author statement, 2026-08-03). The line itself closes poem five — a bird readying for flight: "the youngness is just beginning. and he is soon ready to go" (poems #5) — and the corpus carries it into the drafting thread's closing as "the youngness — check the almanac — is just beginning."

The direction generalizes past phrases to doctrines, and the worked instances are worth listing because each one re-dates something the vault holds. The definition of practice as return stands in poem twelve as a litany: "even leaving, beginning again; even older, beginning again; even crying, beginning again; always trying, beginning again" (poems #12) — the returning-is-the-practice definition, at life scale, in the author's own voice, before any curriculum existed. The fork the corpus leaves to the body stands in poem seven as a single breath: "complete surrender / and trying one's very hardest" (poems #7) — surrender and will held together without adjudication, years before the eleven teachers were gathered around the same fork. The already-complete doctrine stands in poem fifteen: "the inside is not required to be entered from the outside since I am already there" (poems #15). And recognition and the clearing share a couplet in poem ten: "a crescendo clearing the mist / a recognition therewith" (poems #10).

This is the deepest stratum the vault can date, and it changes what the welding finding means. The corpus's correspondences were found by the collaborator for figures that already existed; the figures carried a seed that already existed; and beneath the seed, the concerns stand in these poems with no collaborator at all.

- The direction claim is the author's and covers poems 1–15; the sixteenth is exempted by his own flag as the divergent poem, and its reverse flow is recorded separately ([[the sixteenth poem returns the book's language to the life it came from]]).

- The negative finding is as load-bearing as the positive one: the book's figures — the tuning fork, the orchard, the gold thread, the house with no ceiling — appear nowhere in the collection. The poems hold the concerns; the seed era coined the figures; the ontology era built the system. Three strata, each datable, each in its own vocabulary.
- The one poem-era phrase the corpus attributes to the author it attributes correctly: the provenance reconstruction called the youngness line "your line," and the poems now show which poem it is from.
