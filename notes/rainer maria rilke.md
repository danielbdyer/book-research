# rainer maria rilke

Rilke holds the first chapter-patron seat in the corpus's roster, and the one artwork in the vault's record that performs the book's promised event is his. The Archaic Torso of Apollo ends: "denn da ist keine Stelle, / die dich nicht sieht. Du mußt dein Leben ändern" — there is no place that does not see you; you must change your life (Neue Gedichte, anderer Teil, 1908). The mechanism matters more than the famous ending: the torso has no eyes — "Wir kannten nicht sein unerhörtes Haupt, / darin die Augenäpfel reiften" — and the seeing survives in the stone anyway, "sein Torso glüht noch wie ein Kandelaber, / in dem sein Schauen, nur zurückgeschraubt, / sich hält und glänzt." An eyeless thing sees the viewer, and the seeing commands a change of life. That is recognition arriving from a made object — the book's event, performed in fourteen lines, published a century before the book was conceived.

The artist's-chair finding is that this was craft, not visitation. The poem opens the volume Rilke dedicated to Rodin, whose working discipline — go and look, always work — he had absorbed as the sculptor's secretary; the thing-poems are the harvest of trained looking at things until the thing looks back, the practice his Malte names as "Ich lerne sehen," I am learning to see. Recognition-production was his daily employment. The Letters to a Young Poet carry the reader-facing half of the same craft: "Es kann Ihnen niemand raten und helfen, niemand… Gehen Sie in sich" — nobody can advise and help you, nobody; go into yourself (letter 1, 1903) — and the fourth letter's "Leben Sie jetzt die Fragen," live the questions now, with the answer arriving unnoticed, "eines fernen Tages," some distant day.

Two refusals are kept, both verified this round. He refused analysis in January 1912, and the reason prices every repair-program the book also declines: "wenn man mir meine Teufel austriebe, auch meinen Engeln ein kleiner… Schrecken geschähe" — if my devils were driven out, my angels too would take a small fright (letter to Gebsattel, Jan 1912). The interior is one weather; the dark is not a removable part. And he refused analytic handling of art itself: "Kunst-Werke sind von einer unendlichen Einsamkeit und mit nichts so wenig erreichbar als mit Kritik. Nur Liebe kann sie erfassen" — works of art are of an infinite solitude, reachable least of all by criticism; only love can grasp them (letter 3, 1903). Held whole, the second refusal cautions this book's own use of the torso: a poem conscripted as apparatus is being reached for with exactly the instrument he refused.

Provenance flags, recorded at capture: the circulating English sentence "if my devils are to leave me, my angels will take flight as well" is a paraphrase blending two letters and appears verbatim in neither; the devils-and-angels letter carries a date discrepancy in the sources (14 versus 24 January 1912); verify both against Briefe 1907–1914 before manuscript use.
