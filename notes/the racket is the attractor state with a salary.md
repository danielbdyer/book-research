# the racket is the attractor state with a salary

The racket carries the speech lineage's sharpest diagnostic: a fixed complaint paired with a fixed payoff, a suffering that persists because it earns. The figure changes the diagnostic question from what hurts to what the hurting pays — the one question the privation formulas cannot ask, since a racket is not missing anything.

## Substantiation

- Arc-gloss, the image-system: "The Racket ↔ Landmark's fixed-complaint-plus-fixed-payoff ↔ the attractor state with a salary. The diagnostic that asks not 'what hurts?' but 'what does the hurting pay?'"
- The treatment adds it to the catalogue as a compound — "the Racket = Complaint + Payoff, both fixed" (arc-gloss: ch6) — and to the diagnostic kit as the Payoff Question (arc-gloss: ch15). The atlas seats the donor lineage and its standing warning at §IX.3.
- The figure is second-edition material and does not reach the executed draft; see [[the executed draft carries the arc's sequence without the treatment's five rooms]].
