# the question is not how to avoid influence but how to influence without appropriating

The reframe conversation holds that facilitation is not neutral: the facilitator inevitably shapes a participant's attention, framing, language, pacing, possibility, and emotional tone. Because influence cannot be removed, the question "How do I avoid influence?" is the wrong question. The conversation replaces it with "How do I influence without appropriating?" — which keeps the facilitator's effect while barring the seizure of the participant's meaning and authorship. The conversation marks this substitution as one of the book's central questions, since it converts an unachievable neutrality into a workable ethic of non-appropriation.

## Substantiation

- The reframe conversation states that "Facilitation is not neutral" and that "The facilitator inevitably influences" attention, framing, language, pacing, possibility, and emotional tone. It then reframes the governing question: "The question therefore cannot be: 'How do I avoid influence?' It becomes: 'How do I influence without appropriating?'," adding that "This is one of the central questions of the entire book" (reframe conversation, 2026-08-13: influence without ownership).
