# the field note is the membrane between lived encounter and persistent concept

The conversation names the field note as possibly "one of the most useful ways to think about" the whole system, and gives it a precise structural role: it is the membrane between two things the design keeps distinct. The encounter belongs to lived time; the concept belongs to persistent knowledge; the field note sits between them. Its function is the function of a membrane — it allows material to cross from experience into knowledge while preserving the distinction between experience and abstraction, so that the crossing does not collapse the one into the other. The design states the direction as a diagram: experience, then field note, then knowledge.

The structure is container anatomy applied inside the system. A membrane is a boundary that is permeable in a governed way, which is the book's own account of a container's edge, and the field note is exactly that edge between the lived and the recorded. Without it, either experience is admitted raw as though it were knowledge, or knowledge is held with no lived ancestry; with it, the system can remain faithful to lived process while still accumulating durable structure.
