# the circle is the form in which the naked sentence gets met

The circle carries the facilitator's container: the held round in which a true sentence, said aloud, is received rather than left hanging. It is the relational field's answer to what a practice looks like when witnessing is the mechanism.

## Substantiation

- Arc-gloss, the image-system: "The Circle ↔ the witnessed round ↔ the facilitator's container: the form in which the naked sentence gets met. Pays into Ch. 8, Kata 9, and the book's deepest practice claim."
- The figure is one of the second edition's four additions and is part of the five-rooms material that does not reach the executed draft; see [[the executed draft carries the arc's sequence without the treatment's five rooms]].
