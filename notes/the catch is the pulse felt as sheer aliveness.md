# the catch is the pulse felt as sheer aliveness

The figure gives the pulse its plainest lived statement: an ordinary catch of attention in which a person is arrested into sheer aliveness and can do nothing but be wholly present. The author met the pulse at a meditation retreat, where the tradition's word for it — spanda — named "that fundamental pulse, the liveness of what it is to exist and be an expression of the universe," and he recognized the same thing in the small arrests of everyday experience. The figure is the felt ground beneath the vault's more technical uses of the pulse, and the vault holds it as the author's lived encounter with the tradition rather than as a doctrine the book is built on.

## Substantiation

This is the author's own account of a lived experience, held under the three conditions of `ops/methodology/personal provenance.md` and recorded at the specificity the record supplies.

- The author names the pulse and its lived signature in one breath. The tradition's term is "that fundamental pulse... the liveness of what it is to exist and be an expression of the universe," and it shows in the moment "you just sort of like catch and you're just like, whoa, like I can't do anything but do this in this moment" (reframe conversation, 2026-08-13, transcript line 52). He reaches it through ordinary occasions — a meditative state, the edge of a sneeze, a stubbed toe, an orgasm, the sight of flowers or a mountain — and reports that it "snapped into place" as what meditation had been pointing to all along, "what that finger to the moon is pointing to."
- The encounter is felt origin and not the book's foundation. The reconciliation register keeps the felt experience and the dated record apart: the lived meeting with the pulse is admissible primary material, while the book's structure predates the author's first formal contact with the tradition, so the tradition is held as the deepest well among many rather than the book's substrate (register Fork 8). This figure records the felt half and makes no claim of provenance from it.
- The vault's technical uses of the pulse rest on this lived instant. The pulse supplies the vocabulary for the rhythm of inquiry ([[the pulse of consciousness supplies a vocabulary for the rhythm of inquiry]]) and the ratio behind the per-chapter design ([[the per-chapter dial is a spanda ratio]]); the catch is the experience those instruments abstract from, the plainest form of the aliveness they are tuned to.
