# the jargon charge indicts its own author's prose and supplies no criterion exempting legitimate presence-language

The register attack has a return edge, and it is Adorno's own. His prose is itself maximally auratic and difficult, charged with performing the very non-falsifiable depth the charge condemns, so the weapon cuts the hand that holds it. More consequentially, he supplies no criterion that would separate a legitimate use of presence-language from jargon — no test that says *this* auratic word is earned and *that* one is not — which leaves the charge itself unfalsifiable in his hands, applying alike to any elevated register including his own. Habermas names the general form of this defect as a performative contradiction: a critique that discredits all elevated or totalizing speech discredits the speech in which it is itself conducted. The finding for the book is that the register attack, at full strength, proves too much: if it condemns presence-language as such, it condemns the philosophical prose that condemns it, and it can offer the book no line to write on the right side of.

The consequence is not that the attack fails but that it cannot stand alone as a verdict. Without a criterion, the charge is a suspicion the book must answer sentence by sentence rather than a rule it has already lost, which is why the register attack is held as a live tension with a determinacy test rather than as a prohibition on the register.

## Substantiation

- The dig records the failure on target: the *Jargon* "turns on its author," whose prose is "maximally auratic and difficult," and "he supplies no criterion separating legitimate presence-language from jargon, so the charge is itself unfalsifiable in his hands (Habermas: performative contradiction)" (transmission dig 6: FAILURES). The dig also notes the biographical irony — the theorist of the non-administered life called police on his occupying students in 1969 — which the note records as context rather than as argument.
- The depth is thin and stated: whether Adorno anywhere supplies the missing criterion is precisely the queued reading (dig 6, READINGS TO QUEUE item 2), so the note is marked `nascent`; if he does, the return edge dulls and the register attack gains a real gate rather than only a mood.
- This note is the reason the register attack stays a tension: it is the documented weakness that keeps the charge from resolving into a rule against presence-language.
- The finding does not rescue any particular auratic word; it shifts the burden from a blanket prohibition to a per-word determinacy test the book runs itself.
