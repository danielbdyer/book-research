# the routing surfaces carry four more names alone than the sort filed as company

The reading queue had asked since 2026-08-06 whether a completed function-count over the corpus's routing surfaces would turn up a third name that alone carries a claim while filed among the company, starting with Scharmer, and it had never run. It ran on 2026-09-02, across atlas §IX in full, walked IX, arc-gloss ch. 7 and ch. 13, the first-telling, and the executed draft, against the mesh grid's Tier 5 roster. It does not come back empty. The third name is Donella Meadows, not Scharmer, and three more stand behind her.

Meadows holds two of the sixteen chapter-patron seats — the Molecules of Experience and the diagnostic chapter — which no other name below the front row does; her systems credo is the Molecules chapter's "whole disposition" (walked IV: "we cannot control systems, but we can dance with them"); and in the executed draft she carries the book's answer to its own hardest structural objection, the argument that the molecular vocabulary is not arbitrary jargon — "Donella Meadows… arrived at a list of twelve places to intervene in a system, in order of increasing leverage… The molecular vocabulary, in this sense, is a paradigm intervention" (abridged pp. 19–20) — and the book's closing self-description, "The architecture of this book is an architecture for dancing" (p. 46). If she leaves, two patron seats empty, the Molecules chapter loses its disposition, the diagnostic chapter loses its ethic, and the defence of the scaffolding loses its only support. That is a larger structural hole than Hudson's, made by a name the grid put in its base tier and the vault never gave a chair.

Behind her, Parker, Le Guin, and Eno are three of the exactly five names the atlas and walked both use to demonstrate the book's first axiom, that the Triad is found rather than invented, each holding one medium alone: "Alexander's rooms — literally; Gendlin's felt-sense sessions — procedurally; Parker's gatherings — socially; Le Guin's worlds — imaginally; Eno's generative systems — algorithmically" (atlas §II.2; walked II). The other two are Alexander and Gendlin, and Gendlin is the first misfiled load-bearer the vault ever found. The demonstration of the foundational axiom is three-fifths Tier 5, which is the exact case the grid's own Tier 1 discipline names: "If a claim has no Tier 1 name, either it is not load-bearing or it is being carried by the prose. The second is the dangerous case."

## Substantiation

- **Method.** Function-count per name, matching how Hudson's six were counted — holds a position alone; referral destination; vocabulary in a convergence table; runs a practice; sits in two rooms; explains in the first-telling — plus one function Hudson does not have and the original count therefore never looked for: chapter patronage. All searches word-bounded; the roster is the grid's Tier 5 (p. 8) with the addendum's additions (p. 12).
- **Meadows's functions, with locators.** Patron of the Molecules of Experience (arc-gloss p. 9; atlas §IX.9; atlas §IV, "~15 curated for the book (Ch. 6, patron: Meadows)"; walked X) and of the diagnostic chapter (arc-gloss p. 15, "Meadows as patron (listening as the scarcest resource)"); container-maker (atlas §IX.4; walked IX); the chapter's governing disposition (walked IV); the executed draft's justification of the molecular scheme (abridged pp. 19–20) and statement of its own architecture (p. 46), with two epigraphs (pp. 17, 43); the first-telling roll call, second slot after Alexander. Three things held alone: the leverage-points ordering, the corpus's only ranking of interventions by leverage; the dance-not-control credo; and "listening as the scarcest resource," which appears nowhere else in the corpus.
- **Scharmer, the queued name, is the weakest of the five and was already recounted by the grid itself.** He holds alone only "presencing — the open will ahead of the plan" (atlas §IX.4; walked IX); his presencing is one vocabulary among a dozen in the convergence table (arc-gloss ch. 13), so that function is shared; he is the only Tier 5 name the executed draft stops to explain (abridged p. 40), and what he describes there — "what happens at the bottom of the U-curve, after the descent into not-knowing, before the ascent" — is the book's own arc restated by an outside name, unremarked in the corpus. The grid's addendum re-files him: "Otto Scharmer (F/H) — Tier 4. Presencing, the open will ahead of the plan. Machinery-adjacent rather than directional; sits with the Loops" (mesh grid p. 12). The vault's line that he "is filed in the grid's Tier 5" was true of the pyramid at p. 8 and superseded at p. 12.
- **The rest of the roster.** Patron seats held alone by Kimmerer (the Way of Ripening), brown (the Kata), and Whyte (Erotic-Spiritual Intelligence), each carried by a slogan or an epigraph rather than a mechanism; marginal single functions for Carse ("krīḍā in game-theoretic clothing"), Akomolafe ("the times are urgent; let us slow down," carrying the executed draft's pacing argument), and Oliver, whose question is the executed draft's last (p. 46); Colonna and the Conscious Leadership Group, Crone, Katie, and Singer, Wineland and Deida hold their theories jointly; Hendricks has zero occurrences anywhere in the corpus. None of the names in this note has a chair.
- **The instrument property, which outlasts the names.** Gendlin and Hudson were both found by asking what happens if a name leaves; this count found four more by asking which functions the corpus actually gives a name, and the decisive function was invisible to a count modelled on Hudson because Hudson does not have it. The recorded property holds and needs one more clause: a sort run once reproduces its blind spot until something recounts, and a recount modelled on the last exception reproduces that exception's shape ([[the company was sorted by what each thinker brings and never by what the book cannot lose]]).
- **Two concordance rows fell out of the count.** The arc-gloss second edition assigns all sixteen patron seats (Whyte, Kimmerer, Rumi, Eliot, Tippett, brown, Meadows for the later units), so "the later seats rotating" was the vault's reading, not the corpus's; and atlas §IX.9 differs from arc-gloss at two seats — Lao Tzu against Heraclitus for the Generative Triad, hooks alone against Buber with hooks for the Third Entity — recorded in `ops/chapter concordance.md`.
- What this does not establish: that any of the four should be seated, which is the author's; or that Meadows's leverage ladder is sound. The claim is about the sorting.
