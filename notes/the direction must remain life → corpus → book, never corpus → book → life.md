# the direction must remain life → corpus → book, never corpus → book → life

The conversation distinguishes three layers and fixes the direction that must hold among them. Life is where the encounters actually happen; the corpus is where encounters are remembered, related, and studied; the book is where selected durable perspectives are composed into an encounter architecture for others. The direction of flow must remain life to corpus to book, and must never run corpus to book to life. The prohibition names the failure precisely: the reverse direction "would mean living to populate the system" — arranging a life so as to feed the corpus, rather than letting the corpus record a life already being lived.

The claim is a making-integrity rule rather than a data-model detail. It sits directly above the vault's founding measure, that the vault is judged by what reaches the manuscript, and it states the deeper condition beneath that measure: the whole apparatus is downstream of a life, and the moment the apparatus becomes the thing life is for, the order that made the work worth doing has inverted. The author had already stated the same boundary in his own words in the exchange — "organize what needs to be organized," not everything that can be organized — with lived experience, the research corpus, and the final book named as overlapping but non-identical sets.

## Substantiation

- The three layers and the direction, from the section the model titles the book / corpus / life relationship: Life is "where the encounters actually happen," the Corpus is "where encounters are remembered, related, and studied," the Book is "where selected durable perspectives are composed into an encounter architecture for others"; "The direction must remain: Life → Corpus → Book," not "Corpus → Book → Life," because "the latter would mean living to populate the system" (reframe conversation, 2026-08-13, line 6676).
- The author's own prior statement of the boundary, from the adjacent section on the system's boundary: "Organize what needs to be organized," with the caution that some things belong to "lived experience," some to "the research corpus," and some to "the final book," and that the sets "overlap, but they are not identical."
- The framing is the model's reconstruction of a rule the author had already voiced; the note records the direction and the failure its reverse names.
