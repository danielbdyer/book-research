# reflexive awareness is participatory recognition rather than detached observation

The book specifies what kind of act reflexive awareness is. When awareness turns on itself, it is tempting to picture a spectator standing back and inspecting experience from a distance; the book denies this picture. Reflexive awareness recognizes itself from inside its own activity, so the one who becomes aware is a participant in the very field being noticed rather than an observer positioned outside it. The book carries this into both of its halves symmetrically: the facilitator's reflexive act is to notice "I am participating in this field," and the practitioner's is to notice "I am participating in the way I am experiencing myself." In each case the noticing does not detach the person from what is happening; it is one more way of being in it.
