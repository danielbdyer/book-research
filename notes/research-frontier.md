# research-frontier

This map holds the project's outward-facing research program. It inherits its method from the space-around survey, which walked the book's argument idea by idea and looked outward in three directions at each stop: crossovers (connections between materials the project already holds), broadenings (genuinely new neighbors), and counterweights (honest opposition worth keeping close). The survey's admission bar governs this map and is recorded in [[methods]]: nothing gets listed unless it would actually change how the project thinks.

The survey's own summary judgment: its strongest finds were not new allies but new resistances — the project's current need is stronger opposition, not more agreement.

## The survey's priority five

1. [[gregory bateson]] — his double bind and levels of learning would underwrite three separate central pieces of the framework at once.
2. [[ignatius of loyola]] — the Spiritual Exercises prove the book's central design gamble has a genre and a safety record.
3. [[internal family systems]] — the closest clinical relative; the book needs a stated position relative to it.
4. [[hartmut rosa]] — his concept of resonance, including its uncontrollability clause, completes the theory behind the book's closing image.
5. [[felt shift versus felt confirmation]] — already admitted as a tension; the survey ranks its two humility clauses as first-order because they make every other claim more credible.

## Adjacent maps

Evaluated candidates move to [[interlocutors]] with a seat, or are declined with the reason retained. Counterweights that survive evaluation get tension notes, listed in [[tensions]].

## What an evaluation is

Evaluating a candidate means: read the thinker's relevant primary work (or the concept's primary literature); write an interlocutor note containing the case for a seat, the case against, and the specific connections to existing claims; apply the admission standard (would this actually change how the project thinks?); and end with a recommendation. The author decides the seat. A decline is recorded, not deleted.

## What remains

The pending-evaluation list in `ops/queue/candidates.md` holds roughly sixty entries, none yet researched beyond what the survey itself says about them. The recommended order: the priority five above first, then the rest in the book's own order so that frontier work tracks manuscript work.
