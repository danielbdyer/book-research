# every discrimination the molecule formulas are credited with is made by the prose beside them

The book's third layer writes states of experience as formulas — `Burnout = Obligation − Sanctuary`, `Trust = Bind + Commit + Defer` — and each formula is a list: an unordered collection of terms joined by plus signs, with one subtraction available. What a formula can say is which operations are present and which one is missing. Nothing else is expressible in it.

Every teaching the chemistry actually delivers asks for something else. Trust and paralysis are distinguished by **where** the deferral attaches. Forgiveness is distinguished from spiritual bypass by the **order** of two operations. Grief is distinguished from a completable process by requiring an **agent outside the compound**. The Racket is diagnosed by its terms being **fixed** rather than by which terms they are. Enmeshment turns on a **capacity** rather than an act. Attending and committing share both their atoms and differ in **temporal extent**. And a minus sign cannot say whether the missing element was withheld or was never on offer, which asks after the missing term's **provenance**.

Each of those is a relation: between two atoms, between an atom and time, between the compound and the world. None is a fact about membership. So in every case the corpus states the relation in words beside the formula and the formula does not hold it — "where Defer attaches," the word *then*, the label *catalyst: witness*, the phrase *both fixed*, a suffix, a parenthesis. **The notation records composition, the chemistry teaches structure, and the prose closes the gap in every instance.**

Two consequences follow, and both are checkable without agreeing to the general claim.

**The notation's one relational operator is its one working operator.** The minus sign is the only mark that states a relation rather than a membership: it says a term is absent rather than merely unlisted. Measured independently and before the audit that explains it, the minus is also the only operator that predicts anything. Across the executed catalogue a formula containing a minus is followed by an instruction and a formula of plus signs alone is followed by a description, without exception. The edition states the principle without connecting it to the notation: "In the molecular vocabulary, the diagnosis is the prescription. Burnout = Obligation − Sanctuary. The minus sign tells you precisely what to restore… The structure of the problem contains the structure of its medicine" (abridged ch6).

**The closure that would make the twelve an alphabet rather than a selection is asserted three times and demonstrated nowhere.** No source names a candidate thirteenth verb, shows a reduction, or records the test occurring, and the claim's grammar drifts toward certainty with distance from the event — "run on the page" in the notation-dense atlas, "was actually run" in the prose walk, "the set is closed" in the letter to a friend. The phrase that carries it, that no one proposed a thirteenth, describes a room containing the author and one collaborator.

## Substantiation

**The three on-page failures, all in `abridged ch5`, where the six derived primitives are composed from the six base ones.** Two distinct operations receive the same expression: "ATTEND = Maintain + Bind" appears at one point and "COMMIT = Bind + Maintain" thirteen lines later in the same list, with identical component sets and no comment, while the text plainly means two operations and states their difference — a connection held now against a connection held across time — in words the formula does not contain. Two of the six reach outside the alphabet for their second term: "CONTAIN = Maintain + the boundary-form of the Knife" and "DEFER = Maintain + the not-yet of Create," where neither qualifier is one of the six base primitives, so the closure claim fails for a third of the derived set on the page asserting it. And substitution collapses the molecules: Obligation = Commit + Attend + Maintain (abridged ch6) reduces through the chapter's own definitions to Bind + Maintain, which is the expression for Commit and for Attend, so the molecule and its own component are one formula.

**The corpus supplies the honest defense, and the defense costs the notation its remaining claim.** Molecules are defined as "stable combinations of 3+ primitives with emergent attributes none of the atoms have alone" (atlas §IV), so composition is meant as emergence rather than as arithmetic and substitution was never licensed. On that reading the expressions are mnemonics for propositions stated in prose, and the discriminations the book most depends on are carried by the prose alone.

**The isomer teaching is the clearest instance, and it names the phenomenon that made this notation obsolete elsewhere.** Real isomerism distinguishes compounds by the arrangement of bonds, and this notation has no bond term, since `Bind + Commit + Defer` is a commutative list. The atlas states the discrimination correctly in words — "the difference is where Defer attaches — to the outcome (Trust) or to the Commit itself (Paralysis)" (atlas §IV) — which is precisely what the formula cannot hold. The historical shape is the same one: isomerism named around 1830 was the anomaly that forced structural formulas in 1858, and the book borrows the anomaly while writing in the superseded form. **That history is stated from general knowledge and is the privation on this note**; no work on it has been read here.

**The arithmetic failures the full audit produced.** `Stagnation = Lifecycle − Transform` collapses to Transform's own formula, so the state defined by lacking the operation is the operation. Trust and Paralysis cannot be both isomers and privations, because the privation formula leaves them sharing one atom of three. Six of the twenty-one formulas use a term the corpus never defines, and several of those are the relational terms above smuggled in as nouns, since a *capacity* is a modality and *both fixed* is a state of the terms rather than a term.

**The one formula that resists the sort is informative rather than anomalous.** Grief is written with plus signs and is not a description of a healthy condition, and the edition supplies its missing term as prose rather than notation: "some reactions require a catalyst, and the catalyst for grief is a witness" (abridged ch6). Where the missing element is a person rather than an operation, the notation cannot carry it, which is the general finding seen from the other side ([[grief is sever plus love plus persist]]).

**The working behind this note.** The finding rests on a formula-by-formula inventory that checked all twenty-one formulas from the four sources that write any, for whether their terms are defined, whether substitution holds, and where each teaching sits. Nine of the twenty-one are prose-carried in the sense above, and the nine sort onto one table of relations the notation has no term for. The twenty-one-row ledger is reconstructible from the extracted corpus and is not held as a standing file.

**One gap in this note's own coverage, flagged for the record.** The inventory writes the paralysis discrimination — where Defer attaches, to the outcome or to the Commit itself — but records no discrete entry for Ambivalence, the third of the held-tension molecules the atlas lists at "Tension (held oppositions): Possibility · Trust · Ambivalence" (atlas §IV). A note reaching here for the ambivalence formula therefore finds only that family placement rather than a formula-by-formula reading, which is where [[four molecule names stand in the poems as poem words with their contents]] draws its ambivalence content from.

**What follows for the book is a fork rather than a verdict, and it is the author's.** Either the notation gains relational terms, which is a research program, or the relations stay in prose and the formulas become a display device rather than a diagnostic one, which is what the executed draft already largely does and is consistent with the demotion of the schemes to instruments (2026-08-04). The vault states the fork and does not choose it. One precedent for the first route exists inside this vault and cost almost nothing: the `state:` field inherited a bare privation mark from the same ontology project the molecules came from, found it unusable for exactly the reason the minus sign is, and now requires a clause naming the absence.
