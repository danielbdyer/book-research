# martin orne

Martin Orne was a psychiatrist and experimental psychologist whose 1962 paper named a defect that any claim resting on reported experience has to clear. Demand characteristics are, in his definition, "the totality of cues which convey an experimental hypothesis to the subject." The subject is not passive but a problem-solver who infers what the situation wants and, wanting to be a good subject, supplies it — Orne's subjects persisted for hours at a pointless task because being told "this is an experiment" made the meaningless meaningful. Because ordinary controls do not catch this, he proposed quasi-controls: a preinquiry that gives the cues without the treatment; a postexperimental inquiry, which a tacit pact of ignorance between subject and experimenter tends to defeat; and the signature simulating-subject design, in which instructed fakers are run past a blind assessor, so that if the fake cannot be told from the real the treatment is unproven.

## Why the sort wants him

Reading a beautiful presence-book by an admired author, with cues throughout that a shift belongs here, is a maximal-demand setting in Orne's exact sense, so a felt shift reported inside it may be a demand characteristic rather than a durable change ([[a felt shift reported in a high-demand setting may be a demand characteristic rather than a durable change]]). His instrument states the book's verification crisis precisely: an in-situ felt shift cannot be told from a response-expectancy artifact without a check administered from outside the room ([[an in-situ felt shift cannot be told from a response-expectancy artifact without an out-of-room check]]). And his sharpest device is the one the book most needs and structurally cannot run, because a book can never run its own blind control — the reader is always both the subject who feels the shift and the assessor who certifies it ([[the simulating-subject test cannot be run inside a book because the reader is both subject and assessor]]).

Orne does not attack alone. His demand characteristic and Adorno's non-falsifiable interiority are the same defect named from two disciplines, converging without contact, and both attacks blame the same absence — the missing correction by another person ([[both attacks on the standard blame the absence of correction by another person]]). That shared root is why the book's answer is not a better solitary check but a second chair ([[the second chair is the verification standard's own requirement]]).

## What he would refuse

The provenance is secondary: Dig VI did not cleanly open Orne's 1962 paper — the PDF was binary-encoded — and worked from scholarly knowledge at [S], with the paper queued in `ops/reading queue.md`.

Two refusals, and the first is aimed at the construct itself. The demand-characteristic idea leans on the very self-report it distrusts, since the postexperimental inquiry needs truthful reports of an awareness the pact of ignorance withholds; and it is a near-universal solvent, because almost any result can be reattributed to demand, which dissolves genuine effects along with artifacts and so resists being made to fail ([[the demand-characteristic construct dissolves genuine effects along with artifacts and so resists being made to fail]]). The second refusal runs in the book's favor. Orne's own strongest form predicts that demand-produced effects vanish when the demand is removed, so the attack explains a shift's origin and not its durability, and the one thing a simulator cannot fake is out-of-situation, third-party-verified change ([[the compliance attack explains a felt shift's origin but not its durability]]).

## What his medium contributes

Orne's contribution to the book's medium is a bar and a boundary rather than a device. The bar is that a felt result counts as evidence only when it carries what its setting cannot supply, a delayed and other-administered check; installed as a standard, this is what stops the medium from crediting its own suggestion. The boundary is that his simulating-subject logic, taken seriously, does not dissolve the external verifier but demands one — so the attack that most threatens the book's standard also ratifies the second person the contemplative traditions already built into the loop. The book cannot borrow his method, because the reader cannot be blind to himself; it can only borrow his requirement.

## What the evaluation still requires

Until that reading, the load rests on scholarship rather than on the primary paper.
