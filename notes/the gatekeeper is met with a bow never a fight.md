# the gatekeeper is met with a bow never a fight

The gatekeeper carries the protective part, and the figure fixes the manner of approach: the part is honored as service and met with a bow, never fought. This is the contraction doctrine as an etiquette — the structure that walls a person in is treated as a guard who once had good reasons — and the peak material makes the same figure the anatomy of the bad trip.

## Substantiation

- Arc-gloss, the image-system: "The Gatekeeper ↔ the protective part ↔ saṅkoca honored as service — met with a bow, never a fight." Atlas §X.5g carries the same equation.
- The executed prose runs the figure at the peak — "The terror is the gatekeeper fighting what it perceives, accurately, to be its own dissolution… The gatekeeper has been afraid of an annihilation that is not coming" (abridged ch11) — and as a practice: "Bowing to the Gatekeeper… The part, met with respect, will usually relax" (abridged ch14).
