# the prose drafts use the vetoed triad names

The vault names the three structures of a container Wall, Door, and Floor, following the atlas. Atlas §II and walked II record that the structures were first called Knife, Thread, and Vessel, and that the author vetoed the earlier set with the sentence "nothing hides like the always-there," on the recorded reasoning that mythic names let a reader file the structures under mythology rather than recognize them in the room being sat in.

Both prose drafts use the vetoed names throughout, and neither shows any awareness of a renaming. The Arc and the Gloss treatment states the correspondence between the two sets in a single clause of its fourth chapter's job description, which makes that clause the translation table for reading either draft. The drafts therefore predate the veto on this axis, and a reader who meets Knife, Thread, and Vessel in them is meeting the same three structures the vault calls Wall, Door, and Floor, under names the author has already declined.

The dating is one-sided rather than global. It establishes that these two documents were written before the renaming was made; it does not establish their order relative to each other, or their order relative to any other decision recorded in the founding sources.

## Substantiation

- Atlas §II and walked II carry the veto and its reasoning; the correspondence and the veto sentence are recorded in [[the triad is the anatomy of every container]] and in the `notes/container-anatomy.md` map.
- The treatment states the crosswalk in one clause: "the Knife (differentiation — the walls that define Me), the Thread (relation — the doors that connect Us), the Vessel (persistence — the floor that holds Time)" (arc-gloss: ch4).
- The abridged edition names and derives the three at length under the vetoed names — "Knife. Thread. Vessel. We will call this triple, together, The Generative Triad" (abridged ch4) — and carries them forward into every later chapter, including the Stance's facets at `abridged ch13`, where the Knife and the Vessel are named as operating inside receptivity.
- The abridged edition also states, in the same chapter, the structural-sufficiency claim the vault records separately: the three are offered as enough, with no fourth or fifth required (abridged ch4).
