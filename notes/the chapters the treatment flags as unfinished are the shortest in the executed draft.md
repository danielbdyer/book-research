# the chapters the treatment flags as unfinished are the shortest in the executed draft

The Arc and the Gloss treatment names two chapters as not yet carrying their weight: chapter 9, which it calls the shortest in the scaffold and describes as awaiting an unlocking rather than a writing, and chapter 14, which it says has the most room to expand because its technical instruction was deferred to a companion volume. The abridged edition was written at a different scope and does not refer to the treatment's assessments. Counting its chapters puts chapter 9 at 820 words and chapter 14 at 829, the second and third shortest of the fifteen.

The agreement matters because the two documents are independent on this point. A private craft note and a word count are different kinds of evidence, and their convergence turns an authorial impression into a measured fact about where the manuscript is thin. For a decision that has to pick one chapter and finish it, this locates the two chapters that would need the most new writing, and by elimination the ones that would need the least.

The treatment's own superlative does not survive the count. It calls chapter 9 "the shortest chapter in the scaffold," and in the executed draft chapter 13 is shorter at 706 words. The treatment is describing its own storyboard entries rather than the prose, so the two statements are about different objects; the vault records the discrepancy rather than reconciling it.

## Substantiation

Extent of each chapter in the abridged edition, in words, shortest first: ch13 706, ch9 820, ch14 829, ch10 835, ch3 1012, ch12 1050, ch15 1054, ch8 1086, ch6 1115, ch4 1173, ch5 1254, ch2 1305, ch1 1658, ch11 2172, ch7 2236. The prologue runs 643 words and the epilogue 1654. Mean chapter length is 1160 words.

- The two flagged chapters are quoted in full at [[the treatment records four authorship conditions in its undercurrents]], which also carries the two conditions that cannot be measured.
- The two longest chapters, 7 and 11, are the structural core and the peak, which is consistent with the treatment's designation of each as a chapter the arc is built around (arc-gloss: ch7, ch11).
- Chapter 13 is short without being flagged. The treatment specifies its register as "still, spacious; the prose models the stance" (arc-gloss: ch13), so its brevity is a stated design rather than an omission — which is why extent alone does not identify an unfinished chapter, and the treatment's flags are needed to read the count.
- Craft standing: this note reports two independent measurements and their agreement. No judgment about the quality of either chapter is recorded, per `ops/methodology/craft evidence.md`.
