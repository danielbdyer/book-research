# the map that protects the destabilized reader is the tool that becomes a ranking

Two of the safety findings the book's company supplies prescribe opposite designs, and until now no note had stated the two together. For the reader who has been destabilized, the finding is that a map protects: Daniel Ingram holds that "vagueness about them is what harms people," and the measured version of the same finding is that having an accurate framework for a difficult state is itself a protective factor. Against handing a map over, the record of the thinkers seated as precedents, meaning the people who built a developmental scheme and gave it to practitioners, is unanimous. Wilber's "colour hierarchy became a status ladder among his readers". The Fourth Way's system of a vocabulary plus a teacher "acquires authority dynamics its founder did not intend". And Hofstadter, the one precedent among them whose scheme produced a good outcome, succeeded "partly by refusing to deliver a usable framework", a bargain whose precedent "does not transfer." Bateson warns about Learning III that some who go past the threshold "fall by the wayside… labeled by psychiatry as psychotic", and that warning belongs on the protective side, because it states why an accurate map matters at all.

The candidate resolution comes from reading these thinkers' cases against each other rather than from any one of them. Ingram's protective map is a sequence of expected difficulty, which tells a person where they are on a hard road, while Wilber's ladder is a hierarchy of attainment, which tells a person how high they stand. If that distinction holds, the deliverable can be split where a single design cannot be: the sequence is handed over and the hierarchy is withheld. That split is recorded here as a candidate treatment, derived from the thinkers' own cases rather than tested, and the design decision belongs to the author.

## Substantiation

- The protective side rests on Ingram's evaluation and on [[a framework for what is happening is itself a protective factor]], which reports the measured finding that having no framework for a difficult altered-state experience is among the predictors of harm. The ladder side rests on the evaluations of Wilber, Gurdjieff and Ouspensky, and Hofstadter, and Bateson's warning is anchored to his own text at his evaluation. Where a position is marked on a thinker's note as derived from works about them rather than read in their own work, this claim carries that standing forward.
- The two prior tensions this connects are [[the book cannot tell dissolution from regression and neither can its reader]], which records that the book names no way of telling a breakdown from a breakthrough and is the failure the map protects against, and [[a developmental scheme handed to practitioners becomes a ranking of persons]], which records that every documented staged map given to practitioners turned into a status hierarchy and is what the map becomes. Their two treatments currently point in opposite directions, and what this note adds is the statement that they do.
