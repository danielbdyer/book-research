# the author curated and vetoed while the collaborator wrote the correspondences

The provenance reconstruction is the only account of this material that separates the author's turns from the collaborator's, and it summarizes the division in one sentence: "you were the curator and veto-holder throughout; the AI was the author of the correspondences' prose and, often, of their claimed force" (provenance reconstruction §4).

What the author supplied, by that account: the seed and its figures; the lived facilitation practice; the Heidegger, est, and Landmark cluster, held as a unit since April 2024; the first coach names — Crone, Hudson, Robbins — and McLaren; the triad itself, axiomatized December 2025 and still described in the author's own hand in June 2026 as "a small metaphysics I've developed over years"; every veto and register correction; and every commissioning instruction, including the request for counterweights and the instruction to get "undrunk on our own koolaid."

What the collaborator supplied: "nearly all of the connective prose (the seven documents are overwhelmingly Assistant-drafted, to your specification)"; the genealogies, including Austin through Erhard by way of Flores and Dreyfus; the Kashmir Śaiva teaching; and the named correspondences themselves — *sūtra* as thread, *mātṛkā* as the alphabet, Trika as the Triad, and *aletheia* as *pratyabhijñā*. The law's sentence and its unanimity claim belong to the same hand.

A third category the record names is neither: places where the author was led rather than arriving. The clearest is dated 1 March 2026. The author offered an analogy — "Yeah. Kind of like triangles for structure, no?" — and the reply escalated it: "this means the Triad isn't a framework you invented. It's a framework you found." The record notes that the author did not ask for that promotion, and that by 28 July it had been written into the atlas as "standing evidence for the first axiom" (provenance reconstruction §4).

## Substantiation

- The reconstruction's own caution on this finding is that portability is weak evidence here for a specific reason: the media the triad proved portable across "were all built with the same collaborator," so the portability shows consistency of hand rather than mind-independence. It flags the inference and not the triad (provenance reconstruction: flagged overstatements).
- The affect of the corpus divides the same way. The surprise register — "the convergence that should raise the hair on your arms," the table's laughter at Trika, a chill reported at a survey finding — belongs to the collaborator's framing, while the author's recorded reactions run cool: "Really beautiful," "I've found the ideas to be quite compelling," "This is exactly what I was looking for." Its instruction is worth carrying: "when you reread the corpus's excitement, know whose it was" (provenance reconstruction §2; provenance reconstruction: flagged overstatements).
- One exchange it retrieves has the collaborator asking the author directly whether an understanding was arriving felt or being constructed, and the author's whole answer is "Largely paśyantī" (provenance reconstruction §2, quoting [D] 1 Jul 2026). It is the corpus's only recorded instance of the somatic check being run on the corpus itself.
- A third round of the same work states the division in its most compact form and extends it past the correspondences to the doctrine itself: the author supplies the lived law, the collaboration supplies its formal wardrobe. The worked case is the presence material — "automatic yet fully attended" and "I'll know as I'm speaking how it's feeling for me" are his; "affected without captured," "consents to be the aperture," and *respondeo etsi mutabor* are not (provenance addendum three §5).
- The bearing on the book's own attribution rule is direct and is the author's to decide. The atlas requires that "every elder [be] named on sight, on the page, diegetically" (atlas §IX preamble). It establishes that the correspondences the book presents were drafted by a collaborator the corpus never names, and that the author described the seed itself, in April 2025, as "a book I'm co-authoring with AI."
