# another person can only sound the note already strung in you so influence awakens rather than implants

The reader gains the deep reason that being changed by someone is not being taken over by them: what they awaken was already yours, waiting to be struck. This is why love and influence *enlarge* the self — they can only bring forth frequencies you already carry, never install foreign ones. The stranger who moves you to tears has not written the tears; they have found the string. Its use to the writing is to give the book's own resonance theory a physical model and to dissolve the fear of colonization at its root: a resonator cannot be given a note it does not already own.

## Substantiation

- Plato grounds the anamnesis pole at the primary text: learning is recollection, nothing implanted — "all enquiry and all learning is but recollection" (Meno 81d) — and Socratic midwifery brings forth what is already gestating, the associates finding "in themselves many fair things" and bringing "them forth" (Theaetetus 150c–d). The Pratyabhijñā pole is now read too: Utpaladeva's founding image is the beloved who, after much insistence, stands "in the presence of the maid in love" and yet "does not give her any pleasure until she recognizes who he is" — the qualities she already knew united at last with the one already present (Īśvarapratyabhijñākārikā IV.1.17, Torella); the word adds nothing, it only lets her recognize. The companion pole still owed: sympathetic resonance (Helmholtz, On the Sensations of Tone), strike one tuning fork and a second sounds *only if it already holds that frequency*, the first transferring energy, never pitch (from memory, grounding owed). Independent (Greek epistemology, Śaiva soteriology, 19th-c. physics).
- Refusal kept, and Plato's now placed: Plato's Forms are not the book's, and Socratic midwifery is one-directional; cited for the awakening-not-implanting structure, not the whole doctrine — the anamnesis method travels free of the pre-existent-soul metaphysics (see the reading record's seam).
- Standing: privated — the Plato anamnesis pole and the Pratyabhijñā pole read at the primary texts (Meno/Theaetetus; Utpaladeva IPK IV.1.17); the Helmholtz sympathetic-resonance pole still owed. This stays a reach: the physics is literal, its lift to persons analogical, and its risk is determinism (does the string then never learn a new note?), which the book keeps open rather than resolves.
