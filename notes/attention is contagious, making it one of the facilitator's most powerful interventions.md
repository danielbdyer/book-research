# attention is contagious, making it one of the facilitator's most powerful interventions

The reframe conversation claims that attention passes between people: what the facilitator attends to affects what the participant attends to. Because meaning, salience, and possibility follow attention, the facilitator's own focus is not a private act but one of the most powerful interventions available, often more consequential than anything the facilitator says. The conversation notes that this introduces an ethics of attention, since directing a participant's attention shapes what becomes real and possible for them, and so it can be done skillfully or carelessly. The claim gives the facilitator-as-instrument account one of its concrete channels.

- The reframe conversation states that "What the facilitator attends to affects what the participant attends to" and concludes that "Attention is one of the facilitator's most powerful interventions."
