# nothing enters the lattice the body has not countersigned

The book holds every element of its framework to a single admission standard: an idea about inner life counts only if it produces a felt somatic confirmation — Gendlin's felt shift, the physical loosening when a word genuinely matches a bodily knowing. The atlas states the rule in the sentence this note takes as its title, and specifies its scope: the standard is installed at the primitive level "for the whole stack above," so every molecule formula and every practice inherits it.

The first-telling makes the standard operational for the reader: a thirty-second test, run on anything unresolved, with both outcomes counted as honest data. The first-telling draws the consequence for the book's genre in its own words: the framework "is handing you the means of its own falsification, sentence by sentence," which it classes as "a different kind of object from a belief system" (first-telling: the instrument).

**Held within the ecology.** This standard verifies what the book may admit into its own framework; it is not the book's warrant over the reader's outcome, which the book neither produces nor certifies ([[the book must respect that it cannot verify the reader has done the work]]; [[the book tends conditions for the reader's own facilitation of recognition rather than facilitating transformation]]). Under the ecology no locus is sovereign over the reality it describes ([[no single locus or framework may become sovereign over the reality it describes]]), so the felt shift re-nests as one cultivated locus's trained way of noticing — the somatic discernment the Focusing lineage contributes to the reader's own self-facilitation ([[self-facilitation is creating conditions for one's own becoming, not self-management, optimization, or analysis]]) — rather than as the epistemology of everything built above it. The standard keeps its full internal work as a practice's own check and every recorded limit stays attached; what moves is its standing, from the sovereign floor that decides what is true to one instrument the reader-facilitator uses, on the transcend-and-include move at [[the ecology frame transcends and includes the verification frame rather than replacing it]].

## Substantiation

- Atlas §III: "Nothing enters the lattice the body has not countersigned," with the felt shift named as "the verification standard" and the note that Level 1 is "the epistemology of everything built on it."
- First-telling, "The instrument": the full test protocol, the description of the click ("unmistakable once you've felt it, and impossible to fake"), and the sentence "Misses count as data."
- Gendlin's empirical basis is recorded at [[what is split off not felt remains the same]], and the standard's author now has a chair of his own ([[eugene gendlin]]).
- The vault's own inability to run the test is stated in [[methods]].
- **What the lineage sort of 2026-08-05 established about this standard, and it changes how much the note above is carrying.** The rule stated here is the book's whole test for whether anything is true; everything in the framework runs downstream of it. It rests on one name, and that name held no chair in this vault until the sort — which means the standard cannot be defended the way the rest of the book's material is defended, by showing that unconnected traditions arrived at it independently (the verification standard rests on one name and cannot be defended by company).
- The objection this standard has never been put to is not either of the two recorded limits. Both of those concern whether a reading is reliable under conditions; neither disputes that there is a reading to be had. A literature does dispute exactly that — confabulation research on people confidently reporting causes they cannot have known, and the case that introspection is unreliable even about current experience (introspective access is the felt shift's undefended premise, with nisbett and wilson and eric schwitzgebel seated against it). The reading is queued and unbegun, and the first question it has to settle is whether the confabulation result's own carve-out for present-state reports puts the felt shift outside its range.
- **A refusal of the framing itself was seated 2026-08-10, and it is a different objection from every limit above.** The two recorded limits and the confabulation objection all dispute whether the body's reading is reliable; none disputes that the body's role is to read. Merleau-Ponty disputes exactly that. A reading of his lived body (the reading record is `archive/reading — merleau-ponty, phenomenology of perception on the body-subject (1945).md`, and the finding is at merleau-ponty holds the body as the perceiving subject rather than an instrument that certifies) establishes the body as "the perceiving subject itself," "not external to perception but constitutive of it" (Merleau-Ponty, *Phenomenology of Perception*, PP 245/209) — neither an object the reader has nor a tool the reader uses. On that account the body has no standing as an instrument that certifies whether a change occurred, which is the standing this standard assigns it. The refusal is the finding rather than a dispute over accuracy, because it denies the prior framing that makes the body a test at all, and the vault files it as the fill of the embodied blind spot in `ops/clearing.md` rather than as another limit on the standard.
- Gendlin's own work supplies the counterfeit check the standard needs, which is why the reading order starts with him: the felt sense is characterized by rejecting formulations that do not fit, so a bodily yes that arrives smoothly at the first formulation is the signature of fluency rather than of the felt sense ([[the felt sense rejects formulations and that friction is what separates it from fluency]]).
- Two live tensions elsewhere in the vault turn on how much weight this standard can bear, and both are worth reading beside it: a practice that is not for anything still has to be measured in something ([[the practice is the expression of realization rather than a means to it]]), and a reader cannot tell dissolution from regression by consulting a body that reports both as the ground giving way (the book cannot tell dissolution from regression and neither can its reader).
- One boundary keeps this standard from turning into something else. A verification standard says what may be admitted; it does not say that the admitted thing can be produced on demand. Both of the book's foundations refuse exactly that step, and their refusal is what stops a test of the framework from becoming a promise about the reader ([[both poles of the convergence refuse the engineered epiphany]]).
