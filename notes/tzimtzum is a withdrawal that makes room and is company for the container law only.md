# tzimtzum is a withdrawal that makes room and is company for the container law only

Lurianic *tzimtzum* holds that God contracts and withdraws to make room for creation, and that withdrawal-that-makes-room is the same shape as the book's container, which makes an open center, and as the peak the performing law can honor only by withdrawing ([[the peak is the one unit the performing law can honor only by withdrawing]]). It is a genuine structural cousin of the container-that-withdraws law and is company for that law only. Two cautions are kept with it, because both are easy to get wrong. First, the valence is inverted: in *tzimtzum* the contraction is the generative divine gift, while in the book contraction is the problem, the fullness folded into felt lack, so *tzimtzum* is **not** an arrival at [[suffering is contraction never corruption]]. Second, the corpus's only *tzimtzum* is a provenance-flagged machine pairing, so the note carries the flag rather than a finding. A reading of the *Etz Chaim* confirms the withdrawal shape at the primary text; the flag on the corpus's pairing stays.

## Substantiation

- The book's half is on record: the container makes an open center (atlas §II), and the arc's peak is honored by the prose withdrawing at the climax ([[the peak is the one unit the performing law can honor only by withdrawing]]).
- *Tzimtzum*'s half is now shown, from the *Etz Chaim* Gate 1, ch. 2, Ḥayyim Vital's redaction of Isaac Luria, read at Sefaria (the reading record is `archive/reading — luria (vital), etz chaim gate 1 on tzimtzum.md`). The text: "He contracted Himself… in the middle point… And that light was then contracted and withdrew to the sides surrounding the central point, and then a void, air, and empty space were left, from a central point," the Hebrew leaving *makom panui* (a vacated place) and *chalal reikani* (an empty void). The Cordovero caution is settled: Moshe Cordovero's earlier *tzimtzum* is concentration and pedagogical concealment — light condensing to be revealed — while Luria's is withdrawal to leave an empty space, so the book's cousin is the Lurianic sense specifically. Read at the text in translation with the Hebrew phrase confirmed, not the whole *Etz Chaim*.
- The provenance flag is the second caution. The corpus's only *tzimtzum* is the machine-generated pairing *tzimtzum* ≈ Winnicott (the holding environment), ranked by novelty and labeled by the generating system itself as an unworked pairing that "will read as findings if they migrate into the corpus unlabeled" (provenance of the corpus §7). Under the book's origin-story law — a man noticed things, then found others had too — it fails the did-the-author-notice-it-first test, so it enters as a candidate to work rather than as an arrival.
- The refusal is kept. The register is cosmic and theological where the book is psychological, the withdrawal being the Infinite's rather than a person's fold; and the *shevirat ha-kelim*, the shattering of the vessels that cannot hold the light, is corruption-shaped, which the book's "nothing is broken" denies.
