# the treatment records four authorship conditions in its undercurrents

Under the headings "undercurrent" and "the meta," the Arc and the Gloss treatment carries a kind of material none of the other six sources contains: standing observations about the author's relation to the manuscript, two of them marked as addressed to the author rather than to a reader. They are recorded here because they bear on a decision the vault serves — which chapter can be brought to a finish next — and because they are checkable against the executed draft in two of the four cases.

They are quoted rather than restated. Under `ops/methodology/craft evidence.md`, an authorship condition is the author's own recorded self-assessment; the vault reports that the treatment records it, quotes it, and where possible measures the executed draft against it. The vault does not assess the author, infer motive, or recommend.

## Substantiation

The four, with their locations and, where available, the measurement:

- **Chapter 9, on a chapter the treatment says is waiting rather than unwritten.** Marked "craft note, author's eyes only": "still the shortest chapter in the scaffold, and every book tells on its author. Chapters like this aren't written; they're unlocked… When the giving happens where it costs, watch this chapter double without effort" (arc-gloss: ch9). Measured against the executed draft: chapter 9 runs 820 words, the second shortest of the fifteen.
- **Chapter 14, on deliberate under-treatment.** "this chapter has the most room to expand; full technical instruction belongs to the companion volume — the book keeps the vocabulary, the companion keeps the drills" (arc-gloss: ch14). Measured: chapter 14 runs 829 words and introduces twelve kata, roughly sixty-nine words each, and its sentences are the shortest in the book at a mean of 7.8 words. The compression the note predicts is present and is severe.
- **Chapter 15, on a case study the treatment says cannot be researched.** "of the three case studies, Love is the one that must not be written from research — the book's truest love case study is currently filed under 'archive'… The treatment schedules nothing. It notes only that manuscript and author appear to be waiting on the same sentence, and whichever says it first will teach the other the route" (arc-gloss: ch15). Not measurable: the executed draft's Love case study exists at `abridged ch15` and the treatment's standard for it is not one the vault can apply.
- **The axis, on the author's position relative to the guild.** "The axis is the one point on the wheel that never travels — and this book is a wheel built by the axis. Its author holds every credential in the guild except hours logged in the participant's chair; its truest chapters are the ones that will be written from inside a container someone else is holding" (arc-gloss: the meta). The same section states the corresponding fact about the book: "a tuning fork is the only instrument in the orchestra that cannot play itself."

Two further observations of the same kind sit in the meta and are recorded rather than extracted, because each restates a claim the vault already holds: that the manuscript has been running its method on its maker (see [[the book performs what it describes]]), and that the writing is subject to the book's own test, so that a process which starts to feel like a cathedral to maintain has failed it (see [[ripeness is not refrigeration]]).

The two measurable conditions agree with the executed draft, which is the reason this note exists rather than a footnote: the treatment's private assessment of where the writing is thin is confirmed by counting words in a document written under a different scope. Chapters 9 and 14 are the two the treatment flags and two of the three shortest chapters in the abridged edition.
