# the book is one of the automatisms it must interrupt

The book's own account of recognition names, first among its conditions, the interruption of automaticity — something breaks the usual frame so that what was hidden can be seen. A fluent book induces an automatism of exactly the kind that condition targets: the reading-trance in which coherent, resonant prose produces a bodily assent the book cannot, by its own admission, tell apart from recognition — the false peak the author sighted in his own voice. Turning the first condition on the book itself, as the discipline that keeps the framework subject to itself requires, yields a design obligation rather than a worry: the prose must build de-confirmation into its own rhythm — endings that de-conclude, contrary voices kept audible, the counter-examples made visible — so the reader is periodically returned from the spell the writing casts to the claim the writing carries.
