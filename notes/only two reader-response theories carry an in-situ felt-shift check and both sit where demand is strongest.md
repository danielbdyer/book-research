# only two reader-response theories carry an in-situ felt-shift check and both sit where demand is strongest

A survey of the five reader-response strata for a felt-shift standard — a bodily or in-the-moment check that a real change has occurred — returns almost nothing, and what it does return sits exactly where such a check is least trustworthy. Only two of the five carry any in-situ standard at all. Rosenblatt's is the reader's report of "what was lived through," which verifies experience-during rather than change-after and is gathered in the classroom, the maximal-demand setting. Gadamer's is the felt bindingness of a claim to truth, the closest of the five to a felt shift with content, and the exact point the manufactured-authority objection bites. The other three carry no in-situ check: Iser substitutes retrospective criticism by his own statement, Barthes's jouissance "verifies" only by being unsayable and so unfalsifiable, and Fish has only the assent of an interpretive community.

The finding tightens the book's exposure. The book installs a felt shift as its verification floor, and the literature nearest to reader-response either supplies no such check or supplies one sited where demand or authority contaminates it. So the book cannot borrow an in-situ felt-shift standard ready-made from this region; every candidate it holds arrives either empty or compromised at the point of measurement.

## Substantiation

- The dig's felt-shift audit ran the survey and returned the census: two of five carry an in-situ standard and both sit where demand characteristics are strongest, the other three carry none (`transmission dig 2, 2026-08-13`, Felt-shift audit). Iser's substitution of retrospective criticism is grounded in his 1972 essay at page 295, opened directly [P].
- The audit marks the two exceptions as compromised rather than usable: Rosenblatt's report is a classroom self-report under demand, and Gadamer's bindingness is what the compliance attack targets. The three primary readings behind the other strata remain undone and are registered among dig 2's readings to queue, bound for `ops/reading queue.md`.
