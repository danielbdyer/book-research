# planting the felt shift before naming it makes the standard a retrospective pointer rather than a learnable definition

The book's disclosure order induces a distinction before it explains it, and the offering applies that order to the verification standard itself: never define the felt shift before the reader has plausibly had one the book can point back to. Held that way, the standard arrives as a retrospective pointer at a specific page event rather than as a definition a fluent reader can learn early and then perform on cue. The book already says plainly that nothing having happened is a finding rather than a failure, so a reader who reaches the naming without the event is told the truth rather than flattered into a counterfeit.

This is the planting device — plant early, name late — run on the standard instead of on an image, which is the book performing its own test at the level of its verification. The precedent is the one recorded planting-ledger pair, a Sanskrit formula planted half-erased and finished at a mirror; the candidate plant sites are the entry movement's existing closing instructions, and the device works in either grammatical person.
