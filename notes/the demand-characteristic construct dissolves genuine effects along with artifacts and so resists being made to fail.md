# the demand-characteristic construct dissolves genuine effects along with artifacts and so resists being made to fail

The compliance attack has a return edge that mirrors the register attack's. Orne's demand-characteristic construct is a near-universal solvent: because almost any result can be reattributed to the subject's reading of what the setting wants, the construct dissolves genuine effects along with artifacts, and an explanation that can absorb any outcome resists being made to fail. A charge that fits every result equally is, by the attack's own standard, doing what it accuses the felt shift of doing — surviving all disconfirmation. The construct has a second, sharper self-undermining: its own quasi-controls lean on the self-report it distrusts. Postexperimental inquiry, the method meant to detect whether a subject inferred the hypothesis, needs the subject to report that awareness truthfully — and Orne's own "pact of ignorance," in which subject and experimenter tacitly agree not to expose the demand, is exactly what withholds that truthful report. So the instrument built to catch demand depends on the candid self-report that demand suppresses.

The finding does not clear the felt shift; it bounds the attack. A weapon that can attribute anything to demand cannot, on its own, establish that a particular felt shift was demand rather than transmission — which is why the compliance attack is held as a tension routed to an external check rather than as a proof that the shift is nothing.

## Substantiation

- The dig records the failure on target: the construct "is a near-universal solvent: almost any result can be reattributed to demand, dissolving real effects with artifacts, so it resists being made to fail," and "the method leans on the self-report it distrusts — postexperimental inquiry needs truthful reports of awareness the 'pact of ignorance' withholds" (transmission dig 6: FAILURES). This note carries both edges.
- The depth is thin and stated: the quasi-control sections of Orne 1962 were unread at the primary in dig 6, so the note is marked `nascent`. The queued reading — whether demand produces altered experience or only compliant behavior (dig 6, READINGS TO QUEUE item 1) — bears directly, since a construct that reaches only behavior cannot dissolve an experiential effect at all.
- This note is the reason the compliance attack stays a tension rather than a verdict: the universal-solvent property means the attack over-reaches, so it removes the felt shift's self-sufficiency without proving the shift illusory.
- The two return edges together — this one and Adorno's — establish a symmetry the book can use: both attacks, at full strength, prove too much, which is what licenses holding each as a live tension with a treatment rather than as a defeat.
