# technique without ontology becomes manipulation

The reframe conversation orders its facilitation material so that technique enters last, only after the account of the field, the ethics of non-appropriation, and the facilitator's self-awareness are in place. Its stated reason is that technique without ontology becomes manipulation: the same move — a question, a reflection, an intervention — serves recognition or steers a participant depending on the understanding of persons and relationship beneath it. A technique is therefore never self-justifying; it inherits its ethical character from the ontology it emerges from. The claim is the load-bearing reason the book refuses to become a manual of facilitation techniques, and the conversation states the positive form as well: a technique should draw its warrant from the facilitator's ontology, ethics, and awareness rather than from its own effectiveness.

## Substantiation

- The reframe conversation places technique only after its ontological and ethical material: "Only now should techniques enter. Because technique without ontology becomes manipulation" (reframe conversation, 2026-08-13: the facilitator's practice).
