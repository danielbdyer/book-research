# nothing becomes part of the author's philosophy merely because the AI said it

The conversation states a rule it calls the authorial gate: nothing becomes part of the author's philosophy merely because the model said it. For a model-generated statement to become authorial material, it needs some additional act — encounter, discernment, verification, experience, reflection, or interpretation — and that requirement is what preserves provenance and sovereignty. The rule sorts the model's uses. Research, source discovery, comparison, challenge, red-teaming, gap-finding, and surfacing forgotten material increase the author's capacity to encounter and pass the gate; generating final prose wholesale, synthesizing without the author's encounter, and deciding what the author believes bypass it. The model should increase the author's capacity to encounter rather than substitute for the encounter.

## Substantiation

- The rule is stated in the conversation of 2026-08-13, in the section the model titles the authorial gate: "Nothing becomes part of the author's philosophy merely because the AI said it."
- The required act, quoted from the same section: for something to become authorial material "it needs some additional act of: encounter; discernment; verification; experience; reflection; or interpretation. This preserves provenance and sovereignty."
- The sorting of uses, quoted from the same passage: good uses include "research; source discovery; comparison; challenge; red-teaming; identifying gaps; surfacing forgotten material," while more dangerous uses include "generating final prose wholesale; synthesizing without the author's encounter; deciding what the author believes." The governing sentence: "The AI should increase the author's capacity to encounter."
