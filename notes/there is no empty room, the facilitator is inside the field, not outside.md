# there is no empty room, the facilitator is inside the field, not outside

The reframe conversation denies the facilitator any position outside the encounter. Every facilitation encounter already contains a field — participants, facilitator, history, language, culture, environment, power, expectations, technology, institutions — and the facilitator is part of that field rather than a neutral operator acting upon it. The phrase the conversation uses, "there is no empty room," names the absence of neutral ground: the facilitator's presence is already a variable in the situation before any technique is chosen. The claim closes off the stance of the detached expert and sets up the account of the facilitator as an instrument whose own state is part of the working conditions.

## Substantiation

- The reframe conversation opens its facilitation-field material by stating that "Every facilitation encounter contains a field," lists what the field includes (participants, facilitator, history, language, culture, environment, power, expectations, technology, institutions), and concludes that "The facilitator is not outside this field. They are part of it" (reframe conversation, 2026-08-13: there is no empty room).
