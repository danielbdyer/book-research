# the system is scaffolding rather than the centerpiece

The author decided, 2026-08-02, what the book is centrally about: the convergence of thinkers — fingers pointing at the moon — and the lived practice of walking where they point. The system vocabulary the corpus elaborates at length — the Generative Triad, the twelve primitives, the molecule formulas, the three loops — is scaffolding: an uncertain secondary or tertiary concern, not a given outcome of the book. It may house insights, and it earns the housing where it rests on established research such as McLaren's emotion work or Gendlin's felt shift; it is not the centerpiece, and the book is not the delivery vehicle for a new system.

The corpus's deepest layer already states this, even while its page-count contradicts it. The decision therefore resolves a weighting the sources left unresolved, and it reverses one choice the executed draft made: that draft kept the system and dropped the company of thinkers — they survive in it as three passing traces — which under this decision is exactly backwards.

## Substantiation

- The corpus's own statement of the demotion, at the position of highest authority: "There will be a Triad and a set of Primitives and a small chemistry of Molecules. There will be Three Loops and a Stance and twelve Kata. None of these is the point. They are scaffolding. They are the wooden forms a craftsman uses to coax a curve into existence and then removes when the curve holds" (abridged: prologue).
- The two worked exemplars of the convergence centerpiece: the one law reached "with zero disagreement across every lineage the book loves" (arc-gloss: overlay four), and the basic practice posture's "dozen vocabularies orbiting one attractor" (arc-gloss: ch13). Both are many independent arrivals at one finding, which is also the shape of the corpus's strongest evidence class, the isomorph.
- The somatic ground is not demoted: noticing the current state in the body, and the felt shift as verification, are the practice pole's floor.
- The full directive, with the anti-regression clause about the corpus's volume, is `ops/methodology/center of gravity.md`.
- **The demotion was extended twice after this note was written, and both extensions came from the author.** On 2026-08-04 he moved the schemes from architecture to instrument on the Vijñāna Bhairava's model, where a hundred and twelve practices claim neither completeness nor non-overlap — which means gaps and overlaps in the scheme set are not defects and are never work (`ops/methodology/the book's current shape.md`). On 2026-08-05 he named the company's own taxonomy wrong in the same way: "I think the 29 thinkers were the wrong lens all along," because sorting thinkers by contribution answers what each brings and never asks what the book cannot lose ([[the company was sorted by what each thinker brings and never by what the book cannot lose]]). The two corrections are the same shape applied to the book's two halves.
- Two objections to the scaffolding arrived on 2026-08-05 and neither is about its centrality. One is formal: a compositional scheme in which everything relates to everything has been tested against nothing and survives by never having been at risk ([[paul meehl]]). The other is about what happens to a scheme in use: handed to practitioners, a developmental scheme becomes a ranking of persons ([[a developmental scheme handed to practitioners becomes a ranking of persons]]). Scaffolding that is demoted is still printed, so both apply to the book as it stands.
