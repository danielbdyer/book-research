# every third-person science of interaction loses the first person at the altitude the book lives

The sciences that could supply the book its mechanism are third-person sciences of a second-person event, and each one loses the lived first person precisely where the book locates the change. Bateson's hierarchy can describe nothing at Learning III, which he calls "beyond the reach of language" and for which he offers no measure, so the transformative level is exactly the one his science cannot instrument. Maturana and Varela do not lose the first person so much as dissolve its authority, treating a felt report as one observer's distinction that certifies nothing across observers ([[on the autopoietic account the felt shift certifies nothing beyond the one who feels it]]). De Jaegher and Di Paolo name experience but measure only coordination dynamics — relative phase, recurrence, perturbation-response — so the first person is gestured at and not operationalized. The pattern holds across the region: the mechanism is supplied and the lived interior is not, at the altitude that matters most.

This is also an absence the commission recorded as a hole. No documented case exists of a third-person science of interaction carried into a text read alone rather than into a clinic, a lab, or a dyad, so the sciences supply mechanism without a precedent for the book's own use of them.

## Substantiation

- The three limbs sit at mixed depth: the Bateson and De Jaegher limbs rest on primary texts transmission dig 4 opened, and the Maturana limb on scholarship, with `The Tree of Knowledge` queued in `ops/reading queue.md`. The claim enters nascent because it is a synthesis across readings the vault has not independently made.
- What each science admits differs, and the difference is worth keeping: Bateson admits the loss, since his own method cannot speak where the book locates the change; Maturana and Varela do not admit that demoting the felt report guts a verifying experience, and the founder's later turn is the indirect admission ([[the enactive program's founder conceded its biology had lost the first person]]); De Jaegher and Di Paolo leave the loss honest and unfinished, measuring coordination and not the experience they name.
- Held as a description of where the reading has gone rather than a verdict on the sciences: the first person may yet be operationalized by later work, and the claim records the state of the region as the dig found it.
