# a schedule can be aligned to the nervous system rather than to the clock

The gesture proposes arranging a working day to the body's cycles rather than to institutional time. The survey already holds the grounding research as a queued candidate and states the conflict in one line — modern scheduling "wars against ultradian rhythm" (space-around: around the engine) — so what is missing is only the step from the loop diagnosis to a designed week.
