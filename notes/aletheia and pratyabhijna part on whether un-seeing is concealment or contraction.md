# aletheia and pratyabhijna part on whether un-seeing is concealment or contraction

The founding convergence holds at its destination and parts at its mechanism. Both poles agree that the deepest change is the recovery of what was always there rather than an acquisition ([[aletheia and pratyabhijna converge on truth as un-forgetting]]). They differ on why the always-there goes unseen, and the difference is not a footnote — it is the seam that decides the book's posture.

For Heidegger, the mechanism is **concealment**. Aletheia is built from *lēthē*, and concealment is not a mere lack to be cleared away but belongs to truth itself: the clearing that lets beings appear also withdraws, so un-concealment is an un-veiling of something that hides. The veil is half of the truth.

For the Pratyabhijñā school, the mechanism is **contraction**. The full awareness self-limits into a bounded standpoint — *saṅkoca*, the contraction the three *malas* name — and the Self is never veiled or absent, only clenched into not recognizing its own fullness. Recognition (*prati-abhi-jñā*, knowledge turning back) is therefore an un-clenching rather than an un-veiling: nothing was hidden; a vision that was never obscured was contracted into not being directly perceivable.

The book's own recognition stands on the contraction side, and choosing it is what makes the book cohere. Its doctrine of suffering is already contraction, never corruption ([[suffering is contraction never corruption]]) — suffering as fullness folded, a contraction that has forgotten it is a locus ([[suffering is contraction that has forgotten it is a locus and a tradition is contraction that remembers]]). If recognition's mechanism is the same contraction, then recognition and the theory of suffering are one motion, the un-clenching of a fold, and the whole book runs on contraction rather than on concealment. The posture that follows is affirmative: the work is not to lift a veil off something hidden but to relax a clench in something that was here the whole time, only made not-directly-perceivable — which is the book reading deep change as uncovering rather than addition ([[the book reads deep change as uncovering rather than addition]]), stated now at the level of mechanism.

## Substantiation

- The aletheia side is grounded at the founding isomorph: aletheia is *a-* + *lēthē* (the river of forgetting), and Heidegger's 1964 retraction keeps concealment constitutive — "aletheia, unconcealment thought as opening, first grants the possibility of truth" — so the un-veiling structure is intrinsic rather than incidental (quoted at [[aletheia and pratyabhijna converge on truth as un-forgetting]]).
- The contraction side is grounded in the vault's *saṅkoca* material: the three *malas* are contractions of the full awareness ([[the triads privations map the three malas corner for corner]]), and the productive reading holds *saṅkoca* as a bounded vantage rather than only a bondage ([[contraction is a productive locus and not only a privation]]). The Śaiva primary on the *malas* (Kṣemarāja, *Pratyabhijñāhṛdayam* sūtras 9–10) is text-gated and owed; until then the pole rests on the vault's secondary and in-vault grounding.
- The difference is kept both ways rather than sharpened into an opposition. Kashmir Śaivism does have a concealment act — *tirodhāna*, one of Śiva's five — but it is a self-concealment held as play, a self-contraction rather than a veil imposed from without, so even its "hiding" sits on the contraction side. And Heidegger's concealment is not a corruption. The two mechanisms of un-seeing name different structures, and the book takes the contraction one; this is company held as company, and the divergence is the gold rather than a defect.
- Wittgenstein's independent arrival sits nearer the contraction reading than the concealment one: "hidden by its simplicity and familiarity" is a not-regarding of what is in plain view, an unregarded rather than a veiled ([[the always-there hides by being always there and wittgenstein said so without heidegger]]).
- Provenance: the distinction was surfaced on 2026-08-24 while sounding where the book's own recognition-posture actually rests; its warrant is the two traditions, not the occasion.
