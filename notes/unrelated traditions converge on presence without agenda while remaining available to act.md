# unrelated traditions converge on presence without agenda while remaining available to act

The treatment proposes that the Stance unit show a convergence table rather than argue a definition, and states what the table would demonstrate: "a dozen vocabularies orbiting one attractor: being present without agenda while remaining available to act" (arc-gloss: ch13). The attractor is stated once and the vocabularies are left in their own terms, which makes this the practice pole's counterpart to the guild's one law — the same evidence shape applied to a posture rather than to a mechanism.

The vocabularies named are wu wei, nonattachment, Gelassenheit, Lichtung, receptive awareness, Gendlin's allowing, Scharmer's presencing, Hudson's VIEW, Circling's being-with, and what the corpus calls the framework's own sovereignty. Two more arrive in the sentences immediately after the list. The first is the Kashmir Śaiva posture, which the treatment marks as its loveliest cameo: "the valley kept a posture for exactly this and named it — gaze outward, attention resting inward; eyes open to the room, awareness at home in the source. The Stance has a thousand-year-old mudrā." The second is the working posture of the author's own trade, which the treatment notes is the same thing again: "tracking the room while resting in presence" (arc-gloss: ch13).

The convergence carries a difficulty the corpus names rather than hides — "the paradox of cultivating what can't be cultivated" (arc-gloss: ch13) — and the book's answer to it is the definition recorded at [[the basic practice posture is a point of return rather than a state]].

## Substantiation

- Arc-gloss ch13 is the only document in the corpus that states the convergence. Its list names ten vocabularies and the count given is a dozen; the Kashmir Śaiva mudrā and the facilitator's working posture, supplied in the two sentences following, complete it.
- The table is designed and not yet written. The executed prose of the same unit gives the posture as six facets — available; discerning; held but not holding; becoming and being; felt and revealing; present without agenda — and names exactly one external vocabulary, Scharmer's presencing at the bottom of the U-curve (abridged ch13). The nine other traditions in the treatment's list reach the executed draft nowhere.
- The counterweight, which the survey supplies and the corpus does not: the convergence sits inside a live philosophical argument rather than beside one. "'Automatic, yet fully attended' is the classical Chinese problem of wu wei — effortless action — and the paradox the tradition argued about for centuries: how do you try to be effortless?" The survey treats that phrase as the corpus's; it is the author's own, written in January 2026 about facilitation — "birthing meaning from holding and releasing specific tensions like a design constraint. Automatic yet fully attended to" (provenance addendum three §3, quoting [D] 14 Jan 2026). The survey was matching an ancient debate to a sentence of his without knowing whose it was. The survey names the scholarship (Slingerland) and the two classical answers, Confucian training and Daoist unlearning, "and the honest admission that every approach smuggles the paradox back in." It judges the book's own move — presence as a point of return rather than a state to maintain — "a genuinely respectable move within that ancient argument," and warns that sophisticated readers will arrive already holding it (space-around: around the practice).
- Atlas §VII.1 places the posture at the head of the practice curriculum and records its structural echo at relational scale, Benjamin's rupture-and-repair, which is a convergence of a different kind: the same law at a different scale rather than the same posture in another vocabulary.
- **An adversary was seated against the posture on 2026-08-05, and the charge is descriptive rather than philosophical.** Presence without agenda is describable from outside as detachment, and the contemplative adverse-effects literature documents deadening and flattened affect as outcomes of practice rather than as failures of technique. The grid's judgment is that this absence is currently the book's most defensible criticism and also the cheapest to answer, which makes it a different sort of hole from the others: what it needs is a reading rather than a defence.
- One finding from the same literature runs toward the book instead of against it, and it belongs here because it is about what a convergence table is for: an accurate framework for what is happening is itself among the protective factors ([[a framework for what is happening is itself a protective factor]]). A table that leaves twelve vocabularies in their own terms is a description of the territory rather than an instruction in it, which is the form that finding credits.
- Hudson's VIEW is read at his own material (2026-09-02): "Vulnerability is to speak your truth even when it's scary"; "Impartiality is not trying to achieve an outcome for yourself or others"; "Empathy is to be with a person in their emotions"; "Wonder… you're definitely not looking for the answer" (Art of Accomplishment, "Introduction to VIEW"). Its native domain is conversation — "Practically, it's a way to have conversations. It's a communication methodology" — and he refuses its use as method: "It is not the technique… if you see this and view this as a technique, it won't work." The table quotes a conversational state of mind, which fits the attractor it orbits ([[joe hudson]]).
- The relational vocabulary in the list has an owner the corpus does not name, and naming him changes what "being-with" is claiming: Circling's being-with and the I–Thou relation are the same posture, and Buber's version is ontological rather than technical ([[the relational vocabulary's ontology is buber's rather than benjamin's]]).
