# the sacred in the ordinary is produced by sensory design

The gesture claims the sacredness of ordinary life is produced by sensory arrangement — light, texture, the set of a room — where the corpus's commitment to the ordinary is stated entirely as attention to what is already there. Alexander is the roster's one spatial builder, and the corpus takes his quality without a name while leaving the arranging that produces it untouched.
