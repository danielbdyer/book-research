# distress is a question about machinery not a verdict about character

The book's title names its engine model: a self runs on three loops — a bodily loop (rhythm, appetite, timing), an identity loop (who one takes oneself to be and how that self expresses), and a continuity loop (the thread of meaning that makes yesterday's and tomorrow's self the same project). The corpus notes the model's older form: the Trika tradition's three faces of divine power (will, knowledge, action), inseparable when a person is running well.

The diagnostic move is the question "which cylinder?" — distress stops functioning as a verdict about character and becomes a question about which loop is misfiring. The answer routes: the Three Loops material sorts the book's eleven contemporary teachers by which loop their theory of suffering actually treats, converting the company into a referral network. When the teachers' doctrines conflict, the book seats the body as referee, case by case; no doctrine adjudicates.

## Substantiation

- First-telling, "The engine": the three loops and the map of five theories of where suffering lives, presented with their disagreements intact.
- Atlas §V: the loop-to-Śakti correspondence, the referral-network docking at the Three Loops material, and the body-as-tiebreaker rule (cross-linked to the tensions kept live in §XI.2).
- Walked V records the two additions the book makes to the five theories: suffering carried with meaning (Frankl) and suffering located in structures, where "the cure for a structure is structural."
- The space-around survey identifies a missing voice in the book's map of where suffering lives: biological causes — thyroid, anemia, sleep apnea, medication effects — where no amount of inner work substitutes for a blood panel. The survey marks this as a safety matter, not an enrichment. Drafting that addition has not been done; it is on the pending-evaluation list in `ops/queue/candidates.md`.
- **Three objections to the diagnostic move were seated on 2026-08-05 and none of them is about the loops.** The first is formal, and it is Paul Meehl's: in soft psychology everything correlates with everything, so a scheme that predicts only that some difference exists has been tested against nothing and survives by never having been at risk. Applied here, the question is what observation the loop diagnosis rules out. The second is the same charge in the direction the book's own ethic cares about: naming a person's distress as an internal composition can relocate a condition that is arranged outside them — which is the harder version of the structural voice the walked document already records as one of the book's two additions. The third is about what happens to a scheme after it is handed over, since the referral network is a taxonomy of persons in use.
- The referral network itself was re-read the same day and the reading changes what the routing is doing. The eleven teachers are not eleven independent theories to be matched to a misfiring loop; they are a bloc whose members compete with the compositional scheme for the same job ([[the coaches are a bloc that competes with the compositional scheme]]).
