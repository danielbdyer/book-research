# the minus sign cannot say whether the missing element was withheld or never on offer

The interiority audit asked of each compound diagnosis: does this relocate a structural condition inside a person? Run against the catalogue the corpus actually states, the answer is not uniform, and the pattern is sharper than the question expected.

**The formulas whose missing element is external or relational do not relocate anything.** Burnout is obligation without sanctuary, and the corpus's own prescription is a claim about the world rather than about the sufferer: "Find or build a place where you can let down" (abridged ch4). Grief is more explicit still — its catalyst is witness, so the formula requires another person by construction ([[grief cannot complete alone]]). Trust is relational in all three of its components.

**The formulas whose missing element is an act the person performs do relocate.** Paralysis is possibility without commit, and the prescription is "to make the commitment." Stagnation is lifecycle without transform: "the medicine is, often, to let something die." Foreclosure is commitment without defer. In each, the notation asks a person to supply something, and it has no place to record that the thing may not be available to them. A person who cannot commit because their housing is precarious, and a person who will not commit because they are avoiding, produce the same formula.

That is the defect, and it is smaller and more fixable than the objection the grid raised. **The minus sign says restore this. It cannot say this was never on offer.** Withheld and unavailable are different conditions with identical notation.

## Substantiation

- The catalogue audited is the one the sources state: burnout, possibility, paralysis, foreclosure and stagnation at abridged ch4; burnout, grief, trust and paralysis as "the canonical formulas" at atlas §III.2, with the Isomer Teaching beside them. The audit is an extraction pass over material the vault already held and required no outside reading, which is why it is `full` rather than `privated`.
- The corpus's own defense is real and half-earned, and it should be recorded before the charge. It states plainly that "Burnout is not a disease. It is a structural condition," and its stated purpose for the notation is that "the moral question disappears" — the formulas were built to stop moralization, which is the same enemy the interiority charge names. Where the missing element is sanctuary or witness, they succeed.
- The word doing the concealing is *structural*, which the corpus uses in the molecular sense — a configuration of elements — while the objection uses it in the sociological sense — an institution, a market, a schedule. A formula can be structural in the first sense and locate the whole cause in a person, and the shared word makes that hard to see.
- **What this does not find.** The book does not inherit the blind spot it names in the transformation field ([[the transformation field divides into five theories of where suffering lives]]). Two of the seven formulas name an external missing element outright, and the corpus seats the material voice against the field's interiority. The charge lands on the notation's expressive range rather than on the book's position.
- **The candidate treatment, and why it is not a scheme extension.** The audit's finding is answerable with one question rather than a taxonomy: for any privated formula, *is the missing element withheld or unavailable?* That is a single diagnostic move, not a new set of elements, so it survives the demotion of the schemes to instruments (`ops/methodology/the book's current shape.md`). The corpus already owns a device of exactly this shape — the Isomer Teaching, where trust and paralysis share their atoms and differ only in where defer attaches (atlas §III.2). A distinction that turns on the placement of an element rather than on adding one is the move the book already makes.
- One consequence for the prose if the question is adopted. The medicine for an unavailable element is not the same sentence as the medicine for a withheld one, and the abridged edition currently writes only the second: "The medicine is to make the commitment." A reader for whom commit is unavailable reads that as a demand and receives the moralization the notation was built to remove.
