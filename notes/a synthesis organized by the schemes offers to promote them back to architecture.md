# a synthesis organized by the schemes offers to promote them back to architecture

On 2026-08-04 the author demoted the book's conceptual schemes from architecture to instrument: "I'm not throwing them out. I'm demoting them from architecture to instrument. Useful for seeing with; not the thing the book is wrapped around." The model he named was a text offering a hundred and twelve practices that claims neither completeness nor non-overlap, so that gaps and overlaps in the scheme set are not defects and are never work.

The research synthesis received 2026-08-05 is organized by one of those schemes from its first paragraph to its last. It opens by stating the three loops as the structure of selfhood, gives each a section with neural correlates — the insular cortex for the temporal and somatic loop, medial prefrontal cortex and temporal-parietal junction for identity and expression, the Default Mode Network for continuity and meaning — and then reads every substance, every tradition and every clinical finding through what it does to the three. Its closing sentence returns the whole territory to the book's title.

That is not a criticism of the document, which is careful and useful. It is a description of a specific risk. A demotion is a decision about status, and status is exactly what an empirical warrant restores. Nobody would have to argue for reinstating the schemes; a session would simply find them confirmed by neuroscience and start building on them, and the decision of 2026-08-04 would be reversed by accretion rather than by anyone deciding.

## Substantiation

- The demotion is recorded as a settled row in `ops/decisions.md` and stated at `ops/methodology/the book's current shape.md`, tier one — the tier a session may build on.
- The vault already holds the general form of this risk and named it before this document arrived. `CLAUDE.md` records the named regression as "organizing new material by the system's taxonomy" and warns that the corpus's page-count overweights the system, so semantic retrieval and every coverage table built from it inherit the bias. An outside synthesis written in the system's terms is the same regression arriving from a new direction, and a stronger one, because the corpus's overweighting is at least visibly the corpus's own.
- The observation the vault already recorded about the corpus applies here verbatim and is worth reading beside this note: `ops/observations/the corpus pre-sorts the convergence material by the system it demotes.md`.
- The treatment is stated in the field above and is applied to every claim drawn from this document in the same pass: the finding is stated in plain terms, and where the synthesis attributes something to a loop, the claim says what was actually measured. The synthesis reports DMN suppression under psychedelics; the vault records DMN suppression, not "the continuity loop releases." The difference is small in each instance and is the whole of the treatment.
- One thing the treatment deliberately does not do, because it would overcorrect. The synthesis's findings are not discredited by its organizing principle, and refusing the document would lose real material that fills two of the sort's ranked holes. The corpus's own drafts are read the same way — quoted for what they say, never credited for what they argue ([[the atlas was compiled from the arc and gloss treatment]] and `ops/methodology/source standing.md`, rule 1) — and this is that rule extended to a consulted work.
- A residual question the treatment does not settle, left open rather than resolved. If the three loops turn out to correspond to well-attested neural systems, that is a fact about the scheme regardless of what status the author assigns it, and a demotion cannot make a true correspondence false. What the demotion governs is whether the book is *wrapped around* the scheme, which is a question about the book's architecture rather than about the scheme's accuracy. Keeping those two apart is the work; collapsing them in either direction is the failure.
