# the diagnostic discipline survives the apparatus that carries it

If the compositional notation is set aside as a mnemonic rather than a calculus, the question is what remains, and the answer is two separable things that the apparatus displays but does not underwrite. Neither depends on the set of operations being twelve, or closed, or found rather than chosen.

The first is a discipline of specificity, and the executed draft states it in its own defense of the vocabulary: the ordinary instruction "set boundaries" can mean Sever this connection, Dissolve this internal pattern, Release this old commitment, or Maintain this fragile new one, and these are "four entirely different operations, with four entirely different felt qualities and four entirely different consequences" (abridged ch5). What does the work there is the requirement to say which one, and any vocabulary of comparable grain would support it. The gain is real, checkable in use, and belongs to the practice of naming rather than to a physics of experience.

The second is the privation reading — distress analyzed as a state missing an ingredient rather than containing a wrong one, which leaves self-judgment with no analytical role ([[the diagnosis contains the cure]]). This one has genuine standing outside the conversations that produced the book's version of it, and the corpus's own survey supplies the ancestry: Aristotle's *steresis*, privation as one of the three principles of all change, with the scholastics' *privatio boni* as its sequel — a lineage the survey dates at "two-thousand-four-hundred" years (space-around: around the chemistry). The book's second ground for it is older still in the material the book cares about: the three *malas* of Trika read the primal contractions as diminishments rather than corruptions, and the corpus maps them corner for corner onto the triad's privated structures ([[the triads privations map the three malas corner for corner]]).

## Substantiation

- The two survivors have different kinds of standing, and the difference matters for how each may be used. The specificity discipline is a craft finding — it is visible in the prose, it can be tested by a reader on a Tuesday, and it needs no outside authority. The privation reading is a doctrinal position with independent arrivals behind it, which is the evidence class the book's centerpiece is made of (`ops/methodology/center of gravity.md`).
- Neither survivor needs the closure claim. Wierzbicka's semantic primes are the standard the corpus itself names for a *found* alphabet ([[the corpus names the standard its found-not-invented axiom has not met]]); nothing in either survivor turns on meeting it, because neither asserts that the vocabulary is complete.
- Neither survivor needs the compositional notation either. What fails when the formulas are read as arithmetic is set out at [[the compositional notation does not license the operations its syntax implies]]; every discrimination that failure exposes is one the prose was already making, and the specificity discipline is that prose.
- The third structure, the container anatomy, sits differently and is not treated here. It has its own evidence — the convergence of builders in unrelated media, and the datable fact that the corners were axiomatized before the author's contact with the tradition they rhyme with ([[the triad predates the author's contact with the tradition named the triad]]) — and it is not a compositional claim at all.
- The salvage is stated for the author's judgment rather than as a recommendation about the manuscript. What to keep, drop, or rebuild is his; what the vault can say is which parts would still be standing if the apparatus were removed tomorrow.
