# the orchard's koan is that composting is not death

The orchard carries the metabolic arc: things ripen, fall, feed, and grow again, and nothing in the cycle asks permission to be what it is. Its koan is that composting is not death — return to potential rather than destruction — which the vault has adopted as the last of its own five state names.

## Substantiation

- Arc-gloss, the image-system: "The Orchard ↔ the metabolic arc ↔ krīḍā; ripening-and-composting as the seasonal face of the pulse. Composting ≠ death is the orchard's koan." Atlas §X.5b carries the same equation.
- The executed prose: "The orchard is what the orchard does. Nothing in it asks permission to be exactly what it is" (abridged ch1), and the full arc at abridged ch10: "Composting is not death. It is return to potential."
