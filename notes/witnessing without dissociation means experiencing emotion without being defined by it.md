# witnessing without dissociation means experiencing emotion without being defined by it

The reframe conversation specifies the witnessing posture by marking off what it is not. To witness an emotion is to experience it — to feel it fully — while not being defined by it, so that the person has the emotion rather than becoming it. The qualifier without dissociation distinguishes witnessing from detachment, in which a person avoids the emotion by standing outside it and feels less rather than more. Witnessing keeps full contact with the feeling and adds only that the feeling is not the whole of the person, whereas dissociation breaks contact. The distinction bears directly on the book's sharpest unmet objection, that its basic posture read from outside is describable as dissociation, recorded at the basic posture is describable as dissociation and the harm literature is unread; this claim states the difference the objection presses on — presence with the emotion rather than escape from it — though it does not by itself discharge the objection, which turns on clinical evidence the vault has not yet read.
