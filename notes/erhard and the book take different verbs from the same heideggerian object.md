# erhard and the book take different verbs from the same heideggerian object

The corpus keeps one of its four fault lines between Erhard's speech-that-makes and Heidegger's speech-that-lets, and treats them as two positions about what language does. Erhard's own academic statement of his model shows the disagreement sitting one level lower than that. What he takes from Heidegger is not a theory of speech at all. It is the clearing: "It is as though the context for leader and leadership that a person brings to a leadership situation creates a kind of 'clearing' in which that situation shows up for them, and that clearing shapes and colors the way in which that situation occurs for that person" (Erhard and Jensen 2010/2020, §I). His footnotes cite *Being and Time* by page and rule out the metaphysical readings, and he quotes Heidegger's "Ontology is possible only as phenomenology."

So Erhard and the book are working on the same object. Both hold that a person meets their circumstances inside something prior that determines how those circumstances can show up at all, and both take that structure from Heidegger. What differs is the verb attached to it. Erhard's course asks students to "examine and eliminate the grip of their everyday common-sense worldview" and then to "create for themselves a context," which is making. The book's foundation is un-forgetting, in which what shows up was already there and the work is to stop concealing it, which is letting.

One further sentence complicates the split in the book's favour and was not available before this reading. Erhard's own name for mastery is middle-voiced. He calls the goal "a context that uses you," and describes it against ordinary learning: "instead of you using what you have learned, it has become for you 'second nature', it so to speak uses you. When a context uses you, there is nothing to remember and no rules to apply." Neither making nor being made, but a third grammar — which is exactly the resolution the corpus already proposes for this fault line.

## Substantiation

- All quotations are from the paper read complete on 2026-08-07; the record is `archive/reading — erhard and jensen, creating leaders (2010, rev. 2020).md`. The corpus side is atlas §IX.3, which states the danger of "declaration-as-vestment — the declared self worn as costume; countered by the disclosive pole and resolved only in the middle voice."
- The corpus's fault line is not wrong, and the correction is to its depth rather than its direction. Erhard does perform a making operation, and the book does not. What the corpus states as two positions about language is better stated as two operations on one inherited structure.
- The paper is a leadership course rather than a transcript of the Landmark Forum, so nothing here establishes what the Forum teaches. The claim is about Erhard's stated model.
- Whether Erhard's own system depends on the middle-voiced sentence or holds it as an aside is not settled by one paper, and the scholarly study of the Erhard–Heidegger relation, Hyde and Kopp's *Speaking Being* (Wiley, 2019), is unread.
