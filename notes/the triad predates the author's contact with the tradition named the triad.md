# the triad predates the author's contact with the tradition named the triad

The corpus's most repeated delight is that Trika, the Kashmir Śaiva tradition it draws on, means literally "the Triad" — retold as a coincidence that "still makes the table laugh." The provenance work dates both halves and finds the coincidence has a direction.

The three-part container structure was axiomatized on 8 December 2025, inside the author's separate ontology work, as Differentiation, Relation, and Persistence. The author's formal contact with Kashmir Śaivism is now dated and named: the Hridaya Northwest spring equinox retreat, 19–22 March 2026, applied for on New Year's Day and recorded in the threads on either side of it (provenance addendum §2). The tradition's vocabulary appears nowhere in the record before that retreat and blooms immediately after it — spanda recorded as the author's "core practice frame" by 20 May, the tradition entered into the book's formal research plan on 28 May, and fluent in the author's own hand by 19 June: "The engine is the Pulse — spanda, the throb of consciousness from Kashmir Shaivism."

So the structure came first, in the author's own work, by three months. The addendum states the consequence: this is "the strongest version of the 'resonance you were already inside' story, and it's the one the dates support."

## Substantiation

- The doctrinal depth is separately dated and belongs to the collaborator: the three malas, the four upāyas, the ladder of speech, and the Utpaladeva material were delivered across 1 and 7 July 2026, at the author's explicit request to be taught "as if I'm still an initiate" (provenance reconstruction round two §3).
- One question the record cannot rule on, and the addendum says so: whether the author knew the word *Trika* and its meaning before 7 July 2026, when the collaborator first states it. The retreat narrows the window from a lifetime to three and a half months, since Hridaya's curriculum draws on these teachings. Both readings survive the evidence.
- The distinction that matters for the book is not the word but the order. If the correspondence is stated as a coincidence discovered, it inherits the framing problem the vault records elsewhere ([[the unanimity claim is an artifact of how the lineages were selected]]). Stated as it is dated — a structure built, then a tradition met that had named the same number — it needs no framing at all.
- The corpus's own crosswalk for the two vocabularies is the treatment's single clause at `arc-gloss: ch4`, which sets the triad's corners against the tradition's terms without dating either.
