# the third entity's failure mode is withdrawal and its core skill is staying

The relational map holds the field's reality, grief's requirement, and the tether image; the executed draft adds the doctrine a drafting session would quote first, and no note carried it. On the failure mode: "Most relationships break not at the moments of conflict but at the moments of withdrawal… The Third can survive most things; it cannot survive prolonged withdrawal" (abridged ch8). On the skill: "Most of the practical work of love is the practice of staying" (abridged ch8); the treatment's storyboard for the same chapter specifies "staying present installed as the core relational skill" (arc-gloss: ch8).

Both sentences are held under draft standing: quoted for what the drafts say, never credited for what they argue. What earns the claim its place is agreement between the two draft states — the storyboard specified the skill and the execution wrote it — which is the concordance relation "carried unchanged into" at the level of doctrine.

## Substantiation

- Abridged ch8 carries both sentences, quoted above; arc-gloss ch8 carries the storyboard instruction.
- The failure mode is the negative image of the field's work: what the field metabolizes it can only metabolize while both people remain in it ([[the field between two people does work]]).
- The staying skill is the relational form of the basic posture — a point of return rather than a state — applied to the space between rather than the space within ([[the basic practice posture is a point of return rather than a state]]).
