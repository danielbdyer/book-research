# the topology only exists where it is being performed

The corpus states a limit on its own cartography in walked XI's words: the topology "only exists where it is being performed," present everywhere inside the performance, while "outside it, there are only parts." The atlas applies the limit to itself in its own words — "a map of gravitational wells is not a well" — and holds that reading the map is subject to the same verification standard as everything else. The corpus cites Varela's enaction and Machado's line about the road made by walking as the position's established forms, and offers two facts from the book's own history as instances: the central term (aletheia) was adopted before its philosophical grounding arrived, and the recognition structure was found operating in the House with No Ceiling on rereading rather than planted there.

The question the corpus holds open, and this vault inherits with force: can a map remain play — an aid to performance — rather than becoming a substitute for it? The space-around survey states the sharpest version and recommends the book say it about itself: any framework, its own included, "can become the newest and most sophisticated hallway" — the survey's figure for a place a person stays, describing the door instead of walking through it.

## Substantiation

- Atlas §XI.3 carries the limit, its self-application, and the two biographical instances.
- The space-around survey carries the objection as a counterweight in its section on insight that does not move anything, and recommends the book state it in its own text.
- The vault's operational response is the scope rule in [[methods]] and the question the /rethink review is required to ask on every pass: what vault material has reached the manuscript.
