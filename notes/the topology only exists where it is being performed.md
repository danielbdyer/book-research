# the topology only exists where it is being performed

The corpus states a limit on its own cartography: the framework's territory exists only while it is being walked — in the body, the text, the conversation. Inside the performance it is everywhere; outside it there are only parts. The atlas applies the limit to itself in its own words — "a map of gravitational wells is not a well" — and holds that reading the map is subject to the same verification standard as everything else. The corpus cites Varela's enaction and Machado's line about the road made by walking as the position's established forms, and offers two facts from the book's own history as instances: the central term (aletheia) was adopted before its philosophical grounding arrived, and the recognition structure was found operating in chapter 1 on rereading rather than planted there.

The question the corpus holds open, and this vault inherits with force: can a map remain play — an aid to performance — rather than becoming a substitute for it? The space-around survey states the sharpest version: any framework, including this one, can become the newest and most sophisticated place to hide from the work.
