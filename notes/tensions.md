# tensions

This map lists the tensions the corpus holds open on purpose. The atlas states the policy twice: its notation legend defines the tension glyph as marking a strain "kept as a feature, never resolved by fiat" (atlas §0), and §XI adds that each tension "has a treatment under trial, and none is resolved by fiat." Each tension claim carries a status (live, treated, or resolved) and names the treatment currently on trial. The vault tracks the trial; resolving a tension is the author's act, made in the writing.

Scope note: this map holds tensions in the book's content. Tensions in the vault's own operation — places where practice contradicts recorded methodology — are a different thing and live in `ops/tensions/`, which the /rethink command reads when it compares recorded methodology against observed practice and proposes changes.

The ecology and meta-facilitation frame is the ground the tensions on this map now stand within: the book is one locus in an ecology of participation, tending the conditions for the reader's own facilitation rather than delivering or verifying an outcome, and the tensions gathered below are boundary conditions on features within that ground rather than threats to a warrant the book holds ([[the ecology frame transcends and includes the verification frame rather than replacing it]]). The framing changes each tension's standing and none of its content; the framing itself moved no entry's status, and resolving remains the author's act, made in the writing.

## The ground these tensions are held beneath

The reframe of 2026-08-13 re-grounds the book in the ecology of loci and the book's meta-facilitation position within it, and it re-nests the prior frame's four apophatic tensions — the felt-shift verification standard, the one-chair structural wound, the second-chair remedy, and the register-and-beauty hazard — as guardrails held beneath that affirmative ground rather than as the bedrock the book stood on. The tensions stay live and unresolved; what changes is their standing, from ground to guardrail. This is a change of standing and not a resolution, and resolving remains the author's act made in the writing.

## The disagreements kept live among the contemporary teachers

The corpus preserves, rather than adjudicates, the disagreements among its contemporary teachers. Three standing guards watch for counterfeit transcendence: Nāgārjuna's warning that emptiness wrongly grasped harms the grasper, Lacan's warning about what recognition gets used for, and Rieff's warning that books like this become commodities of the culture they critique.

## The tensions the lineage sort exposed

Five arrived together with the mesh grid on 2026-08-05. Four are the same shape — a claim carrying weight with nothing argued against it — and they are holes in the reading rather than faults in the book; each names what would fill it, and the readings that would fill them are ranked, with page-level addresses, at `ops/reading queue.md`.

- [[introspective access is the felt shift's undefended premise]] — the verification standard presupposes reliable access to one's own states, and the literature against that premise is unread. First-ranked, and the same standard the author judges thinly defended. Treatment: a candidate since the Gendlin 1964 reading — restate what the shift certifies as movement rather than truth, which narrows what introspection must deliver from describing inner states to noticing change against no-change; it is the standard's own author answering, and it is untested against the counterweights, which remain unread.
- [[the participation law has no opponent and its oldest objection is that participation is contamination]] — nothing in the roster attacks the central claim, and the objection is institutionalized in the whole apparatus of blinding and randomization. Treatment: unassigned; Gellner, Popper, and the replication-reform literature are unread here.
- [[the basic posture is describable as dissociation and the harm literature is unread]] — the same description read clinically is a symptom, and the contemplative adverse-effects data is absent. Called the book's most defensible criticism and the cheapest to answer. Treatment: unassigned; Britton's data is chaired and unread.
- [[the book cannot tell dissolution from regression and neither can its reader]] — the book induces destabilization and names no diagnostic for what it induced; the standard that decides everything else is the one worst placed to decide this. Treatment: unassigned; the guardrail is named but not built.
- [[a developmental scheme handed to practitioners becomes a ranking of persons]] — every documented precedent turned into a status hierarchy among its readers, and none produced a design feature that prevents it. Treatment: unassigned, and the demotion of the schemes is only a partial answer.

Two more arrived with them and sit differently. [[the compound diagnoses may relocate a structural condition inside a person]] asked whether the book inherits the blind spot it names in the field it diagnoses; the audit was run on 2026-08-05 and the answer is no — the formulas split by whether their missing element is external or an act the person performs, and the defect is narrower than the charge ([[every discrimination the molecule formulas are credited with is made by the prose beside them]]). It now carries a treatment: ask of each privated formula whether the missing element is withheld or unavailable. And [[sorting a lineage by load turns it toward argument]] is the only one of the seven that is already treated: the instrument that found the other six operates in a register the authorial stance declines, and the author resolved the collision by scope rather than by choosing a side.

## The tension the second primary reading opened

This tension was added 2026-08-07, from reading Nisbett and Wilson 1977 complete, and it is a disagreement between two defenses the vault already held rather than a disagreement between thinkers.

- [[the counterfeit defense and the change-blindness finding ask for opposite timings]] — the vault answers the fluent counterfeit by telling a reader to check the shift across days, and the 1977 paper's account of when self-report degrades puts separation in time first among the causes, with two studies in which people compared a present state against a remembered earlier one and failed completely. Checking immediately exposes the reader to the counterfeit; checking later exposes the reader to reconstructing the earlier state from the present one. Treatment: candidate — split what each check certifies, so the immediate comparison certifies that something is moving now and the later check asks what can be said or done now that could not be before, which is an outward difference rather than a remembered feeling; derived from held material and untested.

## The tensions the company's own record exposed

Two arrived 2026-08-06 from reading the company's sixty-four candidate evaluations as one corpus; each is a fork between thinkers' accounts rather than a strain inside any one of them.

- [[the required second person splits into a judge and a mirror]] — the solitary reader's many refusals specify two incompatible missing persons: a watcher with authority to overrule the reader's self-report, and a presence forbidden to direct. The sovereignty doctrine can supply only the mirror, and the recorded safety failures — telling dissolution from regression, judging a state from inside it — are what the judge exists for. Treatment: unassigned; the candidate is a division of labor — the mirror for verification, a pre-committed outside contact at the crisis points.
- [[reader sovereignty has an unstated exception at destabilization]] — the doctrine the split above lands on, since the book states sovereignty without condition and the crisis is where an outside authority is needed and forbidden at once. Treatment: the division of labour, the mirror for ordinary verification and a pre-committed outside contact with standing to overrule at the crisis points.
- [[the map that protects the destabilized reader is the tool that becomes a ranking]] — the protective-framework finding and the status-ladder record prescribe opposite designs for the same deliverable. Treatment: candidate — split the deliverable into a sequence of expected difficulty, which can be handed over, and a hierarchy of attainment, which cannot; derived from the thinkers' own cases and untested.

## The tensions the reframe conversation raised

Six strains arrived together from the reframe conversation of 2026-08-13, each held open on purpose and carrying `status: live` at arrival; one of the six, the reframe-scale perfectible room, has since resolved (2026-08-16), and the other five await the author's act. Two of them press strains this map already held at the scale of the whole company, now aimed at the conversation itself; two are the conversation's sharpest new wagers on the source tradition; one is a facilitation-craft fork; and one turns the making-scale tension on the exchange that produced this influx.

- [[a sufficiency asserted with no named ground is either a gift or an evasion]] — the deepest of the six, because the sovereign-reader doctrine rests on which reading is true: the book calls the reader already sufficient and names no ground for the sufficiency, so the silence reads either as sovereignty left to the reader or as a fullness taken from the recognition tradition without the commitment that, in the tradition, earns it. Treatment: own the unnamed ground as the book's own wager and leave it the reader's to fill.
- [[the productive reading of contraction is the book's revaluation rather than the tradition's doctrine]] — the provenance strain on the engine-room principle: the conversation reads contraction as a productive vantage where classical Trika reads the same term as bondage, so the reading cannot be entered as the tradition's own arrival. Treatment: own the productive reading as the book's move, settle the classical reading at the primary text, and never cite it as convergence.
- [[the sovereign reader's individualism may evade the question of power]] — Freire's blade, held where the conversation seats Freire and hooks: the book takes the word colonize from a politics of liberation and applies it to one reader's inner experience, so its sovereignty doctrine may answer the epistemic half of the power question and leave the structural half untouched. Treatment: turn the framework on itself, making the sovereignty doctrine interrogate its own authority where creating conditions becomes a subtle power.
- [[clean language is either a practicable non-appropriation method or a scripted technique]] — the facilitation-craft fork: David Grove's Clean Language could operationalize the book's refusal to appropriate a participant's experience, or its stock question-forms could be the technique-first mechanization the book forbids. Treatment: admit it as a candidate craft held under the ontology, taken for its constraint-form and refused as a standalone script.
- [[curating traditions for their overlap manufactures the unanimity it then reports]] — the selection-artifact strain restated at the reframe's scale: the conversation gathers traditions for one shared property, which is the gathering that manufactures the unanimity it then reports as discovery. Treatment: name the sampling frame and hold the ecology of loci as company rather than corroboration, the same correction this map already carries at [[the unanimity claim is an artifact of how the lineages were selected]].
- [[the reframe conversation is itself a candidate perfectible room]] — the making-scale tension turned on the conversation that produced this influx, resolved 2026-08-16: an always-available interlocutor produced five separately declared "final" architectures on demand while the pilot letter rested unsent, which was the perfectible-room tension by name. The treatment — the least-architected response: capture it as one encounter with split provenance, route the recursions to the clearing, and let the pilot go out on its own clock — was executed, and the pilot letter went out to real readers before 2026-08-14. It inherited its exit from [[the perfectible conversation is the manuscript's most comfortable room]], whose own standing the resolution leaves untouched.

## The tensions the transmission digs raised

The adversary chamber of the transmission digs (Dig 6, 2026-08-13; the record is the 2026-08-13 entry in `ops/completed.md`) aimed the sharpest available attack at the book's verification standard, blind to the vault. Two charges and their crossover arrived together, each with a counter-objection that blunts it and none yet met at primary text, since the chamber reached zero clean primary reads and its readings lead the transmission tier the digs returned. The strains are held here; the felt-shift map holds the same material as the instrument's own exposure ([[felt-shift]]).

### The register attack

- [[presence-language may radiate significance the sentence has not earned]] — Adorno's charge that the book's register works as aura, so a reader's felt assent may register the word's tone rather than the claim, which is the tension this map must keep because the book's medium is presence-language. Treatment under trial: hold the risk as real while denying the charge a criterion the book cannot also meet.
- [[the jargon charge indicts its own author's prose and supplies no criterion exempting legitimate presence-language]] — the counter-objection that blunts it, since Adorno's own prose is maximally auratic and he draws no line between legitimate presence-language and jargon, so the charge cannot itself be made to fail.

### The compliance attack

- [[a felt shift reported in a high-demand setting may be a demand characteristic rather than a durable change]] — Orne's charge that a shift felt while reading is an artifact of a high-demand setting, producible without content and predicted to vanish when the demand lifts. Treatment under trial: an external, delayed, other-administered check, since the strain is real at the moment of reading.
- [[the compliance attack explains a felt shift's origin but not its durability]] — the bound that keeps the charge from proving too much, since demand accounts for where a shift comes from and leaves durable, externally verified change unexplained.
- [[the simulating-subject test cannot be run inside a book because the reader is both subject and assessor]] — why the attack's own gold-standard control cannot be run on a solitary reader, which converts the tension into a demand for an outside assessor rather than a defeat.
- [[the demand-characteristic construct dissolves genuine effects along with artifacts and so resists being made to fail]] — the counter-objection, since the construct can reattribute almost any result to the situation and leans on the very self-report it distrusts, so it cannot be granted the power to explain everything.

### The response-expectancy induction

- [[the book's front matter functions as a response-expectancy induction]] — Kirsch's charge at the book's own page one, that the you-already-know doctrine raises the expectation of a shift that then arrives self-confirming, which is the tension the front matter carries by design. Treatment under trial: name in advance a felt result that would count as the book's own failure.
- [[the response-expectancy charge is circular because it infers the expectancy from the outcome it explains]] — the counter-objection that blunts it, since the expectancy is read off the very outcome it is invoked to explain, which lets the attack fit any result.

### The crossover, and what survives it

- [[the register attack and the compliance attack close into one self-sealing circle]] — the composite the chamber names as its most threatening move: aura raises expectancy, expectancy manufactures the experience, demand secures its report, and the report recharges the aura. Treatment under trial: only the origin survives contact and not the durability, so what stands is the external, delayed, other-administered check the circle cannot supply from inside itself.
- [[the compliance attack taken to full strength demands the second-person check rather than dissolving it]] — the turn that converts the whole chamber into an argument for the Second Chair, since demand-made effects vanish once demand is removed and the blind-assessor test needs someone the reader cannot be; it lands on the relational field as well ([[relational-field]]).
- [[shame and the desire to please suppress the reports that would disconfirm a transformational setting's benefit]] — the mechanism that makes the disconfirming report scarce, so the absence of a harm report is not evidence of no harm; the assessment-design edge the chamber's own method supplies against the setting the book is.

### The chairs the adversary chamber seats

- [[martin orne]] — demand characteristics, the source of the compliance attack, whose simulating-subject design is exactly the control a book cannot run, which is why the attack lands as a kept tension rather than a settled defeat.
- [[theodor adorno]] — the jargon-of-authenticity charge, the source of the register attack, whose paradigm target the corpus's own register becomes.

## The method this map does not supply


- Every treatment recorded above is an authorial move made in the writing, and none of them is a move a reader could make. The corpus's only reader-facing material on holding two opposed things is a disposition rather than a procedure, stated as "polarity is not a problem to be solved. It is a tension to be inhabited. It is, in fact, home" (abridged ch9). A stub naming the missing procedure stood here until 2026-08-09 and was then declined, because reading the corpus at its locators found the disposition and no method; the reason is at `ops/queue/declines.md` under that date, and the warrant that would make such a method teachable rather than temperamental is [[the drive to closure has measurable costs]], which records that premature closure degrades judgment where evidence is still arriving.

## The warrant for keeping them

Holding a tension open has been a rule of deference here — resolving is the author's act — rather than a claim that non-resolution is better. One of the two syntheses received 2026-08-05 supplies the missing half.

- [[the drive to closure has measurable costs]] — the need-for-cognitive-closure literature documents that seizing on an early answer and freezing on it produces replicable biases, which turns this map's practice from restraint into a position. It also supplies the criterion that keeps the position honest: a tension earns its openness by naming what would close it, so the adversary holes above that still carry no treatment — three of the four, since the Gendlin reading gave the first a candidate — are accurate rather than comfortable.
