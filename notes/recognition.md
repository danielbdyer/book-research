# recognition

This map gathers the book's central concern. Two traditions with no historical contact built their word for the deepest human change as an undoing of concealment: Heidegger revived the Greek *aletheia*, constructed as "un-forgetting," for truth as an event rather than a correct statement; the Pratyabhijñā school of Kashmir Śaivism named liberation "recognition" — the seeing of what was always there and never left. The book treats this convergence as its foundation, adds the reclaimed sense of *psychedelic* ("soul-manifesting" — the concealed becoming visible), and prints the doctrine in plain language in its front matter: "You already know everything in this book. You've just forgotten. Reading is remembering."

The corpus also stations a warning here, credited to Lacan: recognition can be conscripted by the ego — watch what the recognition is used for. That warning is one of three sentries the book keeps against its own failure modes (the others are recorded in [[tensions]]).
