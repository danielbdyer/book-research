# the arrivals that pass the independence test arrive only at the weak reading of the participation law

The participation law's independence problem has a second face, and the six evaluations that solve the first face jointly reveal it. Each thinker who reached the law with no debt to either founding pole refuses the same two upgrades. Polanyi: "Indwelling is asymmetric: the knower is changed by dwelling in the particulars, and the crystal is not changed by being known." Von Foerster "would refuse the ontological upgrade" — his claim is "epistemological and formal… not that reality is participatory." Garfinkel "would refuse the transposition to the interior," being "pointedly uninterested in mental states." Schön's practitioner converses "with a material… outside the practitioner." Wittgenstein "would refuse the ontological reading flatly" — participation as grammar, dissolving a false problem rather than disclosing a truth. And De Jaegher and Di Paolo "would refuse the third thing as an entity" — a claim about something happening, not something there.

So on current evidence the trade is exact: the arrivals that make the law a finding rather than a lineage position arrive only at its epistemic, exterior, weak reading, and the strong reading — interior, ontological, a real participant — keeps only the company that fails the independence test. The convergence centerpiece can honestly assert independence or strength, and which one is a decision about what the book claims.
