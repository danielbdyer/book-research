# the central correspondence was lived nine years before it was assembled

The corpus's headline law rests on a set of lineages the author gathered on one day, and the vault records that selection as a limit on what the agreement shows ([[the unanimity claim is an artifact of how the lineages were selected]]). The provenance addendum supplies the fact that qualifies the limit without removing it: the author did the Landmark Forum in 2017, found it instrumental, and encountered Heidegger and est through it. The cluster the record first sees in April 2024 — already fused, in the author's own shorthand "Heidegger/EST/Landmark" — had by then been carried as one unit for seven years.

The order that follows is stated in the addendum and is worth keeping intact, because each step belongs to a different hand and a different decade: lived participation in 2017; seven years of holding the three as one thing; the seed's witness-and-enactment kernel by April 2025; the collaborator's scholarly genealogy from Austin through Flores and Dreyfus to Erhard in July 2026; and the law's sentence on 7 July 2026. The addendum's summary: "the correspondence was autobiographical before it was scholarly — by nine years."

What this changes is the character of the curation rather than its fact. The lineages were still selected for the property later reported as unanimous, and that remains a sampling artifact. But the addendum's distinction is the useful one: "You weren't assembling strangers who happened to agree; you were seating a household."

## Substantiation

- The 2017 fact is the author's testimony, entering the record through the addendum rather than from any thread; the addendum marks it as replacing an artifact, since Round One's April 2024 date "was an artifact of where the record begins" (provenance addendum §3).
- The record corroborates the texture without the date: a June 2024 thread asks directly about Landmark's philosophical underpinnings, and the declaration-before-witnesses and completion-conversation mechanics recur through the July 2026 material (provenance addendum §3).
- The corpus seats this lineage for the mechanism and records its standing danger in the same breath, which is consistent with a position held long enough to have been tested: "declaration-as-vestment — the declared self worn as costume" (atlas §IX.3).
- The finding does not extend to the other lineages in the roll-call. The Kashmir Śaiva material has its own dated entry point ([[the triad predates the author's contact with the tradition named the triad]]); the facilitation craft is the author's own practice; the contemporary teachers and the builders were assembled from research.
