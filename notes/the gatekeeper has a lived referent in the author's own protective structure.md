# the gatekeeper has a lived referent in the author's own protective structure

The book's figure for the guarding part — met with a bow, never a fight — is not a borrowed clinical courtesy. It is a portrait. The provenance record carries the referent in three sightings across two years of the author's conversations: a protective structure the author calls "the little guy," described in the May 2026 record as "born as bodyguard from the MDMA experience"; a December 2025 thread's phrase "before the MDMA ever crystallized the wound"; and the experience itself, placed in the author's early twenties (provenance addendum §4).

The material the book builds from this is visible and dated. The unit on the peak experience gives the guarding part its hardest scene — the panic of a protector that finds itself, briefly, without its role — and the corpus's account of that panic is the ethic's origin in miniature: the part is not defeated at the peak, and it returns, and how it is met afterward is the whole question (abridged ch12). The descent-and-integration material, and the high-energy-states research the author commissioned in May 2026 comparing the fast path and the slow, descend from the same history. The reclamation of the book's central word was performed on this same life first ([[psychedelic names a movement not a substance]]).

What this gives a drafting session is the register. The bow is written from the inside: by someone whose own protector has been met, over years, with enough respect that it acquired an affectionate name. The doctrine that suffering is protection persisting past its season — fullness folded, never corruption — has, in this one structure, its first case.
