# aristotle distinguishes activity complete in itself from movement toward an outside end

The book's practice clause holds that a practice expresses realization rather than being a means to it ([[the practice is the expression of realization rather than a means to it]]). Aristotle draws the same line as a metaphysical distinction, and a reading of *Metaphysics* IX.6 confirms it at the text: some activities contain their end within themselves and are complete at every moment — seeing, thinking, living well — while others are mere movement toward an end outside them and are incomplete until finished, as learning and building are. Practice of the first kind is not a means; it is already its own completion. With Dōgen's *shusho-itto* and Deci and Ryan's undermining effect, both read the same day, Aristotle makes the clause a three-tradition arrival — Zen, experimental psychology, and Greek philosophy — which is the caliber of company the convergence centerpiece is built from.

## Substantiation

- The book's half is on record: the anti-instrumentality clause constrains every exercise in the curriculum (walked IX; [[the practice is the expression of realization rather than a means to it]]).
- Aristotle's half is now shown, from the Ross translation of *Metaphysics* IX.6 (the reading record is `archive/reading — aristotle, metaphysics IX.6 on energeia and kinesis.md`): "at the same time we are seeing and have seen, are understanding and have understood, are thinking and have thought (while it is not true that at the same time we are learning and have learnt, or are being cured and have been cured). At the same time we are living well and have lived well," closing "The latter sort of process, then, I call an actuality, and the former a movement." The complete activity (*energeia*) has its end present in it; the movement (*kinesis*) has its end outside and after it. Read in translation, not the Greek; the *praxis*/*poiesis* distinction of *Nicomachean Ethics* VI.4–5 was named but not separately read.
- The refusal is kept, and it is the same shape the privation reading already found. Aristotle's *energeia* is actualization under a teleological metaphysics, which sits closer to the book's genuine-novelty fork than to recognition-as-un-forgetting, so he warrants the structure of the clause and not the recognition frame around it — as [[aristotle on privation]] warranted the absence-form and not the therapeutic step.
