# the morning is where a day acquires its coherence

The gesture makes the start of a day the point where the day becomes coherent. The word morning appears once across the seven sources, in this contents entry: the corpus treats time at the scale of the moment (the temporal loop) and of the season (the metabolic arc), and the day — the scale a reader lives at — is missing between them.
