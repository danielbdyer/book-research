# the morning is where a day acquires its coherence

The gesture asserts that a day has an entry point, that what happens there determines the day's coherence, and that the entry can be designed. The vault does not hold this, and it holds nothing adjacent to it. The corpus treats time at two scales and no others: the moment, through the temporal loop and the felt shift, and the season, through the metabolic arc. The day is missing entirely, which is the scale at which a reader actually lives.

This note is a stub. Its state is `nascent`: the proposition is the gesture's, not the vault's, and it is recorded so the territory is reachable from the graph rather than only from the inventory in `ops/open corpus.md`. The gesture is a named unit of the earliest contents that no document develops; naming a territory is evidence the project has been to its edge, not evidence it belongs in the book.

## What the corpus supplies

- The word morning appears once in the seven sources, in this contents entry. No descriptive source treats the day as a unit.
- The corpus's one commitment at this scale is doctrinal rather than practical: ordinary time is where the work happens, recorded at [[the tuesday is the temple]]. That claim names a Tuesday afternoon and says nothing about how a Tuesday is entered.
- This gesture heads a part of four in the earliest contents, the whole of which the arc drops; the scope finding is at [[the mythic operating system contents hold four territories the arc drops]].

## What would close it

Closing it means deciding whether the book operates at the scale of the day, which would be a new scale for it.
