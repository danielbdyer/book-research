# weil is seated as the opponent the plumbing deserves

The book's commitment to ordinary life as the full site of the work has a genuine opponent, and the corpus chooses to house her rather than refute a weaker version. Simone Weil's position — decreation, attention as the purest generosity, affliction that cannot be transformed but only borne — runs directly against the householder commitment, and she is seated in the book's reference lineage at full strength for that reason. The corpus's stated logic: the axiom is kept honest by its strongest adversary rather than by straw versions of renunciation.

The tension is deliberately unresolved. Weil also does second duty in the coaches' expansion, representing suffering that is carried rather than cured. The classical form of that counterweight — Aristotle's distinction between privation (a missing ingredient) and affliction (a presence that must be borne) — has not been researched for this project; it is on the pending-evaluation list in `ops/queue/candidates.md`.

## Substantiation

- Atlas §XI.2g records the tension; §IX.8d records Weil's seat and her double role.
- First-telling, "The honesty," discloses the arrangement to the reader: the book keeps its strongest opponent in-house.
- Walked IX carries her position summary and the bearers' expansion of the coaches' map.
