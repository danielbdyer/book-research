# the learning arc

This is the vault's developmental spine: the single arc the reframe conversation traced and the two books inherit, laid over the domain maps so the graph reads as a movement rather than a set of bins. The domain maps gather claims by concern; this surface orders the same claims by where they fall on the journey, so an edge here carries direction — one station *carries into* the next — where a domain map's edges only say *relates to*. It was installed by the author's decision of 2026-08-20 (the spine-and-reweave reweighting, `ops/decisions.md`) to make the reframe material the graph's center of gravity, because the load-bearing notes already existed and only needed to be weighted, not multiplied.

Read this before the domain maps, after `ops/orientation.md` and `notes/index.md`. The maps are the terrain; this is the road across it.

## The still center

Every station serves one question, and the graph resolves to it: [[the master question is the conditions under which a person remains the author of what they become while being genuinely changed]] — how something can change a person deeply without taking that person's place as the author of what they become. It is the author's own life-question ("that is the question of my life," reframe conversation, on how much form holds just enough that surprise can still happen), and it is held as an orientation the books equip a life to keep asking, not an answer they close. Its near-identical form at the scale of one reading relation is [[the book can only be auxiliary to a sovereign reader]].

## The arc, in one movement

Both books run one developmental sequence — **recognition → relation → participation → stewardship** — and differ only at the endpoint: Book I ends in stewardship of the field, Book II in stewardship of the self ([[both books move through recognition → relation → participation → stewardship]]). The capacity the final movement names, beneath both books, is [[participatory stewardship is the capacity beneath both books]] — staying authorially present while participating in a reality one does not fully control. The two books are one gesture turned two ways ([[the two books mirror each other on every shared concern]], [[the two books differ in locus of practice not subject matter]]): Book I is the movement turned outward toward another; Book II is the same movement turned inward toward oneself.

## What the arc rests on — the shared foundations

Three bodies of material sit beneath the arc and feed both books; a drafter starts here.

- **The recognition engine.** The Kashmir-Śaiva-derived structure the book draws on — the pulse (spanda) felt as sheer aliveness; contraction and expansion (saṅkoca and vikāsa) as the pulse's two phases; reflexive awareness (vimarśa), a distinct element the book holds on its own relevance and not as contraction's partner; and recognition as un-forgetting of what never left ([[recognition]], [[aletheia and pratyabhijna converge on truth as un-forgetting]]). The terms are held as fingers pointing at a moon, each on its own relevance rather than welded into a circuit, and their standing is uneven (spanda is the author's own; the reflexive reading of vimarśa is model-elaborated and awaits his own words). The engine's consequences for suffering live at [[contraction-not-corruption]].
- **The convergence of thinkers → serves Book I.** Book I's authority is discernment and synthesis, so the company of thinkers gathered in [[interlocutors]] is Book I-shaped evidence.
- **The lived practice / the felt shift → serves Book II.** Book II's authority is embodied realization, so the practice curriculum and its verification in [[felt-shift]] is Book II-shaped evidence.
- **The technology strand — the shared "why now."** In an age of abundant information and pervasive delegation, the scarce thing is participation in one's own life; the book's front-matter doctrine already inverts the attention economy's sentence. It is carried as a cross-cutting frame in front matter plus one applied block per book, not a third strand ([[the same technology can amplify sovereignty or erode it depending on the locus from which it is used]], [[AI is neither master, servant, replacement, nor oracle but interlocutor]], [[the civilizational book is a horizon not a structure to build]]).

## Book I — the facilitator, turned outward: the arc toward disappearance

The movement runs from the problem of helping to the steward who withdraws. Its founding move is the author's own: writing first for facilitators dissolves the double bind of establishing authority in order to relinquish it.

- **Recognition** — what is actually happening when one person helps another become what they could not yet see? The first ethical declaration is that the other is not your project.
- **Relation** — sovereignty is authorship preserved within relationship ([[sovereignty is authorship preserved within relationship, not control over reality]]), and the facilitator's ethic is guarding it in the other: influence without appropriating. The paradox that governs the craft is [[the more powerful the facilitator becomes, the more important sovereignty becomes]].
- **Participation** — what facilitation protects is not autonomy but participation, which cannot be transmitted as information; the facilitator perceives the field's pulse and creates conditions rather than outcomes.
- **Stewardship of the field → the facilitator disappears** — power is stewarded rather than denied, and success is unattributable: "I helped create a field in which something became possible." The endpoint is [[facilitation is stewardship of conditions]] and [[the facilitator must become facilitable, permeability without loss of sovereignty]], and the arc closes by the facilitator becoming less needed — which opens Book II.

## Book II — the practitioner, turned inward: the arc toward return

The same movement, with no one else in the room. It opens on a reversal — you are already participating; the only question is how consciously — and its first mercy mirrors Book I's: the self is not a project.

- **Recognition** — self-facilitation begins from "what is happening?" rather than "what is wrong with me?", so the person becomes an ecology rather than a defect ([[self-facilitation is creating conditions for one's own becoming, not self-management, optimization, or analysis]]).
- **Relation** — the practitioner develops an internal facilitative stance and learns that self-facilitation cannot become isolation; sovereignty is guarded here against dependence *and* against withdrawal ([[self-facilitation requires facilitating oneself without becoming controller or captive]]).
- **Participation** — awareness turns reflexive and the felt pulse is watched inwardly ("what is moving here?"), which is what an unremembered contraction cannot do for itself; the felt form of the whole is [[patriotism for oneself is the felt form of the auxiliary relation to a sovereign reader]].
- **Stewardship of the self → the practitioner returns** — one does not graduate; the point is becoming capable of returning, again and again, better at meeting what arrives. The dramaturgy passes through a peak into descent and integration, and the integration *is* the return: the peak is an interior station, not the destination (which keeps the ending from manufacturing the false peak the author flagged). The vault's crescendo-to-peak shape is held here as reconciled into the return, not as a rival, distinguished at [[the earlier contents distribute transcendence where the arc concentrates a peak]].

## The frame that holds both

The two books are two loci inside one ecology, none sovereign over the reader ([[an ecology is a bounded but permeable domain of multiple irreducible loci of participation, none sovereign]], [[the book is one locus in an ecology of participation, not the sovereign source of the reader's recognition]]). The arc performs what it describes: it front-loads authority and spends it toward disappearance and return, which is the only way a book can hold both poles of that tension at full strength — sequentially, since a page cannot hold them at once.
