# the compositional notation does not license the operations its syntax implies

The book writes its diagnoses as formulas — Burnout = Obligation − Sanctuary, Attend = Maintain + Bind — and the notation makes a promise beyond the words: that these signs behave the way signs behave in chemistry and arithmetic, so that a formula can be substituted into, decomposed, and read off. The executed prose edition is the only source that supplies the compositions rather than just the results, which makes it the only place the promise is checkable. Checked there, it does not hold, and it fails in three independent ways on a single chapter's worth of text.

The failures are structural rather than sloppy, and none of them touches whether the diagnoses are *useful*. What they establish is narrower and load-bearing for the author's question: every discrimination the chemistry is credited with making is in fact made by the surrounding prose, and the notation restates the discrimination afterward without being able to derive it. A vocabulary that names what is missing is doing real work. A calculus that computes what is missing is what the notation depicts, and there is no calculus.

## Substantiation

Three checks, all against `abridged ch5`, where the six derived primitives are composed from the six base ones.

- **Two distinct operations receive the same expression.** "ATTEND = Maintain + Bind" appears at one point and "COMMIT = Bind + Maintain" thirteen lines later, in the same list, with no comment. The component sets are identical, and the glosses nearly are — attending is "to maintain a connection with it," committing is "to bind a connection and maintain it across time." If the notation individuated operations, these would be one operation. The text plainly means two, and the difference between them (a connection held now versus a connection held across time) is stated in words that the formula does not contain.

- **Two of the six reach outside the alphabet for their second term.** "CONTAIN = Maintain + the boundary-form of the Knife" and "DEFER = Maintain + the not-yet of Create." Neither *the boundary-form of the Knife* nor *the not-yet of Create* is one of the six base primitives; each is a qualification supplied in prose at the moment it is needed. The closure claim — that the derived operations are composites of the base set — is therefore false for a third of the derived set on the page where it is asserted.

- **Substitution collapses the molecules.** The catalogue gives Obligation = Commit + Attend + Maintain (`abridged ch6`). Substituting the chapter's own definitions gives (Bind + Maintain) + (Maintain + Bind) + Maintain, which reduces to Bind + Maintain — the expression for Commit, and for Attend. The molecule and its own component are the same formula. Carrying this into the signature diagnosis, Burnout = Obligation − Sanctuary asks the reader to subtract Contain + Maintain + Release from an expression that contains only two of those three terms, and the notation says nothing about what subtracting an absent term does.

The honest defense, which the corpus itself supplies, is that composition here is meant as emergence rather than as arithmetic: molecules are defined as "stable combinations of 3+ primitives with emergent attributes none of the atoms have alone" (atlas §IV), and on that reading substitution was never licensed. The defense works, and it costs the notation its remaining claim. If the plus sign is not addition and terms may not be substituted, then the expressions are mnemonics for propositions stated in prose, and the four discriminations the book most depends on are all carried by the prose alone: what separates attending from committing, where the deferral attaches in trust versus paralysis ([[trust and paralysis are isomers]]), that grief's missing term is a person rather than an operation ([[the minus sign is what makes a molecule formula prescriptive]]), and whether a missing element was withheld or never offered ([[the minus sign cannot say whether the missing element was withheld or never on offer]]).

One consequence for the vault's own reading. The isomer teaching is the clearest instance: real isomerism distinguishes compounds by the arrangement of bonds, and this notation has no bond term — Bind + Commit + Defer is a commutative list. The atlas states the discrimination correctly in words, "the difference is where Defer attaches — to the outcome (Trust) or to the Commit itself (Paralysis)" (atlas §IV), which is precisely the information the formula cannot hold. The corpus knows what it means; the notation cannot say it.

**Extended 2026-08-05 by a complete audit.** All twenty-one formulas in the corpus were checked, not just chapter five's, and two further arithmetic failures came out of it: `Stagnation = Lifecycle − Transform` reduces to Transform's own formula, so the state defined by lacking the operation is the operation; and Trust and Paralysis cannot be both isomers and privations, since the privation formula leaves them sharing one atom of three. The inventory is `ops/formula audit.md`, and the general finding it supports — that the notation records composition while every teaching is about structure — is [[the chemistry's teachings are structural and its notation is only compositional]].
