# curating traditions for their overlap manufactures the unanimity it then reports

The reframe conversation states a research discipline against premature agreement, and its own method performs the move the discipline warns against. The discipline is to never collapse a correspondence before its difference is understood, because ten traditions can seem to say one thing only when their vocabulary has been flattened until they agree. The method is to build an ecology of loci by gathering traditions selected for a property they share — their participatory, inherently interactive character. Selecting a set for a shared property and then reporting the set's agreement on that property is one operation described twice. So the ecology of loci is exposed to the same defect the vault already recorded for the book's headline convergence: the agreement is a fact about how the sample was chosen, not a discovery about the world. The tension is live because the reframe supplies both the guard and the breach in one conversation, and because the book's whole comparative practice depends on the difference between resonance found and resonance manufactured.

## Substantiation

- reframe conversation, 2026-08-13 (transcript lines 3195–3198): "If ten traditions seem to say the same thing only because you've flattened their vocabulary until they agree, that's not discovery. If they remain genuinely different—and yet something survives the crossing—that is much more interesting." At lines 3332–3344 the same caution is named: the danger is "premature correspondence," the reward is "Aha! They're all pointing to the same thing," and the discipline is "Never collapse a correspondence until the difference has been adequately understood."
- The method that trips the guard runs through the conversation as the gathering of traditions by their participatory character. The vault has already dated and named the identical structure in the corpus at [[the unanimity claim is an artifact of how the lineages were selected]]: the lineages were curated by one axis, so agreement within them is "close to guaranteed and evidentially near-worthless."
- The scholarly form of the caution is seated: Steven Katz's constructivism argues that a convergence reported across traditions can be an artifact of the descriptions rather than a finding about the reality described (steven katz).
- The treatment under trial is to preserve difference before any synthesis and keep the remainder — what resists being collapsed — as the more informative output, so agreement inside a curated set is entered as company rather than as corroboration. The treatment is the comparative method the book already names; whether it holds when the curator has a lived warrant for the curation is the part still on trial.
