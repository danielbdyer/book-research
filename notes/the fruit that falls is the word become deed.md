# the fruit that falls is the word become deed

The fruit that falls carries action at ripeness: the word spent as deed, vaikharī as kriyā. The figure is the release doctrine in the orchard's vocabulary, and it travels with the house rule that decides whether finished work ships.

## Substantiation

- Arc-gloss, the image-system: "The Fruit That Falls ↔ ripe action ↔ vaikharī / kriyā. House rule: ripeness is not refrigeration. The archive preserves; the orchard ripens." Atlas §X.5f carries the same equation.
- The walked document applies the figure to the manuscript itself: "fifteen years of poems, seventeen chapters, and the standing question of which fruit falls" (walked XI; atlas §XI.2i).
