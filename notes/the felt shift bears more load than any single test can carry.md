# the felt shift bears more load than any single test can carry

The book makes the felt shift its single test of truth — "nothing enters the lattice that the body has not countersigned" ([[nothing enters the lattice the body has not countersigned]], atlas §III) — and then asks the same instrument to do two further jobs it is not obviously fit for. It seats the body as the referee of the surrender-versus-will fork, case by case ([[surrender and will are adjudicated from the body rather than from doctrine]]). It hands the body the judgment that tells dissolution from regression at the peak, which is the one judgment the body is worst placed to make ([[the book cannot tell dissolution from regression and neither can its reader]]). And the instrument's reliability is never argued for, because the standard presupposes reliable access to one's own states and the literature against that premise is unread ([[introspective access is the felt shift's undefended premise]]). Four separate notes circle this concentration; none states the tension it makes. The tension is that the felt shift is load-bearing beyond what any single test can carry, so one defect in it reaches everything downstream at once.

The consequence follows from the concentration. The instrument has two documented defects — fluent prose can manufacture the somatic yes, and the signal is muted in exactly the readers who most need it ([[felt shift versus felt confirmation]]) — and because the whole apparatus runs through the one instrument, either defect propagates through the verification, the fork, and the crisis judgment together rather than in one place.

## Substantiation

- The load is measured by what routes through the instrument, not asserted. Verification: [[nothing enters the lattice the body has not countersigned]] (atlas §III). The surrender-versus-will adjudication: [[surrender and will are adjudicated from the body rather than from doctrine]] (atlas §XI.2), which the corpus asks the body to settle "for the entire coaching industry." The dissolution-versus-regression judgment: [[the book cannot tell dissolution from regression and neither can its reader]], where the standard that decides everything else is the one worst placed to decide this.
- The reliability the three loads rest on is itself unargued ([[introspective access is the felt shift's undefended premise]], first-ranked of the sort's holes at `mesh grid: tier 1`).
- This tension is adjacent to a different single-instrument finding and not the same one. [[the verification standard rests on one name and cannot be defended by company]] records that the standard has only one bearer, Gendlin, so the convergence the book uses everywhere else is unavailable to defend it. That note is about the instrument's lack of company; this note is about the instrument's overload. The two compound: the felt shift is asked to do the most and defended by the fewest.
- The treatment under trial distributes the load rather than strengthening the one instrument. The felt shift certifies movement, which is the narrowed certificate the Gendlin reading established ([[the felt shift certifies carrying forward rather than correspondence]]); the judgments it cannot make — dissolution from regression, and the crisis calls — route to a pre-committed second person, which is the judge the sovereignty doctrine cannot supply from inside ([[the required second person splits into a judge and a mirror]]).
