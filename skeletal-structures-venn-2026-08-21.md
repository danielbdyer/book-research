Eight Opus 4.8 cartographers each map one latent organizing skeleton of the book (dispatched 2026-08-21), to find the **Venn**: where several skeletons place the *same* material at the *same* structural position, it is high-confidence — near-certain to be in the book, the scaffold a drafter can assume; where they diverge, the structure is open. This ledger captures each skeleton's map and — the synthesis fuel — its **overlap/Venn inputs**, as they return.

When all eight are in, the Venn is woven from here into (a) the forest map, redrawn as the union of skeletons with the high-confidence core lit and the open joints marked, and (b) the **repeatable structure skill** — content-hash "receipt" caching per finding, so it re-verifies rather than re-derives, busting only the findings whose sources changed.

**Standing.** The one skeleton held under an explicit author demotion: admitted **scaffolding** ("an uncertain secondary or tertiary concern," 2026-08-02; extended twice). Plus the praxis face: the twelve Kata (primitives made walkable) and the treatment's **five rooms** (the Guild — thinker material, NOT house rooms).

**The load-bearing base vs. the decorative stories (the key finding):** **L0 (the three structures + open center) is load-bearing** — 29 builders in unrelated media build Wall/Door/Floor without having seen it drawn ("found, not invented," the isomorph/centerpiece evidence class); portable across book/graph/playlist; axiomatized *before* contact with the tradition it rhymes with; carries the project's largest measured doctrinal support; explicitly exempted from the demotion. **L1–L3 are largely decorative** — the vault's audit: "every discrimination the molecule formulas are credited with is made by the prose beside them." ## How the receipts keep this honest and nearly free

Each of the seven high-confidence findings and six open joints above is, in the skill, a **finding record** carrying a fingerprint of the exact source files it was read from (the notes, the concordance, the decisions rows). A later run re-fingerprints those files; where the fingerprint still matches, the finding is affirmed *without re-reading* — the cost is a hash, not a subagent. Where a source changed (a note edited, a decision added), only the findings whose fingerprints broke are re-derived, and only those. The map is the cache; the fingerprints are the receipts; the churn is small because the corpus is stable and the notes move one at a time.
