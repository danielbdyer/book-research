**THIS VAULT'S CONVENTIONS (overrides three stock checks below).** A description here is a complete sentence that ENDS WITH A PERIOD, up to 220 characters (`templates/claim-note.md`); the stock no-trailing-period convention does not apply, and stripping a period is never a fix. Map membership means: listed in a declared map's claims list — maps have no 'Core Ideas' section. Semantic search runs via the qmd CLI; if `.qmd/index.sqlite` is older than the newest note, reindex first or report findability scores as stale. The authoritative cross-field rules (status/treatment on tensions only, privation paired with state: privated, sources required) are checked by `scripts/queries/frontmatter-parse.sh`, and `scripts/queries/strike-links.sh` ranks the body paragraphs thinnest after their links are struck out — candidates for the cold read, never verdicts, because the author has ruled the gesture tic un-regexable. Apply the personality block in `ops/derivation-manifest.md` and the research-diction register (`CLAUDE.md`, Research diction; `ops/methodology/prose register.md`) to every sentence written. **The polyphony of lenses is part of the health pass (added 2026-08-21).** A full `/verify` also runs `scripts/queries/lens-check.sh` (the family files — `the-loci.md`, `the-axioms.md`, `the-paradoxes.md`, `the-constellation.md` — must be all-fresh, no expired cache sections) and `scripts/queries/lens-check.sh --coverage` (the substrate pivot's invariant: a rising uncovered count means new notes have landed in no family, and a REGRESSED line is a finding to report). A newly-created or edited note is not fully verified until the coverage check has seen it — either it belongs in a family (route it via `/lens refresh`) or it is legitimately outside the families, but the check should have been consulted. This does not gate note capture; it reports, like the other health checks.
