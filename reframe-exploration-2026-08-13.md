## III. The architecture — the central recommendation

**Adopt a nested frame, and write the diptych.**

**The nested frame (invariant, and the reframe's own view).** One shared ontology (the ecology of participation / "participatory stewardship") lives *upstream* in the vault, never as a book. Two books are projections that differ in **locus** (self / relation) and in **dramaturgy**. Book III (civilizational/AI) is a bounded **horizon only** — not a structure to build. This is simply true and should be adopted regardless of the writing plan; the reframe states it itself ("the books are downstream of the ontology, not the ontology itself").

**The psychedelic arc is a dramaturgy, not a rival spine.** The ecology-of-participation is the *ontology* (what exists; a recursive loop). The psychedelic arc — preparation → onset → deepening → peak → descent → integration — is a *temporal traversal* of that territory, with a climax and a felt respiratory shape the flat ontology lacks. They are different layers and do not compete. The arc becomes **Book II's dramaturgy**; and because the reframe's own governing formal constraint ("do not explain the reader out of an encounter") is exactly the performing law, the psychedelic arc is the *superior* realization of Book II's own formal aspiration, not merely compatible with it. **Book I needs its own, mirror dramaturgy:** where Book II crescendos to a peak (the self fills — "fires on all cylinders"), Book I diminuendos to a disappearance ("the facilitator disappears" — empties, becomes less necessary). The Stance is the shared hinge pointing opposite directions.

**The one genuine surgery the split requires:** migrate the relational-field well from Book II's center to Book I's ground. This resolves [[a codex is a one-chair technology making a two-chair argument]] rather than aggravating it, and its cost to the near-complete Book II material must be sized by the author (open question below).

### The central fork: which book is written first

The reframe argues **Book I first** (the ethical constraint "I cannot make another become what I want" must be grasped before its recursive self-application). That logic is real. But three considerations from the vault press the other way — **write Book II first**:

1. **Ripeness follows the primary material** (2026-08-05), and the primary material (the poems, the 4am recognition, the lived practice) is Book II material. Book II's authority, in the reframe's own terms, comes from "embodied realization" — which this author has now. Book I's comes from "discernment and synthesis" of a facilitation doctrine he practices but has not yet theorized.
2. **The ethical constraint is already internalized**, so Book I need not be written first to honor it. Book II already encodes the sovereign reader, non-appropriation, and withdrawal-at-the-peak at full strength. Logical priority is satisfied; writing priority is free.
3. **A near-complete Book II already exists** — the seventeen chapters at madhyama, the abridged edition that "executes the arc," the correspondence program, the drafted pilot letter. Book-I-first would strand the ripe material, demote a just-chosen form, restart the reading phase for a new book, and swing the project hard toward its own named failure mode (vault work displacing manuscript work).

**Recommendation:** nested ontology (invariant) + diptych written **Book II first**, with Book I begun as a parallel graduate-student reading phase. Treat the reframe's "Book I first" as an argument about *logical* priority that is *already satisfied in the material*, not a writing mandate. Present to the author the live alternative of **one book with two movements** (lower risk, keeps facilitation subordinate) if two manuscripts is more than he wants to commit to now.

One-directional flow (Tana upstream/capture → markdown downstream/disciplined); do **not** build two-way sync; do **not** migrate the 303 notes; if the seam cannot be held, collapse *toward markdown* (the pipeline/register/provenance are the irreplaceable, markdown-native assets).

Recommendation: keep the proposition-sentence as the authored unit (the register discipline depends on it); let the named-concept be a lighter Tana-side entity; do not collapse them.

## 2. The intellectual engine room — what genuinely deepens
