# The practice-model ecology — what the vault already holds, sorted by structure

## 1. What was asked, and the answer in one paragraph

The author's hunch was that the ways the coaches facilitate "roughly underpin some of the same praxis schematics as other parts of our notetaking" — that Kashmir Śaivism, Heidegger, Landmark's privations and polarities, Wilber's holarchy, and his own account of how recognition lands share a structure the coaches' models also instantiate. The survey confirms the hunch and gives it a shape. Roughly seventy distinct practice-models are already in the graph, and they fall into eleven recurring structural families (section 3). The coaches' five models are all instances of families the vault already holds, and they share one form among themselves — "a location plus an instruction" ([[the transformation field divides into five theories of where suffering lives]]) — which makes them commensurable with each other and incommensurable with the book's own combinatorial diagnosis; that is the exact content of the bloc charge already on record ([[the coaches are a bloc that competes with the compositional scheme]]). Two findings go beyond the hunch. First, the family the whole book runs on — privation — splits four ways under the survey, and the author's own founding experience belongs to only one of them: it is not a subtraction and not an absence but an un-clenching of a folded fullness, a result three regions reached independently (section 4). Second, the one structure the author named for the shape of his experience, Wilber's holarchy, is the one structural family the vault does not carry as a structure of experience at all — the word "holarchy" appears nowhere in `notes/`, transcend-and-include exists only as a method, and a levels-model is constitutionally guarded against by the ecology's law that no locus is sovereign (section 6). Those two findings are what the reading program has to answer to.

## 2. The vault's existing organizing structures, and why none of them is this map

The vault already holds an unusually dense apparatus for organizing the book's material: nineteen lens entry points indexed at `the-lenses.md`, eight of them deepened into checkable family files; twelve topic maps, each organized by concern and, beneath that, by the provenance round each claim arrived in; a sixteen-section scaffold that reads the book eight ways; and a set of methodology directives. None of these is a taxonomy of practice-models. The lenses organize this book's own material; the Loci register sorts standpoints by what each discloses and hides; the Constellation sorts thinkers by the finding each reached; and the System lens is the only place a practice-model is read as a structure, under the standing demotion of the system to scaffolding ([[the system is scaffolding rather than the centerpiece]]). The closest existing prototype of the move this file makes is `ops/methodology/the four kinds of structure.md`, which took four structures that looked like competitors for one slot, declared them four kinds of structure at four altitudes — operator, method, dramaturgy, ontology — and assigned each a destination. That is the sorting move an ecology of practice-models needs, executed once on four items from one conversation and filed backstage. This file executes it across the graph.

Two organizing principles the vault already runs bear directly on any import and are recorded first. The `the-axioms.md` family defines an axiom as "a constraint that protects the ecology's integrity," and the author's own gloss is that "maybe these axioms aren't propositions at all but orientations" — so the vault's deepest organizing layer is already a set of postures toward practice rather than a set of claims about it. And `the-paradoxes.md` holds that every role in the book wears one knot — "participatory sovereignty requires influence without domination" — and fails the same two ways, by control or by abdication; its instruction is that the knot "is not a contradiction to solve. It is a design tension to inhabit." Both families are largely model-produced and unratified (84% and 81% of their attested notes are nascent), but their form is the form the coaches' models will be read against: a posture held, a polarity inhabited.

## 4. The deepest structural finding: the privation family splits, and the author's experience belongs to one branch

The book "runs on a single privative grammar, and it never says so" (author statement, 2026-08-10, recorded at [[the book reads deep change as uncovering rather than addition]]): recognition un-forgets, suffering unfolds, the reclaimed *psychedelic* un-conceals. The survey found that "privation" is carrying four different engines, and that they do not move a person the same way.

1. **Subtractive privation** removes what was added: Advaita's *neti neti*, the Daoist "does less every day," the apophatic stripping of predicates, Erhard's "examine and eliminate the grip of their everyday common-sense worldview," and the operational form of the book's own Privation Principle — "supply the missing element," which the vault already records as reading like an addition against the book's uncovering claim. Its instances disagree about what remains after the subtraction, and the graph already forbids merging the destinations to make the method look unanimous ([[there is a homeward path that goes by subtraction, letting go of what was added]]).
2. **Contractive privation** un-clenches a folded fullness that was never lost: the three malas read at the primary Sanskrit ("though uncontracted, appear contracted," [[the triads privations map the three malas corner for corner]]), the fold that is learned rather than repaired ([[suffering is contraction never corruption]]), IFS's protector met with a bow, Hudson's welcome, and Alexander's preserve-and-strengthen. Nothing is supplied and nothing is removed.
3. **The never-formed gap** is an absence where something should have developed and did not: Hübl's inherited trauma, "an absence in the field rather than a wound in it," and Winnicott's "nothing happening when something might profitably have happened" ([[what was never metabolized lives on as absence and hubl reached it through inherited trauma]]). The survey's inference, stated as such: this is not the same schematic as a folded fullness, and the vault has not yet distinguished them — [[suffering is contraction never corruption]] already flags "a fold with no fold-maker inside the person carrying it" as the case the contraction reading strains at.
4. **The anti-privation** is a suffering diagnosed by a presence: the Racket, "Complaint + Payoff, both fixed," the one additive formula in a catalogue of subtractions and "the suffering the privation formulas cannot reach" ([[the privation move is an independent arrival across five fields rather than a book coinage]]).

The author's own founding experience belongs to the second branch and to no other, and three regions reached that conclusion without contact with each other. The scaffolding read found that his "fundamental clenching that didn't obscure the vision" fits the book's uncovering generalization and strains against its supply-the-missing-element operational form. The source-tradition read ran his account through Aristotle's three principles and found a substratum, a form, and no *steresis* — "nothing was missing and nothing was lost" — so that his account is not a privation in Aristotle's sense at all, and concluded that if the book's grammar is to be unified the unifying term should be *un-clenching* rather than *un-covering*, which is what [[aletheia and pratyabhijna part on whether un-seeing is concealment or contraction]] had already concluded from the traditions' side. And the coaches read found that of the five coach models only Hudson's welcome is contractive; the see-through camp is subtractive, Robbins replaces rather than un-clenches, and Hübl's gap is the third branch. The consequence for the book is already half-recorded: the productive reading of contraction is the book's own revaluation and not the tradition's doctrine ([[the productive reading of contraction is the book's revaluation rather than the tradition's doctrine]], a live tension whose classical side is still unread) — and the author's reflection is the strongest first-person warrant in the vault for the very reading the vault marks as a departure. Whether that warrant is enough to carry the revaluation is his to say.

## 6. The empty cell: holarchy, and what the ecology does to a levels-model

The author named one structure for the shape of what he felt, and it is the one structural family the vault does not hold as a structure of experience. The word "holarchy" appears nowhere in `notes/`. What the vault holds is transcend-and-include, twice: as "an unattributed operating verb… Koestler's holon by way of Wilber, which the author uses as an editing instruction" ([[ken wilber]]), and as the method by which the ecology frame re-nested the verification frame without deleting it — "the larger frame preserves the smaller without collapsing it," and "the move applies to itself, so the ecology frame does not become the new sovereign" ([[the ecology frame transcends and includes the verification frame rather than replacing it]]). The shape appears in three places without the name: the ladder of speech, where "the descent is not degradation — vaikharī is parā at full extension"; the metabolic arc, where "composting is not death. It is return to potential"; and the recognition loop, whose last station is a renewed encounter. Each reaches "resolution opening up into another mystery" sideways — through a speech ladder, an orchard image, or a description of inquiry — and none carries it as what recognition is like from inside.

The reflection's own extrapolation already separated the author's resonance from the vault's reservation: "the alignment is with the transcend-and-include both-and, not the quadrant grid." The survey confirms that these are two threads with different standings. The holarchy is in use at `state: full`; the four quadrants are held as "a coordinate system, not an architecture" — answering "where is it happening?" while the book's own account answers "what is happening?" — at `state: nascent`, with no primary Wilber read ([[wilber's four quadrants are a coordinate system, not an architecture]]). And the lens-families read recorded a complication the vault holds against itself: the Loci family already runs on Wilber's un-attributed four-quadrant grid, so the vault's own no-top structure leans on a levels-theorist's coordinates without crediting them.

What the ecology's law does to any imported levels-model is stated in the graph with unusual precision, and it should be read before Wilber is. A hierarchy enters as a way-in, never as architecture: the one containment hierarchy the reframe produced was "produced on request and disavowed on delivery," and `ops/methodology/the four kinds of structure.md` names the category error a levels-model invites — "a stack of what-contains-what mistaken for a sequence of what-happens-next." The demotion "is what keeps it harmless." Transcend-and-include was admitted as method and not as a stage model: "Wilber does not need to become the master framework"; the goal is "productive non-equivalence." The regression clause has a second wardrobe already: sorting material "into nested orders" is "the same failure as sorting them into triad corners," and a scheme "earns no exemption from the scaffolding decision" for arriving from a new direction (`ops/methodology/center of gravity.md`). And Wilber's own refusal is kept and aimed at exactly this: "his whole position is that the map is the contribution," and "recognition of what was never absent is, in his scheme, a *state* report, and his standing objection is that state reports without structural development are exactly what produces people who have had everything and changed nothing" ([[ken wilber]]). The vault's partial answer — the lost breakthrough was a state and recognition is a station — rests on a privated note and an unread al-Qushayrī.

So the empty cell is real, and it is also the most dangerous cell to fill. The author's holarchy phrase describes a level-transition that opens rather than closes; the vault's only instrument for that is a method applied to frames, not to persons. Whether Wilber's holarchy carries a structure of experience — recognition as a transition that opens a new mystery — or only a structure of systems is the reading question the whole import turns on (section 9).

**Read 2026-09-02.** The empty cell's reading ran (`archive/reading — wilber at the primary texts (ses, the integral spirituality draft, the aqal journal, waves, excerpt a).md`). Wilber's holarchy is a structure of systems by his own placement — the twenty tenets are "how the universe looks from the outside," and the include in "transcend and include" happens outside conscious awareness — with a felt transcend-and-include in two places outside the tenets (the prehensive moment-holarchy of Excerpt A; SES endnote 386 on mystical recognition, "it was there all along, just not seen"). "Each resolution opening into another mystery" has no source in him; his nearest construction runs to new problems, not mystery, so the phrase is the author's own noticing and the cell stays empty on the tradition's side, which is the finding rather than a failure. The cheaper route from inside the source tradition also ran: the ladder of speech's non-degradation clause is Abhinavagupta's, ontological rather than valuational, and Bhartṛhari's own ladder is a fall ([[a word at madhyama can be perfected forever a word at vaikhari can be answered]]).

## 7. The tensions the survey consolidates

Five regions returned fifty-odd tensions; deduplicated, eleven bear on the import and are recorded so the reading program does not walk into them blind. Each is already on record somewhere in the graph; this list only gathers them.

1. **The bloc against the compositional scheme.** The five theories are five complete rival accounts of a territory the book claims to compose; "The book has to either subsume them or beat them, and right now it does neither out loud" ([[the coaches are a bloc that competes with the compositional scheme]]). The survey sharpens the fork: the scheme the coaches attack is one the book has already demoted to scaffolding, so beating them with it is not available; subsuming them into the ecology — as loci, ways-in, instances of families the book already holds — is the move the ecology already runs. Whether "the body decides" is an answer or a deferral stays the open question. The survey also notes a filing question: by the vault's own rule a disagreement that would change what the book is earns `category: tension`, and this one is filed as a claim.
2. **Will against surrender**, the one live tension in the coaches' region. Its treatment — the body as referee — borrows Gendlin's instrument for a job it was not built for, supplies no procedure for the case where the body's reading is itself contested, and stands against a pole held by one unread name. Gendlin's self-engineering clause is "the first material in this vault that explains the fork instead of taking a side of it" ([[gendlin names four kinds of inward attention that are not focusing]]).
3. **A levels-model against no-sovereign-locus** (section 6) — the deepest, and complicated by the author's own resonance.
4. **Subtraction against un-clenching** (section 4): two privative grammars currently unified under one term.
5. **Sequence against simultaneous conditions.** The Stance is "not steps but facets," and the arc, the movements, the diagnostic, and the twelve forms are staged; the vault's own reconciliation is that the arc holds in sequence what a page cannot hold at once. Any imported model must be sorted on arrival.
6. **Compositional against subtractive**, inside the book's own stack: the minus "is the only operator that predicts anything," and the specificity discipline and the privation reading are two survivors with different standings ([[the diagnostic discipline survives the apparatus that carries it]]).
