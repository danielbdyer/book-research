# ecology — the counterfeits

This recipe is for an ecology survey, the vault's name for a study that sorts a collection of the book's material by what each item does; it is not the book's own Ecology of Recognition, the name the corpus gives its orientation.

## The collection

Every tradition the book honours names the thing that looks like its goal and is not. Zen has makyō and makyō after enlightenment. Gendlin has four kinds of inward attention that are not focusing. Ignatius has the angel of light. The Sufis have the state mistaken for a station and the lightning taken for the sun. Wilber has the pre/trans confusion, the stone Buddha, and satori as unity with a fragmented world. Landmark has the racket and its sibling the winning formula. The corpus has felt confirmation against the felt shift, beauty that recruits the wrong posture, and the declared self worn as costume. The vault touches the word counterfeit in forty notes and gathers the instances nowhere. The practice-model survey called this the family the book most lacks, since the book "walks readers toward deliberate destabilization with no way to tell dissolution from regression."

## The sorting axis

The author's reflection names one counterfeit of its own, and it is the one the survey should measure everything against: "not a cognitive interfacing with concept, nor the translation reflection of gestalt" — a reflection that only translates, against a recognition that is "a trueness or a clarity." His second sounding — a question only the author can answer, about his own experience or his intention for the book — refused "hiding" as too strong a word, so the counterfeit of un-seeing in his account is not concealment but a clench misread as a veil. The axis question for each counterfeit: what does it imitate, at what point in the arc does it appear, who catches it, and would his own two counterfeits catch it?

## Provisional families (proposals, not filings)

The families below are the groups this survey would sort its instances into, proposed rather than filed. By what is imitated: counterfeits of the event (makyō; the false peak; the engineered epiphany; felt confirmation); counterfeits of the attainment (the state taken for a station; the stone Buddha; satori with a fragmented world; the possession kept); counterfeits of the practice (self-engineering and the other three of Gendlin's four; management wearing the welcome's face, in Hudson's words; venting as expression); counterfeits of the self (the declared self as costume; the racket; the winning formula; the achievement-subject); counterfeits of the ground (the reflection that only translates; illumination that cannot recognize itself; the map mistaken for the territory). By who catches it: the person at the moment (almost none); the person later (Ignatius's trail; non-recurrence); another who has been there (dokusan; the confessor; the peer community); no one, which is the dangerous cell. The hypothesis to test is that every counterfeit of the event is caught only later or by another, while counterfeits of the practice can be caught by the person at the moment, which would tell the book which guards it can print.

## Empty cells

The vault has no counterfeit of the second person — the false companion, the director who persuades, the witness who wants the event — beyond the witness kept as proof. It has no counterfeit of integration, the phase the book says it addresses; the literature's spiritual bypass sits at survey depth and unread. It has no counterfeit of the letter: the reply that answers the form and not the word. And it has no account of the reader who, holding the book's vocabulary, has been handed a more fluent way to misdescribe their condition, which Engler names and which is the book's own risk.

## Tensions it would consolidate

This section names the tensions the survey would consolidate, each a disagreement inside the book's doctrine that stays open in a note rather than being resolved. And the stance's rule that convergence is company — thinkers who reached the same finding independently, held as company rather than as proof — against the survey's own temptation to count how many traditions name a counterfeit and call the count a finding.

## Candidate claims (springboards)

- Every tradition in the room names a counterfeit of its own goal, and the counterfeits converge more tightly than the goals do. Route: the instances above with their locators; the selection-artifact audit — the check that an agreement among gathered items was not produced by gathering them for the property they share — run first; a `claim` under `felt-shift` if it survives the audit.
- Counterfeits of the event are caught later or by another; counterfeits of the practice can be caught at the moment. Route: Gendlin's four, Hudson's management guard, Ignatius §§333–336, dokusan, Knysh pp. 59 and 78; a `claim` under `felt-shift`.
- The book's vocabulary is itself a counterfeit risk, since a reader in regression can misdescribe their condition in its terms. Route: Engler 1984 p. 39; the register notes; a `tension` under `tensions`, since it bears on what the book is.
- The reflection that only translates is the author's own counterfeit of recognition and the book has no guard for it. Route: `reflections/on recognition (2026-08-24).md` and the hollow-interaction note; a `claim` under `recognition`, stated so that the reflection grounds no doctrinal inference.
