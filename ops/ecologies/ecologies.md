# The ecology surveys — index

An ecology survey takes one collection of things the book uses without having sorted them, finds every instance across the graph, sorts them into structural families by how they work rather than by which tradition supplied them, uses the author's own record as the axis every family is measured against, names the empty cells, gathers the tensions the sort exposes, and turns the cells into readings that can fail. The worked example is the survey of practice-models, `ops/practice-model ecology.md`, with its discussed next steps at `ops/practice-model ecology — candidate next steps.md`; its thirteen readings ran on 2026-09-02 and the records are in `archive/`. This index names the nine further collections that could take the same treatment, in the order recommended, with one recipe file each.

## The recipe, in brief

A recipe file states, in this order: the collection in plain terms; what the vault already holds and where; the sorting axis drawn from the author's record; provisional families, which are proposals rather than filings; the empty cells; the tensions the survey would consolidate; candidate claims, each with a substantiation route, which go to `ops/queue/claim candidates.md` when the survey adopts them; a reading program in the sharp-questions form of `ops/methodology/sharp questions.md`; the safeguards every survey runs under; and what counts as done. Four safeguards apply to all nine. Every survey is a gathering operation, so its families are found by searching for them and the selection-artifact audit runs before any convergence among them is stated ([[the unanimity claim is an artifact of how the lineages were selected]]). Every instance is asked on arrival which of the four kinds of structure it is — operator, method, dramaturgy, or ontology (`ops/methodology/the four kinds of structure.md`, `ops/methodology/the category test on arrival.md`). The writing test governs what the survey files: a family earns a note only if a drafting session would reach for it (`ops/methodology/writing test.md`). And the stance holds throughout: the sort allocates reading and never ranks evidence, and a refusal is kept so its holder survives being cited (`ops/methodology/authorial stance.md`).

## The nine, in recommended order

1. `ops/ecologies/the second person.md` — the roles a companion plays across the traditions, and which of them a text or a world can carry. **Run 2026-09-03**: the survey is `ops/second person ecology.md`. First, because the relational-field surface is next in line and this round's readings supplied half its primary data.
2. `ops/ecologies/the certificates.md` — how each tradition knows something happened. Second, because the sounding on whether the felt shift is the author's standard cannot be put cleanly without it.
3. `ops/ecologies/the counterfeits.md` — each tradition's near-miss of its own goal, and who catches it. Runs well as one survey with the certificates.
4. `ops/ecologies/the refusals.md` — what the company refuses the book, sorted by what is refused, and which refusals the book owes an answer.
5. `ops/ecologies/the diagnostics.md` — the question each model puts to a difficulty, and what each presupposes about where trouble lives.
6. `ops/ecologies/the figures.md` — what each of the book's figures does to a reader, and whether it carries a mechanism or a mood.
7. `ops/ecologies/the arcs.md` — every sequence the book and its material hold, sorted by phase grammar. Elevated by the author's interest on 2026-09-02 and **run the same day**: the survey is `ops/arc ecology.md`, fourteen families, twenty tensions, seventeen readings, seven soundings.
8. `ops/ecologies/the devices.md` — the book's own apparatus, sorted by what each device does to the reader's position.
9. `ops/ecologies/the addressees.md` — who each document speaks to and what it asks of them.

## What is not here

The loci, the axioms, the paradoxes, the arc as the reader undergoes it, the making, and the concerns are already lens families (`the-lenses.md`) and are read there. The six relations of descent are defined at [[the apparatus descends from the settlement the book positions itself against]]. The practice-models are surveyed. A collection graduates from this index to a survey document when a session runs the recipe; it graduates from a survey to the graph one claim at a time, through the pipeline.
