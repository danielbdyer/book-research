# ecology — the arcs

This recipe is for an ecology survey, the vault's name for a study that sorts a collection of the book's material by what each item does; it is not the book's own Ecology of Recognition, the name the corpus gives its orientation.

## The collection

The book walks its reader through the multi-step arc of the psychedelic experience with no substance involved — preparation, onset, deepening, peak, descent, integration — and states one law about itself: the pacing enacts the arc the chapters explain ([[the book performs what it describes]], [[psychedelic names a movement not a substance]]). Around that arc the book and its material hold many more sequences: the four movements of both books, the three loops' misfire and medicine, the ripening states, the ladder of speech, Ignatius's Weeks, the Sufi path with its ordered stations, the apophatic ascent, Gendlin's six movements, the plasticity window's days and the afterglow's decay curve, Moreno's warm-up, the Forum's four days, Robbins's six steps, Wilber's stages, the U-curve, Kolb's cycle, the rite of separation and return. The practice-model survey held these in two rows, staged arc and loop. This survey sorts every sequence by its phase grammar: what marks onset, how the apex is handled, whether there is a return and whether the return completes, what marks the exit, and what governs the transitions.

## The sorting axis

The author's record is the axis, and it is unusual here because his event is not an arc. The reflection reports a recognition "always already there, but just now opened-with," with no onset marked, no peak, and no descent; the practice-model survey read it as "one half-turn, not a cycle" and "no station on any of" the arcs. His phrase "in a state of resolution opening up into another mystery" is the nearest thing to a sequence in it, and the Wilber reading found it has no source in Wilber. Poem #5 ends "the youngness is just beginning." Poem #7 holds "complete surrender / and trying one's very hardest" in one breath rather than in phases. Poem #16 is the sending, "the trembling that is the sound of the sentence leaving / the architecture." The making itself ran an arc — the figures first, the system later, the welding last — and the record shows him saying the plain thing first and the scholarship arriving afterward. The axis question for every arc: does it have a place for an event that arrives without phases; does its return complete; would his record locate itself anywhere on it?

## Provisional families (proposals, to be tested by the survey)

The families below are the groups this survey would sort its instances into, proposed rather than filed. By phase grammar: ascent ladders (the apophatic way, the stations, Bhartṛhari's climb, the ladder of knowing); descent-and-return where the return completes (fanā and baqā's second sobriety, the psychedelic arc's integration, the four movements' return that never graduates); cycles that do not graduate (the three loops, the pulse, the rhythm of inquiry, Kolb, the recognition loop's return to a new encounter); threshold rites (separation, liminality, incorporation; the gatekeeper; the cusp); gated stage models (Wilber's structures, Engler's sequence, the maqāmāt gated on one another); windows and decay curves (the plasticity window, the afterglow, the persistence predictors); conditioning loops (Robbins's six steps, Deida's weekly edge, Hudson's two or three times a day); staged inquiries with a felt sign at each step (Gendlin's six movements, Hudson's inquiry, the Forum's four days); the single half-turn or instant (the reflection, Buber's I–Thou, the letter at four in the morning); and the refusal of any arc (Dōgen's practice-realization, Nāgārjuna's no ground to gain, the Stance's facets). The hypothesis to test is that the book's own arc belongs to the second family while its founding event belongs to the ninth, and that the peak withdrawn is how the book already knows this.

## Empty cells

Before the survey runs, four cells are visible. The vault has no exit criterion: the practice forms specify when to begin and not how to tell one is finished, and no arc in the graph says how it ends without a peak. It has no account of an arc that carries an irreversible event, since every staged arc is walked again and the book promises what is recognized cannot be un-recognized. It has no theory of what governs a transition — clock time, a felt sign, ripeness, a second person's judgment, conduct — as a family of its own, though every arc in the collection answers it differently. And it has no arc for the finite: none of the sequences ends in death, and the one tradition that builds its sequence toward it is the one the book left behind.

## Tensions it would consolidate

This section names the tensions the survey would consolidate, each a disagreement inside the book's doctrine that stays open in a note rather than being resolved. Sequence against simultaneous conditions, which the practice-model survey named and the vault reconciles only by saying the arc holds in sequence what a page cannot hold at once. The peak concentrated against the peak distributed and the peak withdrawn, which the drafts disagree about. The return as completion against the ascent as completion, which the Sufi and the apophatic arcs disagree about. The window that reorganizes only what attention is on against the structure that interprets what it did not change against the lines never developed, three accounts of one failed arc. Irreversibility against every arc walked twice. And the author's event, which has no phases, against a book whose law is that its pacing enacts phases.

## Candidate claims (springboards)

- The book's arc is a descent-and-return whose return completes, and its founding event is an instant with no phases, and the peak withdrawn is where the two meet. Route: the peak note, the reflection, the Sufi arc, the four movements; a `claim` under `book-structure`, stated so the reflection grounds no doctrinal inference.
- Every arc in the room governs its transitions differently, and the book has not said what governs its own. Route: the survey's instances by transition rule; a `claim` under `book-structure` or a `tension`.
- No arc in the graph has an exit that is not a peak, a return, or a second person's say-so. Route: the practice forms note, Gendlin's step, Ignatius §336, dokusan; a `claim` under `felt-shift`.
- An irreversible event cannot be the terminus of an arc that is walked again, so the book's promise sits outside its own dramaturgy. Route: the irreversibility notes, the four movements' return, the loops; a `tension`.

## Safeguards

The four in the index, and one from the structure directive: an arc is a dramaturgy, not an ontology, and a survey of arcs must not become a stack of what-contains-what mistaken for a sequence of what-happens-next (`ops/methodology/the four kinds of structure.md`).
