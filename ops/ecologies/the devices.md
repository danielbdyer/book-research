# ecology — the devices

This recipe is for an ecology survey, the vault's name for a study that sorts a collection of the book's material by what each item does; it is not the book's own Ecology of Recognition, the name the corpus gives its orientation.

## The collection

The book is built to perform what it describes, and it performs with devices: the margins, the thresholds where the reader stops and does something, the fourth wall that falls, the chapter patrons and their epigraphs, the Second Chair, the practice forms, the front matter's three sentences, the last line written so that it can be answered, the reading contract, the reach-and-release rhythm, the naming of the move just made, the reader kept located in a walkable space. The treatment designed seven apparatus devices and the executed draft carries one ([[the drafts execute one of the seven designed apparatus devices]]). The vault holds the devices in craft notes, in the apparatus section of the scaffold — `ops/scaffold.md`, the book's structure as read out of the graph — and in the book-structure map, one of the vault's topic maps, each gathering the claims of one concern, and has never sorted them by what each does to the reader's position: whether it performs on the reader, withdraws from the reader, asks the reader, or certifies the reader.

## The sorting axis

The author's own devices, on the record: the letter, which is the one genre whose every sentence expects an answer; the last line written to be answered; the holding of a container while the writing became more fully itself inside it; and his facilitation craft, "tracking the room while resting in presence," which the treatment notes is the Stance again. The axis for every device: does it do something to the reader, withdraw so the reader does something, ask the reader for something, or tell the reader whether they did it; and would his facilitation craft recognize it as one of its own moves?

## Provisional families (proposals, not filings)

The families below are the groups this survey would sort its instances into, proposed rather than filed. By what the device does to the reader's position: devices that perform on the reader (the register; the pacing; the front matter's promise; the fourth wall falling); devices that withdraw (the peak withdrawn; double reflection; evocation-then-negation; the margins that leave room; naming the move just made, which hands the mechanism over); devices that ask (the thresholds; the practice forms; the last line; the reading contract); devices that certify or refuse to (the felt check; the book's admission that it cannot verify; the Second Chair as designed). By provenance: the author's (the letter, the last line, the container); the treatment's (the seven apparatus devices; the patrons); the lineage's (Kierkegaard's indirect communication, Iser's negation, Ignatius's thresholds, Kapleau's transcript). The hypothesis to test is that the executed draft kept the performing devices and dropped the asking ones, which would explain why the drafts execute one of seven and why the second chair is empty.

## Tensions it would consolidate

This section names the tensions the survey would consolidate, each a disagreement inside the book's doctrine that stays open in a note rather than being resolved. Refusability against hospitality. The performing law against the withdrawing devices, since a book that performs what it describes must at the peak perform withdrawal. Indirect communication's isolating purpose against the book's participatory one. The apparatus designed against the apparatus executed. And the register that recruits against the register that opens.
