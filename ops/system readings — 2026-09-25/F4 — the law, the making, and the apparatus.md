The reader used the design's earlier names, and the design lists each earlier name beside its plain one. The reader's original wording is in the repository's history at commit `418f352`. Where the reader cited a line number in the design document, this version names the part instead, because the design has since been rewritten.

**Words used.** A *directive* is one of the vault's standing rules in `ops/methodology/`, each recorded after a decision of the author's; the folder holds 30 files, 29 directives and the index `methodology.md`. The *decisions register* is `ops/decisions.md`, the table of settled questions: one dated row for each question the author has closed, with a pointer to the reasoning. The *provenance files* in `provenance/` are reconstructions of how the corpus was made, built from the record of the author's conversations with an AI collaborator; the vault treats them as works consulted, not as sources. They mark whose words each sentence is: [D] the author's, [C] the AI collaborator's, and [A] another named person's, such as the writer of a letter as the author transcribed it. The *transmission digs* in `provenance/transmission digs/` are research commissioned on 2026-08-13 into how a book can pass this kind of material to a reader. A source's *standing* says what kind of document it is: *descriptive* (a description of the book from outside it), a *draft* (a state of the manuscript), or *primary* (the poems, which come before the book). A note's `state:` is `nascent` when the claim is stated but not yet supported by a reading of its source, `privated` when it is complete except for one named missing piece, recorded in its `privation:` field, `full` when it is supported, and `composting` when it has been superseded but is still drawn on. A *tension* is a note recording a disagreement inside the book's doctrine, kept open rather than resolved. A *placement* is the author's assignment of material to a chapter, recorded in a note's `supports:` field. A *reading record* in `archive/` states what was read of a thinker's own text and how it is cited. A *thinker page* is the vault's page for a thinker (the vault calls it a chair); its `seat:` field records the role a thinker plays for the book, its `load:` field records what a thinker carries for the book's argument, and its `discipline:` field records the field the thinker's methods come from. *Company* means thinkers who reached the same finding independently; the book cites them as others who noticed the same thing, not as evidence that it is true. A *convergence note* (the vault's word is isomorph) records that two or more traditions or thinkers reached the same finding. A *topic map* is a page gathering the claims of one concern. A *family file* is one of the eight files at the repository root that each read the whole collection of notes by one organizing idea; a *cache marker* is a hidden comment at the head of a section of a family file, with a checksum of the files the section was read from, so that a script can report the section as out of date when those files change. A *drafting document* is a draft in `drafts/` that assembles the material on one concern and stops where the author's own contribution begins (the vault calls it a concern surface). `ops/clearing.md`, which the vault calls the clearing, is the register of what the book's center cannot see. `ops/decision sitting — 2026-09-03.md`, which the vault calls the decision sitting, is the document gathering every open question only the author can answer; it calls such a question a *sounding*. The *chapter comparison table* is `ops/chapter concordance.md`, which compares each named part of the book across the drafts. A *hook* is a script that runs automatically at a set point in a session, such as its start or the end of a reply. The *lexicon* is `ops/plain-language lexicon.md`, the list of the vault's own coined terms with a plain meaning for each. The *pipeline* is the vault's fixed sequence of steps for new material (record, reduce, reflect, reweave, verify, and rethink). The *writing test* is the vault's admission standard for a note: would a drafting session reach for this? The *reconciliation register* (`reframe-reconciliation-register-2026-08-13.md`) lists twenty-five choices the reframe conversation left open; it calls them forks, and the design calls them open choices. The *reframe conversation* is the author's conversation with a language model on the evening of 2026-08-12; its transcript is `reframe-conversation-full-2026-08-13.md`.

### 1b. The four things recorded for every kind of material, and the design's two lists

**The author's level.** The design has one list of six levels for every piece of material (proposed, experienced, inferred, believed, claimed, and written), and only the author may set a level above "proposed." The vault has no such field. It records the same steps in at least eight separate places, each recording one of them. The most important finding is that the vault deliberately keeps apart three things that a single list of levels would combine:

1. *The standing of a document* (`source standing.md`): descriptive, draft, or primary, together with works consulted, reading records, research records, encounter records, and `reflections/`. This sorts documents, not what they say: "A primary source asserts nothing about the book; the datum is the book's use of it" (`source standing.md`).
2. *How well a note is supported*: the `state:` field on claims (nascent, privated, full, flourishing, and composting). By the author's limit of 2026-08-05, this is explicitly not the author's level: "`state:` **decouples.** It records how substantiated a note is: a maturity marker for the vault's own material, informing which pass to run next and nothing the book asserts" (`vocabulary policy.md`). Its values describe conditions, not ranks: "'nascent' is a seed — the proposition stated, not yet substantiated, waiting on writing. 'privated' is a complete claim incomplete in a specific named way, usually waiting on a reading rather than on writing; it REQUIRES a privation field naming the absence, which is what stops the value being usable as a bare rank" (`templates/claim-note.md`). The vault's one operational tension concerns exactly this field, and it records that "Nothing in the schema, the templates, the queries or the hooks resists a session — or a person — reading the five as a ladder" (`ops/tensions/the metabolic arc is a developmental scheme with no anti-ladder feature.md`). An open observation adds that the vault "inverted composting," since in the corpus composting is a return to potential that can come back to life, while in the vault it marks a note that was superseded; and that "privated is a lateral condition rather than a rung" (`ops/observations/the vault inverted composting when it borrowed the five ripening states.md`).
3. *The author's own level for the material*, recorded for each act of his rather than for each note:
   - a thinker's `seat:` changes from `candidate` only by his decision (30 of the 64 are still candidates);
   - a tension's `status:` becomes `resolved` only by his act (1 of 13);
   - `supports:` records only his placements (0);
   - a row of the decisions register records a question he closed (119 rows, each with the date and where the reasoning is kept);
   - a citation of the form "author statement, <date>" in a `sources:` field marks his own words (35 notes);
   - his three levels of confidence of 2026-08-04 grade the whole book by "how sure he is of each part";
   - `reflections/` has `standing: primary — spoken from the heart`;
   - the encounter record keeps the reframe conversation "under split provenance: the author's turns are primary material and the model's cartographies are works consulted."

The design's level "proposed" has two counterparts in the vault. One is the shelf, the lists of material kept outside the notes: "a decorative resonance, a borrowed analogy, or an external critique is shelf material, not a graph node, until the writing or a reading reaches for it" (`decoration and critique are shelf material.md`). The other is the marks of evidence on thinker pages: [P], read in the thinker's own text; [S], read in secondary sources; and [e], evaluated from works consulted (`ops/plain-language lexicon.md`). By the reader's search, only 2 thinker pages carry [P] in their text, and 4 carry [S].

So the vault matches the design at the lowest and the highest levels, and not in between. It records "proposed" (the shelf, a thinker page marked candidate, a nascent note drawn from a work consulted) and "claimed" or "written" (a row of the decisions register, a sent letter, a draft), and it records "believed" only as dated quotations. It has no place to write "inferred" or "believed" as the level of a piece of material, because its directives forbid a session to set one: "a load assignment is a proposal until the author makes it" (`thesis-bearing and company.md`); "Resolving is the author's act: a session records that decision and never originates it" (`CLAUDE.md`). The design's list has one thing the vault lacks: a single field, readable on any piece of material. The vault has two things the list lacks: the separation of a document's standing from the level of what it says, and the rule that no session may raise either.

**Scale.** No field records it. Scale is stated once, for the two books as a whole: the division by setting of practice (with another person, or with oneself), and the three scales, "the self at the micro scale, the relational at the meso scale, and the civilizational at the macro scale" (`the two-book architecture.md`). The `seat:` values on thinker pages (`facilitation`, `emotion-work`, `container-makers`, and others) look like scales of practice, but they name jobs, not scales.

**Quadrant.** The vault has no quadrant field, by a recorded decision. It treats Wilber's quadrants as "a coordinate system, not an architecture" (the note `wilber's four quadrants are a coordinate system, not an architecture`), and the decisions row of 2026-08-13 states that "the four ontological roots, Wilber's four quadrants, and the plotted-lineage stack are registered lenses (research-frontier material), not re-seatings" (`ops/decisions.md`, the row "Which taxonomy governs the interlocutor roster after the reframe?"). ### 1c. Connections

The design states its connections in words, and the vault's rule is the same ("A relation is always stated in words in the link gloss; notation never carries an assertion alone," `CLAUDE.md`), so the two already agree in form. The connections this region records, and how it records them:

## 2. Material the design cannot record

### 2.A How the book was made, as a series of dated stages, and the poems beneath it

*What it is.* The provenance files (`provenance/`: five reconstructions, one analysis of lineage, two summaries on particular subjects, the author's own instructions for writing in his voice, and the seven transmission digs; nine text versions of the PDFs, and nine files of digs) and about ten structure and claim notes built from them, gathered in `the-making.md` (72 links) and in the methods map's section "What the record of the making adds to the rules." The record settles the direction of influence: "the author said the plain thing first, in his own words, about his own life … and the scholarship arrived afterward and dressed it" (`CLAUDE.md`). It is dated in stages:
- the poems (a lifetime of them, fifteen written before the book);
- the images of the first sketch, the seed (9 April 2025);
- the triad, worked out in another project (8 December 2025);
- the author's first formal contact with the tradition the triad is named for (19–22 March 2026);
- the joining of the images to the system (July 2026);
- the corpus, compiled in one month;
- the reframe conversation (12–13 August 2026).

The note on the book's origin story has a representative table, with rows such as "The participation law, lived in a room | 2017 | Assembled into the lineages' roll-call, 7 July 2026" (`notes/the book's honest origin story…`). Beneath the record are the poems: 13 notes cite a poem by number, and 25 mention them. The archive record on the poems holds a partial list of the concerns each poem contains, as starting points for extraction. The poems' standing has what `source standing.md` calls "three faces," set by the author's words of 2026-08-08: "I don't want to overcorrect on this. What I do align with is that the poems are important to the book. And, they are not in the same class of contribution as the research itself. They are a part of the idiosyncrasies of the book for me, as a part of my offering, for now."

*Why the design cannot record it.* Four of the design's kinds of material each record a part of it, and none records the whole.
- An encounter is, in the design's words, "a dated event from the author's life in which something happened, with his note on it." The record holds dated events, but the account of each is the collaborator's reconstruction, marked [C], while the author's own words are marked [D]; and the vault's rule is to keep that division: "Interpretations remain attributed (the reconstruction's reading is cited as its reading)" (`personal provenance.md`).
- A tradition or thinker is a developed way of seeing, and the author's own earlier work is not a standpoint he came to from outside.
- An author's view is what still holds after comparison. The poems came before every comparison, and, by the author's decision, they are not used as evidence for doctrine.
- An image needs a picture, and the poems contain none ("what the poems hold is the layer beneath the figures").

And the design's source field cannot say that a source comes *before* the book (primary material) rather than describing it, or who wrote which sentence.

*What it does for the books.* It serves function 6, "Offer the author's own view as an example," and function 1, "Start from what the reader already has": the origin story "doubles as the book's invitation … what happened to the author reading the traditions is what the book proposes to be for the reader" (`notes/the book's honest origin story…`). It also serves the commitment "The author is the first participant," whose question, "Did this pass through the author's own encounter?", can be answered only against this record.

*How the design would be extended.* The source gains two new fields, and one definition is widened. The source gains a *stage* (which stage of the making a quotation comes from: the poems, the seed, the ontology project, the retreat, the corpus, the reframe conversation, a reading, or a draft) and a *writer* (the author [D], the collaborator [C], another named person [A], or a session). ### 2.B The standing of sources and their independence: the source field as a record of what kind of document a source is

*What it is.* `source standing.md` (three standings, and works consulted); the form of citation for readings (`Gendlin 1964, fn. 13`); the encounter record's separation of the author's words from the model's; the rule that sources compiled from one another count once (the atlas and the walked document were both compiled from the arc-and-gloss treatment); the six kinds of descent; the test for a shared teacher ("the convergence's independence fails where three of its names share one teacher"); the finding on Daoism (Heidegger's "letting" is a lineage, not a convergence); and the rule that the account written nearer an event is the one closer to what happened. Counts: 8 source records, 47 reading records, 3 research records, and 1 encounter record; 176 notes cite the corpus, 190 cite works from outside it, and 109 cite the provenance works.

*Why the design cannot record it.* The design's source field "gives every quotation an address," and it says nothing about what kind of document the address points to. The vault has measured what an address alone allows:
- thirteen notes asserting correspondences, "quoting the corpus and paraphrasing the thinker," which looked fully supported because "the page is full of anchored quotations from one side" (`both halves of a correspondence.md`);
- three documents that agree because they are one document read three times;
- a reason that "moved from three practical problems to a philosophical principle to a line of the book's doctrine" as it was retold (`notes/a recorded reason drifts…`).

And the design's field "whether it reached its finding independently of the others or learned it from them" belongs to a *pair* of traditions or thinkers, not to one.

*What it does for the books.* It serves function 5, "Bring in others who noticed the same thing, without merging them," and the commitment "A difference is understood before a likeness is claimed."

*How the design would be extended.* The source field becomes a small record with three parts: the address; the source's standing (descriptive, draft, primary, consulted, read in its own text, or encountered); and, where two sources agree, how they are related (an independent arrival; compiled from the other; sharing a teacher; descended from the other; a restatement; a debt the corpus's own survey names; credit given to the wrong person; or a genealogy traced along one branch only). A comparison then names that relation before it states what the author still holds.

*An example in the extended form.*
- Source. *Addresses:* atlas §0, the preamble; `walked`, the colophon; `arc-gloss`, "how to hold this."
- *Standing:* descriptive (the atlas and the walked document), and a draft (the arc-and-gloss treatment).
- *Relation:* compiled from one another. "Where the atlas, the walked document, and the Arc and the Gloss agree, the vault is reading one document three times: once as treatment, and twice as compilation of that treatment."
- *What follows for a comparison:* "A claim citing all three anchors is not thereby better supported than a claim citing one" (`notes/the atlas was compiled from the arc and gloss treatment.md`).

### 2.E The vault's proposal for its own ending, the step missing from "The structure exists for the sake of encounters"

*What it is.* It is `ops/the vault's exit.md` (2026-09-03, "A proposal, not a decision"). - five signals, "none of which a session may act on alone";
- three questions, carried into the decision sitting;
- a section "What never ends": the record of sources, the thinkers' refusals, the standing of the poems, and the decisions register.

Its central sentence is this: "What decreases is the making of new structure; what stays is the presence of what was made and the willingness to be entered."

*Why the design cannot record it.* The design states the principle twice, "The system should remain smaller than the inquiry" (transcript line 13344) and "If maintaining the model becomes more interesting than participating in the ecology, the model has failed" (9447), and gives it a question ("Is maintaining this more interesting than using it?"). The design's account of how the work proceeds says "Any step may be skipped" and never says when the structure is to be withdrawn. The vault's proposal supplies the missing step, and it is derived from the books' own doctrine: Winnicott's "natural decrease"; the withdrawal that is completed by a return; and the last position left empty for the person addressed (the book leaves its last word to the reader, and the vault leaves the manuscript to the author).

*How the design would be extended.* The design's account of how the work proceeds gains a step for ending, with the vault's three questions as items on the list of open questions, addressed to the author; and the four tests gain a fifth: does the structure have a plan for its own ending? ### Corrections to the design

1. **`state:` is not the author's level, and the vault says so by decision.** The design's Part 11 asked how the author's level relates to "the fields the vault already uses to record how developed a piece of material is and how certain it is." In the vault these are two different things, on purpose. `state:` records how well a note is supported, for the use of the vault's own steps, and "nothing the book asserts" (`vocabulary policy.md`, 2026-08-05); how firmly a thing is held is recorded for each of the author's acts (1b). A design that treated "nascent" as "proposed" and "full" as "believed" would reverse a row of the decisions register. It would also let a reading, which moves a note from nascent to full, raise the author's level, which the design's own rule forbids ("no AI raises the author's level"). The correct correspondence is this: every note stands at "proposed" until an act of the author's touches it, and `state:` is independent of the author's level. (The design now states this correction.)

2. **A quadrant field on every piece of material contradicts two confirmed choices.** The vault records the quadrants as one way into the material ("registered lenses (research-frontier material), not re-seatings," 2026-08-13; and open choice 25 of the reconciliation register, on Wilber), and the category test says that a coordinate system "answers where inquiry is looking, not what ultimately exists." A field on every piece of material would make the coordinate system a required part of the structure. The design should treat the quadrant as an optional way of reading, not as a field on every piece of material.

3. **Writing technique, as a kind of material, conflicts with the decision of 2026-08-09.** The author decided, "Cut the craft-about-drafts notes too," and the register gives the reason: "a research corpus studies the ideas rather than the manuscript's sentence technique." The design's reason for the kind, that "the facilitator who reads Book I learns from how the book itself is written," is a reason to reopen that row. Reopening a row "is a /rethink proposal with the author's decision, never a session's own act" (`ops/decisions.md`, its header; `/rethink` is the vault's command for reviewing its own configuration). The design should present writing technique as a proposal that would reverse a decision, with both reasons.

4. **The design's unit differs from the vault's.** The design takes the pair as "the basic unit of the books' ideas." The vault settled its unit on 2026-08-13 as "Both, allocated": the sentence stating one proposition, for its notes, and the named concept, for any list of concepts kept later in Tana. Pairs exist in the vault, but inside propositions. The design should define a pair as a way of reading propositions (a search that finds the two terms) rather than as a note that replaces them, or it will have to re-file 500 notes.

5. **A source recorded as an address alone is not enough** (2.B). The vault has measured three failures that an address alone allows.

6. **Independence is a relation between two traditions or thinkers, not a field on one.** The design's field "whether it reached its finding independently of the others or learned it from them" should be a connection between two traditions or thinkers, with the six kinds of descent the vault uses. It should also carry the check on how the traditions were selected, since in the vault's words "the lineages were gathered on one day for the property they are then reported to share" (`CLAUDE.md`).

7. **A comparison needs a mark saying who made it.** The vault's rule that a reading "never asserts that the correspondence holds" means that a comparison a session proposed and a comparison the author made are different things. The author's level can record the difference, but the fields of a comparison should also say that the author's note is the one field a session may not write.

8. **The design's nine commitments leave out three principles the author stated or agreed with.** `the-axioms.md` marks "Let constraint reveal what is essential" and "Collaborate, but keep the authorial center awake — sovereignty in relationship" as "*author-voiced core*," the author's own words, and it marks "The author's experience is an offering, not an authority" as the model's words, which the author agreed with. None of the three is among the nine. The design's Part 10 lists the principle about constraint in its count (transcript lines 3808 and 1074), but leaves it out of the list of commitments. In the other direction, the design's questions are what the vault's list lacks, so the two lists should be combined rather than one chosen over the other.

9. **The topic table can be generated only in part** (Part 11, question 6). The topic maps are arranged by hand, by decision ("A generator would flatten that curation and swap the glosses for descriptions," the row of 2026-08-09). The columns Question, Pair, Company, and Encounter could be computed from the `topics:`, `category:`, and `sources:` fields and the kinds of source. The columns Comparison, Author's view, and What it hides cannot be, because the evidence for them is the author's acts, recorded in prose.

## 4. The three findings that matter most

**1. The vault already records the author's level, but as a set of his acts, never as a field, and its `state:` field is the thing the design must not mistake for it.** The design's Part 11 asked how the author's level relates to the vault's record of "how developed a piece of material is and how certain it is." The vault keeps those two apart by decision. `state:` "records how substantiated a note is … and nothing the book asserts" (`vocabulary policy.md`), and a `privated` note must name what it lacks, "which is what stops the value being usable as a bare rank" (`templates/claim-note.md`). How firmly something is held is recorded only where the author acted: 30 of the 64 thinkers are still candidates, 1 of the 13 tensions is resolved, no placements have been made, the decisions register has 119 rows, 35 notes cite his dated words, and the words of one reflection are "inviolable." The measure of grounding reports 157 of 502 claims nascent, and the family file on principles 78% nascent, and none of those numbers says anything about what the author believes. The design is right that only the author raises the level; it would be wrong to read "nascent" as "proposed" and "full" as "believed." The extension is one field for the author's level, set only by an act of the author's and kept beside `state:` rather than replacing it; and the vault's directives already supply the rule that no session writes it.

**2. The vault records how the book was made in more detail than any kind of material in the design can hold, and the poems show this most clearly.** Seven provenance reconstructions, one analysis of lineage, the author's own instructions for writing in his voice, and about twenty notes record the book's origin as dated stages, with the writer of each sentence marked ([D] the author, [C] the collaborator, and [A] the friend who wrote the letter). They settle the direction of influence: "the author said the plain thing first, in his own words, about his own life … and the scholarship arrived afterward and dressed it" (`CLAUDE.md`). The design's encounter needs the author's note, and the record's notes are reconstructions, attributed as such. The design's author's view needs what still holds after comparison; the poems came before any comparison, and by the author's word they are "a part of my offering, for now," not evidence. The design's image needs a picture, and "the figures … appear nowhere in the collection" (`archive/source — the poems.md`).
