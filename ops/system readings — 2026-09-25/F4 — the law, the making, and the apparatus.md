### 1b. Shared fields and registers

**Standing.** The design has one six-rung ladder on every unit (surfaced, encountered, inferred, believed, claimed, written), settable above "surfaced" only by the author. The vault has no such field. What it has is the same ladder distributed across at least eight mechanisms, each recording one step of it, and — this is the finding that matters — it deliberately keeps three axes apart that the design folds into one:

1. *The document's standing* (`source standing.md`): descriptive, draft, primary, plus consulted works, reading records, research records, encounter records, and `reflections/`. This is a ladder of documents, not of content. "A primary source asserts nothing about the book; the datum is the book's use of it" (`source standing.md`).
2. *The note's substantiation* (`state:` on claims): nascent, privated, full, flourishing, composting. This is explicitly not the author's standing: "`state:` **decouples.** It records how substantiated a note is: a maturity marker for the vault's own material, informing which pass to run next and nothing the book asserts." (`vocabulary policy.md`, the 2026-08-05 bound). Its rungs are conditions, not ranks: "'nascent' is a seed — the proposition stated, not yet substantiated, waiting on writing. 'privated' is a complete claim incomplete in a specific named way, usually waiting on a reading rather than on writing; it REQUIRES a privation field naming the absence, which is what stops the value being usable as a bare rank." (`templates/claim-note.md`). The one operational tension the vault holds is about exactly this field, and it records that "Nothing in the schema, the templates, the queries or the hooks resists a session — or a person — reading the five as a ladder." (`ops/tensions/the metabolic arc is a developmental scheme with no anti-ladder feature.md`). An open observation adds that the vault "inverted composting" and that "privated is a lateral condition rather than a rung" (`ops/observations/the vault inverted composting when it borrowed the five ripening states.md`).
3. *The author's standing on the content*, recorded per act rather than per note: a thinker's `seat:` moves from `candidate` only by his decision (30 of 64 are still candidates); a tension's `status:` moves to `resolved` only by his act (1 of 13); `supports:` records only his placements (0); a decisions row records a question he closed (119 rows, each with the date and where the reasoning lives); an "author statement, <date>" citation in a `sources:` array marks his own words (35 notes); the three confidence tiers of 2026-08-04 grade the whole book by "how sure he is of each part"; `reflections/` carries `standing: primary — spoken from the heart`; and the encounter record holds the reframe "under split provenance: the author's turns are primary material and the model's cartographies are works consulted." The design's "surfaced" rung has a native counterpart in the shelf: "a decorative resonance, a borrowed analogy, or an external critique is shelf material, not a graph node, until the writing or a reading reaches for it" (`decoration and critique are shelf material.md`), and in the interlocutor evidence marks [P] read at the text / [S] secondary / [e] evaluated from consulted works (`ops/plain-language lexicon.md`; only 2 and 4 chairs carry [P] and [S] textually as of my grep).

So the correspondence is exact at the top and bottom of the ladder and absent in the middle: the vault records *surfaced* (shelf, candidate, nascent-from-a-consulted-work) and *claimed/written* (a decisions row, a sent letter, a draft), and it records *believed* only as dated quotations. It has no place to write "inferred" or "believed" as a status on a piece of content, because its directives forbid a session from assigning it: "a load assignment is a proposal until the author makes it" (`thesis-bearing and company.md`), "Resolving is the author's act: a session records that decision and never originates it" (`CLAUDE.md`). What the design's ladder has that the vault lacks is one field, readable on any unit. What the vault has that the ladder lacks is the separation of the document's standing from the content's, and the rule that no session may raise either.

**Scale.** No field. The scale is carried once, at the level of the two books: the locus cut (with another / with oneself) and the three-scale ecology "the self at the micro scale, the relational at the meso scale, and the civilizational at the macro scale" (`the two-book architecture.md`). The interlocutor `seat:` values (`facilitation`, `emotion-work`, `container-makers`…) are scales of practice in disguise, but they are jobs, not scales.

The nearest thing to the design's four quadrant questions is the family `the-loci.md` (F2), which reads the graph by standpoint.

### 1c. Relations

The design's relations are stated in words; the vault's rule is the same ("A relation is always stated in words in the link gloss; notation never carries an assertion alone." — `CLAUDE.md`), so the form matches natively. Which relations this region expresses, and how:

- *A Locus illuminates a Concern, disclosing and obscuring* — expressed as the interlocutor note's body ("what this thinker holds in their own terms, where the author sees a crossover, what this thinker would refuse" — `templates/interlocutor.md`), and as the `clause:` field. The obscuring half is the "refusal kept", required by `authorial stance.md`.
- *A Crossing yields a Durable perspective or a Remainder entry* — expressed in prose in the chain-of-understanding directive as an order of steps with an author-only last step, and in the clearing as the destination for what will not reduce.
- *An Encounter grounds a Durable perspective* — expressed by the split-provenance encounter record and by "This is the author's own material, held under the three conditions of personal provenance" sentences inside notes (e.g., `notes/the axioms are orientations…`, `notes/a durable perspective…`).
- *A Passage realizes content in one Book* — expressed only as the dormant `supports:` field and the concordance's revision relations; no note records that it is realized in a draft.
- *A Book projects a Concern* — expressed once, at the architecture level, as "the two books mirror each other on every shared concern" (a claim in `the-two-books.md`'s region), not per concern.
- *A Commitment protects a function* — not expressed. No directive names a function of the books it protects; the vault's own admission rule was the writing test ("would a drafting session reach for this?"), which is the book-need rule pointed at notes rather than at types.

The vault's five directed relations between states of one element (carried unchanged into, revised into, compressed into, dropped at, added at) have no counterpart in the design, and the vault's `Relevant Notes:` glosses carry many relations the design does not name (an isomorph, a counterweight, a precedent, a lineage-not-convergence). ## 2. Beyond the system

### 2.A The making, as a dated stratigraphy — and the poems beneath it

**What it is.** The provenance layer (`provenance/`: five reconstructions, one lineage analysis, two subject syntheses, the author's own voice prompt, and the seven transmission digs; nine `.md` extractions of the PDFs plus nine digs) and the ten-odd `structure` and `claim` notes built from it, gathered in `the-making.md` (72 attested links) and in the methods map's section "What the record of the making adds to the rules". The record settles a direction: "the author said the plain thing first, in his own words, about his own life, and the scholarship arrived afterward and dressed it" (`CLAUDE.md`). It is dated in strata: the poems (a lifetime, fifteen predating the book), the seed's figures (9 April 2025), the triad axiomatized in another project (8 December 2025), first formal contact with the tradition named for it (19–22 March 2026), the welding (July 2026), the corpus compiled in one month, the reframe (12–13 August 2026). One representative table is the origin-story note's own: "The participation law, lived in a room | 2017 | Assembled into the lineages' roll-call, 7 July 2026" (`notes/the book's honest origin story…`). Beneath it, the poems: 13 notes cite `poems #n`; 25 mention them; the archive note holds a partial concordance of trailheads; the standing has "three faces" by the author's words: "I don't want to overcorrect on this. What I do align with is that the poems are important to the book. And, they are not in the same class of contribution as the research itself. They are a part of the idiosyncrasies of the book for me, as a part of my offering, for now." (`source standing.md`, 2026-08-08).

**Why the design cannot hold it.** Four types each take a piece and none takes the whole. *Encounter* wants "a dated, lived event in which something happened, with the author's note on it"; the record holds dated events but the note on them is the collaborator's reconstruction, marked [C], with the author's turns marked [D], and the vault's rule is to keep that division ("Interpretations remain attributed (the reconstruction's reading is cited as its reading)" — `personal provenance.md`). *Locus* wants a tradition; the author's own prior work is not a standpoint he entered. *Durable perspective* wants what "survived Crossings"; the poems predate every crossing and, by decision, ground no doctrine. *Figure* wants an image; the poems hold none ("what the poems hold is the layer beneath the figures"). And the design's Provenance field has no way to say that a source is *upstream* of the book (primary) rather than about it, or which hand wrote which sentence.

**Which function it serves.** Function 6 (the author's crossing as exemplar) and function 1 (meeting the reader in their sufficiency): the origin story "doubles as the book's invitation … what happened to the author reading the traditions is what the book proposes to be for the reader" (`notes/the book's honest origin story…`). Also the Commitment "The author is the first participant", whose test — "Did this pass through the author's own encounter?" — is answerable only against this record.

**The expansion.** Two field additions to Provenance and one widened definition. Provenance gains a *stratum* (which layer of the making a quotation comes from: poems, seed, ontology work, retreat, corpus, reframe, reading, draft) and a *hand* (author [D], collaborator [C], a third party [A], a session). Encounter is widened to admit a *making event*: a dated act in the book's own production (a chapter authored through the voice prompt; a veto; a commission) whose "note" may be a reconstruction attributed as such. The vault's `personal provenance.md` supplies the three conditions any such record must meet.

**One instance in the expanded form.** Encounter (making event) — *When:* 8 December 2025. *Where/who:* the author's separate ontology project, [D]. *Conditions:* three months before first formal contact with the Trika tradition (Hridaya Northwest, 19–22 March 2026). *What happened:* "The three-part container structure was axiomatized … as Differentiation, Relation, and Persistence." *The note (attributed, [C], August 2026):* "the strongest version of the 'resonance you were already inside' story, and it's the one the dates support." *What changed:* the Trika correspondence is dated as structure-first, tradition-met-after. *What remains:* "whether the author knew the word *Trika* and its meaning before 7 July 2026 … Both readings survive the evidence." *Concerns touched:* Company, Form. *Stratum:* ontology work. *Standing:* encountered (the author's own act), with the note at surfaced. (All quotations from `notes/the triad predates the author's contact with the tradition named the triad.md`.)

### 2.B Source standing and the independence relations — Provenance as a document ladder

**What it is.** `source standing.md` (three standings plus consulted works), the reading-record grammar (`Gendlin 1964, fn. 13`), the encounter record's split provenance, the count-once rule (the atlas and the walked document both compiled from the arc-and-gloss treatment), the six lineage relations, the shared-teacher test ("the convergence's independence fails where three of its names share one teacher"), the Daoism finding (Heidegger's "letting" is lineage, not convergence), and the distance rule for reasons. Counts: 8 source records, 47 reading records, 3 research records, 1 encounter record; 176 notes cite the corpus, 190 outside works, 109 the provenance works.

**Why the design cannot hold it.** "Provenance gives every quotation an address" says nothing about what the address is *of*. The vault has measured what an address alone permits: thirteen correspondence notes "quoting the corpus and paraphrasing the thinker" that looked fully evidenced because "the page is full of anchored quotations from one side" (`both halves of a correspondence.md`); three documents agreeing that are one document read three times; a reason that "moved from three practical problems to a philosophical principle to a line of the book's doctrine" as it was retold (`notes/a recorded reason drifts…`). The design's Locus field "whether it arrived at its finding independently of the others or descends from them" is a property of a *pair* of loci, not of one.

**Which function it serves.** Function 5 (keep company without collapsing it) and the Commitment "Difference is understood before likeness is claimed".

**The expansion.** Provenance becomes a small record with three parts: the address; the source's standing (descriptive / draft / primary / consulted / read-at-the-text / encountered); and, where two sources agree, the independence relation between them (independent arrival; compiled from; shares a teacher; descends from; restatement; self-flagged debt; mis-addressed credit; half-traced genealogy). A Crossing then names the relation before it names what survives.

**One instance in the expanded form.** Provenance — *Address:* atlas §0 preamble; walked, colophon; arc-gloss: how to hold this. *Standing:* descriptive (atlas, walked), draft (arc-gloss). *Relation:* compiled from — "Where the atlas, the walked document, and the Arc and the Gloss agree, the vault is reading one document three times: once as treatment, and twice as compilation of that treatment." *Consequence for a Crossing:* "A claim citing all three anchors is not thereby better supported than a claim citing one." (`notes/the atlas was compiled from the arc and gloss treatment.md`.)

### 2.E The vault's exit — the missing clause in "structure serves encounter"

**What it is.** `ops/the vault's exit.md` (2026-09-03, "A proposal, not a decision"): the diagnosis that "Between those two files there is a door with no drawing, and a door with no drawing is the one that gets invented in a hurry or never opened."; five findings from the graph that draw the door; five stages (an end condition named in advance; the default direction of a session reversed; the instruments decrease; the handover; the return); five signals "none of which a session may act on alone"; three questions carried into the decision sitting; and "What never ends" (provenance, the refusals, the poems' standing, the decisions register). Its center: "What decreases is the making of new structure; what stays is the presence of what was made and the willingness to be entered."

**Why the design cannot hold it.** The design states the principle twice — "The system should remain smaller than the inquiry" (13344) and "If maintaining the model becomes more interesting than participating in the ecology, the model has failed" (9447) — and gives it a test ("Is maintaining this more interesting than using it?") but no operation: no end condition, no direction, no rule for what decreases. The rhythm of the work says "Any step may be skipped" and never says when the system withdraws. The vault's proposal is the missing clause, and it is derived from the books' own doctrine (Winnicott's "natural decrease", the withdrawal completed by a return, the last position left vacant).

**Which function it serves.** Function 7, teach its own exit, applied to the system that serves the books.

**The expansion.** The "rhythm of the work" gains an exit clause with the vault's three questions as Remainder entries addressed to the author, and the four tests gain a fifth: does the system have a drawn door? Instance, in the expanded form — *Exit condition (candidate 1):* "the author's cut of the pilot letter, and his decision about the two real readers"; *(candidate 2):* "one region of the graph meets conditions one through six of `ops/manuscript readiness.md` and he states the seventh, in his own words, about something he walked." *Signal:* "He writes against a built surface rather than asking for a surface to be built." *Counter-signal:* "his word that the season is still open, which outranks all five." (`ops/the vault's exit.md`.)

### Corrections to the design

1. **`state:` is not the standing ladder, and the vault says so by decision.** The design's Part XI asks how its ladder "meets the vault's existing ways of recording how developed a piece of content is and how firmly it is held". Those are two different things in the vault, on purpose: `state:` records substantiation for the pipeline's use and "nothing the book asserts" (`vocabulary policy.md`, 2026-08-05); how firmly a thing is held is recorded per author act (1b). A design that maps nascent → surfaced and full → believed would reverse a decision row and would make a reading (which moves nascent to full) raise the author's standing, which the design's own rule forbids ("no AI raises standing"). The correct mapping: every note is at *surfaced* until an author act touches it; `state:` is orthogonal.
2. **Lens as a shared field contradicts two confirmed forks.** The vault registers the quadrants as a way-in ("registered lenses (research-frontier material), not re-seatings", 2026-08-13; Fork 25 on Wilber) and the category test says a coordinate system "answers where inquiry is looking, not what ultimately exists". A field on every unit installs the coordinate system as schema. The design should hold Lens as an optional view, not a shared field.
3. **Craft move as a type conflicts with the 2026-08-09 decision.** "Cut the craft-about-drafts notes too … a research corpus studies the ideas rather than the manuscript's sentence technique." The design's argument that Book I's reader "learns from the book's own moves" is a reason to reopen that row; reopening "is a /rethink proposal with the author's decision, never a session's own act" (`ops/decisions.md`, header). The design should present Craft move as a proposal that reverses a decision, with both reasons.
4. **The unit.** The design takes the Pair as "the unit of the books' conceptual content"; the vault settled the unit as "Both, allocated" — the proposition-sentence for its notes, the named concept for any Tana layer (2026-08-13). Pairs are real in the vault but live inside propositions. The design should state Pair as a *view over* propositions (a query that finds the two terms) rather than a node that replaces them, or it will have to re-file 500 notes.
5. **Provenance as "an address" is too thin** (2.B). The vault has measured three failures an address alone permits.
6. **Independence is a relation between loci, not a field on one.** The Locus field "whether it arrived at its finding independently" should be a relation with the six values the vault uses, and the selection-artifact guard should sit on it: "the lineages were gathered on one day for the property they are then reported to share" (`CLAUDE.md`).
7. **The Crossing needs a "who performed it" marker.** The vault's rule that the reading "never asserts that the correspondence holds" means a Crossing surfaced by a session and a Crossing the author performed are different objects; the standing field can carry it, but the type's fields should say that the "author's note" is the only field a session may not write.

Part X lists constraint (3808, 1074) in its enumeration but drops it from the register. 9. **The design's per-concern profile can only partly be generated** (Part XI, question 6). The maps are hand-curated by decision ("A generator would flatten that curation and swap the glosses for descriptions", 2026-08-09 row). Question, Pair, Company and Encounter cells could be computed from `topics:`, `category:`, `sources:` and the provenance classes; Crossing, Durable perspective and Exit cells cannot, because their evidence is author acts recorded in prose.

## 4. The three findings that matter most

**1. The vault already runs the standing ladder, but as a set of author acts, never as a field — and its `state:` field is the thing the design must not mistake for it.** The design's Part XI asks how its ladder meets the vault's record of "how developed a piece of content is and how firmly it is held". The vault keeps those two apart by decision: `state:` "records how substantiated a note is … and nothing the book asserts" (`vocabulary policy.md`), with `privated` required to name its absence "which is what stops the value being usable as a bare rank" (`templates/claim-note.md`), while firmness is recorded only where the author acted — 30 of 64 thinkers still `candidate`, 1 of 13 tensions `resolved`, 0 placements, 119 decisions rows, 35 notes citing his dated words, one reflection whose words are "inviolable". The grounding meter reports 157 of 502 claims nascent and the axiom family 78% nascent, and none of those numbers says anything about what the author believes. The design's ladder is right that only the author raises standing; it is wrong if it reads nascent as surfaced and full as believed. The expansion is one field, "standing", set only by an author act, sitting beside `state:` rather than replacing it, and the vault's directives already supply the rule that no session writes it.

**2. The making is held in the vault at a resolution the design has no type for, and the poems are the case that proves it.** Seven provenance reconstructions, one lineage analysis, the author's own voice prompt, and some twenty notes hold the book's origin as dated strata with the hand marked on each sentence — [D] the author, [C] the collaborator, [A] the friend — and the direction settled: "the author said the plain thing first, in his own words, about his own life, and the scholarship arrived afterward and dressed it" (`CLAUDE.md`). The design's Encounter wants the author's note; the record's notes are attributed reconstructions. Its Durable perspective wants what survived crossings; the poems predate them and by the author's word are "a part of my offering, for now", not evidence. Its Figure wants images; "the figures … appear nowhere in the collection" (`archive/source — the poems.md`). Its Provenance wants an address; the vault has shown an address alone permits thirteen one-sided correspondences and a reason retold into doctrine. The expansion is a Provenance record with stratum, hand and standing, an independence relation between sources, and an Encounter widened to the making event; the worked instance (2.A) shows the triad's axiomatization on 8 December 2025 written in that form. This is the material function 6 needs — the author's crossing as exemplar — and function 1, because the origin story "doubles as the book's invitation".

**3. Most of this region belongs outside the books' system, and the vault has already drawn the door the design lacks.** By the book-need rule, the hooks, the 65 cache markers, the meters, the lexicon and reply check, the query scripts, the logs and the decisions register serve the vault's honesty, not a function of the books; the vault measured itself as "619 KB of 1.30 MB in `ops/`+`.claude/` … against 56.5 KB ever drafted toward the book" (`corpus-snapshot-2026-08-13.md`) and has since found its health checks "object-shaped" (`ops/rethink-log.md`, 2026-08-21). Two exceptions are expansions the design should take: the concordance's five revision relations between states of one Passage, and the directives as a second Commitments register — conditions on the parties making the books, each with a test the vault already wrote (2.C). And the design's principle that "the system should remain smaller than the inquiry" has no operation in the design; `ops/the vault's exit.md` supplies one — an end condition named in advance, the session's direction reversed, the instruments decreased, a handover, a return — derived from the books' own doctrine and reserved to the author: "What decreases is the making of new structure; what stays is the presence of what was made and the willingness to be entered." Its three questions are the Remainder entries this region hands to the merger.
