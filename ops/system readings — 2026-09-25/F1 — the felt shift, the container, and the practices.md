# Report F1: the felt shift, the container, and the practices

*About this version.* Reader F1 wrote this report on 2026-09-25, reading one region of the vault against the design in `reframe-map-and-system-2026-09-25.md`. On the same day, after the author said he could not follow much of the study's writing, a session rewrote the report in plain language. The rewrite keeps the reader's findings, counts, quotations, and paths, removes repetition, and uses the plain names the design now uses (topic, tradition or thinker, comparison, the author's view, writing technique, drafted passage, the author's level, the open questions, and the topic table; `system-and-vault-collation-2026-09-25.md`, Part 2, defines each). The reader's original wording is in the repository's history at commit `418f352`. Where the reader cited a line number in the design document, this version names the part instead, because the design has since been rewritten.

**Words used.** A *topic map* is a page gathering the claims of one concern. *Company* means thinkers who reached the same finding independently; the book cites them as others who noticed the same thing, not as evidence that it is true. A *drafting document* (the vault calls it a concern surface, or a surface to write from) is a draft document in `drafts/` that assembles the material on one of the book's concerns and stops where the author's own contribution begins. A note is *nascent* when it is stated but not yet supported by a reading of its source, and *privated* when it is complete except for one named missing reading. A *thinker page* is the vault's page for a thinker (the vault calls it a chair): a note in `notes/` with `category: interlocutor`, recording what the thinker does for the book and where the thinker disagrees with it. The *one chair* is the vault's phrase for the reader reading alone, and the *second chair* for a second person present beside the reader. A question *only the author can answer* is what the vault calls a sounding; the open ones are numbered B1 to B7 in `ops/practice-model ecology — candidate next steps.md`. A *convergence note* (the vault's word is isomorph) records that two or more traditions reached the same structure. A *tension* is a disagreement inside the book's doctrine, kept open in a note rather than resolved. The *felt shift* is a change, felt in the body, in how a situation feels; the author decided on 2026-08-04 that it is the book's single test of truth, and the vault calls that test the verification standard. The *Stance* is the corpus's name for the basic posture of practice, and the *twelve forms* are the twelve short practices in chapter 14 of `abridged` (the corpus's word for them is kata). The *container* is the corpus's name for any setting in which people change (a room, a friendship, a family, a gathering, a self). The corpus says every container has three structures, a Wall that "differentiates and protects," a Door that "relates and exchanges," and a Floor that "persists and bears weight," and an open center, the Clearing, which the three make possible. A *certificate* is the vault's word for a sign by which someone can tell that something really happened, and a *counterfeit* is an experience that gives the same sign without the thing having happened.

The region is the second part of the book's doctrine: the felt-shift and container-anatomy topic maps and their member notes; all nine practice notes; the drafting document on the felt shift; the brief and the draft of the defense of the book's single test of truth; the felt-shift and container sections of `the-concerns.md`; and `ops/practice-model ecology.md`.

### 1b. The four things recorded for every kind of material, and the design's two lists

**The author's level.** The vault's `state:` field (nascent, privated, full, flourishing, composting) records how well supported a note is, not the author's relation to it. `templates/claim-note.md` defines nascent as "the proposition stated, not yet substantiated," and privated as "a complete claim incomplete in a specific named way, usually waiting on a reading." The design's levels (proposed, experienced, inferred, believed, claimed, written) exist in the vault only by implication, in three places: the kinds of source in `sources:` (a source that is the author makes the material at least "experienced," and a source that is the reframe conversation leaves it "proposed" unless one of the author's own turns is cited); the table of settled questions, where a decision of the author's raises a proposition's level (row 24, 2026-08-04: the felt shift "is the book's test for whether anything is true and everything else runs downstream of it — one standard, not one doctrine among six. The author judges it currently underdefended"); and the comments at the head of the drafts, which state plainly that their voice is a placeholder and not the author's.

What this region shows about the levels is clear. The center of the felt-shift map is at "believed" by the author's decision (row 24), while the scaffold file (`ops/scaffold.md`, which compares eight readings of the book's structure) records that inside the transcript "the felt-shift verification standard (the doctrine center's measurement organ) is **never author-ratified inside the transcript** — it appears only as model-supplied material," and that "The author's own verification language is the *non-verifiability trust* ('the book can't verify that you've done the work,' 266), the *durability criterion* ('what survives movement across contexts?', 6872), and the *authorial gate*" (line 220). ## 2. Material the design cannot record

### 2A. Certificates and counterfeits: how anyone knows that something happened

**What it is.** The largest and most thoroughly read set of material in the region is not a claim about inner life but a claim about verification: how the book, its author, its reader, and each tradition in its company can tell that something happened, what each sign shows, and how each sign can be faked. It includes:
- the standard, "nothing enters the lattice the body has not countersigned" (atlas §III, as quoted in `notes/nothing enters the lattice….md`), and its narrowing in the primary text, "the body countersigns *movement*… not *truth*" (`drafts/felt-shift — a surface to write from.md`, line 11);
- the catalogue of counterfeits, Gendlin's four kinds of attention that are not focusing, and the rule about *makyō*;
- the finding that attending to the body is not the same as reading it accurately;
- the two critiques of introspection, by Nisbett and Wilson and by Schwitzgebel;
- the thinkers who describe the felt shift done with another person;
- the conclusion of the comparison of Ignatius, Engler, Kapleau, and Gendlin;
- and two planned surveys not yet done (`ops/ecologies/the certificates.md` and `the counterfeits.md`).

By count, at least 20 of the 79 members of the felt-shift map concern how something is shown to have happened, or how that sign is faked, rather than the felt sense itself; so do the 10,000 words of the three drafts. One quotation states the main finding: "a book read alone can state the whole of the restraint and none of the discrimination" (`notes/a solitary reader can run every guard that declines to evaluate and none that discriminates.md`). Restraint means rules such as not acting on an experience right away, which a book can print; discrimination means judging whether a particular experience is real, which a book cannot do for its reader.

**Why the design cannot record it.** No kind of material, field, or connection in Part 7 asks how anyone knows that something happened. The practice has a field for how it goes wrong, but none for how you would know it went right, and who can tell. The encounter has a field for what changed, but the field assumes the change is known. The commitments state the ethical side of the same fact ("Recognition belongs to the reader"; "the book can't verify that you've done the work," Part 5) and stop there. The author's levels record that the author experienced, believed, or claimed something, and never by what sign. If this material were filed in the design as it stands, it would be divided among the practice's field for how it goes wrong, the tradition's field for what it hides, and the open questions, and the finding that connects it (a book can print the restraint but not the discrimination) could not be recorded anywhere.

**What it does for the books.** It serves function 2 (arrange the conditions for recognition, including "safety with enough friction"); function 3 (direct attention and leave the meaning to the reader: a sign the reader checks is what keeps directed attention from turning into a verdict the book supplies); function 8 (the promise: what the book will not do for this reader); what Book II's authority is based on ("embodied realization"); and Book I's need to show its own techniques openly, since a facilitator reading Book I is exactly the second person the safeguards tell a reader to consult.

**How the design would be extended.** Add a field to the practice and to the drafted passage, **certificate**, with four parts drawn from the survey plan for certificates: the sign (at the moment, over a period, by what follows, by conduct, by the problem not coming back, or by another person); who can check it (the person now, the person later, another who has been there, or a clinician); its counterfeit, and who can detect it; and whether a printed page can present it. A new kind of material would be more than is needed, and the survey plan's own hypothesis says why a field is enough: "the felt sign at the moment is the only family the book can execute on the page and the one every tradition trusts least" (`ops/ecologies/the certificates.md`, "Provisional families"). The author's levels gain one clause: the sign by which the author raised the material, which for this author is currently undecided between the felt shift and durability (question B3).

**One real example, in the extended form.** The example is the practice of Focusing (`notes/the solo practice reaches the felt shift and the primitives then surface by themselves.md`).
- Invitation: the six steps, "locate the felt sense of something in the body without analyzing it; attend to its quality; let words or images arise from inside the sense…; check each against the sense; release what does not match; and wait" (`walked` VII, as quoted).
- What it lets the reader notice: the difference between sheer emotion and the felt sense.
- Scale: alone, "always unsteady and difficult" (Gendlin 1964, fn. 13, as quoted in `notes/the second chair….md`); with another person, the listener's responses "produce direct reference and referent movement."
- Tradition: Gendlin, read in full on 2026-08-28.
- Smallest form that works: it "fits in a paragraph."
- How it goes wrong: the four kinds of inward attention that are not focusing; the quick answer.
- **Certificate.** The sign at the moment is the felt movement ("a directly experienced 'give' or 'movement' which feels right and welcome"), and the sign over days is the step ("after referent movement, the meanings and symbolizations one formulates are different"). Who can check it: the person, at the moment, for the restraint, and the listener for the discrimination. The counterfeit is the fluent yes that "arrives fast… cannot be re-contacted as a body-sense… and has nothing new when revisited" (`notes/gendlin's focusing protocol already guards….md`), detected by returning to the felt sense, by checking whether it is fresh, and by checking again over days. A page can print the safeguards, but not the discrimination.
- The author's encounters with it: none recorded.

### 2B. The survey of models of practice, and its classification of how a practice changes a person

**What it is.** `ops/practice-model ecology.md` (47,453 bytes) sorts "roughly seventy distinct practice-models" already in the notes into eleven structural groups (container and conditions, privation, polarity, staged arc, loop, reflexive, parts, compositional, holarchy (wholes nested inside larger wholes), speech act, and completion). It adds two outside the eleven (change of state, and ways of telling experiences apart), and it measures every group against the author's own account of recognition in `reflections/`, which has six features. Its most useful distinction, which the design lacks, classifies how a practice brings about a change in a person: in another's company, without causing it (the survey's term is opened-with); by causing it (opened-by); or by taking the person into a place or state (opened-into). A practice can also bring about no change and serve only as a safeguard. Its most important finding is that the vault uses the word "privation" for four different explanations of suffering (something added that must be taken away; a fullness that has contracted; something that never developed; and, in one case, something present rather than missing), and that "the author's own founding experience belongs to the second branch and to no other, and three regions reached that conclusion without contact with each other" (section 4).

**Why the design cannot record it.** The design's practice has no field for what kind of structure a practice is, or for how it brings about a change; the design's only field for kind is on the pair (distinction or polarity). The test of which of four kinds a new structure is ("operator, method, dramaturgy, or ontology") cannot be recorded either. Without these, a model of developmental levels, or a coaching method that changes a person's state, would be recorded in the design as a practice, with the same standing as Focusing, which is the mistake `ops/methodology/center of gravity.md` names.

**What it does for the books.** It serves function 4 (keep the two poles unranked): the category "opened-with" keeps the author's own account from being classified as a change that something caused. It serves function 5 (bring in others without merging them): the groups are "a record of where a search went, not a verdict that the traditions agree" (section 10). And it serves Book I's material on facilitation.

**How the design would be extended.** Add two fields to the practice: **structural group** (one of the eleven, or a new one if the evidence requires it), and **how it brings about a change** (opened-with, opened-by, opened-into, or a safeguard), with the rule the survey states: each practice is compared with the author's account, and his account is never assigned to one of the groups. The design's field for the author's encounters with a practice then has something to be measured against.

**One real example.** The example is the crisis triage (`notes/the crisis triage collapses the diagnostic to body holding and contact.md`).
- Invitation: "am I in my body, am I held, am I in I–Thou with someone?" (`abridged` ch15, as quoted).

### 2C. The container account, which describes the design's own way of working as a structure

**What it is.** The container map's 28 members, and the two notes that consolidate the system's schemes. The account's center: "at their center, made possible by the three but identical with none of them, is the Clearing: the open space itself" (`walked` II, as quoted in `notes/the open center….md`). Its function: the Wall "fixes the edge so the center is free to move." Its refusal: Goffman's objection that a total institution "has all three structures intact and a dead center." Other traditions that reached it independently, by taking things away and by withdrawing. And its date: December 2025, three months before the author's contact with the tradition whose name means the triad.

**Why the design cannot record it.** The design's way of working, "Form directs attention, and the meaning is left to the reader," is exactly what the container account states at the scale of a room: enough structure at the edge that the center is free. But the design has no kind of material for the conditions a practice or an encounter happens inside. The encounter's field "the conditions it happened in" has no structure; the practice's field for scale is too narrow to record the edge, the exchange, and the continuity over time; and the topic Form records the question, not the structure. Filed as a topic, the container would contradict the author's demotion of the system ("scaffolding rather than the centerpiece"). Filed nowhere, the design would lose the vault's only structural account of function 2 ("safety with enough friction"), on which the best measured support in the project depends (the participation law, in reader O2's region).

**What it does for the books.** It serves function 2 and Book I's ending, the facilitator as "steward of the field"; and it serves function 7, because what the container account hides is on record.

**How the design would be extended.** Widen the encounter's field "the conditions it happened in," and give the practice a matching field, **conditions**, each with the container's three questions and its open center: what kept the boundary, what allowed exchange, what lasted over time, and what was left free. This keeps the container account as a tool ("useful for seeing with; not the thing the book is wrapped around," `ops/methodology/center of gravity.md`, 2026-08-04) rather than a kind of material, and the design can record the structure of its way of working without a new kind of material.

**One real example.** The encounter: the making of another of the author's projects, an outliner, as he describes it (`notes/an externalized system can be built to hold a self….md`): "I build containers where things — people, ideas, a self — become more fully themselves. This outliner is one more facet of that work, not a productivity app… Build it like an instrument I'll play daily, not a database I'll administer" (the second round of provenance reconstruction, section 1, quoting the author's words of 19 Jun 2026, marked [D]). Its conditions: the edge, capture that is "body-primary, with schema accreting from use rather than being declared"; exchange, "a daily heartbeat that wakes me to survey"; continuity, "a pruned garden, not an accumulating archive"; and what was left free, "the graph metabolizes." What remains: "nothing in the searchable record shows it running with live data."

### 2D. The drafting document, and the brief behind a passage

### Corrections to the design

1. **The commitment "Form directs attention, and the meaning is left to the reader" has a documented way of failing that the design does not name.** The commitment's question is "Is this device directing attention or supplying meaning?" The region's measured finding is that directing attention with fluent form supplies the *feeling* of meaning: "statements shown in easier-to-read contrast are judged true above chance on presentation alone," and "artificially induced aha moments made unrelated statements — including false ones — feel more true" (`notes/the felt sense of truth has a documented counterfeit catalogue.md`). The vault draws a consequence the design lacks: "the prose must build de-confirmation into its own rhythm — endings that de-conclude, contrary voices kept audible" (`notes/the book is one of the automatisms it must interrupt.md`). The commitment's question needs a third option: or producing agreement.

2. **"Recognition belongs to the reader" needs a second question.** Its question, "Does this passage tell the reader what they recognized?", leaves out the case the brief names: telling the reader what a recognition *feels like* prepares the reader to produce the counterfeit. "A reader taught this book's vocabulary and told what a shift feels like has been given a subculture's rules and its feedback" (`drafts/brief…`, section 3, citing Nisbett and Wilson, p. 254, and Katz). The added question: does this passage tell the reader what the recognition will feel like?

3. **The design's topic table in Part 9 gives the Practice column too little credit.** It marks Practice "m" (supplied only by the model) for eleven topics. The vault shows that the practices come from the corpus and from the author's own craft ("the book's practices are, at bottom, the craft of holding a room for people — scaled down to one," `first-telling`, as quoted), and the record of how the book was made holds that the author "supplied the seed, the practice, the lineage choices, and every veto" (`notes/the author curated and vetoed while the collaborator wrote the correspondences.md`, description). For Form, Transformation, Awareness, and Meaning, the Practice column is above "proposed."

4. **The design's largest omission, writing technique, is empty by the author's decision, not by oversight.** The design says each outline "described the inquiry, and none described how a page works on a reader" (Part 7). The vault did record it, with sixteen craft notes (reader O4 counted eighteen on 2026-08-08) and a governing directive ("Record the mechanism, never the verdict," `ops/methodology/craft evidence.md`), and the author retired the category on 2026-08-09, on the reasoning that "a research corpus studies the ideas rather than the manuscript's sentence technique" (`ops/decisions.md`, row 88). That decision came a week before the two-book decision of 2026-08-16. The design should record that Book I's need for writing technique as content conflicts with a standing decision against notes on the drafts' technique, and that reconciling the two is the author's decision.

5. **The image's field for where it came from needs a fourth value.** The design lists "the author, a tradition, or the model." The region's images were drafted in July 2026 by the AI collaborator, working to the author's specification and subject to his veto, and several are dated before the system: "The tuning fork, the house, the orchard, and the gold thread are present in the April 2025 seed; the triad was axiomatized in a separate project that December" (`notes/the figures precede the system by a year….md`, description). The value the record supports is "the collaborator, under the author's veto."

6. **The level "believed" needs its sign recorded.** The felt shift is at "believed" by the author's decision (row 24), while "the author's own verification language" in the transcript is durability and the admission that the book cannot verify (`ops/scaffold.md`, line 220). The same material can reach a level by one test or by another, and the design should record which sign raised it.

## 4. The three findings that matter most

**First: most of the region's material concerns a kind of material the design does not have, the certificate, and the vault has already found what a printed page can provide.** Twenty or more notes and ten thousand words of drafts are about how anyone knows that something happened, not about inner life as such. The design's commitments state the ethical side ("Recognition belongs to the reader"), and its practice records how a practice goes wrong, but nothing asks how you would know it went right, and who can tell. The vault's answer is exact and based on primary readings: "every criterion a reader alone can apply declines to evaluate, and every discrimination proper routes to another person" (`notes/a solitary reader can run every guard that declines to evaluate and none that discriminates.md`, description). That settles what Book II can print (the restraints: continue, do not act on it, check it against time, check it against another person) and what Book I is for (the reader who becomes the second person). The planned survey of certificates states why this matters: "the felt sign at the moment is the only family the book can execute on the page and the one every tradition trusts least." A certificate field on the practice and the drafted passage would record all of it; a new kind of material would be more than is needed.

**Second: the design's largest omission, writing technique, is empty in the vault because of one decision of the author's that a later decision of his now conflicts with.** The sixteen craft notes were retired on 2026-08-09 ("Cut the craft-about-drafts notes too," `ops/decisions.md`, row 88), one week before Book I was made the first book on 2026-08-16, and the design states that Book I needs "its own writing techniques shown openly and taught as content" (Part 6). The accounts of technique are still recorded under other categories, and one complete example of showing the reasons for each choice exists: the brief and the draft of the defense of the test of truth. The draft says of itself, "This book asks you to check things in your own body, and that is the most attackable sentence in it," and the brief records what it was watching for. The survey of devices is planned and has not been done. Whether the decision is reopened is the author's choice; the report records only that the two decisions conflict.

**Third: the author's levels are useful in this region, and the first error they find is in the vault's own drafting document.** The felt-shift drafting document credits two of the model's sentences to the author ("Your own words in the reframe put the instrument plainest," line 33), where the transcript places line 815 under `## Response:`, and "Does this actually live in me?" inside the model's big outline at line 6552. In addition, the topic's center is at "believed" by the author's decision of 2026-08-04, while "the author's own verification language" in the transcript is durability and the admission that the book cannot verify (`ops/scaffold.md`, line 220), and the question that would settle which sign is his (B3) is open. Applied here, the design's levels show that the region's defense is based on primary texts and that its center is the author's by decision, but that the sign by which he raised it is not yet on record. And the design's own way of working, "Form directs attention, and the meaning is left to the reader," needs the warning that follows from this region's evidence: fluent form can produce agreement, so the books must "build de-confirmation into [their] own rhythm."
