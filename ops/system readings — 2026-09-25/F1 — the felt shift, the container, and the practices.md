## 1. Collation

Two words of the vault's own are used throughout and are explained once here. A **chair** is a thinker's page in `notes/` (category interlocutor), recording what the thinker does for the book and where the thinker parts from it. The **second chair** is the vault's phrase for a second person present beside the reader; the **one chair** is the solitary reading. A **sounding** is a question only the author can answer about his own experience or intention. The **Stance** is the corpus's name for the basic practice posture; the **twelve forms** (the corpus's word is kata) are the twelve practice gestures of `abridged ch14`.

## 2. Beyond the system

### Body A. Certificates and their counterfeits: how anyone knows something happened

**What it is.** The largest and most-read body in the region is not a claim about inner life but a claim about verification: how the book, its author, its reader, and each tradition in its company tell that something occurred, what each sign certifies, and how each sign is counterfeited. It holds the standard ("nothing enters the lattice the body has not countersigned", atlas §III as quoted in `notes/nothing enters the lattice….md`), its narrowing at the primary text ("the body countersigns *movement*… not *truth*", `drafts/felt-shift — a surface to write from.md`, line 11), the counterfeit catalogue, Gendlin's four kinds of non-focusing, the makyō rule, the interoception dissociation, the two attacks (Nisbett–Wilson, Schwitzgebel), the paired form's company, the discrimination family's split, and two unrun surveys (`ops/ecologies/the certificates.md`, `the counterfeits.md`). By count: at least 20 of the 79 felt-shift members are about certification or its counterfeit rather than about the felt sense itself, plus the 10,000 words of the three drafts. One quotation for the whole body: "a book read alone can state the whole of the restraint and none of the discrimination" (`notes/a solitary reader can run every guard that declines to evaluate and none that discriminates.md`).

**Why the design cannot hold it.** No type, field or relation in Part VII asks how anyone knows something happened. The Practice type has "how it goes wrong" but not "how you would know it went right, and who can tell". The Encounter type has "what changed" as a field, but the field assumes the change is known. The Commitments carry the ethical face of the same fact ("Recognition belongs to the reader"; "the book can't verify that you've done the work", Part V) and stop there. The standing ladder records that the author encountered, believed or claimed something and never by what sign. Filed as it stands, this body would be scattered across Practice ("how it goes wrong"), Locus ("what it obscures") and the Remainder, and the finding that organizes it (restraint can be printed, discrimination cannot) would have no home.

**Which function it serves.** Function 2 (safety with enough friction), function 3 (attention directed, meaning free: the certificate is what stops the direction of attention from becoming the supply of a verdict), function 8 (the contract: what the book will not do for this reader), Book II's authority ("embodied realization"), and Book I's need for its own moves made inspectable, since a facilitator reader is exactly the second person the guards route to.

**The expansion.** A field on Practice and on Passage, **Certificate**, with four sub-fields drawn from the certificates recipe: the sign (at the moment, over a course, by what follows, by conduct, by non-recurrence, by another); who can run it (the person now, the person later, another who has been there, a clinician); its counterfeit and who catches it; and whether a page can carry it. A new type would over-build; the recipe's own hypothesis says why a field suffices: "the felt sign at the moment is the only family the book can execute on the page and the one every tradition trusts least" (`ops/ecologies/the certificates.md`, Provisional families). The Standing ladder gains one clause: the sign by which the author raised the content, which for this author is currently disputed between the felt shift and durability (sounding B3).

**One real instance in the expanded form.** Practice: Focusing (`notes/the solo practice reaches the felt shift and the primitives then surface by themselves.md`). Invitation: the six moves, "locate the felt sense of something in the body without analyzing it; attend to its quality; let words or images arise from inside the sense…; check each against the sense; release what does not match; and wait" (walked VII as quoted). Opens: the pair sheer emotion / felt sense. Scale: alone, "always unsteady and difficult" (Gendlin 1964, fn. 13 as quoted in `notes/the second chair….md`); with another, the listener's responses "produce direct reference and referent movement". Lineage: Gendlin, read whole 2026-08-28. Smallest living form: "fits in a paragraph". Goes wrong: the four kinds of inward attention that are not focusing; the quick answer. **Certificate:** sign at the moment, referent movement ("a directly experienced 'give' or 'movement' which feels right and welcome"), and sign over days, the step ("after referent movement, the meanings and symbolizations one formulates are different"); runner, the person now for the restraint and the listener for the discrimination; counterfeit, the fluent yes that "arrives fast… cannot be re-contacted as a body-sense… and has nothing new when revisited" (`notes/gendlin's focusing protocol already guards….md`), caught by re-contact, freshness and the check across days; a page can print the guards and not the discrimination. ### Corrections to the design

1. **"Form directs attention, and meaning stays free" has a documented failure the design does not name.** The commitment's test asks "Is this device steering attention or supplying meaning?" The region's measured finding is that steering attention with fluent form supplies the *feeling* of meaning: "statements shown in easier-to-read contrast are judged true above chance on presentation alone" and "artificially induced aha moments made unrelated statements — including false ones — feel more true" (`notes/the felt sense of truth has a documented counterfeit catalogue.md`). The vault's own consequence is a design obligation the design lacks: "the prose must build de-confirmation into its own rhythm — endings that de-conclude, contrary voices kept audible" (`notes/the book is one of the automatisms it must interrupt.md`). The commitment needs a third option in its test: or producing assent.

2. **"Recognition belongs to the reader" needs a second test.** Its test, "Does this passage tell the reader what they recognized?", leaves out the case the brief names: telling the reader what a recognition *feels like* trains the counterfeit. "A reader taught this book's vocabulary and told what a shift feels like has been given a subculture's rules and its feedback" (`drafts/brief…`, §3, citing Nisbett and Wilson p. 254 and Katz). The added test: does this passage tell the reader what the recognition will feel like?

3. **The design's Part IX grid under-grades the Practice column.** It marks Practice "m" (model-only) for eleven concerns. The vault shows the practice curriculum comes from the corpus and from the author's own craft ("the book's practices are, at bottom, the craft of holding a room for people — scaled down to one", first-telling as quoted), and the provenance record holds that the author "supplied the seed, the practice, the lineage choices, and every veto" (`notes/the author curated and vetoed while the collaborator wrote the correspondences.md`, description). The Practice column stands above surfaced for Form, Transformation, Awareness and Meaning.

4. **The Craft move type's "largest gap" is a gap by the author's decision, not by omission.** The design says "every projection modeled the inquiry, and none modeled how a page works on a reader" (line 371).
