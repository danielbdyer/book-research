# Report O1: recognition and contraction

*About this version.* Reader O1 wrote this report on 2026-09-25, reading one region of the vault against the design in `reframe-map-and-system-2026-09-25.md`. On the same day, after the author said he could not follow much of the study's writing, a session rewrote the report in plain language. The rewrite keeps the reader's findings, counts, quotations, and paths, removes repetition, and uses the plain names the design now uses (topic, tradition or thinker, comparison, the author's view, writing technique, drafted passage, the author's level, the open questions, and the topic table; `system-and-vault-collation-2026-09-25.md`, Part 2, defines each). The reader's original wording is in the repository's history at commit `418f352`.

**Words used.** A *topic map* is a page gathering the claims of one concern. *Company* means thinkers who reached the same finding independently; the book cites them as others who noticed the same thing, not as evidence that it is true. A *drafting document* (the vault calls it a concern surface) is a draft document in `drafts/` that assembles the material on one of the book's concerns and stops where the author's own contribution begins. A *placement* is the author's assignment of material to a chapter. A note is *nascent* when it is stated but not yet supported by a reading, and *privated* when it is complete except for one named missing reading.

The region is the first part of the book's doctrine: the recognition and contraction topic maps, their member notes, their two drafting documents, the learning arc, the index, and the author's reflection.

## 1. Where the region's material fits in the design

### 1a. Kind by kind

**Topics.** The region has two doctrine topic maps (`notes/recognition.md`, with 109 members, and `notes/contraction-not-corruption.md`, with 48), their two entries in `the-concerns.md` (lines 11–43), the two drafting documents, and the recognition stage of the learning arc. Nothing in the region corresponds to the Book I form, the Book II form, or the quadrant. Only recognition is among the design's fifteen topics; contraction-not-corruption, the vault's theory of suffering, has no topic in the design (area 2A below).

**Pairs.** The region's pairs are claims whose titles state a distinction or name two things the book values, neither to be given up for the other. By the reader's own count there are 43 distinctions between two things, 13 polarities, and 9 distinctions among more than two things. They fit directly in content and in part in structure. Every one is the title of a claim ("awareness is not attention — attention selects, awareness contains"; "intimacy is a tether rather than a binding"), and no note records which kind of pair it is, the confusion it corrects, how each side goes wrong, or a way in for the reader. **Practices.** The region has one practice note, `the solo practice reaches the felt shift and the primitives then surface by themselves`, which belongs to the felt-shift concern; none of the vault's 9 practice notes names the contraction concern. **What it does for the books.** It serves function 6, and the direction "Life → Corpus → Book."

**How the design would be extended.** The author's view gains the fields: verbatim (never edited); wordings (those offered to him, those he chose, and his own additions); and whether it enters the book (undecided, offered, or placed). An encounter gains the field "whose account of the event it is" (the author's own, or a proposed one).

## 4. The three findings that matter most

**1. The vault has already carried out, once, the design's step from a comparison to the author's view; and because it has no field for the author's level, credit for words goes to the wrong person elsewhere.**
- *The step works.* The recognition drafting document gathered the thinkers, kept where each departs from the book, named its own blind spots, and left the author's view blank. He answered with a reflection in his own words ("occasioned-by: the recognition surface's mirror"). At his direction, the finding it brought out changed the book's founding position from concealment to contraction, while his words stayed unedited and outside the notes. That is the design's sequence of work (a tradition, an encounter, the author's note, a comparison, and his view) working as a prose form. It also answers the design's question "When did an encounter last change this?": on 2026-08-24.
- *Without a field, words are credited to the wrong person.* Three examples: the map describes the model's five conditions as "the author's own catalogue"; the AI collaborator's sentence "[C] “She is the proof that the love you need exists …" becomes, two notes later, "The author's side is his own record," counted as an "independent arrival" beside Zen (`recognition is irreversible …`); and conclusions drawn by sessions carry "Standing: inferred."
- *The vault's `state:` measures something else.* It measures reading, so notes that record the author's own sentences are marked "nascent." The design's author's level and the vault's measure of reading are two separate measures, and the books need both.

**2. The theory of suffering is a whole topic the design lacks, and its material improves the design's founding polarity.**
- *How large the gap is.* The contraction concern has 48 notes. It has an open question ("a fold to open, a wound to be borne, or a condition to be changed"); a statement of what it leaves out that is more exact than any in the design's table; encounters (the protective structure met over years, and the season of burnout); thinkers read in their primary texts; and a comparison that shows exactly where the book departs from its tradition ("the tradition supplies the book's **mechanism** and withholds the book's **valuation**"). The word "suffer" does not appear in the design at all.
- *What it corrects.* The region's newer notes make the corpus's doctrine of the fold to be opened consistent with the design's unranked poles: suffering "is treated by restoring movement rather than by achieving a state," which names how each side goes wrong. They also show that each side has its own false version, the false peak for expansion and the racket for contraction. Keeping the poles unranked does not by itself answer the author's worry about a false peak.
- *What the author himself settled.* He closed the question of the Sanskrit pairing on 2026-08-20 and declined to join the terms into one cycle. The design's reading of the terms, and the table built on it, should follow him.

**3. In this region the entries for the two books are nearly empty, and so are the practices.**
- *Book I and Book II.* Only 4 of the 153 notes mention either book. The drafting documents are called Book I documents but address the reader defined on 2026-08-04, the reader after a peak. The concerns family file places both concerns in the corpus's plan of a single book. No placements exist.
