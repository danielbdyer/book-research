## 2. Beyond the system

### 2.1 The tension note: a fork with a status and a treatment under trial

**What it is.** Thirteen notes with `category: tension`, each carrying `status:` (live/treated/resolved) and `treatment:` (the proposed handling), gathered at `notes/tensions.md`. Example: `notes/the solitary form stands against seven witnesses who hold a second person constitutive.md`, whose treatment reads "the book adds no second chair; it holds that recognition is already a with, so solitude was never alone — a candidate drawn from the author's reflection, not his decision."

**Why the design cannot hold it.** The Remainder holds "open questions and unresolved forks, kept visible rather than closed," with no status, no treatment, no rule about whose act closes a fork, and no grade of the evidence on each side. A Crossing "yields a Durable perspective or an entry in the Remainder"; the vault shows a Crossing can also yield a fork that the book must not resolve *and* must handle in the writing — "polarity is not a problem to be solved. It is a tension to be inhabited" (abridged ch9, quoted at `notes/tensions.md` line 121). A fork with a treatment is neither a durable perspective nor a mere open question.

**Function served.** Functions 4 (hold the poles unranked: the treatment is how a page keeps both) and 7 (teach its own exit: five of the thirteen name what the book's own frame costs). The design's own Craft move "keeping a tension open, 'a design tension to inhabit'" (Part IX) is the page-level form of this type.

**Expansion.** Split the Remainder into two forms. An *open question* keeps the design's shape. A *fork* gains six fields: the two sides, quoted with addresses; what changes under each; status (live, treated, resolved); the treatment under trial; whose act resolves it; the grade of the evidence. The design's relation becomes: a Crossing yields a Durable perspective, a fork, or an open question.

**Instance in the expanded form.**
- Fork: *the solitary form against the constitutive second person.*
- Side one: seven witnesses — "The tree that has grown on its own without a gardener will bring forth foliage, but will never bring any fruit" (al-Qushayrī, Knysh 2007, p. 406); Gendlin 1964 fn. 13, "it is often possible, though always unsteady and difficult, for the individual to focus by himself."
- Side two: the book's native genre and its one recorded event, "no mirror present, no reply possible" (`provenance/provenance fourth addendum.md`, pp. 4–5); the second-person survey's finding that the clinical second person "is a responder rather than a body in the room."
- What changes: "the book's scope and form, the seventh joint of the scaffold... and the relational-field surface" (`ops/decision sitting — 2026-09-03.md`, item 1).
- Status: live. Treatment: as quoted above. Whose act: the author's (RE-08, soundings S1–S3). Grade: "five of seven at primary; two unread."

### 2.2 The clearing: the exit stated per commitment of the center

**What it is.** `ops/clearing.md` (83 lines), with its policy at `ops/methodology/the clearing.md`. It was added at the author's request in his words: "I want the clearing to appear here so we are not biased by the center of gravity but open to welcome all which is not yet seen." It holds four blind spots, each stating "**The commitment that cannot hold it**"; a singularity register; four divergence forks; three questions the poems raise; three reframe cautions.

**Why the design cannot hold it.** The design's Exit is a field on a Concern, "what treating it as a concern hides." The clearing's entries are not per concern; they are per commitment of the *center* — convergence needs a second arrival, the body is a test, recognition is the return of what never left, the company is of thinkers. The design has function 7 ("Teach its own exit... says what its own frame hides") and gives the Book type a role in it, but no Book field or register holds a book-level exit. The clearing also holds *forks the vault refuses to seat* ("named, un-seated, and unanswered, so the book's alternative stays visible rather than being filed as settled," line 37), which the Remainder would file as forks and thereby domesticate.

**Function served.** Function 7 directly. Function 5 by its singularity register, which says where company is impossible by construction.

**Expansion.** Add a *book-level Exit register* beside the Remainder, with the clearing's discipline: every entry names the commitment it falls outside; entries are protected from the book-need rule (they enter because no function reaches for them); an entry graduates to a Crossing when it finds a second arrival or a chain-link; a fork may be kept unseated. Keep the per-Concern Exit field for the finer grain, and let the concerns family's "leaves open / open edge" lines feed it.

**Instance in the expanded form.**
- Entry: *the finite.* Commitment it falls outside: "the center reads deep change as disclosure of the already-present rather than acquisition or repair" (`ops/clearing.md` line 21). What it cannot see: "death, irreversible loss, the ending that is not a concealment of something still present." Where it is already visible: poem #15, "wonder what it is I am afraid about when I think about dying" (line 51). Graduation: chain-link `heidegger's being-toward-death is the finite the recognition doctrine leaves behind` (SZ 263, "individualizes Dasein down to itself"); arrival `buddhist impermanence faces loss as real rather than as veiled presence` (AN 5.57). Whose act: "Whether the book wants the finite back or leaves it unanswered stays the author's" (`notes/open-questions.md` line 45; sounding SO-05/SO-30).

### 2.3 The isomorph's independence and selection apparatus

**What it is.** Across the 64 isomorphs, a set of tests the design's Crossing has no fields for: dating (the pairing "presented on 1 July 2026, three months after" the retreat), shared teacher ("three of the four names around the founding convergence are in conversation with the same German"), both-ways traffic (May's case "run and passed on 2026-08-30"), the count-once rule for compiled sources, and the selection-artifact audit ("what the survey did was find them, not make them agree"). Thirty-eight of 64 notes use "no contact" or "independent" (my count); several record independence *failing* (Hübl "formed by Wilber"; Heidegger's letting "descended from wu-wei, so lineage rather than a co-arrival"; "Heidegger demonstrably read Martin Buber's 1910 German Zhuangzi"). Two honesty rules sit above all of it: "convergence is company, never corroboration" (`CLAUDE.md`) and "It does not rank convergences as stronger or weaker evidence" (`ops/methodology/authorial stance.md`).

**Why the design cannot hold it.** The Locus field "whether it arrived at its finding independently of the others or descends from them" is a yes/no on a Locus. The vault shows independence is a property of a *pairing*, tested three ways, dated, and revisable; and that the gathering itself can manufacture the agreement (the tension `curating traditions for their overlap manufactures the unanimity it then reports`). The design's Crossing has no field for how the loci were gathered.

**Function served.** Function 5 ("keep company without collapsing it") and the commitment "Difference is understood before likeness is claimed."

**Expansion.** Two new Crossing fields: *how the loci were gathered* (found by searching for the property; carried in the corpus before the search; arrived unsought against the gatherer's interest) and *independence tested* (dating; shared teacher; both-ways traffic; result and date). One new rule on the relation "a Crossing yields a Durable perspective": agreement within a set gathered for the property counts as company and never as corroboration.

**Instance in the expanded form.** Crossing: *the privation move.* Loci: Aristotle (Physics I.7–9, Metaphysics V.22, read), the Trika malas (Pratyabhijñāhṛdayam sūtra 9, read), Bowlby, Minuchin, Marcia (unread at their texts). How gathered: "two of its five arrivals were carried in the corpus already and the rest were found by looking for the absence-form" (`notes/the privation move...md` line 30). Independence tested: origin dates (tenth century; 1966; 1969; 1974) predate the gathering; a shared psychoanalytic ancestry was asked about on 2026-08-06 and the author ruled "Let's treat them as independent arrivals for now and collapse the tension." Standing after the audit: "an independent arrival across five fields at the level of origin; company, not corroboration, at the level of the shared property."

### 2.4 The audit's third axis: the book's own voice, the voice against it, and ornament

**What it is.** `epistemic-audit-2026-08-16.md` classifies 574 non-map notes into trunk (395), tier-2 contrapuntive (153) and decoration (26), with 33 boundary hinges; its guard is now the directive `ops/methodology/decoration and critique are shelf material.md`, which found "the graph's own connectivity... had come to measure the book's self-doubt rather than its substance." ### Corrections to the design

1. **A Crossing has more than two outcomes.** The design says a Crossing yields a Durable perspective or a Remainder entry. The vault records at least three more: a *fork with a treatment* (2.1); a *departure*, where the book's move is separated from its company and "recorded as a departure and never cited as convergence" (`ops/decisions.md`, 2026-08-13); and a *demotion to lineage*, where the crossing was made and independence then failed ("Heidegger and the Zhuangzi are not a no-contact convergence," `notes/remembering and forgetting...md`). A Crossing can also return a *counter-case kept* ("exposure therapy stands as the discipline that does not converge"). The relation should read: a Crossing yields a Durable perspective, a fork, an open question, a departure, or a demotion, and it keeps its counter-cases.

2. **The Remainder is four registers with different owners, not one.** Tensions (author resolves; status and treatment), open questions (openness does work; closing is an authorial event to be recorded with "where in the manuscript"), the clearing (outside what the center can see; names the commitment; graduates by pipeline), and the open corpus (apparatus). Filing a clearing entry as a fork "domesticates" it (`ops/clearing.md` line 37); filing a fork as a row "silently loses its treatment tracking" (`CLAUDE.md`, Relations). The design's single register would do both.

3. **The Exit exists at two scales and the design names only one.** The clearing states what the *center* cannot see; the concerns family states what each *concern* leaves open. The design's per-Concern Exit sits between them and is populated by neither. Function 7 needs the book-level form the clearing already is.

4. **The author's note on a Crossing lives in the decisions register, not on the Crossing.** The design puts "the author's note" on the Crossing type. In the vault, 0 of 64 isomorphs carry his note, 7 explicitly reserve the slot ("he confirms whether...", "Reserved for the author: whether meaning is framed as constitutively unfinished," "whether to name Bachelard where the house is introduced is the author's call"), and his actual rulings on convergences are dated rows in `ops/decisions.md` (2026-08-06 arity; 2026-08-13 departure; 2026-08-16 deepest well) quoted back into the notes. The design should make the author's note a *pointer to a dated, quoted decision*, which is what the vault's register already provides, rather than a free-text field a session might fill in his voice.

5. **"Who may set it" is three-way, not two-way.** The vault's decision sitting distinguishes what only the author can move (a sounding), what a reading can settle (with a grade), and what writing settles (a surface). The design's ladder has the author for every rung above surfaced and no place for a reading that changes a Crossing's standing without any author act — which is most of what happened to the 64 isomorphs between 2026-08-21 and 2026-09-02 (25 now full, 24 privated).

6. **Provenance needs a grade and the both-halves rule.** "An address" is not enough for a correspondence; the vault's measured failure was "Every quotation in all thirteen is from the corpus.
