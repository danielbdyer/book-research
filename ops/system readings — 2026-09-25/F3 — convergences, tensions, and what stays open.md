# Report F3: convergences, tensions, and what stays open

*About this version.* Reader F3 wrote this report on 2026-09-25, reading one region of the vault against the design in `reframe-map-and-system-2026-09-25.md`. On the same day, after the author said he could not follow much of the study's writing, a session rewrote the report in plain language. The rewrite keeps the reader's findings, counts, quotations, and paths, removes repetition, and uses the plain names the design now uses (topic, image, tradition or thinker, comparison, the author's view, writing technique, drafted passage, the author's level, the open questions, and the topic table; `system-and-vault-collation-2026-09-25.md`, Part 2, defines each). The reader used the design's earlier names, and the design lists each earlier name beside its plain one. The reader's original wording is in the repository's history at commit `418f352`. Where the reader cited a line number in the design document, this version names the part instead, because the design has since been rewritten.

**Words used.** A *convergence note* (the vault's word is isomorph) records that two or more traditions or thinkers reached the same finding. Thinkers who reached a finding independently are the book's *company*: the vault treats their agreement as company, not as proof that the finding is true. A *tension* is a note recording a disagreement inside the book's doctrine, kept open rather than resolved. Its `treatment:` field states the proposed way of handling the disagreement in the writing, and its `status:` field is `live` (the proposed handling is still being tested), `treated` (a handling has been adopted and the disagreement stays open), or `resolved`. Only the author resolves a tension. An *open question* is a note with `category: open-question`, gathered in `notes/open-questions.md`. The book's *center*, by the author's decision, is two things: the convergence of thinkers, and the practice the reader actually does, checked in the body. `ops/clearing.md`, which the vault calls the clearing, is the register of what that center cannot see; an item enters it because it does not fit. The *open corpus* (`ops/open corpus.md`) lists territory the corpus named and never developed. `ops/decision sitting — 2026-09-03.md`, which the vault calls the decision sitting, is the document gathering every open question only the author can answer; it calls such a question a *sounding*. A note's `state:` is `nascent` when the claim is stated but not yet supported by a reading of its source, `privated` when it is complete except for one named missing piece, recorded in its `privation:` field, and `full` when it is supported. A *primary reading* is a reading of a thinker's own text, and its *reading record* in `archive/` states what was read and how it is cited. A *thinker page* is the vault's page for a thinker (the vault calls it a chair), and to *seat* a thinker is to give the thinker a role in the book, recorded in the page's `seat:` field. A *topic map* is a page gathering the claims of one concern. The vault's six maps for the book's doctrine (`recognition`, `contraction-not-corruption`, `felt-shift`, `container-anatomy`, `relational-field`, and `the call and the answering`) are its six concerns, and `the-concerns.md` is the family file that reads the notes by those six. A *family file* is one of the eight files at the repository root that each read the whole collection of notes by one organizing idea (reader F2's region). A *drafting document* is a draft in `drafts/` that assembles the material on one concern and stops where the author's own contribution begins (the vault calls it a concern surface). The *table of settled questions* is `ops/decisions.md`, one dated row for each question closed. The *epistemic audit* (`epistemic-audit-2026-08-16.md`) sorted the notes into three groups, described in section 1b. The *seven witnesses* are seven thinkers the vault reads as holding that the inner life needs a second person. The *reframe conversation* is the author's conversation with a language model on the evening of 2026-08-12; its transcript is `reframe-conversation-full-2026-08-13.md`.

The region is the vault's material on convergences, tensions, and what stays open: the 64 convergence notes; `notes/tensions.md` and the 13 tension notes; `notes/open-questions.md` and the 10 open-question notes; `ops/clearing.md`; `ops/open corpus.md`; `ops/tensions/`; the list of proposed claims, `ops/queue/claim candidates.md`; `ops/decision sitting — 2026-09-03.md`; and the two audits, `epistemic-audit-2026-08-16.md` and `second-pass-audit-2026-08-19.md`.

### 1b. The four things recorded for every kind of material, and the design's two lists

**The author's level.** The region records three different things, and the design has one list of levels.

First, *how well a claim is supported*: the `state:` field (`nascent`, `privated`, `full`, `flourishing`, or `composting`). A `privated` note must have a `privation:` field "naming exactly what is missing" (`templates/claim-note.md`). Of the 64 convergence notes, 25 are full, 24 privated, and 11 nascent, and 4 have no state.

Second, *the author's decision on a tension*: the `status:` field (`live`, `treated`, or `resolved`), which only the author changes: "Resolving is the author's act: a session records that decision and never originates it" (`CLAUDE.md`). The current counts are 9 live, 3 treated, and 1 resolved (`scripts/queries/tensions-status.sh`).

Third, *whether a note states the book's own position*: the epistemic audit's three groups, "the trunk (Tier 0/1, the dinner table), the contrapuntive prep room (Tier 2), and decoration (evicted from the graph)" (`epistemic-audit-2026-08-16.md`, line 3). The trunk is the group of notes that state the book's own doctrine. The prep room is a separate place outside `notes/` for critiques of the book, its opponents, and questions not yet researched. Decoration is the group of similes and distant analogies that accompany a doctrine without testing it, which were to be removed from the notes. Of 574 notes, the audit put 395 in the trunk, 153 in the prep room, and 26 in decoration, and it left 33 notes on the boundaries between the groups for the author to decide (line 7).

The design's levels correspond to none of these exactly. All 64 convergence notes stand at the design's lowest level, "proposed," except where a decision of the author's is quoted. The vault has no field that says so; the convergence notes say it in their text ("this is recorded as a noticing," `notes/what was never metabolized lives on as absence and hubl reached it through inherited trauma.md`). The region does state the rule that the levels exist to enforce, in `notes/nothing becomes part of the author's philosophy merely because the AI said it.md`, a nascent claim based on the reframe conversation. The audit's rule applies the same caution to what enters the notes: "a resonance, a borrowed analogy, or an external critique is shelf or prep-room material — it enters `notes/` only when the writing or a reading reaches for it" (`epistemic-audit-2026-08-16.md`, line 260).

**Scale.** No note in the region records scale in a field. The tension notes state it in their text: the tension on the solitary form concerns "one person with a text"; the tension on the coaches concerns the whole field of contemporary teachers of personal change; and the cautions from the reframe conversation kept in the clearing concern the surrounding conditions, which the conversation calls the ecology. The open-questions map records something close to scale for one group of questions, "one question at three altitudes" (`notes/open-questions.md`, line 21): the same question is filed once for the author's life, once for the book's design, and once for the vault's record.

**Quadrant.** No note in the region has a field for Wilber's quadrants. The only notes in `notes/` about the quadrants are those on Wilber, in reader O3's region.

### 1c. Connections

The region records the design's connections in four ways.

- **Links, each with a sentence stating the relation.** Every note ends with links, each followed by "a complete clause stating the relation," and the vault's rule is that "notation never carries an assertion alone" (`CLAUDE.md`, Relations). The design's connection "A comparison sets two or more traditions or thinkers side by side on one topic" is recorded as a convergence note's `topics:` field together with its links to thinker pages ("[[martin heidegger]] — the near pole's chair, holding his four refusals"). The design's "A tradition or thinker says something about a topic. It shows some of the topic and leaves some of it out" is recorded in the sentences on what a thinker refuses (in 48 of the 64 convergence notes) and in the constellation file's lines "Reveals / leaves open."
- **The sections of the maps.** `notes/tensions.md` groups its entries by what raised each tension: the book's assessment of itself (five tensions the atlas records at §XI.1); the objections that qualify the felt-shift standard (the section is headed "The instrument's counterweights"); the disagreements among the contemporary teachers; the author; the reframe conversation; the transmission digs, research commissioned on 2026-08-13 into how a book can pass this kind of material to a reader; the derivation of 2026-08-20 that drew the book's premises out of the reframe conversation; and the survey of models of practice. ## 2. Material the design cannot record

### 2.1 The tension note: an unresolved choice with a status and a proposed handling

*What it is.* Thirteen notes with `category: tension`, each with `status:` (live, treated, or resolved) and `treatment:` (the proposed handling), gathered in `notes/tensions.md`. An example is `notes/the solitary form stands against seven witnesses who hold a second person constitutive.md`, whose treatment reads "the book adds no second chair; it holds that recognition is already a with, so solitude was never alone — a candidate drawn from the author's reflection, not his decision." ("Second chair" is the vault's phrase for a second person present with the reader.)

*Why the design cannot record it.* The design's list of open questions keeps unresolved questions and choices visible, with no status, no proposed handling, no rule about whose decision closes a choice, and no grade of the evidence on each side. In the design, what the author still holds after a comparison becomes one of his views, and what fits nowhere goes on the list of open questions. The vault shows that a comparison can also produce a choice that the book must not resolve *and* must handle in the writing: "polarity is not a problem to be solved. It is a tension to be inhabited" (abridged ch9, quoted in `notes/tensions.md`, line 121). A choice with a proposed handling is neither one of the author's views nor only an open question.

*What it does for the books.* Function 4, "Keep the two poles unranked," since the proposed handling is how a page keeps both sides; and function 7, "Say what the book's own framework hides," since five of the thirteen tensions name what the book gives up by adopting its own framework. The design's own writing technique of keeping two goods in play without resolving them, which it calls "a design tension to inhabit" (Part 9), is the form this kind of note takes on the page.

*How the design would be extended.* Divide the list of open questions into two forms. An *open question* keeps the design's form. A *choice between two positions* has six fields: the two sides, quoted with addresses; what changes under each; its status (live, treated, or resolved); the proposed handling being tested; whose decision resolves it; and the grade of the evidence. The design's connection then reads: a comparison produces one of the author's views, a choice between two positions, or an open question.

*An example in the extended form.*
- Choice: *the reader alone, against the thinkers who hold that a second person is required.*
- First side: the seven witnesses, among them al-Qushayrī: "The tree that has grown on its own without a gardener will bring forth foliage, but will never bring any fruit" (Knysh 2007, p. 406). Also on this side, Gendlin 1964, fn. 13: "it is often possible, though always unsteady and difficult, for the individual to focus by himself."
- Second side: the book's own form, a letter, and the one recorded occurrence of the event the book promises, recognition arriving, "no mirror present, no reply possible" (`provenance/provenance fourth addendum.md`, pp. 4–5); and the finding of the vault's survey of the second person (`ops/second person ecology.md`) that the second person the clinical sources require "is a responder rather than a body in the room."
- What changes: "the book's scope and form, the seventh joint of the scaffold... and the relational-field surface" (`ops/decision sitting — 2026-09-03.md`, item 1). The scaffold is `ops/scaffold.md`, the book's structure as read out of the vault's notes; a joint is a place in it where the author has to decide between two structures; and the relational-field surface is the drafting document on the relational field.
- Status: live. Proposed handling: as quoted above. Whose decision: the author's (item RE-08, with soundings S1 to S3). Grade: "five of seven at primary; two unread."

### 2.2 The clearing: what each commitment of the book's center cannot see

Each is about a commitment of the book's *center*: a convergence needs a second arrival; the body is a test; recognition is the return of what never left; and the company is a company of thinkers. ### 2.3 How the convergence notes test independence, and how the traditions were selected

*What it is.* Across the 64 convergence notes, a set of tests the design's comparison has no fields for:
- dates: the author's first formal contact with Kashmir Śaivism was a retreat in March 2026, and the pairing of the two founding traditions "was presented on 1 July 2026, three months after";
- a shared teacher: "three of the four names around the founding convergence are in conversation with the same German";
- influence in both directions: whether Heidegger himself drew on East Asian sources, a test made against Reinhard May's study and "run and passed on 2026-08-30";
- the rule that sources compiled from one another count once;
- a check of whether the agreement came from how the traditions were selected: "what the survey did was find them, not make them agree."

Thirty-eight of the 64 notes use "no contact" or "independent" (the reader's count), and several record that independence *failed*: Hübl was "formed by Wilber"; Heidegger's idea of letting "descended from wu-wei, so lineage rather than a co-arrival"; and "Heidegger demonstrably read Martin Buber's 1910 German Zhuangzi." Two rules of honesty govern all of it: "convergence is company, never corroboration" (`CLAUDE.md`), and "It does not rank convergences as stronger or weaker evidence" (`ops/methodology/authorial stance.md`).

*Why the design cannot record it.* The design's field for a tradition or thinker, "whether it reached its finding independently of the others or learned it from them," takes a yes or a no for one tradition or thinker. The vault shows that independence belongs to a *pair* of traditions, is tested in three ways, is dated, and can be revised; and that the way traditions are gathered can itself produce the agreement (the tension `curating traditions for their overlap manufactures the unanimity it then reports`). The design's comparison has no field for how the traditions were gathered.

*What it does for the books.* It serves function 5, "Bring in others who noticed the same thing, without merging them," and the commitment "A difference is understood before a likeness is claimed."

*How the design would be extended.* Two new fields for a comparison: *how the traditions were gathered* (found by searching for the shared property; already in the corpus before the search; or arrived unsought, against the interest of whoever was gathering them) and *whether independence was tested* (by dates, by a shared teacher, or by influence in both directions, with the result and the date). And one new rule on the connection from a comparison to the author's views: agreement within a set of traditions gathered for the shared property counts as company and never as corroboration.

*An example in the extended form.*

### 2.4 The audit's third way of sorting: the book's own position, the positions against it, and ornament

*What it is.* `epistemic-audit-2026-08-16.md` sorts the 574 notes that are not topic maps into the trunk (395), the prep room (153), and decoration (26), with 33 notes on the boundaries left for the author. *Why the design cannot record it.* In the design, a tradition or thinker "says something about a topic. *An example in the extended form.*

### Corrections to the design

1. **A comparison has more than two possible results.** The design says that what the author still holds after a comparison becomes one of his views, and that what fits nowhere goes on the list of open questions. The vault records at least three more results: a *choice between two positions, with a proposed handling* (2.1); a *departure*, where the book's position is separated from its company and "recorded as a departure and never cited as convergence" (`ops/decisions.md`, 2026-08-13); and a *demotion to lineage*, where the comparison was made and the independence of the traditions then failed, because one had learned from the other ("Heidegger and the Zhuangzi are not a no-contact convergence," `notes/remembering and forgetting name one movement back to the ground you never left.md`). A comparison can also produce a *counterexample kept on record* ("exposure therapy stands as the discipline that does not converge"). The connection should read: a comparison produces one of the author's views, a choice between two positions, an open question, a departure, or a demotion to lineage, and it keeps its counterexamples.

2. **The vault keeps four lists where the design keeps one list of open questions, and each has its own rule for who closes an entry.** They are the tensions (resolved only by the author, each with a status and a proposed handling); the open questions (whose openness is useful; when one closes, the record states who closed it, "where in the manuscript," and what resulted); the clearing (what the center cannot see; each entry names the commitment it falls outside, and its material moves into the ordinary notes by the vault's usual process); and the open corpus (part of the vault's own tools and records). Filing a clearing entry as a tension turns it into one more disagreement to manage ("A tension note domesticates such an opponent," `ops/clearing.md`, line 37). Filing a tension as a row of a table means that it "silently loses its treatment tracking" (`CLAUDE.md`, Relations): nobody follows its proposed handling any more. The design's single list would do both.

3. **What the book hides is recorded at two levels, and the design names only one.** The clearing states what the book's *center* cannot see; the concerns file states what each *concern* leaves open. The design's field for what each topic hides lies between the two, and neither supplies it. Function 7 needs the statement for the whole book, and the clearing already is that statement.

4. **The author's note on a comparison is kept in the table of settled questions, not on the comparison.** The design puts his note on the comparison. In the vault, none of the 64 convergence notes has his note, and 7 explicitly leave a place for it ("he confirms whether...", "Reserved for the author: whether meaning is framed as constitutively unfinished," and "whether to name Bachelard where the house is introduced is the author's call"). His actual decisions on convergences are dated rows in `ops/decisions.md`, quoted back into the notes: 2026-08-06, on whether the five arrivals count as independent; 2026-08-13, on the departure; and 2026-08-16, on naming Kashmir Śaivism the book's "deepest well." The design should make the author's note a *pointer to a dated decision, quoted*, which is what the vault's table already provides, rather than a field of free text that a session might fill in his voice.

5. **The question of who can raise an item's level has three answers, not two.** The decision sitting separates what only the author can settle (a sounding), what a reading can settle (with a grade), and what writing settles (in a drafting document). The design gives the author every level above "proposed" and has no place for a reading that changes a comparison's standing without any decision of his. Yet that is most of what happened to the 64 convergence notes between 2026-08-21 and 2026-09-02 (25 are now full, and 24 privated). (The design has since stated that the vault's `state:` field, which readings change, records how well a note is supported, and is a different thing from the author's level.)

6. **The source field needs a grade, and the rule on quoting both sides.** An address is not enough for a correspondence. The failure the vault measured was "Every quotation in all thirteen is from the corpus. Not one of them quotes the thinker" (`ops/methodology/both halves of a correspondence.md`). The design's field for a comparison, "where they correspond, with both sides quoted," states the requirement, but not what to do when one side cannot be quoted (a note "names the half it cannot show") or the ban ("Never quote from memory to close the gap"). Twenty-one of the 64 convergence notes currently name a side they cannot yet quote.

7. **The topic table needs a mark between Company and Comparison.** The design treats Company (two or more traditions or thinkers say something about a topic) and Comparison (the author has compared them) as neighboring columns. The vault shows a third position between them: a comparison made by a *session*, with the difference stated, the refusals kept, independence tested, and what fits no synthesis named. Sessions have made 64 of these, all at the level "proposed." The table needs a mark for "compared by a session, waiting for the author's note," or every one of the vault's convergence notes will appear as an empty entry in the Comparison column when it is nearly complete.

The rules that decide which list an item goes to are stated ("A disagreement between two things the book holds is a tension. A thing the book has no way to hold is a clearing entry"), and so is the cost of merging the lists: a tension filed as a row of a table "silently loses its treatment tracking," and an opposite conclusion filed as a tension becomes one more disagreement to manage, which the clearing exists to prevent. Neither list supplies the statement for each topic that the design asks for. The concerns file's lines "Reveals / leaves open" and "Open edge" come closest, and they answer a different question.

**3. The vault already sorts its material in two ways that the design's levels cannot record, and the design has no place for the quarter of the notes that argue against the book.** The epistemic audit's groups (of 574 notes, 395 in the trunk, 153 in the prep room, and 26 decoration, with 33 boundary cases left for the author) sort by the question "is this the book's own voice, or the voice against it?" The rule that followed ("a resonance, a borrowed analogy, or an external critique is shelf or prep-room material") concerns where a note is kept and which notes link to it, not how strong its evidence is. The second audit then showed the failure of sorting notes by the notes they are linked to ("the evaluators swept the doctrine in with the remnants"). The decision sitting sorts by who can settle an item: a sounding that only the author can answer, a reading with a grade, or writing in a drafting document ("against the surface, not by a row"). It has 22 items that the most other work waits on, 41 items on roles for thinkers, and a rule that "the grades say how much reading stands behind each question, which is a different thing" from ranking the evidence. The design's author's level gives the author every level above "proposed," and it has no grade, no statement of what would raise a level, and no field for how a tradition or thinker relates to the book. So it cannot record an opponent such as Adorno or Katz at full strength without calling him company, and it cannot record that a comparison moved from nascent to full through a reading alone, which is what happened to most of the 64 between 2026-08-21 and 2026-09-02. (The design has since stated that `state:`, which such readings change, is a different field from the author's level.)
