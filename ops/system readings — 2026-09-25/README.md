# System readings, 2026-09-25

This folder holds the working record of one study: eight readers, each reading one region of the vault against the system designed in `reframe-map-and-system-2026-09-25.md`, and answering the author's three questions for it. The questions were (his words): "(1) the collation of existing material at a high level inside this system (2) where the existing material goes beyond or outside of the system, and how the system expands to receive it (3) where in the system is left to populate the existing material does not have any details or information."

The study ran on 2026-09-25 at the author's direction to use subagents of two model families, "a few apiece". Four readers ran on each family: O1 to O4 on one, F1 to F4 on the other. The merged results are three documents at the repository root:

- `system-and-vault-collation-2026-09-25.md`: the vault's material placed in the system.
- `system-and-vault-beyond-the-design-2026-09-25.md`: what the vault holds beyond the design, the corrections it makes to the design, and how the design grows to receive it.
- `system-and-vault-left-to-populate-2026-09-25.md`: the places the design defines that the vault does not yet fill, with who can fill each.

**Standing.** Every report stands at surfaced: a session's reading, which the author has not taken up. The readers could not write files, so each report came back as text and was saved here with one mechanical change: eight wiki links that the vault's link check could not resolve (abbreviated note titles, two search patterns, and one quotation of a link to a note that no longer exists) were written out in full or as plain text, so that `scripts/queries/link-check.sh` passes. Reader O1's working classification of its region's 153 notes was not kept. Where the merged documents state a count or a quotation, it was checked against the vault on 2026-09-25. The reports themselves may carry small errors that the merge corrected, and the merged documents list the points on which readers disagreed and how each was settled (the collation's Part X). By the book-need rule this folder is the vault's apparatus, not the books' material.
