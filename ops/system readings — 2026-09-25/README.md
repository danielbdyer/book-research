# System readings, 2026-09-25

This folder holds the working record of one study. Eight readers each read one region of the vault against the design in `reframe-map-and-system-2026-09-25.md`, and each answered the author's three questions for that region. The questions were, in his words: "(1) the collation of existing material at a high level inside this system (2) where the existing material goes beyond or outside of the system, and how the system expands to receive it (3) where in the system is left to populate the existing material does not have any details or information."

The study ran on 2026-09-25, at the author's direction to use subagents of two model families, "a few apiece." Four readers ran on each family: O1 to O4 on one, and F1 to F4 on the other. The merged results are three documents at the repository root:

- `system-and-vault-collation-2026-09-25.md` sorts the vault's material into the kinds of material the design defines.
- `system-and-vault-beyond-the-design-2026-09-25.md` describes the vault material the design does not cover, the corrections that material makes to the design, and how the design would be extended to include it.
- `system-and-vault-left-to-populate-2026-09-25.md` lists the places the design defines that the vault does not yet fill, and who can fill each one.

**The author's level.** Every report stands at the level "proposed": it is a session's reading, which the author has not taken up. The readers could not write files, so each report came back as text and was saved here with one mechanical change. Eight wiki links that the vault's link check could not resolve (shortened note titles, two search patterns, and one quotation of a link to a note that no longer exists) were written out in full or as plain text, so that `scripts/queries/link-check.sh` passes. Reader O1's working sort of its region's 153 notes was not kept. Where the merged documents state a count or a quotation, it was checked against the vault on 2026-09-25. The reports themselves may contain small errors that the merged documents corrected; the collation lists the points on which the readers disagreed and how each was settled (its Part 10). By the design's rule that every part must serve the books, this folder belongs to the vault's own tools and records, not to the books' material.

**The plain-language rewrite.** On 2026-09-25, after the author said he could not follow much of the study's writing, a session rewrote the instructions and all eight reports in plain language, under the vault's rule against mannered prose (rule 13 of `ops/methodology/prose register.md`). Each rewrite keeps the reader's findings, counts, quotations, and paths; uses the design's current plain names; and names the parts of the design instead of citing its old line numbers. Where a reader's statement conflicts with what the vault shows, or with a finding of another reader, the rewrite says so in a short note, and a few small errors of detail are corrected in place. Every quotation in the rewritten files was checked against the original file or against the vault. The original wording is in the repository's history at commit `418f352`.
