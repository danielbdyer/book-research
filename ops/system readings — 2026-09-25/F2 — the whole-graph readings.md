# Report F2: the family files, which read the whole collection of notes

*About this version.* Reader F2 wrote this report on 2026-09-25, reading one region of the vault against the design in `reframe-map-and-system-2026-09-25.md`. On the same day, after the author said he could not follow much of the study's writing, a session rewrote the report in plain language. The rewrite keeps the reader's findings, counts, quotations, and paths, removes repetition, and uses the plain names the design now uses (topic, tradition or thinker, comparison, the author's view, writing technique, drafted passage, the author's level, the open questions, and the topic table; `system-and-vault-collation-2026-09-25.md`, Part 2, defines each). The reader's original wording is in the repository's history at commit `418f352`. Where the reader cited a line number in the design document, this version names the part instead, because the design has since been rewritten.

**Words used.** A *family file* is one of the eight files at the repository root that each read the whole collection of notes by one organizing idea: `the-loci.md` (standpoints), `the-concerns.md` (the book's concerns), `the-axioms.md` (principles), `the-constellation.md` (convergences among thinkers), `the-arc.md` (the book's sequence), `the-making.md` (how the book was made), `the-paradoxes.md` (tensions, by role), and `the-two-books.md` (the two books). The vault calls them lens families. `the-lenses.md` is their index; it has nineteen numbered sections, each a way of reading the notes, which it calls lenses (Lens 1, "The Arc," through Lens 19, "What It Is Not"). A *cache marker* is a hidden comment at the head of a section of a family file, naming the files the section was read from and a checksum of their contents, so that `scripts/queries/lens-check.sh` can report the section as out of date when those files change. *Coverage* is whether every claim is cited by at least one family file, and *grounding* is how many notes are supported by a reading of their source. Each family entry ends with an *open edge*, its list of what is still undecided. `the-conversation-catalog.md` is a list of the reframe conversation's ideas, sorted into groups A to K and marked by who said each one. A *topic map* is a page gathering the claims of one concern. *Company* means thinkers who reached the same finding independently; the book cites them as others who noticed the same thing, not as evidence that it is true. A *drafting document* (the vault calls it a concern surface) is a draft document in `drafts/` that assembles the material on one of the book's concerns and stops where the author's own contribution begins. A note is *nascent* when it is stated but not yet supported by a reading of its source, and *privated* when it is complete except for one named missing reading. A *convergence note* (the vault's word is isomorph) records that two or more traditions reached the same structure. A *thinker page* is the vault's page for a thinker (the vault calls it a chair); its `load:` field records what the book's argument needs the thinker for (its thesis, an opponent, an independent arrival at the same finding, company, a named practice, or a precedent). A *tension* is a disagreement inside the book's doctrine, kept open in a note rather than resolved. The *two-book table* in `the-two-books.md` sets ten topics side by side in their Book I and Book II forms; the vault calls it the Mirror. The places where the author has to decide between two readings of the book's structure are what the vault calls *joints*.

The region is the set of files that read the whole collection of notes: `the-lenses.md` and the eight family files; `the-conversation-catalog.md`; and four documents of 2026-08-20 and 2026-08-21 (`generative-core-harvest-2026-08-21.md`, `reframe-derivations-2026-08-20.md`, `reframe-deep-harvest-2026-08-20.md`, and `the-working-stance-2026-08-20.md`).

### 1b. The four things recorded for every kind of material, and the design's two lists

**The author's level.** The vault records three things close to the design's levels, and none of them is the levels themselves.

First, `state:` records how developed a claim is (`templates/claim-note.md`: "'nascent' is a seed — the proposition stated, not yet substantiated"; "'privated' is a complete claim incomplete in a specific named way"). The counts are nascent 157, privated 54, full 131, composting 1, and flourishing 0, and 343 of the 516 notes have no `state:` line at all.

Second, the documents about the reframe conversation mark who said each thing: the catalog's "*author-voiced* (a `## Prompt:` turn, primary material), *model-produced* (a `## Response:` turn, works consulted), or *mixed*," with a status of "*installed* ... *candidate* ... *self-disavowed-as-schema*" (`the-conversation-catalog.md`, line 5); the derivations document's key, "`[av]` = the author's own voice ... `[av-ratified]` ... `[model]` ... `[inferred]` = first-principles derivation beyond the source" (`reframe-derivations-2026-08-20.md`, line 5); and the harvest's rule that "**every quote and locator below is attributed-but-unverified until grounded**; minted notes mark it so and sit `nascent`" (`generative-core-harvest-2026-08-21.md`, line 5).

Third, the author's own acts are recorded where they happened: in rows of the table of settled questions; in the author's statements cited in `sources:` (43 notes have the author among their kinds of source, by the index); and in `reflections/` ("His words are inviolable," `reflections/README.md`), which 6 notes cite.

The design's six levels therefore correspond to the vault as follows. *Proposed* is everything a session wrote: all 502 notes that are not topic maps, by the design's own statement that everything in the design document is at the level "proposed." *Experienced* is the author's turns, statements, and reflections, which can be found through the sources but are not recorded as a level. *Inferred*, *believed*, and *claimed* have no field and no document. *Written* is `drafts/`. No note has a field for the author's level (`rg -l '^(scale|lens|standing): ' notes/` gives 0). The paradoxes file states what this costs: "every register statement below is model-produced ... so the register carries no author-voiced core, and its whole frontier is which of these the author actually holds" (`the-paradoxes.md`, line 3).

**Scale.** The index records the design's three scales and more. Lens 16, "The Fractal," lists "The seven rungs — facilitator↔participant · practitioner↔self · author↔reader · AI↔author · corpus↔future-self · tradition↔generations · ecology↔civilization," and "The three scales it implies — micro (self) / meso (relation) / macro (civilization, the horizon)" (`the-lenses.md`, lines 222–225); the catalog's group E has the same. Scale is used for reading, never recorded as a field: no note records a scale.

**Quadrant.** Section 8 of `the-loci.md` uses the four quadrants "as a coverage instrument rather than as an ontology," and states what they show about the book: "it over-weights *interior/individual* (felt experience, awareness) and under-weights *exterior/collective* (institutions, technology, civilization)" (`the-loci.md`, line 77). The claim [[wilber's four quadrants are a coordinate system, not an architecture]] is `full`. Nine notes mention a quadrant, and none is tagged with one. So the quadrants are used in reading but absent as a field, and the vault's demotion of them agrees with the design's ("become a coordinate system, rather than an ontology," transcript line 17834).

**Source.** The vault already records sources, in more detail than the design does. Every note has a `sources:` list with locations; every family file quotes the transcript by line and the corpus by section and page; completed readings cite a work by its own page numbers, with a reading record as the evidence; and the family files' cache markers record the sources of each reading itself: "a hidden marker naming the vault files it was read from and a checksum of their contents" (`the-axioms.md`, line 114). There are 48 markers across the eight files, all current by `lens-check.sh --stale` ("# no expired entries across the polyphony"). The vault has two things the design's source field lacks: the standing of each source document (descriptive, draft, or primary; `ops/methodology/source standing.md`), and the rule that "agreement among those three counts once, not three times" (`CLAUDE.md`).

**The commitments.** The axioms family file is the vault's list of commitments, organized by a division the author made himself: "a distinction I would allow here between the axioms of authorship and the axioms of what the book portends itself" (transcript line 526). It has three layers: the author's axioms (thirteen, "held backstage"); the work's axioms (fifteen); and the participant's layer, "deliberately un-axiomatized" ("The third layer's content is its emptiness, and that is the point," `the-axioms.md`, line 83). It adds nine named axioms, and a master axiom "held hedged." `ops/methodology/axiom register.md` lists seventeen principles, each checked against the vault, and finds that "Roughly four in five were already present in the vault before the conversation named them" (line 18).

Compared with the design's nine commitments:
- *Recognition belongs to the reader*: principle 1 of the list, and [[recognition is participatory, a move the reader makes rather than a content the reader receives]].
- *No standpoint has authority over the others*: the loci file's one rule, and [[no single locus or framework may become sovereign over the reality it describes]].
- *What is encountered may change the framework*: the list's candidate principle that a framework changes itself, "the only one of the three the vault had already enacted before the conversation named it" (line 46), and [[a framework is transformed by what its own encounters disclose]].
- *A difference is understood before a likeness is claimed*: principle 7, [[the comparative method does not collapse a correspondence before its difference is understood]], and `ops/methodology/both halves of a correspondence.md`.
- *The author is the first participant*: the axioms file's central principle, and [[the author is the first participant and must undergo the transformation the book asks of the reader]].
- *The structure exists for the sake of encounters*: the axioms file's "The system serves the inquiry rather than becoming it," and [[the system exists to help the author remain in relationship with the inquiry]].
- *Relationship can increase sovereignty*: principle 10, and the claim with that title.

Seven of the nine are already present. Two are partial. *Form directs attention, and the meaning is left to the reader* is recorded only as rules for facilitators in Lens 14 ("invitation ≠ direction ... responsible for conditions, not conclusions") and as [[reflection that returns authorship differs from reflection that takes it]], never as a commitment with a question to ask of a draft. *The two poles stay unranked* is recorded as the author's own "Bubble" ("Contraction is normalized *before* any doctrine is built," `the-conversation-catalog.md`, line 33) and as [[seeing the frame that holds both self and whole frees one from a double bind]], a claim rather than a commitment.

The design has what the vault's list lacks: for each commitment, a question a drafting session can ask of a draft. The axioms file asks "what does holding this protect, and what does it foreclose?" ### 1c. Connections

## 4. The three findings that matter most

**1. The eight family files are the design's kinds of material already built as ways of reading, at a different level of detail, and what they lack is a record of what the books need.** The concerns file is the design's topic, filled with the doctrine's six concerns (the design has fifteen, from the transcript). The loci file is the design's tradition or thinker, one level up (standpoints that group thinkers). The axioms file is the design's list of commitments, with the author's division into three layers, which the design lacks. The two-books file is nearly the design's record for a book, lacking only the promise to the reader and how much method each book shows. The constellation file is the design's comparison, prepared by sessions. The paradoxes file is the design's polarity, with a field for roles that the design lacks. The family files also record four bodies of material the design cannot record: the arc's phases and its parallel readings, the versions of the manuscript and their differences, the history of the book's making, and the vault's methods for keeping its account of the company honest. And the vault has twice declined to adopt the conversation's list of kinds as its own structure ("leaves aside the entity machinery the conversation set on the 'what we should not build' list," `the-lenses.md`, line 7; "The diagram was produced on request and disavowed on delivery. The vault does not install it as schema," `ops/methodology/ecology names a movement not a taxonomy.md`). So the design should neither absorb the family files nor become a structure that every note must follow. It should be treated as one more family file in form, written by hand and checked by the same cache markers, with a purpose no other family file has: it is the only one that reads the notes by what the books need rather than by what the notes contain. The vault's own observation of 2026-08-04 states why that reading is missing ("None of them asks what a drafting session will *need*"). Two of the design's fields should be added to the notes themselves, because the family files need them: the author's level (section 3.3), and, for tensions, whose tension it is (section 2D).

**2. The author's level is the one field every family file needs and none can record.** The paradoxes file's "whole frontier is which of these the author actually holds"; the axioms file's open edge is "the provenance split itself"; the two-books file calls its entries "model-elaborated, still awaiting the author's own words"; and the constellation file has no author's note on any comparison. The counts agree: 125 notes are based only on the reframe conversation, 43 have the author among their kinds of source, 0 have a field for the author's level, and 157 are nascent. The vault records how developed a claim is and who first said it; it has nowhere to record that the author has experienced, inferred, believed, or claimed it. Only he raises a level, and the vault has no place to record that he has done so. This is also why the design's columns Comparison and What it hides looked empty: the material is plentiful, but the author's own comparisons and statements of what a framework hides are absent (correction 7).

**3. The primary material settles two disagreements against the design and one against the vault.** Against the design: the books' concerns are the six that the poems contain ([[all six doctrine concerns appear in the first fifteen poems]], full), not the transcript's fifteen; and the books need a kind of material for the reader's own test, because "nothing enters the lattice that the body has not countersigned" is the corpus's own rule, and the design, built from a transcript in which the felt shift "is never author-ratified," cannot record it. The list of what the center cannot see also keeps the opposite blind spot in view ("the cry that tests no proposition"). Against the vault: the design's record of each book's order must allow two dramaturgies (the author's decision of 2026-08-16), and the design's condition about the false peak is the right constraint on the arc the author gave Book II, since he noticed the false peak himself (transcript line 286) before any scholar did: "The false peak is the author's own sighting" (`reframe-deep-harvest-2026-08-20.md`, line 102).
