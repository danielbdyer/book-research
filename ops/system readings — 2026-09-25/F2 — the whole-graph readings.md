### 1b. Shared fields and registers

**Standing.** The vault records three different things near the design's ladder, and none is the ladder. First, `state:` records *how developed* a claim is (`templates/claim-note.md`: "'nascent' is a seed — the proposition stated, not yet substantiated"; "'privated' is a complete claim incomplete in a specific named way"); the counts are nascent 157, privated 54, full 131, composting 1, flourishing 0, and 343 of 516 notes carry no state line at all. Second, the reframe surfaces carry a *who-said-it* tag: the catalog's "*author-voiced* (a `## Prompt:` turn, primary material), *model-produced* (a `## Response:` turn, works consulted), or *mixed*" with a standing of "*installed* ... *candidate* ... *self-disavowed-as-schema*" (`the-conversation-catalog.md`, line 5); the derivations' legend "`[av]` = the author's own voice ... `[av-ratified]` ... `[model]` ... `[inferred]` = first-principles derivation beyond the source" (`reframe-derivations-2026-08-20.md`, line 5); and the harvest's rule that "**every quote and locator below is attributed-but-unverified until grounded**; minted notes mark it so and sit `nascent`" (`generative-core-harvest-2026-08-21.md`, line 5). Third, the author's own acts are recorded where they happened: decisions rows, author statements cited in `sources:` (43 notes carry an author provenance class by the index), and `reflections/` ("His words are inviolable," `reflections/README.md`), cited by 6 notes. The design's six rungs therefore meet the vault as: *surfaced* = everything a session wrote (all 502 non-map notes, by the design's own rule that "This document itself stands at surfaced"); *encountered* = the author's turns, statements and reflections, recoverable from provenance but not written as a level; *inferred*, *believed*, *claimed* = no field, no surface; *written* = `drafts/`. No note carries a standing field (`rg -l '^(scale|lens|standing): ' notes/` → 0). The Paradoxes family says exactly what this costs: "every register statement below is model-produced ... so the register carries no author-voiced core, and its whole frontier is which of these the author actually holds" (`the-paradoxes.md`, line 3).

**Scale.** The index holds the design's three levels and more: Lens 16 "The Fractal" gives "The seven rungs — facilitator↔participant · practitioner↔self · author↔reader · AI↔author · corpus↔future-self · tradition↔generations · ecology↔civilization" and "The three scales it implies — micro (self) / meso (relation) / macro (civilization, the horizon)" (`the-lenses.md`, lines 222–225); the catalog's Family E carries the same. It is a lens, never a field: no note records a scale.

**Lens (the quadrants).** The Loci's §8 holds the four quadrants "used here as a coverage instrument rather than as an ontology" and states the book's own result under it: "it over-weights *interior/individual* (felt experience, awareness) and under-weights *exterior/collective* (institutions, technology, civilization)" (`the-loci.md`, line 77). The claim `[[wilber's four quadrants are a coordinate system, not an architecture]]` is `full`. Nine notes mention a quadrant; none is tagged by one. Native as a reading, absent as a field, and the vault's demotion agrees with the design's ("become a coordinate system, rather than an ontology," 17834).

**Provenance.** Native and richer than the design's. Every note carries a `sources:` array with a location; every family quotes the transcript by line and the corpus by section and page; completed readings cite a work's own locators with a reading record as trace; and the families' cache markers are a provenance of the *reading itself* — "a hidden marker naming the vault files it was read from and a checksum of their contents" (`the-axioms.md`, line 114), 48 markers across the eight families, all fresh at `lens-check.sh --stale` ("# no expired entries across the polyphony"). What the design's Provenance lacks and the vault has: the *standing of a source* (descriptive, draft, primary; `ops/methodology/source standing.md`) and the rule that "agreement among those three counts once, not three times" (`CLAUDE.md`).

**Commitments.** The Axioms family is the register, built on the author's own sort: "a distinction I would allow here between the axioms of authorship and the axioms of what the book portends itself" (526), rendered as three layers — the author's axioms (thirteen, "held backstage"), the work's axioms (fifteen), and the participant's layer, "deliberately un-axiomatized" ("The third layer's content is its emptiness, and that is the point," `the-axioms.md`, line 83) — plus nine named axioms and a master axiom "held hedged". `ops/methodology/axiom register.md` holds seventeen reconciled orientations and finds that "Roughly four in five were already present in the vault before the conversation named them" (line 18).
