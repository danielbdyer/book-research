## What was measured, and how

Five surveys ran in parallel on 2026-08-09, each read-only, each reading the notes and files themselves rather than searching them. The first sorted all 310 files in `notes/` by what each is about, distinguishing a proposition about the book's subject from a proposition about the vault's own machinery. The second measured where the graph's mass sits against the two centerpieces the author named on 2026-08-02, counting words as well as files. The third judged all sixty-four thinker notes against the deliverable that `ops/methodology/center of gravity.md` specifies for them, which is one quotable finding plus the refusal worth keeping. The fourth looked for places where the graph says one thing twice and for notes too thin to hold a file. The fifth inventoried the whole operational layer, every file under `ops/` and `.claude/`, against what actually reads each one.

Three headline counts were verified independently of the surveys, by direct count: `notes/` holds 310 files and 244,954 words; `ops/` and `.claude/` together hold 202,591 words; the four files in `drafts/` hold 9,654 words.

## The finding, before the detail

The notes are the healthy part of this vault and the machinery around them is not.

The first survey sorted all 310 notes and gave a disposition to each. It marked none of them for cutting. Its verdict was that the admission standard has plainly been applied file by file, that declines are recorded, and that the audit surfaces sit correctly in `ops/` with their reasons written into their own headers. The fourth survey independently reached the compatible result: executing every merge and cut it could justify removes fifteen to nineteen files and about 5,000 words, which is six percent of the files and two percent of the words. Every note in the graph has at least one link in and at least one link out; there is no orphan.

Against that, the fifth survey found that 619 KB of the 1.30 MB in `ops/` and `.claude/` is the vault describing or maintaining its own operation, which is forty-seven percent. Everything the vault has ever drafted toward the manuscript is 56.5 KB.

So the author's suspicion is half right, and the half that is right is not where he pointed it. There is very little navel-gazing in `notes/` — twenty-one files out of 310, and those are misfiled rather than worthless. There is a great deal of it in the apparatus.

## The imbalance, stated in numbers

The two centerpieces are the convergence of thinkers and the lived practice. Measured by words, the convergence holds 103,614 and the practice holds 35,524, which looks like three to one. That ratio understates the problem by an order of magnitude, because most of what falls under the practice is not practice. The material that says what a practitioner actually does — the posture defined as a point of return, its six simultaneous components, the twelve forms with their triggers, the design law that the practices are the author's room-holding craft scaled to one reader, and the clause that an exercise justified by its results has already broken itself — is six notes and 4,332 words, which is 1.7 percent of the graph. The remaining 31,192 words under that heading are the epistemology of the test the practice is verified by, and the hazards a practitioner might meet. Set against practice as practice, the convergence outweighs it twenty-four to one.

Three further measurements sharpen it. The sixty-four thinker notes hold 55,121 words, which outweighs the book's whole doctrine, its whole demoted system vocabulary, and every comparison between its seven source documents added together. Of those sixty-four thinkers, four rest on a completed reading of the thinker's own work, and fifty-one have no contact with the thinker's own writing at all. The demoted system vocabulary permanently occupies one of the twelve topic maps and the lived practice owns none of them; the practice material lodges inside the map for the verification standard.

One thing the practice measurement is not is a discovery. The vault already recorded it, in the extraction queue's amendment of 2026-08-08 and in the directive at `ops/methodology/the chain of understanding.md`, which states that the roster grew to sixty-four while the lived practice was carried by three notes. What the survey adds is that the number is worse than three when the epistemology is separated out, and that the reason is documented: the book's own draft defers the practice instructions to a companion volume, and the vault's note records the fact that decides it, which is that the deferred-to volume does not exist.

## Where the surveys disagree, and how I read it

The second survey recommends cutting the thirty thinker notes that rest on nothing outside the research documents commissioned for this project, on the ground that 21,511 words assert what a reading would confirm. The third survey recommends the opposite and states the reason: those notes report their own standing accurately, twenty of them are already entries in the reading queue, and a cut on unread status would take four of the seven thinkers the author has actually seated.

The third survey is right that cutting is wrong, and the second is right that the current state cannot stand. A thinker note that says honestly "derived rather than sourced, and here is the page-level address of the reading that would settle it" is a link in the chain of belief, which is the unit the 2026-08-08 directive makes the research's deliverable. Deleting it destroys a research question. But the same note presented at nine hundred words, with a proposed seat, a proposed load, and a full evidence apparatus, reads as finished research when it is a hypothesis.

A third option survives both recommendations. The vault changes what those notes are rather than deciding whether they exist: a thinker note with no contact with the thinker's own work is compressed to the reading question it actually is, which means the finding it proposes, the refusal it holds, the page-level address, and the stake. That preserves every question, removes the appearance of settled evidence, and recovers most of the words. It is the subject of stage four below.

# The working ethos

Six commitments, each derived from a decision the author has already made, and each stated so it can decide a case rather than only be agreed with.

**The unit of value is a link in the chain, not an entry in a collection.** This restates the directive of 2026-08-08, that the research builds the chain of belief needed for sensible thinking about the book's largest choices. A note justifies itself by naming the belief above it that it supports and the decision below it that it unblocks. Material that names neither waits, and waits without shame.

**Depth at the primary layer outranks breadth at the secondary one.** One thinker read at their own text outranks twenty characterized from the seven source documents, because the directive at `ops/methodology/the primary material.md` says the corpus's account of a thinker cannot outrank the thinker. Four of sixty-four thinker notes currently rest on a reading. That ratio is the research's actual state, and the roster's size conceals it.

**The two centerpieces get proportional mass.** Six notes carrying one of the two things the author said the book is, against sixty-four carrying the other, is not a scheduling backlog. It is the research base failing to represent half of its own subject.

**The vault does not study itself.** This is the first sorting consequence of the admission standard at `ops/methodology/writing test.md`, already written and not being followed at the margin. When the vault finds a flaw in its own sorting, the output is the smallest record that stops the flaw recurring — never a claim note, plus an audit, plus an instrument that watches the audit.

**An instrument is a cost that must be re-earned.** Every standing surface, register, script and skill charges attention at every session start. One that nothing has read since the day it was written is deleted rather than maintained. The vault has one worked example of this behaviour already: the session that built the third reach-list stopped voluntarily and recorded why, that its findings had "started to agree with the reading queue rather than tell it anything new," and the fourth was never run.

**A new surface is built by exchange, not by addition.** This is the clause the rule of 2026-08-07 was missing. That rule excluded new skills, surfaces and scripts outright, and three new surfaces landed the next day — the roll-up of what the material asserts, the correspondence program, and the forest panel's curated results — every one of them at the author's own request. A prohibition that his own next request overrides is not a rule. The workable form is that a new surface names what it retires: the file it replaces, or the maintenance that stops being done to pay for it.

# The plan

Five stages, ordered so that the largest gain comes first and the riskiest judgment comes last. Stages one and two need no decision about the book. Stages three, four and five each need one, and each is marked.

## Stage one — the machinery. About 316 KB, and it touches no research

Three removals, all inside `ops/` and `.claude/`, none of which changes a proposition.

**Delete the upstream text inside the sixteen generated skills, keeping each skill's vault-override paragraph and its genuine procedure. This is 313 KB, which is 95.9 percent of the generated skill files.** The override paragraphs already state this vault's real queue, note shape, status vocabulary and map anatomy. Beneath them sit instructions that contradict those overrides — the extraction skill's stock text says under a heading reading "THE MISSION (READ THIS OR YOU WILL FAIL)" that judgment "must err toward extraction, not rejection," 1,110 lines below an override requiring strict admission — plus forty-nine references to four paths and commands that do not exist in this repository. `CLAUDE.md` already states that where stock text conflicts with the vault, the vault wins, so the deletion enacts a rule that is already written.

**Change the register's write-time gate from blocking to reporting, keeping the detector.** The gate at `.claude/hooks/pre-write-register.sh` refuses a file write when the pattern detector fires, and the vault's own count of its record is fourteen false catches against four true ones. That count is now seventeen against four, because the gate blocked the first two attempts to write this file, on three sentences that each carry a named subject and a finite verb. A reporting twin running the identical detector already exists at `.claude/hooks/post-bash-register.sh`. The observation that recorded the earlier count also recorded the cost, and the sentence is worth keeping because it is the argument: each forced rewrite "took a sentence that read well and replaced it with one chosen to satisfy a regular expression, which is a second author in the room with a vote and no stake in the meaning." Keeping the detector keeps all four true catches.

**Drop the stamp-check from every turn to session start alone, and delete the nine empty session-tracking files with the maintenance condition that counts them.** The verifier at `scripts/queries/countermeasure.sh` performs 115 separate grep invocations per run to confirm that six files still contain ten phrases, and it runs on every turn; a fifty-turn session spends 5,865 grep invocations on it. The six files it checks change about weekly. Separately, the nine files in `ops/sessions/` hold a session identifier, a timestamp and the word "active" and no other content — one holds the literal identifier `test`, and two pairs share identifiers — while the session hook counts them and fires a recommendation to mine them for methodology corrections at five or more. That recommendation has been firing at every session start against files with nothing in them to mine.

## Stage two — five merges that fix a correctness defect, not a volume problem

Five thinker evaluations each have a claim note beside them restating the same finding in nearly the same sentences: Polanyi, Hübl, de Jaegher with Di Paolo, Alexander, and Wittgenstein. The Wittgenstein pair states the same sentence about *Philosophical Investigations* §129 twice; the Hübl pair repeats a sentence word for word.

The argument for acting is not the 2,450 words recovered. It is that one of the five has already gone stale in exactly the way the arrangement invites. The claim note on Thomas Hübl records in bold that his independence certification failed the blind repeat check of 2026-08-06, because his documented formation includes teachers the certification excluded. His evaluation note was never updated. It still says he reached the book's concept "through trauma work with no Kashmir Śaiva metaphysics under him," and its footer still describes the independence test as one "his arrival passes." A drafting session opening that note today would read a withdrawn certification as current. Each merge closes one more instance of that risk.

**One cost, which is the author's to weigh rather than to inherit.** Four of the five claim notes carry `category: isomorph`, the value marking a structure reached twice independently, and the clustering query that produces `ops/convergence points.md` reads that field. Folding them into thinker notes removes four independent arrivals from the convergence count. Since the convergence is a named centerpiece, a census that shrinks for filing reasons is a real loss, and the alternative — keeping the claim note and stripping the evaluation back to its seat, works, recommendation and refusal — recovers less text and preserves the count.

## Stage three — nineteen misfiled notes leave `notes/` for `ops/`. This needs one decision

The first survey found twenty-one files whose subject is the vault's own machinery rather than the book, and marked nineteen for demotion and one for merging. They fall into three groups, and each group has a mechanism behind it.

**Twelve are the unfilled stubs minted so that an operational table would be reachable from the graph.** The inventory of undeveloped territory at `ops/open corpus.md` states the reason in its own words: a table row "is not reachable by /reflect and is not traversable from the graph, which made this file a leaf that nothing would consume." Each stub therefore exists to make a row findable. They average 154 words, none has an evidence section, eight quote nothing, and each asserts an absence in the corpus rather than a proposition about the subject. Every one is already a row in the inventory carrying the same finding. Graph reachability is a maintenance reason, not a writing reason.

**Four are the notes an instrument wrote about itself.** When the `load:` axis was built on 2026-08-05 to record what the book cannot lose, four of its first outputs took the sorting rather than the sorted as their subject. The clearest is the note calling the research instrument a violin rather than an oracle, which contains a substantiation heading beginning "Why it is a claim in `notes/` rather than only an operational note" — a note arguing for its own admission is a note that failed the test.

**Three are surfaces that duplicate `ops/`.** The vocabulary crosswalk maps most of its rows to pipeline phases rather than to book content. The methods map spends 2,243 of its 3,150 words on prose about house rules and the source registry, and the research-frontier map spends 2,067 of 2,768 words describing the outward research program while listing only 24 of the 43 notes that declare it. The second says so about itself: "This map's reading program now lives at `ops/reading queue.md`."

**The decision this needs.** Moving the vocabulary crosswalk and the two prose maps requires a change to `CLAUDE.md`, which currently sites all three in `notes/` by design, and the two maps still need somewhere to gather their 26 and 43 member notes. My recommendation is to move the twelve stubs and the four instrument notes now, and to hold the three navigation surfaces until the map structure is decided, because that is a change to how the graph is entered rather than a cut.

## Stage four — the roster is compressed, not cut. This needs one decision

Fifty-one of the sixty-four thinker notes have no contact with the thinker's own writing. Seventeen rest on the seven source documents alone, which is precisely the material the primary-material directive says cannot outrank the thinker. Thirty-four rest on analyses commissioned for this project, and twenty-three of those open their refusal section with the same four-word admission, "Derived rather than sourced." Three of the seven seats the author has actually filled rest entirely on the book's own paraphrase of their occupant.

The roster's honesty is not in question and should be said plainly: every one of the sixty-four carries a refusal, which the survey judged the most consistently executed property in the whole graph, and every unread note states its own standing.

The proposal is that a thinker note with no contact with the thinker's own work is rewritten to the four things it actually holds — the finding it proposes, the refusal it keeps, the page-level address of the reading that would settle it, and the stake in that reading — and loses the proposed seat and the full evidence apparatus until a reading exists. Eight of the sixty-four are pure roster entries by the third survey's judgment, each conceding in its own words that its deliverable is a reading not yet done; those eight are the natural first batch, and Erikson, Kurt Lewin and the experiential learning cycle are the clearest, since no claim note links to any of them.

**The decision this needs.** Seventeen thinkers are load-bearing, meaning a named claim breaks without them, and thirty-nine supply one quotable finding. Whether the compression applies to all fifty-one unread notes or only to the eight roster entries is a judgment about how much apparent settledness the author wants the base to carry, and it is his.

## Stage five — the hole, which is not pruning and is flagged as such

Nothing in stages one through four raises the practice from six notes. Filling it is new work, it competes with the reading queue, and it is listed here only so the plan does not present itself as complete.

Two things make it cheaper than it looks. The extraction pass for the practice curriculum's second half — the four diagnostic moves of the engineer's eye, the crisis triage, channeling and the Focusing protocol — needs no outside reading, because the material is in the executed draft already, and it is already queued at `ops/queue/extractions.md` and already elevated to stand beside the reading queue. And the most operational practice material the vault holds is not in the graph at all: the author's own facilitation record, which is his Authentic Relating training in Houston, the community he held there, and the event of roughly thirty-five people in May 2026 run from five agreements with trio rotations, a witness role and a closing circle, currently sits as one bullet inside a topic map. The centerpiece is named as the lived practice verified in the body, and the only body in the record is his.

# What must not be touched

**The three practice notes stay at three and are not merged to two.** Two of them are close enough to tempt a merge, and they are the entire holding of a named centerpiece; cutting to two would worsen the one imbalance that matters most by acting on it.

**The record of what the practice curriculum lacks is a work order, not waste.** The finding that only three of twelve forms name a signal a practitioner could check, and that the missing instruction was deferred to a volume that does not exist, is the most actionable sentence in the vault.

**The register of seventy-nine settled questions at `ops/decisions.md` is untouched**, because `CLAUDE.md` instructs every session to check it before treating an apparent inconsistency as work, and the file records what happened when closure was invisible: one fresh session spent itself re-litigating the notation glyphs.

**Four methodology directives are not duplicated anywhere and must survive**: the author's statement of 2026-08-04 on where the book stands in three confidence tiers; his directive of 2026-08-08 that completeness is never the standard; the five properties that make a research question sharp; and his instruction that propositions carry the names and images they need. Eleven of the nineteen directives are fully restated in `CLAUDE.md` and may be consolidated. These four are not, and one of them is his most recent.

**Every directive quotes the author's own words, and those quotations survive even where the rule around them is duplicated.** A duplicated rule can be consolidated. The sentence he said and the occasion that produced it cannot, because that sentence is the evidence the rule rests on.

**The log of flagged sentences at `ops/register violations.md` survives whatever happens to the enforcement apparatus around it**, since it is the only place his five corrections are quoted in his own words.

**The eleven crossover thinker notes at 386 to 500 words each look thin and are not**, because each carries a quotable finding, a kept refusal, and an explicit statement that its primary work is unread.

**Fourteen groups of notes look like duplicates in any similarity search and preserve real distinctions**, and the fourth survey lists them with the distinction each keeps. One of them protects itself in its own text, saying in bold that the two mechanisms it separates are genuinely different and the vault should not merge them.

# What needs the author's decision before anything moves

1. Whether stage one proceeds as a whole. It touches no research and no proposition, and it is the largest single reduction available.
2. Which direction the five merges in stage two run, given that merging into the thinker notes costs four entries in the convergence count.
3. Whether the three navigation surfaces in stage three move, which requires a `CLAUDE.md` change and a decision about where two maps gather their members.
4. Whether the compression in stage four applies to all fifty-one unread thinker notes or only to the eight roster entries.
5. Whether the practice extraction in stage five is scheduled now or held, since it is additive work competing with the reading queue he has already made central.

One thing is deliberately absent from this plan. No stage proposes a new script, a new check, or a new standing surface to prevent the regrowth it removes. The exchange rule in the ethos above is a rule and not an instrument, and the evidence that a rule can hold without machinery is already in the record: the reach-list that stopped itself did so because a session read its own output and judged it, with nothing enforcing anything.
