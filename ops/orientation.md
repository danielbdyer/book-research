# Orientation

## The center

The book's governing orientation, by the author's decision, is the Ecology of Recognition: the inquiry is an ecology of loci with none sovereign, and the book is one non-sovereign locus within it, auxiliary to a sovereign reader rather than the source or certifier of the reader's recognition (the working name is recorded at `ops/methodology/the center held as layers.md`; the decision at `ops/decisions.md`). Facilitation stands two steps back from the outcome, tending the conditions for the reader's own facilitation rather than producing a transformation, and the subject beneath every lens is the conditions under which a person remains the author of what they become while being genuinely changed.

Within that ground the centerpieces stand unchanged as the content's center: the convergence of thinkers — independent arrivals at the same finding, quoted with their refusals intact — and the lived practice, verified in the body (`ops/methodology/center of gravity.md`). They sit within the ecology now rather than supplying its floor. The felt shift is one cultivated locus's trained noticing, an instrument the reader uses rather than the book's whole warrant — the apparatus that had grown up defending it as evidence was excised in the epistemic audit of 2026-08-16, because the book points at an external check and never claims to be evidence (`ops/methodology/decoration and critique are shelf material.md`). The schemes — the triad, the primitives, the molecules, the loops — stay demoted from architecture to instrument, so gaps and overlaps in the scheme set are never work; and Kashmir Śaivism is named the book's deepest well, disciplined by the dated record that the triad predates the author's contact with the tradition.

The research is about the primary material, not the seven documents in `sources/`: "this research is and has always been about the primary material… not the sources I've provided" (`ops/methodology/the primary material.md`). A thinker's own work is primary; the corpus's account of that thinker is not; where the two conflict the primary material governs and the conflict is recorded. Beneath the corpus sit the layers it quotes — the author's poems and the provenance record — where he said the plain things first and the scholarship arrived afterward and dressed them. The vault is measured by what reaches the manuscript.

## What is being worked now

The work is two books, projections of one ecology differing by locus of practice, and Book I — the facilitator book — is written first (`ops/methodology/the two-book architecture.md`). Its dramaturgy is the reframe's four movements toward the facilitator's withdrawal; the psychedelic arc toward a peak is Book II's. The transcend-and-include re-grounding is executed, so the graph and the maps now take their footing from the ecology ground rather than holding it beside the old frame, and the pilot letter is sent — the answerability clock the whole vault ripened toward has struck once. Book II's near-complete material is banked and ripening while Book I is written.

The lead is Book I's own foundations, and its relational bench is now read at the primary texts and grounded, so the lead turns from grounding that bench to building Book I's writing-ready surfaces — the durable-perspective layer the Recognition surface prototyped in `drafts/`, which assembles a concern and then withdraws at the crossing rather than stating the reader's own move. The next such surface is answerability, and it is built only after deep immersion in the research: primarily the conversation artifact (`the-conversation-catalog.md` and the reframe transcript), then the lens families, then down into the notes. Grounding the remaining gauge-moving reading clusters stays the honest background, and the author's own heart-spoken soundings in `reflections/` supply the withdrawn center each surface needs. The detailed current work and its ordering are the torch in `ops/standing-consequences.md`. Author-gated and never jumped: Book I's drafting itself, which is his to begin; the residual seatings whose evaluations name a reading or a load rather than a seat; the naming refactor; the remaining tension resolutions; placements; release; and Book I's own form and grammatical person ([[seventeen chapters rest at madhyama]] resolved by the pilot's sending; the spatial-figure fork and the title's final pick stay his).

## The safeguards

- **Check `ops/decisions.md` before treating an apparent inconsistency as work.** The vault renders openness loudly and closure quietly, so several deliberate choices look like defects at first sight; query the register for the topic before acting (`scripts/queries/decided.sh <topic>`).
- **Silence is health.** Quiet maintenance conditions at session start are the healthy state, and the hook says so when nothing fires; the pending lists in `ops/queue/` are deliberately long and are not a fired condition.
- **The primary material governs the corpus**, convergence is held as observation and never as proof (`ops/methodology/authorial stance.md`; no efficacy is postulated), and every correspondence shows both halves — both sides quoted, or the missing side named with a page-level address, never from memory (`ops/methodology/both halves of a correspondence.md`).
- **Write with research diction, plainly**, so the information is never lost in the prose (CLAUDE.md; `ops/methodology/prose register.md`): named subjects and finite verbs, references that carry their content, abstract nouns expanded into the thing itself, figures of speech only inside quotation marks, and every file reread as the reader it will meet. Complexity that delivers is welcome; compression that withholds is not. Drifted prose is repaired with `/recompose`.
