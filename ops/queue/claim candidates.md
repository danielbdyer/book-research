## The reader's side of the kept tensions, 2026-08-06

Ten reader-facing moves, one per tension, from the same sweep — the first candidates for the genre the tensions registry says is missing (every recorded treatment is an authorial move). 1. *Felt shift versus felt confirmation* — on a click, offer the felt sense two or three of your own paraphrases, one deliberately slightly wrong, and watch for a refusal; agreement can only say yes, and the refusal event is the verification. Re-contact the surviving formulation next day.
2. *Beauty recruits the wrong posture* — the underline is the trigger: on catching the collector's impulse, perform what the admired sentence describes for one minute before reading on; the check runs on the doing, not the sentence.
3. *Forward hunger defeats the pause* — place the gate at maximal hunger: mark the spot, say aloud what the next page should deliver, close the book until tomorrow; check whether the pull survives the night and whether the expectation matched.
4. *The reader's resistance has nowhere to land* — stage your own refusal: decline one practice aloud in your own words, write the objection in the margin, reread it aloud next day; the whole move verifies by whether reading can continue after the no.
5. *A codex is a one-chair technology* — re-read a click-producing passage aloud toward one named person and run the felt check twice, solitary and witnessed; a divergence is data.
6. *Surrender and will* — at a live fork, speak both instructions aloud in your own words and watch for the felt sense to refuse one; if neither is refused, shrink the horizon to the next hour and re-contact tomorrow — a returned-nothing is the muted signal, not a failed reading.
7. *Dissolution versus regression* — the one move that refuses introspection by design: before the peak material, write one named person and one concrete signal ("if X happens, I call"); during the difficult stretch, write one plain sentence naming what is happening and check next day whether it still describes it.
8. *Introspective access* — answer every body check in one bit, moved or did not move, and write nothing else; the constraint is checkable (did describing words creep in?) and a real movement stays moved on return.
9. *A developmental scheme becomes a ranking* — catch-and-convert at the moment of use: say the ranking sentence aloud, restate it as the machinery question ("what is the machinery doing in that situation?"), and feel the difference between the verdict and the question.
10. *The compound diagnoses may relocate a structural condition* — before working on a configuration, speak both addresses aloud — "I could not rest" and "rest was not on offer there" — and check which sentence the body countersigns; look outward once before working inward.
