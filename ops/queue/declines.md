# Declines

Material that was read and set aside, with the reason. The admission standard in `notes/methods.md` requires this record: "Misses count as data" (first-telling: the instrument), and a declined candidate keeps its note and its reason.

A decline is not a deferral. An entry in `ops/queue/extractions.md` or `ops/queue/candidates.md` is future work that has not been done; an entry here is a decision that was made. Keeping them apart is what stops the next pass from re-litigating the same material. A decline can be reversed — the source is unchanged and every entry names its location — but reversing one is a decision too, and the reversal is recorded in place of the original reason.

Format: `- **what** (location) — why declined.`

Each is sustained second-person address that establishes mood without stating a proposition the vault could record or a mechanism a future chapter could be built against. They remain the strongest available evidence of the voice the author names a high-water mark, and the pages that do carry propositions are queued separately in `ops/queue/extractions.md`. Declined as claims, retained as register evidence.

**Self-evaluation, excluded by directive 4.**

- **The treatment's per-chapter "jewel" verdicts** (`arc-gloss`, all fifteen chapters) — including "the most-underlined page in the book," "already written and perfect," and "un-improvable" (arc-gloss: ch6, ch11). These are the manuscript grading itself and carry no evidential weight under `ops/derivation.md` directive 4 and `ops/methodology/craft evidence.md`. Where a verdict sat on top of a checkable mechanism, the mechanism was extracted instead and the verdict discarded; see `notes/the minus sign is what makes a molecule formula prescriptive.md`.
- **The abridged edition's claims for its own framework** (`abridged: prologue`, `abridged ch4`) — that the architecture is structurally sufficient, that the reader has been performing the Triad their whole life, and that the naming lands as relief. Declined under source standing: a draft is quoted for what it says and not credited for what it argues.

**Restatement of literature the vault does not need in its own voice.**

- **The abridged edition's research summaries** (`abridged ch7`, `abridged ch11`, `abridged ch12`) — the polyvagal states, the window of tolerance, James's four marks, REBUS, the default mode network, and the reopened critical period. These are competent summaries of published work, and recording them as claims would put secondhand accounts of other people's research into a vault whose claims are about this book. The thinkers themselves are queued for evaluation as candidates instead, which is where the admission standard can be applied to them properly.
