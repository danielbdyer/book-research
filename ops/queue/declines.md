# Declines

Material that was read and set aside, with the reason. The admission standard in `notes/methods.md` requires this record: "Misses count as data" (first-telling: the instrument), and a declined candidate keeps its note and its reason.

A decline is not a deferral. An entry in `ops/queue/extractions.md` or `ops/queue/candidates.md` is future work that has not been done; an entry here is a decision that was made. Keeping them apart is what stops the next pass from re-litigating the same material. A decline can be reversed — the source is unchanged and every entry names its location — but reversing one is a decision too, and the reversal is recorded in place of the original reason.

Format: `- **what** (location) — why declined.`

## The seventeen trial chapters, whole (2026-08-04)

**The entire set, declined as intake by the author** (held in his local storage; five were sent and read, the Prologue and Chapters One through Four) — the vault stays with the research it already holds rather than consuming the drafts. This is a decline of the whole seventeen, not of the five that were read, and it closes the extraction entry that had stood since 2026-08-02.

Their standing made the decline cheap: another AI wrote them, the author had not read them entire, and he did not cosign them, so they were never evidence that the book had said anything. Two findings from the batch of five are recorded here only so that no later pass re-proposes the set as an unlock. They are built with the conceptual schemes as their spine — one chapter exists to install the three-part frame and another to pre-verify its count as exactly three — against a directive that demotes those schemes to instruments claiming neither completeness nor non-overlap. And their content is almost entirely the arc the abridged edition already executes; what was genuinely new was apparatus rather than doctrine, chiefly an executed marginal layer, a per-unit dial map, and a stated policy of naming lineage inside the body text.

One observation about the drafts is recorded without an inference attached, because the inference a session drew from it was wrong and the author corrected it the same day: the chapters are written in immersive second person and carry no authorial *I* anywhere. That is a fact about the drafts. It is not evidence that the book will be written in the first person, and it was not part of why the set was declined. Whether the book says *I* or *you* is open (`ops/methodology/the book's current shape.md`, tier three).

Nothing from them is in this repository, and nothing from them entered the graph. Reversing this is the author's decision and would be recorded here in place of this reason.

## The twelve open-territory stubs (2026-08-09)

**All twelve are declined as claims, and each one's finding is preserved here in full.** They were created on 2026-08-01 so that the rows of `ops/open corpus.md`, the inventory of territory the corpus named and never developed, would be reachable by following a link from the graph rather than only by opening a table. That is a maintenance reason rather than a writing reason, and the admission standard's rule for an absence — "one seed, not an essay" — is satisfied by the inventory row plus the reason written here. Every one carried `state: nascent`, none had a Substantiation section, eight quoted nothing, and no claim was ever built on any of them in the 279 notes written after them.

The reading that decided them ran on 2026-08-09 against the extracted corpus at each stub's own locators, and it found one pattern with no exceptions. Every one of the twelve territories is a chapter title in the table of contents of the earliest edition (`mythic-os: contents`) with no chapter written behind it. The word *ritual* appears fourteen times across the eight documents: five are chapter titles in that same contents list, and the rest are figures of speech in the invocation prose ("It is an offering. A tuning fork. A ritual"). *Morning* appears once in all eight documents, in the contents entry itself. Declining them is therefore not a judgment that the territories are uninteresting; it is the record that the corpus names them and supplies nothing, which is what the inventory already says.

- **Habit as designed repetition** (`mythic-os: contents`; `abridged ch12`) — the gesture makes design the difference between a habit and a ritual. The four descriptive sources never use the word *habit*, so the corpus has no account of repetition at all. The nearest executed material asserts automaticity as a goal without examining it, a form practised "until the form is no longer practice; it is who one is" (abridged ch14), and whether an automatic practice can still pass the felt-shift test is a question the corpus never asks. That question is the part worth keeping and it belongs to the verification-conditions pass rather than to a claim here.
- **Paradox mapping as a reader's procedure** (`mythic-os: contents`; `space-around: around the honesty`; `atlas §XI`) — the gesture names a reader-facing method for holding two opposed things without resolving them. The corpus is built on kept tensions and every treatment it records is an authorial move made in the writing; its only reader-facing material is a disposition rather than a procedure, stated as "polarity is not a problem to be solved. It is a tension to be inhabited. It is, in fact, home" (abridged ch9). The warrant the stub gained after it was written is real and is not lost: premature closure has measured costs where evidence is still arriving ([[the drive to closure has measurable costs]]), which makes holding a tension open a describable skill rather than an authorial temperament. A procedure would be new work rather than extraction.
- **Deliberate entering and leaving of a partnership** (`mythic-os: contents`; `atlas §V`; `abridged ch8`) — the gesture treats a partnership as having designed thresholds at both ends, so that leaving well is a practice rather than a failure. Everywhere else in the corpus, decoupling means the three loops releasing at the peak. The chemistry holds the components for a chosen ending, being Sever and the ordering rule inside the forgiveness formula, and it names no molecule for one.
- **Symbolic infrastructures a person lives inside** (`mythic-os: contents`; `atlas §X`; `walked X`) — the gesture treats dreams, playlists and personal codes as readable systems a person inhabits, and it was the best-evidenced of the twelve. The corpus performs it exactly once: the ontology "has now run in three media — a book, a knowledge graph, and once, without strain, a running playlist, where sidechain compression turned out to be spanda and a split clock turned out to be kairos" (walked X; atlas §X.1). That instance is the author reading his own system, and all three documents deploy it as evidence for the triad's portability rather than as a method a reader could run on their own material. One worked instance used for a different purpose is not a proposition about readers.
- **Ritual as a structure that holds a self** (`mythic-os: contents`; `arc-gloss: ch13`; `abridged ch13`) — the gesture proposes structures built once that go on holding, where the corpus's account of steadiness is a posture explicitly defined as not a structure but a point of return. Whether the two accounts are complementary or rivals is undecided in the corpus, and the word *sovereignty* never reaches the four descriptive sources. The unresolved question between a built structure and a returned-to posture is worth keeping and is a question for the author rather than a claim.
- **A schedule aligned to the nervous system** (`mythic-os: contents`; `space-around: around the engine`; `atlas §V`) — the gesture proposes arranging a working day to the body's cycles rather than to institutional time. The survey states the conflict in one line, that modern scheduling "wars against ultradian rhythm" (space-around: around the engine), and holds the grounding research as a queued candidate. What is missing is the step from a loop diagnosis to a designed week, which is design work rather than extraction.
- **Curating beauty as a practice** (`mythic-os: contents`; `atlas §XI.1a`; `space-around: around the honesty`) — the gesture treats the curation of beauty as having spiritual content. The vault's entire position on beauty is the hazard, that fine prose recruits a connoisseur where the book needs a practitioner, so the corpus holds a theory of beauty's danger and none of its use. The connection worth keeping is that the hazard runs through the verification standard, because beauty counterfeits the felt shift's signal, and that connection is already stated at [[the beauty tension restates the tradition's own boundary between rasa and recognition]].
- **Metaphor as a faculty that weaves a self** (`mythic-os: contents`; `mythic-os: page 12B`; `atlas §X.5`) — the gesture asserts that a person becomes coherent partly through the figures they reach for. Everywhere else the corpus treats metaphor as the book's own instrument, which is the image system at atlas §X.5, and that is a claim about how the book works on a reader rather than how a reader works on themselves. The two subjects are genuinely different and only the second is developed.
- **Nostalgia as continuity across a threshold** (`mythic-os: contents`; `mythic-os: page 1`; `atlas §II`) — the gesture treats nostalgia as the felt form of persistence through change rather than as sentiment. The earliest draft opens on it as its first truth: "The sweetness of becoming puckers into nostalgia whether you choose it or not" (mythic-os: page 1). The container anatomy gives its persisting corner no affective account at all, and the stub's own observation is the one worth keeping — if the gesture is right, nostalgia is what the Floor feels like from inside. That is a proposition the corpus does not state, so it waits for the author rather than entering as extraction.
- **Seasonal and weekly templates** (`mythic-os: contents`; `atlas §V.3`) — the gesture proposes reusable forms for seasons, weeks and endings. The corpus holds the seasonal doctrine, which is the metabolic arc whose five phases became this vault's own state vocabulary, and supplies no form a reader could put on a calendar. The template is the missing artefact between the doctrine and a practice, and building one is authorship rather than research.
- **The morning as the day's coherence** (`mythic-os: contents` alone) — the gesture makes the start of a day the point where the day becomes coherent. This is the only one of the twelve whose sole citation is the contents entry, because the word *morning* appears exactly once across all eight documents and that once is the entry itself. The real finding underneath is a gap in scale rather than a missing chapter: the corpus treats time at the scale of the moment, in the temporal loop, and at the scale of the season, in the metabolic arc, and the day — the scale a reader actually lives at — is missing between them. That gap is worth the author's attention and is recorded in the inventory.
- **The sacred in the ordinary produced by sensory design** (`mythic-os: contents`; `atlas §VII`) — the gesture claims the sacredness of ordinary life is produced by sensory arrangement, meaning light, texture and the set of a room. The corpus's commitment to the ordinary is stated entirely as attention to what is already there, which is a different claim and arguably an opposed one. Christopher Alexander is the roster's one spatial builder and the corpus takes his quality without a name while leaving the arranging that produces it untouched, which is a provenance question already held at [[the apparatus descends from the settlement the book positions itself against]].

**What reversing this would take.** Each entry names its locators, so any one of the twelve can be reopened by reading there and finding what a session in 2026-08-09 did not. The likeliest reopening is not a rereading of the corpus but a decision by the author to write one of these territories, at which point the material is his rather than the corpus's and enters as authorship rather than as extraction.

## Reversals

One reversal stands, and it is the only one so far. On 2026-08-07 the author asked for the candidate list to be cut, a session cut it from ninety-six unevaluated names to twenty and wrote a reason against each of the other sixty-one, and on reading the result the author reversed the cut in full the same day. Every name went back onto `ops/queue/candidates.md`, no decline record from that pass survives in this file, and the arguments the pass wrote are kept on the candidate entries themselves as notes that decide nothing. His reason for the reversal is recorded as a settled question in `ops/decisions.md`: the list is a high-level survey he wants to keep whole, because he expects to commingle claims from thinkers on it.
