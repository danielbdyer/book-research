# the corpus pre-sorts the convergence material by the system it demotes

The center-of-gravity directive names a regression to avoid: "organizing new material by the system's taxonomy — sorting thinkers into triad corners, translating findings into molecule formulas." Working the company-of-thinkers and practice material on 2026-08-02 showed that the regression is not only an agent's temptation. In two places the corpus has already performed it, and the pre-sorted version is the only version that carries the quotations the directive asks for.

**The contemporary teachers' axioms exist only inside the loop-sorting.** The five theories of where suffering lives are stated in three documents. The atlas gives them as a table with prescriptions (§IX.5); the walked document gives them in prose (walked IX); neither quotes the camps' own sentences. The one place each camp's axiom appears verbatim — Crone's "you are living inside a sentence you didn't choose," Katie's "suffering is arguing with what is," Hudson's "every feeling, fully welcomed, completes itself," Robbins's "your state is a choice, available this instant," Deida's "give your gift at your edge" — is `arc-gloss: ch7`, the chapter that docks the eleven teachers into the three loops. The passage presents them sorted by which cylinder each theory lives in, with Robbins working "temporal-by-force" and the responsibility camp working "identity's ownership circuitry." The quotable material and the taxonomy arrive in the same sentence. The note written from that page could very easily have been the loop-mapping, which is the named regression exactly.

The atlas compounds it at a second level. Its roster tags several of the teachers with one of the Trika tradition's four means of recognition — Katie as "śāktopāya's American storefront," Robbins as "āṇavopāya at stadium scale" (§IX.5) — so following the atlas's own cross-references from a thinker leads into the system's architecture rather than into the thinker.

**The convergence table contains the framework.** The treatment's list of vocabularies orbiting the basic practice posture runs "wu wei, nonattachment, Gelassenheit, Lichtung, receptive awareness, Gendlin's allowing, Scharmer's presencing, Hudson's VIEW, Circling's being-with, the framework's own sovereignty" (`arc-gloss: ch13`). The last item is the book's own vocabulary seated as a peer among ten traditions it is otherwise being tested against. A convergence table that includes the framework is doing something different from a convergence table the framework argues from, and the difference is invisible unless the list is read for it.

**A third instance, smaller and in the practice pole.** Two of the posture's six facets are stated in the executed draft only in the triad's corner names — "the Knife is operating, even in the receptivity"; "the Vessel is intact; the Vessel is not clenched" (`abridged ch13`). The practice pole is a centerpiece and the triad is scaffolding, and at that point the centerpiece cannot be quoted without the scaffolding.

## What was done this round

The material was extracted with the taxonomy demoted to a working function rather than an organizing principle: the five theories are recorded as a standing argument with each camp's axiom quoted, and the loop-sorting appears as one line of substantiation naming what the sorting is used for (a referral network). The convergence table was recorded as the treatment gives it, including the framework's presence in it, with that presence stated rather than silently dropped.

Input to /rethink. The condition to watch is that this pull is structural rather than incidental: the corpus's most quotable convergence passages are the ones written to dock the convergence into the system, so every future pass over this material will meet it again. The candidate responses are to record the pre-sorting as a standing reading instruction alongside the source-standing policy, to keep it as an observation and let each pass handle it, or to treat the framework's presence in its own convergence table as a doctrinal question for the author rather than an operational one.
