# the constellation build found four more record discrepancies

On 2026-11-13 a read-only subagent built the dataset behind the prototype's map of thinkers, which places twenty-eight thinkers by date and region and draws the relations the vault records between them (`prototypes/the book as a site — reader reports/who noticed, and when — the map's data (2026-11-13).json`). Checking each quotation and relation against its file surfaced four discrepancies, none repaired, because each touches a note or a reading record and belongs to a pipeline pass. They continue the eight logged the day before at `ops/observations/the site parallel surfaced eight record discrepancies.md`.

1. **The first Laozi reading record is superseded and still says so.** The record of 2026-08-10 states that Daodejing chapter 11 had "no contact with *Lichtung*." The later finding that Heidegger's letting and Way descend from Daoism (`notes/heidegger's letting and way descend from daoism so pairing him with laozi is lineage not convergence.md`) supersedes that line.
2. **Polanyi's thinker page says no primary work has been read,** though a reading record of 2026-08-24 exists (`archive/reading — polanyi, the tacit dimension (1966).md`). This is the same class of lag as the Aristotle page in the earlier observation.
3. **One note repeats itself.** `notes/erhard and the book take different verbs from the same heideggerian object.md` has its heading and first two paragraphs duplicated.
4. **The Śaṅkara record claims an independence nobody checked.** It says Advaita had no contact with the Kashmir school; the vault never ran that test, so the pair must not be drawn or cited as an independent arrival until it is.
