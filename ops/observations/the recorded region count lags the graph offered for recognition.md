# the recorded region count lags the graph offered for recognition

The 2026-08-05 decision on units of readiness (`ops/decisions.md`, "What are the vault's units of readiness?") set the graph's computed regions as what is offered for the author's recognition — regions of mass, never declared units. That offering is recorded in three places — the decisions row itself, `ops/standing-consequences.md`, and `ops/goals.md` thread 5 — and all three still state the 2026-08-05 shape: three content regions, one apparatus-and-provenance region, one bridge.

Re-run on 2026-08-15, `scripts/queries/clusters.sh` prints eight regions over 546 claim-carrying notes and 2,233 links. The three masses with no recorded offering are the destabilization-and-psychological-risk mass (38 notes, five tensions), the indirect-communication satellite around Kierkegaard (21 notes, nearly a clique), and the empirical-attack chamber against the felt shift (17 notes, holding the two live transmission-dig tensions). Each is a candidate unit of the kind the decision says the author recognizes rather than declares, and none has been put in front of him.

The offering surface has no refresh trigger: the census prints on demand, no maintenance condition invites re-running it after a bulk intake, and the two intakes of 2026-08-13/14 — 274 notes between them — moved the graph without moving the offering. The surface built so that units can emerge from the graph is the one surface that does not learn when the graph changes. The adjacent prior is `ops/observations/every maintenance condition counts unfinished work and none counts unsynthesized work.md`. Input to /rethink: whether the region census joins the counted conditions with a growth trigger (for example, re-offer when `notes/` has grown by a stated fraction since the last recorded census), weighed against the standing exclusion on new operational surface — the script exists, and the question is only when its output is read and recorded.
