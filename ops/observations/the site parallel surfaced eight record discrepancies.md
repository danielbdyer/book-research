# the site parallel surfaced eight record discrepancies

On 2026-11-12 six read-only readers each held one region of accelerateinward.com against one region of the vault to build the prototype at `prototypes/the book as a site (2026-11-12).html`. Because each reader had to print a fact as a public-facing card, each had to settle which of two files to believe, and eight disagreements surfaced. None was repaired, because each touches a note or a thinker page and belongs to a pipeline pass (/reweave or a fix session), not to a prototype. The full evidence sits in the reader reports under `prototypes/the book as a site — reader reports/`.

## The eight

1. **Evidence-ranking language the stance forbids survives in five files.** The phrases are "counts for more" (`the-constellation.md`), "worth more than either alone" (the shared-teacher note), "the strongest class of evidence the book has" (the engineered-epiphany note), "strongest genuinely independent arrival" (the Deci–Ryan note), and the mesh grid's "materially stronger," which one note quotes. `ops/methodology/authorial stance.md` says the vault "does not rank convergences as stronger or weaker evidence." Source: `reader report — who noticed this too.md`, finding 2.
2. **Hübl's load disagrees with his claim note.** His thinker page records `independent-arrival` and calls the shared-teacher test "the test his arrival passes," while `notes/what was never metabolized lives on as absence and hubl reached it through inherited trauma.md` records that his formation includes Wilber and makes him company. Source: the same report, finding 3.
3. **The participation law's outside witnesses are counted three ways.** `the-constellation.md` counts six, including Lewin and Maturana and Varela, then five once Lewin is removed; the participation note and the Lewin reading record name a different five (Polanyi, von Foerster, Garfinkel, Schön, Johnstone). A draft should name the witnesses rather than count them. Source: the same report, finding 3.
4. **The three front-matter sentences are attributed to the wrong document.** `notes/the founding inversion restates the author's own sufficiency inheritance.md` cites them to `mythic-os: contents`; the extraction places "You already know everything in this book. You have just forgotten. Reading is remembering." at `abridged` p. 1 only, and the earliest draft carries only "the art of remembering who you are" (p. 2) and "risk remembering what you already know" (p. 7). Source: `reader report — the manifesto and the making.md`, finding 2.
5. **Aristotle is recorded as both read and unread.** `notes/aristotle on privation.md` says the *Physics* "has not been read here"; `archive/reading — aristotle, physics I and metaphysics V on privation.md` (2026-08-09) records the reading. This is the same class of lag as `a fresh lens section can call a thinker unread after the archive holds the reading.md`. Source: `reader report — the company.md`, cautions.
6. **The felt shift's standing is stated two ways.** The felt-shift map's description and a decisions row dated 2026-08-04 still call it the book's test for whether anything is true; the orientation page `ops/orientation.md` holds it as an instrument the reader uses since the epistemic audit of 2026-08-16. Source: `reader report — the readings.md`, cautions.
7. **"Company" carries three senses.** It is a value of the `load:` field, the standing of every convergence ("company, not proof"), and a demotion ("company, not an outside arrival," said of Vervaeke). Source: `reader report — who noticed this too.md`, finding 4.
8. **Two-fifths of the thinkers declare no concern, and one discipline is empty.** 25 of 64 thinker notes declare no doctrine map, among them Robbins, Adorno, Kierkegaard, Henry, Wilber, and Ignatius; no thinker's `discipline:` is poets-and-artists, though most chapter patrons are poets and Rilke's note records none. Source: `reader report — the company.md`, finding 4.

## What closing it looks like

Items 1–6 are factual repairs a /reweave or fix session can make against the cited files, each checked against `scripts/queries/decided.sh` first. Items 7 and 8 are questions about the vault's vocabulary and schema, which belong to /rethink.
