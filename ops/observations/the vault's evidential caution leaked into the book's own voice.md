# the vault's evidential caution leaked into the book's own voice

On 2026-11-13 the author looked at the plain-language site prototype (`prototypes/the book as a site, in plain words (2026-11-12).html`) and said: "I think that this is a little too ambivalent about the core theses of the book. Can we 10x the call to action?"

## What happened

The prototype was built under the vault's standing rules: convergence is company and never proof, no efficacy is postulated, the book is never the one who certifies a reader's experience, and every refusal is kept (`ops/methodology/authorial stance.md`). Those rules were applied to the book's own address to its reader, not only to the vault's claims about the book. So the reader-facing pages said "company, not proof" on the comparison of thinkers, "you can skip this" and "if nothing fits, that's fine" in the rooms, "what this page won't do" and "you can say no" on the reply page, and "it is not a launch" on the front page. Each sentence was accurate, and together they made the book sound unsure of what it says.

## What the record supports instead

The corpus states the book's theses with conviction, and none of its strongest lines claims proof or promises an outcome: "You are not broken. You are obstructed. The obstructions can be named. The clearing is already here. Step into it." (`abridged` p. 46); "suffering is contraction, never corruption. Nothing is ruined. Something is folded, and folds can open." (`walked` p. 4); "Nothing transforms from the audience — including the audience of this book." (`arc-gloss` p. 3). A thesis stated firmly and an invitation made directly are compatible with the stance, because the stance governs evidential claims — what the convergence proves, what the practice achieves — and not how confidently the book states what it holds true. The rebuilt surface (`prototypes/the book as a site, with its call to action (2026-11-13).html`) keeps exactly two lines of restraint, no promised outcome and no claim that company is proof, and moves every other caution to an author-only page.

## The distinction for a later session

The vault's evidential register is for the vault's prose about the book. The book's voice to its reader is a different register, and the corpus already models it. A surface written for the book's reader should state the theses in the book's own words and confine restraint to the two places the stance actually binds. Whether this belongs in `ops/methodology/` as a standing correction is a question for /rethink or /remember, and the author's confirmation of the two-line boundary is the deciding fact.

## Follow-up, 2026-11-13: the remedy is pairing, not hedging

Later the same day the author asked for the site to be critiqued "for legibility and corrigibility," clarifying that he meant "semantic legibility and corrigibility… mostly not citation based but around the levers of epistemic rootedness/trustworthiness." Three independent readers (`prototypes/the book as a site — reader reports/review — …(2026-11-13).md`) converged on one finding: "the more certain a page sounds, the less support it shows." The confident rebuild had kept the vault's honest material, but on its last pages, away from the claims it tests, and it had reintroduced an overclaim the vault had already corrected ("That's how you'll know," said of the felt shift). The fix that worked was neither the hedging of the first version nor bare conviction: each claim stays unhedged and is paired, beneath it, with where it comes from, a way to test it, and where it could be wrong. That is the vault's own rule from `notes/a claim stated crisply enough to be refused is a gift to the reader's sovereignty.md`, that provisionality limits a claim's scope and never weakens its assertion. A /rethink pass could record this as the standing form for any reader-facing surface.
