# the register's failures concentrate where compression pressure is highest

The author read a sentence from `notes/jack kornfield.md` back to the session with the note that he had no idea what it said: "The refusal kept: Kornfield's after is communal and accompanied." He was right, and the failure has three stacked parts, each instructive. The opener is a fragment built from vault shorthand — "the refusal kept" is the methodology rule about keeping refusals intact, worn as a sentence, with no subject and no finite verb; prose register rules 1 and 10 forbid exactly this. The middle nominalizes a preposition into a collision with an idiom: "Kornfield's after" reads in ordinary English as *what Kornfield is pursuing*, not as *the years after awakening*, so the sentence points two directions at once. And the predicate pairs two abstract adjectives, one missing its argument (accompanied by whom?) and the pair near-redundant — the cadence of a finished thought carrying half the content of one.

Three things make this an observation rather than only a fix. First, the failure's location is systematic: it sat in a topic sentence, and the surviving instances of the same formula sit in topic sentences and one-line glosses — the positions where compression pressure is highest, because the writer is summarizing a move they have just made and reaches for the internal label of the move instead of its content. The frontmatter defect recorded earlier in this folder had the same shape: the register's own habits produce the defect at the point of maximum habit. Second, no automated check can see it. The session's structural self-check passed this note minutes before the author failed to parse it — YAML valid, links resolving, description under length — because parseability has no linter; the only instrument that catches this class is a cold human read, which is also what /verify's recite step approximates and what the author's tired-friend test names. The author is currently the only reliably firing detector. Third, this is the third register correction from the author and the second on the density pole, and it confirms the prediction recorded in rule 11's own history: "I worry other agents will continue this concise praxis of explication and get saturated in their own density too."

The fix applied: the sentence and its echoes in the same day's notes, maps, queue rows, and log entries were rewritten into plain sentences (the load-bearing content, in six words: nobody did the laundry alone). The formula "The refusal kept: [full clause]" survives in the crossover-round notes of 2026-08-03, where the clause after the colon parses on its own; those were left, and whether the formula should be retired everywhere is left to /rethink. What /rethink might weigh: whether /verify's cold-read should sample topic sentences and glosses specifically, since that is where this class concentrates; and whether the register needs one added line — a statement line never opens with an internal formula, and a gloss must parse for a reader who has never seen the vault.
