# six vault coinages each name more than one thing

The plain-language repair of 2026-09-03 sent seven agents over thirty-one files with one lexicon (`ops/plain-language lexicon.md`) and asked each to report where the lexicon's expansion did not fit the sentence. Their reports agree on a pattern that is a vocabulary fault rather than a prose fault. Six words the vault uses as names each carry more than one referent inside the vault itself: **the clearing** is `ops/clearing.md` and also Heidegger's own opening and the book's part title; **the spine** is `notes/the learning arc.md` and also the two-book allocation decision of 2026-08-16; **the grid** is the discipline-by-load table, the outside mesh grid the author received, and Wilber's four quadrants; **the register** is the prose rules, the decisions table, and a family file's own list of roles; **chair** is a thinker's page and, in "the second chair," a person present; and **seat** is a thinker's role and, in the chapter comparison table, a patron's epigraph. The vocabulary policy (`ops/methodology/vocabulary policy.md`) requires the reference surface to be denotative, and a name with two referents is not. The lexicon now records each collision as a note on its row, which lets a reader disambiguate but does not remove the collision. What would close it: a naming decision per word, either a rename of the vault's own thing (the clearing register, the learning-arc spine, the lineage table) or a recorded ruling that the collision is tolerated because the book's own sense must keep the word.
