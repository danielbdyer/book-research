# the crossings table reads a marked synthesis as a convergence

The arc survey's read of the vault's own theory of sequence (`ops/arc ecology.md`, tension 3) found one place where the vault does the thing its own lens index forbids. `the-lenses.md` says that "a family that reports only where its members agree has begun erasing the difference that is the gold," and `ops/scaffold.md` §16 lists "the peak≈participation and return≈stewardship alignments" under "Synthesis, marked as such, not a source claim," with §14 J6 keeping the six-phase arc and the four movements distinct on purpose. The crossings table in the same index nevertheless gives the Psychedelic Moment a cell in every column — "participation→stewardship" under the Movements, "Book II hinge" under the Two Books — and draws the conclusion that "the House and the Psychedelic Moment appear under almost every lens — that is the interpolation you were reaching for." The return row runs the same way.

The defect is small in extent and exact in kind: a marked synthesis, read back one hop later as a convergence. It is the same shape as the unanimity artifact the vault already records for the corpus's lineages, arriving inside the vault's own instrument. What would close it: recount the crossings table against the scaffold's synthesis list and re-mark the peak and return rows as synthesis, which needs no reading and one pass.
