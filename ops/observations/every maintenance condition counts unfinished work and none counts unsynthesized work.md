# every maintenance condition counts unfinished work and none counts unsynthesized work

The author observed on 2026-08-08 that the vault holds many notes and few statements of higher-order outcome: asked to define the overarching outline of the material, a session would have had to parse thirteen topic maps and 246 notes by hand. The observation is worth recording as process friction rather than only as a task, because the gap had grown for a week without any instrument reporting it, and the reason it went unreported is a property of the condition set rather than an oversight in any one session.

## What the conditions measure, and what they therefore cannot see

The nine maintenance conditions in `CLAUDE.md` are: items waiting in the inbox, open observations, operational tensions, unprocessed session records, a missing or stale semantic index, methodology notes behind config changes, claims with no topic map, nascent stubs outnumbering the claims filled from them, and wiki links that no longer resolve. Every one of them counts an item of work that has not been done. Nothing in the set counts a quantity of material that has grown past the last surface that summarized it, so the vault's fastest-growing quantity — claims — had no condition attached to it at all.

The effect is asymmetric in a way that matters. A single unprocessed inbox item fires a condition at every session start; a hundred and forty new claims added since the last synthesis fires nothing, and the graph reports itself healthy the whole time. The maintenance table's own closing line — that quiet conditions are the healthy state — is true of everything the table measures and silent about everything it does not.

## The measurement taken while building the roll-up

Two numbers from the census that `scripts/queries/outline.sh` now generates:

- **246 notes sit beneath the thirteen maps, joined by 1,404 links.** The largest single map, `book-structure`, carries about sixty entries against the roughly-twenty-five guideline and has self-flagged that for a /rethink pass; `interlocutors` carries about eighty-five and has self-flagged the same. Both flags are in the maps' own closing sections, which is to say the vault knew two of its gathering surfaces were oversized and had no mechanism that acted on knowing.
- **66 of the 246 carry a draft anchor precise enough to name one of the corpus's fifteen units.** The other 180 — the thinkers, the tensions, the record of the making, and the outward research — were not extracted from a chapter. This is why the roll-up is organized by concern and by found region rather than by unit: an outline keyed to the corpus's chapters would have shown roughly a quarter of the material and hidden the centerpiece company entirely.

## What was done, and what is not settled

`ops/outline.md` was built, and a condition now fires when any note is newer than its census, which closes the specific instance. The general question is open and is a /rethink candidate: whether the condition set should carry a second kind of condition entirely — one that fires on growth past a threshold rather than on work left undone. The two self-flagged oversized maps are the evidence that at least one more such condition would have something to report today.
