# authorial stance

This is an author directive, issued 2026-08-02, global and standing. The author's sentences, which it operationalizes:

> The intention I have is to not be an author that presupposes a righteousness but to gesture towards that which has its own lineage and the crossovers I see. I'm not sure my role as an author yet is to postulate about what's successful and not but just to share my observations that there seems to be something there.

> So far I don't have any evidentiary remarks.

## The distinction this rests on

The vault's prose is **evidential**: every claim says where it came from, quotes what was actually said, and names its location. That is unchanged and is not what this directive touches.

The project is not thereby **evidentiary**. Provenance here serves accurate pointing, not proof. The vault had begun to treat its own material as a case being built — ranking convergences by evidential weight, describing what the book "can safely lean on," reading a thinker's refusal as a counterweight that strengthens a position. That is the presupposed righteousness the author is declining, and it arrived through the apparatus rather than through any decision.

## Three rules

1. **A crossover is an observation, not an argument.** The author noticed that two or more lineages appear to be pointing at the same thing. The vault records the noticing and quotes each lineage at full strength in its own terms. It does not rank convergences as stronger or weaker evidence, and it does not convert agreement into support for a thesis. Each tradition has standing that does not depend on this book, and the record should leave that standing intact.

2. **Outside reading keeps the gesture accurate; it does not make a claim strong.** A primary text is read so the thinker is quoted rightly and so what they would refuse is known. The refusal is kept because a finger with its refusal removed is no longer that thinker's finger — not because opposition seasons an argument. Where a citation would imply an empirical warrant the author does not have, the note says so, and that is an accuracy correction rather than a strategic one.

3. **The vault does not postulate efficacy.** Whether any of this works is not a claim the author is making. Material that bears only on outcome — does it succeed, for whom, how often — is not what this project is for, and asking for it is a category error about the book. What the author is claiming is narrower and stated plainly: there seems to be something here.

## What this does not license

Looseness about sources. Pointing accurately is harder than arguing, not easier, because an argument survives a misquotation and a gesture does not: a finger aimed wrongly points at nothing. The quote-marking rule, the anchors, the source standing policy, and the tension apparatus all become more load-bearing under this stance, not less.

It also does not license removing the counterweights already recorded. They stay, in their proper function: what each thinker would refuse, kept so the thinker survives being cited.

## The failure this guards

An apparatus built to hold claims will, unprompted, begin to defend them. Every device in this vault — the categories, the substantiation layer, the evidence notation the corpus supplied — has a gravity toward advocacy, and the drift is invisible because each individual sentence looks like rigor. This directive names the difference between saying where something came from and saying that it is therefore true.
