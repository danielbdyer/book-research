# decoration and critique are shelf material

This directive, global and standing, was established by the author on 2026-08-16 after the epistemic audit found the research graph epistemically flat: decorative resonances and external critiques were interlinking and being reweaved with the same weight as load-bearing doctrine, so the graph's own connectivity — the signal the vault reads for structure and importance — had come to measure the book's self-doubt rather than its substance. The correction deleted 163 such nodes; this rule keeps them from regrowing.

## The rule

Three kinds of material do not enter `notes/` as first-class nodes on the strength of being interesting, valid, or thorough:

- **A decorative resonance or borrowed analogy** — a simile or far-field correspondence that, in its own words, accompanies a doctrine rather than testing it ("company, not evidence"). Its home is `ops/queue/commonplace.md` if a drafting session might reach for the image, or `ops/queue/declines.md` if not.
- **An external critique or adversary** — an attack on the book, a stress-test, or an unearned self-doubt. Its home is the reading queue as a deferred decision, taken up when a reading or the writing reaches it and admitted only if it earns a seat.
- **Vault-system-design material** — a note whose subject is the vault's own architecture rather than the book. Its home is `ops/`.

A node enters `notes/` when the writing or a completed reading actually reaches for it — the writing test applied at the edge, not the node. Thoroughness is not admission: that a point is valid, or that a catalogue would be complete with it, is not a reason to make it a node. And `/reweave` never pulls shelf or deferred material back into the graph; the backward pass connects load-bearing claims to each other, not decoration to doctrine.

## Why the edge, not the node

The failure mode is topological. In a graph whose importance is read from connectivity, a node admitted below the writing test is an unearned attachment surface: nothing may depend on it today, but nothing stops later material from branching off it, and once it accretes edges the instruments report it as real structure. The discipline is therefore not "no leaf nodes" — a load-bearing claim nothing has yet built upon is a healthy leaf — but "nothing in the interlinking graph that the writing would not reach for." The distinction the audit drew, and this rule preserves, is between the dinner table and the shelf.
