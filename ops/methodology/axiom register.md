# axiom register

The reframe conversation of 2026-08-13 produced several competing lists of axioms and, in the same breath, decided where the axioms belong. They belong backstage. The author drew the line himself: the axioms that guide the maker are distinct from whatever the finished book holds out to its reader, and the first set stays behind the scenes, "just like a facilitator doesn't need to share the way in which they're facilitating with the audience" (reframe conversation, 2026-08-13, line 526). The model restated the division as the engineering of a bridge — "the axioms that guide you as creator, live mostly behind the scenes... The reader doesn't need to study those load calculations. They experience the structure" (line 531). So the axioms fall away from the finished book, and this register is where they stay, on the ops side of the vault, rather than entering the manuscript as its printed content. The claim that records the decision itself is [[the axioms are orientations that fall away from the finished book]], which holds the reclassification and the backstage line as a single move; this register is its operational companion.

## The routing key: an axiom is an orientation, not a proposition

The one property that sends a statement here rather than into `notes/` as a claim is that an axiom does not assert something true about the world but names a posture toward experience. The conversation reclassified them on the spot — "Maybe these axioms aren't propositions at all but orientations... That's a posture towards experience" (reframe conversation, 2026-08-13, line 401) — and the author ratified it, "Yeah, exactly right" (line 406). An orientation keeps the work porous where a proposition would close it into doctrine; the author named this the whole point, that the axioms "keep the system porous" and "prevent closing into dogma" (line 391). A statement that reads as a testable proposition about the book's subject is a claim and belongs in the graph; a statement that reads as a stance the maker holds while building belongs here.

## The seventeen, and where each already lives

Three lists were collated — the first of twelve (reframe conversation, 2026-08-13, line 3784), the second of thirteen (line 8429), the third of ten (line 11321) — and reconciled in the reframe harvest, section 5. Roughly four in five were already present in the vault before the conversation named them, which is itself the register's most important finding: the conversation did not deliver a new foundation so much as re-describe one the graph had been building. Each orientation is stated below in plain terms, with the vault material that already carries it and a note of standing — already in the vault, partially present, genuinely new, or candidate-only.

1. **Do not colonize another's experience.** Already in the vault: [[the book can only be auxiliary to a sovereign reader]] holds the book as an instrument in service of a life that stays the reader's own, and [[nothing transforms from the audience]] closes the same law from the reader's side.
2. **Every locus is revelatory and partial.** Already in the vault: [[every locus is potentially revelatory and necessarily partial — partial truths become dangerous mistaken for total truths]] states it directly, and the correspondence discipline holds it at method scale in [[the comparative method does not collapse a correspondence before its difference is understood]] and [[the unanimity claim is an artifact of how the lineages were selected]].
3. **No locus becomes sovereign over what it describes.** Already in the vault: [[no single locus or framework may become sovereign over the reality it describes]] is the doctrine, and the candidate-holding discipline in `ops/methodology/naming and epistemic humility.md` is the same refusal applied to the vault's own names.
4. **Experience precedes its representation.** Already in the vault: felt shift versus felt confirmation separates a bodily change that registers something landing from assent to fluent prose, and `ops/methodology/the primary material.md` records the author's ruling that the lived material governs the corpus that represents it.
5. **A work is completed by participation.** Already in the vault: [[the book performs what it describes]] is the book's one law about itself, and [[transformation is enacted participation inside a held container]] is the mechanism it rests on.
6. **Constraints produce fluency.** Already in the vault: [[constraint produces fluency and deprivation reveals the essential]] states it, and [[contraction is a productive locus and not only a privation]] reads the same law in the Śaiva register of saṅkoca, where a limitation is productive precisely because it is not everything.
7. **Preserve difference before synthesizing; the remainder carries information.** Already in the vault, with a sharpening: [[the comparative method does not collapse a correspondence before its difference is understood]] and [[the remainder that resists synthesis is often where the deepest insight lives]] hold the method, and the clearing register (`ops/methodology/the clearing.md`) is the standing place for what will not reduce. The conversation's addition is to treat the remainder as an output worth keeping rather than a residue.
8. **A durable perspective is acquired through encounter, not selected once.** Partially present: [[a durable perspective is a recognition whose value persists across contexts]] holds the perspective, but the criterion assembled in the conversation — what survives movement across loci — is new and stated as the author's own coinage (reframe conversation, 2026-08-13, line 2975).
9. **The framework is subject to itself.** Already in the vault, and enacted: [[a framework is transformed by what its own encounters disclose]] states it, the candidate-holding discipline in `ops/methodology/naming and epistemic humility.md` practices it, and the vault performed it on 2026-08-09 by retiring its own enforcement machinery (see the master-axiom section below).
10. **Relationship can increase sovereignty rather than consume it.** Already in the vault: [[relationship can increase sovereignty rather than consume it]] is the general claim, and [[sovereign intimacy names intimacy without surrender of authorship]] is the conversation's new weld of closeness to preserved authorship.
11. **Sufficiency: flourishing requires the capacity to experience enoughness.** Already in the vault: [[the founding inversion restates the author's own sufficiency inheritance]] and [[sufficiency is the capacity to remain present without needing the next input to complete oneself]] carry it as the fourth of the book's privative conditions.
12. **Attention constitutes the lived world.** Genuinely new and least settled, appearing on one list only: [[the architecture of attention is partially the architecture of the self]] is the nearest vault material, and the relevance-realization ground in [[relevance realization is the engine of meaning, and the attention economy fragments relevance rather than multiplying information]] is where it would be developed.
13. **Not everything valuable should be reduced to explicit structure.** Partially present: the clearing register (`ops/methodology/the clearing.md`) is the vault's standing organ for what the center cannot bring into focus, which is the operational form of the same reservation about exhaustive articulation.
14. **An encounter changes the conditions of the next encounter.** Partially present: [[a framework is transformed by what its own encounters disclose]] carries the reflexive form, and the reweave pass is the vault's mechanism for letting new material change the context of older claims; the "return to life" re-test the conversation asks for is the organ the text-only reading queue still lacks.
15. **Technology should increase the capacity to participate; an intelligence should facilitate rather than replace.** Candidate-only, because it is book content tied to the latent third book: [[build technologies that increase capacity to participate rather than maximize dependence]], [[every technology is evaluable by whether it increases or decreases capacity to participate]], and [[AI is neither master, servant, replacement, nor oracle but interlocutor]] carry it.
16. **The ecology matters as much as the individual.** Partially present: [[the ecology itself can become intelligent]] and the relational-field material carry the shift of weight from the single person to the field they participate in.
17. **Information and realization differ; knowing-about is insufficient where the subject is participatory.** Already in the vault: felt shift versus felt confirmation, [[knowing about is not knowing through — information can be transmitted, realization cannot]], and [[there are four kinds of knowing, and the felt shift lives at the edge of the participatory]] carry it.

## The master axiom, held plural

The conversation reached for a single axiom beneath the rest and offered three, declining to declare any final — "I wouldn't declare this final" (reframe conversation, 2026-08-13, line 7109). The register keeps all three and does not choose:

- **The ethics candidate** — "Create conditions for recognition without claiming sovereignty over what is recognized" (reframe conversation, 2026-08-13, line 7113).
- **The pluralism candidate** — "Allow every locus to disclose without allowing any locus to become sovereign" (line 7117).
- **The reflexivity candidate** — "Let what is encountered transform the framework that made the encounter possible" (line 7121), which the conversation itself marked "perhaps the deepest" (line 7119).

The reflexivity candidate is held first among equals for a reason the vault can show rather than assert: it is the only one of the three the vault had already enacted before the conversation named it. On 2026-08-09 the vault retired the enforcement apparatus that once stamped the prose register at fourteen surfaces and retired its standing self-measurement instruments, recorded in `CLAUDE.md` and read by the reconciliation register as its Fork 11 — a case of the framework being changed by what its own encounters disclosed. The claim that carries this is [[a framework is transformed by what its own encounters disclose]]; the master axiom names, four days later, a discipline the vault had already practiced on itself. That precedence is the whole argument for ranking the reflexivity candidate first, and it is an argument from record, not from preference.

## How a session uses this register

A session reads this register to place a stated axiom rather than to enforce one. When a reframe-derived statement reads as an orientation the maker holds, it lands here and does not become a claim; when it reads as a proposition about the book's subject, it routes to `notes/` through the ordinary pipeline. The register ranks nothing as evidence and settles no fork — the choice of a single master axiom, if the author ever wants one, is his to make. Its one discipline is the routing key above: an axiom is a way in, not a thing asserted, and the finished book shows the reader the invitation rather than the load calculation behind it.
