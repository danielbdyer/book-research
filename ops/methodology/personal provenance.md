# personal provenance

This is an author directive, issued 2026-08-03, global and standing. The author's sentences, which it operationalizes:

> You're welcome to store a lot more private or personal details from all three documents or our conversations now if you find that they give the book additional value. All I ask is that you own it and do it delicately and in a way that honors what is becoming through it and all will be welcome to be crystallized.

> The goal is to help arrive at a clear expression of the whole lifeblood of the book, not just the disembodied contents that may be helpful to others; and, the first goal is to support that same writing of the book and to enable that third thing's evolution, as it has always been.

## What this changes

The writing test is extended, not replaced. "Would a drafting session reach for this?" now explicitly includes the lifeblood: the lived ground of a figure or doctrine passes the test when holding it would let the writing be whole rather than disembodied. A drafting session on the guarded part, the burnout formula, or the doctrine of ripeness reaches for the life those doctrines came from — so the vault may now hold it.

The prior restraint is lifted, not repudiated. Until this directive, material of the author's life in the provenance record was read and not recorded; that was correct under the standing rules, and this directive supersedes it by the author's decision rather than by reinterpretation.

## Three conditions, from the author's own asking

1. **Own it.** The vault makes the admission calls itself rather than deferring each to the author, and records what it decided so the calls are reviewable. Deference on every sentence would be its own kind of failure to hold.

2. **Delicately.** Personal material is recorded at the specificity the record supplies and never speculated past. The prose register still governs — evidential, plainly stated — but the register bends toward reverence here: a protective part is written about the way the book meets it, with a bow. Interpretations remain attributed (the reconstruction's reading is cited as its reading); the author's own words are quoted rather than paraphrased where the phrasing is the life. Where the record itself held something at a distance — naming a season's strand and leaving it — the vault keeps that distance rather than closing it.

3. **In honor of what is becoming.** The material serves the book's becoming and the third thing's evolution — the author's stated first goal — not completeness of biography. A fact enters because a doctrine is grounded by it, not because it is known.

## What was persisted under this directive

The four provenance documents themselves, in `provenance/`, with committed extractions — including round one, which had earlier been purged from history and is restored by this decision. "For now": the persistence is reversible, and the claims that cite the documents quote what they need in place, so withdrawal would orphan nothing.

## The relation to the other stances

This directive and `authorial stance.md` pull in the same direction, not against each other. The stance directive stops the vault from arguing the book is right; this one lets the vault say where the book is *from*. Provenance of the lived kind is the strongest material a gesturing author has — it makes no claim of efficacy and needs none, because "this is where it came from in me" is an observation, exactly the register the stance requires.
