# the four kinds of structure

The reframe conversation of 2026-08-13 produced four structures that look like competitors for a single slot — a recurring pulse, a set of five questions, a sequence of four movements, and a stack of nested orders — and presented several of them as candidate spines for the book. They are not rivals. They are four different kinds of structure operating at four different altitudes, and they share vocabulary only because the model re-derived each from the others, so the word "recognition" appears in all four without meaning the same structural thing in any two. The reconciliation register holds this as its Fork 18. This note names the four kinds, gives each its altitude and its destination in the vault, and states the confusion that dissolves once a containment hierarchy stops being read as a plot.

## The operator: the pulse that beats within a single act of inquiry

The smallest of the four is an engine, not an arc: a beat that recurs inside a single chapter or a single encounter. The conversation compressed it as "Constraint → Locus → Encounter → Recognition → Reflexivity → Integration → New Locus," and named it in the Śaiva register "Saṅkoca → Encounter → Vimarśa → Recognition → Participation → Renewal," explicitly as "a resonant generative grammar" rather than a literal one-to-one mapping (reframe conversation, 2026-08-13, lines 7009-7019). Its felt form is the arising and subsiding the sources call spanda, and the vault already carries it several ways: as the vocabulary for the rhythm of inquiry at [[the pulse of consciousness supplies a vocabulary for the rhythm of inquiry]], as the two-lunged movement of contraction and expansion at [[the pulse of experience moves through contraction and expansion]], as the assembled circuit at [[the kashmir shaiva tradition's five principles assemble into one circuit]], and as the per-chapter setting at [[the per-chapter dial is a spanda ratio]]. Its destination is the prose itself, where it governs the beat of a single unit; it is not the book's overall traversal.

## The method: the five questions a research pass runs

The second kind is a procedure — the sequence of questions by which the author studies the material, not the sequence by which a reader moves through the book. The conversation offered five: "From where am I seeing?" (locus), "What is actually happening?" (encounter), "What has become apparent?" (recognition), "What remains when I move?" (durable perspective), and "What becomes possible now?" (practice), calling the set "almost a complete research methodology in five questions" (reframe conversation, 2026-08-13, lines 7424-7444). Its destination is backstage, in `ops/`, because it describes how research is done rather than what the book says; it is largely congruent with the vault's existing pipeline, and its two contributions are steps that pipeline can absorb — the comparative method's insistence on the remainder, held at [[the comparative method does not collapse a correspondence before its difference is understood]] and [[the remainder that resists synthesis is often where the deepest insight lives]], and a return-to-life re-test the text-only reading queue currently lacks. A research method printed as a manuscript arc would be the category error of showing the reader the load calculations, which the axiom register (`ops/methodology/axiom register.md`) exists to prevent.

## The dramaturgy: the four movements a reader travels

The third kind is a plot — the developmental traversal a reader makes through the book. The conversation stated it as four movements, "Recognition → Relation → Participation → Stewardship," each carried per book so that the first book stewards the field and the second stewards the self (reframe conversation, 2026-08-13, line 16932); the vault holds it at [[both books move through recognition → relation → participation → stewardship]]. Its distinguishing feature is direction: it matures toward the facilitator's withdrawal rather than toward a peak, ending with the facilitator disappearing. Its destination is the manuscript, as the candidate spine, and it is held as a candidate under the vault's naming discipline (`ops/methodology/naming and epistemic humility.md`) and run through the demotion test before any elevation — the more so because it competes with the vault's existing psychedelic arc, a genuine spine fork the reconciliation register holds separately as its Fork 19. The dramaturgy is the one kind of the four that is a plot; the other three are not, and the rivalry among the four is largely the result of reading the non-plots as if they were this one.

## The ontology: the nested orders the domain contains

The fourth kind is a containment hierarchy — what the domain holds inside what. The conversation delivered it as five nested orders, an ecology containing conditions, participation, recognition, sovereignty, and stewardship (reframe conversation, 2026-08-13, line 17239), produced on demand and disavowed in the same passage as not the deepest structure. Its destination is neither the prose beat, nor `ops/`, nor the manuscript arc, but the status of a way-in only, held under the demotion test exactly as the ecology invariant requires; the reasoning is in `ops/methodology/ecology names a movement not a taxonomy.md`, which records that the book describes movements rather than a taxonomy and keeps every hierarchical rendering as a lens rather than an architecture. An ontology read as a plot is the specific confusion Fork 18 names — a stack of what-contains-what mistaken for a sequence of what-happens-next — and it is the confusion that most makes the four look like four answers to one question.

## The discipline

A session that meets one of these four asks first which kind it is, because the destination follows from the kind: the operator governs a chapter's beat, the method routes to `ops/`, the dramaturgy is a candidate manuscript spine, and the ontology is a way-in. The four share the words recognition, participation, and stewardship because the model built each by re-deriving it from the others, so shared vocabulary across them is evidence of common parentage, not of common structure. The rivalry the conversation staged among them dissolves the moment each is returned to its own altitude, and none of the four is elevated to "the spine" without the author's decision and a pass through the demotion test.
