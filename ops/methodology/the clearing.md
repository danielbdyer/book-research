# the clearing

The book has a chosen center: the convergence of thinkers and the lived practice, by author decision (`center of gravity.md`). A center is a lens, and every lens has a blind spot — the things it cannot bring into focus because of the very commitments that make it a lens. The clearing is the standing place where those things are kept visible. The author named the need in his own words when he asked for this work: "I want the clearing to appear here so we are not biased by the center of gravity but open to welcome all which is not yet seen."

The register itself is `ops/clearing.md`. This note is its policy.

## The inverted admission rule

Everywhere else in the vault, material earns its place by the writing test: a note is admitted when a drafting session would reach for it, and what only describes the vault's own state routes to `ops/` (`writing test.md`). The clearing runs the opposite rule, and the opposition is the point.

**A thing enters the clearing precisely because it does not yet fit.** It has no convergence — no second independent arrival to make it company. It has no link into the chain of understanding the research is building (`the chain of understanding.md`). It has no manuscript use anyone can name. By the writing test it would be declined; the clearing exists so that a decline is not the last word on something the center simply has no organ to perceive.

So the clearing is **protected from the writing test**, not subject to it. A session does not ask of a clearing entry "would a drafting session reach for this?" — the answer is no by construction. It asks instead the one question that is the clearing's whole discipline.

## The one discipline

Every clearing entry must name **which commitment of the center cannot hold it.** An entry that cannot name the commitment it falls outside of is not a clearing entry; it is either ordinary material that belongs in the pipeline, or noise. The discipline is what keeps the clearing from becoming a junk drawer: it holds only things that are invisible *for a reason the register states*, and the reason is always a specific property of the center — that convergence needs a second arrival, that recognition is defined as the return of what never left, that the felt shift is a verification, that the company is a company of thinkers.

## What the clearing is not

- **It is not a backdoor into the graph.** A clearing entry grounds no claim and no doctrine. If an entry later finds a second arrival, a chain-link, or a manuscript use, it graduates by the normal pipeline — a seed, then /reduce, then the graph — and its clearing entry becomes a pointer to where it went. Nothing is admitted to `notes/` *from* the clearing without meeting the ordinary standard.
- **It is not the tensions list.** A tension is a contradiction kept live *inside* the book's doctrine (`notes/tensions.md`); a clearing entry is something *outside* what the center can see at all. A disagreement between two things the book holds is a tension. A thing the book has no way to hold is a clearing entry.
- **It grounds no inference from the poems.** Where the clearing points at the poems it does so as offering and as question, never as evidence, exactly as the poems' standing requires (`source standing.md`, the 2026-08-08 amendment). The poems are one place the not-yet-seen is already visible; naming that is not deriving doctrine from it.
- **It is not the author's lane.** The clearing surfaces what the center cannot see; what the book does about any of it — whether a blind spot becomes a subject, stays a margin, or is deliberately left outside — is the author's, like every question of scope and shape.

## Why it is an `ops/` register and not a topic map

The map set is capped to the corpus's own architecture, and adding one requires a /rethink pass with a recorded rationale (`CLAUDE.md`, Topic maps). The clearing is by definition *not* part of that architecture — it is what the architecture cannot reach — so it is the wrong thing to model as a thirteenth concern-map. It is a standing register, kept beside the reading queue and the decisions register, where a surface can hold items under a discipline of its own without being a claim in the graph.

## How a session uses it

Read `ops/clearing.md` when a reading or a pass turns up something that will not fit and the instinct is to decline it — the clearing is where it goes instead, with its commitment named. Add to it freely; the register is meant to grow. A reading that returns "resemblance, not arrival" has found a candidate for the singular; a divergence that will not reduce to a tension has found a fork to keep; a poem that faces what the corpus converted has raised a question. None of these is a failure, and none of them has anywhere else to live.
