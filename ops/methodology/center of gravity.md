# center of gravity

This is an author directive, issued 2026-08-02, global and standing. It is an authorial decision about the book's content, recorded here because the vault's rule is that such decisions belong to the author and are never inferred from the corpus.

## The decision

The book's centerpieces are two, and the author's role is to get out of the way of both:

1. **The convergence of thinkers** — fingers pointing at the moon. Independent arrivals at the same finding, quoted, with their refusals kept intact. The corpus's own strongest evidence class, the isomorph, is this shape, and its two worked exemplars are the guild's one law ("transformation is enacted participation inside a held container — word said aloud, witness present," reached "with zero disagreement across every lineage the book loves" — arc-gloss: overlay four) and the Stance attractor ("a dozen vocabularies orbiting one attractor: being present without agenda while remaining available to act" — arc-gloss: ch13).
2. **The lived practice** — walking the trail; seeing and feeling the moon rather than reading about it. The somatic ground is kept undiminished: noticing the current state in the body, and the felt shift as verification, are the practice pole's floor and are not part of what this directive demotes.

**The demotion.** The system — the Generative Triad (Wall, Door, Floor), the twelve primitives, the molecule formulas, the three loops — is scaffolding: an uncertain secondary or tertiary concern, not a given outcome of the book. It may house insights, and it earns that housing exactly where it rests on knowledge established outside the project — McLaren's emotion research, Gendlin's felt shift, the polyvagal work. It is never treated as the book's presumed core, and a new proprietary taxonomy is not the centerpiece.

## The divergence clause

This intentionally diverges from the corpus's weighting, and the divergence is one-directional: the corpus's deepest layer already states the decision — "There will be a Triad and a set of Primitives and a small chemistry of Molecules… None of these is the point. They are scaffolding. They are the wooden forms a craftsman uses to coax a curve into existence and then removes when the curve holds" (abridged: prologue) — while the corpus's page-count elaborates the system at length. **Volume in the corpus is not centrality in the book.** Semantic search, coverage tables, and "contested" markings all inherit the sources' volume, so an agent following the instruments will drift back toward the system. Where a source's emphasis conflicts with this directive, the directive wins.

## Vocabulary note, added 2026-08-02

The decision above is recorded in the terms it was issued in, including the corpus's own labels — the guild, the five rooms, the valley, the Stance. A later directive the same day, `naming and epistemic humility.md`, walks those labels back: they are candidate names for concerns rather than the concerns, and vault prose states the concern and attributes the label. That directive governs how this one's material is written up from here; this text is left as issued, because a record of an author decision is quoted rather than edited.

## Refinement, 2026-08-04

The author sharpened the demotion two days later, in a statement recorded whole at `the book's current shape.md`. Two things changed. The schemes move "from architecture to instrument" — "useful for seeing with; not the thing the book is wrapped around" — which names what they are rather than only what they are not. And he supplied the model: the Vijñāna Bhairava offers a hundred and twelve short practices while claiming neither completeness nor non-overlap, so that "nobody asks whether practice forty-seven duplicates practice twelve, because they're ways in rather than a map of the territory." A session applying this directive uses that specification, because it decides cases the word *scaffolding* leaves open — most usefully, that gaps and overlaps in the scheme set are not defects and are never work.

The same statement raises the somatic floor this directive already refused to demote. The felt shift is not one doctrine among several but the book's whole verification standard, and the author judges it currently underdefended. Research priority follows accordingly.

## Consequences for work

1. **Research and extraction priority** runs the guild and the practice curriculum ahead of system detail: the five rooms, the coaches' five theories of suffering, the valley, and the convergences they carry; the Stance, the kata, and channeling as practices with verification conditions. The primitives, the molecule catalogue, and the engine detail are extracted as-needed — when a convergence or a practice requires the vocabulary — not as a program.
2. **A thinker note's deliverable is the pointing**: the one finding a drafting session would quote, the refusal worth keeping (counterweight), the anchors, and the connections. A literature review fails the writing test, and so does a roster entry with no convergence needing it.
3. **The named regression**: organizing new material by the system's taxonomy — sorting thinkers into triad corners, translating findings into molecule formulas. When the housing serves the insight, use it; when the taxonomy is doing the organizing, stop.

## Material arrives pre-sorted by the system it demotes

Added 2026-08-05 after the same treatment had to be invented twice from opposite directions, three days apart.

The corpus's most quotable convergence passages are the ones written to dock the convergence into the system — the roster's rooms, the fault-line register, the referral network — so a session searching for the centerpiece finds the scaffolding's version of it first, and finds it in better prose (`ops/observations/the corpus pre-sorts the convergence material by the system it demotes.md`, three instances). An outside document commissioned on the book's subject inherits the same organization for the same reason: the schemes are what the project's own vocabulary makes available. The synthesis received 2026-08-05 is organized by the three-loop scheme from its first paragraph to its last and supplies each loop with neural correlates ([[a synthesis organized by the schemes offers to promote them back to architecture]]).

The treatment, which both cases arrived at independently and which is now written down so it stops being re-derived: **take the findings and decline the organizing principle.** Every claim drawn from such material states its finding in plain terms and says what was actually measured or said. Where the source attributes something to a scheme's element, the claim records the observation rather than the attribution — the synthesis reports suppression of a named brain network under psychedelics, so the vault records that, not that a loop released.

The reason this needs stating rather than trusting to judgment is that the failure is silent. A demotion is a decision about status, and an empirical warrant restores status without anyone arguing for it: a session finds the scheme confirmed, builds on it, and the decision of 2026-08-04 is reversed by accretion. Nothing in the maintenance apparatus would fire.
