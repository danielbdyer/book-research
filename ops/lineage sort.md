## What this file is for

Tier alone gives a pile-count. Crossing load against discipline produces cells that are visibly empty, and an empty cell is a reading question. The grid states its own purpose this way and it is the reason the second axis exists: "Crossing tier against where the person got their epistemics is what makes the grid generative, because it produces cells you can see are empty. An empty cell is a search query" (`mesh grid: the second axis`).

Standing cautions, extended 2026-08-06 by the adverse round the author directed against the grid's own recommendations (the verification trace is `archive/research — counterpoint round background.md`, round two).

The sorting below is the mesh grid's, cosigned by the author at 65–75% and "gently," so every assignment below is a proposal rather than a seating; the `[v]` marks say which ones the vault has independently checked. The header's reading-allocator disclaimer is load-bearing rather than decorative: the grid's native framing weighs evidence ("value is proportional to damage"; "two independent witnesses… materially stronger than one"), the stance forbids that ranking, and every unmarked assignment copied from the grid carries the weighing with it unless this file strips it.

**The grid's volume instruction is kept as a specimen of an instruction that failed twice, not as guidance.** It read: "Do not sort all thirty. The result is known: they are Tier 5, you said so yourself, and confirming a premise is not research" (`mesh grid: how to run this`) — and the two known misfiles, Gendlin and Hudson, both sat in exactly the cell that instruction protected. The recount that supersedes it is the proposed function-count in `ops/reading queue.md` (the one-day check starting with Scharmer), and any recount runs with its count open: the grid's own recount instruction pre-announced "expect two or three yeses" and named its own prior finds as the winners, which is anchoring by the book (Tversky and Kahneman, *Science* 185, 1974, verified at web depth).

**The method itself has a named literature, and the vault's instruments are its answer.** Confident single-pass expert sorting is the method the clinical-versus-mechanical literature says to check — mechanical combination consistently matches or beats impressionistic expert judgment (Grove, Zald, Lebow, Snitz and Nelson, *Psychological Assessment* 12(1), 2000), the halo error names the Hudson shape exactly (Thorndike, *Journal of Applied Psychology* 4(1), 1920), and the literature's founder is Meehl, whom the grid itself seats as an adversary — so the vault trusts an instrument its own seated adversary's research says to recount mechanically. `scripts/queries/lineage-sort.sh` ("where the script and this file disagree, the script is right") and the `[v]` marks are the recount posture already installed. One more reflexive fact, kept: the grid's nine provenance families held one contemplative tradition and marked every other by hand as "non-Indic," so its own sharpest diagnosis — the empty cells are "the shape of the reading that has happened so far" — applies to its category list, and every future family scheme is read as a search-history artifact too. The company row stays unelaborated as a default, but no longer under the grid's instruction — the function-count decides.

**A blind repeat pass confirmed the adverse case on 2026-08-06 and extended it** (round three of `archive/research — counterpoint round background.md`; the pass read only the grid, the stance, and the register, so its agreements with round two arrive independently). It re-found the pre-announced recount, citing the expectancy literature this time — experimenters produce the results they are led to expect (Rosenthal and Fode, 1963) — the Wilber–al-Qushayrī uniqueness contradiction, the Scharmer double-filing, and the clash between the grid's witness-weighing and the stance. Four agreements between passes that never met is the strongest standing this file can record for those cautions. The pass added two instrument-level findings carried here: the empty-cell method can find witnesses and adversaries but never absence-as-evidence, which makes it a confirmation engine by construction (Nickerson, 1998, is the named literature); and the grid's severity is distributed in the pattern the advisory-sycophancy literature predicts — staged attacks delegated to future reading, ratifying sentences aimed at its addressee, contempt reserved for an absent third party (Sharma and colleagues, ICLR 2024). Both findings apply to every advisory document a model writes for this project, this vault's own outputs included.

Two rows of that table were the first thing the sort found, before any thinker was moved, and they are now filled. On 2026-08-05 the vault held no claim note for the proposition that selflessness is reached through the fullness of the I, and only a near neighbour for the proposition that practice and realization are one — while holding seated adversaries against both: Nāgārjuna, Loy, Nishitani and Weil against the first; Han against the second. The vault held objections to two things it had never stated.

Writing them settled the grid's question in the safer direction. Both propositions turned out to be stated plainly and repeatedly in `sources/` — the first as the ladder's crown, the diagnosis of contracted fullness, and the book's closing gesture; the second as a clause the atlas cross-references from the roster into the practice curriculum itself. So neither was being carried by the prose, which was the dangerous case the grid's discipline warns about: "either it is not load-bearing or it is being carried by the prose. The second is the dangerous case, and the prose is good enough to hide it" (`mesh grid: the Tier 1 discipline`). What had happened was smaller and is worth naming as a pattern rather than an incident: the vault had extracted the corpus's *figures* and its *company* thoroughly, and had not extracted two of its plainest propositions, because neither arrives attached to a name or an image.

One finding came out of writing them and is left open. The anti-instrumentality claim carries a contradiction inside the book rather than outside it — if the practice is not for anything, why does the book promise transformation — recorded in the note and available for a tension if the author wants one.

## The holes, ranked

**The sharpened form of this queue now lives at `ops/reading queue.md`** — each hole restated as a question that can fail, with its stake, a page-level address, what it inherits from completed readings, and the grade of the current evidence. The ranking below — the grid's search map in its order, with its two addendum entries appended — stands as the record of what the sort found; the queue is where the work is.

**What changed on 2026-08-05, and what did not.** Every hole below now has at least one chair in `notes/` — the sixth gained its chair, [[ubuntu personhood]], later the same day — each written from the grid alone with no primary work read. That converts the queue from *find the names* to *do the readings*, which is progress of a specific and limited kind: a chair records what the vault expects a thinker to say and what it expects them to refuse, and both expectations are the grid's until a primary text is opened. Nothing below is closed. A hole closes when a reading has been done and the note says what the text actually holds.

1. **An adversary for the felt shift.** Chairs: [[nisbett and wilson]], [[eric schwitzgebel]], and [[steven katz]] reseated against this claim specifically. Highest priority in the whole sort, and it coincides with the torch's first item and with the author's own statement of 2026-08-04 that the standard has "little defense." Read Nisbett and Wilson first: their own carve-out for present-state reports may put the felt shift outside their range, and that finding decides how much the rest of the reading has to carry.

   **A candidate defence arrived 2026-08-05 and it changes what this hole needs.** The felt sense is precise enough to *reject* a formulation, and a rejection is a cost paid in the world that fluency never pays — which yields a test separating a felt shift from a felt confirmation ([[the felt sense rejects formulations and that friction is what separates it from fluency]]). It comes from Gendlin, so it is the standard's own author answering his standard's objection. It addresses the fluency limb and not the introspective-unreliability limb, so the reading queue above stands unchanged; what has changed is that the hole is no longer answerless while it waits.
2. **An adversary for the participation law.** Chair: [[ernest gellner]]. Still missing: Popper, the replication reformers. Hunt specifically the argument that participation-epistemics immunize themselves against disagreement.
3. **Independent arrivals from empirical science, systems, and analytic philosophy.** Chairs: [[michael polanyi]] first, then [[heinz von foerster]], [[harold garfinkel]], [[ludwig wittgenstein]], with [[donald schon]] and [[keith johnstone]] added from practitioner pedagogy. This is what converts the participation law from a lineage position into a finding. Still missing: Deci and Ryan.
4. **An adversary for the Stance.** Chair: [[willoughby britton]], extended 2026-08-05 with her actual taxonomy, prevalence figures, and the negative-valence-against-adverse-effect distinction that makes her an adversary worth having rather than a stick. Still missing: the depersonalization and derealization literature. One finding from the same literature runs the other way and the vault now holds it — an accurate framework is itself protective ([[a framework for what is happening is itself a protective factor]]), which is the first material here in the book's favour.
5. **An independent arrival for the householder claim from outside the Indic world.** Chair: [[hasidic worship through corporeality]], which is the strongest of the candidates because it adapts nothing — the tradition never located the sacred elsewhere. [[al-qushayri]] partly fills it from the same discipline, and [[ignatius of loyola]] is the weaker adaptation case. Still missing: Brother Lawrence read directly, Layman Pang.
6. **A third pole for the self-primacy fork.** Chair: [[ubuntu personhood]], built 2026-08-05 at the author's request. It was the sort's only chairless hole; African philosophy remains the only discipline with a single occupant. The entry points are Menkiti's 1984 essay and Gyekye's 1987 reply read together — the tradition's own disagreement maps onto the book's fork — with Metz as the analytic reconstruction and Matolino, Kwindingwi and Praeg supplying the capture critique.
7. **The pre/trans guardrail.** The book cannot distinguish dissolution from regression, and neither can its reader. [[ken wilber]] names it; [[daniel ingram]] supplies the most practically developed answer, a named sequence a person can locate themselves in; [[willoughby britton]] measures the same territory from outside and now carries real figures; Grof remains queued; and [[gurdjieff and ouspensky]] is the case history of a system that induced destabilization deliberately, had a teacher present to read it, and still produced casualties. Rising with every peak-state passage.
8. **The interiority-of-cause audit.** Run every compound diagnosis against the question: does this relocate a structural condition inside a person? The counterweights are already seated and the audit is not.
9. **Precedent-bench post-mortems.** What design feature, if any, prevents a developmental scheme from becoming a status ladder among the people who adopt it? No one on the bench solved it, and the grid calls it the most valuable unanswered question in the set. It has an operational twin in this vault's own `state:` field (`ops/tensions/the metabolic arc is a developmental scheme with no anti-ladder feature.md`). The bench is now four chairs — [[ken wilber]], [[douglas hofstadter]], [[gurdjieff and ouspensky]], [[mihaly csikszentmihalyi]] — and what each needs is a reception history rather than a primary reading, which is the right size for a precedent. One candidate answer surfaced while writing them and is not an answer yet: Hofstadter is the bench's only good outcome and the only one who handed the reader no tool.

**A finding the four chairs produced together, which no single one of them carries.** Four traditions with nothing else in common refuse the same thing about this book's form, and they were written on the same day without the pattern being looked for: [[gurdjieff and ouspensky]] hold that the work is impossible alone and that reading about it produces the illusion of having done it; [[al-qushayri]] requires a shaykh because a person in a state is the worst-placed judge of it; [[thomas hubl]] holds that what was never individual cannot be metabolized individually; and [[martin buber]] holds that the Thou must be addressed by someone. The vault's own finding is that the book's native genre is a letter read alone ([[the book's native genre is the letter from a friend read alone]]). That is either the book's central vulnerability or the exact place its form is doing something the four could not have anticipated, and the vault does not settle which. A letter is the one solitary form that is nonetheless addressed.

## The discipline-level read

The crossing's two most populated rows are now `adversary` and `precedent`, and the predominance of precedents inverts the grid's original picture: the grid sorted for what the book cannot lose and found mostly holes, and the apparatus sweeps that followed found mostly parents. Eight of the nine uncredited ancestors belong to one formation — American and American-adjacent clinical and human-potential psychology between about 1930 and 1975 ([[the apparatus descends from the settlement the book positions itself against]]) — so the apparatus's company is people who got there first, not people who got there separately. The one exception is the privation move, which has five independent arrivals across unrelated fields ([[the privation move is an independent arrival across five fields rather than a book coinage]]), and none of the five has been read. The sweep that found the ancestors also added `classical-philosophy` as a discipline, because Aristotle would otherwise have been filed under a family that is not his — the same rule that earlier added `abrahamic-contemplative` and `african-philosophy`.

Of the three disciplines the grid originally found near-empty — analytic philosophy, systems and design, practitioner pedagogy — two have since gained chairs: analytic philosophy holds Wittgenstein as an arrival and Schwitzgebel as an adversary, and practitioner pedagogy holds Johnstone, Schön, and Robbins. Systems and design still holds only von Foerster, Alexander, and Wilber, none of them read. The grid's reading of the original coincidence stands as the sort's most useful single output, and it is about the reading rather than the field: "That is not a coincidence. It is the shape of the reading that has happened so far, and the grid's most useful output is making that shape visible from outside" (`mesh grid: the family-level read`). The same caution governs every concentration this file reports: a shared formation among the ancestors describes the search history, not the field.

Two concentrations persist. Every seated adversary of the central verb is a continental philosopher, so the strongest objection the book has met comes from inside the tradition it is objecting within. And every independent arrival currently in play is in conversation with Heidegger — studying with him, translating him, or building against him — which is what makes the independence test fail.

African philosophy is the crossing's thinnest discipline in a way the others are not: one occupant, [[ubuntu personhood]], built 2026-08-05 at the author's request, with every primary reading undone — Menkiti's 1984 essay and Gyekye's 1987 reply together, then Metz's analytic reconstruction. The sixth hole therefore has a chair and no reading, where it once had neither, and the grid's sharper framing survives the fill: the self-primacy fork has two poles because the room has two traditions, and after every addition the room still has two.

Two load assignments run against the grid and say so on their chairs. [[joe hudson]] carries `thesis-bearing` by the vault's count of functions — he holds one of the five theories of suffering alone and is the referral network's named destination for a somatic misfire ([[the referral network sends readers to a name the sort filed as company]]) — where the grid files him in its company tier twice. [[tony robbins]] keeps `adversary` and gains the NLP genealogy the corpus never prints (Grinder; modelling Erickson, Satir, and Perls), which puts the will pole downstream of the same settlement the apparatus descends from. Hudson is the second instance of the defect this axis exists for, Gendlin the first, and the property that generalizes is that a sort run once reproduces its blind spot until something recounts.

Two rows of the crossing report zeros that are not backlogs, and `scripts/queries/lineage-sort.sh` says so in its own output so a future session does not read them as work. The company row is empty by design — those roughly thirty names get notes only when a claim reaches for one, and the grid's instruction is not to sort them. The names-existing-practice row is thin for the same reason.
