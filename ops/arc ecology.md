# The arc ecology — every sequence the book and its material hold, sorted by phase grammar

## 1. What was asked, and the answer in one paragraph

The book walks its reader through the arc of the psychedelic experience with no substance involved, and it states one law about itself: the pacing enacts the arc the chapters explain. The question was what other sequences the book and its material hold — every phase model, cycle, ladder, threshold, or staged progression that says what a person, a practice, or a work undergoes in order — and how they sort when sorted by their phase grammar rather than by the tradition that supplied them. The five regional reads returned roughly sixty instances, and they fall into fourteen structural families. The answer has three parts. First, the book's own arc is a single concentrated traverse whose apex the apparatus vacates, and the traditions the book honours almost never hold that shape: what they hold is ordered paths with gates, withdrawals that make room, instants that refuse staging, and refusals of any arc at all, so the "staged arc" row of the practice-model survey does not survive a per-instance read. Second, the real axis under the families is what governs a transition — the text's pacing, a felt sign, ripeness, a second person's judgment, conduct, grace, repetition, or an external act — and no arc from the source traditions governs a transition by a felt sign in the moment, which is the governor the book seats as its referee. Third, the author's own record carries movement everywhere and a staged arc almost nowhere: his event has no phases, the poems' dominant grammar is recurrence without a terminus and the act named and handed over, and one poem refuses destination-shaped movement in his own words. So the six-phase arc describes the experience the book stages and not the event the author reports, and the peak withdrawn is the place where the book already knows this.

## 2. The vault's existing organizing structures, and why none of them is this map

The vault already reads sequence in five places, and each answers a different question. Lens 1, the Arc (`the-arc.md`), one of the family files at the repository root that each read the whole graph through one organizing idea, asks what the reader undergoes in order, and holds the book's own traverse with its four overlays. Lens 3, the Movements, asks what grows station by station, and holds the four movements of the two books. Lens 13, the Loop, asks where in the returning cycle one is, and says outright that the loop "is the researcher's and the reader's motion, not the reader's single journey." The practice-model survey held sequence in two rows, staged arc and loop, and named the tension "sequence against simultaneous conditions" (`ops/practice-model ecology.md`, sections 3 and 7). And `ops/methodology/the four kinds of structure.md` sorts an arc as a dramaturgy — "a plot — the developmental traversal a reader makes through the book" — and names the category error this survey has to avoid, "a stack of what-contains-what mistaken for a sequence of what-happens-next." The chapter concordance, the table comparing the book's drafts chapter by chapter, adds the vault's notation for order-change between draft states, the five directed relations, and the reference directive adds the rule that "a claim about sequence is a claim about named units in an order" (`ops/methodology/reference by name.md`). None of these sorts the sequences by how they work. Each holds one sequence or one kind and reads the book through it; this map holds all of them and asks what marks their onset, how their apex is handled, whether their return completes, what marks their exit, and what moves a person from one phase to the next.

## 4. The deepest structural finding: the governor and the exit

Three findings arrived from separate regions and meet at one point.

**What governs a transition is the real axis beneath the families, and the book's own governor has no company among the traditions.** No other tradition arrives at that governing mechanism independently, which is the condition under which the vault counts a finding as company rather than proof. Sorted by governor: the text's pacing and a leader's clock (the book's six phases; the Forum); a felt sign in the moment (Gendlin, Hudson, McLaren; the loops' "you can usually feel which one"); ripeness (the metabolic arc; Yasutani's "Makyos do not occur when you are dawdling, neither do they appear when your practice has ripened"); a second person's judgment (Ignatius's director, dokusan, Winnicott's calibration, Moreno's director reading physiological indicators, Deida's friends, Engler's clinician); conduct (al-Qushayrī, p. 106); grace (śaktipāta, Buber, anupāya); repetition and time (Daodejing 48, Robbins, Wilber's practice, the five remembrances); the course reviewed afterward (Ignatius §§333–336); and an external act (the sending). The traditions' region states its own conclusion: "Nothing in this region governs a transition by a felt signal in the moment. The one rule that tries is Ignatius §335, and it inverts by direction of travel." The felt-sign family is entirely modern practitioner material, and the book's own arc is not in it either: the six phases are governed by the prose's respiration, which puts the reader in the patient's position for exactly the traverse the book calls participation. That is an inference from the sort, and it is the sharpest thing the sort produced.

**Every arc marks its onset; almost none marks its finish inside the person.** The book's own case is already a note, [[the practice forms specify when to begin and not how to tell one is finished]], and it generalizes across the whole collection. Of the eighteen instances in the book's own region, exactly two locate their terminus outside the reader's judgment — the sending, in a second person's answer, and the metabolic arc, in ripeness — and every other arc governs its transitions by the text's pacing or leaves them to a felt sign the reader reads alone. The vault's only exit criterion is stated for a word: perfectible forever against answerable, "and therefore, in a precise sense, not yet real" (walked VI). It is never stated for a practice, a phase, or a reading. The clinical region found the same from its side: every exit marker that is not somatic belongs to somebody else — a director's read of indicators, a clinician's assessment, a group's evening review, a mother's calibration, friends with an agreed consequence, a leader with a syllabus. That is the discrimination finding arriving from the arcs' side rather than the guards' ([[a solitary reader can run every guard that declines to evaluate and none that discriminates]]).

**The return is the vault's most-asserted and least-built station, and the traditions split on it.** The vault says in three places that the return completes — the Stance's "point to return to," the learning arc's "the integration is the return," the Sufi second sobriety — and the scaffold, the book's structure as read out of the graph, names Book II's return "the thinnest position in the whole graph… no dedicated 'the practitioner returns / stewardship of the self' claim exists." The traditions answer four ways: the Sufi return completes; the apophatic ascent terminates and never returns; the Stance recurs; the four movements end in withdrawal. And the one textually read tradition whose return completes grades the road the book declines to grade: al-Qushayrī's path is "a single ordered path rather than two axes," with states gated on stations fulfilled.

Put together: the book's arc is governed by pacing and ends in a return; its founding event has no phases and, in the primary layer, ends in a sending; and the governor the book seats as its referee for everything else, the felt sign, governs no arc in the room except the three modern inquiries. Whether that is a defect or the book's own precision is the author's call, and it is the same call the practice-model survey handed him from the other side.

## 6. The empty cells

- **An exit criterion for a practice, a phase, or a reading.** The vault owns one, for the word. The book's own reading-side offer is a non-criterion, "If you finish quickly, you have not finished."
- **A reader-side transition mechanism.** The six phases are governed by prose density, which governs the text; nothing states what moves a person between phases, and the four movements state nothing at all.
- **The return as a built position.** Asserted three times, thinnest in the graph; "afterglow" appears in six notes.
- **Descent craft.** The descent's science is grounded and full; almost no craft claim says how a page performs a descent, against a whole cluster on how a page performs a peak. The executed peak chapter "loops and slows as designed but does not fully go silent — it still carries heavy scientific exposition" (`ops/scaffold.md` §3).
- **Mid-arc failure.** No account of a reader stranded between deepening and peak; [[daniel ingram]] is the one candidate and is unread; no timeline of harm exists beside the measured curve of benefit.
- **An ending that is neither a peak nor a return.** The finite: every terminus the vault holds is a return, and the poems touch loss constantly and refuse to let it terminate.
- **Book I's phase grammar.** "Its form, grammatical person, and imagery are unwritten" (`ops/scaffold.md` §14).
- **Re-reading, and duration.** The metabolic arc moves back and forth; the traverse runs once; nothing says what a second reading is, and "you move at the speed of trust" refuses a schedule without supplying a theory of pacing at the scale of a person.
- **The inter-letter interval.** The correspondence asks the reader to answer between letters and says nothing about what the interval is.
- **Sources the book's vehicle rests on and nobody has read.** Van Gennep, whose three beats carry the sixteen days; Teresa of Ávila's mansions and John of the Cross's night, named together at abridged p. 49 with no vault presence; Augustine's turning, present as memory and form and absent as an arc; the four upāyas as a set; Scharmer's U, the one outside arc the executed draft prints; Hudson's "Stages of Emotional Development," read on 2026-09-02 and unrecorded; Days Two and Four of the Forum; Lewin's 1951 unfreeze, change, refreeze.

## 7. The tensions the survey consolidates

Grouped, deduplicated across the five regions, each already on record somewhere or quotable from the vault; the survey gathers them so the reading program does not walk into them blind. Where the vault's filing rule applies — a difference between two states of one element is a row, a disagreement that would change what the book is earns a tension — the destination is named.

**Inside the book's own arcs.**
1. Where the descent sits. `arc-gloss` and `abridged` place the Afterglow inside Part IV, "peak + descent"; `atlas §X.3` and `walked` head Part IV as the peak alone and fold descent into Part V. A row, now added to `ops/chapter concordance.md`.
2. Event or theme. The arc concentrates the central experience at one unit; the earliest edition distributes it across a themed part with no peak; the primary layer adds a third position, apex as terminus (poems #10, #15). Allocated on 2026-08-16 rather than resolved.
3. One spine or two — the developmental sequence recorded in `notes/the learning arc.md` and placed above the topic maps. The six phases and the four movements are kept distinct on purpose, and the peak-to-participation alignment is marked synthesis — yet the crossings table — a table crossing the book's units against the family files, the documents at the repository root that each read the whole graph through one organizing idea, in the lens index `the-lenses.md` — gives the Psychedelic Moment a cell in every column and reads the result as "the interpolation you were reaching for." A vault-state finding, filed as an observation.
4. Whether the return completes. "The returning is the practice" (a cycle), against reincorporation and the second sobriety (a terminus), against the movements' withdrawal (neither). Three incompatible answers and no note stating them against each other. A tension candidate.
5. Sequence against simultaneous conditions. "Not steps but facets" against "paced to that curve"; the reconciliation on offer, [[the arc is the instrument that holds both poles of a tension a page cannot hold at once]], is a nascent first-principles derivation, so the bridge is weaker than either bank.
6. Inhabit against spend. `the-paradoxes.md` says a tension "is not a contradiction to solve. It is a design tension to inhabit"; the arc-instrument note says the book "front-loads one pole and spends toward the other." The same knot, both prescribed, never in one sentence.
7. Two senses of "arc." The metabolic arc is "not linear… fractal"; the traverse runs once and is irreversible; the corpus uses one word for both inside two chapters of one document.
8. The five states are not five stations. Atlas §V.3 orders five; abridged ch6's Stagnation formula orders three; the vault's template makes privated a lateral condition, not the second rung; and the vault glosses composting as "superseded but drawn on" where the corpus's composting is reanimatable. A vault-state finding, filed as an observation.
9. Irreversible event, reversible arc. [[what is recognized cannot be un-recognized]] against every arc walked twice; the bridge, state against station, is now known to be the book's assignment rather than al-Qushayrī's.

**Between the book and its traditions.**
10. State into station, against the *Risāla*. The executed draft: "the integration that converts an ahwal into a maqam — a state into a station" (abridged p. 38). Knysh 2007: a state that endures becomes stability, *tamkīn*, and the one item traced across the boundary travels station to state (pp. 79, 100–102, 206). A doctrinal disagreement, unfiled; candidate 45.
11. "Not a ladder" against the grading by grace. "The teaching insists this is not a ladder; the doors telescope" (walked p. 13) against the anupāya record's strict grading by śaktipāta with a fall-through order. Unfiled; candidate 46, pending the Tantrāloka reading.
12. The two five-term circuits. The tradition's five acts against the reframe's assembled five, which terminates in a new locus where the author's phrase terminates in a new mystery.
13. Ascent against descent inside one ladder. Bhartṛhari climbs; the Trika contains; the book uses the ladder to say the spoken word is the completion, and the reading limits the warrant to "nothing of parā is lost on the way down."
14. The arc against the arc's refusers. "If practice is not for anything, why does the book promise transformation?" — pressed from four incompatible directions by Dōgen, Nāgārjuna, Advaita, and the Daoist decrement, which must not be merged to make the refusal look unanimous.
15. Buber's lapse against a staged relation. Every Thou "must become an It" when a means is applied, so a curriculum that stages the relation as a phase runs the mechanism of the lapse.
16. Engler's gate against "already free." Something must be built first, and the contraindication is clinical; the softening withdraws the ladder and not the gate.
17. The corpus's Focusing six against Gendlin's six. A correction the notes have not taken, neither a row nor a tension.
18. Three disciplines refuse the arc-with-a-peak on one ground: Moreno's cultural conserve, Alexander's "discontinuous change is the mechanism by which living structure is destroyed," Dewey's absence of any category for the transformative episode. Candidate 51.

**Between the book and the author's record.**
19. Terminal apex against mid-arc peak; integration against recurrence; the destination arrow refused; write-from-the-now against narrated-from-the-future; sending against integration (section 5). None is filed as a tension; each is a sounding first.

**Among the mechanisms for the failed arc.**
20. Three accounts, deliberately unmerged — the unchanged interpreting structure, the spent window, the lines never developed — and beneath them a difference of units the vault has not converted: the window counts days, the lattice counts repetitions.

## 8. Where the arcs attach to the book's units

The Psychedelic Moment attaches to the traverse's vacated apex and to the instant family, and the primary layer's apex-as-terminus is a third position it has to hold. ## 10. Safeguards the survey mandates for any import

Four, and the first is the one the practice-model survey also named. **An arc is a dramaturgy, never an ontology**: every sequence that enters is asked which of the four kinds of structure it is, and a stack of what-contains-what is not a sequence of what-happens-next (`ops/methodology/the four kinds of structure.md`; `ops/methodology/the category test on arrival.md`). **Sequence claims are orderings of named units**, never of numbers, since the arc's shape is the author's live commitment and the chapter scheme fitted to it is not (`ops/methodology/reference by name.md`). **The primary layer grounds no doctrine**: the reflection and the poems supplied the axis, and every proposition drawn from them above is a fact about a text's form or a dated sentence, offered for his correction. And **the sort is a gathering**: fourteen families found by searching for them, so the selection-artifact audit — the check that an agreement among the gathered families was not produced by gathering them for the property they are reported to share — runs before any convergence among them is stated, and the traditions' four refusals of an arc are four refusals and not one.
