# chronology of the book's making

This file holds the origins of the book as a subject of its own — how it came to be, as distinct from what it claims. It exists because that subject had no single surface. Roughly forty dated fixture points were established across the five provenance reconstructions and are carried in the vault, but only as substantiating clauses inside notes whose subject is a doctrine; assembling the sequence cost a pass over eighteen files. This is the dated spine, in one place.

It sits in `ops/` for the same reason `ops/chapter concordance.md` does: it is provenance tooling for the manuscript, not a proposition of the book's framework. Claims arising from it are notes and are linked from the rows that produced them.

## How to read this table, and what it cannot do

**The dating is thread-level, not message-level.** The reconstructions state the limit plainly: "Timestamps attach to threads (last-updated stamps on retrieved chunks), not to individual messages… Where I say 'July 7,' I mean 'in the thread stamped July 7'" (provenance reconstruction: method and coverage). A date here locates a thread, not a minute.

**The search had a floor and a blind spot.** Only conversations outside Projects were searchable, and the reconstructions say so: "If any of the book work lived inside a Project, it is invisible to this reconstruction and nothing below accounts for it." Absence of a thing from this table is therefore weak evidence, never proof.

**One document arrives already written, and it is the important one.** The seed enters the record complete on 9 April 2025, described by the author as co-authored with an AI; which AI, over what span, in what venue, is unrecoverable. The reconstructions call this the wall: "Every question about the figures' true origin bottoms out at that wall" (provenance reconstruction: method and coverage). Every row before that date is context for a document whose composition is not in evidence.

**Speaker marks are load-bearing.** The reconstructions distinguish `[D]` the author's words from `[C]` the collaborator's, and the distinction changes what a row means: an idea in the author's hand is a different fact from the same idea in the collaborator's. Rows below carry the mark where it matters.

## What the spine shows that no single row does

**The order that recurs.** In every case the record can date, the author's plain formulation precedes the tradition's arrival, and the scholarship dresses it afterward. The claim is held at [[the book's honest origin story is noticing first and finding company after]]; the spine is its evidence in one column.

**Three strata, each in its own vocabulary.** Concerns in the poems, figures in the seed, system in the ontology work, welding in July 2026 — the vault's stratigraphy, stated at [[the figures precede the system by a year and the two were welded together later]]. The spine adds that the strata are also three different hands: the poems have no collaborator at all, the seed's is off-record, and the system's is documented.

**The corpus is four weeks old and the book is not.** Six of the seven documents in `sources/` were produced in July 2026, four of them in a single sitting on 28 July. The material they describe runs back to 2015. A reader of `sources/` alone would date the project to one month.

**Volume and origin run opposite.** The system occupies most of the corpus's pages and is the most recent and most collaborator-driven stratum; the poems occupy none of it and are the oldest and the only wholly unassisted one. This is the center-of-gravity directive (`ops/methodology/center of gravity.md`) visible as chronology rather than as instruction.

## What is not established

These are the origins questions the record leaves open. Each is carried in a note or a queue entry; they are gathered here so the gaps are visible beside the facts.

- **The seed's composing venue, collaborator, and duration.** The wall. Every figure's origin except one stops here.
- **Where the rainbow thread was first coined** — with Alise, or in the seed's composition. The third possibility the reconstruction offered, a poem, is closed: the collection contains no weaving vocabulary at all ([[the gold thread is the rainbow thread re-owned]]).
- **How *Aletheia* became a title, and when.** The vault has no note on either half of its own subject's name. The other half is traced to a June 2024 self-optimization catalogue and, beneath that, to a knowledge-management primitive imported in March 2024.
- **Whether the word *Trika* reached the author before 7 July 2026.** The retreat narrows the window to three and a half months; both readings survive.
- **The derivation, if any, from the earlier Presence/Process/Potential triad to the current one.** Project continuity established; triad-level derivation absent.
- **The making-order of the four descriptive documents relative to each other**, beyond the atlas's stated dependency on the treatment.
- **What was considered and dropped.** The original source-conditions request asked for it; nothing has answered. The record "shows almost no discarding of traditions… accretion, re-housing, and renaming."
- **Which of the three abridgments is the file held in `sources/`.**
- **The author's verbatim veto sentence** for the triad's first names. The phrase later installed as its reason is his own line from three nights earlier, about a different subject.
- **The identity of the second reader.** The first is real and has already answered.
- **The seventeen trial chapters, the Third Drawer, the valley letter, and the 29 July eighth artifact** — named, located in local storage, never held. The queue entry is in `ops/queue/extractions.md`.

## Maintenance

A new dated fixture goes in the table with its anchor and its speaker mark. A closed question moves from the last section into the table. This file records the sequence; it makes no claim about causation, and any causal reading belongs in a note where it can carry its own evidence.
