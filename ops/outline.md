## What this file is, and what it is not

This file is the vault's roll-up. Its upper half states, in one sequence, what the material currently asserts, with each step naming the claim that carries it, so that the argument the notes have accumulated can be read in a few minutes rather than reconstructed from thirteen topic maps. Its lower half is a census regenerated from the notes themselves: counts, the propositions the rest of the graph leans on hardest, the material grouped by concern and by where it has clumped, and what is thin.

**It is not a table of contents and no part of it assigns material to a chapter.** The distinction is not decorative, because the vault already made the opposite mistake once: on 2026-08-05 a session built a readiness rubric keyed to the fifteen named units in `ops/chapter concordance.md`, and the author removed it the same hour on the ground that those are prior drafts' chapter names he has not reified — "I'd rather let them emerge organically" (`ops/decisions.md`, the units row). Measuring against a structure is the strongest available way to make that structure permanent, so the sequence below is ordered as an argument — one claim standing on the one before it — and never as a chapter progression. Placement remains the author's act, the `supports:` field stays dormant until he performs one, and the census panel that counts claims per named unit reports only which claims cite that unit's locators in the drafts, which is a fact about citations rather than a proposal about chapters.

The sequence below is also this session's reading of the material and is revisable on sight. It was assembled from what the notes assert and from what the graph's link structure shows the material leaning on; where the author disagrees with a step, the step is wrong and the notes beneath it are not.

## The argument the material currently states

Fourteen steps in three movements. Each step names the claim that carries it, and the claim is where the substantiation and the source anchors live. The first version of this sequence (2026-08-08, morning) had ten steps; the review the author asked for the same day found that those ten stated the book's epistemology and its self-relation while omitting its reader, its practice, its account of agency, and its hazards — the corpus's own name for the missing stratum is "what the hands do" (atlas §VII) — and the four steps of the second movement repair that. The review's method and full findings are in `ops/completed.md` under 2026-08-08.

### First movement — what is found, and how anything gets to count as true

1. **The book reports one finding, and the finding is that deep change is disclosure of something already present rather than acquisition of something new.** It reclaims *psychedelic* for that movement — the concealed becoming visible, a movement rather than a molecule ([[psychedelic names a movement not a substance]]) — and states the same thing about its own reading at its smallest scale, in the front matter's promise that reading is remembering ([[reading is remembering]]).

2. **Two traditions with no historical contact arrived at that finding and named it the same way.** Heidegger's *aletheia*, truth as un-forgetting, and the Pratyabhijñā school's liberation as recognition of what never left are one structure reached twice ([[aletheia and pratyabhijna converge on truth as un-forgetting]]). This is the book's foundation and the author's named centerpiece.

3. **Others walked the same crossing, and the independence of their arrivals is audited rather than asserted.** The vault holds the footsteps and the company already seated ([[the crossover has footsteps and the book already seats some of the walkers]]), and it holds the finding that the independence fails in one place, where three of the names share a single teacher ([[the convergence's independence fails where three of its names share one teacher]]). Convergence is therefore company and never corroboration.

4. **Nothing counts as true in this book until the body countersigns it.** The felt shift is the single verification standard and everything else runs downstream of it ([[nothing enters the lattice the body has not countersigned]]). The author judges it currently underdefended, and the vault holds the attack in full: bodily assent can respond to fluent prose rather than to the claim ([[felt shift versus felt confirmation]]), and the premise that introspective access is reliable is the standard's undefended one ([[introspective access is the felt shift's undefended premise]]). One defense has arrived from the standard's own author: the felt sense is precise enough to reject a formulation, and a rejection is a cost fluency never pays ([[the felt sense rejects formulations and that friction is what separates it from fluency]]).

5. **Suffering on this account is fullness contracted rather than a self damaged, and the analysis of an absence points at its own remedy.** The doctrine is stated at [[suffering is contraction never corruption]], and the field it sits in divides into five theories of where suffering lives ([[the transformation field divides into five theories of where suffering lives]]). The operative content is the book's signature move: a suffering-state analyzed as a compound with a missing ingredient names what to supply ([[the diagnosis contains the cure]]). The privation reading that underwrites it is an independent arrival across five unrelated fields ([[the privation move is an independent arrival across five fields rather than a book coinage]]), which by the vault's own criteria makes it centerpiece material presented as system material. And the tradition's answer to why fullness contracts at all is play — a hiding whose whole point is the finding, with Winnicott arriving at the same boundary rule independently: it is a joy to be hidden and a disaster not to be found ([[a hiding that never intends to be found has stopped being play]]).

6. **What does the transforming is participation inside a held container, and this is the one claim that runs underneath every other concern — held with its audit attached.** The law is [[transformation is enacted participation inside a held container]]; its practice-side statement is that nothing transforms from the audience ([[nothing transforms from the audience]]); its two-person case is that the field between two people does work solitude cannot ([[the field between two people does work]]), with grief as the proof-text — the one emotion that structurally cannot complete without a witness ([[grief cannot complete alone]]). The graph's clustering puts the law's witnesses in a group of their own because they connect three regions equally, which is what a cross-cutting claim looks like from outside (`ops/convergence points.md`, region 5). The audit stays attached because the notes contest what the outline's first version settled: the six lineages' unanimity is an artifact of their having been selected for the property they then agree on ([[the unanimity claim is an artifact of how the lineages were selected]]), the law has no seated opponent and its oldest objection is that participation is contamination ([[the participation law has no opponent and its oldest objection is that participation is contamination]]), and its best independent support is a witness who reached it through crystallography with no debt to the book's lineages ([[the participation law has a witness who came to it through crystallography]]).

7. **A held container is not a metaphor; it has an anatomy, and the self is the container every other container presupposes.** The claim — boundary, aperture, and ground compose every container whatsoever, argued from twenty-nine builders in unrelated media — is [[the triad is the anatomy of every container]], and what the three structures exist to make possible is an open middle that is identical with none of them ([[the open center is made possible by the three structures and identical with none of them]]). The vocabulary rule belongs to the same step: the anatomy wears ordinary names because the deepest structures hide by being always there, and mythic names would let a reader file them as mythology ([[ordinary names keep the deepest structures visible]]). This step was absent from the first version, which mentioned the anatomy only to demote its notation; the atlas's own closing section makes it one of the book's six masses, and the demotion in the last step below strips the formalism, not this.

### Second movement — the reader, the practice, and what the practice costs

The first version of this sequence had none of the following four steps, and the omission had a measurable shape: the lived practice is one of the author's two named centerpieces and the whole graph carries it in three practice-category notes out of 246, so a ranking by links could not surface it. These steps are carried by claims that already exist; where the claims are thin, the step says so.

8. **The book's addressee already had the big experience, and it did not hold — and the mechanism for why is one sentence.** The author defined the reader by that biographical fact and shifted the promise accordingly: "you already did, it didn't hold, here's why, and here's what to do about that" (`ops/methodology/the book's current shape.md`, his statement of 2026-08-04, which this step restates). The mechanism: a state is interpreted by the structure that receives it, and nothing about having the experience changes the interpreting structure ([[an enormous experience is interpreted by the structure it did not change]] — the most-linked claim in the graph that the first version did not cite). The clinical literature grounds the same premise: the afterglow decays on a measured curve, and what persists tracks the completeness of the experience and the practice that follows it rather than the peak ([[what the big experience leaves behind depends on what follows it]]).

9. **The practice is one posture, defined so that failing at it is doing it.** The posture is presence without agenda while remaining available to act, and it is a point of return rather than a state — falling out and returning is the practice, not a failure of it ([[the basic practice posture is a point of return rather than a state]]). A dozen unrelated vocabularies orbit the same posture, which is the practice pole's counterpart to the thinkers' convergence ([[unrelated traditions converge on presence without agenda while remaining available to act]]). The practice refuses instrumentality on its own authority: it is the expression of realization rather than a means to it, which sits unresolved against the book's own promise of transformation ([[the practice is the expression of realization rather than a means to it]]). The honest hole is recorded beside the doctrine: the practice forms specify when to begin and never how to tell one is finished ([[the practice forms specify when to begin and not how to tell one is finished]]).

10. **Between doing and being done to, the book takes a third grammar, and the body referees the oldest fight in its field.** The guild's sharpest fork — surrender against will, let-go against take-hold — is refused settlement from doctrine and referred to the felt check case by case ([[surrender and will are adjudicated from the body rather than from doctrine]]), which gives the verification standard of step 4 a second job: not only a truth test but the referee between rival authorities. The grammar of that third position is the middle voice — action happening through the speaker with the speaker's consent — and the vault holds the honesty condition that keeps it from being a dodge: the middle voice is only earned against the strongest statement of the voluntarist pole ([[the middle voice is only earned against the voluntarist pole at full strength]]).

11. **The path has hazards the book can name and one it cannot yet tell apart, and the record keeps both.** The book cannot distinguish dissolution from regression, and neither can its reader — the diagnostic the object lacks, sized at a chapter by the lineage sort ([[the book cannot tell dissolution from regression and neither can its reader]]). The central posture is describable in clinical vocabulary as depersonalization, and the adverse-effects literature has never been put to it ([[the basic posture is describable as dissociation and the harm literature is unread]]). Two findings run the other way and are kept beside the hazards: an accurate framework for what is happening is itself a protective factor ([[a framework for what is happening is itself a protective factor]]), and the practice lineages police their own counterfeit — Zen's makyō instruction refuses the self-certifying experience ([[zen's makyo instruction refuses the self-certifying experience]]).

### Third movement — what the book is, and where it came from

12. **The participation law decides what the book can be to its reader, and the answer is an instrument rather than an authority — an authority the reader must be able to attack.** The book can only be auxiliary to a sovereign reader, serving a life that remains the reader's own ([[the book can only be auxiliary to a sovereign reader]]), and it installs that relation on page one by presenting itself as a tuning fork whose sound depends on the reader striking it ([[the book is a tuning fork and the reader is the striking surface]]). The everyday case the doctrine has to survive is that the ordinary Tuesday is the site, not the retreat ([[the tuesday is the temple]]). The design debt is recorded with the doctrine: the book asserts that a genuine refusal is sacred and never stages one, so the resisting reader has no modeled place to put the resistance ([[the reader's resistance has nowhere to land]]).

13. **The same law decides what the book's own completion requires, which is being answered.** A word held at the inner level can be perfected indefinitely and a word spoken aloud can be answered ([[a word at madhyama can be perfected forever a word at vaikhari can be answered]]); the manuscript's own unsent state is the live tension this produces ([[seventeen chapters rest at madhyama]]); and the book's native genre is the letter, because the one confirmed sighting of the recognition it promises happened at 4am reading a decade-old letter from a friend, alone ([[the book's native genre is the letter from a friend read alone]]). The counterface is kept: the held container is also the room where a word can be perfected forever ([[the perfectible conversation is the manuscript's most comfortable room]]). Release and readership are the author's lane by his own decision.

14. **The record of the making supports a smaller and stronger origin story than the corpus tells, and the audit of the system says what survives its demotion.** The author noticed things first, in his own words about his own life, and the scholarship arrived afterward and dressed them ([[the book's honest origin story is noticing first and finding company after]]); the poems are the headwaters and the corpus quotes them rather than the reverse ([[the corpus quotes the author's poems rather than the poems quoting the corpus]]); the making itself ran the law the book states, container-built at every documented scale ([[the making of the book enacts the law the book states]]). The author's decision demoting the system vocabulary from centerpiece to support is [[the system is scaffolding rather than the centerpiece]]; the complete formula audit found the notation records composition while every teaching is about a relation ([[the chemistry's teachings are structural and its notation is only compositional]]); and two things stand once the apparatus is set aside — a discipline of specificity ([[the diagnostic discipline survives the apparatus that carries it]]), and the kindness of structural diagnosis, which turns distress from a verdict about character into a question about machinery ([[distress is a question about machinery not a verdict about character]]).

The law that binds all fourteen to each other and to the prose is the book's one statement about itself: it performs what it describes ([[the book performs what it describes]]).

## What the sequence deliberately leaves out

Three things, each because including them would misrepresent the state of the work rather than because they are minor.

**The order of telling.** The sequence above is the order in which the claims support each other, and a book almost certainly does not open at step one. The disclosure constraint the author has stated — nothing revealed early that he learned late (`ops/methodology/the book's current shape.md`) — is a constraint on telling order and cuts across this list, and the arc the drafts execute begins with an entry movement that induces before it names.

**Everything the sequence compresses.** Fourteen steps stand over 246 notes. The seventeen live tensions are the clearest case, since a tension is held open on purpose and a spine that carries eight of them still understates how much of this material is deliberately unsettled; the registry is [[tensions]]. The census below carries the counts so the compression is visible rather than silent.

**The company itself.** Sixty-four thinkers hold chairs, forty evaluations await the author's seating decisions, and the map that gathers them is [[interlocutors]], the vault's centerpiece map by the author's own decision. They are absent from the steps because a step names a proposition and a chair names a person, not because they sit outside the argument.

## How this file stays current

The census below is regenerated by running `scripts/queries/outline.sh`, which rewrites everything between the two census markers and touches nothing above them. It reads the notes directly, so it cannot drift from the graph; running it after any batch of note work is the whole maintenance obligation, and the session hook fires a condition when the notes are newer than this file.

**The history of what emerged is the file's own git history.** Because the census is committed rather than generated on demand, `git log -p -- ops/outline.md` shows exactly which propositions appeared between any two runs, which rose in the rankings, and which fell out — the dynamic record, at no extra cost and with no separate log to maintain.

The written half above is a session's obligation rather than a script's. It is rewritten when the census shows the material has moved under it: a new proposition entering the most-linked-to list, a region changing shape, or a step's claim being superseded. /reflect is the phase that notices, since it is already reading connections across the graph.

The anti-census carries the same obligation in the opposite direction, and no script can relieve it: a census counts notes, and a missing thesis has no note to count. Its maintenance rule is retirement — when a claim lands that states a registered thesis, the entry comes out and the step that gains the claim absorbs it. Its refill rule is the sweep that seeded it: when the written half is next revised, the reviser checks the corpus's section theses and the author's tier-one statements against the current steps, and registers what neither carries. The poems are not part of the refill sweep, by author decision (2026-08-08, `ops/decisions.md`). The first sweep's full evidence is recorded in `ops/completed.md` under 2026-08-08, with the same day's back-out entry beside it.
