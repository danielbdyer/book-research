## What this file is, and what it is not

This file is the vault's roll-up. Its upper half states, in one sequence, what the material currently asserts, with each step naming the claim that carries it, so that the argument the notes have accumulated can be read in a few minutes rather than reconstructed from thirteen topic maps. Its lower half is a census regenerated from the notes themselves: counts, the propositions the rest of the graph leans on hardest, the material grouped by concern and by where it has clumped, and what is thin.

**It is not a table of contents and no part of it assigns material to a chapter.** The distinction is not decorative, because the vault already made the opposite mistake once: on 2026-08-05 a session built a readiness rubric keyed to the fifteen named units in `ops/chapter concordance.md`, and the author removed it the same hour on the ground that those are prior drafts' chapter names he has not reified — "I'd rather let them emerge organically" (`ops/decisions.md`, the units row). Measuring against a structure is the strongest available way to make that structure permanent, so the sequence below is ordered as an argument — one claim standing on the one before it — and never as a chapter progression. Placement remains the author's act, the `supports:` field stays dormant until he performs one, and the census panel that counts claims per named unit reports only which claims cite that unit's locators in the drafts, which is a fact about citations rather than a proposal about chapters.

The sequence below is also this session's reading of the material and is revisable on sight. It was assembled from what the notes assert and from what the graph's link structure shows the material leaning on; where the author disagrees with a step, the step is wrong and the notes beneath it are not.

## The argument the material currently states

Fourteen steps in three movements. Each step names the claim that carries it, and the claim is where the substantiation and the source anchors live. The first version of this sequence (2026-08-08, morning) had ten steps; the review the author asked for the same day found that those ten stated the book's epistemology and its self-relation while omitting its reader, its practice, its account of agency, and its hazards — the corpus's own name for the missing stratum is "what the hands do" (atlas §VII) — and the four steps of the second movement repair that. The review's method and full findings are in `ops/completed.md` under 2026-08-08.

### First movement — what is found, and how anything gets to count as true

1. **The book reports one finding, and the finding is that deep change is disclosure of something already present rather than acquisition of something new.** It reclaims *psychedelic* for that movement — the concealed becoming visible, a movement rather than a molecule ([[psychedelic names a movement not a substance]]) — and states the same thing about its own reading at its smallest scale, in the front matter's promise that reading is remembering ([[reading is remembering]]).

2. **Two traditions with no historical contact arrived at that finding and named it the same way.** Heidegger's *aletheia*, truth as un-forgetting, and the Pratyabhijñā school's liberation as recognition of what never left are one structure reached twice ([[aletheia and pratyabhijna converge on truth as un-forgetting]]). This is the book's foundation and the author's named centerpiece.

3. **Others walked the same crossing, and the independence of their arrivals is audited rather than asserted.** The vault holds the footsteps and the company already seated ([[the crossover has footsteps and the book already seats some of the walkers]]), and it holds the finding that the independence fails in one place, where three of the names share a single teacher ([[the convergence's independence fails where three of its names share one teacher]]). Convergence is therefore company and never corroboration.

4. **Nothing counts as true in this book until the body countersigns it.** The felt shift is the single verification standard and everything else runs downstream of it ([[nothing enters the lattice the body has not countersigned]]). The author judges it currently underdefended, and the vault holds the attack in full: bodily assent can respond to fluent prose rather than to the claim ([[felt shift versus felt confirmation]]), and the premise that introspective access is reliable is the standard's undefended one ([[introspective access is the felt shift's undefended premise]]).

5. **Suffering on this account is fullness contracted rather than a self damaged, and that move is not the book's own.** The doctrine is stated at [[suffering is contraction never corruption]], and the field it sits in divides into five theories of where suffering lives ([[the transformation field divides into five theories of where suffering lives]]). The privation reading that underwrites it turns out to be an independent arrival across five unrelated fields ([[the privation move is an independent arrival across five fields rather than a book coinage]]), which by the vault's own criteria makes it centerpiece material presented as system material.

6. **What does the transforming is participation inside a held container, and this is the one claim that runs underneath every other concern.** The law is [[transformation is enacted participation inside a held container]]; its practice-side statement is that nothing transforms from the audience ([[nothing transforms from the audience]]); its two-person case is that the field between two people does work solitude cannot ([[the field between two people does work]]). The graph's own clustering puts its witnesses in a group of their own not because they are isolated but because they connect three regions equally, which is what a cross-cutting claim looks like from outside (`ops/convergence points.md`, region 5).

7. **The participation law decides what the book can be to its reader, and the answer is an instrument rather than an authority.** The book can only be auxiliary to a sovereign reader, serving a life that remains the reader's own ([[the book can only be auxiliary to a sovereign reader]]), and it installs that relation on page one by presenting itself as a tuning fork whose sound depends on the reader striking it ([[the book is a tuning fork and the reader is the striking surface]]). The everyday case the doctrine has to survive is that the ordinary Tuesday is the site, not the retreat ([[the tuesday is the temple]]).

8. **The same law decides what the book's own completion requires, which is being answered.** A word held at the inner level can be perfected indefinitely and a word spoken aloud can be answered ([[a word at madhyama can be perfected forever a word at vaikhari can be answered]]); the manuscript's own unsent state is the live tension this produces ([[seventeen chapters rest at madhyama]]); and the book's native genre is the letter, because the one confirmed sighting of the recognition it promises happened at 4am reading a decade-old letter from a friend, alone ([[the book's native genre is the letter from a friend read alone]]). The counterface is kept: the held container is also the room where a word can be perfected forever ([[the perfectible conversation is the manuscript's most comfortable room]]). Release and readership are the author's lane by his own decision.

9. **The record of how the book was made supports a smaller and stronger origin story than the corpus tells.** The author noticed things first, in his own words about his own life, and the scholarship arrived afterward and dressed them ([[the book's honest origin story is noticing first and finding company after]]); the poems are the headwaters and the corpus quotes them rather than the reverse ([[the corpus quotes the author's poems rather than the poems quoting the corpus]]); and the making itself ran the law the book states, container-built at every documented scale ([[the making of the book enacts the law the book states]]).

10. **The system vocabulary is scaffolding, and the audit of it says what survives.** The author's decision demoting the triad, the primitives and the molecule formulas from centerpiece to support is [[the system is scaffolding rather than the centerpiece]]. The complete formula audit found that the notation records composition while every teaching in the chemistry is about a relation ([[the chemistry's teachings are structural and its notation is only compositional]]), and what stands once the apparatus is set aside is a discipline of specificity rather than a formalism ([[the diagnostic discipline survives the apparatus that carries it]]).

The law that binds all ten to each other and to the prose is the book's one statement about itself: it performs what it describes ([[the book performs what it describes]]).

## What the sequence deliberately leaves out

Three things, each because including them would misrepresent the state of the work rather than because they are minor.

**The order of telling.** The sequence above is the order in which the claims support each other, and a book almost certainly does not open at step one. The disclosure constraint the author has stated — nothing revealed early that he learned late (`ops/methodology/the book's current shape.md`) — is a constraint on telling order and cuts across this list, and the arc the drafts execute begins with an entry movement that induces before it names.

**Everything the sequence compresses.** Ten steps stand over 246 notes. The seventeen live tensions are the clearest case, since a tension is held open on purpose and a spine that mentions three of them understates how much of this material is deliberately unsettled; the registry is [[tensions]]. The census below carries the counts so the compression is visible rather than silent.

**The company itself.** Sixty-four thinkers hold chairs, forty evaluations await the author's seating decisions, and the map that gathers them is [[interlocutors]], the vault's centerpiece map by the author's own decision. They are absent from the ten steps because a step names a proposition and a chair names a person, not because they sit outside the argument.

## How this file stays current

The census below is regenerated by running `scripts/queries/outline.sh`, which rewrites everything between the two census markers and touches nothing above them. It reads the notes directly, so it cannot drift from the graph; running it after any batch of note work is the whole maintenance obligation, and the session hook fires a condition when the notes are newer than this file.

**The history of what emerged is the file's own git history.** Because the census is committed rather than generated on demand, `git log -p -- ops/outline.md` shows exactly which propositions appeared between any two runs, which rose in the rankings, and which fell out — the dynamic record, at no extra cost and with no separate log to maintain.

The written half above is a session's obligation rather than a script's. It is rewritten when the census shows the material has moved under it: a new proposition entering the most-linked-to list, a region changing shape, or a step's claim being superseded. /reflect is the phase that notices, since it is already reading connections across the graph.
