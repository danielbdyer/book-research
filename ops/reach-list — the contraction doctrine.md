That is a gap in the sources themselves, recorded on the minus-sign note.
7. The outside check on the mapping to the three malas (tier 2) has not run, so "corner for corner" cannot be asserted beyond what the corpus says.

**Assessment.** The material for stating the doctrine, tracing where it came from, and naming its limits is assembled, and the limits are the best-audited of any concern in the vault. The evidence is the weak part: the convergence and both outside arrivals rest on characterizations of books nobody here has read, and the strongest counterweight has no primary material at all. A drafting session could write the doctrine's plain statement and its honesty frame today. It could not yet write the convergence or the counterweight, which are the parts the center says the book is made of.
