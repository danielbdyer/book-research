## The method, so the result can be argued with

The formula audit worked one layer: every expression written with `=`, `+` or `−`. This pass works the layer above it — the book's *operational moves*, whether or not they carry notation. For each, the question was whether a named literature already holds the construct, and then whether any of the eight documents names it.

The search was run in three ways, and the third is the one that produced the finds:

1. **From the corpus's own admissions.** The outward survey has a recurring form — "an ancient patent-holder we've never cited," "strangely, nowhere in the project," "never been drawn, by us or, as far as I know, by anyone." Those sentences are a list of debts the corpus keeps and does not settle.
2. **From the figure glossary and the section heads**, taking each named move in turn.
3. **By candidate name.** Where a construct had a specific originator in mind, `rg -ci '<name>' .corpus/*.md` was run against the whole extracted corpus. This is the productive method and also the one whose coverage is bounded by what the searcher already knows. **Every negative below is a negative about this corpus, not about the field.**

## The shape they make

Eight of the nine ancestors belong to **one formation**: American and American-adjacent clinical and human-potential psychology, roughly 1930 to 1975 — Reich, Rogers, Maslow, Bowlby, Minuchin, Marcia, Leary, Zinberg. The exception is Aristotle, and the privation move is also the one part of the apparatus with independent arrivals across unrelated fields rather than a single lineage.

That formation is the one the corpus diagnoses. Walked IX names "the American transformation canon's radical interiority of cause" as the defect the contemporary teachers share and the book does not intend to inherit. The claim is [[the apparatus descends from the settlement the book positions itself against]]; the interiority audit already established that the *error* was not inherited, which is a different question from the descent.

Two sharpest instances, both worth stating on their own:

- **Maslow.** The book uses his coinage as a technical term and then makes his own late self-correction — the plateau is the point, not the peak — without knowing whose correction it is. To say it, the corpus borrows the Sufi state/station distinction, which it credits.
- **Reich.** The lineage is credited from the middle down. Every somatic authority the book leans on descends from the body-psychotherapy line he began, and the survey reaches for hysteresis, attractor landscapes and Bateson's learning levels to explain a construct a psychologist had already stated in the book's own register, which is the body.

## What is queued rather than chaired

Three where a lineage is plausible and the correspondence is looser than the nine above. Recorded so the next pass starts from something rather than from nothing.

- **Kurt Lewin** — action research and the T-group, and the dictum that to understand something you try to change it. This is the participation law in social science, and the T-group line runs Lewin → NTL → the encounter movement → est, which is the other half of a genealogy the corpus traces only through Austin's speech acts. Weaker than the nine because Lewin's claim is epistemological (you learn by intervening) and the book's is transformational (you do not change by watching); those are adjacent rather than identical.
- **Erikson** — Marcia's own frame, and the identity loop's ancestor. Absent from the corpus. Secondary to Marcia rather than a debt of its own.
- **Dewey and Kolb** — the experiential-learning cycle, which is the practice curriculum's pedagogy. Both absent. The correspondence is at the level of a whole tradition rather than a construct, which makes it a reading rather than a citation.

## Two cautions on this register

**The negatives are bounded by the searcher's knowledge.** Method 3 finds a construct only if someone already suspected it. A construct whose originator nobody thought to name would not appear here, and there is no way to bound how many those are. The same limit was recorded for the propositions the corpus states and the vault never wrote (`ops/observations/nothing counts propositions the corpus states and the vault never wrote.md`).

**Ancestry is not agreement.** These nine constructs largely do not agree with one another, and four of the ancestors would refuse the compression the book makes of them — Reich on the fold's gentleness, Rogers on the diagnostic apparatus, Zinberg on the individual address, Marcia on the prescription. A shared formation says something about where the reading went, which is a fact about the search history rather than about the constructs. The vault's own reading queue was found to have the same property on the same day, and both findings should be read the same way.
