# decision sitting — 2026-09-03

Two terms first, since they are the vault's and not yours. A **sounding** is a question about your own experience, your intention, or the book's shape — one only you can answer, since no reading could answer it for you. The second is what a tension note — a note recording a disagreement inside the book's doctrine, kept open rather than resolved — carries in its `treatment:` field: the proposed way of handling that disagreement. A session records that proposal and never originates its resolution, so every one below waits on you to decide. A **seat** is the role a thinker plays for the book — source tradition, reference lineage, adversary, company (thinkers who reached the same finding independently, held as company rather than proof), and so on — and a **load** is what a thinker carries for the book's argument, evident in what happens to the book if they leave.
