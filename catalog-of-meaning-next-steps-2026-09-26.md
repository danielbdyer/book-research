# The catalog of meaning: next steps

**What this document is.** This is the sixth of six documents answering the author's request of 2026-09-26 to enumerate "the catalog of meaning which would be explored in the future" from what pull request #23 brings into scope, "in a way to stimulate substantiation of next steps in a diffuse brainstormy way." It lists next steps. The first document, `catalog-of-meaning-2026-09-26.md`, names the space and explains how it is divided; the other four elaborate the catalogs this list draws from. Every step is a session's proposal. None is a task anyone owes, and none should be started because it is listed here.

**How the steps are sorted.** The steps are sorted by who can take each one, and within that by size. The vault already holds many of the questions that only the author can answer: 22 items and 41 questions about where each thinker belongs in `ops/decision sitting — 2026-09-03.md`, the decision sitting, which is the document gathering the questions only the author can answer, and seven more, B1 to B7, in `ops/practice-model ecology — candidate next steps.md`. Where a step repeats one of those, it names the existing item rather than adding a new one.

**One caution.** The author named his own recurring difficulty in the conversation: "already over engineering the system again, which was the recurrent um blocker that I hit in um getting into this system and using it in the first place" (1138). The vault counted about 415,000 words in its claims and about 18,000 in its drafts toward the manuscript (`ops/the vault's exit.md`). A list of next steps can feed that pattern. So the list starts with the steps that produce writing in the author's own words, and it ends with the steps that would add structure, which are the ones to take last or not at all.

## How this document is organized

1. The smallest steps, each a paragraph or less, for the author.
2. Larger steps for the author.

4. Readings, in the order the books need them.

Each changes the vault's structure, and each is the author's to decide. This catalog adds none.
