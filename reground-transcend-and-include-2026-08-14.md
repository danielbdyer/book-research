# Re-grounding the vault by transcend-and-include — a drafted plan (2026-08-14)

This document drafts the surgical re-grounding the author reserved for himself. The reframe of 2026-08-13 judged the current research frame over-embedded: its felt-shift verification standard, its four apophatic tensions, the second-chair remedy, the register-and-beauty hazard, and the convergence-plus-lived-practice center are seated as the ground the vault rests on, when the reframe makes them features nested inside a larger ground. The move the author instructed is transcend-and-include in Wilber's sense: the ecology of loci and the book's meta-facilitation position become the new ground (transcend), and nothing in the current frame is deleted, refuted, or weakened — each tension is preserved and re-nested as a guardrail within the new ground (include). The master claim already records the move at [[the ecology frame transcends and includes the verification frame rather than replacing it]] (the vault node that installs the ecology as ground while keeping the four tensions nested); this document is the drafted execution plan beneath it.

Sections 2 and 3 are drafted, not executed. Every re-nesting paragraph in section 2, every edge in section 3, and every ordering flip is proposed for the author to bless before it touches a load-bearing note.

Three terms recur below and take their referents here. A **ground seed** is a first-class note in `notes/` that states the new ground (the ecology, the book as one non-sovereign locus, the meta-facilitation position, the un-verifiability limit). An **include-seed** is an existing old-frame note (a tension, the standard, the second chair, the register hazard, the convergence) that the plan re-nests under the new ground without altering its content. **Re-nesting** is moving a claim from ground to a feature nested within a wider ground, changing its standing and leaving its full content in place — the "include" half of the law.

## 1. The map — the new ground stated plainly

### The new ground

The new ground is a position and a stance, never a claim about results. It states where the book stands and whose office the facilitation is; it asserts nothing about what the book produces in a reader, in keeping with the no-efficacy stance the vault holds as non-negotiable (`ops/methodology/authorial stance.md`, the directive that the vault does not postulate efficacy). Five seeds and two moves carry it, and each already lives as a first-class note.

The five ground seeds:

- **The ecology of loci, none sovereign.** The whole inquiry takes the shape of an ecology — a bounded but permeable field of multiple irreducible loci of participation, each disclosing what another cannot, none of them sovereign — recorded at [[an ecology is a bounded but permeable domain of multiple irreducible loci of participation, none sovereign]] (the field-definition seed), grounded verbatim in the transcript ("a living field in which multiple loci can disclose, encounter, challenge, and transform one another without any single locus becoming sovereign"; reframe conversation, 2026-08-13, line 4161, long form at line 4157).
- **The book as one non-sovereign locus.** The book takes a single position inside that field, auxiliary to a sovereign reader rather than the source or certifier of the reader's recognition, recorded at [[the book is one locus in an ecology of participation, not the sovereign source of the reader's recognition]] (the seed that dissolves the trust-the-author posture by removing the sovereign seat entirely).
- **No locus sovereign over what it describes.** The constraint stated for frameworks — the reason no locus, the book's included, may rule the rest — recorded at [[no single locus or framework may become sovereign over the reality it describes]] (the none-sovereign clause on its own).
- **The recursive meta-facilitation position.** The book stands two steps back from the outcome: it tends the conditions for the reader's own facilitation of recognition rather than facilitating transformation, presence, or recognition directly, recorded at [[the book tends conditions for the reader's own facilitation of recognition rather than facilitating transformation]] (the two-steps-back seed, the author's own words: "it tends the conditions for the reader's own facilitation of presence, arriving, and recognition. It is two steps back from the outcome"; author direction, 2026-08-14).
- **The un-verifiability limit made central.** The book cannot confirm the reader has done the work, and honoring that inability is a core trust the book builds in rather than a defect to remedy, recorded at [[the book must respect that it cannot verify the reader has done the work]] (the seed that relocates the outcome to the reader, whose participation no text can observe or certify).

The two moves that operate the ground:

- **The reader-facilitator owns the outcome.** The activity is the reader's own opportunity and responsibility, the prerequisite condition of their outcomes, recorded at [[taking up the activity is the reader-facilitator's own responsibility and the precondition of their outcomes]] and, turned on the self, at [[self-facilitation is creating conditions for one's own becoming, not self-management, optimization, or analysis]] (the practice mode for the self). The outcome was never the book's to produce or verify.
- **The recursion re-nests the old frame without discarding it.** The ecology frame transcends and includes the verification frame: the larger frame preserves the smaller without collapsing it, and the move applies to itself so the ecology does not become the new sovereign, recorded at [[the ecology frame transcends and includes the verification frame rather than replacing it]] (the master re-nesting node, which already names the four kept tensions and marks each moved from ground to guardrail).

### The transcend-and-include law as it now applies to this vault

The law has two halves and two failure modes, and the plan is built to obey both.

**Transcend:** the ecology and meta-facilitation frame becomes the ground. In the maps and the blessed ground-layer seeds it is stated as the frame rather than held as a late section beside the old frame, and the axioms sit beneath even the seeds as the progenitor bedrock.

**Include:** nothing in the current frame is deleted, refuted, or softened. The four apophatic tensions, the felt-shift standard, the second chair, and the register hazard remain real and live; each is preserved entire and re-nested as a feature or guardrail within the new ground. No note loses content; only its standing changes.

The two failure modes the plan must avoid, stated so the author can check the drafts against them:

1. **Do not delete or soften a current-frame note.** That is not "include." Every re-nesting paragraph in section 2 is purely additive, and section 3 adds edges rather than cutting them — the inventory's own instruction, since cutting an edge would be delete, not include.
2. **Do not answer the old anxiety on its own terms.** Defending the felt-shift standard against the confabulation literature, or proving the Second Chair suffices, re-entrenches the sovereign-author ground the reframe dissolves. The move is always to re-ground: state the new ground first, then show the old concern nested within it. Where an existing note already answers an anxiety on its own terms — the introspective-access note narrows what the shift certifies to shrink the introspective demand — the plan does not remove that answer (include forbids it); it adds a clause that re-nests the answer as a guardrail on a feature, so the defense stops reading as a rescue of the ground.
