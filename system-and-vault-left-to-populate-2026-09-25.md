# Left to populate: the places the design defines that the vault does not yet fill

**What this document is.** `reframe-map-and-system-2026-09-25.md` designs, from the transcript of the reframe conversation alone, a list of the kinds of material the two books need. This document lists the places in that design that the research vault leaves empty or nearly empty. For each one, it says what exists, how the absence was established, and who can fill it. It answers the third of three questions the author asked on 2026-09-25, in his words: "where in the system is left to populate the existing material does not have any details or information." The first companion document, `system-and-vault-collation-2026-09-25.md`, sorts the vault's existing material into the design. The second, `system-and-vault-beyond-the-design-2026-09-25.md`, describes the vault material that the design does not cover and how the design would be extended to include it; several of the empty places below exist only in that extended design. All three documents draw on eight readers' reports, which are kept with the readers' instructions in `ops/system readings — 2026-09-25/` and cited here by label (O1 to O4, F1 to F4). The collation's Part 10 gives the key to the labels.

**How to read an empty place.** An empty place is not a failure. Some places are empty by design, because only the author can fill them. Some are empty by the author's own decision. And some are empty because nobody has filled them yet, and a reading, a drafting session, an extraction from the corpus in `sources/`, or an extraction from the transcript can fill them. This document sorts every empty place into one of those three groups, and it orders the work by what Book I, the book for facilitators, which is written first, needs soonest. The order is a proposal, and the author decides.

**Names used.** This document uses the plain names the design now uses: topics, pairs, practices, images, encounters, traditions and thinkers, comparisons, the author's views, writing techniques, drafted passages, and books; the author's level, whose lowest value is "proposed"; the commitments; the open questions; and the topic table. The collation's Part 2 defines each one. Four of the vault's own words recur below. A *topic map* is a page gathering the claims of one concern. *Company* means thinkers who reached the same finding independently; the book cites them as others who noticed the same thing, not as evidence that it is true. A *tension* is a disagreement inside the book's doctrine, kept open in a note rather than resolved. The *decisions register* is the table of settled questions in `ops/decisions.md`.

## 3. Book I: the book for facilitators

Book I is written first, and the vault's orientation page says the next piece of work is Book I's drafting document for the relational field (`ops/orientation.md`). Book I's own needs, by the design's Part 6, are cases from groups the author has facilitated, its own writing techniques shown to the reader, and material on the practice of facilitation and on power. All three are among the emptiest entries (O2).

### 1. Book I's main material is the model's outline

**What exists.** The relational-field topic map gives Book I fifteen notes on facilitation craft. All fifteen are stated but not yet backed by a reading, cite only the reframe conversation, and cite the name of a section in one of the model's outlines rather than a transcript line. None says whose words it contains. Reader O2 checked six key sentences against the transcript, and all six are inside the model's responses: in the two-book handoff at line 13056, and in the chapter lists at lines 15489, 15534, 15577, 16020, and 16777. The vault's survey of the second person calls one of them, "Do not solve what the participant has not yet had the opportunity to encounter" (15227), "the author's own craft" (`ops/second person ecology.md`, line 57); it is the model's.

**What is empty.** The author's own facilitation is not in the notes.
- His description of it at transcript line 214, "being present and welcoming what is and then also guiding, directing, moving… following that, tracking that, not getting attached to the impartiality, but also having a certain agency or telos," is quoted only in the file at the repository root `reframe-deep-harvest-2026-08-20.md`.
- His line 244, that "the framing of the book is meant to facilitate and not like change, fix or heal or like proclaim righteousness," appears nowhere in `notes/`.
- A search for "agency or telos" and "proclaim righteousness" in `notes/` finds nothing (O2; checked again).

**Who can fill it.** An extraction from the transcript can record his lines 214, 244, and 934 ("And so that's that's my book") as his own words, at the level "experienced," without a session raising anything. The fifteen notes need a mark saying their words are the model's.

### 2. No group session he facilitated is recorded

**What exists.** The notes record that he practices facilitation, and they record no group session he facilitated. The line on his own tradition in the list of thinkers says he "trained as an Authentic Relating facilitator in Houston, held a facilitation community there before moving to Seattle" (`notes/interlocutors.md`, line 249), and the note on his move to Seattle dates the Houston training by his own statement of 2026-08-03 (`notes/the book was written across the author's move to seattle to live in earnest.md`). One sentence in the list of thinkers names one event: "an event of roughly thirty-five people in May 2026, run from a script of five agreements, trio rotations with a witness role, and a closing circle" (`notes/interlocutors.md`, line 249). The provenance record has more, all of it outside the notes (O2):
- the event of 16 May 2026, and the prompt he chose that night: "Think of someone who loves you. Describe how they see you." (`provenance/provenance of the corpus round two.md`);
- a design for an exercise in groups of three, dated 12 March 2026;
- the community he left when he moved to Seattle, "whose details (name, city, duration, cadence) are not in the searchable record" (`provenance/provenance of the corpus round two.md`).

The corpus itself named what is missing: "the missing room is not exotic. It’s your own workshop." (`provenance/provenance of the corpus.md`, marked [C], the AI collaborator's words). The one group event the notes record is the Landmark Forum of 2017, where he was a participant.

**How the absence was established.** Reader O2 searched `notes/`, `archive/`, `reflections/`, `drafts/`, and `ops/` for "houston," "circling," "authentic relating," "in a room," "thirty-five," "five agreements," "trio rotation," "closing circle," "may 2026," phrases for a facilitated room, session, group, or workshop, and the text of the prompt. The prompt appears only in `provenance/`.

**Who can fill it.** Only the author can supply his group sessions and his note on each one. A session can copy the provenance material into encounter records, leaving the note blank for him, under his three conditions for personal material. One observation for him to consider: the prompt he chose on 16 May puts the relation described in the letter from his first love into practice, as an exercise for a group.

### 3. The thinkers on facilitation have no thinker pages

**What exists.**

**Who can fill it.** Readings of primary texts can fill it: Grove's Clean Language, the method the design cites for function 3 ("the facilitator can constrain the form of inquiry while remaining radically unconstrained about its content," transcript line 1709); hooks's *Teaching to Transgress*, already proposed for the reading list; Freire; and Heron and Reason. ## 4. Book II: the return, the practices, and the reader alone

### 1. The return

**What exists.** "The return has no first-class claim — no dedicated 'the practitioner returns / stewardship of the self' note exists; it is carried by the arc's integration window" (`the-two-books.md`, as F2 quotes it). `ops/scaffold.md` lists, in §14, seven points where the vault's readings of the book's structure differ or the material is thin (it calls them "open joints"); the second says "Book II's return is the thinnest position in the whole graph." The design, working from the transcript alone, found Integration the topic with the fewest entries. The author's one decision here is his choice of 2026-08-20, "peak resolves into return."

**Who can fill it.** The author can, in his own words on the return. A reading of al-Junayd's own texts (reading 15 in the survey of the arc) can add to it, and so can the writing of the descent and integration in the drafts: the survey of the arc names "descent craft" as an empty entry, which one of the removed notes on writing technique had filled ("The peak passes. The loops re-couple. Time returns to its ordinary motion. The body becomes, again, this body," O4).

### 2. Practices small enough to remain alive

**What exists.** The design's rule for the smallest practice, "Practice should be small enough to remain alive" (16499), has no note; a search of `notes/` finds nothing (F1). The practices the transcript names for Book II have no notes:
- the questions a person asks as their own facilitator (13620–13632); "What is moving here?" appears only in `notes/the learning arc.md`;
- noticing moments of contraction and expansion (1037);
- "try this on" (176).

**Who can fill it.** An extraction from the transcript can fill it at the level "proposed," and the author judges what is small enough.

### 3. Criteria for finishing, and what moves a reader from one phase to the next

**What exists.** Neither exists. "Nothing states what moves a person between phases," and of criteria for an exit the vault "owns one, for the word" (`ops/arc ecology.md`, §6, as O4 quotes it). What the book offers the reader instead is "a non-criterion, 'If you finish quickly, you have not finished.'"

**Who can fill it.** The readings planned in the survey of the arc (Ignatius §336, Gendlin, Kolb, and van Gennep) can fill it, and then the author.

### 4. The limit the book's thinkers set on what a reader can do alone

**What exists.** "Roughly nineteen chairs with nothing else in common say *this cannot be done alone*" (`the-constellation.md`; a chair is the vault's word for a thinker's page). The vault's notes on discrimination, meaning the ability to tell a real change from a false one, were checked against primary texts, and they conclude that "a book read alone can state the whole of the restraint and none of the discrimination" (`notes/a solitary reader can run every guard that declines to evaluate and none that discriminates.md`). No field for Book II states this limit, and the design gave Book II only one danger, becoming "another performance target" (14606) (O3).

**Who can fill it.** A drafting session can state the limit in Book II's statement of what it leaves out and in its promise to the reader. Whether and how the book answers it is the open choice recorded in `notes/the solitary form stands against seven witnesses who hold a second person constitutive.md`, where the seven witnesses are the seven thinkers who hold that the inner life needs a second person. That choice is the author's.

### 5. Book II's promise to the reader, and its method named at the end

**What exists.** The design's promise for Book II, "I will not tell you how to become a better version of yourself. I will invite you to develop a different relationship with the fact that you are already becoming" (17077), and its method named at the end ("Now the book finally names what the reader has been learning," 16658) have no note (F2). The drafts of Book II's arc name the system in their second part instead, and how much of that part remains, now that the system has been ranked as secondary, is the author's decision (the second companion document, Part 2, item C16).
