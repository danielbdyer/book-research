# Left to populate: the places the system defines that the vault does not yet fill

*This document records where the system in `reframe-map-and-system-2026-09-25.md` has places that the research vault leaves empty or thin, and says, for each, what exists, how the absence was established, and who can fill it. It is the third of three documents written on 2026-09-25 from one study. The first, `system-and-vault-collation-2026-09-25.md`, places the vault's existing material inside the system. The second, `system-and-vault-beyond-the-design-2026-09-25.md`, records what the vault holds beyond the design and how the design grows to receive it, and several empty places below exist only in the grown design. The eight reader reports and their brief are kept in `ops/system readings — 2026-09-25/`, and they are cited by label (O1 to O4, F1 to F4); the collation's Part X gives the key.*

*An empty place is not a failure. Some are empty by construction, because only the author can fill them. Some are empty by the author's decision. Some are empty by omission, and a reading, a drafting session, an extraction from the corpus in `sources/`, or the transcript can fill them. The document sorts every empty place into one of those three and ranks the work by what Book I, the facilitator book written first, needs soonest. The priorities are proposals; the author decides. Three of the vault's words recur below. *Company* means thinkers who reached the same finding independently, held as company rather than as proof. A *tension* is a disagreement inside the book's doctrine, kept open in a note rather than resolved. The decisions register is the table of settled questions in `ops/decisions.md`.*

## How to read this

- **Part I** states the finding in brief.
- **Part II** covers the author's own layer, which only he can fill.
- **Part III** covers Book I.
- **Part IV** covers Book II.
- **Part V** lists the empty cells of the per-concern profile, concern by concern.
- **Part VI** lists the types and fields that have no instances.
- **Part VII** lists what the transcript holds that the vault never took in.
- **Part VIII** lists the readings the books need.
- **Part IX** lists faults found on the way: places where the vault misattributes words, points at notes that no longer exist, or has gone stale. None is the design's, but each matters before drafting.
- **Part X** gathers everything by who can fill it, in Book I's order.
